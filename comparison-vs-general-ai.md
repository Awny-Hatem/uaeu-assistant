# UAEU Assistant vs a general AI model

Generated 2026-11-17 from `test-cases/comparison-50-v3.json` and `test-cases/comparison-50-v3-graded.json`.

## Method

- **Questions:** 50 matched questions from the live test set: 2 personalized questions for each of the 10 fictional tester accounts, the 12 course-code phrasing variants, 10 program-directory questions and 8 guest questions.
- **Our system:** the full UAEU Assistant (retrieval over official UAEU pages, verified answers, cohort study plans, the student's saved academic record, source checking).
- **General AI:** the same base model (gpt-4.1-mini) with no retrieval, browsing or UAEU documents. For personalized questions it received the same fictional student record as text.
- **Grading:** for each question a judge model (gpt-4.1-mini) saw both answers in random order (A/B, not labelled), the test rubric and an official reference built from the project's official data (the student's cohort plan and record, the official program directory, catalog course records). It scored correctness (0–2), completeness (0–2), personalization (0–1) and hallucination. Deterministic checks were added: official uaeu.ac.ae link, course codes that do not exist in UAEU's catalog, outdated names, reply language.
- **Hand review:** every case the general model won was read by hand against the official source; judge mistakes are labelled below rather than silently changed.

## Why our system wins

- **Grounded in official UAEU documents.** Every factual answer comes from official uaeu.ac.ae pages, plans or catalog records, with the link shown. The general model cites an official link far less often and fills gaps with plausible-sounding guesses.
- **No invented courses or programs.** The general model used course codes that do not exist at UAEU and mislabelled real ones (for example it called CSPB 219 "Cell Biology" with a made-up prerequisite). Our system maps the typo to CSBP219 and gives the catalog prerequisite, CSBP119 with a minimum grade of D.
- **Cohort-specific study plans.** Our answers use the exact published model plan for the student's catalog year and entry season (224 parsed plans, 45 programs) and say when a plan's printed totals don't reconcile. The general model has no access to these plans and guesses semester contents.
- **Profile and history awareness.** With the saved record, credit counts follow the audit rules (unique passing applied courses, in-progress counted separately) and are broken down by requirement block; next semester is worked out from the student's position.
- **Current names.** Answers use the College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence and Computer and Artificial Intelligence Engineering, from the current official pages.
- **Honest boundaries.** When a fact is not in an official source the system says so instead of inventing it, and it never claims access to Degree Works or the portal.

## Limits of this comparison

- 50 questions drawn from our own test set; they favour UAEU-specific and personalized questions, which is where a general model is weakest.
- The judge is itself an LLM (gpt-4.1-mini). Every general-model win was checked by hand; 4 of the 6 were judge errors and are labelled as such above, not removed from the counts.
- The general model is the same base model as ours. A stronger general assistant with web browsing could do better on public pages, though it would still lack the student's record and the parsed cohort plans.
- Personalized questions use fictional accounts, not real student records.
