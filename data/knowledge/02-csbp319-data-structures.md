# CSBP319 Data Structures

The current UAEU course catalog identifies **CSBP319 Data Structures** as a three-credit course.

## Course content

The official description covers structured data and its internal representation, operations on data structures, and applications. Topics include **arrays, strings, linked structures, files, lists, stacks, queues, trees, heaps, and graphs**, together with **recursion** and related data-structure algorithms.

## Prerequisites and corequisites

- **CSBP219** is a prerequisite with a minimum grade of **D**.
- **CSBP221** is a prerequisite or corequisite with a minimum grade of **D**; it may be taken before or alongside CSBP319 as allowed by the registration system.

Course codes and study plans can differ by catalog year or student cohort. Students should compare the current catalog entry with their own degree audit or study plan before registration.
