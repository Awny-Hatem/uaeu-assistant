# Academic calendar 2026–2027

These are the dates published in the UAEU academic calendar as inspected on 29 September 2026, scoped only to academic year 2026–2027. They are not admissions application deadlines and must not be reused for another year.

## Fall 2026 dates shown on the calendar

- Classes start: **24 August 2026**.
- Last day to add courses: **28 August 2026**.
- Last day to drop a course without failure: **18 September 2026**.
- Leave-of-absence and withdrawal deadline: **2 October 2026**.
- Midterm examinations: **5–16 October 2026**.
- Registration for Spring 2027 begins: **2 November 2026**.
- Last day of classes: **1 December 2026**.
- Final examinations: **6–15 December 2026**.
- Final grades: **18 December 2026**.

## Spring 2027

The calendar lists **4 January 2027** as the start of Spring 2027 classes. Students must use the live calendar and their portal for any later revision and for deadlines that depend on their program or individual schedule.
