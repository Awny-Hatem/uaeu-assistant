# Academic calendar 2026–2027

The live UAEU academic calendar is the controlling source for term, registration, withdrawal, examination, and holiday dates.

## Fall 2026 dates shown on the calendar

- Classes began on **24 August 2026**.
- The last day to add courses was **28 August 2026**.
- The last day to drop a course without failure was **18 September 2026**.
- The leave-of-absence and withdrawal deadline was **2 October 2026**.
- Midterm examinations were scheduled for **5–16 October 2026**.
- Registration for Spring 2027 was scheduled to begin on **2 November 2026**.
- The last day of classes was **1 December 2026**.
- Final examinations were scheduled for **6–15 December 2026**.
- Final grades were scheduled for **18 December 2026**.

## Spring 2027

The calendar lists **4 January 2027** as the start of Spring 2027 classes. Students must use the live calendar and their portal for any later revision and for deadlines that depend on their program or individual schedule.
