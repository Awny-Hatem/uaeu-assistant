# Current Students Documents service

Enrolled students can request official student documents through the UAEU portal or mobile application. The service lists Arabic or English transcripts, schedule letters, student-account letters, and To Whom It May Concern letters among the available documents.

## Process and service level

Sign in through an official UAEU channel, choose the Current Students Documents service and document type, review the request, and submit it. The completed document is sent by email. The current Service Catalog lists the service as free with a delivery time of about ten minutes. An older official guide for generating enrolled-student documents says an original transcript costs AED 10 and takes one working day. Public sources do not establish whether this reflects a different format or a superseded fee. For a transcript request, verify the current fee, format, and delivery estimate in the authenticated portal before submitting; contact Registration at 800-8238 if unclear. The older guide is https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/pdf/generating_enrolled_students_documents.pdf.

A To Whom It May Concern letter issued through this service is valid for three months. Alumni document requests use a separate alumni service.
