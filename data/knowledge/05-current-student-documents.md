# Current Students Documents service

Enrolled students can request official student documents through the UAEU portal or mobile application. The service lists Arabic or English transcripts, schedule letters, student-account letters, and To Whom It May Concern letters among the available documents.

## Process and service level

Sign in through an official UAEU channel, choose the Current Students Documents service and document type, review the request, and submit it. The completed document is sent by email. The public service catalog lists the service as free with a normal processing time of about ten minutes.

A To Whom It May Concern letter issued through this service is valid for three months. Alumni document requests use a separate alumni service.
