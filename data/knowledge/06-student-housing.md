# Student housing

Eligible students apply for a room through the official UAEU portal or mobile application, authenticate with UAE Pass or UAEU credentials as directed, pay any applicable charge, select an available building and room, and monitor their UAEU email for the result. The catalog labels the electronic service completion duration as **5 minutes** and service delivery as **immediate**; those labels do not guarantee room availability or assignment.

## Eligibility and documents

The service catalog states that a new student generally must be enrolled and live more than 50 kilometres outside Al Ain. A **current student requesting a transfer between housing units** must be enrolled, continuing at UAEU, and have completed at least **60 credit hours**. Transfers open before the beginning of each semester.

For a transfer supported by a medical report, the report must be issued by a government hospital or approved specialist and be no more than one month old. The specialized Medical Committee meets every Thursday, and the service says students are notified within two working days after that meeting. Exact eligibility still depends on student category, availability, and the live service rules.

## Listed charges for international students without a housing scholarship

- Undergraduate housing: **AED 5,000 per semester**.
- Postgraduate housing: **AED 5,600 per semester**.
- Summer housing: **AED 1,575**.

Students should confirm the amount shown for their own category before payment because eligibility, room availability, and charges can change.
