# Bachelor of Science in Information Security

The published 120-credit degree structure comprises general education 21, college requirements 41, major requirements 46, internship 6 and major electives 6. This current structure must not replace a student's older assigned 130-credit plan.

## Major courses

The required major courses are CSBP320 Data Mining; CSBP121 Programming Lab I; CENG210 Communication & Networks Fundamentals; ITBP301 Security Principles & Practice; CSBP221 Programming Lab II; CSBP340 Database Systems; ISEC311 Network Security I; ISEC321 Network Security II; ISEC312 Cryptography; ISEC322 Design and Analysis of Security Protocols; ISEC323 Secure Software Design and Engineering; ISEC324 Cryptography Lab; ISEC411 Privacy and Anonymity; ISEC412 Digital Forensics; ISEC413 Security Architecture and Mechanisms; ISEC414 Network Security Lab; ISEC421 Risk Analysis and Management; ISEC422 Security Policy, Laws, and Governance; and ISEC423 Systems Security Lab.

The 6 major-elective credits are two courses from ISEC416, ISEC417, ISEC424, ISEC428 and ITBP280. Not every elective is necessarily offered each semester.

## Internship and individual planning

ITBP496 Internship carries 6 credits in this curriculum. The program page explicitly places internship in the last semester and prohibits registering other courses during it. ITBP480 and ITBP481 are college-required senior projects, not substitutes for internship.

A personalized remaining-semester plan needs the assigned cohort/catalog, completed and in-progress requirements, any approved equivalencies and intended term. Do not ask for a student ID or full transcript when a list of remaining requirements is sufficient.

## أمن المعلومات

تتكون الخطة الحالية المنشورة لبكالوريوس أمن المعلومات من 120 ساعة: 21 للتعليم العام، و41 لمتطلبات الكلية، و46 للتخصص، و6 للتدريب، و6 لاختيارات التخصص. من المساقات: أمن الشبكات، التشفير، الأدلة الجنائية الرقمية، تحليل المخاطر، الخصوصية، وتصميم البرمجيات الآمنة. التدريب ITBP496 في الفصل الأخير، ولا يسمح بتسجيل مساقات أخرى خلاله. قد تختلف خطة الدفعات الأقدم؛ يلزم معرفة خطة الدفعة والمتطلبات المتبقية لتخصيص الجدول.
