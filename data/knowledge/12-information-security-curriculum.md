# Bachelor of Science in Information Security

The published 120-credit degree structure comprises general education 21, college requirements 41, major requirements 46, internship 6 and major electives 6. This current structure must not replace a student's older assigned 130-credit plan.

## Major courses

The current program catalog names the required courses as CSBP320 Data Mining; CSBP121 Programming Lab I; CENG210 Communication & Networks Fundamentals; ITBP301 Security Principles & Practice; CSBP221 Programming Lab II; CSBP340 Database Systems; ISEC311 Network Security I; ISEC321 Network Systems Security; ISEC312 Cryptography; ISEC322 Design and Analysis of Security Protocols; ISEC323 Secure Software Design and Engineering; ISEC324 Cryptography Lab; ISEC411 Privacy and Anonymity; ISEC412 Digital Forensics; ISEC413 Security Architecture and Mechanisms; ISEC414 Network Security Lab; ISEC421 Risk Analysis and Management; ISEC422 Security Policy, Laws, and Governance; and ISEC423 Systems Security Lab.

**Official-source title discrepancy:** the linked 2025–2026 cohort model-plan PDF labels ISEC321 “Network Security II,” while the current degree catalog labels it “Network Systems Security.” Keep both source labels visible when relevant; do not infer a different course from the title alone. The student's assigned Degree Works plan is the reference for their cohort.

The 6 major-elective credits are two courses from ISEC416, ISEC417, ISEC424, ISEC428 and ITBP280. Not every elective is necessarily offered each semester.

## Internship and individual planning

ITBP496 Internship carries 6 credits in this curriculum and is placed in the last semester. The current degree page says no courses are allowed during internship; the linked 2025-onward cohort plan says additional courses are not allowed without College approval. Preserve this source difference and direct students to confirm any exception with CIT advising. ITBP480 and ITBP481 are college-required senior projects, not substitutes for internship.

A personalized remaining-semester plan needs the assigned cohort/catalog, completed and in-progress requirements, any approved equivalencies and intended term. Do not ask for a student ID or full transcript when a list of remaining requirements is sufficient.

## أمن المعلومات

تضم الخطة الحالية المنشورة لبكالوريوس أمن المعلومات 120 ساعة: 21 للتعليم العام، و41 لمتطلبات الكلية، و46 للتخصص، و6 للتدريب، و6 لاختيارات التخصص. تسرد صفحة البرنامج المقرر ISEC321 باسم **Network Systems Security**، بينما تسميه خطة الدفعة المنشورة لعام 2025–2026 **Network Security II**. هذا اختلاف في التسمية بين مصدرين رسميين؛ لا تستنتج من الاسم وحده وجود مقرر مختلف، وارجع إلى خطة الطالب المعتمدة في Degree Works. قد تتبع الدفعات الأقدم خطة من 130 ساعة. التدريب ITBP496 في الفصل الأخير، ولا يسمح البرنامج بتسجيل مقررات أخرى خلاله.
