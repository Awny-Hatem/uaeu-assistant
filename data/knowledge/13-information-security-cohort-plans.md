# Information Security model plans: cohort matters

The one-page official PDF contains three separately labelled plans. The tables were visually inspected, not merged from their interleaved extracted text.

## 2025–2026 cohort onwards: Fall entrants, 120 credits

Year 4 Semester 8 (Spring), the final taught semester before internship, lists ISEC412 Digital Forensics (3), ISEC422 Security Policy, Laws, and Governance (3), ITBP481 Senior Graduation Project II (3), ISEC413 Security Architecture and Mechanisms (3), and ISEC423 Systems Security Lab (1): 13 credits total.

Year 5 Semester 9 (Fall), the final model semester, contains ITBP496 Internship alone: 6 credits. It is not the same as Semester 8.

## 2022–2023 cohort onwards: older 130-credit tables

The older Fall-entry and Spring-entry tables each total 130 credits. Their ninth semester contains ITBP495 Internship, 12 credits, rather than the newer ITBP496 six-credit internship. The Fall-entry plan labels semester 9 Fall; the Spring-entry plan labels semester 9 Spring. These older tables coexist in the PDF with the newer cohort table; the student's assigned plan determines applicability.

## Personalization boundary

These are published model sequences, not confirmation that a student may register a particular course or that it is offered in the intended term. Ask for cohort, entry semester, outstanding/completed requirements and intended term before personalizing. If the user means the final taught semester, distinguish it from the internship semester. Do not infer cohort from being a final-year student.

## الفصل الأخير في أمن المعلومات

لخطة دفعة 2025–2026 وما بعدها للملتحقين في الخريف، الفصل التاسع والأخير هو ITBP496 تدريب عملي من 6 ساعات. أما الفصل الثامن السابق للتدريب فيضم ISEC412 وISEC422 وITBP481 وISEC413 بواقع 3 ساعات لكل منها، وISEC423 بساعة واحدة، بإجمالي 13 ساعة. تعرض الوثيقة أيضاً خططاً أقدم من 130 ساعة تضم ITBP495 تدريباً من 12 ساعة. حدد خطة دفعتك وهل تقصد فصل المساقات الأخير أم فصل التدريب، ثم المتطلبات المتبقية لتحديد ما يناسبك.
