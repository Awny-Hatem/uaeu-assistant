# Campus vehicle access permit — female students from Al Ain

This service applies specifically to female UAEU students from Al Ain who need access to designated parking facilities within the university campus.

It must not be treated as a general parking policy for all UAEU students, visitors, employees, or other student categories.

## Eligibility

According to UAEU, the student must:

- Be admitted to UAEU.
- Be from Al Ain City.

## Service

The permit enables eligible female students to access parking facilities designated for them within the university campus.

The service is provided by the Campus Supervision Office.

## Cost and processing

- Service fee: Free.
- Service completion: Immediate.
- No documents are listed as required on the current service page.

## Procedure

The UAEU service page states that students receive an activation notification and then download the authorized application to their mobile device to use the service.
