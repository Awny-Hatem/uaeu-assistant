# Bachelor of Science in Information Technology — study plan

This record contains the published UAEU curriculum requirements for the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology.

It should be used when answering questions about required IT courses, college requirements, major requirements, or the overall structure of the Information Technology degree.

## College requirements

- CENG202 — Discrete Mathematics — 3 credit hours
- CENG205 — Digital Design & Computer Organization — 3 credit hours
- CSBP119 — Algorithms and Problem Solving — 3 credit hours
- CSBP219 — Object Oriented Programming — 3 credit hours
- CSBP315 — Operating Systems Fundamentals — 3 credit hours
- CSBP319 — Data Structures — 3 credit hours
- STAT210 — Probability and Statistics — 3 credit hours
- MATH105 — Calculus I — 3 credit hours
- MATH110 — Calculus II — 3 credit hours
- PHYS105 — General Physics I — 3 credit hours
- BIOC100 — Basic Biology I — 3 credit hours
- CHEM111 — General Chemistry I — 3 credit hours
- ITBP270 — Professional Responsibility in Information Technology — 2 credit hours
- ITBP480 — Senior Graduation Project I — 3 credit hours
- ITBP481 — Senior Graduation Project II — 3 credit hours

The catalog states that either BIOC100 or CHEM111 should be taken.

## Major requirements

- CSBP121 — Programming Lab I — 1 credit hour
- CSBP221 — Programming Lab II — 1 credit hour
- CSBP316 — Human Computer Interaction — 3 credit hours
- CSBP340 — Database Systems — 3 credit hours
- CSBP301 — Artificial Intelligence — 3 credit hours
- CSBP320 — Data Mining — 3 credit hours
- CENG210 — Communication & Networks Fundamentals — 3 credit hours
- CENG530 — Computer Network Protocols — 3 credit hours
- CENG529 — Networking Lab — 1 credit hour
- ITBP280 — Information Technology Project Management Exhibition — 3 credit hours
- ITBP301 — Security Principles & Practice — 3 credit hours
- ITBP321 — Web Application Development Lab — 1 credit hour
- ITBP322 — Web and Mobile Systems — 3 credit hours
- ITBP323 — Systems Integration and Administration — 3 credit hours
- ITBP324 — Cloud Computing Fundamentals — 3 credit hours

Questions about course prerequisites should use the dedicated official course record where available, because this study-plan record lists curriculum requirements but does not define all prerequisite relationships.
