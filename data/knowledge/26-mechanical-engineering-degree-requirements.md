# Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering — published degree requirements

The current UAEU program page states a minimum of 132 credit hours. A student's assigned cohort plan and degree audit control individual graduation requirements; older cohorts may differ.

## Mechanical Engineering degree credit breakdown

- General education (university requirements): 21 credit hours.
- College of Engineering required courses: 35 credit hours.
- Mechanical Engineering required specialization courses: 68 credit hours.
- Internships MECH485 and MECH490: 2 credit hours in total.
- Mechanical Engineering specialization electives: 6 credit hours (two courses).
- Total: 132 credit hours.

## Mechanical Engineering required college courses

CHEM111 General Chemistry I (3); CHEM175 Chemistry Lab I for Engineering (1); MATH105 Calculus I (3); MATH110 Calculus II (3); MATH210 Calculus III (3); MATH140 Linear Algebra I (3); MATH275 Ordinary Differential Equations (3); STAT210 Probability and Statistics (3); PHYS105 General Physics I (3); PHYS110 General Physics II (3); PHYS135 General Physics Lab I (1); PHYS140 General Physics Lab II (1); GENG215 Engineering Ethics (2); GENG315 Engineering Economics (3). The 2026–27 model plan lists MATH130/MATH135 instead of MATH105/MATH110, so students must use their own cohort plan.

## Mechanical Engineering required general education components

The 21-credit general-education block includes GEEM110 Contemporary Emirati Studies (3), GEAE101 Academic English for Humanities and STEM (3), GEIE222 Fundamentals of Innovation and Entrepreneurship (3), one approved Theme 3 Innovation course (3; ELEC290 or GEIT113 on the program page), one approved Theme 5 Sustainability course (3; CIVL290 or GESU121), and 6 credits chosen from Themes 7–10 with at most 3 from each theme. The choice groups are requirements, but their individual options are not all mandatory.

## Mechanical Engineering required major courses

MECH200 Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Workshop (1); CIVL240 Statics (3); MECH210 Measurement and Instrumentation Lab (1); MECH240 Introduction to Computing Lab in ME (1); MECH305 Mechanics of Materials (3); MECH306 Manufacturing Processes (3); MECH395 Materials and Manufacturing Lab (1); MECH310 Dynamics (3); MECH311 Applied Thermodynamics (3); MECH315 Geometric Modeling (2); MECH340 Fluid Mechanics (3); MECH348 Fluid Mechanics Lab (1); MECH350 Introduction to Mechatronics (3); MECH390 Engineering Materials (3); MECH407 Machine Design I (3); MECH409 Dynamic Systems and Control (3); MECH411 Heat Transfer for Mechanical Engineering (3); MECH412 Machine Design II (3); MECH417 Kinematics Design of Machinery (3); MECH426 Thermofluid System Design and Analysis (3); MECH430 Thermal Engineering Lab (1); MECH433 Introduction to Computer Aided Manufacturing (3); MECH450 Dynamic Systems and Control Lab (1); MECH585 Design and Critical Thinking in Mechanical Engineering (3); MECH590 Capstone Engineering Design Project (3); ELEC372 Electro-Mechanical Devices (2); GENG220 Engineering Thermodynamics (3); GENG230 Computer Programming (3). These are required, not major electives. Prerequisites and corequisites must be checked in individual current course records.

## Mechanical Engineering internship requirement

MECH485 Internship I (1 credit hour) and MECH490 Internship II (1 credit hour) are required. The catalog permits the two courses in two different summers or both in regular Fall/Spring semesters.

## Mechanical Engineering major electives

Complete two courses (6 credit hours) from the published specialization groups; each listed course is an option, not individually mandatory. Design and Manufacturing: MECH520 Selected Topics in Bioengineering, MECH541 Non-conventional Manufacturing, MECH547 Intermediate Mechanics of Material, MECH540 Selected Topics in Design and Manufacturing, MECH545 Maintenance Engineering. Thermo-Fluids: MECH510 Selected Topics in Thermal Sciences, MECH513 Air Conditioning Systems, MECH514 Heat Engines, MECH516 Energy Management, MECH517 Turbomachinery. Mechatronics and Control: MECH515 Optimization Methods in Engineering Systems, MECH530 Selected Topics in Mechatronics, MECH531 Introduction to Robotics, MECH532 Design of Mechatronics Systems, MECH533 Mechanical Vibration. Combustion and Propulsion: AERO562 Rocket Propulsion, AERO563 Kinetics and Thermodynamics of Gases. Each is 3 credit hours. Confirm availability and any group/cohort rules with the advisor.
