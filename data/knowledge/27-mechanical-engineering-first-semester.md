# Mechanical Engineering first-semester model plans

These are recommended Fall-entry model sequences, not registration guarantees. Match the student's admission cohort and entry term before applying one.

## Mechanical Engineering first semester — Fall 2025–26 cohort

The Fall-entry 2025–26 model plan has 17 credit hours in Year 1, Semester 1: MATH105 Calculus I (3, college requirement); PHYS105 General Physics I (3, college); PHYS135 General Physics Lab I (1, college); GEAE101 Academic English for Humanities and STEM (3, general education); CHEM111 General Chemistry I (3, specialization in model plan); CHEM175 Chemistry Lab I for Engineering (1, college); GEEM110 Contemporary Emirati Studies (3, general education). The plan's total degree credit hours are 132. The model plan does not establish all registration prerequisites or corequisites.

## Mechanical Engineering first semester — Fall 2026–27 cohort

The Fall-entry 2026–27 model plan has 17 credit hours in Year 1, Semester 1: MATH130 Calculus I for Engineering (3, college requirement); PHYS105 General Physics I (3, college); PHYS135 General Physics Lab I (1, college); GEAE101 Academic English for Humanities and STEM (3, general education); CHEM111 General Chemistry I (3, specialization in model plan); CHEM175 Chemistry Lab I for Engineering (1, college); GEEM110 Contemporary Emirati Studies (3, general education). The plan's total degree credit hours are 132. This cohort uses MATH130, not the MATH105 shown for Fall 2025–26.
