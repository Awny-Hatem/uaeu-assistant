# Bachelor of Science in Computer Science — published catalog requirements

## Computer Science degree credit breakdown

The published minimum is 120 credit hours: 21 general education (university requirements), 41 college requirements, 38 major requirements, 6 internship, 12 major electives (four courses), and 2 free elective credit hours. Cohort-specific degree audits govern individual graduation.

## Computer Science required college courses

CSBP219 Object Oriented Programming (3); CSBP119 Algorithms and Problem Solving (3); MATH105 Calculus I (3); PHYS105 General Physics I (3); CENG202 Discrete Mathematics (3); CENG205 Digital Design and Computer Organization (3); CSBP319 Data Structures (3); CSBP315 Operating Systems Fundamentals (3); MATH110 Calculus II (3); STAT210 Probability and Statistics (3); either BIOC100 Basic Biology I or CHEM111 General Chemistry I (3); ITBP270 Professional Responsibility in Information Technology (2); ITBP480 Senior Graduation Project I (3); ITBP481 Senior Graduation Project II (3). CSBP319 is a mandatory college requirement for this major, not a major elective. Check its separate course record for prerequisites.

## Computer Science required major courses

CENG210 Communication and Networks Fundamentals (3); ITBP301 Security Principles and Practice (3); CSBP316 Human Computer Interaction (3); ITBP321 Web Application Development Lab (1); CSBP340 Database Systems (3); CSBP301 Artificial Intelligence (3); CSBP400 Modeling and Simulation (3); CSBP411 Machine Learning (3); MATH140 Linear Algebra I (3); CSBP421 Smart Computer Graphics (3); CSBP461 Internet Computing (3); PHYS135 General Physics Lab I (1); SWEB450 Analysis of Algorithms (3); SWEB300 Software Engineering Fundamentals (3). CSBP411 is mandatory, not an elective. Prerequisites and corequisites belong to individual course records.

## Computer Science internship and major electives

ITBP496 Internship is 6 required credit hours in the last semester; the catalog says no other courses may be registered during it. Choose four major electives (12 credits) from CSBP320 Data Mining, CSBP431 Bioinformatics, CSBP476 Robotics and Intelligent Systems, CSBP483 Mobile Web Content and Development, CSBP487 Computer Animation and Visualization, CSBP491 Computational Intelligence for Data Management, CSBP499 Special Topics in Computer Science, SWEB451 Game Development, CSBP441 Applied Computer Vision, and CSBP477 Natural Language Processing. Each listed elective is 3 credits and is an option, not individually mandatory. There are also 2 free elective credits.
