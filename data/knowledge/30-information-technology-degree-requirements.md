# Bachelor of Science in Information Technology — catalog requirements

## Information Technology degree credit breakdown

The published catalog page specifies a minimum of 120 credit hours: 21 general education, 41 college requirements, 37 major requirements, 6 internship, 9 major electives (three courses), and 6 free electives. The separate 2025–26 cohort model plan PDF is labeled 130 total credit hours, so do not apply the catalog's 120-credit total to that cohort without a degree-audit confirmation.

## Information Technology required-course classification

CSBP340 Database Systems is a 3-credit mandatory major requirement, not a major elective. CSBP319 Data Structures is a 3-credit mandatory college requirement. ITBP496 Internship is a 6-credit required course in the last semester, with no other courses allowed during the internship under the catalog. The companion IT study-plan record lists the remaining required college and major courses; consult each official course record for prerequisites and corequisites.

## Information Technology major electives

The catalog requires three courses (9 credit hours) chosen from CSBP483 Mobile Web Content and Development; ISEC411 Privacy and Anonymity; ITBP410 The Internet of Things; ITBP420 Data Analytics; ITBP421 Big Data Analytics; ITBP430 Mobile Computing. Each is a 3-credit elective option, not individually mandatory. There are also 6 free elective credit hours. The 2025–26 model plan PDF has different total credits; use the assigned cohort's degree audit when selecting electives.
