# Information Technology — Fall-entry 2025–26 cohort model

## Information Technology first semester — Fall 2025–26 entry

Year 1, Semester 1 has 16 credit hours: GEAE101 Academic English for Humanities and STEM (3, general education); MATH105 Calculus I (3, college requirement); CSBP119 Algorithms and Problem Solving (3, college); PHYS105 General Physics I (3, college); an approved general-education elective from Theme 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, or 11 (3); CSBP121 Programming Lab I (1, specialization). The PDF labels this 2025–26 cohort plan as 130 total degree credit hours, whereas the catalog program page lists 120. Confirm the assigned cohort plan and degree audit; this model does not establish all course prerequisites or live availability.
