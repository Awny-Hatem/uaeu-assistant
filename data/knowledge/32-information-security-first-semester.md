# Information Security — Fall-entry 2025–26 cohort model

## Information Security first semester — Fall 2025–26 entry

Year 1, Semester 1 has 16 credit hours: GEAE101 Academic English for Humanities and STEM (3, general education); MATH105 Calculus I (3, college requirement); CSBP119 Algorithms and Problem Solving (3, college); PHYS105 General Physics I (3, college); GEIT113 Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals and Applications (3, general education); CSBP121 Programming Lab I (1, specialization). The PDF labels this cohort plan as 120 total degree credit hours. Check the assigned cohort plan and current course prerequisites before registration.

## ISEC421 placement in Information Security 2025–26 plan

ISEC421 Risk Analysis and Management is a 2-credit required specialization course in Year 4, Semester 7 (Fall) of the Fall-entry 2025–26 model plan. This is recommended sequencing, not a guarantee of term availability or personal eligibility. The separate ISEC421 catalog record specifies its prerequisites.
