# Mechanical Engineering course requirements — UAEU course catalog

These course-level rules are from the official UAEU Engineering course catalog. A recommended semester in a model plan is not a substitute for the registration prerequisite or pre/corequisite shown here.

## MECH305 Mechanics of Materials — prerequisite

MECH305 Mechanics of Materials is 3 credit hours and is a required Mechanical Engineering major course. Prerequisite: CIVL240 Statics with a minimum grade of D. The catalog entry does not list a corequisite for MECH305. Do not treat CIVL240 as concurrent permission; the catalog labels it a prerequisite.

## MECH340 Fluid Mechanics — prerequisite

MECH340 Fluid Mechanics is 3 credit hours and is a required Mechanical Engineering major course. Prerequisite: CIVL240 Statics with a minimum grade of D. Taking MECH340 without having completed CIVL240 at the required grade is not supported by the published catalog rule. The catalog entry does not list a corequisite for MECH340.

## MECH348 Fluid Mechanics Lab — prerequisite and pre/corequisite

MECH348 Fluid Mechanics Lab is 1 credit hour and is a required Mechanical Engineering major course. Prerequisite: MECH210 Measurement and Instrumentation Lab with a minimum grade of D. The catalog separately lists MECH340 Fluid Mechanics as "Pre/Co" with a minimum grade of D: MECH340 may be completed beforehand or taken concurrently, subject to the university's enrollment rules. MECH340 therefore has two permitted timings: prior completion or concurrent study. MECH210 remains a strict prior prerequisite that must be completed before MECH348.


توضيح عربي لعلاقات MECH348 في السجل نفسه: يجب إتمام MECH210 مسبقاً بدرجة لا تقل عن D. أما MECH340 فهو متطلب سابق أو متزامن (Pre/Co) بدرجة لا تقل عن D؛ يمكن إتمامه قبل MECH348 أو دراسته معه وفق قواعد التسجيل. لا تعني إمكانية التزامن مع MECH340 إسقاط المتطلب السابق MECH210، ولا يُعد MECH210 متطلباً متزامناً.
