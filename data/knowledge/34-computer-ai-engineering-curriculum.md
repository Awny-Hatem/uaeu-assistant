# Bachelor of Science in Computer and Artificial Intelligence Engineering

The UAEU catalog lists a minimum of 130 credit hours. The published total comprises 21 general-education, 41 college, 44 major, 6 internship, 15 concentration-or-General-Pathway, and 3 free-elective credit hours. Students select one of three alternatives for the 15-credit concentration/pathway requirement: AI Systems Engineering, Robotics and Autonomous Systems, or the General Computer and AI Engineering Pathway.

## General Pathway course choice

The General Pathway requires five courses (15 credits) from SWEB300, CENG530, CENG531, CENG532, CENG533, and CENG580. The catalog identifies CENG532 as Network Security and CENG580 as Selected Topics in Computer Engineering. Both are listed choices in this pathway, so the published list permits choosing CENG532 instead of CENG580, subject to the student's assigned curriculum and remaining pathway requirements. The catalog also says the five-course selection may include up to two courses from AI Systems Engineering and up to two from Robotics and Autonomous Systems.

This substitution is not established for the AI Systems Engineering or Robotics and Autonomous Systems concentration requirements, which each specify their own five-course set. CENG532 must not be renamed "Networking Security" in the answer; the catalog title is Network Security.

## AI Systems Engineering concentration

The 15-credit required course set is CENG500, CENG510, CENG525, CENG540, and CENG550.

## Robotics and Autonomous Systems concentration

The 15-credit required course set is CENG560, CENG570, CENG585, CENG590, and CENG595.

## General education and college choices

General education is 21 credits. The catalog specifies GEEM110, GEAE101, CENG110, ITBP218, and GESU121, plus 6 credits chosen from Themes 6–11 subject to theme limits. A language sequence selected from Theme 11 requires the complementary second course.

College requirements are 41 credits. The student must take either BIOC100 or CHEM111; the other listed college courses are CENG202, CENG205, MATH105, MATH110, PHYS105, STAT210, CSBP119, CSBP219, CSBP315, CSBP319, ITBP270, ITBP480, and ITBP481.

## Required major courses and internship

The required 44-credit major set is CENG201, CENG210, CENG221, CENG231, CENG320, CENG310, CENG324, CENG325, CENG328, CENG329, CENG340, CENG341, CENG529, MATH140, MATH275, PHYS110, PHYS135, PHYS140, CENG315, and ITBP301.

ITBP496 is a 6-credit internship in the last semester. The catalog says students may not register for other courses during the internship.

The catalog describes published requirements and pathways. It does not verify an individual student's assigned cohort, approved substitutions, completed courses, current availability, or registration eligibility. Check Degree Works and the assigned study plan for the student's record.
