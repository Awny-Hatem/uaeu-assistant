# Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering

The UAEU catalog page states a minimum of 131 credit hours: 21 general-education, 41 college, 45 major, 6 internship, 12 major-elective, and 6 free-elective credits. The catalog page itself does not identify the effective cohort; the separate CIT model-plan PDF states that the 131-credit plan applies to students admitted from Fall 2025 onward. The prior Fall/Spring model plans are 144 credits. Keep catalog requirements and semester sequence tied to the applicable source version, and verify the student's assigned plan for transfer, readmission, or other nonstandard cases.

## Computer Engineering elective choices

Students select four courses from the published major-elective list. Both CENG532 Network Security and CENG580 Selected Topics in Computer Engineering appear in that list, so both are eligible choices within the published elective group, subject to the assigned curriculum and remaining degree requirements. The catalog also displays CENG400, CENG410, CENG430 and CENG440 with 0.00 credits and no titles; this record does not treat those placeholders as selectable positive-credit electives.

## Scope

This Computer Engineering catalog is distinct from the Bachelor of Science in Computer and Artificial Intelligence Engineering, which has AI Systems Engineering and Robotics and Autonomous Systems concentrations plus a General Pathway. Do not use those pathways to answer a Computer Engineering question unless the student identifies that program.

The catalog does not establish a student's completed courses, remaining credits, Spring-entry applicability of the revised 2025–2026 plan, current course availability, prerequisites, or registration eligibility. Confirm the assigned study plan in Degree Works.

## Internship code source discrepancy

The current Computer Engineering catalog lists **ITBP496 Internship, 6 credits**, in the last semester and says no other courses may be registered during the internship. The CIT model-plan PDF for Fall 2025 onward labels its 6-credit internship row **ITBP495**. The current course catalog includes ITBP496 as Internship, while the available sources do not establish whether the model-plan code is a typographical error or a cohort-specific code. State the discrepancy when a student asks about the exact internship code and direct them to their assigned Degree Works plan or CIT advising; do not silently merge the two codes.
