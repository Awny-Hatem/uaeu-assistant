# Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering

The UAEU catalog page states a minimum of 131 credit hours: 21 general-education, 41 college, 45 major, 6 internship, 12 major-elective, and 6 free-elective credits. The page was last updated 18 August 2026. This published catalog snapshot does not identify an individual student's assigned cohort plan.

## Computer Engineering elective choices

Students select four courses from the published major-elective list. Both CENG532 Network Security and CENG580 Selected Topics in Computer Engineering appear in that list, so both are eligible choices within the published elective group, subject to the assigned curriculum and remaining degree requirements. The catalog also displays CENG400, CENG410, CENG430 and CENG440 with 0.00 credits and no titles; this record does not treat those placeholders as selectable positive-credit electives.

## Scope

This Computer Engineering catalog is distinct from the Bachelor of Science in Computer and Artificial Intelligence Engineering, which has AI Systems Engineering and Robotics and Autonomous Systems concentrations plus a General Pathway. Do not use those pathways to answer a Computer Engineering question unless the student identifies that program.

The catalog does not establish a student's completed courses, remaining credits, historic cohort applicability, current course availability, prerequisites, or registration eligibility. Confirm the assigned study plan in Degree Works.
