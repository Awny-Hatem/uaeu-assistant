# Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering — historical cohort model plan

The official UAEU model study plan states **144 total degree credit hours** for its listed cohorts: Fall 2022–2023 onward, Spring 2022–2023 onward, revised Fall 2024–2025 onward, and Spring 2023–2024 onward. It presents separate Fall-entry and Spring-entry course sequences, so this summary does not merge them into a single semester plan.

## Relationship to the newer catalog

The separately published Computer Engineering catalog page was updated on 18 August 2026 and states a minimum of **131 credits**. That page does not identify the first cohort to which the 131-credit structure applies. Therefore, the 144-credit plan is retained only for entry cohorts 2022–2025 in this assistant's current cohort lookup; 2026 and later remain unresolved pending an assigned study plan or explicit cohort applicability from UAEU.

## Scope

The PDF is a model sequence, not a personal degree audit. It does not verify the student's assigned plan, completed courses, remaining requirements, current offering, prerequisites, or enrollment eligibility. Confirm the governing plan in Degree Works. Do not use the newer 131-credit elective group to decide a historic cohort's course substitution unless that group is confirmed in the assigned plan.
