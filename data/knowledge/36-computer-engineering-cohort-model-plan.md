# Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering - cohort plans and source conflict

The official UAEU model-plan PDF contains separate 144-credit Fall and Spring plans labeled **2022–2023 cohort onward**. A second official PDF is explicitly labeled **“Cohort fall 2024 and prior”** and also totals 144 credits, but its course codes, titles, and semester sequence differ from those in the 2022–2023-onward document. The published sources overlap in cohort labels and do not explain which revision supersedes the other. The 144-credit record is therefore retained as an unresolved source variant and is not used for requirement-by-requirement planning until UAEU confirms the mapping.

The model-plan PDF also contains a revised **131-credit Fall-entry plan for the 2025–2026 cohort onward**. It explicitly says “For Students Admitted to the University from the Fall Semester”; it does not establish that this revision applies to Spring entrants. Do not merge the Fall and Spring semester orderings or infer applicability from total credits alone.

In the 131-credit plan, the internship row is labeled **ITBP495 (6 credits)**. The current Computer Engineering catalog separately lists **ITBP496 Internship (6 credits)** for the last semester and prohibits other courses during the internship. The sources do not explain this code mismatch. Keep the model-plan row and current catalog listing distinct, and tell students to confirm the applicable code in their assigned Degree Works plan or with CIT advising.

The 2024-2025 revised Fall model plan changes course placement and codes relative to earlier plan pages, even though both list 144 credits. A student's semester-by-semester sequence therefore depends on their entry semester and cohort plan, not just the total-credit figure.

## Relationship to the newer catalog

The separately published Computer Engineering catalog page states a minimum of **131 credits**. The model-plan PDF states that the **2025–2026 cohort onward Fall-entry** uses the 131-credit Fall plan. The older documents both publish 144-credit plans but conflict in their course sequences and overlapping cohort descriptions. Do not infer the revised plan's applicability to Spring entrants or resolve the older source conflict from the catalog total. A student's assigned Degree Works plan remains authoritative for transfer, readmission, and other nonstandard cases.

## Network Security and elective choice

The current catalog page says students choose **four courses** from its Computer Engineering elective list, which includes both **CENG532 Network Security** and **CENG580 Selected Topics in Computer Engineering**. It does not say that one universally replaces the other or that either one is required for every student. For a student's personal substitution or remaining-elective choice, confirm their assigned cohort/pathway and Degree Works audit.

The UAEU course-catalog snapshot lists these course-specific prerequisites:

| Course | Published prerequisite |
| --- | --- |
| CENG532 Network Security | CENG530 and ITBP301 |
| CENG530 Computer Network Protocols | CENG210 with minimum grade D |
| CENG580 Selected Topics in Computer Engineering | Senior standing is stated in the catalog description; the structured prerequisite field is blank, so this condition is not encoded as a course-code prerequisite. |

These are course-catalog facts and do not by themselves establish that a course is in a particular student's plan, offered in a term, or available for registration. The older generic model PDF has elective prerequisites that may differ from the separately published course catalog snapshot; where they disagree, show the source/version difference and do not silently substitute one rule for another.

## Scope

The study-plan PDFs are model sequences, not personal degree audits. The separate 2025–2026 Fall-entry PDF provides a readable course-code sequence and labels its total as 131 credits; individual eligibility, completed courses, remaining requirements, current offerings, and enrollment eligibility still require the student's assigned record. Course prerequisites should come from the course catalog record for the applicable catalog version. Confirm Spring-entry applicability, nonstandard cases, and elective substitutions in Degree Works or with the CIT advising unit.
