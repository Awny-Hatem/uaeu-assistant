# Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity

The current UAEU program page states a minimum of 120 credit hours: 21 general-education, 41 college, 46 major, 6 internship, and 6 major-elective credits. Its page footer reports an update on 9 November 2026.

## College requirements

The college requirement includes MATH105, MATH140, CENG221, CENG202, CENG205, CSBP119, CSBP219, CSBP315, CSBP319, STAT210, ITBP270, ITBP480, and ITBP481, plus one of CHEM111, BIOC100, or PHYS105. The official page lists the required college block as 41 credits.

## Major requirements

The major block is 46 credits. It requires CENG210, ITBP301, CSBP340, ISEC312, ISEC313, ISEC321, ISEC322, ISEC323, ISEC324, ISEC411, ISEC412, ISEC413, ISEC414, ISEC415, ISEC425, ISEC422, and ISEC423, plus either STAT240 or CSBP320.

## Internship and electives

ITBP496 is a 6-credit internship in the final semester; the page says students may not register for other courses during the internship. Students select two 3-credit electives from ITBP420, ISEC417, ISEC424, ISEC428, and ISEC430.

## Scope

This current Cybersecurity record is modeled separately from the older Information Security curriculum record. The cited program page does not establish that historical Information Security cohorts transfer into this plan. Course catalog prerequisites, current offerings, assigned cohort plans, student records, and registration eligibility must be checked separately.
