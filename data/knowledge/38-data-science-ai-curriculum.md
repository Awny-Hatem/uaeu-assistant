# Bachelor of Science in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence

The current UAEU program page states a minimum of 120 credit hours. Its page footer reports an update on 6 November 2026.

## Major requirements

The major block is 54 credits. Required courses are CSBP270, CSBP119, CSBP219, CSBP323, CSBP320, CSBP301, CSBP411, CSBP224, STAT210, STAT240, STAT470, MATH105, MATH110, MATH140, MATH240, MATH290, and CENG202, plus one of CHEM111, BIOC100, or PHYS105.

## Compulsory computing and internship requirements

The 15-credit Advanced Computing Thinking block requires SWEB450, CSBP340, ITBP324, ITBP480, and ITBP481. CSBP495 is a 6-credit internship in the last semester; the page says no other courses may be registered during the internship.

## Elective and supporting requirements

Students select three 3-credit specialization electives from CSBP441, CSBP475, CSBP477, CSBP476, and CSBP325. Supporting Group 1 requires MATH350 and ISEC411 (6 credits). Supporting Group 2 requires three courses (9 credits) from MGMT201, BANA200, MKTG200, MKTG320, and PHI440.

## General education and scope

General education is 21 credits. The page identifies 12 credits across Themes 1, 2, 4, and 5, and 9 credits from Themes 6–11, with a maximum of 3 credits per theme except the 6-credit Theme 11 language sequence. This summary does not encode the student's course selections within those themes.

This published catalog model does not establish an assigned cohort study plan, remaining credits, course availability, student record, or registration eligibility. Course prerequisites must be checked against the official course catalog.
