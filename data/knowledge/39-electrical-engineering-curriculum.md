# Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering

The current UAEU catalog page states a minimum of 132 credits: 21 general-education, 35 College of Engineering, 62 specialization, 2 internship, and 12 elective credits. The page footer reports an update on 9 November 2026.

## College requirements

The 35-credit college block lists MATH210, MATH110, MATH105, CHEM111, GENG315, STAT210, GENG215, MATH140, MATH275, PHYS105, PHYS135, PHYS110, PHYS140, and CHEM175.

## Specialization requirements

The 62-credit specialization block lists ELEC585, ELEC305, ELEC310, ELEC315, ELEC320, ELEC325, ELEC335, ELEC345, ELEC360, ELEC370, ELEC375, ELEC395, ELEC411, ELEC431, ELEC433, ELEC451, ELEC461, ELEC462, ELEC472, ELEC481, ELEC590, ECOM360, ECOM432, ECOM442, GENG230, and CHEM270.

## Internship and electives

The catalog labels the internship requirement as 2 hours, but its rows show ELEC485 as 1 credit and ELEC490 as 0.00. It states that ELEC485 and ELEC490 are taken in two different summers or both in a regular Fall or Spring semester. This discrepancy is preserved; the model does not infer an unlisted credit.

Students select four courses (12 credits) from ECOM451, ELEC512, ELEC521, ELEC522, ELEC523, ELEC530, ELEC531, ELEC533, ELEC534, ELEC551, ELEC561, ELEC562, ELEC570, ELEC580, ELEC582, ELEC592, and ECOM412.

## Scope

The catalog course listing is not a cohort-specific semester plan and does not establish personal progress, course availability, prerequisites beyond the official course catalog, or registration eligibility. General-education theme choices are not audited course by course in this model.
