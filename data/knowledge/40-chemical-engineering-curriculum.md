# UAEU Chemical Engineering degree requirements

The official Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering page lists a minimum of **132 credit hours** and was last updated 9 November 2026. This is the published catalog model; a student's assigned Degree Works plan and cohort remain controlling for individual planning.

## Credit structure

- General Education: 21 credits.
- College of Engineering: 35 credits.
- Chemical Engineering required courses: 45 credits.
- Graduation project: 6 credits (CHME585 and CHME590, 3 credits each).
- Internship: 2 credits (CHME485 and CHME490, 1 credit each).
- Compulsory supporting courses: 17 credits.
- Chemical Engineering electives: 6 credits; choose two from the published elective list.

## Required Chemical Engineering courses

CHME320, CHME455, CHME300, CHME322, CHME330, CHME357, CHME360, CHME390, CHME411, CHME413, CHME415, CHME417, CHME421, CHME506, CHME508, CHME510, CHME517, and CHME528.

## Compulsory supporting courses

CHEM113, CHEM351, CHEM282, CHEM355, CHEM377, GENG220, and GENG230.

## Electives

Choose two (6 credits) from CHME555, CHME560, CHME565, CHME533, CHME541, CHME542, CHME544, CHME561, CHME562, CHME564, and PETE424.

## Internship and limits

The page says CHME485 and CHME490 may be taken in different summers or together during a regular Fall or Spring semester. This source does not establish a student's cohort, completed requirements, prerequisites beyond the official course catalog, course availability, or registration eligibility. General Education is a 21-credit block with options; this model does not claim every option is individually required.
