# UAEU Civil Engineering degree requirements

The official Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering page lists a minimum of **132 credit hours** and was last updated 6 November 2026. This is the published catalog model; a student's assigned Degree Works plan and cohort remain controlling for individual planning.

## Credit structure

- General Education: 21 credits.
- General Engineering requirements: 35 credits.
- Civil Engineering core: 65 credits.
- Internship: 2 credits (CIVL485 and CIVL490, 1 credit each).
- Civil Engineering specialization electives: 9 credits across four baskets.

## Required Civil Engineering core courses

CIVL200, CIVL240, CIVL270, CIVL305, MECH305, CIVL310, CIVL330, CIVL335, CIVL340, CIVL345, CIVL361, CIVL362, CIVL365, CIVL375, CIVL400, CIVL412, CIVL417, CIVL433, CIVL442, CIVL445, CIVL585, CIVL590, and GENG230.

## Specialization electives

Complete 9 credits from the following baskets, not necessarily from one basket:

- Geotechnical and Construction Management: CIVL540, CIVL541, CIVL547, CIVL548.
- Structural Engineering: CIVL510, CIVL515, CIVL517, CIVL552.
- Surveying and Transportation Engineering: CIVL530, CIVL531, CIVL534, CIVL538, CIVL539.
- Water Resources and Environmental Engineering: CIVL520, CIVL522, CIVL526, CIVL525, CIVL529.

The page permits 0–9 credits from each basket. It says CIVL485 and CIVL490 may be taken in different summers or together during a regular Fall or Spring semester. General Education options are not audited here; this source does not establish a student's cohort, completed requirements, prerequisites beyond the official course catalog, course availability, or registration eligibility.
