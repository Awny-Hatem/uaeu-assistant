# UAEU Aerospace Engineering degree requirements

The current official Bachelor of Science in Aerospace Engineering page lists a minimum of **132 credit hours** and was last updated 6 November 2026. The linked 2022–2023 and 2023–2024 model study plans also state 132 credits, but their course-code versions and theme assignments differ. Use the assigned Degree Works plan to determine individual cohort requirements.

## Published credit structure

- General Education: 21 credits.
- College of Engineering: 35 credits.
- Aerospace Engineering core: 68 credits.
- Internship: 2 credits (AERO485 and AERO490, 1 credit each).
- Electives: 6 credits; choose two courses from the listed elective baskets.

## Required Aerospace Engineering core

CIVL240, MECH200, AERO280, MECH305, MECH310, MECH315, MECH350, ELEC372, MECH390, MECH409, AERO301, AERO215, AERO220, AERO300, AERO305, AERO310, AERO315, AERO350, AERO402, AERO411, AERO496, AERO450, AERO585, AERO590, GENG220, and GENG230.

## Elective options

The source lists AERO505, AERO506, AERO515, AERO500, AERO501, AERO520, MECH515, AERO511, AERO562, and AERO563. It organizes these under five baskets and requires two courses totaling 6 credits. The structured model preserves the combined choices but does not infer basket-specific caps or distributions.

The general-education choices are not audited here. This model does not establish personal cohort, completion status, current section availability, prerequisites beyond the official course catalog, or registration eligibility.
