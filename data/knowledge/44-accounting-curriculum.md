# Bachelor of Accounting

The current published program page lists a minimum of **120 credit hours**. The linked model-plan PDF identifies the **2025–2026 cohort onwards** as a 120-credit plan. Confirm an individual student's assigned Degree Works plan before applying these requirements.

## Credit structure

- General education: 21 credits.
- College core: 30 credits, including business essentials, research skills, and a choice between the 12-credit internship and entrepreneurial venture.
- Supporting courses: 30 credits.
- Accounting major requirements: 27 credits.
- One professional track: 12 credits.

## Required courses

College core: BANA200, MGMT201, MKTG205, STAT102, STAT202, and GBUS300. For the 12-credit learning-in-action requirement, the program page says students choose GBUS460 Internship or ENTR415 Developing an Entrepreneurial Venture.

Supporting courses: MATH115, ACCT111, ACCT120, ECON105, ECON125, FINC210, SCML150, SCML201, MGMT415, and PRVT265.

Required major courses: ACCT210, ACCT212, ACCT220, ACCT240, ACCT330, ACCT340, ACCT341, ACCT410, and ACCT440.

Choose one 12-credit track:

- Professional Management Accounting: ACCT420, ACCT421, ACCT441, FINC341.
- Professional Auditing: ACCT431, ACCT432, ACCT441, ACCT442.

## Important limits and source issue

The program page says the internship is a 13-week placement after a two-week preparation session, occurs in semester six, does not allow other courses at the same time, and requires at least 75 completed credit hours before joining. The assistant cannot verify a student's placement approval or completed-credit total.

The program page displays SCML150 as 0.00 credits in its table, but the official course catalog and linked 2025–2026 study plan show it as a 3-credit course. The structured model follows the study plan and course catalog value and flags the inconsistency for UAEU resolution.

The linked PDF also includes 2022–2023 and 2023–2024 model plans with older course codes. This record does not claim those plans apply to older entrants. The model does not establish an individual cohort, completed requirements, class availability, or registration eligibility.
