# Bachelor of Finance and Banking

The current published program page lists a minimum of **120 credit hours** and two 15-credit concentrations. The page was updated 9 November 2026, but does not say which incoming cohort first uses this version. Confirm the assigned Degree Works plan before giving student-specific planning advice.

## Credit structure

- General education: 21 credits.
- College requirements: 30 credits, including the 12-credit choice between internship and entrepreneurial venture.
- Supporting requirements: 24 credits.
- Major requirements: 24 credits.
- One concentration: 15 credits.
- Major electives: 6 credits (two courses).

## Required courses

Business essentials and research skills: CSBP119, MGMT201, MKTG205, STAT102, STAT202, and GBUS300. Choose GBUS460 Internship or ENTR415 Developing an Entrepreneurial Venture for the 12-credit learning-in-action requirement.

Supporting requirements: ACCT111, ACCT120, ECON105, ECON125, FINC210, MATH115, MGMT415, and SCML201.

Major requirements: ECON215, ECON231, FINC341, FINC348, FINC378, FINC380, FINC434, and FINC475.

Choose one concentration:

- Financial Management: FINC344, FINC351, FINC474, FINC477, FINC480.
- Financial Technologies (FinTech): FINC340, FINC370, FINC440, FINC470, FINC481.

Choose two major electives from FINC422, FINC463, ECON211, ECON212, ACCT210, ACCT240, and STAT240.

## Important limits

The page says the internship is 12 credits in the final semester, lasts 14 weeks after a two-week preparation session, and does not allow other courses during it.
