# Bachelor of Education in Early Childhood Education

The current degree page lists **120 credits** and was updated 18 August 2026. The 2025–2026 model plan separately identifies Fall 2025 entrants and later at **120 credits**, while older 2020–2021 and 2022–2023 plans publish **126 credits**. The current page's course groups differ from the 2025 model-plan sequence, so the cohort-specific record supports the total only; students must use the assigned Degree Works plan for course requirements.

## Current 120-credit structure

- General education: 21 credits.
- Required Early Childhood Education courses: 57 credits — CURR101, CURR103, CURR211, CURR212, CURR233, CURR311, CURR312, CURR314, CURR317, CURR319, CURR320, CURR324, CURR400, CURR414, CURR416, FOED350, SPED101, CURR425 and FOED102.
- Student teaching: CURR465, 9 credits, in the last semester. The degree page says CURR425 capstone should be taken during that semester.
- Supporting courses: 30 credits — ARB210, GEO432, PHED200, PHED206, CURR231, CURR114, SPED210, SPED313, SPED321 and LNG220.
- One elective: 3 credits, CURR411 or FOED101.

## Cohort limits

The linked model plan includes 126-credit plans for 2020–2021 and 2022–2023, and a separately labelled 120-credit plan for Fall 2025 entrants and later. Historical and Fall 2025 cohort course requirements have not been fully structured here. Do not apply the current webpage's 120-credit course set to an individual cohort unless its assigned Degree Works plan confirms the match. General-education choices, individual completion, prerequisites beyond the official course catalog, section availability, and registration eligibility are not assessed.
