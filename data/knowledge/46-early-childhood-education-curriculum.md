# Bachelor of Education in Early Childhood Education

The current degree page lists **120 credits** and was updated 18 August 2026. The Fall-entry 2025-2026 model plan separately lists 120 credits. The 2022-2023 Fall-entry model plan lists 126 credits and has been separately structured; the 2020-2021 plan also lists 126 credits, but its table has #REF! in course-title and credit-hour cells, so that version is retained as total-only. Do not merge these versions or apply one without confirming the assigned Degree Works plan.

## Current 120-credit structure

- General education: 21 credits.
- Required Early Childhood Education courses: 57 credits — CURR101, CURR103, CURR211, CURR212, CURR233, CURR311, CURR312, CURR314, CURR317, CURR319, CURR320, CURR324, CURR400, CURR414, CURR416, FOED350, SPED101, CURR425 and FOED102.
- Student teaching: CURR465, 9 credits, in the last semester. The degree page says CURR425 capstone should be taken during that semester.
- Supporting courses: 30 credits — ARB210, GEO432, PHED200, PHED206, CURR231, CURR114, SPED210, SPED313, SPED321 and LNG220.
- One elective: 3 credits, CURR411 or FOED101.

## Fall-entry 2025?2026 model plan

The official plan identifies a 120-credit model for students admitted from Fall 2025. Its named courses are grouped by semester as follows:

| Semester | Published courses | Credits |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Year 1 Fall | GEIT113, CURR101, FOED102, CURR233, GEAE101 | 15 |
| Year 1 Spring | one GE-theme choice, SPED101, GESU121, GEEM110, GEO432 | 15 |
| Year 2 Fall | CURR103, CURR212, CURR312, ARB210, CURR231, one GE-theme choice | 18 |
| Year 2 Spring | CURR211, CURR320, CURR311, CURR114, PHED200 | 15 |
| Year 3 Fall | GEIE222, CURR314, CURR317, CURR414, PHED206, SPED321 | 18 |
| Year 3 Spring | SPED313, CURR400, CURR319, CURR324, CURR416 | 15 |
| Year 4 Fall | LNG220, FOED350, one Major Elective, SPED210 | 12 |
| Year 4 Spring | CURR425, CURR465 Student Teaching (9 credits) | 12 |

The Fall-entry 2025 plan leaves two 3-credit General Education theme choices and one 3-credit Major Elective unnamed. The separate Fall-entry 2022-2023 plan lists General Education courses PHI180, GEIT112, GEIE222, and GESU121; specialization courses SPED101, CURR101, FOED102, CURR103, CURR211, CURR320, CURR212, CURR314, CURR312, CURR317, CURR414, CURR324, CURR319, CURR416, CURR425, FOED350, and CURR311; supporting courses MATH305, ARB210, CURR231, HIS212, MATH335, GEO432, and LNG220; FOED101 as the listed major elective; and CURR465 student teaching (9 credits). It also lists three unnamed 3-credit General Education choices, several named courses absent from the current imported course catalog, and ISLM100/ISLM101 as a slash-separated Islamic Culture/Sira row. These source limitations are represented explicitly in the cohort model. The 2020-2021 plan's #REF! cells prevent reliable course-by-course structuring. Confirm the student's assigned plan before planning.

The model is Fall-entry only. A year-only profile or Spring entrant does not establish applicability. Confirm the assigned Degree Works plan with the College Advising Unit.
