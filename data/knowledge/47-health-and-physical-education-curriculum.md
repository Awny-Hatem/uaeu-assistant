# Bachelor of Education in Health and Physical Education

The current degree page lists **120 credits** and was updated 9 November 2026. The 2025–2026 model plan separately identifies Fall 2025 entrants and later at **120 credits**. The PDF also gives separate **126-credit Fall-entry plans** for 2020–2021 and 2022–2023. Their named course requirements are now modeled separately: the 2020 plan lists ISLM100, while the 2022 plan lists ISLM100/ISLM101 as alternatives. Both plans include two unnamed General Education choices and elective rows whose complete eligible-course lists are not published in the PDF. The cohort-specific records do not establish an individual student's assigned Degree Works plan.

## Current 120-credit structure

- General education: 21 credits.
- Compulsory professional requirements: 33 credits — CURR101, FOED102, FOED350, PHED200, PHED205, PHED206, PHED305, PHED312, PHED314, PHED318 and PHED402.
- Choose two professional electives (6 credits) from FOED101, PHED211, PHED403 and SPED321.
- Field experiences: PHED212 and PHED303 (3 credits each), plus PHED409 student teaching (9 credits) in the last semester. PHED408 capstone should be taken during the student-teaching semester.
- Academic major requirements: 39 credits — PHED202 (2), PHED203 (2), PHED204 (4), PHED207 (3), PHED208 (3), PHED209 (2), PHED302 (3), PHED306 (3), PHED308 (3), PHED309 (2), PHED315 (3), PHED407 (3), PHED408 (3), and PSYC206 (3).
- Choose two major electives (6 credits) from PHED304, PHED400, PHED404 and PHED405.

## Fall-entry 2025?2026 model plan

The official plan identifies a 120-credit model for students admitted from Fall 2025. Its course schedule is:

| Semester | Published courses | Credits |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Year 1 Fall | GEEM110, GEAE101, GEIT113, one GE-theme choice, one Professional Elective | 15 |
| Year 1 Spring | one GE-theme choice, CURR101, FOED102, PHED205, PHED208 | 15 |
| Year 2 Fall | PHED200, PHED202 (2), PHED203 (2), PHED204 (4), PSYC206, one Professional Elective | 17 |
| Year 2 Spring | GESU121, PHED206, PHED305, PHED207, PHED209 (2) | 14 |
| Year 3 Fall | GEIE222, PHED302, PHED308, PHED314, PHED315 | 15 |
| Year 3 Spring | PHED312, PHED402, PHED310, PHED407, PHED306, PHED309 (2), one Professional Elective | 20 |
| Year 4 Fall | PHED406, FOED350, one Major Elective, one Professional Elective | 12 |
| Year 4 Spring | PHED408, PHED409 Student Teaching (9 credits) | 12 |

The plan leaves two General Education theme choices (6 credits), four Professional Electives (12 credits), and one Major Elective (3 credits) unnamed. The current degree page instead lists two Professional Electives (6 credits) and two Major Electives (6 credits), and has different required course groups. Keep those source models separate. The 2020–2021 and 2022–2023 historical plans have separate structured records; the PDF lists FOED101 and PHED405 as the elective selections in those model plans but does not publish complete eligibility lists, so those rows are represented as credit blocks rather than fixed required courses.

## Fall-entry 2020–2021 and 2022–2023 model plans

Both historical plans total 126 credits and share the named course structure: 33 General Education credits (including 6 credits of unnamed student choices), 36 Professional Requirements credits, 42 Specialization credits, a 3-credit Professional Elective row, a 3-credit Major Elective row, and 9 credits of PHED409 Student Teaching in the final semester. The 2020–2021 plan names ISLM100; the 2022–2023 plan allows ISLM100 or ISLM101. The PDF labels each plan for students admitted from the Fall semester. A cohort year alone, a Spring entry, and this public plan do not prove a student's assigned plan; confirm it in Degree Works with the College Advising Unit.

The model is Fall-entry only. A year-only profile or Spring entrant does not establish applicability. Confirm the assigned Degree Works plan with the College Advising Unit.
