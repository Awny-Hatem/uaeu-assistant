# Bachelor of Education in Health and Physical Education

The current degree page lists **120 credits** and was updated 9 November 2026. The 2025–2026 model plan separately identifies Fall 2025 entrants and later at **120 credits**; older 2020–2021 and 2022–2023 plans publish **126 credits**. The current page and 2025 plan use different course lists, so the cohort-specific record supports the total only. Follow the assigned Degree Works plan for an individual student.

## Current 120-credit structure

- General education: 21 credits.
- Compulsory professional requirements: 33 credits — CURR101, FOED102, FOED350, PHED200, PHED205, PHED206, PHED305, PHED312, PHED314, PHED318 and PHED402.
- Choose two professional electives (6 credits) from FOED101, PHED211, PHED403 and SPED321.
- Field experiences: PHED212 and PHED303 (3 credits each), plus PHED409 student teaching (9 credits) in the last semester. PHED408 capstone should be taken during the student-teaching semester.
- Academic major requirements: 39 credits — PHED202 (2), PHED203 (2), PHED204 (4), PHED207 (3), PHED208 (3), PHED209 (2), PHED302 (3), PHED306 (3), PHED308 (3), PHED309 (2), PHED315 (3), PHED407 (3), PHED408 (3), and PSYC206 (3).
- Choose two major electives (6 credits) from PHED304, PHED400, PHED404 and PHED405.

## Cohort limits

The older 2020–2021 and 2022–2023 model plans publish 126 credits and use different course sequences. The Fall 2025 model plan's course list also differs from the current degree page. Cohort-specific course requirements are not structured here; general-education choices, the student's assigned plan, completion status, section availability and registration eligibility require the assigned plan and student record.
