# Bachelor of Education in Special Education

The current degree page lists **120 credits** and was updated 22 September 2026. The separate 2025-2026 model-plan PDF identifies four Fall-entry concentrations at **120 credits**; 2020-2021 and 2022-2023 plans publish **126 credits**. The current page's requirements and the 2025 model-plan sequences are stored separately. Use the student's assigned Degree Works plan.

## Current 120-credit structure

- General education: 21 credits.
- College of Education requirements: 18 credits — CURR101, CURR102, FOED101, FOED350, SPED101 and FOED102.
- Special Education major: 30 credits — SPED210, SPED211, SPED220, SPED221, SPED222, SPED313, SPED314, SPED321, SPED332 and SPED400.
- Supporting requirements: 24 credits — CURR317, PHED205, CURR324, ENG310, HIS373, MATH305, PSYC100 and PSYC307.
- Choose one 21-credit concentration: Mild/Moderate Disabilities; Gifted and Talented; Sensory Impairments; or Severe Disabilities. Each includes its own 9-credit final-semester student-teaching course, and its matching capstone should be taken during that internship semester.
- Free electives: 6 credits.

The concentration course lists above describe the current degree page, not a cohort-specific audit. The Fall-entry 2025 model plans are separately structured by concentration, but the PDF has apparent inconsistencies: it repeats SPED321 in semesters 3 and 5 in the Sensory Impairments and Severe Disabilities plans, and lists SPED312 Individuals with Mild/Moderate Disabilities as a concentration course in the Sensory Impairments plan. Those entries are preserved as published and require College of Education confirmation before personal planning. General-education and free-elective choices, student-record completion, course availability and registration eligibility are not assessed. The 2020-2021 and 2022-2023 model-plan structures are now represented separately at 126 credits each. Their four concentration choices and named course groups are captured; see below for the source limitations.

## Historical 126-credit model plans

The official model-plan PDF publishes separate **Fall-entry** plans for the 2020-2021 and 2022-2023 cohorts, each totaling 126 credits. The structured model records 33 General Education credits, 18 College of Education requirements, 30 Special Education specialization credits, 18 supporting credits, one 21-credit concentration, and 6 free electives. The model groups General Education selections in a credit block because course choices vary in the PDF.

Each concentration contains three named 3-credit courses, a named 3-credit capstone, and a named 9-credit student-teaching internship: Gifted and Talented (SPED331, SPED326, SPED416, SPED544, SPED564); Mild/Moderate Disabilities (SPED312, SPED361, SPED415, SPED541, SPED561); Sensory Impairments (SPED315, SPED322, SPED412, SPED542, SPED562); Severe Disabilities (SPED330, SPED324, SPED413, SPED543, SPED563).

The PDF also displays Spring-entry sequences; their term ordering and variant-specific General Education choices are not modeled. Several source-listed course codes are absent from the imported catalog, so those items are retained as named credit blocks. A published model plan does not establish a student's assigned Degree Works plan or completed credits.
