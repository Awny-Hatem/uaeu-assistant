# Bachelor of Education in Special Education

The current degree page lists **120 credits** and was updated 22 September 2026. The 2025–2026 model plan separately identifies Fall 2025 entrants and later at **120 credits**; 2020–2021 and 2022–2023 plans publish **126 credits**. The current page's detailed course groups are not mapped to the 2025 plan's course sequence. Use the student's assigned Degree Works plan.

## Current 120-credit structure

- General education: 21 credits.
- College of Education requirements: 18 credits — CURR101, CURR102, FOED101, FOED350, SPED101 and FOED102.
- Special Education major: 30 credits — SPED210, SPED211, SPED220, SPED221, SPED222, SPED313, SPED314, SPED321, SPED332 and SPED400.
- Supporting requirements: 24 credits — CURR317, PHED205, CURR324, ENG310, HIS373, MATH305, PSYC100 and PSYC307.
- Choose one 21-credit concentration: Mild/Moderate Disabilities; Gifted and Talented; Sensory Impairments; or Severe Disabilities. Each includes its own 9-credit final-semester student-teaching course, and its matching capstone should be taken during that internship semester.
- Free electives: 6 credits.

The concentration course lists above describe the current degree page, not a cohort-specific audit. The older and Fall 2025 cohort course requirements have not been fully structured here; do not apply the current page's course model to a student without confirming their assigned plan. General-education and free-elective choices, student-record completion, course availability and registration eligibility are not assessed.
