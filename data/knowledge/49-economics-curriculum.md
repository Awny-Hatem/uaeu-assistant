# Bachelor of Economics

The current degree page lists a minimum of **120 credits**. It describes 21 General Education credits; 30 business-core credits; 30 supporting credits; 30 required economics credits; and 9 economics elective credits. These are current page requirements, not a confirmed student's assigned cohort plan.

## Required program courses

- Business essentials: MGMT201, MKTG205, BANA200.
- Research skills: STAT102, STAT202, GBUS300.
- Learning in Action: choose ENTR415 (Developing an Entrepreneurial Venture) or GBUS460 (Internship), 12 credits.
- Supporting courses: PRVT265, MATH115, SCML150, ECON105, ACCT111, ACCT120, ECON125, MGMT415, FINC210, SCML201.
- Economics major: ECON211, ECON212, ECON215, ECON231, ECON236, ECON330, ECON344, ECON350, ECON360, ECON460.
- Choose 3 courses / 9 credits from ECON311, ECON331, ECON441 (Global Competitiveness) and ECON320, ECON321, ECON420 (Sustainability). At least 3 credits must come from each cluster.

The page says General Education requires 21 credits, including 15 fixed credits and 6 credits selected across four themes, with at most 3 credits from each selected theme.

## Fall-entry 2025-2026 model plan (separate source version)

The official model-plan PDF labels this version **2025-2026 Cohort onwards** and says it is for students admitted from the Fall semester. Its 8-term sequence is:

| Term | Courses and choice slots | Published load |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 Fall | ACCT111, MATH115, GEAE101, ECON105, MGMT201 | 15 |
| 2 Spring | ACCT120, ECON125, STAT102, FINC210, one 3-credit General Education choice from Themes 6-10 | 15 |
| 3 Fall | GEIE222, MKTG205, ECON211, ECON215, STAT202 | 15 |
| 4 Spring | BANA200, ECON236, SCML150, ECON212, ECON231 | 15 |
| 5 Fall | ECON350, ECON360, one 3-credit Major Elective choice, PRVT265, SCML201, GBUS300 | 18 |
| 6 Spring | ECON330, ECON344, one 3-credit Major Elective choice, GEBS220, one 3-credit General Education choice from Themes 6-10 | 15 |
| 7 Fall | MGMT415, ECON460, one 3-credit Major Elective choice, GEIT113, GEEM110 | 15 |
| 8 Spring | Choose GBUS460 Internship (12 credits) or ENTR415 Developing an Entrepreneurial Venture (12 credits) | 12 |

The PDF names ECON215 in a row labeled Major Elective, while the current degree page lists ECON215 among required Economics courses. The PDF leaves the three Major Elective course choices unnamed; do not fill them with the current page's elective pool without confirmation that it applies to this cohort. Its two General Education theme choices likewise have no course codes. The PDF's course-type labels do not reconcile cleanly with the current page's 21-credit General Education summary, so the two source versions remain separate. The plan's total is 120 credits.

## Cohort applicability and limits

The linked model-plan PDF also contains separate 2022-2023 Fall-entry, 2022-2023 Spring-entry, and 2023-2024 Fall-entry layouts, each listing 120 total credits. Their detailed course lists remain separate from the 2025-2026 Fall-entry version and are not yet structured in the academic model. A year without an entry semester is insufficient to auto-assign a Fall-only plan. Confirm the student's assigned plan in Degree Works.
