# Bachelor of Economics

The current degree page lists a minimum of **120 credits**. It describes 21 General Education credits; 30 business-core credits; 30 supporting credits; 30 required economics credits; and 9 economics elective credits. These are current page requirements, not a confirmed student's assigned cohort plan.

## Required program courses

- Business essentials: MGMT201, MKTG205, BANA200.
- Research skills: STAT102, STAT202, GBUS300.
- Learning in Action: choose ENTR415 (Developing an Entrepreneurial Venture) or GBUS460 (Internship), 12 credits.
- Supporting courses: PRVT265, MATH115, SCML150, ECON105, ACCT111, ACCT120, ECON125, MGMT415, FINC210, SCML201.
- Economics major: ECON211, ECON212, ECON215, ECON231, ECON236, ECON330, ECON344, ECON350, ECON360, ECON460.
- Choose 3 courses / 9 credits from ECON311, ECON331, ECON441 (Global Competitiveness) and ECON320, ECON321, ECON420 (Sustainability). At least 3 credits must come from each cluster.

The page says General Education requires 21 credits, including 15 fixed credits and 6 credits selected across four themes, with at most 3 credits from each selected theme.

## Cohort applicability and limits

The linked model-plan PDF includes separate 2022–2023, 2023–2024, and 2025–2026 plan layouts, each listing 120 total credits. The 2025–2026 plan classifies ECON215 as a major elective, while the current page lists it among required major courses. The model therefore keeps cohort-specific course requirements unresolved and uses the 2025–2026 plan only to establish its published total. Confirm the student's assigned plan in Degree Works.

This record does not establish completed requirements, current sections, personal eligibility, or graduation approval.
