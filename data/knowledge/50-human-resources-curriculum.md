# Bachelor of Business Administration in Human Resources Management and Development

The current degree page lists a minimum of **120 credits**: 21 General Education; 9 college requirements; 9 research-skills credits; 12 Learning in Action; 30 major-core; 30 supporting; and 9 business-elective credits.

## Required program courses

- College requirements: BANA200, MGMT201, MKTG205.
- Research skills: STAT102, STAT202, GBUS300.
- Learning in Action: choose ENTR415 or GBUS460 for 12 credits. The page says the internship is in the final semester, lasts 12 weeks after a four-week preparation session, and allows no other courses during the internship.
- HR major core: HRMD320, HRMD330, HRMD340, HRMD360, HRMD370, HRMD400, HRMD410, HRMD420, HRMD430, MGMT350.
- Supporting courses: ACCT111, ACCT120, ECON105, ECON125, FINC210, MATH115, MGMT415, PRVT265, SCML150, SCML201.
- Choose three business electives from MKTG300, SCML300, SCML340, ENTR325, BANA250, GBUS201, PHI226, and PSYC315.
