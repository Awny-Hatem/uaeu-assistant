# UAEU masters admission — general and conditional requirements

## General English-taught masters admission

Audience: applicants to masters degrees taught predominantly in English. Inspected 2026-11-03. General requirements include a related accredited bachelor degree, GPA 3.0/4.0 or equivalent, and IELTS Academic 6.0 or equivalent. The language test must be less than two years old at application, subject to the published English-medium/native-speaker exemptions. Programs can impose higher standards; meeting the baseline is not an admission offer. Arabic-taught masters use a different language-test baseline.

## Conditional GPA admission

A bachelor GPA at least 2.5 and below 3.0 may qualify for conditional masters admission, subject to other criteria and the university's decision. In that route the student may take at most 9 credit hours in semester one and must obtain GPA 3.0 in the first 9 credit-bearing hours, or face dismissal. A GPA of 2.7 therefore does not establish automatic rejection or guaranteed admission. A separate work-experience-adjusted route is also listed; it is not a blanket GPA waiver.

## Conditional IELTS admission

For the published IELTS Academic 5.5 conditional route, obtain 6.0 by the end of the first semester, initially take no more than 6 credit hours excluding intensive English, and obtain GPA 3.0 in the first 9 credit-bearing hours. This is separate from the conditional GPA route; do not silently combine all conditional routes as automatic entitlements.
