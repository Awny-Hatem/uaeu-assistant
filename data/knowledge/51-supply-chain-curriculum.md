# Bachelor of Business Administration in Supply Chain Management and Logistics

The current degree page and the 2025–2026 model plan each list a minimum/total of **120 credits**. The current page groups them as 21 General Education; 9 college; 9 research-skills; 12 Learning in Action; 30 major-core; 30 supporting; and 9 business-elective credits.

## Required program courses

- College requirements: BANA200, MGMT201, MKTG205.
- Research skills: STAT102, STAT202, GBUS300.
- Learning in Action: choose ENTR415 or GBUS460, 12 credits. The page places internship in the final semester, after a four-week preparation session and a 12-week placement, with no other courses during the internship.
- Major core: SCML300, SCML310, SCML320, SCML340, SCML400, SCML450, BANA250, BANA310, STAT240, CSBP119.
- Supporting courses: ACCT111, ACCT120, ECON105, ECON125, FINC210, MATH115, PRVT265, MGMT415, SCML150, SCML201.
- Choose three business electives from MKTG210, MKTG300, HRMD320, HRMD330, ENTR310, and ENTR325.

## Cohort applicability and limits

Although the page and 2025–2026 plan identify the same 120-credit total, the plan classifies MGMT415 differently from the current page. This model keeps current-page requirements separate from cohort-specific plans and reports only the verified 2025–2026 total for that cohort. Confirm the assigned plan in Degree Works.
