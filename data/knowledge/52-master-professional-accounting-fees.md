# UAEU Master of Professional Accounting — dated tuition table

## Master of Professional Accounting fees — AY 2025–2026 validity

Audience: Master of Professional Accounting applicants/students. The published graduate table expressly limits tuition validity through academic year 2025–2026. Its MPA row lists 36 credits, AED 2750 per credit, and AED 200 application fee. The page says application fees exclude VAT. Inspected 2026-11-03. This publication does not certify 2026–2027 or later fees; request the current official program letter/invoice for a later cohort. These are graduate fees, not international undergraduate rates. The same page states that UAEU does not provide graduate housing.
