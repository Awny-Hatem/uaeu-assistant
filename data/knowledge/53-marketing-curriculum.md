# Bachelor of Business Administration in Marketing

The current page lists **120 credits**: 21 General Education; 9 college; 9 research skills; 12 Learning in Action; 30 major core; 30 supporting; and 9 major-elective credits.

## Published requirement groups

- College: BANA200, MGMT201, MKTG205. Research skills: STAT102, STAT202, GBUS300.
- Learning in Action: choose ENTR415 or GBUS460, 12 credits. The page says the internship is in the final semester and no other courses are allowed during it.
- Major core: MKTG210, MKTG300, MKTG305, MKTG310, MKTG360, MKTG405, MKTG350, MKTG410, MKTG420, and choose ENTR310 or BANA310.
- Supporting: ACCT111, ACCT120, ECON105, ECON125, FINC210, MATH115, SCML150, SCML201, PRVT265, MGMT415. Choose three major electives from MKTG355, MKTG365, MKTG400, SCML300, SCML340, HRMD330, and HRMD340.

## Cohort limits

The current page and linked 2025-2026 model plan classify some courses differently. The model uses the current page for general questions but does not assign those course groups to a cohort. The plan total is recorded separately as 120 credits. Confirm the assigned Degree Works plan for cohort-specific requirements and personal remaining courses.
