# Bachelor of Business Administration in Marketing

The current page lists **120 credits**: 21 General Education; 9 college; 9 research skills; 12 Learning in Action; 30 major core; 30 supporting; and 9 major-elective credits.

## Published requirement groups

- College: BANA200, MGMT201, MKTG205. Research skills: STAT102, STAT202, GBUS300.
- Learning in Action: choose ENTR415 or GBUS460, 12 credits. The page says the internship is in the final semester and no other courses are allowed during it.
- Major core: MKTG210, MKTG300, MKTG305, MKTG310, MKTG360, MKTG405, MKTG350, MKTG410, MKTG420, and choose ENTR310 or BANA310.
- Supporting: ACCT111, ACCT120, ECON105, ECON125, FINC210, MATH115, SCML150, SCML201, PRVT265, MGMT415. Choose three major electives from MKTG355, MKTG365, MKTG400, SCML300, SCML340, HRMD330, and HRMD340.

## Fall-entry 2025?2026 model plan

The official model plan is for students admitted from Fall and totals **120 credits**. Its semester sequence is:

| Semester | Published courses | Credits |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Year 1 Fall | ACCT111, MATH115, GEAE101, ECON105, MGMT201 | 15 |
| Year 1 Spring | SCML150, MKTG205, ECON125, STAT102, GEIT113 | 15 |
| Year 2 Fall | GEIE222, MKTG210, ACCT120, MKTG300, STAT202 | 15 |
| Year 2 Spring | BANA200, FINC210, PRVT265, MKTG305, MKTG310 | 15 |
| Year 3 Fall | GBUS300, SCML201, MKTG360, BANA310, one Major Elective | 15 |
| Year 3 Spring | GEEM110, MKTG350, MKTG405, MKTG410, one Major Elective | 15 |
| Year 4 Fall | one GE-theme choice, MKTG420, MGMT415, GEBS220, one GE-theme choice, one Major Elective | 18 |
| Year 4 Spring | ENTR415 **or** GBUS460, 12-credit option | 12 |

The plan leaves **9 major-elective credits** and **6 General Education theme-choice credits** unspecified. It does not list eligible course codes for those choices, so do not fill them from the current degree page without confirming the assigned plan.

The sources differ in course grouping: the model plan lists BANA310 as a course while the current degree page presents it as an alternative to ENTR310; the plan places MGMT415 in Learning in Action while the current page lists it among supporting requirements. Preserve both source models and confirm the student's assigned Degree Works plan. The model plan is Fall-entry only; a year-only profile or Spring entrant does not establish applicability.
