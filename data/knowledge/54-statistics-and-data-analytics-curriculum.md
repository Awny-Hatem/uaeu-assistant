# Bachelor of Science in Statistics and Data Analytics

The current page lists **121 credits**: 21 General Education; 30 college requirements; 12 supporting; 40 major; 15 concentration; and 3 free-elective credits.

## Published requirement groups

- Supporting: BANA220, MATH105, ECON105, CSBP119. Major requirements: BANA250, BANA310, MATH110, MATH140, STAT230, STAT240, STAT300, STAT330, STAT360, STAT380, STAT400, STAT470, STAT430, and STAT488 (1 credit).
- Choose Statistics or Analytics for Business concentration. Each concentration includes 9 required concentration credits and 6 elective credits. Statistics electives: STAT250, STAT370, STAT410, STAT475. Analytics for Business electives: BANA410, BANA420, BANA430.
- College requirements include business essentials (9 credits), research skills (9 credits), and Learning in Action (12 credits). Use the assigned plan for the exact course mapping where the current page and cohort plan differ.

## Cohort limits

The current degree page was updated 9 November 2026. Linked 2022-2023 and 2024-2025 study plans contain different course sets and credit details, including CSBP121 or CSBP123 in place of the current page's STAT488. The current page's groups are not assigned to a student's cohort; the two older plans are recorded as total-only at 121 credits.
