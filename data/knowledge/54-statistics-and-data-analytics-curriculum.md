# Bachelor of Science in Statistics and Data Analytics

The current page lists **121 credits**: 21 General Education; 30 college requirements; 12 supporting; 40 major; 15 concentration; and 3 free-elective credits.

## Published requirement groups

- Supporting: BANA220, MATH105, ECON105, CSBP119. Major requirements: BANA250, BANA310, MATH110, MATH140, STAT230, STAT240, STAT300, STAT330, STAT360, STAT380, STAT400, STAT470, STAT430, and STAT488 (1 credit).
- Choose Statistics or Analytics for Business concentration. Each concentration includes 9 required concentration credits and 6 elective credits. Statistics electives: STAT250, STAT370, STAT410, STAT475. Analytics for Business electives: BANA410, BANA420, BANA430.
- College requirements include business essentials (9 credits), research skills (9 credits), and Learning in Action (12 credits). Use the assigned plan for the exact course mapping where the current page and cohort plan differ.

## Cohort limits

The current degree page was updated 9 November 2026. Direct inspection of the currently linked official PDF on 16 November 2026 found four plan tables for each of the 2022-2023 and 2024-2025 cohort labels, followed by four 2025-2026 plan tables. The PDF therefore confirms the 2024-2025 label that an earlier extraction had incorrectly recorded as absent. Each version lists 121 credits and varies by concentration and Fall/Spring admission. Fall-entry 2022 and 2024 plans are now modeled separately with their named course groups and two concentration options; Spring-entry sequences remain outside those models. The plans differ from current-page requirements, including CSBP121 or CSBP123 in place of current-page STAT488. A published model plan does not establish an individual student?s assigned Degree Works plan.

## Fall-entry historical model plans

The Fall-entry 2022-2023 and 2024-2025 plan models each reconcile to **121 credits**: 33 General Education, 6 Business Core, 6 Business Analytics Core, 34 Statistics Core, 9 Research Learning Line, 18 Learning in Action (including a 12-credit internship), 9 concentration, and 6 major electives. Each plan offers Analytics for Business or Statistics. The 2022 Fall-entry plan lists CSBP123 (4 credits); the 2024 plan lists CSBP121 (1 credit) in Statistics Core. General Education and major-elective choices remain student-selected credit blocks.

The Spring-entry tables are not combined with the Fall-entry models. In particular, the Analytics for Business Spring table labels STAT480 as the capstone where the Fall table lists STAT482; the student's assigned plan must be checked before applying the Fall sequence. The 2024 Spring Statistics table also varies its Business Core course from the Fall table. Do not infer a personal audit or registration eligibility from these public models.
