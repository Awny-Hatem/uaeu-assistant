# Bachelor of Science in Architectural Engineering

The current degree page lists a minimum of **132 credits**: 21 General Education; 35 College of Engineering; 65 Architectural Engineering; 2 internship; and 9 architecture electives.

## Published requirements

- College requirements: MATH105, MATH110, MATH210, MATH140, MATH275, CHEM111, CHEM175, STAT210, PHYS105, PHYS110, PHYS135, PHYS140, GENG215, GENG315.
- Architectural Engineering requirements: ARCH302, ARCH313, ARCH316, ARCH320, ARCH326, ARCH335, ARCH341, ARCH342, ARCH345, ARCH366, ARCH422, ARCH425, ARCH430, ARCH433, ARCH440, ARCH450, ARCH585, CIVL240, CIVL345, CIVL358, MECH305, ARCH590.
- Internship: ARCH485 and ARCH490, each 1 credit. The degree page says to take them in two different summers or both in regular Fall/Spring semesters. Choose three electives from ARCH510, ARCH515, ARCH503, ARCH509, ARCH525, ARCH526, ARCH530, ARCH532, ARCH542, ARCH551.

## Cohort limits

The current page lists 132 credits. The linked model plans list 132 credits for 2023-2024 entrants and 147 for 2022-2023 entrants, with different course classifications and internship arrangements. This record keeps current-page requirements separate from cohort-plan totals. Confirm your assigned Degree Works plan.
