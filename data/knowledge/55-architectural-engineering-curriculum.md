# Bachelor of Science in Architectural Engineering

The current degree page lists a minimum of **132 credits**: 21 General Education; 35 College of Engineering; 65 Architectural Engineering; 2 internship; and 9 architecture electives.

## Published requirements

- College requirements: MATH105, MATH110, MATH210, MATH140, MATH275, CHEM111, CHEM175, STAT210, PHYS105, PHYS110, PHYS135, PHYS140, GENG215, GENG315.
- Architectural Engineering requirements: ARCH302, ARCH313, ARCH316, ARCH320, ARCH326, ARCH335, ARCH341, ARCH342, ARCH345, ARCH366, ARCH422, ARCH425, ARCH430, ARCH433, ARCH440, ARCH450, ARCH585, CIVL240, CIVL345, CIVL358, MECH305, ARCH590.
- Internship: ARCH485 and ARCH490, each 1 credit. The degree page says to take them in two different summers or both in regular Fall/Spring semesters. Choose three electives from ARCH510, ARCH515, ARCH503, ARCH509, ARCH525, ARCH526, ARCH530, ARCH532, ARCH542, ARCH551.

## Cohort limits

The current page lists 132 credits. The model-plan PDF separately identifies:

- 2022-2023 cohort plan: 147 credits.
- 2023-2024 and later plan: 132 credits; its course sequence is not safely reconciled to the current degree page and remains total-only in the structured model.
- 2025-2026 and 2026-2027 cohort plans: each publishes distinct Fall-entry and Spring-entry sequences at 132 credits. The structured model preserves all four entry-term schedules, their named course codes, two one-credit internships, and student-choice credit slots. Choice slots remain credit blocks where the PDF gives no eligible course codes.

The older plans remain total-only because of specific source issues. The 2022-2023 plan uses slash-separated legacy/current course labels and lists 2-credit GENG215 twice in the Fall-entry table; the official 2016-2017 catalog defines GENG215 as a 2-credit course but does not explain the duplicate. The 2023-2024 tables include an ISLM101 row shown as 0 credits, and their historical course groupings do not map cleanly to the current degree page.

Some course classifications differ across the 2025-2026 and 2026-2027 Fall and Spring tables, and the PDF uses different titles for ARCH341 across versions. These are retained as source ambiguities; confirm the assigned plan in Degree Works or with the college before using it as a personal graduation audit. Model plans do not verify offerings, registration eligibility, or completed credits.
