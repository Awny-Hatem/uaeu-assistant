# Bachelor of Science in Communication Engineering

The current degree page lists a minimum of **132 credits**: 21 General Education; 35 College of Engineering; 62 specialization; 2 internship; and 12 electives.

## Published requirements

- College requirements: MATH210, MATH110, MATH105, STAT210, GENG215, MATH140, MATH275, PHYS105, PHYS135, PHYS110, PHYS140, CHEM175, GENG315, CHEM111.
- Specialization: ECOM320, ECOM360, ECOM402, ECOM412, ECOM422, ECOM432, ECOM442, ECOM451, ECOM461, ELEC305, ELEC310, ELEC315, ELEC325, ELEC335, ELEC345, ELEC360, ELEC370, ELEC375, ELEC395, ELEC451, ELEC461, ELEC462, ELEC590, GENG230, CHEM270, ELEC585.
- The degree page lists internship components ELEC485 (1 credit) and ELEC490 (0 credits) under a 2-hour internship category; it says to take them in different summers or regular Fall/Spring semesters. Confirm the category against your assigned plan. Choose four electives from ECOM532, ECOM542, ECOM561, ECOM562, ECOM571, ECOM580, ELEC431, ELEC523.

## Cohort limits

The current page lists 132 credits. The linked model plans list 132 credits for 2023-2024 entrants and 147 for 2022-2023 entrants. Course groupings differ, so this model does not assign current-page requirements to older cohorts. Confirm your assigned Degree Works plan.
