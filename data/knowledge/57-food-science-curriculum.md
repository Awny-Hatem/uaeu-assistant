# Bachelor of Science in Food Science

The current degree page lists **120 credits**: 21 General Education; 12 College; 63 Food Science; 3 internship; 15 major electives; and 6 free electives.

## Published requirements

- College: MATH105, PHYS105, STAT235, BIOC100.
- Food Science requirements: FDSC466, CHEM111, CHEM113, CHEM282, FDSC285, FDSC260, FDSC265, FDSC309, FDSC319, FDSC340, FDSC347, FDSC350, FDSC351, FDSC355, FDSC450, FDSC453, FDSC454, FDSC470, NUTR301, ARAG323, FDSC480, FDSC482.
- Internship: FDSC425, 3 credits; the page describes an 8-week placement during the last study year. Choose five electives from FDSC357, FDSC363, FDSC378, FDSC402, FDSC455, FDSC458, FDSC477, FDSC510, FDSC525. The page lists 6 free-elective credits.

## Cohort limits

The linked 2020-2021 study plan also lists 120 credits but differs from the current degree page in course codes and category placement. The model keeps the current page separate from the cohort plan total. Some General Education credits can overlap with major requirements; do not add these categories as distinct credits.
