# Graduation ceremony attire: use the applicable invitation

## Evidence limit for ceremony clothing

Audience: graduates asking about clothing for a UAEU graduation ceremony. The inspected public Annual Graduation Ceremony page contains graduation statistics, graduate interviews and contact/map links. It does not establish current gown, cap, colour, footwear or other ceremony-specific attire requirements. This is a limit of the checked evidence, not a claim that the university has no dress rules or that no invitation exists elsewhere.

Do not substitute an employee dress policy, clinical elective dress rules or a historic photograph for the requested ceremony instructions. The graduate needs the invitation/instructions for their own degree level, ceremony and cohort; the public commencement page and official contact link are appropriate places to seek confirmation. No attire rule is inferred from statistics or another cohort's event.
