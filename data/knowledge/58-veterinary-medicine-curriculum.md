# Bachelor of Veterinary Medicine

The current degree page lists **175 credits**: 21 General Education, 124 major, 27 clinical core rotations, and one 3-credit clinical elective. Four courses listed in General Education are also credited toward the major; the category totals must not be double-counted.

## Published requirements

- Major course list: BIOC100, VMED108, VMED111, VMED122, VMED125, VMED130, VMED135, VMED145, VMED200, VMED206, VMED211, VMED215, VMED221, VMED225, VMED230, VMED235, VMED244, VMED255, VMED305, VMED311, VMED315, VMED322, VMED326, VMED333, VMED335, VMED344, VMED345, VMED353, VMED355, VMED375, VMED401, VMED405, VMED411, VMED415, VMED422, VMED425, VMED433, VMED435, VMED444, VMED448, VMED451, VMED580.
- Clinical core rotations: VMED500, VMED505, VMED511, VMED515, VMED522, VMED525, VMED533, VMED535, VMED540, VMED545. The page says these occur in Year 5 semesters 1 and 2 after all Year 1-4 courses and VMED580 are complete. Choose one clinical elective from VMED550, VMED555, VMED560, VMED565, VMED570, VMED575, VMED585, VMED588, VMED595.

## Cohort limits

The current page lists 175 credits. Linked model plans list 152 credits for 2022-2023 and 175 credits from 2024-2025 onwards, with different course sets. The model stores those plan totals separately and does not assign current page course details to cohorts. The degree structure is not an admission eligibility decision or an individual Degree Works audit.
