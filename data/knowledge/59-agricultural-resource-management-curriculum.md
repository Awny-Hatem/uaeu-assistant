# Bachelor of Science in Agricultural Resource Management

The current UAEU degree page states a minimum of **120 credits**: General Education 21, College requirements 12, Agribusiness requirements 59, Internship 4, elective courses 18, and free electives 6. The linked model-plan PDF contains separate 2022-2023 and 2025-2026 cohort versions, each with Fall- and Spring-admission schedules and a 120-credit header. These plans differ from the current degree page and from each other in General Education course structure. The PDF does not show a 2020-2021 plan; do not apply a later plan to a 2020-2021 entrant.

## Current published requirements

- College: MATH105, STAT130, BIOC100, PHYS105.
- Required Agribusiness courses displayed on the page: AGRB200, AGRB210, AGRB220, AGRB300, AGRB333, AGRB335, AGRB352, AGRB360, AGRB365, AGRB391, AGRB422, AGRB444, AGRB450, AGRB480, ARAG200, ARAG307, ARAG330, ECON125, ECON231, FDSC250.
- The page asks for six 3-credit electives from AGRB392, AGRB395, ARAG443, ARAG452, AGRB445, AGRB341, AGRB371, AGRB374, AGRB377, ARAG220, ARAG305. It lists 6 free-elective credits without naming eligible courses.
- Internship AGRB410 is 4 credits, after 90 completed credits. UAEU describes three scheduling options, including schedules that allow other courses on other days of the semester.
- General Education includes 15 credits in named themes and 6 credits selected from five themes, with a maximum 3 credits from each. AGRB352 appears as a General Education option and as a required Agribusiness course, so check Degree Works for cross-counting.

## Published model-plan versions

- **2022-2023 cohort onward:** the linked PDF provides separate Fall- and Spring-entry semester sequences. The schedules share the same listed required course set, but sequence and choice-slot placement differ. It includes the choice `ISLM100` or `ISLM101`, six major-elective slots, one General Education choice slot, and two free-elective slots. The newer 2025-2026 version supersedes this plan for its named cohort.
- **2025-2026 cohort onward:** the PDF also provides separate Fall- and Spring-entry sequences. It lists six required General Education courses and two 3-credit General Education choices labelled Themes 6-10, seven major-elective slots, and one free-elective slot. The PDF repeats Theme 8 in that theme label; the intended option set is not inferred.
- Both plans show a 4-credit AGRB410 internship in the final listed semester. Follow the term order for the matching admission season, and confirm the assigned plan in Degree Works.

## Source discrepancy and limits

The program page labels the required Agribusiness section as 59 credits, while its displayed coded course rows total 53 credits using the current catalog credit values. The assistant preserves the published section total and displayed course list, does not invent the missing six credits, and cannot certify that those rows reconcile. The individual student's assigned Degree Works plan controls cohort requirements.
