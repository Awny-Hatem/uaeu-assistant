# Bachelor of Science in Agricultural Resource Management

The current UAEU degree page states a minimum of **120 credits**: General Education 21, College requirements 12, Agribusiness requirements 59, Internship 4, elective courses 18, and free electives 6. The linked 2020–2021 cohort-onward model plan also states 120 credits, but its sequence and course categories differ from the current page. Keep the current catalog requirements separate from that plan until the student's assigned version is known.

## Current published requirements

- College: MATH105, STAT130, BIOC100, PHYS105.
- Required Agribusiness courses displayed on the page: AGRB200, AGRB210, AGRB220, AGRB300, AGRB333, AGRB335, AGRB352, AGRB360, AGRB365, AGRB391, AGRB422, AGRB444, AGRB450, AGRB480, ARAG200, ARAG307, ARAG330, ECON125, ECON231, FDSC250.
- The page asks for six 3-credit electives from AGRB392, AGRB395, ARAG443, ARAG452, AGRB445, AGRB341, AGRB371, AGRB374, AGRB377, ARAG220, ARAG305. It lists 6 free-elective credits without naming eligible courses.
- Internship AGRB410 is 4 credits, after 90 completed credits. UAEU describes three scheduling options, including schedules that allow other courses on other days of the semester.
- General Education includes 15 credits in named themes and 6 credits selected from five themes, with a maximum 3 credits from each. AGRB352 appears as a General Education option and as a required Agribusiness course, so check Degree Works for cross-counting.

## Source discrepancy and limits

The program page labels the required Agribusiness section as 59 credits, while its displayed coded course rows total 53 credits using the current catalog credit values. The assistant preserves the published section total and displayed course list, does not invent the missing six credits, and cannot certify that those rows reconcile. The individual student's assigned Degree Works plan controls cohort requirements.
