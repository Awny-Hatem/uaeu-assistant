# Student clubs listed by Campus Life

## Joining and example clubs

Audience: UAEU students seeking clubs to join. The Campus Life overview describes approximately 23 clubs and teams in its listing and says membership is open to UAEU students. Its talent/interest examples include People of Determination Club, Ibtikar Club, Emirates Astronomy Club, Environmental Club, Arts Club, Music Club, Photography Club and Reading Club. Cultural examples include Watani Al Emarat Club, Sakura Club Japan, Indian Club, Chinese Club, Pakistani Club and Bahraini Club.

This is the inspected published overview, not a live guarantee that a club is recruiting or that an event has places. Confirm current joining arrangements through the club or Campus Life contact route on the page. Do not turn rules for founding a new club into universal conditions for joining an existing club, and do not present this approximate Campus Life count as a census of every departmental association in the university.
