# Bachelor of Science in Horticulture

The current UAEU degree page states a minimum of **120 credits**: General Education 21, College requirements 12, Horticulture requirements 45, Internship 3, one 21-credit concentration, 12 elective-specialization credits, and 6 free-elective credits. Its entry-cohort applicability is not identified on the page.

## Current published requirements

- College: MATH105, PHYS105, BIOC100, STAT235.
- Required Horticulture courses: ARAG200, ARAG220, ARAG242, ARAG307, ARAG308, ARAG310, ARAG311, ARAG327, ARAG443, ARAG465, BION215, BION225, BION270, CHEM111, CHEM282, FDSC285, ARAG485.
- Choose one concentration:
  - **Environment Horticulture (21 credits):** ARAG321, ARAG402, ARAG451, ARAG453, ARAG454, plus two electives from AGRB200, ARAG313, ARAG442, ARAG408, ARAG456.
  - **Crop Production and Organic Farming (21 credits):** ARAG305, ARAG404, ARAG407, ARAG410, ARAG452, plus two electives from ARAG320, ARAG376, ARAG412, ARAG442, ARAG456.
- Choose four elective-specialization courses (12 credits) from BION230, ARAG323, ARAG401, ARAG414, ARAG437, ARAG439, ARAG455, ARAG304.
- Internship ARAG445 is 3 credits during a semester in the last study year; the page says courses can be taken on the other days. Six free-elective credits are listed without eligible course options.
- General Education includes 15 credits in named themes and 6 credits selected from five themes, with a maximum 3 credits from each.

## Cohort model plans in the linked PDF

The PDF now contains separate schedules for **2022–2023 cohorts onwards**, **2025–2026 cohorts onwards**, and **2026–2027 cohorts onwards**. Each version states 120 total credits. The 2022 plan distinguishes Fall-entry and Spring-entry schedules and has separate Environment Horticulture and Crop Production and Organic Farming concentrations. The 2025 and 2026 versions each publish the two concentration schedules for students admitted in Fall or Spring.

- The 2022 schedules use BIOL215, BIOL225, and BIOL270; the current degree page uses BION215, BION225, and BION270. The historical codes are preserved as printed; no equivalence is assumed.
- The 2025 schedule uses CHEM283 while the 2026 schedule uses FDSC285.
- The 2025/2026 PDF labels MATH105 as Calculus I in one table and as Islamic Culture/Sira in another. Page 8 repeats the conflicting title. The code and credit value are recorded, but the title conflict is unresolved.
- Model-plan elective rows often say “Student choice” without listing eligible course codes. No options are inferred from another cohort or the current degree page.
- The current degree page and cohort PDFs differ in courses and category structure. Do not combine them into a personal degree audit; use the plan assigned to the student in Degree Works and confirm uncertainty with the college advising unit.
