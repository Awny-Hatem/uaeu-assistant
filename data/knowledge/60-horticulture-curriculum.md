# Bachelor of Science in Horticulture

The current UAEU degree page states a minimum of **120 credits**: General Education 21, College requirements 12, Horticulture requirements 45, Internship 3, one 21-credit concentration, 12 elective-specialization credits, and 6 free-elective credits.

## Current published requirements

- College: MATH105, PHYS105, BIOC100, STAT235.
- Required Horticulture courses: ARAG200, ARAG220, ARAG242, ARAG307, ARAG308, ARAG310, ARAG311, ARAG327, ARAG443, ARAG465, BION215, BION225, BION270, CHEM111, CHEM282, FDSC285, ARAG485.
- Choose one concentration:
  - **Environment Horticulture (21 credits):** ARAG321, ARAG402, ARAG451, ARAG453, ARAG454, plus two electives from AGRB200, ARAG313, ARAG442, ARAG408, ARAG456.
  - **Crop Production and Organic Farming (21 credits):** ARAG305, ARAG404, ARAG407, ARAG410, ARAG452, plus two electives from ARAG320, ARAG376, ARAG412, ARAG442, ARAG456.
- Choose four elective-specialization courses (12 credits) from BION230, ARAG323, ARAG401, ARAG414, ARAG437, ARAG439, ARAG455, ARAG304.
- Internship ARAG445 is 3 credits during a semester in the last study year; the page says courses can be taken on the other days. Six free-elective credits are listed without eligible course options.
- General Education includes 15 credits in named themes and 6 credits selected from five themes, with a maximum 3 credits from each.

## Cohort limits

The linked 2020–2021 model plan states 120 credits, but differs from the current page in its course sets and structure. The current page does not identify the first cohort to which its updated requirements apply. Do not use its concentration lists as a personal cohort audit until the assigned plan is confirmed.
