# Bachelor of Science in Marine Fisheries and Animal Science

The current UAEU degree page states a minimum of **120 credits**: General Education 21, College requirements 12, major requirements 45, Internship 3, one 21-credit concentration, 12 supporting elective-specialization credits, and 6 free-elective credits. The current page does not identify the entry cohort to which these requirements apply.

## Current published requirements

- College: MATH105, PHYS105, BIOC100, STAT235.
- Listed major courses: ARAG205, ARAG220, ARAG230, ARAG310, ARAG314, ARAG316, ARAG319, ARAG335, ARAG434, ARAG440, BIOL210, BION270, CHEM111, CHEM282, FDSC285, ARAG485. UAEU shows BIOL210 with 0.00 credits and no course description.
- Choose one concentration:
  - **Marine Fisheries (21 credits):** ARAG325, ARAG326, ARAG424, ARAG425, ARAG433, plus two electives from ARAG401, ARAG426, ARAG430, FDSC319, FDSC355, BION230.
  - **Animal Science (21 credits):** ARAG423, ARAG318, ARAG322, ARAG432, ARAG435, plus 6 elective credits. The page lists five 3-credit options (BION230, ARAG304, ARAG339, ARAG436, AGRB200) but says to select three courses. That implies 9 credits, so the listed count and stated credit total conflict.
- Choose four supporting elective-specialization courses (12 credits) from AGRB352, ARAG323, ARAG329, ARAG428, ARAG450, ARAG459.
- Internship ARAG445 is 3 credits during a semester in the last study year; the page says courses can be taken on the other days. Six free-elective credits are listed without eligible course options.
- General Education includes 15 credits in named themes and 6 credits selected from five themes, with a maximum 3 credits from each.

## Cohort plans in the linked PDF

The linked PDF contains plans labeled **2022–2023 cohort onwards**, **2025–2026 cohort onwards**, and **2026–2027 cohort onwards**. Each concentration plan states 120 credits. The 2022 version has separate Fall/Spring entry schedules and two concentrations: Marine Fisheries and Animal Science, and Animal Science. The 2025 and 2026 versions each provide two concentration schedules and state they apply to Fall and Spring admits.

- The PDF's cohort labels correct the older 2020–2021 label; there is no 2020 plan in the currently linked file.
- The 2022–2025 plans list BIOL270, while the current degree page uses BION270. The codes are preserved by version; equivalence is not inferred.
- The 2025 plan lists CHEM283; the 2026 plan lists FDSC285. Do not mix those model-plan versions.
- Elective slots shown only as “Student choice” have no eligible course codes in the PDF. Those choices remain unspecified.
- The current degree page reports a 45-credit major section whose displayed coded course rows total 42 using current catalog values, and shows BIOL210 as 0 credits. Its Animal Science elective count also conflicts with the stated credits. These page facts are not reconciled by importing model-plan courses.
- Use the student's assigned Degree Works plan for personal requirements; public cohort model plans do not establish the plan assigned to a particular student.
