# Bachelor of Science in Marine Fisheries and Animal Science

The current UAEU degree page states a minimum of **120 credits**: General Education 21, College requirements 12, major requirements 45, Internship 3, one 21-credit concentration, 12 supporting elective-specialization credits, and 6 free-elective credits.

## Current published requirements

- College: MATH105, PHYS105, BIOC100, STAT235.
- Listed major courses: ARAG205, ARAG220, ARAG230, ARAG310, ARAG314, ARAG316, ARAG319, ARAG335, ARAG434, ARAG440, BIOL210, BION270, CHEM111, CHEM282, FDSC285, ARAG485. UAEU shows BIOL210 with 0.00 credits and no course description.
- Choose one concentration:
  - **Marine Fisheries (21 credits):** ARAG325, ARAG326, ARAG424, ARAG425, ARAG433, plus two electives from ARAG401, ARAG426, ARAG430, FDSC319, FDSC355, BION230.
  - **Animal Science (21 credits):** ARAG423, ARAG318, ARAG322, ARAG432, ARAG435, plus 6 elective credits. The page lists five 3-credit options (BION230, ARAG304, ARAG339, ARAG436, AGRB200) but says to select three courses. Since that would be 9 credits, the number of courses cannot be resolved from the page alone.
- Choose four supporting elective-specialization courses (12 credits) from AGRB352, ARAG323, ARAG329, ARAG428, ARAG450, ARAG459.
- Internship ARAG445 is 3 credits during a semester in the last study year; the page says courses can be taken on the other days. Six free-elective credits are listed without eligible course options.
- General Education includes 15 credits in named themes and 6 credits selected from five themes, with a maximum 3 credits from each.

## Source discrepancies and limits

The page labels the listed major-course section as 45 credits, but the displayed coded rows total 42 credits using current catalog values; BIOL210 is listed as 0.00.
