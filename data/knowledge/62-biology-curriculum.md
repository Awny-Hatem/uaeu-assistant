# Bachelor of Science in Biology

The current degree page states a minimum of **120 credits**: General Education 21, specialization 27, supporting courses 30, internship 6, one 27-credit concentration, and 9 free electives. The page does not identify which entry cohorts use these current-page requirements.

## Common requirements

- **Specialization (27 CH):** BIOC100, BIOC155, BIOC160, BIOC165, BIOC250, BIOC270, BIOC275, BIOC290, BIOC490, BIOC480, BIOC280.
- **Supporting courses (30 CH):** CHEM111, CHEM113, CHEM115, CHEM241, CHEM245, MATH105, MATH110, STAT235, GEOL100, PHYS105, PHYS135, PHYS110.
- **Internship:** current degree page lists BIOC500, 6 CH, for half a semester (8 weeks) during the last study year. The linked model-plan PDF instead lists BIOL500; preserve this source discrepancy and confirm the assigned cohort plan.
- **Free electives:** 9 CH, with no eligible-course list on the degree page.

## Concentration choice (27 CH)

Choose one:

- **Ecology and Organismal Biology:** BIOE250 (3 CH) plus 24 elective credits. The page requires at least 12 credits from the EOB list; the other 12 may be EOB or Cellular and Molecular Biology courses. EOB selection must include at least 3 CH at Level 2, 6 CH at Level 3, and 6 CH at Level 4. Level 2: BIOE212, BIOE214, BIOE230. Level 3: BIOE310, BIOE320, BIOE330, BIOE332, BIOE340, BIOE350, BIOE355, BIOE360, BIOE370, BIOE380, BIOE391. Level 4: BIOE435, BIOE436, BIOE450, BIOE454, BIOE457, BIOE459.
- **Cellular and Molecular Biology:** BIOM335 (3 CH) plus 24 elective credits. At least 12 CH must come from the CMB list; the other 12 may be CMB or EOB. CMB electives: BIOM339 (2 CH), BIOM350, BIOM310, BIOM370, BIOM380, BIOM390 (2 CH), BIOM420, BIOM430, BIOM433, BIOM461, BIOM462, BIOM470, BIOM481, BIOM489 (1 CH), BIOM490.

The linked study plan labels the internship BIOL500 while the current degree page says BIOC500. Do not combine cohort plans and current-page course sets without the student's assigned plan.
