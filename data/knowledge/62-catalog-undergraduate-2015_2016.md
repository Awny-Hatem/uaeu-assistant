# UAEU undergraduate catalog 2015–2016 — historical edition

## Historical undergraduate catalog 2015–2016

This official UAEU undergraduate catalog PDF is the historical 2015–2016 edition. The document's opening pages were checked against its printed academic-year labels. Direct official document: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcaa/aau/pdf/ugprogramcatalog2015_2016.pdf

Use this PDF when the student requests this publication and year. It is a historical source, not a statement of current program availability, current admission eligibility or the student's assigned cohort requirements. Do not replace it with the current catalog or a different year's publication.
