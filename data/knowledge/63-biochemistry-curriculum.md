# Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry

The current UAEU degree page states a minimum of **120 credits**: General Education 21, Biochemistry major 51, internship 6, supporting courses 21, major electives 9, supporting electives 6, and free electives 6. The page does not establish which entry cohorts use this exact current-page curriculum.

## Requirements

- **Required Biochemistry major (51 CH):** CHEM111, CHEM113, CHEM115, CHEM222, CHEM241, CHEM242, CHEM245, CHEM251, CHEM321, CHEM355, BIOL114, BIOE230, BIOC270, BIOC275, BCHM361, BCHM362, BCHM462, BCHM418, BCHM472, BIOM489.
- **Internship:** CHEM419, 6 CH, half a semester (8 weeks) during the last study year.
- **Supporting courses (21 CH):** BIOC100, BIOL105, ENG310, STAT235, CSBP119, PHYS105, MATH105.
- **Major electives (9 CH):** choose 3 from CHEM231, CHEM422, CHEM423, CHEM442, BIOM445, CHEM480, BIOC290, BCHM483.
- **Supporting electives (6 CH):** choose 2 from CSBP224, GEOL100, MATH110, PHYS110.
- **Free electives:** 6 CH; the page gives no eligible-course list.

General Education is 21 CH. Its page describes required themes plus 6 CH selected from other themes, including a 6-credit language sequence when a Theme 11 language is chosen.
