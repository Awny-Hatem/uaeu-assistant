# UAEU course catalog 2016–2017 — historical edition

## Historical course catalog 2016–2017

This official UAEU course catalog PDF is the historical 2016–2017 edition. The document's opening pages were checked against its printed academic-year labels. Direct official document: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcaa/aau/pdf/course_catalog_2016_2017.pdf

Use this PDF when the student requests this publication and year. It is a historical source, not a statement of current program availability, current admission eligibility or the student's assigned cohort requirements. Do not replace it with the current catalog or a different year's publication.
