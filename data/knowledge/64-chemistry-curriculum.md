# Bachelor of Science in Chemistry

The current degree page states **120 credits**: General Education 21, required Chemistry courses 48, internship 6, supporting required courses 21, Chemistry electives 9, supporting electives 6, and free electives 9. Cohort applicability is not specified.

- Required Chemistry: CHEM111, CHEM113, CHEM115, CHEM222, CHEM231, CHEM241, CHEM242, CHEM245, CHEM251, CHEM321, CHEM331, CHEM337, CHEM345, CHEM351, CHEM355, CHEM356, CHEM332, BCHM361, CHEM418.
- Internship CHEM419 (6 CH), half a semester (8 weeks) during the last study year.
- Supporting required: MATH105, MATH110, BIOC100, PHYS105, PHYS110, ENG310, CSBP119.
- Choose 3 Chemistry electives (9 CH): CHEM422, CHEM453, CHEM441, CHEM423, CHEM442, CHEM446, CHEM454, CHEM480.
- Choose 2 supporting electives (6 CH): GEOL100, MATH275, STAT235, PSYC313, PHYS250, CSBP224.
- General Education 21 CH; free electives 9 CH, eligible codes not listed.
