# Bachelor of Science in Geosciences

The current degree page states **120 credits**: General Education 21, Geoscience major 45, internship 6, non-Geoscience supporting courses 24, Geoscience electives 18, and free electives 6. Cohort applicability is not specified.

- Major: GEOL100, GEOL215, GEOL220, GEOL260, GEOL300, GEOL340, GEOL370, GEOL390, GEOL400, GEOL410, GEOL425, GEOL430, GEOL460, GEOL470, GEOL499.
- Internship GEOL500 (6 CH), half a semester (8 weeks) during the last study year.
- Supporting: MATH105, MATH110, BIOC100, PHYS105, PHYS110, CHEM111, CSBP119, STAT210.
- Choose six Geoscience electives (18 CH): GEOL345, GEOL350, GEOL395, GEOL398, GEOL415, GEOL428, GEOL440, GEOL445, GEOL475, GEOL486, GEOL488.
- General Education 21 CH; free electives 6 CH, eligible codes not listed.
