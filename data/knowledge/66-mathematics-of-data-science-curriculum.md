# BSc Mathematics of Data Science

The current page states **120 credits**. It publishes a 54-credit foundation, 15-credit Mathematics of Data Science core, 6-credit internship, and 24 credits of electives; General Education accounts for 21 credits. The linked model plan is for Fall 2024 entrants onward.

- Foundation (54): PHYS105, MATH105, MATH110, MATH140, MATH210, MATH240, CSBP119, CSBP219, CSBP323, STAT210, MATH205, MATH290, STAT240, STAT470, CSBP224, CSBP301, CSBP320, CSBP411.
- Core (15): MATH215, MATH350, MATH360, MATH496, MATH497.
- Internship MATH501 (6 CH), half a semester during the last study year.
- Electives (24 CH): choose 3 specialization electives from MATH275, MATH246, MATH310, MATH315, MATH320, MATH340; 2 advanced mathematics electives from MATH342, MATH391, MATH470, MATH475; and 3 supporting electives from SWEB450, CSBP325, CSBP341, CSBP475, STAT300, STAT360, STAT380.
- General Education is 21 credits, including required themes and selected theme courses.

Source: [UAEU Mathematics of Data Science degree requirements](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/cos/programs/undergraduate/bachelor-of-science-in--the-mathematics-of-data-science.shtml) and [model study plan](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/cos/pdf/cos_bsc_mathematics_of_data_science_study_plan.pdf).
