# UAEU graduate catalog 2018–2019 — historical edition

## Historical graduate catalog 2018–2019

This official UAEU graduate catalog PDF is the historical 2018–2019 edition. The document's opening pages were checked against its printed academic-year labels. Direct official document: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcaa/aau/pdf/graduate_programs_2018_2019.pdf

Use this PDF when the student requests this publication and year. It is a historical source, not a statement of current program availability, current admission eligibility or the student's assigned cohort requirements. Do not replace it with the current catalog or a different year's publication.
