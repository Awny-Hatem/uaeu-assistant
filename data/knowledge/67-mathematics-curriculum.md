# Bachelor of Science in Mathematics

The current degree page states **120 credits**: General Education 21, Mathematics major 45, internship 6, major electives 12, non-Mathematics required support 18, non-Mathematics support electives 12, and free electives 6. Cohort applicability is not specified.

- Required major: MATH105, MATH110, MATH140, MATH205, MATH210, MATH215, MATH246, MATH275, MATH310, MATH315, MATH320, MATH340, MATH462, MATH372, MATH495.
- Internship MATH500 (6 CH), half a semester (8 weeks) during the last study year.
- Major electives: choose 2 from MATH260, MATH321, MATH342, MATH344, MATH374, MATH391, MATH470, MATH471, and 2 from MATH313, MATH341, MATH413, MATH422, MATH443.
- Required supporting: ENG310, CSBP119, CSBP224, STAT230, PHYS110, PHYS105.
- Supporting electives: choose 4 from ARB110, ENG250, CSBP219, STAT210, STAT240, PHYS235, PHYS262.
- General Education 21 CH; free electives 6 CH, eligible codes not listed.
