# Bachelor of Science in Physics

The current degree page states **120 credits** and two concentration choices: General Physics or Space Sciences. It does not identify which entry cohorts use this page's requirements.

- General Education 21 CH; required common Physics courses 32 CH; internship PHYS500 6 CH; concentration 24 CH; required non-major support 25 CH; supporting electives 6 CH; free electives 6 CH.
- Common Physics courses: PHYS105, PHYS110, PHYS135, PHYS140, PHYS205, PHYS210, PHYS220, PHYS233, PHYS234, PHYS235, PHYS250, PHYS255, PHYS335, PHYS494.
- General Physics concentration: PHYS262, PHYS313, PHYS355, PHYS470, PHYS483 plus 3 electives from PHYS330, PHYS345, PHYS385, PHYS390, PHYS430, PHYS450, PHYS475, PHYS495.
- Space Sciences concentration: PHYS200, PHYS270, PHYS310, PHYS316, PHYS410, PHYS420 plus 6 CH from PHYS313, PHYS330, PHYS345, PHYS355, PHYS385, PHYS390, PHYS495.
- Supporting required: CHEM175, MATH105, CSBP119, CHEM111, CSBP224, MATH110, MATH140, STAT210, MATH275. Choose two supporting electives from GEOL100, MATH210, CSBP400, ENG310.
