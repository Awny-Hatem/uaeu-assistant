# Bachelor of Science in Space Sciences

UAEU lists Space Sciences as a distinct undergraduate degree. Its current degree page states **120 credits**: General Education 21, major 60 (including 6-credit internship), major electives 9, required supporting courses 24, and supporting electives 6. The page does not identify cohort applicability.

- Major (60): PHYS105, PHYS110, PHYS135, PHYS140, PHYS205, GEO211, PHYS212, PHYS220, PHYS225, PHYS235, PHYS255, PHYS260, PHYS270, PHYS310, PHYS335, PHYS360, PHYS370, PHYS400, CSBP478, PHYS494, PHYS500 (6-credit internship).
- Choose 3 major electives (9 CH): PHYS233, PHYS325, PHYS330, PHYS333, PHYS415, GEO490, AERO506.
- Supporting required (24): MATH105, MATH110, CHEM111, CSBP119, MATH140, STAT210, MATH275, MATH372.
- Choose 2 supporting electives (6 CH): BIOC100, GEOL100, CHEM113, GEOL215, CSBP224.
- General Education 21 CH.
