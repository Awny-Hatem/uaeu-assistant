# Bachelor of Law

The current official degree page lists a minimum of **130 credit hours**: 21 General Education, 96 required major credits, 9 elective credits, and 4 training credits. The page does not identify which entry cohorts use these requirements; the student’s assigned model plan controls.

- Required major courses (96 CH): LAW115, LAW228, LAW230, LAW400, PRVT113, PRVT211, PRVT213, PRVT214, PRVT227, PRVT302, PRVT304, PRVT307, PRVT338, PRVT406, PRVT407, PRVT408, PRVT410, PRVT453, PRVT455, PRVT462, PUBL114, PUBL206, PUBL207, PUBL215, PUBL216, PUBL305, PUBL311, PUBL335, PUBL112, PRVT204, PRVT327, PUBL329.
- Electives: choose two Arabic-group courses (6 CH) from PRVT201, PUBL306, PUBL309, PUBL401, PRVT339, PUBL341, PRVT314; and one English-group course (3 CH) from PUBL308 or ECON110.
- Training: LAW340 Training 1 (3 CH) and LAW440 Training 2 (1 CH).
- General Education: 21 CH; follow current theme selection and language-pairing requirements in the assigned plan.

Do not assume these current-page requirements apply to a cohort without checking its assigned model plan or Degree Works record.
