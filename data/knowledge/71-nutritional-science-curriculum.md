# Bachelor of Science in Nutritional Science

The current official degree page lists a minimum of **120 credit hours**: 21 General Education, 75 required courses, a 3-credit NUTR491 internship, 15 program-elective credits, and 6 free-elective credits. Cohort applicability is not identified on the page.

- Required courses (75 CH): BIOC100, MATH105, BION275, BION230, BION270, BIOM229, CHEM111, CHEM113, CHEM115, CHEM282, CHEM283, PHYS105, STAT235, FDSC330, NUTR101, NUTR220, NUTR320, NUTR321, NUTR330, NUTR331, NUTR355, NUTR352, NUTR360, NUTR375, NUTR380, NUTR401, NUTR410, NUTR460, NUTR480, NUTR482.
- Choose five (15 CH) from PHYS110, AGRB395, AGRB360, FDSC309, NUTR379, NUTR396, NUTR443, NUTR478.
- NUTR491 internship is 3 CH during a complete semester in the last study year; no other courses may be registered during the internship.
- General Education is 21 CH; free electives total 6 CH.

Confirm the assigned cohort plan before using this as a personal graduation audit.
