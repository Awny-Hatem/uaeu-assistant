# Bachelor of Science in Dietetics

The current official degree page lists a minimum of **120 credit hours**: 21 General Education, 81 required course credits, 6 internship credits, 6 program-elective credits, and 6 free-elective credits. The page does not establish cohort applicability.

- Required Coordinated Program in Dietetics courses (81 CH): BIOC100, MATH105, CHEM111, CHEM113, CHEM115, CHEM282, CHEM283, PHYS105, MGMT201, BION230, BION270, BION275, STAT235, FDSC331, NUTR101, NUTR220, NUTR320, NUTR321, NUTR330, NUTR331, NUTR355, NUTR352, NUTR371, NUTR372, NUTR376, NUTR378, NUTR403, NUTR404, NUTR446, NUTR460, NUTR481, NUTR484, NUTR485, NUTR486, NUTR487, NUTR492, NUTR489.
- NUTR490 internship: 6 CH over 24 weeks after completing all coursework; no other courses may be registered during it.
- Choose two (6 CH) from FDSC352, NUTR396, NUTR443. Free electives total 6 CH.
- General Education totals 21 CH and follows the current university theme and language-pairing rules.

The official page describes the Coordinated Program in Dietetics and supervised-practice requirements; use the assigned cohort plan for an individual audit.
