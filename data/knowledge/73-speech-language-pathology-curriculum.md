# Bachelor of Science in Speech-Language Pathology

The current official degree page lists a minimum of **120 credit hours**: 21 General Education, 75 core credits, and 24 practical clinical-training credits. Cohort applicability is not identified on the page.

- Foundation courses (24 CH): SLP100, SLP220, SLP290, SLP450, PSYC305, PHYS105, BIOC100, STAT180.
- Introductory courses (18 CH): SLP106, SLP236, SLP216, SLP246, SLP276, PSYC201.
- Pre-clinical courses (33 CH): SLP256, SLP286, SLP306, SLP316, SLP326, SLP336, SLP356, SLP366, SLP426, SLP446, SLP346.
- Clinical training (24 CH): SLP406 (3), SLP416 (3), SLP456 Practicum 1 (9), SLP466 Practicum 2 (9). Practicum 1 requires at least 200 clinical hours and completion of all other major requirements except SLP406 and SLP416; no concurrent courses are allowed. Practicum 2 requires at least 300 clinical hours, completion of all other major requirements, and no concurrent courses.
- General Education totals 21 CH and follows the current university theme and language-pairing rules.

The current page does not identify the student's assigned cohort; verify the model plan and Degree Works record before planning enrollment.
