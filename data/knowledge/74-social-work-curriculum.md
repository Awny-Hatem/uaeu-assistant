# Bachelor of Social Work

The official model study plan is labelled **2020–2021 cohort onwards** and lists **120 credit hours**. The plan assigns 33 credits to General Education, 66 to the Social Work major, 18 to a minor, and 3 to free electives. The plan's own cohort label is preserved, but an individual student's applicability must be checked against their assigned plan.

The required Social Work courses listed by the model plan are SWK200, SWK210, SWK220, SWK230, SWK240, SWK250, SWK251, SWK320, SWK350, SWK351, SWK355, SWK360, SWK361, SWK365, SWK375, SWK376, SWK380, SWK385, SWK465, SWK466, SWK470, SWK485, and SWK499. The 2021–2022 undergraduate catalog describes SWK465 and SWK470 as internships conducted over two semesters and limits registration to at most 6 additional course credits during each internship semester.

The credit total and required course list describe the published model plan; they do not establish a student's completed credits, assigned catalog year, offered sections, or eligibility to register. Confirm the assigned curriculum and audit with the College Advising Unit.

Sources: [UAEU Bachelor of Social Work Model Study Plan](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/pdf/chss_bsc_social_work_study_plan.pdf); [UAEU Undergraduate Program Catalog 2021–2022](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcaa/aau/pdf/undergraduate_programs_2021_2022.pdf).
