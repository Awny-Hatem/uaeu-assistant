# Bachelor of Arts in English Literature

The current official degree page requires **120 credits**: 21 General Education, 6 college requirements, 57 compulsory courses, 6 supporting language credits, 6 internship credits, 12 program electives, and 12 free-elective credits.

Compulsory courses: ENG151, ENG220, ENG240, ENG315, ENG320, ENG335, ENG330, ENG355, ENG365, ENG370, ENG385, ENG410, ENG420, ENG250, ENG310, ENG450, ENG489. Supporting language: choose one language and complete Level 1 and Level 2 in that same language: KOR101/102, GER101/102, SPN101/102, CHIN101/102, or FCH101/102. Internship ENG490 is 6 credits in the final semester, with no concurrent courses.

Choose four electives (12 credits) from ENG300, ENG354, EWR380, EWR390, ENG330, ENG314, ENG340, ENG360, ENG361, and ENG368. The official page lists ENG330 in both its compulsory and elective groups; students should confirm with the College Advising Unit whether it can count in both categories for their assigned plan. The page also requires HSSR101 and HSSR103 (6 credits) and 12 free-elective credits.

The page's current course lists and linked 2020–2021 model plan do not prove which plan applies to an individual student's entry cohort. Verify the assigned plan and degree audit before course planning.
