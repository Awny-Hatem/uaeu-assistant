# Bachelor of Arts in Tourism Studies

The current official degree page requires **120 credits**: 21 General Education, 6 college requirements, 45 Tourism major, 6 internship, 21 program electives, 9 supplemental language, and 12 free-elective credits.

Required major and history courses: TOR101, TOR140, TOR205, TOR222, TOR263, TOR350, TOR403, TOR404, TOR416, TOR421, TOR485, HIS121, HIS133, HIS212, HIS381. Internship TOR440 is 6 credits in a complete semester; no other courses may be registered during it.

Choose three courses (9 credits) from cluster 1: HIS312, PSG120, PSG250, MKTG200, MGMT201, TOR202, GEO432. Choose four courses (12 credits) from cluster 2: HIS122, HIS215, HIS217, HIS310, HIS318, HIS372, HIS440, HIS471, TOR322. For supplemental language, choose three courses (9 credits) from one language sequence: Chinese CHIN101/102/201, French FCH101/102/202, Korean KOR101/102/202, Spanish SPN101/102/201, or German GER101/102/201. College requirements are HSSR101 and HSSR103.

The current degree page does not establish the student's assigned cohort, completed credits, course availability, or individual eligibility. Verify the assigned plan and Degree Works audit with the College Advising Unit.
