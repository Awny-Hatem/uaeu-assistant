# Bachelor of Arts in History and Archaeology

The current official degree page requires **120 credits**: 21 General Education, 6 college requirements, 42 specialization core, 18 in one concentration, 6 internship, 9 supporting courses, 6 major electives, and 12 free electives.

Specialization core: HIS121, HIS201, HIS202, HIS204, HIS212, HIS230, HIS302, HIS312, HIS335, HIS332, HIS381, HIS485, TOR322, MSC370. Choose one 18-credit concentration:

- History: HIS122, HIS133, HIS200, HIS318, HIS374, HIS440.
- Archaeology: HIS217, HIS308, HIS310, HIS311, HIS350, HIS372.

HIS402 is a 6-credit full-semester internship; no concurrent courses are allowed. Supporting courses are GEO211, GEO220, PSG250. Choose two major electives from HIS123, HIS124, HIS213, HIS215, HIS239, HIS241, HIS243, HIS373. College requirements are HSSR101 and HSSR103. The published General Education theme rule requires a Level 2 course in the same language after a Level 1 language course.

The page does not establish which curriculum applies to a student's cohort, completed credits, offered sections, or personal registration eligibility. Verify the assigned plan in Degree Works or with the College Advising Unit.
