# Bachelor of Arts in Political Science

The current official degree page requires **120 credits**: 21 General Education, 6 college requirements, 45 compulsory courses, 3 internship, 12 program electives, 15 major supporting courses, and 18 free electives.

Compulsory courses: PSG110, PSG120, PSG130, PSG140, PSG204, PSG242, PSG250, PSG261, PSG270, PSG303, PSG321, PSG402, PSG386, PSG425, PSG485. Internship PSG440 is 3 credits in a complete semester with no concurrent courses. Choose four electives from PSG255, PSG302, PSG305, PSG312, PSG313, PSG315, PSG352, PSG422, PSG430.

For 15 supporting credits, choose either (a) five Option 1 courses from GEO210, HIS212, ARB106, HRMD310, PUBL206, PUBL114, ECON105, MSC211, MSC412, SOC314, or (b) three Option 1 courses plus one two-course language sequence: CHIN101/102, FCH101/102, KOR101/102, SPN101/102, or GER101/102. College requirements are HSSR101 and HSSR103.

The official model plan is labelled 2020–2021 cohort onwards, but the current page does not map requirements to each student's assigned cohort. Verify the plan and Degree Works audit with the College Advising Unit.

Sources: [UAEU BA Political Science degree requirements](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/undergraduate/programs/bachelor-of-arts-in-political-science.shtml); [UAEU Political Science Model Study Plan](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/pdf/chss_bsc_political_sciences_study_plan.pdf).
