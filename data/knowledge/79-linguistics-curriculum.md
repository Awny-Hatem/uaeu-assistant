# Bachelor of Arts in Linguistics

The current official degree page requires **120 credits**: 21 General Education, 6 college requirements, 63 Linguistics major, 6 practicum, 9 supporting courses, 3 major elective, and 12 free-elective credits.

Required major courses: LNG100, LNG220, LNG231, LNG241, LNG250, LNG290, LNG321, LNG331, LNG341, LNG342, LNG370, LNG390, LNG410, LNG420, LNG450, LNG460, LNG480, LNG485, LNG489, LNG490, and LNG301. Full-semester practicum LNG456 is 6 credits; no other courses may be registered during it. Supporting courses are SLP106, SLP276, and SLP306. Choose one elective from LNG415, LNG470, LNG475.

General Education Theme 11 requires both Level 1 and Level 2 in one language sequence: CHIN101/102, FCH101/102, GER101/102, KOR101/102, or SPN101/102. College requirements are HSSR101 and HSSR103.

The degree page does not establish the student's assigned cohort, completed credits, offered sections, or personal registration eligibility. Verify the assigned plan and audit with the College Advising Unit.
