# Bachelor of Arts in Mass Communication

The current official degree page requires **120 credits**: 21 General Education, 6 college requirements, 27 Mass Communication core, 6 practicum, 27 in one concentration, 18 supporting courses, and 15 free electives.

Core courses: MSC200, MSC203, MSC211, MSC235, MSC370, MSC396, PUBL421, MSC480, MSC484. Practicum MSC490 is 6 credits in a complete semester; no courses may be registered concurrently. Choose one concentration:

- Journalism (27 credits): MSC250, MSC264, MSC325, MSC356, MSC390, MSC401, MSC450, MSC485, MSC488.
- Public Relations and Advertising (27 credits): MSC243, MSC250, MSC342, MSC377, MSC400, MSC418, MSC422, MSC452, MSC485.
- Television Broadcasting (27 credits): MSC257, MSC316, MSC344, MSC352, MSC355, MSC388, MSC399, MSC485, MSC488.

Choose six supporting courses from ARB205, ARB110, TRS200, TRS370, MSC266, MSC240 and the foreign-language pairs SPN101/102, CHIN101/102, GER101/102, KOR101/102, FCH101/102. If taking a foreign language, both courses in the same language are required. College requirements are HSSR101 and HSSR103.

The current page does not establish cohort applicability, completed credits, offered sections, or individual eligibility. Verify the assigned plan and Degree Works audit with the College Advising Unit.
