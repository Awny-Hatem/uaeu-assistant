# UAEU Bachelor of Arts in Translation Studies — 2025–2026 cohort model

Verified: 2026-11-11.

## Official evidence

- Current degree requirements: https://uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/programs/undergraduate/bachelor-of-arts-in-translation-studies.shtml
- Model study plan, including separate entry-cohort plans: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/pdf/chss_bsc_translation_stuides_study_plan.pdf

The structured model below uses the official 2025–2026 cohort-onwards study plan and current degree-requirements page. It is not a substitution for a student's assigned Degree Works plan. The PDF also publishes earlier cohort plans; those are not merged into this version because course and internship details differ between cohorts.

## Credit structure

The published degree requires 120 credits:

- General Education: 21 credits.
- College requirements: 6 credits, HSSR101 and HSSR103.
- Compulsory Translation Studies courses: 42 credits.
- Internship: 6 credits, TRS490.
- Major electives: 15 credits, choose five courses from the approved list.
- Third-language sequence: 18 credits, choose one language pathway.
- Free electives: 12 credits.

## Compulsory courses

ARB110, ENG250, ENG450, ENG310, TRS200, TRS210, TRS220, TRS312, TRS340, TRS400, TRS411, TRS440, TRS433, and TRS485. Each is listed as three credits on the degree page, for 42 credits total.

## Major electives

Choose five (15 credits) from ARB106, MSC235, ENG354, ENG355, TRS230, TRS310, TRS320, TRS355, TRS370, TRS412, TRS425, and TRS455.

## Third-language pathways

Choose one complete six-course, 18-credit sequence:

- French: FCH101, FCH102, FCH202, FCH310, FCH315, FCH420.
- Korean: KOR101, KOR102, KOR202, KOR310, KOR315, KOR420.
- German: GER101, GER102, GER201, GER310, GER315, GER420.
- Spanish: SPN101, SPN102, SPN201, SPN310, SPN315, SPN420.
- Chinese: CHIN101, CHIN102, CHIN201, CHIN420, CHIN310, CHIN302.

## Internship and General Education constraints

The degree page states TRS490 is a six-credit practicum conducted over a complete semester and that students may not register for other courses during the internship. It does not explicitly state that the internship must be the final semester.

General Education includes five fixed three-credit themes (15 credits), then six credits selected across themes 6–10 with no more than three credits from any one theme. The assigned cohort determines the eligible theme courses.

## Applicability limits

This model applies to the published Fall/Spring 2025–2026 cohort-onwards plan. Earlier cohorts have their own model plans and must not be given this course/internship structure by default. The published course list does not reveal a student's completed credits, prerequisites, registration eligibility, available sections, or final degree audit. Confirm those in Degree Works and with the College Advising Unit.
