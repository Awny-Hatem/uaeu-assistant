# UAEU Bachelor of Arts in Psychology — 2025–2026 cohort model

Verified: 2026-11-11.

## Official evidence

- Degree requirements: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/programs/undergraduate/bachelor-of-arts-in-psychology.shtml
- Model study plan, with separate entry-cohort plans: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/pdf/chss_bsc_psycology_study_plan.pdf

The structured model is the official 2025–2026 cohort-onwards plan and current degree page. The study-plan PDF also contains older cohorts; do not apply this model to an earlier cohort without its assigned plan.

## Credit structure

The degree page lists a 120-credit minimum:

- General Education: 21 credits.
- College requirements: HSSR101 and HSSR103, 6 credits.
- Required Psychology major courses: 54 credits.
- Internship and research project: PSYC452 and PSYC454, 3 credits each.
- Major supporting courses: choose two from SOC101, SOC190, CURR314, SWK205, and SWK230 (6 credits).
- Psychology electives: choose five from the approved 13-course list below (15 credits).
- Free electives: 12 credits.

## Required major courses (54 credits)

PSYC100, PSYC200, PSYC201, PSYC203, PSYC206, PSYC303, PSYC305, PSYC306, PSYC308, PSYC310, PSYC317, PSYC318, PSYC319, PSYC401, PSYC402, PSYC413, STAT180, and STAT280. The degree page lists each at 3 credits.

## Internship and project constraints

- PSYC452 Internship in Psychology is 3 credits and spans a complete semester. No other courses may be registered while taking it.
- PSYC454 Research Project is 3 credits and spans a complete semester. Up to 6 additional course credits may be registered with it.

These concurrent-course rules differ. Do not combine them into one generic internship condition or infer permission beyond the PSYC454 six-credit limit.

## Psychology electives (choose five)

PSYC307, PSYC312, PSYC315, PSYC321, PSYC322, PSYC323, PSYC405, PSYC406, PSYC408, PSYC409, PSYC410, PSYC411, and PSYC420.

## General Education constraints

Themes 1–5 contribute 15 fixed credits. Students select the remaining 6 credits from themes 6–11. No more than 3 credits may come from any one of themes 6–10. Theme 11 must be a complete 6-credit sequence in one language. If a student registers for CHIN101, FCH101, KOR101, GER101, or SPN101, the matching Level 2 course is required.

## Applicability limits

The published plan is explicitly labelled for students admitted from Fall or Spring 2025–2026 onwards. Earlier cohorts have separate plans and may have different course codes or sequencing. The published requirements do not establish a student's passed-course eligibility, availability of sections, completed credits, or graduation audit; confirm the student's assigned plan and record with Degree Works and the College Advising Unit.
