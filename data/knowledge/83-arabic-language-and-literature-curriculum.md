# UAEU Bachelor of Arts in Arabic Language and Literature — 2025–2026 cohort model

Verified: 2026-11-11.

## Official evidence

- Current degree requirements: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/undergraduate/programs/bachelor-of-arts-in-arabic-language-and-literature.shtml
- Model study plan, including separate cohort plans: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/pdf/chss_bsc_arabic_language_study_plan.pdf

This model applies to students admitted from Fall or Spring 2025–2026 onwards. Earlier cohort plans appear in the PDF and must not be replaced with this plan without confirming the student's assigned cohort model.

## Credit structure

The detailed degree-requirement tables and the 2025–2026 model plan support a 120-credit minimum:

- General Education: 21 credits.
- College requirements: 6 credits (HSSR101 and HSSR103).
- Supporting requirements: choose two courses, 6 credits total.
- Required Arabic Language and Literature major courses: 51 credits.
- Internship: ARB490, 6 credits.
- Major electives: 18 credits, split into two required clusters.
- Free electives: 12 credits.

The degree page's introductory summary conflicts with its detailed tables: it claims 33 General Education credits and 66 major credits while the detailed requirements specify 21 and 51, respectively. The detailed buckets above sum to 120. The assistant should disclose this source inconsistency when a student asks about the credit breakdown and should not repeat the conflicting introductory figures as if they were consistent with the table.

## Required major courses (51 credits)

ARB110, ARB120, ARB160, ARB170, ARB180, ARB206, ARB210, ARB211, ARB230, ARB250, ARB311, ARB313, ARB321, ARB430, ARB444, ARB450, and ARB485. Each is listed as three credits on the degree page.

## Supporting requirements

Choose two (6 credits) from TRS200, MSC235, and CURR101.

## Major electives (18 credits)

Choose four Language and Literature courses (12 credits) from ARB220, ARB260, ARB299, ARB303, ARB316, ARB324, ARB401, and ARB409; and choose two Writing courses (6 credits) from ARB106, ARB204, ARB205, ARB306, and ARB404.

## General Education choices

Themes 1–5 are fixed for 15 credits. Select the remaining 6 credits across themes 6–11, with no more than 3 credits from each theme except theme 11, where all 6 credits must be one complete language sequence. A level-1 language course must be followed by its matching level-2 course.

## Internship and applicability limits

ARB490 is taken in the last semester. No other courses may be registered during that semester. The source does not establish a student's completed requirements, course offerings, or personal graduation audit; confirm the assigned study plan and record with the advising unit.
