# UAEU Bachelor of Arts in Visual Studies and Creative Industries — 2025–2026 cohort model

Verified: 2026-11-11.

## Official evidence

- Degree requirements: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/undergraduate/programs/bachelor-of-arts-in-visual-studies-and-creative-industries.shtml
- Model study plan: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/pdf/chss_bsc_visual_studies_and_creative_industries_study_plan.pdf

The study plan labels a 2025–2026 cohort-onwards model and a 120-credit degree. Confirm the student's assigned plan before applying these details to a personal degree audit.

## Credit structure

- General Education: 21 credits.
- College requirements: 6 credits (HSSR101 and HSSR103).
- Core major courses: 54 credits.
- Internship: VIS451, 6 credits.
- Major electives: 21 credits according to the detailed course groups.
- Free electives: 12 credits.

The degree page says to select six major electives overall, but its three explicit groups require two, two, and three courses (21 credits total). The model preserves each explicit group count and flags the conflict; students should confirm it with the College Advising Unit.

## Core major courses (54 credits)

VIS104, VIS201, VIS214, VIS215, VIS412, VIS413, VIS301, VIS302, VIS303, VIS304, VIS311, VIS312, VIS313, VIS314, VIS382, VIS404, VIS411, and VIS485.

## Major electives

- Group 1: choose two from VIS401, VIS216, VIS315, and VIS320.
- Group 2: choose two from TOR140, VIS424, VIS425, PRVT462, ENTR310, and MSC243.
- Group 3: choose three from VIS123, ENG242, ENG314, MSC250, MSC257, MSC462, CIVL201, and MECH201.

VIS451 is a six-credit internship in the last semester. The degree page prohibits registering for other courses during that semester.
