# UAEU Bachelor of Arts in Geography — 2025–2026 cohort model

Verified: 2026-11-11.

## Official evidence

- Degree requirements: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/undergraduate/programs/bachelor-of-arts-in-geography.shtml
- Model study plan with separate concentration plans: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/pdf/chss_bsc_geography_study_plan.pdf

The 2025–2026 cohort-onwards plan states a 120-credit total and offers separate Environmental Geography, Geoinformatics, and Urban Planning paths. A student's selected concentration and assigned cohort plan must be confirmed before an individual audit.

## Shared credit structure

- General Education: 21 credits.
- College requirements: 6 credits (HSSR101 and HSSR103).
- Geography core: 24 credits (GEO200, GEO201, GEO210, GEO211, GEO220, GEO221, GEO334, and GEO336).
- One concentration: 57 credits.
- Free electives: 12 credits.

## Concentrations

Each concentration has 27 required credits, a six-credit full-semester internship with no concurrent courses, two supporting courses (6 credits), and six concentration electives (18 credits).

- Environmental Geography: GEO482 internship; supporting options BIOC100, BIOC250, GEBS220, ARAG304. Electives: GEO231, GEO335, GEO341, GEO395, GEO402, GEO413, GEO432, GEO450, GEO460, GEO462.
- Geoinformatics: GEO483 internship; supporting options TOR101, TOR263, TOR350, HIS312. Electives: GEO341, GEO430, GEO432, GEO440, GEO450, GEO460, GEO462, GEO470, GEO490, GEO497.
- Urban Planning: GEO481 internship; supporting options TOR101, HIS381, SOC301, SOC327. Electives: GEO340, GEO341, GEO370, GEO430, GEO432, GEO450, GEO460, GEO462, GEO470, GEO495.

The degree page's General Education Theme 11 list omits SPN101 while listing SPN102. Confirm an approved Spanish sequence before selecting it. The model does not establish which concentration a student selected, completed courses, current offerings, or a personal graduation audit.
