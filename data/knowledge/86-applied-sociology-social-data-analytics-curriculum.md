# UAEU Bachelor of Science in Applied Sociology and Social Data Analytics — 2025–2026 cohort model

Verified: 2026-11-11.

## Official evidence

- Degree requirements: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/undergraduate/programs/bachelor-of-science-in-applied-sociology-and-social-data-analytics.shtml
- Model study plan, with separate concentration plans: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/chss/pdf/chss_bsc_applied_sociology_and_social_data_analytics_study_plan.pdf

The 2025–2026 cohort-onwards study plan states a 120-credit total. The program offers three concentrations; the student's selected concentration and assigned plan must be known before an individual audit.

## Credit structure

- General Education: 21 credits.
- College requirements: 6 credits (HSSR101 and HSSR103).
- Compulsory core courses: 48 credits.
- Internship: SOC404, 6 credits.
- Supporting course: STAT101, 3 credits.
- One concentration: 33 credits (18 required concentration credits and five electives worth 15 credits).
- Free elective: 3 credits.

The compulsory core courses are SOC100, SOC101, SOC103, SOC203, SOC280, SOC290, SOC304, SOC324, SOC327, SOC328, SOC335, SOC350, SOC403, SOC485, STAT216, and STAT316.

## Concentrations

- Sociology of Consumption: required MGMT201, MKTG200, SOC104, SOC329, SOC345, SOC338; choose five electives from SOC260, SOC306, SOC312, SOC313, SOC325, SOC332, CSBP119, CSBP224, CSBP325, and STAT240.
- Social Policy Analysis: required SOC308, SOC313, SOC334, SOC318, SOC344, PSG315; choose five electives from SOC309, SOC314, SOC315, SOC332, SOC337, SOC345, CSBP119, CSBP224, CSBP325, and PSG255.
- Quality of Life Analysis: required SOC301, SOC306, SOC309, SOC336, SOC339, PHCM309; choose five electives from SOC312, SOC314, SOC325, SOC337, PSG120, PSG130, BSTA110, BSTA218, CSBP119, and CSBP224.

SOC404 is a six-credit internship held five days a week over a complete semester; students cannot register for other courses during it.

## General Education note

The degree page fixes 15 credits under themes 1–5 and requires six additional credits from the five listed choices: themes 6, 7, 8, 10, and 11. Theme 9 is not listed on this program page, so the assistant must not assume Theme 9 options apply to this degree. Theme 11 requires a complete language sequence.
