# UAEU Doctor of Medicine — current published requirements

Verified: 2026-11-11.

## Official evidence

- Degree requirements and program structure: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/undergraduate/programs/doctor-of-medicine.shtml

## Credit structure

The current degree page states a 233-credit minimum: 21 General Education credits and 212 major credits. Its named major course rows total 212 credits in the official course catalog. Three General Education courses (MEED411, MEED403, and MEED103) are marked as also counting toward the major. The page does not explain how this cross-counting is handled on individual student audits; the assistant must not calculate a student's remaining credits from this summary.

## Program phases and graduation sequence

The current page describes six years in three two-year phases: Foundations of Medicine, Pre-Clerkship, and Clerkship. Students complete all three phases before eligibility for the Final Integrated Examination (FIE). The page says the FIE qualifies students to graduate and proceed to a one-year internship at a healthcare facility. That post-graduation internship is not part of the 233-credit degree total.

## Applicability limits

The page does not establish a student's cohort-specific plan, completed-course status, progression or FIE eligibility, clinical placement, or personal graduation audit. Confirm these with the College of Medicine and Health Sciences. Do not apply these summary totals to an individual record without the assigned plan and official audit.
