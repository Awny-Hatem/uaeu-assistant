# Bachelor of Science in Information Technology — plan published for 2025 onwards

The official CIT advising page links a generic Information Technology study plan labeled "2025 Onwards." That PDF states a total of **120 credit hours** and shows Fall and Spring semester sequences. This is a published plan, not a student's personal degree audit.

## Published sequence

- **Year 1 Fall:** GEAE101, MATH105, CSBP119, PHYS105, a General Education elective, CSBP121.
- **Year 1 Spring:** CENG202, MATH110, CSBP219, CENG205, CSBP221, GEEM110.
- **Year 2 Fall:** STAT210, CENG210, CSBP319, ITBP280, GESU121.
- **Year 2 Spring:** CSBP340, CENG530, ITBP301, a General Education elective, CENG529.
- **Year 3 Fall:** ITBP322, a Basic Biology or General Chemistry choice, CSBP315, CSBP320, ITBP321.
- **Year 3 Spring:** a program elective, ITBP323, CSBP316, ITBP270, ITBP324.
- **Year 4 Fall:** a program elective, ITBP480, ITBP218, CSBP301, and a General Education elective.
- **Year 4 Spring:** two free electives, ITBP481, and a program elective.
- The listed internship block is six credits and says additional courses require College approval. The PDF prints the course code as **ITBP495 496**, without clarifying which code applies.

## Version and source limits

A separate 2025–26 model-plan snapshot in the local evidence set states **130 credits**, while this currently linked 2025-onwards plan states **120**. The sources do not explain whether the 130-credit plan was superseded or which version applies to each student's record. Do not silently combine their semester sequences or totals. Ask the student to check the plan assigned in Degree Works and confirm the conflict with the College Advising Unit.

The 2025-onwards PDF also prints **BOIC100** for one science choice, while the current catalog lists **BIOC100**. Treat that code as a source ambiguity until the official course record or advising unit confirms it.

Use the official course catalog for course prerequisites. This model plan does not establish a student's completed courses, remaining credits, available sections, or eligibility to register.
