## Frequently Asked Questions > What are the Library’s open hours?
Library hours depend on time of the year, such as Regular Semester, Summer Semester, Ramadan, etc. It is best to see the current hours in our library hour’s .

## Frequently Asked Questions > Can the general community (non-UAEU) use the library?
Yes, the general community (non-UAEU) may use the library during appropriate male/female timings . If you wish to borrow any books, you can apply for Community User Membership, which requires a deposit and registration fee.

For more information, please contact the Circulation staff at circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae or by telephone at 03 713 4684 , 03 713 4671

## Frequently Asked Questions > How do I get clearance from the library?
You should first make sure your library record is clear (all books returned, and any fines/fees are paid) by contacting the Lending Desk staff:

By email request at: circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae

Onsite at the Lending Desk

By telephone at 03 713 4684 - 4677- 4671

In person at any lending desk

## Frequently Asked Questions > How much money do I owe in library fines or fees?
The amount depends on whether or not you have overdue books that have accrued fines, and replacement and/or processing fees for lost or missing books.

Usually, you will receive email notification for any overdue books or fees, but please check with the Lending Desk staff to confirm your fees at circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae or by telephone at 03 713 4684 - 4677- 4671.

## Frequently Asked Questions > How can I pay my fines?
Please contact the Lending Desk staff by email at circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae or by telephone at 03 713 4684 - 4677 - 4671 to confirm the exact amount that you owe. Then, you can pay by using one of the following methods.

If you have a Visa or MasterCard, you can pay directly at the lending Desk.

Alternatively, you may deposit the amount into the Library Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank account (# 11447524920001), and obtain a receipt. You can then either:

send the original bank deposit receipt via email circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae ,

Or bring it in person to the library service desk.

## Frequently Asked Questions > How do I find a book in the library?
You can search for a book using the library’s online catalog at:

https://uaeu.on.worldcat.org/advancedsearch?queryString=&spellcheck=true , Once you find the book, note the call number, which tells you where to find it on the shelf.

## Frequently Asked Questions > Where is the Library located?
The Main Library is located at UAEU in Building D2. Access is via the basement of the Crescent Building.

## Frequently Asked Questions > If I am not a member of UAEU, may I still access your e-resources?
Yes, e-resources are available for the non-member physically present in the library.

## Frequently Asked Questions > I can’t access the e-resources, even though I entered my username and password. What do I need to do?
Please make sure that you are using the correct email username (without the extension "@uaeu.ac.ae") and your password.

Make sure you are accessing the e-resources from our website or try clearing your browser history.

Or request the assistance via e-mail: digital.library@uaeu.ac.ae

## Frequently Asked Questions > Can the libraries provide assistance with publishing my research?
Yes, the Libraries offer support for free for faculty, students and staff with: literature reviews (including systematic reviews); accessing documents and papers from other institutions; selection of journals for publication; manuscript editing; and support for publishing with commercial publishers or publishing via the UAEU institutional repository as open access material. Or you can contact Dr. (Shahab Majoud) via e-mail: Smajud@uaeu.ac.ae

## Frequently Asked Questions > Tipasa Interlibrary Loan of books for LIWA Consortium
Who can request and borrow via LIWA Tipasa?

Registered students, staff and faculty of UAEU.

How many items can be requested?

A patron can borrow maximum of 10 items for faculty member, students and staff per visit.

For how long can an item be borrowed?

LIWA Tipasa's item can be borrowed for one month for all faculty member, students and staff. The item can be renewed for two times.

What happens if the book is returned late?

Overdue notices will be sent to the patron as a reminder to return the book.

What happens if an item is lost?

Patrons will be required to pay for replacement of lost or damaged items at their local library. A default fee is DHS100 will be added to the price of the lost or damaged item.

What can be borrowed?

All items can be borrowed except for non-circulating items e.g., multimedia materials and Reference books (e.g., Encyclopedia, dictionaries) and thesis.
