## About
The Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair 2026 is a dynamic fair designed to bring together researchers, practitioners, students, athletes, and community members to explore the latest advancements in sports science and physical activity.

Held at the UAE University – Sheikh Zayed Founder Hall , the fair provides a modern, interactive environment divided into four specialized stations, each offering a unique experience in research, innovation, technology, and community engagement.

This fair marks the first-ever sports-focused event hosted at UAEU, demonstrating the university’s commitment to advancing sports sciences and promoting a culture of physical activity on campus and in the community.

In organizing this event, we are committed to aligning with the UAE Sports Strategy 2031 and supporting national goals for developing sports excellence, innovation, community engagement, and active, healthy lifestyles.

Under the slogan:

“Sport and Science… For a Healthier Emirati Society”

the fair aims to highlight the vital role of physical activity in improving health, wellness, and overall quality of life.

## Objectives
The main objective of the Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair 2026 is to bring together experts and specialists in sports science and physical activity to discuss the latest innovations, exchange knowledge and experiences, and raise awareness of physical health.

Promote awareness of the importance of physical activity and physical fitness in enhancing overall health and individual well-being.

Encourage knowledge exchange on the latest innovations, technologies, and scientific research in sports science and physical activity.

Provide a collaborative platform for communication between researchers, practitioners, and stakeholders in the sports and health sectors.

Highlight challenges and opportunities within the sports and physical activity ecosystem, as well as incentives that foster increased community participation.

Develop strategic directions that promote regular physical activity across society and integrate active lifestyles into daily routines.

## Key Thematic Areas
The Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair is positioned to explore how existing research and practical knowledge can inform and enhance the field of sports science, physical activity, and health promotion. It aims to foster innovation, guide evidence-based practice, and create new insights that advance performance, well-being, and inclusivity in sport and physical activity. The fair also provides a platform to bridge the gap between academic research, professional practice, and community engagement in the field of sports and exercise sciences.

## Format and Activities
The two-day Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair is an ongoing event. Many national sports authorities and organizations will be invited to participate, providing a unique platform for knowledge exchange, collaboration, and community engagement.

Call for Abstracts:

Researchers are invited to submit abstracts for consideration. Accepted submissions will be showcased through poster presentations , highlighting the latest developments and innovations in sports science, exercise physiology, biomechanics, and related fields.

Panels and Workshops:

The fair will feature thematic panel discussions and interactive workshops covering topics such as coaching, performance optimization, injury prevention, sports technology, and talent development. These sessions provide participants with hands-on learning experiences and practical insights that can be applied in professional practice.

Community Engagement:

A key feature of the fair is its focus on community participation. Attendees will have opportunities to engage in sport games, competitions, and wellness initiatives , promoting health, inclusion, and accessibility in physical activity. The fair bridges the gap between research, professional practice, and community involvement.

Activities:

Research Poster Station

Interactive Workshop Station

Sport Technology Station

Community Engagement Station
