## General Terms and Conditions
This year’s theme: “ The Family” . Any submission that is not in accordance with the theme, will not be considered for this competition.

All UAEU communities, i.e., students, faculty and employees are eligible to participate in the competition.

Students’ teams may have a coach/advisor from the UAEU community considered a team member.

An individual can participate in a maximum of three categories, with only one entry allowed in each category.

Each participant, whether participating individually or as part of a team, shall be eligible to receive only one prize throughout the competition. If a participant qualifies for more than one prize, including by achieving high scores in multiple categories, the Committee shall award the participant only one prize, as determined by the Committee.

A competition category must receive at least three eligible submissions in order to proceed to the evaluation stage. If fewer than three eligible submissions are received, the Committee reserves the right to cancel the category and withhold the corresponding prize.

Submissions must not infringe upon any individual's personal or proprietary rights.

The submission should not have won another award either inside or outside the UAE for the same artwork.

Submissions from students must abide by UAEU policies and procedures such as the Student Code of Conduct and standards or expected behavior for students and student organizations at the university.

Applicants from UAEU faculty members, instructors, and staff must abide by UAEU’s policies and procedures from the Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct, which outlines the values and the ethical, professional, and legal standards that apply to administrative work, teaching, scholarship, and service of UAEU and the UAE community.

Any application that violates UAEU policies and procedures as outlined in the Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct will be rejected.

The Higher Award Committee reserves the right to disqualify a participant from any of the categories, if deemed appropriate by the Committee.

The decisions of the Evaluation Committee and the Higher Award committee shall be final .

The university reserves the right to use the winning entries as they see fit.

Applicants must submit their artist pieces in accordance with the requirements outlined in the award guide.
