## ECME Abstract Book 2025 > Proceedings of Emirati Conference on Medical Education 2025
Mohammed Al Houqani, Fouzia Shersad, Asma Syeda, Hadeel Aboueisha, Aysha Al Dhaheri, Alanoud Muhsen Al Thaibani, Alhanouf Khthail, Abdul Muqsid, Amal Al Shaiba, Ayah I. Eshbair, Fatema Almheiri, Wafa Alawlaqi, and Ibrahim Balla

The Emirati Conference on Medical Education, organized by the National Institute for Health Specialties (NIHS)—the accreditation body for postgraduate medical education in the UAE— is one of the country’s most prestigious scientific events. Held on February 15–16, 2025, at the Fairmont Bab Al Bahr Hotel in Abu Dhabi, the conference aimed to enhance the medical education experience and facilitate the exchange of expertise among professionals in the field. This dynamic platform brought together experts to discuss the latest advancements in medical education, with a focus on improving training and assessment in healthcare.
