## Course Reserves
The National Medical Library offers a large collection of medical course literature online. The e-books are accessible for all UAEU members, both on and off campus, and include helpful navigation and study tools. In addition, a printed Course Reserve collection is available in the library for short term lending of course books that exists in printed format only.

Both electronic and printed course materials available in the library can be searched and accessed/located online in the following database: http://library.uaeu.ac.ae/screens/course.html .

Please also see http://nml.uaeu.ac.ae/en/ebooks.shtml to read more about, and explore all electronic course books and related titles with the help the library’s discovery tool Summon or through any of the library’s e-book platforms.

For Faculty:

Faculty can place assigned course materials on reserve for their students. The printed Course Reserves is especially useful when many students need to access an item that is rare, difficult to obtain, only exists in printed format, or is expensive to purchase. Faculty can contact the library to place personal or library materials on Course Reserves or request for a title to be purchased. Requests are processed immediately, depending on availability of the items.

If you have any inquiries or want to place a request, for either an electronic or printed title, please contact Ms. Reem Al Sheryani – Technical Services Librarian ( reem.alsheryani@uaeu.ac.ae ).
