## Work Ethics and Professionalism
Title Workshop: Work Ethics and Professionalism

Date: Wednesday , 22 July 2026

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Prof. Naeema Al Hosani

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: The Work Ethics and Professionalism workshop aims to enhance participants’ understanding of workplace ethics and professional behavior. It equips them with the knowledge and practices needed to create a positive work environment, build trust, and maintain a strong professional image within and beyond the organization.

What will participants gain?

Participants will learn the fundamentals of work ethics and the importance of professional values such as integrity, responsibility, respect, and transparency. They will also explore how to demonstrate professionalism when interacting with colleagues and clients, and how to improve their communication and workplace conduct.

Expected impact:

The workshop is expected to strengthen participants’ professional awareness, improve workplace interactions, and support the development of a positive and sustainable professional reputation that reflects the organization’s values.
