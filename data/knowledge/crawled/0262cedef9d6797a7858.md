                                           Inter- and Intra-Institutional Transfer


I.                                                                                              Policy

                 The United Arab Emirates University shall establish standards governing
                 inter- and intra-institutional transfer to the University in conformity with the
                 policies of the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research and shall
                 implement procedures that effectively and efficiently achieve the end of
                 enrolling qualified students in the University.

                 1.                                                                                         Transfer from One College to Another

                                  Students may choose to transfer from one college to another only once.
                                  The transfer shall take place within the framework of the general
                                  admission policies of the United Arab Emirates University.

                                  Applications will be submitted to the Admission and Registration
                                  Department during the last two weeks of the semester.
                                  Recommendations will be made by a committee appointed annually
                                  for this purpose by the Vice Chancellor.

                 2.                                                                                         Transfer from Another Educational Institution

                                  a) Students may apply for transfer from another educational
                                           institution to the United Arab Emirates University.

                                           The transfer shall take place within the framework of the general
                                           admission policies of the United Arab Emirates University.

The conditions of eligibility for transfer shall be:

                                               •                 Students must have successfully completed at least two
                                                       semesters or one academic year in an accredited university.
                                                       His/her overall grade in that university should correspond
                                                       to a minimum of good (C).
                                               •                  Students must satisfy the admission requirements of the
                                                       University and the qualifying conditions for the college.
                                               •                       There are seats available within the quota assigned to the
                                                       college in the semester in question.
                                               •            The maximum number of credit hours that can be
                                                       transferred is sixty credit hours (eighty credit hours for the
                                                       College of Engineering, the College of Education [Bachelor
                                                       Degree in Education with Teaching License] and the
                                                       College of Information Technology).

                                       Nationals of the United Arab Emirates may be transferred to/from
                                       the College of Medicine and Health Sciences from within the
                                       University or from another accreditated university.  The transfer
                                       must satisfy the rules and criteria of the regulations of that college.

                                       Transfer will be granted only from educational institutions that
                                       require the students to attend classes on a campus.

                               b)            Transfer of all or some of the credit hours shall be granted upon
                                       the recommendation of the academic department.  A minimum
                                       grade of C will be the condition for the transfer of credits of any
                                       course.

                               c)              Credit hours completed in another educational institution may be
                                       transferred on a pass/fail basis only.


II.                                                                              Application

               This policy shall apply to all students seeking to transfer from one college to
               another within the United Arab Emirates University and shall apply to transfer
               from another educational institution to the UAE University.
