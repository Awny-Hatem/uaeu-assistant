## PRVT113 Introduction to Law
The course aims to introduce the student to two fundamental subjects in this field: Law and Rights, emphasizing their flexibility and ability to cope with ongoing social changes. In relation to the concept of Law, students study topics such as the nature, necessity, and purposes of law, in addition to the characteristics of legal rules, branches of law, sources of law, its applicability in terms of time and place, and interpretation. Concerning the concept of Rights, students study topics such as the nature, elements, and types of rights, with special emphasis on the concept of legal personality. Students are introduced to some of the new challenges facing these concepts, including the idea of a legal personality for AI and the rights related to digital ID and electronic dealing in personal data. This course aims to enable the student to grasp the basic legal concepts that qualify them to study various branches of law, to apply those concepts to the rapid development in their society, and to begin training in developing legal skills in oral and written expression, and to provide the student with the necessary foundations of values for legal practice.

## PRVT612 Advanced Studies in Insurance Contract
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_2961.shtml?id=PRVT612
This course includes studying the definition of insurance contract, identifying its characteristics, and distinguishing it from other named contracts. Its pillars are based on mutual consent and the subject matter that fundamentally involves risks. The course examines its effects by defining the obligations of the insured and the insurer. It also addresses the study of contractual models (insurance documents) in the insurance contract and how the insured is protected from arbitrary provisions therein as a consumer, especially since these models are unilaterally drafted by the insurer as the stronger party in the contractual relationship and the insured has no right to amend them. It explains and analyzes the levels of legal protection for the insured in terms of general contract theory and the judicial authorities concerning arbitrary contractual provisions in insurance contracts, considering the insured as a consumer and how to protect them according to this legal description, then in terms of decisions of the Higher Insurance Authority in Abu Dhabi and the extent to which the insured benefits from them. The course instructor selects a deep topic from these terms after covering an overview of all the course vocabulary in the first two weeks of the semester. For example, the instructor may choose the risk insured against, or discuss the terms of compensation in compulsory insurance, or address the topic of contingent rights in an insurance contract.
