## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Who Can Apply?
The National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) is open to talented UAE Nationals who are alumni of the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) and are passionate about pursuing careers in academic or applied research.

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Eligibility Criteria
Nationality : UAE Nationals only

University : UAEU graduates

Graduation Window : Must have graduated within the last 2 years

Minimum GPA : 3.0 on a 4.0 scale

English Proficiency :

TOEFL (PBT): 500 or higher, or

IELTS: 6.0 or higher

If you meet the above criteria and are motivated to explore research as a career, we encourage you to apply.

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Required Documents
A personal statement (300–500 words) explaining your interest in research and career aspirations

Your CV

Copy of Emirates ID and Passport

(If shortlisted) A letter of recommendation from a faculty member or academic advisor confirming your research interest and performance

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Application Timeline
Application Opens : 21 July 2025

Deadline to Apply : 15 August 2025

Program Duration : 1 September – 5 December 2025 (12 weeks, full-time)

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Important Note
This is a full-time, in-person program. Participants are expected to commit fully for the 12-week duration. Spaces are limited and admission is based on eligibility and alignment with the program goals.
