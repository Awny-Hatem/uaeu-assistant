Paper on Tolerance
    For Educational Staff and Mosque Imams to renounce Discrimination and Prejudice

The UAE's promotion of human rights without discrimination based on origin, sex, religion or
language, and renouncement of violence and hate reflects our traditions and values and stresses
on the principles Sheikh Zayed Bin Sultan Al Nahyan instilled in us, which we abide
by to be one of the pioneering countries in protecting human rights .
His Highness/ Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Al Nahyan – May Allah bless him, President of the UAE

The United Arab Emirates is a government that aims to build a decent society … a tolerant
environment ... cohesive families … educated generations… and equal economic opportunities
for everyone
His  Highness/ Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum –                May Allah bless him, Vice  President, Prime
Minister and Ruler of Dubai

Tolerance is one of the key values of the UAE society that is deeply rooted in our true Islamic
religion. It is, also, one of the essential values established by the constitution,                reflecting the
UAE's abidance by the international conventions and agreements which focus on tolerance,
coexistence, and renouncement of violence, extremism and hatred.
Based on the National Tolerance Program and the aspirations of our wise leadership for UAE to
be a role model in tolerance and renouncement of hatred,                contributing in spreading such high
values  in  all  countries,  as  stressed  by  His  Highness/  Sheikh  Mohammed  Bin  Rashid  Al
Maktoum, Vice President, Prime Minister and Ruler of Dubai – May Allah bless him.

I, the undersigned, being fully willing and able, undertake to abide by the following as long as I
work as (a faculty member/Staff) at public and private educational institutions:
• Abiding by teaching curricula and preparing students accordingly
• Respecting the UAE constitution and all applicable legislation and regulations in the UAE.
• Complying to the values and principles of tolerance, mercy and cooperation in dealing with all
elements of the educational system inside and outside the system, in order to build a coherent
society based on tolerance, peace and positive coexistence.
• Being governed in my behaviors and transactions with others by non-discrimination on the
basis of origin, country, religion or social position, aiming at instilling good values inside and
outside the educational system.
• Maintaining society's stability and coherence, and avoiding any saying, doing or act that may
threaten societal peace and safety.
• Ensuring that the content supporting curriculum does not contain any hint or reference to
discrimination, violence or hatred.

I, also, acknowledge that I received and read a copy of the Federal Decree-Law no. (2) of 2015
on Combating Discrimination and Hatred, and undertake not to commit any of the acts that are
punishable as per the said Law or that breach the provisions of this Document.
