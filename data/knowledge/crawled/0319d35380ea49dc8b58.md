Science Week 2019 Program
                                              April 22 – 24 2019


   The program daily starts at 9:30 am.
   Some activities will be concurrent.
   For the workshops Better to register in the website before the event.
   The activities in the program are divided into two main streams: Scheduled Program and
    Constant Program.
   Although most activities are repeated every day, 1st day (Scientist) will have some focus on
    the undergraduate programs, 2nd day (Pioneer) concentrate on the internships, careers &
    employments, and 3rd day (Scholar) is more about research & graduate studies.
