## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Camels
A herd of around twelve female and one male camel is permanently kept on Al Foah Experimental farm. These animals serve mainly educational purposes. The size of the herd may increase significantly during times when additional animals need to be kept for experiments. Feeding trials have been conducted in the past, as well as experiments addressing fertility aspects in camels.
