## SPED101 Education of Exceptional Children
This course provides broad knowledge and skills in special education for all UAEU candidates. It mainly covers models, theories, etiology, philosophies, legal provisions, ethical and professional commitment, assessment and identification procedures, and instructional strategies for students with exceptional learning needs. It also provides knowledge on the different characteristics of learners with special needs and their educational implications. This course stresses differentiating instructions to meet the educational and behavioral needs of individuals with exceptional learning needs.
Education of Exceptional Children (SPED101) > Credit Hours : 3

Education of Exceptional Children (SPED101) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Identify the basic principles, theories, and legal provisions of special education.

Examine collaboration skills with all concerned stakeholders.

Classify characteristics of individuals with exceptional needs.

Discuss the impact of cultural diversities on students with exceptionalities’ approaches to learning and preferences.

Differentiate instruction to meet students with exceptionalities’ educational and behavioral needs

Explain the development of educational and quality-of-life potential for students with exceptionalities.

## SPED211 Technology Applications in Special Education
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_2969.shtml?id=SPED211
This course provides a comprehensive understanding of assistive technology's role in special education. As technology evolves, special education teachers must stay informed about innovations and best practices. The course structure includes lectures and lab sessions. Lectures cover theoretical knowledge, legislative frameworks, and best practices in assistive technology, addressing topics such as the history, evolution, and ethical considerations of assistive technology. Lab sessions offer hands-on experience with assistive technology tools, including hardware and software, through practical exercises, simulations, and collaborative projects. Including a hands-on lab component is to helps novice special educators bridge theory with real-world practice. It gives them the chance to work directly with assistive tools, build confidence, and develop the skills needed to support students with disabilities effectively. This hands-on experience ensures they’re not just learning about assistive technology but can apply it in meaningful ways to improve the outcomes of their future students. Students are expected to engage actively in both lectures and labs, complete readings, and participate in activities. Assignments will involve creating assistive technology plans, adapting educational materials, and presenting case studies. Assessments include quizzes, midterm and final exams, and practical lab assessments. By the end of this course, students will effectively utilize assistive technology to support students with disabilities.

## SPED222 Language & Communication Disorders
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_2969.shtml?id=SPED222
This course explores the etiology (causes), epidemiology, assessment, and educational implications of speech and language disorders. Designed as a core component for special education students, the course addresses a wide spectrum of communication challenges and related areas such as oral motor function. Students will learn to identify and assess various speech and language disorders, which can range from simple sound substitutions to more severe issues like the inability to understand or use language or the oral-motor mechanism for functional speech. The course will also explore various causes of these disorders, including hearing loss, neurological conditions, brain injuries, intellectual disabilities, physical impairments like cleft lip or palate, and vocal abuse or misuse. Practical lab sessions will provide hands-on experience in evaluating and addressing these disorders, equipping students with essential skills for their future careers in special education. This course includes a lab component to provide hands-on experience in assessing and managing language and communication disorders. Through case studies, real-world observations, and the use of diagnostic tools, students will develop practical skills essential for identifying, evaluating, and implementing interventions for individuals with speech and language challenges.
Language & Communication Disorders (SPED222) > Credit Hours : 3

Language & Communication Disorders (SPED222) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Examine the normal aspects of communication development

Define the various categories of communication disorders

Identify characteristics, some etiologies, and concomitants of communication disorders

Implement appropriate assessment instruments to diagnose individuals with communicative disorders and design treatment plans

Develop appreciation and respect for cultural differences and the influence of such differences upon the evaluation and treatment of all individuals with communicative disorders.

Discuss issues of appreciation and respect for cultural differences for all individuals with communicative disorders
