9. Discontinuation of   Graduate Programs

Overview
Details the quality assurance framework that regulates the discontinuation                                                                                                             of                Graduate
Programs.

Scope
Applies to all College                                       s and Departments of the UAEU.

Objective
Changing circumstances may dictate that a College discontinues a graduate program either for
operational or academic reasons. The                 Universit y    has identified key elements that ensure that
this process is carried out with due notice and without affecting the enrollment     or rate of
progress        of currently enrolled students. These elements constitute a quality assurance
framework that prescribes the minimum requirements of proposals to discontinue                             graduate
programs and the processes through which they are approved.

Policy
1.                      Changes in the needs of society and the interests of students require that the University
      has policies not only for the establishment of new graduate programs, but for
      discontinuation of programs that are no longer responsive to societal needs and student
      interests.  These policies ensure the wise stewardship of the University’s resources.
2.                               The College of Graduate Studies has the primary responsibility for coordinating dis-
      continuation of existing graduate programs.  In this connection, the University must
      ensure adequate notice to students and facult y.
