## Program Overview > Program Overview
Our MBA program is currently offered in three locations: Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, and Dubai (starting from Fall 2014). Our Al Ain program has expanded our outreach and services to the Al Ain area. The City of Al Ain has grown considerably in the past few years and we intend to serve the growing number of professionals who choose to live here. We are also excited to start our MBA branch in Dubai, offered in partnership with Etisalat Academy, which extends our presence to serve the Dubai and Northern Emirates area. In the meantime, our Abu Dhabi location continues to thrive with each year attracting more students who join the program.

All of our programs are now accredited at both levels--graduate and undergraduate by the AACSB International . While we express our pride to be the first business school in the UAE with full accreditation of AACSB at the graduate level, we are also continuously striving to improve our curriculum and delivery system. To enhance our offerings we allow our students to select content ‘tracks’ as part of our elective course system. The tracks can now be found for Human Resource Management and Development, E-Business, Organization Excellence, and Finance. We plan to test more specializations in the near future.

We are also anxious to stay in touch with our many graduates of the program. We are extremely proud of our graduates and consider them to be the best in the nation. We always have time to listen to their input and to gain their assistance in strengthening our program through having them as guest speakers, participating in classroom appearances, hosting MBA events, and by serving as members of our graduate alumni association.
