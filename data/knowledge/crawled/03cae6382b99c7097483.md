Developing and Enhancing Leadership Potential in Higher Education

March 9-11, 2015

United Arab Emirates University

Center for Public Policy & Leadership

For over 30 years the Centre for Higher Education Research and Development ( CHERD ) has been involved in the delivery of professional development programs for higher education administrators, both in Canada and around the world. In March 2015 CHERD, in partnership with the United Arab Emirates University, Center for Public Policy & Leadership, will be offering a three day professional development workshop targeted to Heads and Chairs in higher education institutions.

Facilitated by a team of three experienced higher education administrators, you will have an opportunity to explore the following topics through a range of activities including presentations, case studies, simulations and discussion groups:

Global and regional trends in higher education

University governance

Teaching, learning and engagement in higher education

Definitions and models of leadership

Emotional Intelligence

Expectations of academic leadership

Self awareness and choosing your appropriate leadership style

Integrated planning

Setting department goals and objectives

Developing and monitoring KPIs

Building a positive department environment and culture

Sustaining internal quality control

Participants can also sign up for 15 minute private consultation with one of the facilitators. These consultations are held at the end of daily sessions
