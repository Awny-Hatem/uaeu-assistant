## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > General Enquires
The Al Foah Research Station belongs to UAEU’s College of Agriculture and Veterinary Medicine. It is located North of Al Ain and comprises of around 30 ha of agricultural land. It provides state of the art research facilities, such as greenhouses, shadehouses, open field sites, animal stables and laboratories.

The Al Foah Research Station is home to ARIFSID’s greenhouse research, insect and mushroom laboratories, as well as the poultry breeding unit.
