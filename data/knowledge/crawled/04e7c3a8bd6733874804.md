## December > Cancellation and Postponements
Requests for postponing or cancelling your test must be made at least 5 weeks before the test date, and the following fees will be applicable.

Postponement fee: AED 400

Cancellation fee: AED 400

You will lose your full test fee if requests are made within 5 weeks of the exam date, or if you are absent on the test without giving prior notice. If you miss the exam for medical/ personal reasons, you need to submit appropriate evidence within 2 days of the test date. If approved, you will receive 750 AED refund or we will give you a new test date. Only following cases will be considered:

Serious illness- e.g. hospital admission or serious injury (does not include minor illness such as a mild cold).

Loss or bereavement death of a close family member.

Hardship/ trauma victim of crime, victim of a traffic accident.
