## Request an Appointment
Students may schedule an initial appointment by visiting the Student Counseling Center and filling out a brief 'Request for Service' form. Students may also call our offices to complete the form over the phone:

Female: +971 3 7136686

Male: +971 3 7540048

Students are notified of their scheduled appointment within 24 hours, however, there are limited daily walk-in openings should a student need to be seen right away.
