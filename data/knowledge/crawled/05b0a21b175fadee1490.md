                                                 Reporting Accidents On and Off Campus


I.                                                                                               Policy

                     In case of auto accidents, the police should be called to the scene to complete
                     an accident report.  In the case of other accidents, medical help should be
                     obtained.

                     All accidents must also be reported to the Office of the Secretary General.
                     Forms and procedures for reporting accidents are available in the General
                     Services Department.


II.                                                                             Application

                     This policy shall apply to all employees of the United Arab Emirates
                     University.
