## HE Saeed Rashed Al-Yateem > Assistant Undersecretary for Resources and Budget, UAE Ministry of Finance
Saeed Al-Yateem is the Assistant Undersecretary for Resources and Budget at the Ministry of Finance. He is also a board member in the Arab Fund for Economic and Social Development, Etihad Rail, UAE Council of the Fourth Industrial Revolution, UAEU University Council, Emirates Council for Governmental Services and Federal Tax Authority.

Al-Yateem was the Director of the Budget Department at the Ministry of Finance (2002-2009) and also worked as the Deputy Director of the Investments Department in the Ministry in 2002. In 2001 Al-Yateem worked as a Senior Administrative in the Administration of Investments at the Ministry of Finance and the General Director Office Manager of Dubai Customs Department (1998-2001). He was also a Researcher in the Research and Studies Department at the Dubai Customs Department (1996-1998).

Al-Yateem holds a Bachelors Degree in Political Sciences from the United Arab Emirates University (1995).
