Chapter (1): General Principles

Overview
UAEU is committed to developing and maintaining a positive, robust and holistic OHS culture and
strives to ensure that this culture becomes part of our day to day activities. UAEU is committed to
ensuring that applicable legal requirements are met and that  a program of continual improvement is
in place.

Scope
This policy applies to all employees, students, contractors and visitors in the University campuses.

Objectives
The University aims to maintain a responsible place to work study and live for all employees,
students, contractors and visitors and to ensure continual improvement of occupational health and
safety management system.

Article (2):                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    Management Commitment
UAEU affirms its commitment to the following:

1.                                  UAEU executives and senior management shall be actively involved and commit sufficient
        resources (financial and otherwise) to ensure superior OHS performance. They will ensure that
        OHS responsibilities are defined for role-holders and take overall responsibility and
        accountability for the provision of safe and healthy workplace and work practices.

2.                                                                   Deans shall ensure that all activities are carried out in a safe and responsible manner. In any event
        of a conflict with high risk activities, safety shall ALWAYS be a priority.

3.                                                         Heads of Departments shall work in coordination with the dean to ensure OHS requirements are
        fully met

4.                                                All employees, students, contractors and visitors have a responsibility for their own health and
        safety and must ensure that their actions do not adversely affect others.

5.                                                           Co-operation, participation and support is critical if the aims and objectives of this OHS policy

6.                                                 Facilities Management Department (FMD) is responsible for ensuring all campuses, equipment
        and facilities are maintained to the highest OHS standards.

7.                                                Facilities Management Department Health and Safety (FMDHS) shall ensure that occupational
        health and safety requirements, relevant documentation are implemented, monitored and
        reviewed.



Article (3):                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    Arrangements -Occupational Health and Safety Management
In order to achieve this, UAEU will:

1.                                                           Provide and maintain safe and healthy work environment.

2.                                                           Develop an OHS management framework based on UAEU’s procedures.

3.                                          Identify hazards to assess the risks and the management and control of these risks as “low as
        reasonably practicable”.

4.                                                           Prevent work-                        related injury and ill-                                                    health.

5.                                                                              Provide appropriate information, training, supervision and instruction where appropriate to enable
        employees, students, contractors and visitors to perform their activities safely and effectively.

6.                                                           Comply with relevant local and international OHS legislation and recognized good practices.
