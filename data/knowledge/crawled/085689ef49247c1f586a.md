industry, government and the wider community, each story reflects our commitment to creating positive and lasting impact.
Collectively, these achievements demonstrate the important role the College continues to play in supporting national priorities

4.2 CBE Faculty Partners with Bank of China (Dubai) to
Pioneer a Contextualised ESG Rating Framework

   "Research that bridges academia and industry to support sustainable finance and the UAE's Net Zero
   2050 vision."


The College of Business and Economics (CBE) has successfully completed its second collaborative research project with Bank
of China (Dubai), led by Dr. Ahmed Elsayed and Prof. Kathy Ning Shen. The project, titled Decoding ESG Rating Divergences:
Evidence from the Bank of China – Call for a Holistic ESG Ratings Framework in the UAE, addresses one of the most important
challenges in sustainable finance: the inconsistency of Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) ratings across different
providers.

The research critically examined existing ESG rating methodologies and proposed a context-sensitive framework tailored to the
UAE. Through an in-depth case study, the project demonstrated how organisations can use ESG assessments to strengthen
investment decisions, stakeholder engagement and continuous improvement while supporting the UAE's Net Zero 2050
agenda and broader economic transformation.

This collaboration reflects CBE's commitment to impactful research that bridges academia and industry. By providing practical
recommendations for businesses, regulators, financial institutions and policymakers, the project contributes to the
advancement of sustainable finance and reinforces the College's growing reputation for producing research with real-world
impact.

4.3 Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research
(ECER)

The Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research (ECER) continues to strengthen the College of Business and Economics'
research ecosystem by producing high-quality research, supporting entrepreneurship education and building partnerships with
industry and government. Led by Prof. Maurizio Sobrero, with Dr. Ishara Maharaj and Dr. Paul Savage, the Centre is emerging
as a national platform for entrepreneurship research, innovation and policy development.

    The UAE's Largest Student Entrepreneurship Dataset

Since Spring 2024, ECER has conducted a longitudinal study of students' entrepreneurial attitudes, behaviours and intentions
through the GEIE222 Fundamentals of Innovation & Entrepreneurship course. The project has expanded to include
entrepreneurship courses at the American University of Sharjah, creating a dataset of 3,358 student responses by Spring
2026. The first paper from this project, co-authored by Prof. Maurizio Sobrero, Dr. Ishara Maharaj and Dr. Paul Savage, has
been accepted for presentation at the Academy of Management Annual Meeting, Philadelphia (2026).

4.3 Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research
(ECER)

    Industry Report: Mapping the UAE's Women's Entrepreneurship Ecosystem

As part of her research on women's entrepreneurship, Dr. Ishara Maharaj, together with CBE graduate Ms. Mira Razzouk,
conducted an ecosystem-level mapping of the support available to women entrepreneurs in the UAE. Their findings
demonstrate the positive direction in which women's entrepreneurship has moved over the past decade, bolstered by a
supportive national policy framework. The study also found that growth-stage finance and consistent mentorship remain
challenging as women-owned enterprises grow. From a data-availability perspective, the project revealed a lack of coordination
and standardised reporting across ecosystem actors. The report proposes a simplified reporting framework to stimulate
further discussion among institutional actors, strengthening ECER's convening role in the ecosystem.

4.4 UAEU Research Team Delivers Strategic Seminar to
Dubai Government Human Resources Department

A UAEU research team delivered an exclusive seminar to the Dubai Government Human Resources Department (DGHR) on 13
January 2026, presenting the Humachine Capability for Digital Sustainability framework. The project explored how
organisations can effectively integrate artificial intelligence with human capabilities to support sustainable digital
transformation.

The seminar showcased the outcomes of a joint research project conducted by Prof. Kathy Ning Shen, Prof. Omar Hujran and
Dr. Ananth Chiravuri. The proposed framework provides practical guidance for government organisations seeking to strengthen
workforce capability while accelerating AI adoption.

The seminar was well received by DGHR leadership, highlighting the practical relevance of the research and demonstrating
CBE's growing contribution to public-sector innovation and evidence-based policymaking in the UAE.

4.6 Competitive UAEU Program for Advanced Research
(UPAR) Grant on Energy Transition and Sustainable
Growth

Dr. Zakaria Boulanouar secured a competitive UAEU Program for Advanced Research (UPAR) grant for a two-year,
internationally collaborative research project on energy transition and sustainable growth. Led from the CBE and the National
Water and Energy Center, the project examines how energy-transition investments in areas such as infrastructure, electrified
transport, carbon capture and related transition technologies may help economies pursue growth while reducing environmental
degradation. Bringing together collaborators from the National University of Singapore, Sultan Qaboos University and EM
Normandie Business School/Swansea University, the project is positioned to generate rigorous and policy-relevant evidence
for countries seeking to align development with climate and sustainability goals.

    Hackathon Participation by CBE Undergraduate Students

Four CBE undergraduate students participated in the "AI 4 Industry Hackathon", which took place on 27–29 October 2025 at
the Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology headquarters in Dubai.

The students developed a novel analytics framework, titled "Back2Boost: Predicting and Boosting Back-to-School Sales", to
analyse masked real-world sales data provided by Nestlé UAE. They competed in "Track 2: Commercial Strategy & Analytics,
Challenge 3: Consumer & Product Innovation Mining", with the analysis focusing on the children's sub-segment of cereals. The
team set out to: (1) understand sales trends and seasonal patterns in the children's segment; (2) identify which products have
high versus low demand; (3) predict key sales drivers (price and promotion); (4) improve prediction accuracy and ensure model
reliability; (5) analyse and visualise the data; and (6) communicate insights clearly.

6.3 Industry Talk: Why Banks Fail at Fintech – A
Machine–Platform–Crowd Perspective

The College of Business and Economics was pleased to host Mr.
Richard Hills, Head of the UAE Office at K2 Integrity, as a guest
speaker in the MBA course Digital Transformation
Management.

In his talk, titled "Why Banks Fail at Fintech: A Machine–
Platform–Crowd Perspective", Mr. Hills examined the structural
and strategic challenges facing traditional banks in the fintech
era. He emphasised that fintech failures are often driven not by
technology limitations, but by legacy systems, siloed governance
structures and outdated operating models.

The session addressed key themes including artificial
intelligence, open banking, platform ecosystems and the UAE's
national digital strategy as a global benchmark for innovation.
Hosted by Prof. Fauzia Jabeen, the discussion provided MBA
students with practical industry insights and reinforced the
College's commitment to bridging academic learning with real-
world digital transformation challenges.

6.7 AutoDunn: Novel Statistics Software by CBE
Faculty

CBE faculty member Dr. Gurdal Ertek, in joint research with colleagues from Australia, the UAE and Türkiye, has developed a
novel statistics software package. The software, titled "AutoDunn", conducts automated statistical testing of group means, as
well as posterior analysis and visualisation. AutoDunn provides an easy way to conduct non-parametric comparison of means
automatically, saving analysts a considerable amount of time. Furthermore, the code is open source, enabling transparency and
reproducibility. Most importantly, AutoDunn visualises the results of all statistical analyses using the most effective
visualisation techniques, including network-based visualisation of statistical dominances.
