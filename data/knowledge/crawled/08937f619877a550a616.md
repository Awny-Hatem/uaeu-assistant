                                                                              Emergency Evacuation


I.                                                                                               Policy

                    The United Arab Emirates University believes it is important to protect the
                    lives of students, faculty, staff and visitors.  Part of this responsibility lies in
                    being prepared should an emergency evacuation of part or all of the
                    University be necessary.  The Secretary General will develop and publicize
                    emergency evacuation procedures under various scenarios.


II.                                                                             Application

                    This policy shall apply to all faculty, staff and students of the United Arab
                    Emirates University.
