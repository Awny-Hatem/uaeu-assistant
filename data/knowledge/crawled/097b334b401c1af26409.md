## Teaching Assistant Appointment Requirements
UAE national.

Bachelor’s degree from an accredited university.

Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.3 out of 4.0.

Good conduct and moral standing.

Successful completion of required tests and interviews.

Minimum IELTS score of 6.0 or equivalent TOEFL iBT score (60–78).

For male applicants: proof of completion or exemption from national service.

Any additional conditions stipulated by university regulations.
