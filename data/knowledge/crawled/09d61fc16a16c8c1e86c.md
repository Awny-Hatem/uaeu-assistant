## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Community Service
In line with its educational mandate, Al Foah Experimental Farm can offer training courses to groups and individuals outside of UAEU upon demand. Inquiries about training opportunities must be made through the Continuing Education Center

Though the farm is not always freely accessible to the public, we like to open our doors to allow school children and other interested groups of people from outside UAEU to get in touch with agricultural research.
