## Events and Colloquium > Aims
Provide updates on the state of the art in different research fields

Encourage the dialogue across research groups, disciplines, institutions, and with industry partners

Coordinate research activities to avoid duplication

Identification of future collaboration partners and opportunities

Brainstorming and joint discussion of difficulties and open research questions

## Events and Colloquium > Contributions
The Colloquium sessions usually have a duration of 40 to 60 minutes. They feature an oral presentation of 20 – 30 minutes, followed by a discussion of approximately the same length. Possible contributions involve the following:

Reports on the progress and recent outcomes of research activities

Presentations of potential or actual collaboration partners from the public or private food and agricultural sector

Discussion and development of ideas for future research projects or experiments

Senior Project / Master / Ph. D. students presenting their work

Reports from conference visits, field trips, or project meetings

Reviews of the contents of recent scientific articles or books

Presentation of novel research methods or research equipment

Presentation of innovative strategies for academic teaching

The colloquium does not feature presentations serving commercial purposes, such as the advertisement for products or brands.

For further information, please contact ARIFSID@uaeu.ac.ae.
