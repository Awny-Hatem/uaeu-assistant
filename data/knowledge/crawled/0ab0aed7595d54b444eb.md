## Sports Activities Unit > Sports Facilities
The United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) is replete with many sports facilities that are equipped at the highest level in all sports to serve the development and aspirations of the desired sports excellence of the United Arab Emirates.

Facilities include:

Outdoor Courts (Olympic Swimming Pool - Fitness Hall - CrossFit Hall - Ground Tennis - Beach Volleyball)

Indoor Courts (Futsal - Basket - Volleyball - Badminton - Table tennis)

Sports facilities are open to all UAEU students , faculty and staff as follows:

Monday – Thursday from 08:30 AM – 08:30 PM, Friday from 0 7.30 AM – 12:00 PM
