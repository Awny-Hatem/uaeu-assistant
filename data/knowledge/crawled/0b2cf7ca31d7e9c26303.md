## Department of Tourism and Heritage > Overview
The Department of Tourism and Heritage combines two exciting fields with major creative potential. The Tourism industry encompasses a vast range of opportunities, from cultural institutions to travel and hospitality. Heritage studies involves not only history and archaeology as theoretical disciplines, but also examines the relevance of these fields to dynamic, real-life situations. Both fields of study stimulate the development of practical, adaptive skills, and equip students with a wide and flexible global insight.

In addition to two minors (in Tourism and in Cultural Resource Management), the Department offers a Bachelor of Art program in Tourism Studies. It aims to provide a nationally and internationally recognized program of excellence in teaching, research, and service in leisure, specifically in the areas of Tourism, Cultural Heritage, and Tourism Planning and Management. UAEU'S Tourism discipline aims to educate, train and assist students, individuals, businesses, and other stakeholders to take full advantage of the opportunities available through the use of responsible tourism development. The program philosophy is driven by the belief that Tourism can be a powerful driver for economic development in emerging, transitioning and developed economies, and can also fulfil a significant role in a community social-cultural development, congruent with the cultural norms and values of the multicultural populations of the UAE.

Research conducted by faculty members within the Department ranges from topics on hospitality and the travel industry to archival analysis and archaeological fieldwork.

## Department of Tourism and Heritage > Accreditation
The BA in Tourism Studies is accredited by the United Nations World Tourism Organization through its Tourism TedQual Certification System. The process is based on a series of evaluation criteria (universally applicable to any tourism education, training and research programme) that makes it possible to measure the effectiveness of the pedagogical system as well as to which extent are covered the needs of the tourism sector, civil society and the students in such programmes.

The UNWTO evaluated the BA in Tourism Studies positively in all six areas: employers, students, curriculum and pedagogical system, faculty, management, and the accomplishment of the Global Code of Ethics for Tourism.

## Department of Tourism and Heritage > Mission
Providing high-quality academic courses and innovative research in a vibrant department environment that prepares graduates through an interdisciplinary and bilingual liberal education to become independent, resourceful and enterprising citizens, well-prepared professionals, and leaders in government, business and civil society.
