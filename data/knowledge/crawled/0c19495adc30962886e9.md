## Admission Application for Bachelor's Program
This service is an online platform that enables prospective students to apply for Bachelor's programs at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU). This user-friendly service simplifies the application process and provides a seamless experience for applicants seeking to join our academic community.

Start Service

## Admission Application for Bachelor's Program > Procedure
Sign up / Log in to apply.uaeu.ac.ae portal

Start, fill, and submit a new application (pay the application fee upon submission)

Upload all required documents and forms , Keep an eye on emails received from UAEU for updates and admission decisions.

## Admission Application for Bachelor's Program > Conditions & Requirements
Admission to United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) is competitive and based on the academic skills and performance levels of the applicants, in addition to the instructional capacities of the academic programs at the university.

The eligibility requirements are based on the Admissions Policy approved and announced by UAEU annually, setting the guidelines for each prospective group.

For details: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/admission/undergraduate_admissions.shtml
