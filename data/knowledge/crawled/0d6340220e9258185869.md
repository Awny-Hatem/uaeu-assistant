## COFA760 Advance Scientific Writing
Students train their ability to write a text in English language that satisfies international standards for research publications and project proposals. The course deals with the structuring of text, maintenance of coherence and means by which the desired emphasis on the most relevant aspects can be achieved. Students further learn how to simplify texts, and to avoid unclear or non-scientific expressions.
Advance Scientific Writing (COFA760) > Credit Hours : 2

Advance Scientific Writing (COFA760) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Discuss the process scientific writing and its pattern of writing.

Analyze a research problem being addressed in a scientific paper.

Develop skills to write scientific paper based on the IMRAD (Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion)

Analyze critically and review scientific papers in terms of its importance, consistency and justifications.

Acquire knowledge on the ethics in scientific writing.

## FDSC265 Introduction to Food Microbiology
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_4972.shtml?id=FDSC265
Introduction to Food Microbiology (FDSC 265) explores the role of microorganisms in food systems, including their impact on food safety, spoilage, and preservation. The course provides an overview of the microbial ecology of food, focusing on factors affecting microbial growth and survival. Students will examine both beneficial and harmful microorganisms, including foodborne pathogens, and their mechanisms of action. Theoretical knowledge is complemented with hands-on laboratory sessions to develop fundamental microbiological techniques, such as culturing, isolation, enumeration, and identification of food-associated microorganisms. The course also emphasizes the applications of microbes in fermentation and biotechnology, equipping students with practical skills to assess and ensure food safety and quality. This foundational course is essential for understanding microbiological principles in the context of food science and serves as a stepping stone for advanced studies in food microbiology and related disciplines.
Introduction to Food Microbiology (FDSC265) > Credit Hours : 3

Introduction to Food Microbiology (FDSC265) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Identify the different types of microorganisms in food and describe their roles in food safety and spoilage.

Explain the factors influencing microbial growth in food and their implications for food preservation.

Apply fundamental microbiological techniques to isolate, identify, and quantify microorganisms in food samples.

Describe the major foodborne pathogens, their mechanisms, and prevention strategies.

Evaluate the role of beneficial microorganisms in food fermentation and biotechnology.
