## Programs and Certificates
The Continuing Education Center provides the community with training courses to increase the knowledge and skills of individuals and participants from the public and private sector allowing them to accomplish their job in an efficient and effective manner.

The courses, certificates and training diplomas are offered through the following six units:

Professional & Business Programs Unit:

Certificate in Human Resources Management

Certificate in Leadership iLead

Dynamic Decision Making CBP

Effective Planning and Scheduling

Certificate in Supervisory Skills

Certificate in Team Leadership

Social & Community Programs Unit:

Social Responsibility ISO 26000

Event Management

Etiquette and International Protocol

Family Counseling Diploma

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)

Science & Technology Programs Unit:

ICDL Training and Certifications

IC3 Training and Certifications

Social Media for All Training and Certifications

Strata Composites Training Program

Robotics Courses

Multimedia Courses

Scientific and Technical short courses

Arabic Language Programs Unit:

Al Ain test to measure proficiency in the Arabic Language for Arabic speakers

Modern Standard Arabic course for non-native speakers (beginners)

Modern Standard Arabic course for non-native speakers (Advanced and intermediate –levels)

Modern Standard Arabic course & Arabic culture

Preparation course for Al Ain Test

Foreign Languages Programs Unit:

IELTS Preparation Courses

General English & Conversation English Courses

Special English Courses for Women Only

Private One-On-One Tutorials (IELTS, TOEFL, General English, etc.)

English Presentation & Email Skills Courses

Other Language Courses: Japanese, Urdu, Korean, Spanish, etc.

TOEFL ITP® and iBT® Exam Registration

Tawazun English Language Training Program (TELTP)

English Language Testing Unit:

IELTS Exams

TOEFL Exams

Contractual Programs:

The Center is ready to create, develop and implement training programs and certificates for organizations to meet their specific needs.
