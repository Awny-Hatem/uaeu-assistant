## Initiatives
Students Employment in UAEU Campus

The Center assists in employing the students on a part-time basis to work at the different units of the Student Affairs Deanship. This contributes to their employability skills and professional development.

Call Center Service

The Student Call Center was established in August 2018 and is specialized in answering all inquiries in Arabic and English related to the student affairs sector and completing transactions that do not require personal attendance. Official working days where calls are received from 7:30 am to 7:00 pm.
