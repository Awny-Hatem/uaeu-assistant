## THE CHALLENGE
Humanity is currently facing some of its toughest challenges that threaten its own existence; infectious diseases, poverty, un-employment, inequality, sustainability, food and water security, among others. The COVID-19 pandemic has deepened many of those threats, and has changed the way we see our future.

## REIMAGING POSSIBILITIES
Paradigm-shifting innovations require that we abandon old ways of solving familiar problems, and look for inspiration in unfamiliar territories. To redefine current boundaries, and break thinking chains, the first iteration of the Pathfinders Program is designed around humanity’s yet most daring adventure of creating and sustaining a human colony on Mars. We can think of Mars as a fresh start for humanity, but we can also think of it as a place of new inspirations for solving some of Earth’s toughest challenges. The challenges facing us to achieve the momentous dream of building a human colony on the red planet are in many ways similar to the challenges facing us here on Earth.

## Application Process > Eligibility Criteria
Applicants must have good proficiency in English.

Applicants should be over 18 years old by October 1, 2021

Applicants can be recent high school graduates, enrolled

Students or recent graduates (within one year) from a UAE, Arab, African, or Southern Asian University.

Be able to commit on a full-time basis for the full /whole duration (October 1, 2021, to March 31, 2022) of the program and be on board with the intensive yet rewarding workload.

## Application Process > Program Cost
The Pathfinders Program is a full time six-month unique learning experience which starts in October 2021 and concludes in March 2022.

Selected students will pay only AED 5,000 as a subscription fee to the program.

All other costs (tuition fee, accommodation, and travel) will be covered for the participants except food. Light snacks will be provided during workdays while diners and weekend meals need to be self-arranged.

The collected subscription fees will be invested back as SEED funding to support the best-generated ideas from the program.

The seed funding will support the best three solutions of the program at 50%, 30%, and 20% respectively.

## The Program
The Pathfinders Program will be held at the UAEU Science & Innovation Park, Al Ain from March - August 2021. The program will use a blended delivery method of both online & face-to-face. The select participants, otherwise known as pathfinders will go through an immersive experience to find their own passion and carve their own impact path. Pathfinders will be able to choose between four Impact Paths:

EXTEND LIFE

Re-imagining ways to Extend Life through technology integration in healthcare.

SUSTAIN LIFE

Conceiving innovative means to Sustain Life by ensuring food security for sustainable human-development.

ENHANCE LIFE

Envisioning strategies to Enhance Life through youth employment and economic growth.

EXPAND LIFE

Designing concepts to Expand Life through space exploration and artificial intelligence.
