 Deadline for Settlement of Tuition & Fees for Fall 2017:
                  1st Payment Deadline (50% of total tuition fees ) on, 20 Aug – 13 Aug  2017

 Tuition Payment Installment Plan
        To arrange a tuition payment installment plan, students must apply at the SAO before 18
         September 2017. If you were entitled to an installment plan in the previous semester you must
         apply again for the current semester

        Non-Payment of total tuition fees on or before 25 October 2017 will result in hold on
         registration and records. A hold means that you will not receive course grades until you pay
         your fees. It also means that you will not be able to register for courses in the next semester
         until payment is made. Unfortunately it may also mean forfeiture of grades and credits earned
         during the semester.

        (SPONSORED STUDENTS) Students whose tuition charges are paid or guaranteed by a
         sponsor must submit a valid official letter of financial agreement from the sponsor no later than
         28 September 2017, or risk having their registration for Fall 2017 cancelled.
