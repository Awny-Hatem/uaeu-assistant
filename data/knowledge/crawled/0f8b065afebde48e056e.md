## Why Choose MBSCEC?
The Center provides a range of comprehensive services at nominal fees for exceptional children. I ncluding diagnostic assessments, early intervention plans and Academic and educational programs, therapeutic services, counseling, and professional development for teachers, professionals working in schools, and parents.

The center includes a team of professional specialists with high expertise and skills, committed to developing innovative educational programs designed according to the needs of each child and based on evidence and scientific research .

The center places great emphasis on inclusion and diversity, working with all children regardless of their cultural or linguistic backgrounds, ensuring they receive the support they need to succeed.

The center is dedicated to conducting ongoing scientific research based on best practices to provide the latest educational methods, early intervention strategies, and exceptional therapeutic programs for children with special needs.

The center offers a variety of comprehensive services at symbolic fees for exceptional children.
