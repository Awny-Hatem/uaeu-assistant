## Self-Help and Resources
The Counseling and Wellbeing Service focuses on self-development and the importance of self-educational resources as supportive means for counseling. Many scientific aids and materials are available to students in the educational resources’ hall at the service, as well as brochures on the shelves, and in the waiting areas in the counseling offices.

Books and Scientific Materials: We have an important collection of books and various scientific materials that include academic, social, and psychological topics.

Brochures: The service publishes several educational brochures dealing with academic, psychological, and developmental topics.
