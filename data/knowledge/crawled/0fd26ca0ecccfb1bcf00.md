          Guide for Paying Fees for Bachelor, Master & DR Program for spring 2020\2021



 The Student Accounts Office (SAO), is responsible for the following of all student fees
and charges. Our working hours for student enquiries are 8am – 3pm, Sunday through
Thursday.

             o   Email address:  Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae





 Payment method:

to pay: https://ssb.uaeu.ac.ae/prod/bwykstpy.p_display_opt Or through UAEU Application.



                       Deadline for Settlement of Tuition & Fees for Spring 2021
   1st Payment Deadline (50% of total tuition fees )                       10 Jan – 4 Feb 2021
   2nd Payment Deadline (Remaining 50% of total tuition fees)              7 Feb – 24 March 2021
