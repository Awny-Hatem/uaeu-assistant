## Overview
The Institutional Research Unit (IRU) provides accurate and timely institutional data and analyses about the University to the internal units and external stakeholders in supports of their planning, assessment, and evidence-based decision-making needs. IRU accesses, collects, and analyzes institutional data, and disseminates information through dashboards, web applications, as well as ad hoc and periodic reports.
