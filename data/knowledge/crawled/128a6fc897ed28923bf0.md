## Overview
The Advising Center is committed to providing the best advising practices to each UAEU student by providing a holistic approach of support, encouragement and vital information regarding their curriculum, choice of major/minor, registration guidelines and academic activity engagement. The Advising Unit encourages and promotes university core policies and procedures that create a nurturing environment. We strive to help students realize their potential as outstanding leaders in their field, with the ultimate goal of supporting UAE national goals in education. The mission of the Academic Advising Office is to guide and support students during their academic journey to ensure they succeed in achieving their goals and career plans.

This is done through constant and consistent communication with each student, thereby forming a partnership with academic advisors to create and maintain a solid foundation of engaged learning, an inner drive for proactive participation, and a strong sense of personal responsibility.
