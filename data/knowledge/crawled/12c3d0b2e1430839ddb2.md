## Frequently Asked Questions > How do I apply for a program?
To apply, click on the "Apply Now" button located at the bottom of each program page. All applications are to be submitted online. To ensure a place on the program, participants are encouraged to submit their applications as early as possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions > Where are the classes conducted?
Open Enrolment Programs are held either at hotels or at UAEU facilities in Abu Dhabi, easily accessible.

In specific instances, courses will be hosted online via MS Teams.

## Frequently Asked Questions > What happens if I miss a class?
In case you miss a class due to illness or unavoidable organizational requirements, you may attend a subsequent class or intake, subject to the availability of seats. It is strongly recommended, however, that delegates complete the course they have registered for.

## Frequently Asked Questions > Is there accommodation arranged for participants from abroad?
Participants may choose to stay at hotels closest to the venue. The CBE will be able to provide recommendations in this regard.

## Frequently Asked Questions > Can credits be transferred to academic programs such as an MBA?
Currently credits cannot be transferred to any academic programs.

## Frequently Asked Questions > Can an open enrolment program be customized?
Yes, we can tailor any of our open enrolment programs to your organizational needs and objectives.

## Frequently Asked Questions > Payment, Discounts and Cancellation Policies
Full payment is due upon receipt of the invoice, prior to the program start date.

The registration form will contain the payment instructions. The CBE accepts payment by EFT.
