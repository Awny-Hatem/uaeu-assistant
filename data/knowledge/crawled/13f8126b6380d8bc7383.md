## Department of Physical Education > Overview
The Department of Physical Education at UAEU is deeply committed to shaping the next generation of highly skilled, influential health and physical education educators across all grade levels. Our program empowers students to become exceptional role models and advocates for healthy lifestyles, making a lasting impact on society. Through rigorous training, students master a wide range of movement skills and physical fitness techniques while also gaining the ability to analyze, synthesize, and apply scientific principles to health and physical education. By fostering the knowledge, skills, and professional values of our undergraduate students, we ensure they are fully prepared to lead and inspire future generations in health, fitness, and physical education.

## Department of Physical Education > Vision & Mission
Vision

The Physical Education Department at UAEU seeks an active leadership role in the development of health and physical activity at all levels in the UAE through the application of health and physical education theories and practices. In cooperation with the other University colleges, the Department aims to improve society's overall health.

Mission

The Physical Education Department seeks to contribute to the ongoing development of education in the field of health and physical education at all levels in the U.A.E. through effective partnership and collaboration with the Ministry of Education, Abu Dhabi Education Council, clubs, sports associations, the armed forces, and other State institutions. The Department works to achieve its mission through the preparation of specialists in health and physical education, conducting scientific research, participating in scientific activities and community services.

## Department of Physical Education > Facilities
Biomechanics Laboratory

Physical Fitness and Health Laboratory

University Sport Facilities:

Our Physical Education program offers hands-on learning experiences by utilizing a

comprehensive range of university sports facilities. These include:

Swimming Pools: A 50-meter Olympic indoor swimming pool is available on both the male

and female campus for lap swimming, training sessions and competitions.

Multipurpose Halls and Gyms: Dedicated spaces for both male and female students to

engage in various physical activities. Two indoor courts that can be used for playing

basketball, volleyball, handball, badminton or tennis in both organized sports and free

recreation.

Outdoor Sports Facilities: Extensive facilities located within the main campus, as well as

additional facilities outside the campus.

These top-notch facilities ensure that students receive practical training in a professional and wellequipped

environment, enhancing their learning experience and physical skills.
