Vice Chancellor Recruitment Policy
Overview
This policy aims to create a structured, transparent and fair framework for the employment of the Vice

Chancellor at the United Arab Emirates University. The Vice           Chancellor plays a pivotal role in shaping the
academic, administrative, strategic and future direction of the University. As the chief executive officer,
the Vice Chancellor is responsible for driving innovation, ensuring academic excellence, fostering a culture
of research and collaboration, and representing the University on national and global                                   platforms. A robust
and effective recruitment process ensures that the selected candidate has the vision, leadership skills and
commitment to advance the University's mission and strengthen its position as a leading institution in
higher education and research. The policy also aims to ensure that the recruitment process is in line with
the University's strategic objectives, reflects its commitment to diversity and inclusion, and commitment
to the UAE's Emiratization policy.
Scope
This policy applies to the recruitment, selection, and appointment process for the position of Vice
Chancellor at UAE University.
Objectives
This policy aims to ensure a rigorous, transparent, and merit-based recruitment process for the Vice
Chancellor of UAE University. By prioritizing excellence in leadership, the policy seeks to identify and
appoint a candidate with exceptional academic, administrative, and strategic capabilities who can drive

Article 1: Policy Principles
      1.                               Excellence in Leadership
             The recruitment process will aim to identify candidates with exceptional leadership skills, a proven
             track record in academic and administrative excellence, and the ability to drive the university
             toward achieving its vision and mission.
      2.                             Diversity and Equal Opportunity
             UAE University is committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive academic community. The
             recruitment process will ensure equal opportunities for all qualified candidates, regardless of
             gender, nationality, ethnicity, religion, or other characteristics.
      3.                             Candidate Job Package Setup
             UAE University prepares a comprehensive package for candidates and shares it with the selected
             candidates. This package will include detailed information about the role, vision and strategic
             objectives of the University, as well as responsibilities, qualifications and expectations for the
             position of Vice                  Chancellor. It also determines the rewards, benefits and conditions of

Article 2: Policy Guidelines
The recruitment of the Vice Chancellor shall follow a structured and transparent process, guided by the
following key principles:
      1.     Formation of the Recruitment Committee
             A Recruitment Committee shall be established by the University Board of Trustees, comprising
             representatives from the Board, university leadership, and external experts, to oversee the
             recruitment process.

      2.     Position Profile Development
             A comprehensive position profile, outlining the roles, qualifications, and expectations for the
             Vice Chancellor, shall be developed and approved before the recruitment process begins.

      3.     Advertising the Position
             The position shall be advertised across diverse platforms, including the university's official
             channels, international academic platforms, and Emirati talent networks, to ensure broad and
             inclusive outreach.

      4.     Candidate Evaluation and Assessment
             Applicants will be evaluated based on predetermined selection criteria, including academic and
             leadership credentials, cultural alignment, and strategic vision. The assessment process may
             include structured interviews, presentations and benchmarking tests.
      5.     Emiratization Priority
             Aligned with the Emiratization policy, qualified Emirati candidates who meet the required
             standards will be given priority consideration, with leadership development opportunities
             offered as needed.
      6.     Appointment Process
             The Recruitment Committee shall recommend a final candidate to the University Board of
             Trustees for approval, followed by endorsement by the appropriate governmental authority.


Article 3: Monitoring and Review
The Recruitment Committee shall document and review the recruitment process to ensure compliance
with this policy. Feedback from stakeholders shall be gathered to improve future recruitment processes.
