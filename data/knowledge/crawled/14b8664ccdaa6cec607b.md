  Instructions Regarding Registration:

     Students who did not register without providing an excuse to the college, will not be allowed to register in the add
      and drop week of the Spring 2014/2015.
     Student in good academic standing may register 12 to 19 credit hours.
     Students who are under Probation cannot register for more than 13 credit hours.
     Honors students and students who expect to graduate in Spring Semester 2014/2015 may register for up to 22 credit hours
      with college approval.
     Students who are failed in any of UFP course at the end of the Fall 2014/2015 will have to correct their registration.
     To learn how to register please view the link by clicking here
     If you do not know your password please contact the Helpdesk at Tel: 03 -7136111.
