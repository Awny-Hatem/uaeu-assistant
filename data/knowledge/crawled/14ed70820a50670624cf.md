## Associate of Applied Science in Health Care Management > Associate of Applied Science in Health Care Management
Degree Program

This program is designed to provide the student knowledge and expertise to perform effective healthcare management in the current market. Graduate of the programs can find positions in office management and admitting, work in hospitals, clinics, physician s offices, insurance companies, and long-term care facilities.

Program Outcomes:

Demonstrate knowledge of healthcare delivery systems.

Exhibit familiarity with the healthcare associated operational and financial aspects.

Describe regulations and laws that govern the healthcare industry.

Apply knowledge of elements that determine the policies to run healthcare facilities.

Demonstrate managerial and leadership qualities as required within the healthcare environment.
