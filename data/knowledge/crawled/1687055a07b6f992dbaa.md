## Overview
Welcome to the UAEU Alumni Community

Our Ambassadors in the Journey of Nation-Building

The alumni of United Arab Emirates University represent the true extension of our mission and serve as our ambassadors, embodying our values and vision across diverse fields of work, leadership, and community service. Since the graduation of our first cohort in 1981, comprising 472 students, the University has continued to prepare generations of talented professionals and leaders who have played an active role in serving society and supporting the nation’s development journey.

Today, we take pride in a vibrant alumni network of more than 77,000 alumni, many of whom hold influential leadership positions across government and private sector organizations within the UAE and beyond.

Lifelong Partnership and Meaningful Engagement

At UAE University, we believe that graduation marks the beginning of a lifelong journey of partnership and engagement. Through this platform, we strive to provide a dynamic and supportive environment that enables our alumni to remain connected with their alma mater and benefit from a wide range of exclusive services, programs, and professional and social opportunities. The platform also offers valuable avenues for networking, knowledge sharing, and specialized programs that support lifelong learning and continuous professional development.

Partners in the Future and Leadership

Our alumni are not merely a part of the University’s distinguished history; they are a cornerstone of our present and future, and enduring partners in shaping our journey of excellence, leadership, and innovation.
