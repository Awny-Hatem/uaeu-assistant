## UAEU Strategic Plan
The 2023–2026 Strategic Plan serves as a forward-looking roadmap guiding the development of the United Arab Emirates University in alignment with national priorities and global trends. It reflects the University’s commitment to excellence in education, research, and innovation through a clear set of strategic priorities, goals, and initiatives.

We invite you to explore this plan to learn more about the University’s goals, projects and the key steps being taken to shape a brighter and more impactful future.
