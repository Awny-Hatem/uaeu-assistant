## Program Calendar
The Executive Education Department at the College of Business and Economics (CBE) of the UAEU is committed to empowering the leaders of tomorrow. Our open enrolment and customised executive and management development programs are specially designed to help motivated individuals sharpen their competitive edge, enhancing their ability to lead local, regional and global dynamic markets. Corporations and executives will benefit from the robust networking and interactions with other talented leaders as well as the expertise of our world-class faculty. Our programs equip participants with the latest in management thinking and industry best practices.
