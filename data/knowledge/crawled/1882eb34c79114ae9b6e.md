## Online Library
Service Name:

Online Services

Service Description:

United Arab Emirates University currently houses two outstanding libraries that include the Main Library and the National Medical Library . The libraries are the gateway to finding a wide-range of resources and services put together to support the learning, teaching and research of the UAEU students, faculty, staff and the wider community.

The UAEU libraries provide a plethora of services including circulation, reference, document delivery, book purchase, information literacy and course reserve amongst others.

Service Procedure:

All library users can utilize our service offerings and browse resources. However, to download or access subscribed materials, users are required to authenticate their identity by entering UAEU account credentials . In addition, to borrow a book or other resources, users must present their university ID. External users can utilize the library resources by filling in the required form and paying a refundable one thousand Dirhams. For more information, please contact the Service Desk.

Target Audience:

UAEU students, faculty, staff and the wider community.

Required Documents:

University ID for borrowing and UAEU account credentials for downloading material, nothing for other activities.

Service Fees:

The library offers its services free of charge.

Service Duration:

On average users will be served within a few minutes.

Problem Resolution Time frame:

For online access problems (5 15 minutes)

For catalog and other resources (1 hour to 1 day)

Contact Us:

For information about our services, please email us at: libref@uaeu.ac.ae or call: 03-713-4671.
