## Field Experience Office > Meydan Form
Informative presentations have been delivered via online Meydan Forum for a wide-range of attendees from schools and other educational institutions. Topics have covered areas such as the Practical Experience Framework, Successful School Practices, Designing Digital Interactive Content, Current Educational Platforms used in UAE Schools, Entrepreneurial Teachers and Dealing with The Child's Psychology. These highlighted the role of teachers’ professional development in developing learners’ personal skills in parallel with academic skills. Topics were presented by knowledgeable educators from the field including members from the HE Ministry of Education, the Educational Innovation Program, school principals, academic professionals, and faculty members from the UAEU.

Agenda ( October 2020).
