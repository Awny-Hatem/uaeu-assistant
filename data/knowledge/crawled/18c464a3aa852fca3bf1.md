## Cooperative Relationships
United Arab Emirates University established cooperative relationships with a wide spectrum of partners from public and private sectors. The cooperative relationships take different forms such as:

Funded research project (e.g., Al Ain Municipality, ADNOC, Etimad Research and Development, Schlumberger Middle East SA, Rural Development Authority-South Korea, etc.);

Scholarships for students (e.g., Al Ain Zoo, TOTAL, etc.);

Training and professional development (e.g., Abu Dhabi Health Services Company, Dubai Health Authority, IBM, Cisco, Abu Dhabi Systems and Information Center, etc.);

Offering tailored academic services (Zayed Military College, Mubadala, edX, etc.) and

Offering internship and training for UAE Students.
