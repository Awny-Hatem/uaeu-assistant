## Research > Cultivation of the native Cleome brachycarpa for essential oil extraction
The objective of this experiment is to assess the effect of different water stress levels on the quantitative and qualitative yield of the essential oil,extracted from Cleome brachycarpa, an indigenous herb species, grown from the UAE wildlife seeds, under greenhouse conditions. In addition,the experiment aims to analyze and identify the essential oil constituents,recommending possible applications.

Ph.D. Student Ms. Suzan M. Shahin ( Suzan.Shahin@uaeu.ac.ae ) and Dr. Mohammed A. Salem Alyafei
