                            Student Use of University Property and Facilities


I. Policy

                In furtherance of its educational vision and mission, the United Arab Emirates
                University shall establish regulations governing aspects of student life on
                campus.

                Student Identification Card

                Students are required to present their student identification card when
                requested by a University official.  The card is University property and must
                be returned if students withdraw from the University, suspend registration, are
                dismissed or graduate.  There is a replacement fee for lost cards.

                Laptop Computer

                Certain students are required to purchase a laptop computer specified by the
                University and provided with a standard set of software by the University.

                Internet Access and E-Mail

                The UAE University provides access to the Internet and e-mail to all currently
                enrolled students.     Students are responsible for checking and reading their e-
                mail messages on a regular basis.  Students are required to observe the
                guidelines for use of these privileges.  Violators are subject to discipline.

Textbooks

             The UAE University provides textbooks and other learning materials to
             students at no cost.  They are required to return them to the University at any
             time upon demand or at the end of the semester or term.  Students are
             responsible for keeping them in good condition.  They may be required to pay
             to replace lost or damaged materials.

             Lockers

             The University provides lockers for student use.  The locker key must be
             returned when students withdraw from the university, suspend registration, are
             dismissed or graduate.  There is a charge if a set of keys is lost.

             Library

             Students may check out materials by presenting their student identification
             card.  Students are responsible for any materials they borrow.  All materials
             must be returned before semester grades are issued.  Students with outstanding
             fees or overdue materials will not receive grade reports.  There is  a charge to
             replace lost or damaged materials.  Library hours are posted on each campus.

             Food Service

             The University provides food service on class days at the campus and hostels,
             serving breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks.  With prior permission, students
             may bring food to the hostels.  All food must be eaten in designated areas and
             not in instructional areas.  Only the campus food service may sell food on
             campus, except with prior written approval.

             Bulletin Boards

             The University provides bulletin boards on each campus for the posting of
             informational notices.  Student organizations must submit notices to Student
             Activities for prior approval and stamping.  Notices may be displayed only on
             designated bulletin boards for a designated period of time.  Notices may not be
             posted on glass doors or building walls.  Unless approved by the college,
             student organizations may not post notices on college or department bulletin
             boards.  Student organizations violating this policy are subject to the loss of
             posting privileges.

             Display Areas for Student Projects

             Academic departments are responsible for designating appropriate display
             areas for student work.  Student projects may not be posted on notice boards,
             glass doors or building walls.
