## UAEU Libraries Editing Services & iThenticate Check
UAE University Libraries offer manuscript editing and IThenticate, similarity check software services.

Faculty members, graduate students and staff can use both services for free.

Manuscript editing: research papers, grant proposals and book chapters or reviews are edited for free for faculty, graduate students and staff only. Fill in and email form with manuscript in WORD format to: smajud@uaeu.ac.ae

UAEU Faculty members, graduate students and staff can send their manuscripts and dissertations in English or Arabic for iThenticate check. Just email the manuscript to smajud@uaeu.ac.ae to run it through the program. For further details, please contact: Shehab Majud, Coordinator of Editing Services, smajud@uaeu.ac.ae
