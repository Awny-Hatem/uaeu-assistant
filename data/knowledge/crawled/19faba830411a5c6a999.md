## Research Collaborations at UAEU
United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) is committed to advancing knowledge, innovation, and societal impact through strategic research collaborations. We partner with leading academic institutions, government entities, industry leaders, and international organizations to address global challenges and deliver transformative solutions.

At UAEU, collaboration is not just encouraged-it is central to our mission. By connecting world-class researchers with forward-thinking partners, we translate scientific discoveries into practical, sustainable outcomes that benefit society today and shape the future.

## International Research Collaboration Transformation (IRC-T) Projects
The International Research Collaboration Transformation (IRC-T) projects are strategic initiatives primarily funded by UAEU in partnership with leading international universities. These projects are designed to strengthen global research collaboration, enhance knowledge exchange, and drive high-impact scientific outcomes. The program also facilitates mobility and collaboration through:

Travel support for UAEU Principal Investigators (PIs) to visit partner universities

Support for international collaborators to visit UAEU

Joint research activities, publications, and capacity building

Current partnerships include:

Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

McGill University, Canada

University of Belgrade, Serbia

University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, USA

These collaborations aim to build long-term partnerships and position UAEU as a global hub for research excellence.

## Asian Universities Alliance (AUA) Joint Research Program
United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) supports joint research projects with member institutions of the Asian Universities Alliance (AUA), with a focus on areas of strategic importance to the UAE.

This program promotes collaborative research across the AUA network, fostering knowledge exchange and innovation. A portion of the funding may also be allocated to support research activities in fundamental science disciplines that contribute to the advancement of knowledge.
