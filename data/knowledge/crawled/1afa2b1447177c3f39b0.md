## Workshop: Dealing with Contemporary Societal Challenges
Title: Dealing with Contemporary Societal Challenges

Date: Thursday, 09 July 2026

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Mr. Younis Alhosani

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: This workshop explores the key societal challenges affecting individuals and organizations in light of digital transformation and changing patterns of life and work. It examines the impact of technology on behavior and productivity, highlights the evolving relationship between people and technology, and presents practical approaches for developing balanced responses that enhance well-being, improve performance, and strengthen adaptability.

Workshop Topics:

Contemporary societal challenges in the UAE and around the world.

The impact of digital transformation on behavior and productivity.

The relationship between humans and technology.

Building conscious responses that foster balance and productivity
