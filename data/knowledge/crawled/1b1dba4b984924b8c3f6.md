# Registration

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## Registration > College transfer By choice
Students are permitted to transfer to another college only once during their studies at the UAEU.

Students must not have completed more than 50% of the credit hours required for their current degree program.

Students in Good Academic standing may transfer among colleges according to the procedures and deadlines established by the University.

The transfer may be limited due to instructional capacity or specific regulations held by the college.

The student must have fulfilled the university admission requirements (Therefore, the registrar's office must check related requirements for the admission like EMSAT and other admission criteria).

## Registration > Transfer Conditions For national students & children of national mothers
The student must have completed, with a passing grade, at least 6 credits of transferable college-level courses, and achieved a cumulative GPA of 2.3 or higher.

The student must have completed one Math course, i.e. Calculus I (MATH105) or equivalent, with at least a grade of C or higher.

For those students who are transferring from colleges that cannot offer Calculus I (MATH105), There should be an equivalent such as EMSAT with 1100.

The student must have completed Algorithms and Problem Solving (CSBP 119) with a grade of C or higher.

The student must have completed at least two of the following courses with a grade of C or higher.

General Physics I (PHYS105)
Basic Biology I (BIOC100)
General Chemistry I (CHEM111)
Introduction to Academic English for Information Technology I (ESPU1081)

## Registration > Transfer Conditions for International students
The student must have completed, with a passing grade, at least 15 credits of transferable college-level courses, and achieved a cumulative GPA of 3.00 or higher.

The student must have completed one Math course, i.e. Calculus I (MATH105) or equivalent, with at least a grade of C or higher.

For those students who are transferring from colleges that cannot offer Calculus I (MATH105), There should be an equivalent such as EMSAT with 1100.

The student must have completed Algorithms and Problem Solving (CSBP 119) with a grade of C or higher.

The student must have completed at least two of the following courses with a grade of C or higher.

General Physics I (PHYS105)
Basic Biology I (BIOC100)
General Chemistry I (CHEM111)
Introduction to Academic English for Information Technology I (ESPU1081)

## Registration > College Transfer By Probation (complete 21 hours and GPA < 2.)
Students on academic probation may transfer among colleges once under limited circumstances according to the deadline established by University (calendar)

Students must fill out the probation form, then send it to the advising Unit.

CIT Advising Unit will communicate with other colleges to process student's requests for approval or disapproval

Please note that the requested college does the approval of transfer
