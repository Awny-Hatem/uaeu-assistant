## CBE Steps > Seminars and Talks on Executive Education and Postgraduate Studies
The UAEU College for Business and Economics (CBE) has developed a channel to inform its UAE business, academic and government stakeholders on issues relevant to the country and the region. This will take the form of a webinar series that will be hosted on a monthly basis.

The CBE STEPS Forum is one of the ways in which the CBE builds relationships with its stakeholders. It is also used as a forum where knowledge on the country and the region is shared. In addition to focusing on the UAE and the GCC, the geographic focus will include areas of interest and potential interest, such as Africa, South and Southeast Asia.

Guest speakers will include the CEOs of leading companies operating in the UAE and the region, opinion leaders from various industries, academics and other thought leaders. Their talks will be available on the website of the CBE, which will serve as a searchable repository of the videos, presentations and summaries from past STEPS events.

The session will be a short and focused presentation by the speaker(s), after which there will be time for questions and answers.

Topics, dates and venues are published on the website of the CBE, UAEU.

There are no costs involved.
