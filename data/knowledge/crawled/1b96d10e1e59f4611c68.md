## High Energy Physics > Overview
High Energy Physics (HEP) explores the fundamental particles and forces that govern our universe. The field has a rich history, with milestones such as the discovery of the Higgs boson, advances in quantum field theory, and groundbreaking experiments in particle accelerators. Today, CERN, the European Organization for Nuclear Research, stands at the forefront of this research. Home to the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), CERN conducts high-energy proton-proton collisions, unlocking new insights into the building blocks of the cosmos.
