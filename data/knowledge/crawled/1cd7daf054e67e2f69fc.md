Procedures of Policy No. (2) -  Undergraduate Admissions

1. Initial Admission to the University
     a)                  Initial admission to the University is to a particular College. However, a student may
           be required to complete the University Foundation Program before progressing to
           his/her intended degree program. Subsequent entry to the degree program is
           contingent upon:
           (i)                         Completion of all applicable Foundation Program requirements and proficiencies;
           (ii)            Acceptable student conduct and academic performance;
           (iii) Meeting all College requirements for the intended major; and
           (iv) The available instructional capacity in the College.
     b)        Students who have satisfied all Foundation Program requirements may proceed
           directly to the College.
     c)            Students are admitted to the University for a specific academic semester and are
           expected to register in that semester.
     d)                 Students who do not register for classes in the academic semester for which they have
           been admitted will have their admission cancelled.


2. Undergraduate Admissions by External Transfer
     External transfer students may apply for admission to UAEU within the framework of the
     general admission policies of the University.
     a)                  Students from the Higher Colleges of Technology (HCT) and Zayed University (ZU)
           who have met the requirements for enrollment in a degree program in those
           institutions may apply for transfer, contingent upon the following:
           (i)              The student must be in Good Academic Standing.
           (ii) The student must satisfy the minimum level of English proficiency, as

           (iii) There is sufficient instructional  capacity in the relevant College/program.
     b)             Students from other institutions of higher education within UAE or outside, that are

           (i)         Only those courses taken at another institution in which the final grade is C- or

           (iii) Credit hours transferred from instituti ons operating on an academic quarter system
                 will receive two (2) semester-hours of credit for each three (3) quarter-hours of
                 credit.

3. Application for Admission
     a)                    All prospective students must complete the ap plication that is specific to the requested
           college and submit the full official documentation required for admission; no person
           may be admitted to the University without submission of the complete official
           documentation.
     b)              Falsification of an application for admission and/or any required documentation will
           be considered major academic misconduct and the applicant will be denied admission.
           In the event that falsification is discovered after the student has been admitted and/or
           enrolled, the student will be dismissed from the University and will not be considered
           for readmission.
     c)        Provision of all required documentation in support of the application is the
           responsibility of the applicant. Failure to submit the application and required
           documentation by the applicable deadlines may result in the denial, cancellation, or
           deferral of admission.

     d)                  For applicants who apply through the Admission Office, the University will determine
           the validity, content, meaning, and equivalence of all academic credentials with
           respect to University admission policy requirements.
     e)          The University reserves the right to require supplemental documentation and/or
           independent verification of the applicant’s qualifications for admission.
     f)                  All applicants for admission at the University should specify on their application if

4. Readmission
     Students who withdraw or leave the University during the course of an academic semester
     (Fall/Spring and Summer Semester) or break their continuous enrollment in regular
     academic semesters may apply for readmission, contingent upon the following:
     a)                      The student should be in Good Academic Standing.
     b)                 The student should apply for readmission within three years from the beginning of the
           first academic semester of the break in registration.
     c)         The student should not be dismissed from the University due to academic or
           disciplinary reasons.
     d)         Readmission to the University is not guaranteed, and readmission is normally
           permitted only once.

5. Admission with Advanced Standing
       a)                    Entering undergraduate students may earn credit towards University degree courses or
              other degree requirements on the basis of examination scores on internationally
              recognized college entrance examinations and various other enhanced instructional
              programs in secondary schools such as the Advanced Placement (AP), the
              International Baccalaureate (IB) program, and others approved by the University.
       b)                 Specific course equivalencies, minimum examination scores, applicable credit hours,
              and eligibility requirements are decided by the Enrollment Deanship in consultation
              with the relevant Deans.


6. Student Identification Cards
       a)                Upon admission to UAEU, all students will receive an official Student Identification
              (ID) Card for use while an active student at UAEU.  Students are required to present
              their Student ID Card to secure various University services, for admission to final
              examinations, for campus access, and for other purposes, and students must present
              their Student ID Card to University officials upon request.
       b)           The Student ID Card is the property of UAEU, and a student must return his/her
              Student ID to the University upon graduation or any other cessation of active
              enrollment for whatever reason.
       c)         Lost or stolen Student ID cards must be reported promptly to the Enrollment
              Deanship. A replacement Student ID card will be obtained for a fee.
