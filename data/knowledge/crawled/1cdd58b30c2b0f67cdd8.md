## Research Data Services
Find the right service for your research and publishing.

Data Visualization

Get help producing the best visualizations for your data and your target audience. We even have a DGX-1 supercomputer for machine learning modeling!

Data Cleaning

Most large data sets have flaws that need to be fixed before they can be used properly. We can help with that!

Data Management Planning & Open Science

We can help you create better data management plans and meet funder requirements for your grants, as well as provide guidance regarding metadata, data sharing, discovery, preservation, reuse, and the FAIR standards (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reproducible).

Data Coaching & Statistical Consultations

Book a customized session to learn which options are the best for your data situation.

Digital Publishing

Want to publish digitally, green open access, or create your own journal? Come see us!

Data Hosting & Storage

Find a data repository: We recommend using a discipline-specific repository or one that is mandated by funding agencies or journals. Find research data repositories from various academic disciplines using:

re3data.org: Registry of Research Data Repositories
