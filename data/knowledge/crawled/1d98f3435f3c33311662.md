## MSc in Forensic Science > MSc in Forensic Science
About the Program

The MSc in Forensic Science (MSFS) at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) prepares graduates to apply advanced scientific principles to the investigation and resolution of crime.

The program combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on laboratory experience, supporting the UAE’s national vision for innovation, security, and justice.

Program Highlights

Duration: 2 years (full-time) | 3 years (part time) and Credit hours: 30, 21 Credit CHs taught and 9 CH thesis

Core disciplines: Forensic Toxicology, DNA Analysis, Criminalistics, Analytical Chemistry

Research-based thesis project

Collaboration with law-enforcement and research agencies
