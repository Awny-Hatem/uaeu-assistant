2. Undergraduate Admissions

Overview
Covers policies and procedures relating to:
1.   The admission of undergraduate students, including the application for admission, transfer
     admission, readmission, admission with advanced standing, and
2.   Student identification cards and campus access for admitted students.

Scope
Applies to all undergraduate students and all academic and related administrative units of the
UAEU, except those in the College of Medicine and Health Sciences.

Objective
Assures that the student admission function of the University
1.   Is conducted in a manner that is consistent with the academic principles, standards, and
     expectations  of  the  University  appropriate  to  its  educational  mission  and  academic
     programs; and that it
2.   Operates with fairness, consistency, integrity and effectiveness.

Policy
1.  The University sets all requirements and standards for admission to the University within
    the context of applicable general requirements established by the University Council.
2.  Priority  among  academically  qualified  applicants  will  be  given  to  Emirati                      citizens.
    Qualified international students will be considered for admission, subject to capacity and
    current policies of the UAE University.
3.  The University       issues and    announces the requirement for admission and documentation
    annually.
4.  All individuals seeking admission or readmission or transfer admission must submit a UAE
    University application for admission as described in the Admission Policy.
5.  Offers of admission made on an early or rolling basis are conditional, subject to verification
    of final documentation and eligibility.
6.  As the University’s capacity to accept students may be constrained by resources and other
    factors, applicants are not guaranteed an offer of admission, even if they meet the published
    requirements and minimum standards for admission.
7.  Admission  to  the University is selective and  competitive based                      upon the applicant’s
    credentials and the instructional capacity of the University.
8.  The University is entitled to declare the conditions, criteria, fees and required documents
    integral to international student admission.

Procedures of Policy No. (2) - Undergraduate Admissions

1. Initial Admission to the University
    a)   Initial admission to the University is to a particular College. However, a student may be
         required to complete the University Foundation Program before progressing to his/her
         intended degree program. Subsequent entry to the degree program is contingent upon:
         1) Completion of all applicable English Foundation Program requirements and proficiencies;
         2) Acceptable student conduct and academic performance;
         3) Meeting all College requirements for the intended major; and
         4) The available instructional capacity in the College.
    b)   Students who have satisfied all English Foundation Program requirements may proceed
         directly to the College.
    c)   Students are admitted to the University for a specific academic                       semester    and are
         expected to register in that semester.
    d)   Students who do not register for classes in the academic semester for which they have
         been admitted will have their admission cancelled.


2. Undergraduate Admissions by External Transfer
    Students may apply for admission to UAEU through external transfer within the
    framework of the general admission policies of the University.
    a)   Students from the Higher Colleges of Technology (HCT) and Zayed University (ZU)
         who have met the requirements for enrollment in a degree program in those institutions
         may apply for transfer, contingent upon the following:
         1) The student must be in Good Academic Standing.
         2) The student must satisfy the minimum level of English proficiency, as
            demonstrated by IELTS Academic Module or its equivalent, according to the
            approved Admission policy in each Academic year.
         3)  There is sufficient instructional capacity in the relevant College/program.
    b)   Students from other institutions of higher education within UAE or outside, that are approved
         by the UAE Ministry of Education, may apply for transfer contingent upon the following:
         1) The student must be in Good Academic Standing.
         2) The students must         have   completed      at least 12 credit hours or          have   completed
            successfully at least two regular academic semesters.
         3)  The student must satisfy the minimum level of English proficiency, as demonstrated
            by IELTS Academic Module or its equivalent. According to the approved Admission
            policy in each Academic year.

3. Application for Admission
    a)   All prospective students must complete the application that is specific to the requested college
         and submit the full official documentation required for admission; no person may be admitted
         to the University without submission of the complete official documentation.
    b)   Falsification of an application for admission and/or any required documentation will be
         considered major academic misconduct and the applicant will be denied admission. In
         the event that falsification is discovered after the student has been admitted and/or
         enrolled, the student will be dismissed from the University and will not be considered
         for readmission.
    c)   Provision  of  all  required  documentation  in  support  of  the  application  is  the
         responsibility  of  the  applicant.  Failure  to  submit  the  application  and  required
         documentation by the applicable deadlines may result in the denial, cancellation, or
         deferral of admission.
    d)   For applicants who apply through the Admission Office, the University will determine
         the validity, content, meaning, and equivalence of all academic credentials with respect
         to University admission policy requirements.
    e)   The  University  reserves  the  right  to  require  supplemental  documentation  and/or
         independent verification of the applicant’s qualifications for admission.
    f)   All applicants for admission at the University should specify on their application if they
         authorize the University to release their academic and/or directory information to third parties.

    (Fall/Spring  and  Summer  Semester)              or   break  their  continuous  enrollment  in  regular

6. Student Identification Cards
    a)   Upon admission to UAEU, all students will receive an official Student Identification
         (ID) Card for use while an active student at UAEU.  Students are required to present
         their Student ID Card to secure various University services, for admission to final
         examinations, for campus access, and for other purposes, and students must present their
         Student ID Card to University officials upon request.
    b)   The Student ID Card is the property of UAEU, and a student must return his/her Student
         ID to the University upon graduation or any other cessation of active enrollment for
         whatever reason.
    c)   Lost or stolen Student ID cards must be reported promptly to the                         Admission and
         Registration Deanship. A replacement Student ID card will be obtained for a fee.
    d)   Where Student ID cards are used for physical access to the University campus, access
         permission will be activated, deactivated, or otherwise modified according to changes
         in the student’s enrollment status.
    e)   Unauthorized possession or use of a Student ID Card is a violation of University
         regulations  and  may  subject  the  person(s)  involved  to  appropriate                    disciplinary
         procedures.
