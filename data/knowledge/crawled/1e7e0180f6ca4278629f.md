Overview

The Cardio-Metabolic Disorders Research Priority Group (CMD-RPG) brings together basic scientists, clinical
investigators, and translational researchers from the College of Medicine and Health Sciences and its affiliated
clinical partners to catalyse high-impact cardiometabolic research, improve patient outcomes, and elevate the
regional and international research profile of UAE University.

About the CMD-RPG


Background
The College of Medicine and Health Sciences has a longstanding tradition of collaborative research in cardiometabolic
medicine. Building on the legacy of the former Diabetes and Cardiovascular Research Priority Group, the CMD-RPG
was formally re-established in 2026 to provide a structured, sustainable platform for advancing multi-disciplinary
research across CMHS and its SEHA clinical partners in Al Ain.

Leadership
  •  Patron: Associate Dean for Research and Graduate Studies, CMHS
  •  Chair: to be appointed / elected
  •  Co-Chair: to be appointed / elected — alternates between CMHS and Adjunct / SEHA faculty
  •  Secretary: to be appointed / elected

Research subgroups
The Group encompasses eight focused subgroups, each led by two Co-Leads — one from CMHS Core Faculty and one
from Adjunct / SEHA Clinical Faculty:

1. MASLD (Metabolic-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease)
Scope: NASH, hepatic-fibrosis biomarkers, non-invasive diagnostics, steatosis phenotyping, liver–metabolic crosstalk.

2. Diabetes Mellitus
Scope: Type 1, Type 2, and gestational diabetes; DKA prevention; diabetes technology; continuous glucose monitoring;
insulin-delivery systems.

3. Endocrinology
Scope: Thyroid disorders, adrenal disease, pituitary conditions, calcium metabolism, rare endocrine conditions.

4. Obesity
Scope: GLP-1 receptor agonists, bariatric outcomes, metabolic phenotyping, lifestyle interventions, body-composition
analysis.

5. Renal / Nephrology
Scope: Diabetic kidney disease, CKD progression markers, cardiorenal syndrome, dialysis outcomes.

6. Cardiovascular
Scope: Premature ACS, heart failure, lipoprotein(a), hypertension management, vascular biology, cardiac-imaging
biomarkers.

7. Translational & Laboratory-Based Research
Scope: Ceramide biology, pharmacogenomics, biomarker discovery, omics-based research, bench-to-bedside
translation.

8. Drug Discovery
Scope: Novel therapeutics, drug-repurposing studies, clinical pharmacology, PK/PD modelling.

Additional subgroups may be proposed by any three voting members and approved by the Executive Committee.

Governance

Pre-Clinical and Adjunct Faculty Involvement


Bridging the bench and the bedside
CMD-RPG is built on the principle that meaningful cardiometabolic research requires the integration of basic-science
discovery with clinical application. We actively seek involvement from pre-clinical scientists and adjunct clinical faculty
to create research teams that span the full translational pipeline.

Roles for pre-clinical faculty
  •  Lead or co-lead the Translational & Laboratory-Based Research and Drug Discovery subgroups
  •  Provide mechanistic expertise (molecular biology, pharmacology, genomics, metabolomics) to clinically driven
     research questions
  •  Collaborate on grant applications that require both a basic-science and a clinical component
  •  Supervise PhD and MD-PhD students on joint translational projects
  •  Contribute biomarker discovery and validation to clinical studies

Roles for adjunct and SEHA clinical faculty
  •  Co-lead subgroups alongside CMHS Core Faculty to ensure clinical relevance and patient access
  •  Provide access to clinical cohorts, patient registries, and biobank samples at Tawam Hospital and STMC                        –SEHA
  •  Identify unmet clinical needs that can be translated into research questions
  •  Serve as site principal investigators for multi-site and industry-sponsored studies
  •  Mentor medical residents and fellows in clinical-research methodology

The dual co-lead model
Every subgroup is jointly led by one CMHS Core Faculty member and one Adjunct / SEHA Clinical Faculty member. This
ensures:

  •  Research questions are clinically grounded and scientifically rigorous
  •  Access to both laboratory infrastructure (CMHS) and patient populations (SEHA hospitals)
  •  Grant applications demonstrate institutional collaboration across the academic                  –clinical interface
  •  Balanced representation in governance and decision-making

How to get involved
  •  Complete the CMD-RPG registration form, indicating your research interests and preferred subgroup(s)
  •  Indicate whether you are interested in a Co-Lead or Deputy role
  •  Attend the monthly General Meetings — open to all CMHS and Adjunct / SEHA faculty
  •  Contact the CMD-RPG Secretary to discuss specific collaboration opportunities

How CMHS Faculty Can Submit a Study

CMD-RPG welcomes research proposals from all CMHS faculty and adjunct colleagues. Whether you have a fully
developed protocol or an early-stage idea, we can help you refine it, find collaborators, and navigate the approvals
process.

Step 1 — Submit your research idea
  •  Complete the CMD-RPG Study Proposal Form
  •  Provide a brief summary: research question, study design, target population, and anticipated outcomes
  •  Indicate which subgroup(s) your study aligns with
  •  Note any specific support you need (co-investigators, statistical design, data management, funding)
Step 2 — Subgroup review
  •  Your proposal is reviewed at the next relevant subgroup meeting
  •  The Subgroup Co-Leads assess alignment with the subgroup's research agenda
  •  Feedback is provided within two weeks, including suggestions for methodology, collaborators, and funding
     sources
Step 3 — Investigator matching
  •  CMD-RPG matches you with co-investigators who have complementary expertise
  •  Matches may include basic scientists, biostatisticians, clinical specialists, or data scientists across CMHS and SEHA
  •  Joint meetings are arranged to develop the study protocol

Step 4 — Support services
Once your study team is formed, CMD-RPG can facilitate access to:

  •  Study-design and methodology consultation
  •  Biostatistics and sample-size calculation
  •  Data management and REDCap / database setup
  •  Research-ethics application support (UAEU and SEHA IRBs)
  •  Grant-writing and budget-development assistance
  •  AI and computational research support
Step 5 — Ongoing support
  •  Present progress updates at monthly subgroup and General Meetings
  •  Access peer review and feedback from fellow CMD-RPG members
  •  Report outputs (manuscripts, grants, presentations) for inclusion in the Group's Research Output Dashboard

Faculty Matching and Research Support Services

CMD-RPG offers a comprehensive suite of research-support services to help CMHS and SEHA faculty move from idea
to publication. All services are available to registered CMD-RPG members.

For Industry and CRO Partners

CMD-RPG offers a unique platform for pharmaceutical companies, medical-device manufacturers, and contract
research organisations (CROs) seeking clinical-research partnerships in the UAE. Our integrated academic–clinical
network provides access to diverse patient populations, experienced investigators, and world-class research
infrastructure.

Site capabilities
  •  Integrated academic–clinical network across three institutions: CMHS (UAE University), Tawam Hospital, and
     Sheikh Tahnoon Medical City – SEHA Al Ain
  •  Access to diverse, multi-ethnic patient populations representative of the Gulf region
  •  Experienced principal investigators across all major cardiometabolic therapeutic areas
  •  Research-ethics approval infrastructure (UAEU REC, SEHA / DOH-REC) with established timelines
  •  GCP-trained clinical-research teams
  •  Biobank and biospecimen storage facilities
  •  Advanced imaging, genomics, and laboratory infrastructure at CMHS

Therapeutic areas
CMD-RPG investigators have active expertise across:

  •  Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes mellitus (including diabetes technology and GLP-1 receptor agonists)
  •  Obesity and metabolic syndrome
  •  Non-alcoholic / metabolic-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD / NASH)
  •  Cardiovascular disease (premature ACS, heart failure, hypertension, lipoprotein(a))
  •  Chronic kidney disease and cardiorenal syndrome
  •  Thyroid and endocrine disorders
  •  Pharmacogenomics and precision medicine

How to place a trial
  •  Initial enquiry. Contact the CMD-RPG Chair or Secretary with a brief study synopsis, including therapeutic area,
     study phase, target population, and estimated enrolment.
  •  Feasibility assessment. The relevant subgroup Co-Leads assess site feasibility, including patient availability,
     investigator interest, and infrastructure requirements. A feasibility report is provided within four weeks.
  •  Site selection and contracting. If feasible, CMD-RPG identifies and nominates a Principal Investigator and study
     team. Contract and budget negotiations proceed through UAEU's Research and Sponsored Projects Office and/or
     SEHA's research-contracts unit.
  •  Regulatory approvals. CMD-RPG coordinates ethics submissions to the UAEU and/or SEHA research-ethics
     committees, with experience of DOH-REC requirements.
  •  Study conduct and monitoring. The PI and study team conduct the trial with support from CMD-RPG's data-
     management and biostatistics services, with regular updates to the sponsor.

Partnership models
  •  Investigator-Initiated Studies (IIS) — CMD-RPG investigators propose and lead the study with industry funding
     support
  •  Industry-Sponsored Clinical Trials — sponsor-designed protocols placed at CMD-RPG network sites
