## Water Desalination Research Program > How to Apply
Candidates must Apply Online

To apply for the Water Desalination Research Program , applicants will need to upload the following documents on the online application form as PDF files:

One certified copy of the official diploma from each university attended;

One official transcript from each university attended;

An official copy of the IELTS certificate or equivalent;

Two letters of recommendation from professionals familiar with the student’s academic work;

Passport copy;

Applicant's CV; and

Statement of the applicant's research experience and interest in the area of water desalination.
