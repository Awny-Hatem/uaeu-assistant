Statistical Consulting Unit

                                          Online Workshops/Training courses
                                                        Workshop #2

Title: Introduction to Data Analytics using SPSS - Introductory course

Statistical Software: SPSS

Requirements: Basic knowledge in statistics

Instructor: Dr. Ibrahim Abdalla

Target Group: Professionals, Under/Postgraduate students

Date: 14 March., 2022 – 27 March, 2022

Period: 2 days, 4 hours/day

Platform: Zoom Meeting

Inclusive of Scientific Materials: Seminars include live lectures, hands-on exercise assignments,
and plenty of chances to ask questions. PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.

Description:

The workshop introduces participants to IBM SPSS for Windows. Participants will be trained on comparing
and protecting original datasets, and on handling data files in a variety of formats, including Excel, database
tables, SAS, and Stata. They will also be trained on how to explore SPSS Data Editor which provides for
the display of data and variables. The workshop also offers hands on training on data entry, variable, and
data manipulation, including finding and replacing data and variables, data transformation and descriptive
statistics. In addition to other important SPSS features, participants will also explore working with SPSS
output, pivot table, and command syntax.

Topics Covered:

1) Getting started with IBM SPSS for Windows. 2) Data files in SPSS: Types of data files, Opening/Reading
different data files. 3) File information. 4) Comparing datasets. 5) Protecting original data. 6) Data editor:
Data view. Variable view. Entering data. Editing data. Finding cases/variables/imputation. Finding and
replacing data and attribute. Obtaining descriptive statistics. Case selection. 7) Data editor printing. 8)
Working with multiple data sources. 9) Data transformation. 10) Working with output. 11) Pivot table. 12)
Working with command syntax. 13) SPSS utilities. 14) SPSS options. 15) Customizing menus and tools.

Workshop Fee: 1000 AED

Note: A certificate of completion of the workshop will be provided to participants.
