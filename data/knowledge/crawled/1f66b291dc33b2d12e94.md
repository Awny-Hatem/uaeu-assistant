## Submission Guidelines > Theme for 2026: The Family > Online Submission Deadline: August 24, 2026
All artworks must be first digitalized and uploaded with the following specifications:

JPG files only.

Each image must be named as follows: date/month/year/name of applicant/UAEU ID number/ name of artwork.

300 DPI resolution (Please try to keep the file size under 10 MB to ensure proper loading).

Images that have been framed or that contain watermarks should not be submitted.

Artwork will not be altered without the applicants' express permission.

Any written or performed entry should be in either Arabic, English, or Both.

Participants are solely responsible for the costs associated with preparing and submitting their designs. The Chancellor’s Award for Art will not bear any financial responsibility in this regard.

ANY submission that does not adhere to the theme for this year, will not be considered by the committee.

If any dispute or grievance arises out of or in connection with the Chancellor Arts Award, the individual participant or team leader agrees that the dispute or grievance will notify the Chancellor Arts Award in writing and discuss any issues accordingly to settle the dispute.

The individual participant or team leader hereby unconditionally and irrevocably renounces any right it may have to challenge in the UAE courts.

Sculptural works must fall within a size range of 40 cm (minimum) to 150 cm (maximum) in any single dimension. All submissions must be suitable and safe for indoor exhibition. The weight must permit safe manual handling and installation without the need for mechanical equipment.
