## Academic Affairs Unit > Overview
The Academic Affairs Unit (AAU) has been established in September 2017 as a central entity to the mission of UAEU. The wide spectrum of the activities and functions of the unit demonstrate its commitment and dedication to supporting faculty members and students in all academic aspects of their pursuit of knowledge. The AAU is charged by the Provost with assuring high standards of undergraduate and graduate academic programs, and designing and implementing effective policies and procedures to achieve this.
