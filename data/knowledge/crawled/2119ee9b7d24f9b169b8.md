ADMISSIONS


Admission into the UAEU MBA Program (Manufacturing Excellence) is highly selective in nature. Most candidates are
assessed and then selected by their employer is based on their roles within the organization.  Admission is also open to
self-sponsored candidates of any nationality,  provided they are senior employees within their organizations,  and pass
the selection process.

A critical requirement is that successful candidates are capable of undertaking postgraduate-level studies and have
been identified as potential leaders within their organizations. The MBA Admissions Committee, consisting of members
of the UAEU MBA Joint Advisory Committee, along with the Director of the MBA Program, evaluate each applicant to
determine their suitability for admission to the program.

A limited number of highly qualified students  will be admitted to each cohor t.  The final decision concerning acceptance
into the program rests with UAEU’s MBA Admissions Committee.

OVERVI   EW      OF TH   E PROGRAM









The curriculum includes 12 core courses and 4 electives. The 4 elective courses are project based and determined based
on the participants’ industrial abilities and core competencies. The program is taught at the UAEU MBA educational
facility, in the Sky Tower on Reem Island in Abu Dhabi. Classes are held two evenings a week, from 15:00 PM to 19:00
PM. The program includes a total of 16 courses (48 credit hours), in addition to a bridging course, and adheres to AACSB
requirements. The project-based electives are built around the mastery of skills developed through dealing with real-
world applied business problems and opportunities.

International Business Module
The international business module aims at providing the participants with a comprehensive view of best practices
adopted by various manufacturing and industrial organizations outside the UAE.  This week-long module involves visits
to companies and R&D centers, presentations by subject matter experts,  practical demonstrations and assignments
to be completed after the trip.
