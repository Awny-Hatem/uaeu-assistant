## Facilities > TISSUE CULTURE LAB
The lab consists of five premium horizontal laminar flow cabinets providing an area with reliable aseptic conditions for plant tissue culture.

## Facilities > PLANT GROWTH FACILITIES
High-tech greenhouse, growth rooms and a wide range of controlled environment chambers at the KCGEB allows users to customize and control various parameters achieving precise environmental requirements for their experiments.

## Facilities > MOLECULAR BIOLOGY LAB
The molecular biology laboratories at the KCGEB are at the leading edge of innovation with state-of-art molecular instrumentation allowing our scientists to pursue complex experimental designs.

## Facilities > BIOINFOMATICS LAB
KCGEB possess world class computing and storage infrastructure which allows our scientists to perform some of the most challenging date-intensive research in the field of genomics and computational biology.

## Facilities > BIOIMAGING
Bioimaging offers sample preparation and imaging facilities to our investigators and researchers through available range of advanced imaging equipment.

## Facilities > AL FOAH FARM
Around 40 hectares of land and a range of cutting-edge research facilities are made available for scientists and students from various specializations to develop and test innovative strategies that support food security in the region.
