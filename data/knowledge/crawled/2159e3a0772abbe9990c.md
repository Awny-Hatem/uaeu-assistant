## NUTR100 Nutrition and Well-being
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_4981.shtml?id=NUTR100
This course introduces the fundamentals of nutrition and its impact on personal health and wellness. Essential nutrients, healthy eating patterns, fad diets, and the relationship between diet and disease prevention will be explored. Students will learn how to make informed dietary choices, develop lifelong wellness habits, and understand the effects of lifestyle factors—such as physical activity, sleep, and stress—on overall health. Emphasis is placed on applying nutrition principles to support physical, mental, and emotional well-being in diverse populations.
Nutrition and Well-being (NUTR100) > Credit Hours : 3

Nutrition and Well-being (NUTR100) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Identify the major nutrients and their role in the body

Explain how nutrition impacts the physical, mental and emotional health

Identify the appropriate nutrition recommendations promoting wellbeing throughout the lifecycle

## NUTR800 Comprehensive Exam
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_4981.shtml?id=NUTR800
Every PhD student must pass a Comprehensive Examination (CE) designed to evaluate the breadth and depth of the student’s knowledge of his or her discipline, as well as the student’s scholarly potential. The CE consists of a written and an oral part and will be prepared, administered, and evaluated by an examination committee from the student’s concerned department. It must be taken before the start of the student’s fifth semester in the program. Students taking the CE must be in good academic standing after completion of the required coursework. The CE may be repeated only once, no later than the end of the student’s fifth semester. A second unsuccessful attempt leads to immediate termination of the student’s enrollment in the PhD program. ## NUTR805 Advanced Macronutrient Metabolism
