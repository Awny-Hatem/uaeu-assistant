## UAEU Expo 2020 Volunteers
UAEU will be launching an ambitious project at Expo 2020 Dubai. Our objective is to become one of the most important educational endeavors of the 21 st century.

We are seeking fun, enthusiastic and engaging volunteers to create a vibrant, welcoming environment and unforgettable experience for our anticipated 380,000 visitors.

Volunteering duties will involve everything from:

people management, visitor registration;

visitor experience, guest relations;

operations, retail;

to event management and much more.

## Perks of Becoming a Volunteer

A skills enriching experience

Meet new people from different countries

Be part of one of the biggest events in the region
