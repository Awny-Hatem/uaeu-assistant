                                  Addition, Discontinuation or Change of Course


I.                                                                                               Policy

                   Course changes may be required to meet the needs of new or revised
                   academic programs and may also be proposed when there is evidence that a
                   course change will result in program improvement.  Any proposed changes in
                   courses must be consistent with the United Arab Emirates University’s vision,
                   mission and strategic plan.  Proposals for substantive change will be reviewed
                   and approved through the University’s procedures.  Proposals for non-
                   substantive change do not require a full review but will follow the
                   University’s procedures to ensure that all parties concerned are properly
                   notified.

                   This policy applies to all credit courses offered by the United Arab Emirates
                   University and to non-credit courses that are required or elective in the degree
                   programs of the United Arab Emirates University.


II.                                                                             Application

                   This policy shall apply to all academic units of the United Arab Emirates
                   University.
