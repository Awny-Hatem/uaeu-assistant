                                                                       Policy on Policies


I.                                                                                               Policy

                This policy outlines procedures for the proposal of new University policies,
                for amendments to existing policies and for policy approval and distribution.

                New policies or changes to existing University policies may be proposed by
                any operating unit through the management structure to the Vice Chancellor.

                If the policy recommendation is accepted for consideration by the Provost, it
                will be written in the appropriate format, vetted through a procedure
                determined by the Vice Chancellor and forwarded to the Chancellor.

                When a policy or amendment has been recommended through the vetting
                process established by the Vice Chancellor and approved by him, he will seek
                the Chancellor’s approval.  After formal approval, the policy will be
                incorporated into the appropriate section of the Policy Manual and will be
                assigned a policy number by the Office of the Vice Chancellor.  The
                University community will be advised of the new policy or policy amendment
                through the Office of the Vice Chancellor.

                Policy suggestions which are not accepted will be returned to the initiating
                party along with feedback on the decision.


II.                                                                             Application

                This policy shall apply to all operating units of the United Arab Emirates
                University.
