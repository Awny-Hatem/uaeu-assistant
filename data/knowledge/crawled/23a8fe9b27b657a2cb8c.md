## FINANCIAL AID > Overview
The Financial Aid Department aims to support deserving students from low-income families who are facing financial difficulties that might inhibit their ability to continue with academic studies. This is done by providing financial and moral support.

Any student from a low-income family currently at UAEU has the right to apply for financial aid through eServices. Each case will be reviewed and the required documentation assessed in order to ascertain the level of support necessary.
