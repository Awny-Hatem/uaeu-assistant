## PDF page 1 — continuation 2
         Students who did not register in the early registration without providing an excuse to the college, will not be allowed
          to register in the add and drop week of the Fall 2014 Semester
          Students who are currently under Probation 2 are allowed to register at beginning of Fall 2014 Semester.
         Each student should meet with his/her academic advisor prior to registering.
         The student assumes total responsibility for his/her choice regarding courses and study plan.
         Student in good academic standing may register 12 to 19 credit hours.
         Students who are under Probation 1 cannot register for more than 13 credit hours.
         Honors students and students who expect to graduate in Fall Semester 2014/2015 may register for up to 22 credit
          hours with college approval.
         Questions regarding section capacities, closed sections and all types of restrictions should be directed to your College
          Advising Units.
         Students who will receive Probation by the end of Spring semester 2013/2014 must correct their registration during
          the add/drop of the Fall Semester.
         Student who are continuing and failed in any of their UGRU course will have to correct their registration during the
          add/drop of the Fall Semester.
         If you do not know your password please contact the Helpdesk at Tel: 03 -7136111.
