## Fees > UAEU Students
Each course available to audit students is equivalent to 3 credit hours. The fee for UAEU students is AED 300, plus 5% Value Added Tax (VAT), bringing the total to AED 315 inclusive of VAT.

## Fees > UAEU Employees and Alumni
Each course available to audit students is equivalent to 3 credit hours. The fee for UAEU employees and alumni is AED 900, plus 5% Value Added Tax (VAT), bringing the total to AED 945 inclusive of VAT.

Audit students are required to complete the payment within 24 hours of being accepted into the program .
