4.2 EPP Programs Supporting Completer Employment

                   The EPP maintains its partnership and collaboration with the MOE, ESE                      and ADEK schools, and
              other stakeholders to ensure that its completers are provided with additional qualifications and
              opportunities for employment.

                   Below are some of the support programs provided to the completers:

                   1.   The EPP has forged an agreement with the Emirates Schools Establishments to develop a joint
                        road map to prepare, qualify, and employ highly qualified teachers in public education schools
                        in UAE. ESE has developed a flexible and fast mechanism to enable the direct employment of
                        the ECE, SPED, and HPE completers. The agreement ensures the training and follow-up after
                        employment and sharing of the completers’ performance to the EPP’s continuous program
                        improvement.

                   2.   Since August     2022, the ‘Upskilling Program for Early Childhood Teacher Employability’ was
                        reactivated by the Abu Dhabi Early Childhood Authority (ECA) to develop                       educational and
                        practical  programs  that  achieve  the  strategic  objectives  of  early  childhood.  It  has  also
                        conducted several workshops for the upskilling of fresh and old graduates of ECE.

                   3.   The HPE program has built a strong collaboration with key stakeholders such as the Emirates
                        School Establishment, Ministry of Defense, Ministry of Education, Abu Dhabi Police, Abu Dhabi
                        Sports Council, Fatima Bint Mubarak Ladies Sports Academy, Abu Dhabi Cycling Club, Al Ain
                        Football Club, UAE Football Association, and other sports clubs (e.g., Abu Dhabi Archery Club)
                        to create opportunities for employment for its completers.

                   4.   The SPED program is currently collaborating with the Zayed Higher Organizations for People
                        of Determination (ZHO) through scholarships and future employment to its scholars.
