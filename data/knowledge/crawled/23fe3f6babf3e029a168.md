7. Governance of Graduate Degree                Programs

Overview
Describes the governance of the University’s graduate academic programs and explains how
this operates in practice with respect to:
1.                                                           Creation of new academic programs or their component parts;
2.                                                           Modifications to established academic programs or their component parts; and
3.                                                           Periodic internal and external review of academic programs.

Scope
Unless specified otherwise           , the policies in the following sections apply to all graduate
academic programs.

Graduate academic programs are designed, implemented and managed by the College                             s of the

structures,  policies and p                                rocedures.  This governance operates to ensure                                       the high standards of
the graduate academic programs consistent with           the mission of the UAEU.
