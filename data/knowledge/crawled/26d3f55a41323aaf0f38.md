## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > OVERVIEW
When progressing up the managerial ladder, the process of managers having to stop doing some things they have done in the past and start doing new things continues. This process of unlearning and learning is an important one for managers to be successful. The Senior Management Program (SMP) has been designed to equip managers with the necessary knowledge and skills to function effectively in a UAE and regional environment. Due to the constantly changing nature of this environment, the content of the SMP, which focuses on both present and future national and international realities, is continually adapted. A generalist rather than a specialist approach is adopted.

## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > COURSE DESCRIPTION AND MAIN OBJECTIVE
The SMP focuses on a number of selected management functions. Participants eventually integrate these into a group project which they present to a panel at the end of the program.

## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > LEARNING OUTCOMES
After completion of the program, participants will be able to:

Apply the principles underlying entrepreneurship in a business context

Apply identified change models to real-life situations and case studies

Recommend ways of improving the financial viability of organizations

Assess the effect of economic drivers on industries and companies

Plan and execute projects and processes in their own organizations by utilizing the techniques provided by systems thinking and project management

Develop an advanced marketing plan incorporating all the facets of a sound and comprehensive marketing approach

Develop a comprehensive business plan integrating all the disciplines of the program

## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > CONTENT
To maximize the learning experience of delegates, the program is divided into four workshops. The subjects on the SMP are as follows:

Implementing Strategy

What is Strategy?

Process of Strategy Implementation.

Role of senior and middle managers in implementing strategy

Using the Balanced Scorecard to implement strategy

Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurial principles and mindset

Management of the future

Management of growth and creativity

Corporate intrapreneurship

Financial Management

Advanced financial ratios: liquidity, turnover, leverage and profitability ratios

Drafting of budgets

Working capital management

Management of cash flow

Calculating and interpreting Net Present Value

Leadership & Change Management

Leadership in the digital era

Coaching as leadership style

Managing Change

Managing self

IQ, EQ and SQ

Supply Chain Management

Supply chains as driver of value

Global vs Regional vs Local Supply Chains

Managing risk in the supply chain

Economics

The meaning and operation of the economy and economic systems

Behavior and interaction of participants in economic activities

The significance of money and monetary policy in the economy

Macroeconomic objectives and analysis, evaluation of the impact of economic changes on a company

Economic policy initiatives in the UAE

Design Thinking

Empathizing: understanding the human needs involved

Defining: Re-framing and defining the problem in human-centric ways

Ideating: Creating many ideas in ideation sessions

Prototyping: Adopting a hands-on approach in prototyping

Testing: Developing a prototype/solution to the problem

Marketing Management

The role of marketing in the organization

The changing and uncertain marketing environment, including the role and impact of social media marketing

Marketing research

Positioning of the company and segmentation of target markets

B2B marketing

Information & Communications Technology

The impact of technology on business and business models

Understanding the 4th Industrial Revolution and related concepts such as AI, Blockchain, Smart Cities and digital transformation

Digital Transformation

Business Ethics and Corporate Governance

Business Plan Template

Presentations

## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > FACULTY MEMBERS
The UAEU will employ faculty with superb theoretical knowledge and skills, as well as rich practical experience to ensure that delegates complete the course with developed practical skills and not just theoretical knowledge.

## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > TARGET GROUP
The majority of the participants in the SMP are middle and senior managers.

## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
No formal tertiary qualifications are required. Participants should have appropriate experience and a basic knowledge of the different program topics. The SMP is presented in English only. It is preferred that participants have at least 5 years of experience at middle management in an organization. Participants should have a mastery of the concepts and learning content of the Management Development Program or related management development programs.

## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > TEACHING METHODS
Workshops will be presented on a face-to- face basis, unless synchronous online teaching is mandated by the UAE Government

Class discussions, case studies, articles, and relevant videos

## Senior Management Development Program (SMP) > CERTIFICATE
Delegates who successfully complete all assignments will obtain a Certificate of Successful Completion by demonstrating their knowledge, understanding and skills of the learning outcomes in practical assessments during and at the end of the course.
