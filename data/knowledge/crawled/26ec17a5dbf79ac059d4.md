Procedures of Policy No. (9) -  International Student Services

1. International Students Office
       The International Students Office (ISO) provides assistance for coordinating the
       administrative needs of registered international students of the University and for
       supporting their academic and personal development and success requirements while
       enrolled at the University.


2. International Students Benefits and Entitlements
      a)                     The University Enrollment Deanship coor dinates the admission of international
             students through its Office of Admission.
      b)                  Based on their admission type, international students may be entitled to receive
             benefits including entry visa, housing, health insurance, financial assistance, and travel
             tickets.
      c)                     Funding for the above mentioned benefits may be covered by the University Budget,
             the student or his/her sponsor, or a third party, depending on the availability of funds.
      d)                  In cases where a student’s residency visa is sponsored by a party other than the
             University and the sponsor is not able to continue such sponsorship while the student
             completes his/her academic degree, the University may sponsor the residency visa of
             the student provided that the student takes responsibility for all administrative fees.
