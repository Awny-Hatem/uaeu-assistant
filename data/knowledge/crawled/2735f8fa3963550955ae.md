12. Graduate Scholarships, Fellowships, and Assistantships

Overview
The University awards Scholarships, Fellowships and Assistantships to graduate students for
two main reasons: (1) To enhance students’ access to graduate education by helping the students
financially and, hence, make their graduate education more affordable and (2) to further the
professional development of students as future teachers and researchers by engaging them in
teaching and research experiences in their areas of studies.
This Policy defines terms, conditions and eligibility criteria associated with scholarships,
fellowships and assistantships offered by the University to graduate students.

Scope
Applies to all eligible graduate, degree-seeking students at UAEU.

Policy

Definition and Explanation of Terms

Scholarships
1.  Graduate Research         Scholarships are awards designated for                talented   students    who are
    enrolled full-time in research-based graduate programs at UAEU. Scholarships are awarded
    to talented students based on evidence of academic achievement and potential for research.
    The University sets the criteria for awarding Scholarships, and the University determines
    the  number  of  Scholarships  it  will  make  available  in  a  given  year.    Recipients  of
    scholarships are required to work on research projects under the guidance of faculty during
    the term of their award.  It is the responsibility of the individual faculty in the Colleges to
    ensure that each scholarship recipient is assigned the appropriate duty.

2.  Other providers (whether a foundation or any other external organization or individual) may
    fund additional Scholarships. They may set specific conditions for recipient selection in
    addition to the University’s criteria. If this is the case, an agreement between that party and
    UAEU must be approved prior to granting any scholarships sponsored by the donor.

Fellowships
1.  Graduate Research Fellowships are awards designated for                     any eligible students who are
    enrolled full-time in research-based graduate programs at UAEU.  Fellowships are awarded
    to eligible students based on evidence of academic achievement and research potential. The
    University sets the criteria for awarding Fellowships, and the University determines the
    number of Fellowships to be made available in a given year, based on the recommendation
    of the respective Colleges taking into consideration the resources available to award the

Graduate Assistantships
1.  Graduate Assistants are typically full-time graduate students (Master’s and Doctoral levels)
    who participate in teaching, research, or administrative activities in exchange for financial
    support at the University.

2.  Graduate  Assistantships  may  be  awarded  to  graduate  students  who,  based  on  their
    credentials, are deemed likely to be highly successful as students and are also assessed as
    being capable of contributing to research, teaching, or service for the university.

3.  Graduate Assistantships contribute to student professional development with the primary
    purpose of assisting students in the successful completion of their academic program.
    Activities incorporated in the work of a Graduate Assistant should therefore be relevant to
    the  student's  program  of  study  as  well  as  contributing  to  the               University's  teaching,
    research/creative activity, or service efforts. The department/unit providing the assistantship
    determines the nature and scope of the Assistantship assignment.

Chancellor’s Fellowships, Other Fellowships or Financial Awards for Graduate Students

    The University or other national or international organizations may provide other types of
    fellowships to Master or PhD students in pursuance of educational goals. The terms and
    support provided is determined by the awarding entity, and in cases in which the fellowships
    or awards are from outside the UAEU, such awards to the UAEU students should be agreed
    upon between the UAEU and the awarding entity.

Procedures of Policy No. (12) - Graduate Scholarships, Fellowships, and
Assistantships

UAEU offers different types of financial support programs to eligible graduate students in the
forms of Graduate Research Scholarships, Graduate Research Fellowships and Graduate
Assistantships.

1. Graduate Research Scholarships

   A. Award Benefits
   a)  Graduate Research Scholarships are granted to eligible students and provide a full
       package of benefits, which includes             full tuition waiver, monthly stipend, housing or
       housing allowance, health insurance and may also include research support.
   b) The housing benefit is provided to UAE national students if they reside outside the Al
       Ain vicinity, and to international students if their visa is sponsored by UAEU.
   c)  Health insurance is provided to          UAE national students if needed, and to international
       students if their visa is sponsored by UAEU.
   d) Research support may be available if student’s supervisor has a funded project.
   e)  Award recipients who take official leave of absence from their study will have their
       stipend payments and any applicable housing allowance suspended for the duration of the
       leave period, and the period of their Scholarship award will be extended accordingly.

   B. Student Eligibility Requirements
   The program is designed to recruit and support primarily PhD students. However, talented
   undergraduate Emirati students at UAEU may be recruited to pursue graduate studies and
   can be awarded scholarships starting at the Master's level.
   Applicants for Graduate Research Scholarships must meet the following criteria:
   a) General requirements (for all):
        (i) Student must enroll full-time in a minimum of nine graduate credits per semester or
            obtain approval of the Dean of College of Graduate Studies for lower than nine credit
            hours. Students are also expected to be available at UAEU on full-time basis.
        (ii) Student must not be financially sponsored by any other organization or hold any
            active employment.
   b) At the Master's level (for UAE national students only):
         (i) Student must have a GPA of 3.5 or higher in the Bachelor's degree from UAEU.
         (ii) Student must be admitted into a Master's program with a research thesis that leads
             to a PhD program at UAEU.
         (iii)Student must commit to pursue both of the Master's and PhD degrees consecutively.
         (iv) Student must have and maintain a GPA of 3.5 or higher at the Master's level.
   c) At the PhD level (for all):

2. Graduate Research Fellowships

   A. Award Benefits
   a)  Graduate Research Fellowships are granted to eligible students and provide a partial
       package of benefits, which includes full tuition waiver and health insurance. Depending
       on availability of funds, monthly stipend, housing allowance and/or research support may
       also be provided.
   b) Health insurance is provided to            UAE national students if needed, and to international
       students if their visa is sponsored by UAEU.
   c)  Research support may be available if student’s supervisor has a funded project.
   d) Award recipients who take official leave of absence from their study will have their
       stipend payments and any applicable housing allowance suspended for the duration of the
       leave period, and the period of their Fellowship award will be extended accordingly.

   B. Student Eligibility Requirements
   Applicants for Graduate Research Fellowships must meet the following criteria:
   a) General requirements (for all):
        (i)  Student must enroll full-time         in a minimum of 9 graduate credits per semester or
             obtain approval of the Dean of College of Graduate Studies for lower than nine credit
             hours. Students are also expected to be available at UAEU on full-time basis.
        (ii) Student must not be financially sponsored by any other organization or hold any
             active employment.
        (iii)Student must have a GPA of 3.3 or higher in the Master's degree.
        (iv) Student must have and maintain a GPA of 3.0 or higher at the PhD level.

3. Graduate Student Assistantships

   A. Award Benefits
   a)   Graduate     Student     Assistantships      are  granted      to  full-time  graduate  students  who
        participate in teaching, research, or administrative activities at the university in exchange
        for financial support in the form of a monthly stipend.
   b)   The University provides health insurance to Graduate Student Assistants if their visa is
        sponsored by UAEU.

   B. Student Eligibility Requirements
   Graduate students must meet the following conditions and minimum eligibility criteria for
   Assistantship awards:
   a)  Newly enrolled graduate students must               have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0                  on a
       scale of 4.0 or equivalent in the Bachelor degree, or in their Master if they have already
       earned a Master degree.
   b) Other graduate students must have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 (on a scale of 4.0)
       or equivalent in their graduate studies.
   c)  Other conditions imposed by the granting college/unit, if applicable.

4. Chancellor Graduate Student Fellowships
   The Chancellor Graduate Student Fellowship program provides assistance in the form of
   tuition waivers to qualified UAE nationals              who desire to pursue graduate studies at the
   UAEU.  The program is available to two groups of UAE national                         students: (1) Full-time
   employees of the University and (2) UAE nationals at-large.

   A. Award Benefits
   The benefits of the Chancellor’s Graduate Student Fellowship Program                          are  available to
   eligible UAE national students as follows:
   a)   First Category:  UAE nationals who are full-time employees of UAEU:  Candidates in
        this category are offered a partial tuition waiver of 20% of the total cost of tuition.
   b)   Second Category:  Other UAE nationals:  Candidates in this category will be offered a
        partial tuition waiver of 15% of the total cost of tuition.

   B. Student Eligibility Requirements
   To be eligible, a candidate in any of the categories must:
       a)  Have completed a minimum of one year of study in a Graduate program at UAEU,
