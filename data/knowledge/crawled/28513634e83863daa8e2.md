## PDF page 1
Steps to apply for the installment service


Students must complete their course schedule before applying for the installment plan. Any
changes, such as adding or dropping a course, will result in the plan being canceled.



1- Go to E-service and choose 'Student Tuition Installment & Payment.

















2- Under Tuition Installment Request, select 'Apply for Installment.

      Under this option, you have three choices:
       1. Apply for the Installment Service only.
       2. Apply for the Installment Service and initiate the first payment.
       3. Apply for payment as a full amount.
