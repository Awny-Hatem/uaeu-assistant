## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Farm News
05 April 2016

The farm management awarded the workers Mr. Mujahid A. Khan Zada, Mr. Anbalagan Venkatachalam, and Mr. Kifayatulla M. Azeem with the ‘Best Work Performance Recognition’ for Fall 2015, Winter 2015, and Spring 2016, respectively.

January – February 2016

The greenhouse section has been significantly upgraded. Four formerly broken greenhouses have been thoroughly renovated, and are now available for research and educational activities.

29 November 2015

Together with the Date Palm Development and Research Unit, Al Foah Experimental Farm organized and hosted the 1st Meeting to Foster Interdisciplinary Research and Education on Date Palms. There were twelve participants, including representatives of the CFA, the KCGEB, New York University of Abu Dhabi, and the University of Sydney.

23 – 25 February 2015

With a small ruminant exhibition, a haybale café, and a miniature garden workshop, Al Foah Experimental Farm participated in the College of Food and Agriculture Festival 2015.

17 February 2015

Al Foah Experimental Farm visited Al Foah Girl’s School, and participated in their ‘Healthy Life Day’.
