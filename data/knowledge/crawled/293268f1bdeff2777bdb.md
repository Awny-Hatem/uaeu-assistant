## Division of Information Technology > Overview
Welcome to the Division Of Information Technology (DoIT) at UAEU. DoIT serves as a centralized administration of Information Technology which provides a wide variety of technical services to UAEU community with enterprise infrastructure, applications, telecommunication and end-user support services. As the main IT services provider at UAEU, DoIT values excellence, innovation, discovery and search for truth, academic freedom, collaboration, accountability of resources and relationship, sharing knowledge in a learning environment as well as the application of knowledge and discovery to enhance the quality of learning and research and work experience for students, faculty and staff.

## Division of Information Technology > CIO Message
As UAEU advances its strategic vision for excellence in education, research, and community engagement, digital transformation remains a foundational priority for the institution. The Division of Information Technology (DoIT) is committed to delivering innovative, secure, and reliable digital solutions that align directly with the university priorities. Our main objective is to support and sustain a digitally enabled environment that strengthens teaching and learning, supports high impact research, and enhances administrative effectiveness and efficiency. We aim to provide meaningful and transformative digital experiences for UAEU community by ensuring the IT digital services are seamlessly aligned with UAEU’s strategic goals and directions.
