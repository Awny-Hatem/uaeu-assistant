# UAEU Cultural and Social Club

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## UAEU Cultural and Social Club > Overview
UAEU Cultural and Social Club was established with the aim of connecting with faculty members, staff and their families at the university and finding a place that embraces the university community to enhance social and cultural interaction through various activities and active participation to enrich their university life. Al Multaqa also offers many services to the UAEU family through cultural, social, sporting and recreational activities, holding meetings, seminars, training courses, scientific programs, national and social celebrations.

## UAEU Cultural and Social Club > Emtiaz Program
As part of the UAEU Cultural and Social Club commitment to providing distinguished services, the “Imtiaz” Program offers exclusive discounts at 50 commercial establishments to members of the university community, students, and alumni upon presenting their university ID card.
