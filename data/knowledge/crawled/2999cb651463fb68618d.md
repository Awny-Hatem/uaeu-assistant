Title: Data Analytics using SPSS - Intermediate course
Statistical Software: SPSS
Requirements: Basic Statistics
Instructor: Dr. Samir Safi
Target Group: Postgraduate students and interested researchers
Period: 3 days, 4 hours/session, 12 hours total
Date: 17-23 March, 2025
Platform: Face-to-face Meeting
Inclusive of Scientific Materials: PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.
Description:
This workshop introduces participants to the moderate methods and techniques of statistical
inference and applications from different fields. Topics include inference involving univariate
statistical analysis, Chi-square tests, nonparametric tests, Reliability Analysis, and regression
analysis.
Topics Covered:
Day 1:

    •    Introduction to Parametric and Non-Parametric tests.
    •    Analyze data to make inferences about one, or two population Means and Proportions.
    •    Compare several independent groups’ means (One and Two-Way factor ANOVA).
    •    Case study
Day 2:

    •    Compare several dependent groups’ means (ANOVA for Repeated measures).
    •    Mixed ANOVA
    •    Nonparametric Test
    •    Case study
