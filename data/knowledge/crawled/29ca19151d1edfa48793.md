Notes:
1)	The	deadlines	for	foreign	applicants	who	reside	outside	the	UAE	are	one	month	prior	to	the	aforementioned	dates.
2)	All	applicants	have	up	to	one	month	after	a	deadline	to	submit	all	required,	official	documents.
3)	Applications	submitted	to	a	program	after	the	deadline	will	be	processed	for	the	program's	next	admission	cycle.
4)	UAEU	students	may	apply	for	admission	into	a	graduate	program	during	their	last	semester,	however,	their	admission	will	require	their	completion
				of	all	degree	requirements	before	they	can	be	accepted	in	the	program.
