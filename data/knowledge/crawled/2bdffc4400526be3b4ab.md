## Research Themes (2025–2027)
ZCHS supports interdisciplinary research across seven core thematic groups, each aligned with the UAE’s health research priorities:

Precision Medicine and Genomics

Healthy Aging and Geriatric Health

Maternal, Child, and Adolescent Health

Non-Communicable Diseases and Lifestyle Disorders

Population and Public Health

Infectious Diseases, Planetary Health, and Antimicrobial Resistance

Environmental and Food Safety and Security

Each theme is led by experts and supported through strategic grants, collaborative studies, and national and international partnerships.
