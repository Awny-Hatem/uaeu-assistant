## The Great Student Competition > Short Movies
To Register: submit video of no longer than one minute.

The movie must be MP4, AVI, MOV or MPEG.

Submit a brief description no longer than 100 words.

Group participation is allowed for this category

The theme should be suggested by the general topics humanity, society or national message or any other approved topic.

Approval for campus photography will be provided if necessary.

The student can present the work in any appropriate manner.

The duration is no longer than 5 minutes.
