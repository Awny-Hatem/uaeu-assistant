Procedures of Policy No. (12) - Student Volunteering

1.                                                                              A volunteer is defined as:
               a)         An uncompensated individual who performs services directly related to the
                       University.  Volunteer work aims to support the humanitarian, charitable or public
                       service activities of the University, and/or  to gain experience in specific areas.
               b)          An uncompensated student who performs services for entities external to the
                       University.

2.                                                                              Categories of individuals who can volunteer within the University:
               a)                      University students.
               b)                   University Alumni.
               c)               Non-university high school students under the age of 18. (School students under
                       the age of 15 are not eligible to apply for volunteering.)

3.                                                                     School students may volunteer as part of the school program with the agreement of the
             responsible departments.

4.                                                                              School students must get parental approval before being approved for volunteering.

5.                                                       If a person seeks to volunteer for services that are considered high risk (either to the
             volunteer or to the University), the volunteer must sign a disclaimer releasing the
             University from responsibility and liability for the consequences of their volunteer
             service.

6.                                                                              The following services are some examples of high-risk services:
               a)                      Laboratory work.
               b)                   Working with animals.
               c)                      Working with minors, patients, human research subjects.
               d)       Professional services, such as those performed by accountants, architects,
                       engineers.
               e)                      Services involving travel of any sort.

7.                                                             A University volunteer’s term of service may be terminated for cause at any time and
             with no prior notice.  An appeal to this decision can be submitted to the DVC for
             Students Affairs.
