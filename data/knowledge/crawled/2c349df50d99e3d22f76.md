Instructions         Regarding Registration:


           Students who did not register in the early registration without providing an excuse to the college, will
            not be allowed to register in the add and drop week of the Spring 2016/2017 Semester
           Each student should meet with his/her academic advisor prior to registering.
           Questions regarding section capacities, closed sections and all types of restrictions should be directed to your
            College Advising Units.
           Student in good academic standing may register 12 to 19 credit hours& students who are under Probation
               Cannot register for More than 13 credit hours & Honors students and students who expect to graduate in
                Spring 2016/2017 may register for up to 22 credit hours with college approval.
           Student who are continuing and failed in any of their UFP course will have to correct their registration during
            the add/drop of the Next Semester.
