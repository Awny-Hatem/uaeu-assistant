UAE University and EADS Defence & Security sign MOU



   Al Ain, 26th May 2010: In a significant move that will
   see closer cooperation to increase the intellectual
   capital within UAE, as well as the exchange of ideas
   and sharing of knowledge between the nation’s
   premier educational institution and a global leader in
   aerospace, defence and related services, the United
   Arab Emirates University and EADS Defence &
   Security signed today a Memorandum of
   Understanding to increase cooperation in several
   areas of common interest.


   The MOU envisages collaborations in various areas such as internship opportunities for UAEU students
   in European EADS facilities and possibilities to align exchange between UAEU and other European
   universities to foster opportunities for graduates to join EADS DS in GCC based assignments. Other
   parts of the agreement include EADS sponsorships of appropriate undergraduate and postgraduate
   candidates and the possibility of participating in joint research projects that aim at supporting and
   revitalizing technological development.


   Speaking at the announcement of the MOU,  Dr. Abdullah Saad Al- Khanbashi, Vice Chancellor of
   UAE University, said: “In today’s world, where success is often the result of a combination of
   theoretical knowledge with sound, practical skills in the real world, this step we have taken today, I am
   hopeful, will result in richer, broader experiences for our students, preparing them better for the
   corporate world once they graduate.”


   Briefing on the agreement, Mr. Franz Peraus, Chief Executive Officer Middle East of EADS DS  said:
   "We know about the importance of higher education and  exchange in a globalized world. We feel it our
   responsibility to support the next generation of national workforce, enabling them with latest know how
   and also, growing local talent to take up responsibilities in an international corporate world.”
