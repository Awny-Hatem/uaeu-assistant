Science Week 2019 Program
                                               April 22 – 24, 2019

   Daily program: 10:00 am – 4:00 pm.
   Final & Timing of each activity will be settled later.
   Some activities will be concurrent.
   You need to register in the website before the event for the workshops and competitions.
   Although most activities are repeated every day, 1st day (Scientist) will have some focus on
    the undergraduate programs, 2nd day (Pioneer) concentrate on the internships, careers &
    employments, and 3rd day (Scholar) is more about research & graduate studies.

MAIN ACTIVITIES


            Exhibition (Internal & External different exhibitors)
            Chemistry Café
            Chemistry Perfume Workshop
            Discover Physics laws (on site demonstrations)
            Mathematician Games (intellectual & fun games)
            Planetarium (Space Dome Imitation)
            Telescope
            Geology Mobile Lab
            Rock & Minerals display
            Alumni interview
            Hologram (3D show)
            Biology Models
            Be Scientist (Short workshop)
            Poster presentations (COS Students)
            Meet COS students
            Sweet Science Talk
            Innovated/Patent project.
            Periodic Table Contest (for the Periodic Table International Year)
            Innovation roundtable lab (onsite innovation thinking)
