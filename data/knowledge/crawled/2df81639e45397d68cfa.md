## College of Humanities and Social Sciences > uaeu-alumni
Connections with Alumni are an important component of the University’s community engagement activities. In 1981, UAEU graduated its first cohort of 472 students. The alumni body has now grown to more than 57,000, many of whom occupy key positions in governmental and non-governmental organizations across the country and beyond. We are proud of their achievements and aspire to build a strong connection with all our alumni. Our aim is to ensure that our alumni have a platform to stay connected with each other and with the University.

Our Alumni members are entitled to a range of professional and social networking benefits. So join why not join the Network and stay connected with UAEU !
