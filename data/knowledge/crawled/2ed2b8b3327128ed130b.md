## Internal Medicine Department > Clinical & Community Services
Members of the department provide inpatient and out of hours on call consultant general and specialist service for the two main teaching hospitals on a daily basis in the following areas:

Acute medicine

Allergy Asthma

Cardiovascular disease

Chest Medicine

Dermatology

Diabetes and endocrine disease

Gastrointestinal diseases

Gastro nutrition/Obesity

Hypertension

Haematological diseases

Oncology

Renal Disease

Rheumatic

Sleep disorders

Stroke/neurological disorders
