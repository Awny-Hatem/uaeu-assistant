## Payment Methods
The only accepted payment method at the University is electronic payment (E-Payment) through the E-Services system, using one of the methods listed below.

Cash payments and personal bank transfers are not accepted.

*Note: Students must ensure that the type of fee is accurately selected before completing the payment process. Whether it is for housing fees , housing fines , or tuition fees to avoid any delays or processing errors.

## Accepted Payment Methods
Smart Wallets | Bank Cards
Apple Pay | Debit Card
Samsung Pay | Credit Card
| Prepaid Card

*Please note that the customer will bear the bank transaction fee, which is currently 0.68% of the transaction amount (subject to change).

## Steps to Pay Tuition Fees:
Go to the E-Services page via the UAEU website or mobile application.

Click on the E-Service option at the top menu.

From the list, click on Financial Services and select Student Tuition Installments & Payments .

Enter the required payment amount, then click Submit to proceed to the secure e-Payment page.

## Steps to Pay Housing Fees:
Go to the E-Services page via the UAEU website or mobile application.

Click on the E-Service option at the top menu.

From the list, click on Financial Services and select Student Housing Payment .

From the available options, choose the correct reason for payment—either Housing Fees or Housing Fines .

Enter the required payment amount, then click Submit to proceed to the secure e-Payment page.
