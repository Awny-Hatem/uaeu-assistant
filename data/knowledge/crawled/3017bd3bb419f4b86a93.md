# Student Accounts Office Policies

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## 1. Tuition Fee and Student Account Management
The Student Accounts Office is responsible for administering tuition fees and student financial accounts, monitoring payments, installment plans, and other financial transactions related to students’ studies, in accordance with the approved policies and procedures of United Arab Emirates University (UAEU).

Students may view and monitor their financial accounts through the University’s electronic services.

## 2. Payment of Tuition Fees
Tuition fees must be paid in full and on time at the beginning of each semester, in accordance with the deadlines specified in the published Tuition Fee Schedule.

Students are personally responsible for all financial obligations owed to the University and for any consequences arising from failure to meet such obligations.

## 3. Tuition Fee Installment Plan
Students who are responsible for paying all or part of their educational expenses may enroll in the Tuition Fee Installment Plan, provided that the initial payment is made by the specified deadline and all subsequent installments are paid in accordance with the approved installment schedule.

## 4. Sponsored Students
Students whose educational expenses are funded by an external organization or sponsoring entity must submit written confirmation from the sponsoring entity confirming its commitment to pay the applicable tuition fees. Such confirmation must be provided before the deadline specified in the Tuition Fee Schedule.

If the required confirmation is not submitted by the applicable deadline, the student will become personally responsible for the payment of all outstanding tuition fees and related charges to maintain continued enrollment.

## 5. Non-Payment of Tuition Fees
Failure to pay tuition fees, or late payment of such fees, may result in restrictions on access to certain academic services, a requirement to pay tuition fees in advance for subsequent semesters, or cancellation of current or future course registration, in accordance with the University’s approved regulations and procedures.

## 6. Cancellation of Registration Due to Non-Payment
If a student’s registration is cancelled due to non-payment of tuition fees, the student will not be permitted to attend the remaining classes or sit for final examinations during that semester.

Registration cancelled due to non-payment may not be reinstated. Any academic work completed during the affected semester will also be cancelled, and the student will remain responsible for all outstanding fees and financial obligations.

## 7. Methods of Payment
Tuition fees and other applicable charges must be paid using the payment methods and channels specified in the Tuition Fee Schedule and in accordance with the University’s approved payment instructions.

## 8. Tuition Fee Notification
A tuition fee notice issued by the Student Accounts Office and sent to the student’s official University email address, or a financial account statement made available to the student through the University’s electronic platform, constitutes official notification of the student’s financial obligations and the amounts due for payment.

## 9. Refund of Fees
All tuition fees and fees charged for services provided are non-refundable unless otherwise specified in the Tuition Fee Schedule or the University’s approved Refund Policy.

## 10. Withdrawal from the University or Courses
Students wishing to withdraw from the University, withdraw from a course, or submit a request to suspend their registration must comply with the applicable add/drop and withdrawal deadlines.

If a student withdraws after the applicable deadline, the student will remain liable for the full tuition fees, even if a request for “Withdrawal Without Failure” is approved.
