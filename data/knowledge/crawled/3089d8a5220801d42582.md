5. Domain Names

Overview
Defines policy and procedures governing requesting and assignment of domain names.

Scope
Applies  to  all   Colleges     and   Departments      that  apply   for  domain  and  sub-domain  name
registration within     the UAEU for websites or other use of the Internet and guards against
improper use of other domain names.

Objective
The objective of this policy is to ensure that all UAEU information and Internet services operate
under the uaeu.ac.ae domain name and not under other domain names.

Policy
1.  The Internet domain name assigned to UAEU is uaeu.ac.ae. UAEU recognizes uaeu.ac.ae
    as its official domain name.
2.  UAEU owns the uaeu.ac.ae domain and governs its use for the benefit of its academic,
    research, communication, and other activities and interests. UAEU has the right to protect
    the use of its name.
3.  The integrity and reputation of UAEU rely on consistent and accurate content on the
    uaeu.ac.ae domain and on any websites that relate to, refer to, or could be perceived as
    representing UAEU.
4.  UAEU       Information  Technology          Division    (DoIT)  manages  the  uaeu.ac.ae  domain,
    coordinates naming for new domains, and manages website resources for the faculty,
    administrators, and students operating on behalf of UAEU or any of its units.
5.  DoIT has sole rights of discretion in the administration of the domain name, and may consult
    as appropriate with other interested parties.
6.  There are three types of domain names used by the UAEU:
    a)   First-Level Domain Name             -  for example: uaeu.ac.ae
    b)   Lower-Level Domain Names -  for example: library.uaeu.ac.ae
    c)   External Domain Names               -  for example: www.ain.ae

Procedures of Policy No. (5) - Domain Names

    Subdomain names serve practical and technical purposes by providing an “address” for the
    website. In addition, subdomain names serve as communication vehicles to convey the
    identity of the College or Department, indicating the nature or content of information the
    site offers to its    guests. The following procedures support and promote best practices
    concerning subdomain names that connect to, refer to, or represent UAEU.


1. First-Level Domain (uaeu.ac.ae)
    UAEU owns first-level domain name: uaeu.ac.ae



2. Lower-Level Domains
    a)  Lower-level subdomains in the uaeu.ac.ae domain can be assigned to:
         1)    Enterprise services or applications (e.g. elearning.uaeu.ac.ae, webmail.uaeu.ac.ae,
               mysupport.uaeu.ac.ae)
         2)    Accepted standards by the          Internet-at-large    community      (e.g. www.uaeu.ac.ae,
               ftp.uaeu.ac.ae, wiki.uaeu.ac.ae)
         3)    Colleges (e.g. cit.uaeu.ac.ae, cbe.uaeu.ac.ae, cos.uaeu.ac.ae)
         4)    Departments that provide services to the entire UAEU community and the external
               community (e.g. library.uaeu.ac.ae, cec.uaeu.ac.ae, multaqa.uaeu.ac.ae)
         5)    Server names which are part of an enterprise service (e.g. server1.uaeu.ac.ae and
               server2.uaeu.ac.ae, which are main web-hosting servers)
    b)   An exception of the above categories will be evaluated by DoIT and proper approval
         obtained from the concerned management unit.
    c)   Conditions for valid subdomain names:
         1)    Use of the requested subdomain does not conflict with any UAEU Policy or
               practice.
         2)    The requested subdomain is not likely to be ambiguous if                    used without unit
               identification.
         3)    Use of the requested subdomain should not conflict with any UAEU interest, as
               determined by DoIT.
         4)    Domain names requested by the Colleges should be approved by the Dean and the
               CIO.


3. External Domain Names
    a)   External domain names are those that do not end with uaeu.ac.ae domain. These domain
         names may be allowed if the proposed name will be used by many users from many
         different organizations outside UAEU (e.g. www.it-innovations.ae, www.ain.ae)
    b)   Conditions for valid external subdomain names:
         1)    The proposed name is not likely to be confused with the name of a UAEU
               department or unit.
         2)    The proposed name signifies an organization or venture, commercial or non-
               commercial that is not explicitly part of UAEU.

4. General Rules for Valid Domain Names
    a)   Domain    names    may    not    contain    dates    for    one-time    events    (Example:
         researchweek2011.uaeu.ac.ae will not be granted).
    b)   Domain names are limited to 20 characters before the “. uaeu.ac.ae”.
    c)   Each domain name must use either abbreviations, acronyms or full words. Partial words
         are not permitted. (Example: lib.uaeu.ac.ae will not be granted; use a full name such as
         library.uaeu.ac.ae.)
    d)   Domain names must consist of numbers                  and/or letters and not punctuation marks.
         (Example: creativity-club.uaeu.ac.ae will not be granted.)
    e)   Domain    names    may    not    contain    trademarked    brand    names.                     (Example:
         sunguard.uaeu.ac.ae will not be granted.)
    f)   Domain   names   that   are   clearly   repetitive   will   not   be   granted.                (Example:
         alumniof2002.uaeu.ac.ae is one of many similar names; a better alternative would be
         alumni.uaeu.ac.ae/2002.)
    g)   Sites   for   students’      organizations   will   be   assigned   URLs   beginning   with
         studentorgs.uaeu.ac.ae  and  will  not  have  individual  domain  names.                       (Example:
         studentorgs.uaeu.ac.ae/enviro.)
    h)   Conference websites will be assigned URLs beginning with conferences.uaeu.ac.ae and
         will not have individual domain names. (Example: conferences.uaeu.ac.ae/mathday.)


5. Requesting a Domain Name
    To apply for a new domain name, check the services section on DoIT web site. All domain
    name requests are reviewed by DoIT.


6. Revocation of a Domain Name
    a)   Any subdomains registered under uaeu.ac.ae that do not conform to this policy will be
         reviewed. UAEU reserves the right to revoke any previously assigned subdomain name
         at any time if it conflicts with this Policy or other UAEU Policies, priorities or interests.
    b)   If a domain name is deemed to be in conflict with this Policy, DoIT will send a Notice
         of Revocation to the owner of the subdomain name, who will have                           four  weeks to
         conform to the requested changes.
