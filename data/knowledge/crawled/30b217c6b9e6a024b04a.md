## PDF page 3
The College of Humanities and Social Sciences at United Arab Emirates University                                                                                  is committed to fostering
intellectual curiosity, critical inquiry, and impactful research that addresses both local and global societal
challenges. The year 2024 has been marked by a wealth of scholarly activity, as our faculty and students have
pursued innovative projects spanning disciplines such as history, literature, sociology, political science,
cognitive sciences and beyond. This report offers a comprehensive over view of these efforts, highlighting the
ways in which our research not only advances academic knowledge but also contributes to the cultural, social,
and economic development of the United Arab Emirates and the wider region.

By bringing together diverse perspectives and methodologies, our college continues to play a pivotal role in
contributing to national development and enriching the academic landscape. The research featured (  Q1, Q2)
in    this    report    reﬂects    our    dedication    to    excellence,    collaboration,    and    the    pursuit    of    solutions    to    real-world
problems. Each project underscores our belief that the humanities and social sciences are essential to
understanding the complexities of contemporary society and to building a more inclusive and sustainable
future. As you explore the achievements of the past year, we invite you to consider the profound impact of our
collective work and the ongoing mission of the College of Humanities and Social Sciences at United Arab
Emirates University                                                                                           .
