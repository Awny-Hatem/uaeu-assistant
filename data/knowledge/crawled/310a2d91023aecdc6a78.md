## Frequently Asked Questions > What is the minimum Grade Point Average (GPA) required from a transfer applicant to be considered for admission?
The student must be in Good Academic Standing.

Yes

## Frequently Asked Questions > What is the difference between withdrawal from semester and withdrawal from university?
Withdrawal from a semester is a postponement of study for one term only, then The student can register for the following terms, while withdrawal from the university ends student study in all and can’t register to follow up the study.

## Frequently Asked Questions > Will my application be considered without my TOEFL, IELTS, EmSAT or SAT score?
Yes

## Frequently Asked Questions > How can a student print the class schedule, the Final Exam schedule and the study plan?
This is all available in the student registration report "اشعار تسجيل" that is available for them to request.

## Frequently Asked Questions > What steps should I follow to change my major?
The student has one chance to change major by personal wish “and one time fir probation students to enhance their GPA”. The student should check the major requirements that he/she meets them then apply to the college during the times announced by the college to apply fir majors.

## Frequently Asked Questions > If I select HCT or ZU as my first choice in the NAPO application, can I later change my mind?
Changes can be made during the service period of modifying educational institution and program options

## Frequently Asked Questions > When will I be informed if I am accepted at UAEU?
The student is notified after completing the application review procedures.

## Frequently Asked Questions > Is UAEU recognized internationally?
Yes
