## Frequently Asked Questions
How to become an Audit Learner?

To become an auditor the student must visit UAEU website or contact Continuing Education Center 037135555 .

What are the procedures for withdrawal?

The Auditor should contact the Continuing Education Center to submit their withdrawal request.

Are Auditors required to take quizzes and exams?

Auditors are not required to take any quizzes or exams their fellow classmate students take.

What is the attendance Policy?

There will be an attendance sheet for every Auditor, they will receive their attendance sheet when their registration is accepted. An attending certificate from the Continuing Education Center (CEC) at the end of each course is to be issued if the student attended at least 75% of the classes and submitted the attendance sheet signed by the instructor.

What are the payment methods?

After being registered the auditor will receive an online payment link, the auditor then will have 24 hours to pay for the course after receiving the email. If the payment link is not working, please contact the Continuing Education Center (CEC) with the issue.

How to get the Audit Program certificate?

To receive an Audit Program Certificate the auditor should attend at least 75% of the classes and submit the attendance sheet signed by the instructor.

Can an auditor take more than one course?

Yes, there are no limits for classes but acceptance by the Continuing Education Center (CEC) should be ensured first.

Where is my class being held?

To determine the location of an Auditor’s class, check the UAEU Campus Map .
