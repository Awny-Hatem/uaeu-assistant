MATU1415 - Algebra (0 credit hours) This mobile-learning enhanced course is designed to develop proficiencies in algebra necessary for success in future Math or Statistics courses. It covers the following topics: arithmetic and algebraic expressions; linear equations and inequalities in one variable; coordinate geometry; linear equations in two variables; quadratic equations (formulas and factoring); rational expressions; definition of functions and graphs of basic functions; and applications. Prerequisite: ENGU 1301 Prerequisite: ENGU1301 or ENGU1301X or ENGU1301 Corequisite: Semester: All Go To Index MATU1425 - College Algebra (0 credit hours) This mobile-learning enhanced course is designed to develop the proficiencies in college algebra necessary for success in future Statistics or Calculus courses. It covers the following topics: complex numbers; factorials; properties of functions, such as graphs and transformations; inverse functions; composition of functions; polynomial functions (definition, zeros, factors and graphs); exponential and logarithmic functions and equations; basics of arithmetic and geometric sequences and series; basics of matrices and determinants; and applications. Prerequisite: MATU1415X or MATU1415ENGU1301 or ENGU1301X or ENGU1301 Corequisite: Semester: All Go To Index MATU1435 - Trigonometry (0 credit hours) This mobile-learning enhanced course is designed to develop the proficiencies in trigonometry necessary for success in future Calculus courses. It covers the following topics: angles and their measures; trigonometry of right triangles; unit circle in trigonometry, trigonometric functions, including properties and graphs; trigonometric identities and equations; and applications. Prerequisite: MATU1425X or MATU1425ENGU1301X or ENGU1301 or ENGU1301 Corequisite: Semester: All Go To Index
