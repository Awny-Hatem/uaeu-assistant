10. Student Fees and Accounts

Overview
Covers policies and procedures relating to the management of tuition and fees.

Scope
Applies to all students and all academic and related administrative units of the University.

Objective
Ensures that the tuition and fee assessment and student accounts functions of the University
are conducted in a manner that is consistent with academic principles, standards, and
expectations of the University appropriate to its educational mission and academic programs,
and operates with necessar y integrity, fairness, consistency, and effectiveness.

Policy
1.                                                      Within the context and limits of applicable UAE federal laws, the University is authorized
      to assess and collect tuition and fees for various academic and related services               as
      approved by the Signatory Authority           . Tuition is charged for all instruction to graduate
      students.  Tuition and fee schedules are published on the UAEU website.
2.                                        Tuition charges are due and payable in full at the specified deadlines of each academic
      semester. The final responsibility for payment of tuition and fees charges    rests with the
      individual student, and the student will be held responsible individually for all properly
      documented financial liabilities to the University.

Procedures of Policy No. (10)  - Student Fees and Accounts

1. Tuition and Fees
     a)          The University will publish annually a Schedule of Tuition and Fees including
           relevant applicability and amounts.
           (i)                      Tuition is assessed on a per credit hour basis at rates established by the
                    University.  Additional fees may be assessed for textbooks. Tuition charges are
                    based on student classification, residency, academic program, and degree level.
           (ii)                     Fees are charges for specific academic, student support, and related services
                    provided by or through the University, including but not limited to, application
                    processing, enrollment functions and services, special course activities and
                    services, housing, dining, direct services for individual students, academic
                    records services, tuition installment payment fees, fines and late processing/late
                    action fees, refundable and non-refundable deposits, textbooks, and other fees as
                    specified by the University.
           (iii)                  In the case of courses offered for zero (0) credit hours, tuition and fee charges
                    equivalent to the instructional time of the course will apply.
     b)             Changes in the published Schedule of Fees may be made at any time upon approval
           according to the Signatory Authority.


2. Payment of Tuition and Fees
     a)                     Tuition charges are due and payable in full, according to the relevant deadlines in each
           academic semester in the Schedule of Tuition and Fees.
     b)              Students paying all or a portion of their tuition charges directly (“self-pay” students)

           are eligible to participate in an installment payment program provided that they make
           the required initial payment by the initial payment deadline and follow the installment
           plan.
     c)          Students claiming external sponsorship for the payment of tuition charges must
           provide written confirmation of the sponsorship as specified in the Schedule of Tuition
           and Fees not later than the initial payment deadline. Sponsored students who do not
           submit the required confirmation of sponsorship and continue in enrollment will
           assume personal responsibility for all tuition charges and applicable fees.
     d)           Students who fail to pay all applicable tuition charges by the established payment
           deadline(s) or who are late in paying their  fees may be subject to denial of academic
           services, required to pay in advance fees in full for future semesters, and/or
           cancellation of current and/or future registration.
     e)            Unless otherwise specified, fees are due and payable at the time the services are
           provided or within (30) days of billing:
           (i)                      Application processing fees, selected individual services fees, and required
                    enrollment and housing deposits are due and payable in advance. Applications
                    for admission will not be processed and speci fied individual services will not be
                    provided in the absence of the required fees, and students who have not paid any
                    required enrollment and housing deposits will be denied the associated services.
