## Wellbeing Society > Overview
The Wellbeing society is an initiative of the Emirates Center for Happiness Research to engage students to carry the center s vision and mission of promoting happiness and wellbeing forward.

This society will be a great medium for the students to engage more students and spread happiness and positivity through various innovative ideas within the University community.
