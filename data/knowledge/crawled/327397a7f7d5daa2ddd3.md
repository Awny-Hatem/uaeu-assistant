## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Companies Service
Al Foah Experimental Farm does not affiliate with commercial entities. However, the farm is happy to host activities of companies that are in line with its educational and research goals, and involve our students, and/or UAEU faculty members.

Services to companies include:

Joint development and test of new technologies

Product tests

Cultivar tests

Demonstration plots

For further information on farm services please contact us .
