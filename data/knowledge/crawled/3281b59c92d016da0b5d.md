ENROLLMENT DATA (2021-2022)

     The following shows the enrollment data of the College of Education initial licensure-level programs:
Early Childhood Education (ECE), Special Education (SPED), and Health & Physical Education (HPE).


     A.   Program Enrollment Trend from 2018 to 2022

                    An increasing overall enrollment trend is observed from Fall 2018 to Fall 2022 in the
          initial licensure-level programs of the EPP. An average of 1% increase in the last five years was
          observed with an average enrollment of 212 in ECE is 212, 135 in SPED, and 32 in HPE. The
          average enrollment from Fall 2018 to Fall 2022 in the ECE was maintained, a 1% increase rate
          was observed in SPED, while a 10% increase rate was observed in HPE (see Figure 1).
