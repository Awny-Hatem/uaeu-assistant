## Vision
Achieve excellence in the quality and educational effectiveness of academic programs at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU).

## Mission
Make a substantial positive contribution to the quality and educational effectiveness of academic programs at UAEU by applying best practices in program development and review, fostering the culture of outcomes-based education and promoting learning outcomes assessment awareness within the university community, and implementing state-of-the-art curriculum support systems.

## Goals
Ensure that academic programs are developed in accordance with UAEU, national and international standards.

Implement advanced electronic systems for facilitating curriculum development, review, and management as well as the assessment of learning outcomes.

Create and maintain a learning outcomes assessment culture and infrastructure at UAEU.

Create and maintain outcomes-based periodic program review process and infrastructure at UAEU.
