## Vision, Mission and Objective
Vision:

To be a premier global laboratory that excelling in Health Data Science.

Mission:

To lead in the development of innovative technologies and holistic talents in Health Data Science for improving health care and well-being of the UAE society.

Goal:

To act as a focal point for leading and coordinating Health Data Science research at CMHS, UAE University, and UAE as well.

To support education in Health Data Science.

To create strategic partnerships at the national and international levels.

To develop innovative solutions and products for health sectors and society.
