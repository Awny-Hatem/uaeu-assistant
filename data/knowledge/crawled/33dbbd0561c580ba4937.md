Statistical Consulting Unit

                                                  Workshops/Training courses

                                                               Fall 2022

                                                            Workshop #3

Title: Data Visualization using Tableau

Statistical Software: Tableau

Requirements: No prior knowledge of Tableau required

Instructor: Dr. Kursad Asdemir

Target Group: professionals and students interested in data visualization

Period: Two days,        4 hours/day

Date: 31 Oct. 2022 - 6 Nov. 2022

Platform: Online/Zoom Meeting

Inclusive of Scientific Materials: Seminars include live lectures, hands-on exercise assignments, and
plenty of chances to ask questions. PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.

Description:

Tableau is a popular, easy-to-use data visualization software. The aim of this workshop is to develop
participants' knowledge, skills, and capacity to utilize Tableau in creating attractive visualizations with little
effort & time.

Topics Covered:

1) Import data from various sources (flat files, database, or other formats). 2) Dimensions & measures,
3) Tableau user interface, 4) Simple visualizations, 5) Aggregate functions & calculated fields &
parameters, 6) Maps, and 7) Dashboards.

Workshop Fee: 1000 AED

Note: A certificate of completion of the workshop will be provided to participants.



Contact US: statconsult@uaeu.ac.ae
