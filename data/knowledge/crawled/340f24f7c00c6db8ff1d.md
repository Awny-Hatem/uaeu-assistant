Session - NML Databases Worksheet

    1.  The three Boolean operators are “AND,” “OR,” “____.”

    2.  Complete this sentence: If your search phrase is "heart disease NOT
        emphysema," you will retrieve records to articles that do NOT discuss
        the topic ____________.

    3.  Complete this sentence: To search for an exact phrase in PubMed or
        Academic Search complete, enclose the phrase in double
        ______________.


    4.  How many publication types are available from the Academic Search
        Complete interface?  (Note: Do not count the option “All.”)


    5.  Using Academic Search Complete, how many results are received
        searching on the term “thalassemia” in the title field and limiting the
        publication type to “newspapers”?


    6.  How many of articles for the results received for Question 5 were
        published in the New York Times?


    7.  Searching Academic Search Complete and with the limits set to
           “References Available,”
           “Scholarly (Peer Reviewed) Journals”,
           “English,” and
           the years 1970 to 2005,

        how many results do you receive searching on the term “genetic code”
        in the “Subject Terms” field?


    8.  In Academic Search Complete, what is the last year for which full text
        articles are available for the journal Pediatrics?


    9.  In Academic Search Complete, what is the earliest year for which
        bibliographical records are available for the publication Saudi Journal
        of Gastroenterology?


    10. According to its record in Academic Search Complete, how many times
        a year is the Middle East African Journal of Ophthalmology published?
