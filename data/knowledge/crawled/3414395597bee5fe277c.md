  Instructions Regarding Registration:

            Summer semester study days will be 6 days a week (from Saturday to Thursday).
            Students in good academic standing may register for 6 Credit Hours or 2 courses.
            All Students under probation may register for no more than one course or 4 Credit Hours.
            Honors students and students who expect to graduate in summer 2017 may register for up to 9 credit hours.
