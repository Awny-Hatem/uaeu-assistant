Student Accounts Office
                    Guide for Paying Fees for Bachelor Program for Fall 2020/2021



 The Student Accounts Office (SAO), is responsible for the billing of all student fees and
charges, and is located on the First floor of Crescent Building, Wing D4, Room 1050,
Al Ain. Our working hours for student enquiries are 8am – 3pm, Sunday through Thursday.

SAO Contact information:

             o   Phone:  03 7134265, 037134287
             o   Email address:  Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae





 Payment method:
You can pay for the tuition via the e-payment (online Payment), please log on to this page
to pay: https://ssb.uaeu.ac.ae/prod/bwykstpy.p_display_opt Or through UAEU Application.



                      Deadline for Settlement of Tuition & Fees for Fall 2020/2021
   1st Payment Deadline (50% of total tuition fees )                       23 – 27 Aug 2020
   2nd Payment Deadline (Remaining 50% of total tuition fees)              30 Aug – 17 Sep 2020


** Non-Payment of total tuition fees on or before 17 Sep 2020 will result to withdrew
courses that student registered.
