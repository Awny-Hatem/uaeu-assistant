 Internal Transfer between colleges

 UAEU   regulations   allow   students   to   transfer
 internally from one       college to another only once
 during his/her enrollment at the University. The
 transfer  applications       will  be  submitted  online
 through  eServices        or  UAEU  mobile  application
 during  the  announced  dates  on  the  academic
 calendar. To accept a student’s transfer application
 from a college to another, he/she should meet the
 terms & conditions. Student need to contact the
 college    want  to  transfer  to      know    the  terms  &
 conditions.







Steps to apply for internal
transfer between colleges:

     1.    Go to UAEU portal https://my.uaeu.ac.ae
     2.    Choose “eServices”
     3.    Click on “Student”
     4.    Click on “Student College Transfer”
     5.    Below page will display transfer application and conditions.
     6.    Select college (First option) and Optional select (Second option).
     7.    Application will complete by clicking Submit
