                     iWIL Instrumental in New Partnership Agreement:
                 UAE University and Agthia Group (PJSC) sign MOU

Technical and educational cooperation between the two entities sought to encourage knowledge transfer
                       and more career development opportunities for national students

Al Ain, 21st December 2008: In a significant move that will
see closer cooperation to integrate UAE nationals in the
corporate sector, as well as greater exchange of ideas and
sharing of knowledge between the nation’s premier
educational institution and one of UAE’s homegrown,
publicly listed corporate entities, Al Ain based UAE
University and Agthia Group (PJSC) signed a
Memorandum of Understanding today to increase
cooperation in several areas of common interest.

                                                  Speaking at the announcement of the MOU,  H.E.                  Dr.
                                                  Abdullah Saad Al- Khanbashi, Vice Chancellor, UAE
                                                  University, said: “In today’s world, where success is often the
                                                  result of a combination of theoretical knowledge and sound,
                                                  practical skills, this step we have taken today, I am hopeful,
                                                  will result in a richer, broader experience for our students,
                                                  preparing them better for the corporate world once they
                                                  graduate.”


Briefing on the landmark agreement , H.E. Rashed Al Hajeri, Chairman of Agthia Group (PJSC) – the
group that controls Al Ain Mineral Water Company, Al Ain Vegetables and Grand Mills – said:
“Nationalization of the UAE’s workforce is a priority of the Federal Government as no true progress can
take place if the nation’s own citizens are unable to   participate in the country’s development. I am happy
that as Agthia, we are able to contribute to this great cause.”

## PDF page 2
Commenting on the MOU, Ilias Assimakopoulos, Chief Executive Officer, Agthia Group (PJSC), said:
“As Agthia Group (PJSC), we feel it is our responsibility to help nurture the next generation of managers
and technocrats, enabling them with cutting edge skills. Furthermore, we want to support growing local
talent to take up positions of responsibilities in the corporate world.”


To take this MOU forward, a committee will be formed by the University and the Agthia team. The joint
committee will collaborate in student placement in meaningful internship assignments and different areas
of graduate placement, supporting Graduate Studies Progr ams offered by the University which are of
common interest to both parties and undergoing joint research projects that aim at supporting and
revitalizing technological development.


In addition, the MOU envisages collaboration in education, student research, applied research and career
consultations. Cooperation in higher education studies for existing Agthia employees will be undertaken
as well. Other co-supported activities such as organizing educational conferences, workshops and
seminars that focus on latest educational developments are also part of the agreement.
