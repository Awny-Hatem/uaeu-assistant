## PDF page 5
Form Completion

     7. Before you begin filling out the  form, carefully read all instructions and guidelines at the top.
     This will help  you understand the  requirements   and avoid errors.  Next , click the  "Reason for
     Withdrawal" dropdown menu and select the most appropriate reason for your request from the
     available options. In the  " Reason" field (a required  field), provide a detailed  explanation for your
     withdrawal, adhering to the  300-character limit. Finally, click the  "                      Submit " button."
































Instructions
•                                                                         If the request is submitted within the first four weeks of classes, no grade will be recorded on the
          transcript .
•                                                                      If the request is submitted between the 5th and 6           th weeks of classes, a grade of "W " (Withdrawal) will be
          recorded on the transcript.
•                                                                            If the  request  is submitted after  6 weeks of classes a grade of "F " (Fail) will be recorded  on the
          transcript .
