         Instructions Regarding Registration (for Bachelor Students):















               Students who did not register without providing an excuse to the college, will not be allowed to
                register in the add and drop week.
               Student in good academic standing may register 12 to 19 credit hours.
               Students who are under Probation cannot register for more than 13 credit hours.
               Honors students and students who expect to graduate in Fall Semester 2015/2016 may register
                for up to 22 credit hours with college approval.
