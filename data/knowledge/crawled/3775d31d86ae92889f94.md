## Computer Science and Software Engineering > External Collaboration
Dr. Saad Harous Research Collaboration Visit to The National University of Singapore :

Dr. Saad Harous, an Associate Professor from the Department Computer Science and Software Engineering, visited the School of Computing at the National University of Singapore twice during the academic year 2016/2017. He initiated a research project in the field of HTTP adaptive streaming for video streaming with Dr. Roger Zimmermann . The aim of the project is to help content providers and network operators to maintain QoE at a high satisfactory level, leading to increase in their business. As a result of this collaboration, Dr. Harous has already published a paper entitled “SDNHAS: An SDN-Enabled Architecture to Optimize QoE in HTTP Adaptive Streaming”, IEEE Transactions on Multimedia .
