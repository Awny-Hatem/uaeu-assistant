## What software can I use to record myself and my slides?
We recommend using Panopto. This is a software which is used within Blackboard, and you can find out full details on its functionality here: https://www.panopto.com/features/

## How do I get access to Panopto?
Should you require a Panopto license, then please email CETL at cetl@uaeu.ac.ae and state which course you will be using the software for. This will then be processed and you will be informed when this license has been assigned to you.

## Do I have access to LinkedIn Learning?
Yes, all faculty have a license to access LinkedIn Learning. Simply login to https://my.uaeu.ac.ae/en/Pages/default.aspx with your UAEU ID and select LinkedIn Learning from the Apps Section.

## What is the process for starting to blend my course?
Each Semester the CETL department carries out a Blended Course Transformation Program, which consists of a 4 Week Training Program, assisted creation of blended course material and reviewing results of the transformation over an academic year. Should you wish to be a part of the next BCT Program, then please email CETL at cetl@uaeu.ac.ae to express your interest.

## What software do I need to record my screen?
We recommend Screen Cast-O-Matic ( https://screencast-o-matic.com/ ), which is a free piece of software to record your screen and also voice while you navigate to provide demonstration lessons.

## What software can I use to edit my videos?
We recommend using Shotcut ( https://shotcut.org/ ), which is a free and intuitive video editing software that provides a basic but effective set of features to meet the vast majority of requirements.

## How do I become a faculty fellow?
To become a member of the Faculty Fellow program, please email CETL at cetl@uaeu.ac.ae and advise what topics you would be looking to facilitate in the Further Professional Development.

## How many FPD sessions can I provide as a Faculty Fellow?
Between 2 and 3, depending on the schedule and topics being covered.

## How do I access the e-textbooks?
E-Textbooks are accessible on your Blackboard site, however, if you experience issues, then please email CETL at cetl@uaeu.ac.ae .

## What can I use for Gamification?
To incorporate Game elements into your courses, there are several resources you can use to facilitate this. The most popular are Quizlet ( https://quizlet.com/ ) and Kahoot ( https://kahoot.com/ ).
