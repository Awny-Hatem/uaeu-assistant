## Vision and Mission > Vision and Mission > Vision
Recognized national and global hub that fosters innovation and entrepreneurship that support boasting knowledge-based economy

## Vision and Mission > Vision and Mission > Mission
To lead innovation and foster an entrepreneurial ecosystem in the United Arab Emirates by building an effective and productive bridge between the Park and national and international stakeholders, supporting the development and commercialization of innovative products, and contributing to the growth of the business leaders of the future.

The UAEU Science and Innovation Park will address national weaknesses in global competitiveness in terms of innovation, market share and entrepreneurship. This will be done by enhancing start-up skills, understanding what constitutes acceptable risk taking, networking, use of modern technology and improving innovative processes.

## Vision and Mission > Vision and Mission > Areas of Focus
Leadership : To inspire, engage, coach and guide Emirati students, graduates from UAE universities and investors to play a leading role in the development of a knowledge-based economy through developing a training and mentoring program.

Commercialization : Support the commercialization of new ideas, products and inventions by providing emerging business talent with the skills, startup support and advice.

Partnerships : To become a bridge between stakeholders by developing a network of national and international partners.
