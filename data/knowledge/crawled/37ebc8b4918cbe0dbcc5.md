Change Specialization

Changing specialization will start from the date of (drop without failure ) period until the last week before early registration.

The students can request a change of specialization only once during their study

Changing specialization is allowed after spending at least one semester in the current specialization.

The steps to change specialization:

Students have to fill out the change of specialization form

Submit the form to the department chair secretary

Joining a Minor Program

College of IT students can join the following Minor Programs:

Minor in Artificial Intelligence | All IT majors can join a minor in AI, except the computer Science students.
Minor from BE college | All IT majors can take a minor in BE

A student should apply to join the minor program of their choice by filling out an application form.

With this application, a student should submit the minor program study plan to the academic advisor.

A student must be specialized in a major program before submitting this application.

A student must complete at most 72 hrs before applying to the minor program.

A student should finish his minor specialization requirements within or before the minor specialization requirements.

If the student decides not to complete all minor program requirements, the transcript will not include any previously completed minor program courses.

Completion of the minor is noted on the students' printed transcript only.

## UAEU Summer Courses
Students may register in no more than six (6) credit hours in the summer semester except for honors and are expected to graduate students who may register in up to nine (9) credit hours.

Students under probation can't register for more than four (4) credit hours.

## Outside the UAEU Summer Courses
Students may register in courses for degree credit at other institutions accredited by the UAE Ministry of higher education.

Only Courses with a final grade of C- or higher can be considered for transfer for degree credit.

Grades in courses completed at other institutions are not included in students' UAEU grade point average.

## How to register for courses outside UAEU
Students must submit to their department chair the subject's syllabus of the other institution's course for approval.

Students must Fill out the form for (Summer courses outside the UAEU University) and submit it to the Advising Unit

The form will be sent to the registration department to prepare an official form for the other

The students will submit the official form to the other institution to register the subject.

## Repeating Courses
A student must repeat a failed mandatory course itself, no equivalent or substitute course is allowed.

A student must repeat a failed elective course or substitute it with other elective courses from the study plan.

A student can repeat any passed course with a grade of C- or below given the following conditions:

The student GPA is less than 2.00

The student can repeat a maximum of two courses in one semester.

The student should repeat the same course

The student can repeat the course only once.
