## Website Information > Abbreviations & Glossary
Term and Glossary | Description
UAE | The United Arab Emirates
University, or UAEU | The United Arab Emirates University
University Council | The Governing Board of the United Arab Emirates
Chancellor | The Chancellor of UAE University
Vice Chancellor | The Vice Chancellor of the United Arab Emirates University
Provost | The Deputy Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs
Student | Any male or female student in the University
Faculty Members | Professors, Associate Professors, and Assistant Professors
Academic Department | The Academic Department in the colleges
University Community | All UAEU faculty members, staff, and students
Academic Year | A one-year period established annually in the official University calendar
Manual | A policies and procedures manual is a functional guide for conducting the operations of a division or a department. The manual typically consists of policies, procedures and signatory authority matrix
Policy | A definite course or method of action to guide and determine present and future decisions. A policy is a guide to decision-making under a given set of circumstances within the framework of corporate objectives, goals and management philosophies
Procedure | A particular way of accomplishing something, or an established way of doing things. A procedure is a series of steps followed in a definite regular order that ensures the consistent and repetitive approach to actions
Emsat | Emarati Standardized Test
NAPO | National admissions and placement office
GPA | Grade Point Average, a calculated value representing the student s academic performance for a specific period of time
CGPA | Cumulative Grade Point Average
Professional graduate program | A post-baccalaureate degree that prepares students for a particular profession by emphasizing skills and practical analysis over research
Blackboard | Blackboard - UAEU Online Education system, for students and faculty members
Degree works | Degree works is a web-based advising tool, it helps both students and their advisors successfully navigate curriculum requirements in order to achieve success throughout their educational life. The main goal of Degree Works is to help students graduate in time.
CEC | Continuing Education Center provides training courses, workshops, seminars and conferences.
