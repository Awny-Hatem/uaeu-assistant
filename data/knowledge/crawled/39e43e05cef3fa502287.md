1.                   Preamble
The United Arab Emirates University is establishing a single Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) Program to
realize His Highness Sheikh Nahayan Mabarak Al Nahayan’s , Chancellor of the United Arab
Emirates University, vision to transform the University into one that is both research intensive and is
internationally-recognized. A PhD Program of the  highest quality and international standing is a key
element in this transformation.

2.                       Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the  overarching principles for the establishment and
management of the PhD Program. Detailed administrative processes are described in the PhD
Program Administrative Procedures.

3.                   Scope
All PhD programs at the United Arab Emirates  University are covered by this policy. Masters
Programs are not covered by this policy.

4.                   Principles
    4.1.         The University of the United Arab Emirates will have a single PhD program;

5.                   Implementation
     5.1.  The Board of Graduate Studies
          5.1.1. The Board retains the authority for all decisi ons regarding, but not limited to, admission,
                  supervision, and candidacy.

          5.1.2. The Board will comprise of no less than 6 a nd no more than 10 Faculty nominated by
                  the Provost, in consultation with the D eans and will be chaired by the Provost.

          5.1.3. The Provost may delegate the chairmanship of the Board.

     5.2.  Candidacy
          5.2.1. Applicants for admission to the PhD Program will be required to meet minimum
                  disciplinary requirements for advanced research in the field of study and the proposed
                  dissertation topic.

          5.2.2. Applicants will be required to submit the following:
                •                                        Academic record (transcripts and GPA)
                •                                        TOFEL score for English as a Second Language applicants
                •                                        Graduate Record Exam (GRE) score
                •                                        Referee reports
                •                                        Dissertation Proposal

          5.2.3. Candidates will be proposed to the Bo ard for enrolment by an academic host
                  Department of the University, and the Board  will consider both the qualifications of the
                  candidate and the suitability of the r esearch supervisor(s) and thesis committee
                  members, each of whom will also act as an  examiner, as well as the proposed courses of
                  study and area of research, in determining whether or not to approve the proposed
                  enrolment.

          5.2.4. Students accepted into the program will  advance to candidacy only following
                  satisfactory completion of qualifying (pre liminary) examinations and recommendation
                  to the Board by the host Department.

          5.2.5. Candidature for the degree will be full-time.

          5.2.6. Candidates will, absent an exception being  granted by the Board based on proof of
                  accomplishment, undertake coursework equivalent to at least one year of full-time study
                  to a level of attainment determined by the Board for each candidate in both general,
                  research-related and in discipline-specific areas.

     5.3.  Supervision
          5.3.1. Thesis supervision will be provided by a primary research supervisor and a thesis
                  committee

          5.3.2. The thesis committee will be made up of at least four members, and will include the
                  primary research supervisor (who will be a member of the host Department) as chair.

     5.4.  Progress
          5.4.1. Progress requirements will be defined and documented at the time of admission by the
                 Board following a proposal by the Supervisor.

          5.4.2. Progress will be reviewed by the Board annually and will be assessed on submissions by
                 the supervisor and the candidate. Failure to  meet progress requirements will require the
                 candidate to show cause why candidature should not be terminated.

          5.4.3. Where the Board determines unsatisfactory progress candidature will be terminated.

     5.5.  Admission to the Degree
          5.5.1. Admission to the degree will require submission of a thesis, in a form to be approved by
                 the Board, and a unanimous recommendation in the form of a summary report to the
                 Board by the thesis committee that the thesis be approved.

          5.5.2. Prior to a recommendation to the Board by  the thesis committee, each candidate will
                 undertake a public thesis defense conducted by the host Department.

          5.5.3. The Board will, based on consideration of  the thesis committee’s report, recommend
                 candidates to the Vice Chancellor for the award of the degree.
