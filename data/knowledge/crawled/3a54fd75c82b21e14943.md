Ph.D. Studentship at the Department of Mechanical Engineering





       Positions               for               a               Ph.D.               student               is               available               in               the               Department               of Mechanical

Engineering         at         the         College         of         Engineering,         United         Arab         Emirates University. The

research            is            focusing            on            pulsed            laser            ablation            process            to            produce            colloidal            alloys

nanoparticles.                   Candidate                   must                   have                   obtained                   MSc                   degree                   in                   material                   science,

mechanical                                engineering,                                or                                related                                fields.                                Experience                                in                                fabrications                                and

characterization of nanomaterials is a plus. The candidate must register for his PhD for

fall    2022    in    Mechanical    Engineering-UAEU.    The    requirements    to    be    accepted    to    the

UAEU Ph.D. program are:

                                          A minimum GPA of 3.0 out of 4 with MSc or a minimum GPA of 3.7 with BSc

                                          A minimum IELTS score of 6.5

                                          Two letters of recommendation

       If     accepted,     the     Ph.D.     candidate      may     be     supported     by     the     UAE     University     for     4

years    with    stipend,    full     tuition    fee    waiver,    and    health    insurance    (for    more    detail    see:

http://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/cgs/scholarship.shtml). Only highly qualified candidates will

be contacted. Interested candidates can send CVs to ahmourad@uaeu.ac.ae.
