## Happiness Research Talks > Social Trust: The first Vaccines against Covid 19
This talk was delivered by Mr. Francesco Sarracino and highlighted Covid-19 as an extreme example of the threat posed by other environmental emergencies, such as climate change, that have less apparent or immediate consequences. In this talk he mentioned how trust is the first vaccine against pandemics and proposed some ideas for a socially and environmentally sustainable future.
