Excellence Award
                                                                                      In Students Affairs






Award Categories:

1.                                                             Excellent Campus Partner for Students Affairs.
2.                                      The Most Influential Employee in Students Affairs.
3.                                                             University Loyalty in Students Affairs.
4.                                  Distinguished Students Affairs Leadership.
5.                                                             Students Excellent Service.

General rules of the award:
•                                                     The employee has the right to apply for one category only.
•                                                     Submission is through the electronic link of the award.
•                                                     In case of applying for the award as a team, the submission
       is through the team leader.
•                                                     The nominated or winning application shall be canceled if it
       is proved that any of the evidences presented is incorrect.
