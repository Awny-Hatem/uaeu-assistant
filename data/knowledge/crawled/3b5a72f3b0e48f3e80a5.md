## Department of Radiology > Teaching
All aspects of diagnostic radiology are taught to undergraduates with the emphasis on integration with other specialties during the Organ Systems Course and various radiology, lectures, practical, tutorial andand conferences during the Clinical Sciences Course. The Department is also taking an increasing part in continuing and graduate medical education with regular radiological conferences in Surgery, Paediatrics, Medicine, ICU, ER and Oncology.
