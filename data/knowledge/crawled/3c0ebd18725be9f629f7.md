## Provide Medical Care Service for Students
Through this service, UAEU provides students with the highest quality healthcare through clinics located In- Campus, off- Campus and government hospitals located in Al Ain city as well. The team of health professionals are committed to overall well-being of students during their stay at UAEU. Strives to provide a comprehensive and valuable primary healthcare service and deliver educational and promotional programs to the students as per best international standards.
