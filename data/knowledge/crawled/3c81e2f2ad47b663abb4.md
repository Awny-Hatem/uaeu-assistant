## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > Activities for Students ​ > Extracurricular Student Research Groups
Extracurricular research groups offer undergraduate students of various disciplines an opportunity to get together, engage in research activities, and learn something new. Though Faculty support and advice is provided, student working groups have the freedom to discuss and plan research activities with as much independence as possible. Students interested in joining a research group should send an email to ARIFSID@uaeu.ac.ae

## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > Activities for Students ​ > Learn & Help Opportunities for Students
Learn & Help activities are opportunities for UAEU and KU students of various disciplines to help with the conduction of research activities and learn something new at the same time. These activities are usually restricted to the duration of a few hours or days. ​ https://volunteer.uaeu.ac.ae/app.jsp#/
