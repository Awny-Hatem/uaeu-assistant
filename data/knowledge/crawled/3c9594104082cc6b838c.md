## Parenting Project
The parenting project is an initiative by the Emirates Center for Happiness Research to help working parents learn parenting skills which can reduce their stress levels and help enhance their occupational wellbeing. As part of this project, training workshops were conducted covering topics of positive parenting and the impact it has on family wellbeing and cohesion of community. These workshops have also covered risk factors for children especially those that can hinder the child s wellbeing and it briefly touched on the alarming signals as parents need to be known in case if the child is experiencing any psychological problem.
