## Universities Challenges in Food Security and Sustainability > Submission Requirements
Participant Information :

The form should include fields for participants (graduate and undergraduate student + Team leader) to provide the following details:

First Name

Last Name

Email Address

Phone Number

University Name (Team Leader and 2-5 Members)

Abstract Submission :

Participants must submit an abstract (in .docx or .pdf format) outlining their innovative solutions for food security and sustainability.

The abstract should not exceed 350 words and must briefly describe the problem being addressed, the proposed solution, and its potential impact.

3. Poster Presentation :

Accepted abstract participants are required to prepare a physical poster with dimensions of 90 cm X 100 cm (W x L). They must bring their poster on the day of the event, Oct 22nd, 2025, for display during a special session. Three Judges will assess each poster separately during this session.
