Regulations for Principal Investigators Awarded Funding through
the Zayed Bin Sultan Center for Health Sciences

This document sets forth the regulations and requirements that Principal Investigators (PIs) must comply
with when receiving research funding support from the Zayed Bin Sultan Center for Health Sciences (ZCHS).
These rules are designed to ensure accountability, transparency, and alignment with the Center’s strategic
objectives.

General Responsibilities of PIs:
     •    Ensure that the funded research project is conducted in accordance with the approved proposal,
          objectives, and deliverables submitted to and approved by ZCHS.
     •    Maintain the highest standards of research integrity, ethics, and compliance with UAEU and
          national regulations.
     •    Manage funds responsibly and solely for the purposes outlined in the approved budget.

Financial Accountability
     •    Funds must be used exclusively for activities approved in the grant budget.
     •    Only a PI may initiate requests related to the grant.
     •    A PI should keep financial records of all project expenditures. The budget should be used as per
          the approved budget in the project contract and according to the UAEU Policies and Procedures
          on Finance, Procurement, and Human Resources.

Non-Compliance
Failure to comply with these regulations will result in:
     1.   Immediate suspension of funding support, including publication fee coverage.
     2.   Exclusion from eligibility for future funding calls through ZCHS.
     3.   Reporting of non       -compliance to      Associate Provost for       Research Office for further action.

Effective Date
These regulations are effective as of          01. Oct.   2025 and apply to all new and ongoing projects funded by
ZCHS. Conti    nuous updates to the rules and regulations will be applied.
