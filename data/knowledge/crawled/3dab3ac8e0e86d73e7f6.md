                                               Professional Development for Staff Appointments


I.                                                                                             Policy

                      The University encourages the personal and professional development of its
                      employees.                  To this end, the University will provide job-specific training
                      opportunities to enable staff to perform better in their present jobs and general
                      development opportunities to enhance employees’ overall current and future
                      performance.


II.                                                                             Application

                      This policy shall apply to all non-faculty employees at the United Arab
                      Emirates University.


III.                                                           Related Policies



IV.                                                              Administration

                      Questions concerning the interpretation and administration of this policy are
                      to be directed to the Director, Human Resources.
