## Covid-19 Information > CMHS Community
COVID-19 safety and precautionary measures have been put in place at the University in line with the Ministry of Education and Department of Health.

UAEU is currently following the National Emergency Crisis and Disaster Management Authority (NCEMA) to ensure a healthy and safe education environment.

The University requires every student to take a mandatory COVID-19 Prevention Training Course with the certification before school resumption.

Classes are being delivered mostly online, with few that are on site.

For onsite learning, students have been divided into batches and are alternating coming on campus. This is to achieve the requirement for social distancing

Safety monitoring measures are in place to ensure all staff and students follow the COVID-19 prevention protocols

Free COVID-19 testing is provided every 2 weeks for Faculty, Staff and students
