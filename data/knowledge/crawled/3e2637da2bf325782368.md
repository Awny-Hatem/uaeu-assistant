## Admissions > Student Exchange Unit
The Undergraduate Student Exchange Program at United Arab Emirates University enables undergraduate students from UAEU and partner universities to enroll in subjects at the host institution for credits, which will count towards their home institution degree. UAEU welcomes international students from our partner institutions. This adds to the cultural diversity of UAEU where students of 60+ different nationalities are currently studying. We offer students an exciting opportunity to study and work with others while growing both personally and professionally. Our students leave UAEU with broader horizons, many lifelong friendships, cultural sensitivity and global awareness.
