## College of Information Technology > Dean's Message
The College of Information Technology (CIT) is a vibrant community for bright students to experience immersive learning from award class faculty members and to conduct scholarly research at state of the art facilities.

Our world-class graduates have made us proud of their achievements and influential positive contribution to the UAE and the world.

Our faculty members lead the way in up-to-date teaching and learning methodologies and in conducting cutting edge research published in top ranked international scientific venues.

Our students consistently demonstrate their academic credentials, innovation and leadership through participating in national and international conferences, winning competitions and registering inventions.

Our well-designed curricula are unique in that they are infused with state-of-the-art technology and cater for the growing needs of our great and beloved nation in the new era of 4 th Industrial Revolution and Artificial Intelligence.

I invite you to explore our website and encourage you to visit us. Once you discover our state-of-the art facilities, see our high-tech labs and teaching technologies, meet our motivated students, accomplished faculty and dedicated and supportive staff, you’ll decide that the CIT at UAEU is the place for you.

Prof. T aieb Znati

Dean, College of Information Technology
