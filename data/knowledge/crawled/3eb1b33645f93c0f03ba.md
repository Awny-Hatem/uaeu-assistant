## Format and Activities
The two-day Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair is an ongoing event. Many national sports authorities and organizations will be invited to participate, providing a unique platform for knowledge exchange, collaboration, and community engagement.

Abstract Submission:

Researchers are invited to submit abstracts for consideration. Accepted submissions will be showcased through poster presentations highlighting the latest developments and innovations in sports science, exercise physiology, biomechanics, and related fields.

Panels and Workshops:

The fair will feature thematic panel discussions and interactive workshops covering topics such as coaching, performance optimization, injury prevention, sports technology, and talent development. These sessions provide participants with hands-on learning experiences and practical insights that can be applied in professional practice.

Community Engagement:

A key feature of the fair is its focus on community participation. Attendees will have opportunities to engage in sport games, competitions, and wellness initiatives , promoting health, inclusion, and accessibility in physical activity. The fair bridges the gap between research, professional practice, and community involvement.
