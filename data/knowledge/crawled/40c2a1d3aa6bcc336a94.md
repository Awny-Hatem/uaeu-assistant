International Journal for Research in Education (IJRE), the Journal of the College of Education, was established at the United Arab Emirates University. It is a bilingual (Arabic and English), scholarly, refereed journal that has developed to serve educational researchers, practitioners, policy-makers and administrators in the United Arab Emirates and the Arab world.
