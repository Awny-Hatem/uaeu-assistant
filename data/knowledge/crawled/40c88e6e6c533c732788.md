International Student Admission Requirements for Undergraduate
                                                  Programs
                                               AY 2016/2017


Admission Details:

1.  International Students are eligible to be admitted to the United Arab Emirates University
    (UAEU) and on a provisional basis to the following Colleges during the AY 2016/2017, in
    accordance with the criteria and conditions set forth by UAEU and subject to the enrollment
    limits established for the University and each College:

    College of Humanities and Social Sciences, College of Science, College of Education,
    College of Business & Economics, College of Law, College of Food and Agriculture,
    College of Engineering and College of Information Technology,

    Admission to UAEU is based on the student’s academic preparation and performance levels,
    and the instructional capacities of academic programs.  Applicants’ eligibility for admission
    will be based on their Grade 12 Grade Point Average.
2.  Students admitted to the University shall be assigned provisionally to the various Colleges,
    in  accordance  with  the  instructional  capacity  available  in  each  College,  preferences
    expressed by each student and the academic qualifications of the student.  In the case of equal
    qualifications, preference shall be made           based on student grade averages in           the  subjects
    qualifying for admission to the specific College.
3. Students holding a National Grade 12 Certificate - Literary/Arts Track, or its equivalent, may
    apply for admission to the following Colleges: Humanities & Social Sciences, Education,
    Business & Economics and Law.
4. Students holding a National Grade 12 Certificate -              Scientific Track, or its equivalent may
    apply for admission to        the following Colleges:       Humanities & Social Sciences, Science,
    Education, Law, Business & Economics, Food & Agriculture, Engineering and Information
    Technology.
5. All  international     applicants  should  apply  online  through  the  following               link:  Online
    Admissions Application.

Admissions Requirements

1.  The following terms and conditions apply:
       Applicants must hold a National Grade 12 Certificate or its equivalent obtained from a
        school in UAE, or its equivalent dated 2015/2016. (Current High School Graduate)
       Applicants should have achieved scores of TOEFL 500/ iBT 61;Or IELTS 5.0
       Applicants should pass the Arabic and Math foundation level exams
2.  Applicants applying to the College of Engineering must hold a Grade 12 Certificate with a
    grade average of 95% or higher plus an average of 85% or higher in Mathematics and 85%
    in either Physics or Chemistry.

3.  Below please find the minimum average required for each college:


                              College                      Minimum Average Required
              Humanities & Social Sciences                                85%
              Science                                                     85%
              Education                                                   85%
              Business & Economics                                        90%
              Law                                                         85%
              Food & Agriculture                                          85%
              Engineering                                                 95%
              Information Technology                                      95%

Documents Required:

1.  The original or certified copy of the applicants Grade 12 Certificate obtained from a school
    in UAE, or its equivalent.
2.  A copy of a valid passport for National and GCC Countries.
3.  A copy of a valid passport for other nationalities is required including a copy of a valid
    residency visa.
4.  A copy of the applicant’s birth certificate or a certified copy of a birth certificate, or other
    certificate indicating the student’s age.
5.  A copy of identity card.
6.  A copy of medical insurance card.
7.  A medical fitness certificate including blood test report.
8.  Four recent colored passport size photos.
9.  A written statement in which a student vows to abide by the University’s bylaws, policies,
    and code of ethics.
10. A non-refundable online application fee of AED 200.
11. Evidence of English language proficiency certificate with a minimum of TOEFL IBT (61),
    TOEFL local (500) or IELTS (Academic 5.0).
12. An equivalency letter for Grade 12 high school certificate from the Ministry of Education
    (for schools that do not follow the Government curriculum).
