                                         LESSONS FROM
                                         SOCIAL ENTREPRENEURS
                                         IN THE CIRCULAR ECONOMY

                                         The  Circular  Economy     (CE)  is an  area  of growing    interest  in the  GCC.   Despite   ongoing   CE
                                         initiatives and  projects  in the  region,  potential  market   opportunities    are  far from  being  fully
                                         exploited.  The  CE  is gaining  ground    as a new   economic    development     model   that  supports   a
                                         more   efficient  utilization  of  resources   and   can  support    economic    diversification   and   the
                                         development      of  new    industrial   and   service-led   activities,  such    as  recycling   and    the
                                         production   of bio-based    products.

                                         Various   projects  have   seen   the  light in  the  GCC   that   go  beyond    the  conventional    linear
                                         economic    model    of  “take,  make,    use,  and   waste”.   The   CE  models    are   now   embracing
                                         elements   such   as reduce,   refurbish,  repair, reuse,  and   recycle. Some    involve  even  more    R’s.
                                         Social  entrepreneurs    are  embracing    the  tenets  of  the CE   and  are doing   well by  doing   good
                                         through    building  businesses    to  deal   with  plastic,  food  waste,   wastewater,    and   waste   in
                                         general.

                                         This webinar    hosts  various  social entrepreneurs     that  willshare  their  stories  of development,
                                         their visions, challenges,   and  successes,   allwithin  the  context  of the  CE.

                                         Attendees can expect to gain insights into the following:
                                              The importance of the Circular Economy
                                              The journeys of the social entrepreneurs
                                              Crystalizing lessons for the road ahead
