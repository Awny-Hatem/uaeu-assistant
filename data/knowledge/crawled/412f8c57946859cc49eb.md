Department of Statistics and Business Analytics
                                       Statistical Consulting Unit
                                      Workshops/Training courses
Title: Data Visualization using Tableau
Statistical Software: Tableau
Requirements: No prior knowledge of Tableau is required
Instructor: Dr. Kursad Asdemir (Tableau Certified Data Analyst)
Target Group: Professionals, faculty members, undergraduate, and graduate students
Period: Two days, 4 hours/day
Date: 7-13 April, 2025
Platform: Face-to-face Meeting
Inclusive of Scientific Materials: PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.
Description:
Public  and  private  organizations  have  huge  amounts  of data.  There  is  a  great  need  for
individuals who can make sense of this data.  Tableau Desktop is a powerful data visualization
platform which can produce meaningful information from very large datasets.  It is intuitive,
easy-to-use, and produces appealing visualizations.  The ability to use Tableau Desktop is a
vital skill of future managers and  analysts who want success in using data to improve
performance and a valuable skill to include in your CV as a job seeker.
Topics Covered:
Day 1:
1) Dimensions & measures,
2) Tableau user interface,
3) Basic charts.
Day 2:
4) Aggregate functions & calculated fields & parameters,
5) Maps, and Dashboards.
Workshop Fee: AED 1,000
