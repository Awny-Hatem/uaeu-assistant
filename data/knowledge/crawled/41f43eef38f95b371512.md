## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Advisory Board
Al Foah Experimental Farm is proud to have gained a number of senior scientists and highly qualified experts from within and outside of the UAEU as Advisory Board Members.

The Advisory Board provides recommendations on important decisions concerning the Farm. It also helps the Farm to direct its future development towards the demand of the national agricultural sector for research, education and innovation.
