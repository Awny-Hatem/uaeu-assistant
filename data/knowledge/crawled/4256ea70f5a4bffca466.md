Achievement Report
                                                       Prof. Abdel-Hamid Mourad

     I received my B.Sc. degree in Mechanical Engineering (1981) from Helwan University, Cairo, Egypt. I worked as a
demonstrator in the department (Sept. 1981-Oct. 1984), then worked as assistant lecturer from October 1984 up to May
1989. I received my M.Sc. degree (1989) in Mechanical Engineering (stress analysis area) from Helwan University then
worked as a lecturer from May 1989 up to June 1991. In 1991, I got a scholarship from the INDO/ARE Cultural Exchange
Program under which I earned my Ph.D. degree in the Mechanical Engineering (Fatigue and Fracture Mechanics area) from
the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay (IITB), Mumbai, India, in 1995. After awarding the PhD degree, I have started
working as a full-time lecturer (1995) at the Mechanical Design Department, Helwan University. I was promoted to the rank
of assistant professor in 1996. In 1998 I have been invited as a visiting scholar at the Ohio State University (Department of
Aerospace Engineering, Applied Mechanics and Aviation) USA. In September 2000, I joined the UAEU as assistant
professor. In September 2003, I was promoted to the rank of associate professor at Helwan University. In 2005, I was
promoted to the rank of associate professor at the UAEU. In 2007, I was invited (fully accommodated with monthly
honorarium) as a visiting scholar/professor at IITB (June 30th -August 20th). In 2009 I was promoted to the rank of full
professor at Helwan University and promoted to the same rank at the United Arab Emirates University in Spring 2014.
