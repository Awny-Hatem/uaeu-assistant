Procedures for entering the UAEU campus & Hostel



          Dear Students,

          Greetings,,

          We would like to inform you about the updated procedures for entering the
          university campus and hostels, which are as follows;

             •  Students who have received two doses + the booster dose (if required)
                and who are exempted from the vaccine are allowed to enter the
                campus with the green pass in the Al-Hosn application, and the period of
                the negative nasal swab examination PCR should not exceed 7 days

             •  Students who have taken two doses + the booster dose (if required) are
                allowed to enter the UAEU Hostel with the green pass in Al-Hosn
                application, and the period of the negative nasal swab examination PCR
                should not exceed 48 hours.

          Note: Non-vaccinated students are not allowed to enter.

          Please adhere to the updated procedures for entering the Campus and
          Hostels

          You can contact us through the following email and phone:
          Email: fmdhs@uaeu.ac.ae, Phone: 037134033 - 037136888
