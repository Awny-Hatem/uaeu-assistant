## Internships
UAEU will host different programs and events aiming to spread knowledge and motivate students to take part in Expo 2020. Students will learn the different approaches to solving challenges, then based on their knowledge and interests, they can apply to the internships offered by UAEU. Full time interns will get the chance to either offer assistance to visitors in the pavilion or support the UAEU team in delivering their Expo2020 programs and events.

## Pathfinders Fellowship
The UAEU Expo 2020 Dubai Fellowship is a nine-month program designed to empower fresh graduates and graduate-level students and help them develop their full potential by strengthening their professional skills, build their leadership skills and expose them to real work environment.
