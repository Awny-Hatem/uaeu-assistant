## Multaqa > General Courses > Children’s Touch (Drawing Class for Children)
Target Audience : Children between (6 - 12) years of age

Course Objective: Through these lessons, children will explore themselves through drawing and painting. During the education process they will learn how to match and mix colors, configure a painting and put their ideas in the drawing.

Course Commences : The beginning of each month (8 hours)

Venue : Multaqa building

Day/Time : Mondays only from 5.00 to 7.00 PM

Registration Fees: Dhs. 200 - Materials not included (Payable in advance at the social club)

Important Notice :

Class maximum 10 children.

The last Date for payment of course fees is on the tenth day of each month.

The number of places are limited and therefore priority will be given to those who register first.
