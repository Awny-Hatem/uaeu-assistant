                                            Off-Campus Programs and Activities


I. Policy

                Instructionally-related off-campus work experiences, field trips, activities and
                research projects are all key elements of the University curriculum, providing
                interaction with local and international communities.

                Permission of Parent or Guardian

                A condition of enrollment in courses at the University is participation in all
                required instructionally-related activities.  Before th e student will be permitted
                to participate in any off-campus programs or activities, there must be a form
                on record, signed by the student’s parent or guardian, authorizing participation
                in off-campus activities.

                Placement of Interns

                The University reserves the right to determine the placement of interns,
                without further permission of the parent or guardian.

                Student Conduct Off Campus

                The student is expected to demonstrate the same high standard of behavior
                required on campus while engaged in University sponsored or authorized
                activities off campus.  The student is expected to obey any orders of the trip
                sponsor or chaperone.
