## Student Rights
All students will be asked to sign a “Letter of Commitment” to the Code of Conduct upon their joining the University, indicating that they will conduct themselves professionally and follow the Student Code of Conduct and other relevant policies. In this letter, it is clearly stated that cheating and other forms of serious misconduct lead to dismissal from the University.
