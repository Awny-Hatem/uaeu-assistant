## Research > Test of suitability of a deep pipe irrigation system for date palms
In their natural habitat, date palms take up water mainly from the subsoil. Here we test the hypothesis that date palms would exhibit a higher water use efficiency when supplied with water through the sub soil, instead of being irrigated from the surface.
