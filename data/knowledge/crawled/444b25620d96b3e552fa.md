## Languages and Testing Units
The Continuing Education Center (CEC) offers a wide range of language programs designed to bridge language and cultural gaps while promoting effective communication and lifelong learning. In addition to teaching foreign languages, the Center is committed to strengthening Arabic language proficiency among native speakers and promoting Arabic to non-native speakers locally and internationally.

The CEC also serves as an official test center for internationally and nationally recognized language proficiency examinations, including IELTS, TOEFL, the Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT), and the Al Ain Arabic Language Proficiency Test, supporting learners and professionals in achieving their academic and career goals.
