## Professional Development Unit
Dedicated to empowering educators, parents, and future professionals with the knowledge, skills, and tools necessary to effectively support children with exceptionalities.

## Objectives:
Strengthen Professional Skills Train teachers, specialists, and future professionals on identifying, understanding, and supporting children with exceptionalities and giftedness.

Promote Awareness and Inclusion Advance community understanding and inclusion through outreach initiatives, forums, and awareness campaigns that align with the Center’s vision and the UAE’s values of inclusivity and excellence.

Develop Future Educators Provide UAEU students and graduates with practical training and real-world experience to prepare them for successful careers in education and related fields.

Improve Teaching Practices Offer professional development programs and workshops that enhance instructional methods and support effective, inclusive classroom environments.

Empower Families Provide training and guidance that help parents better understand and support their children’s learning and development journey.
