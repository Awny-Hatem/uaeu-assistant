# Using Complaints & Feedback to Improve Services

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## Using Complaints & Feedback to Improve Services
United Arab Emirates University is committed to regularly reviewing received complaints and feedback, with the aim of improving service quality and enhancing the efficiency of procedures.

## Service Response Timeframes
Type of Case | Description | Response Time
Comment | General note to improve services | Within 5 working days
Suggestion | Customer proposal to enhance services | Within 5 working days
Inquiry | Request for clarification or information | Within 1-2 working days
Regular Complaint | Expression of dissatisfaction with a service | Within 3 working days
Urgent Complaint | High-impact issue requiring immediate attention | Within 2 working days (48h)
Complex Complaint | Issues involving multiple departments or in-depth review | Within 7 working days

Your feedback will be handled with the utmost attention, transparency, and confidentiality. You will be kept informed throughout the resolution process.
