## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP)
The National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) is a prestigious, full-time, 12-week training program developed by United Arab Emirates University to prepare UAE National graduates for careers in research, academia, and innovation.

Participants will gain exposure to world-class research practices, industry collaboration, and professional skill development through a combination of expert-led academic modules and real research placements.

Participants will receive a monthly stipend upon meeting performance and attendance criteria.
