Recovery After a Disaster Policy

Overview
This policy aims to create a structured and proactive framework to guide UAE University in disaster
preparedness, response and recovery —whether natural, human                                                                                   -made, or other extraordinary events
that may disrupt normal operations. Readiness programs are essential to protect the lives and well-
being of university staff, students, contractors, visitors and colleagues; minimizing damage to and
loss of university property and assets; and ensuring the rapid restoration of normal University
operations.

Scope
This policy applies to all individuals, operations, and facilities associated with UAE University,
including the following:

1.                                                           Types of Disasters: Natural and human-made disasters, including but not limited to:
       a)                      Natural Disasters: Floods, hurricanes, earthquakes, sandstorms, extreme heatwaves, etc.
       b)                   Public Health Emergencies: Pandemics such as Covid-19 and other health-                        related crises.
       c)                      Human-Made Disasters: Fires, cyberattacks, chemical spills, and other incidents.
       d)                        Hybrid Scenarios: Complex emergencies involving a combination of natural and human-                       made
              factors.

2.                               Institutional Activities: All academic, research, administrative, and extracurricular activities
       conducted on or off campus.

3.                           Stakeholders: All employees, students, contractors, visitors, alumni, partner organizations,
       regulatory authorities, and the broader community.

4.                                              Facilities and Infrastructure: Physical and digital infrastructures, including academic buildings,
       research / teaching laboratories, IT systems, and financial resources, hostels

5.                                            Geographical Scope: All UAE University campuses, both within the UAE and internationally,

       where University-sponsored activities are held.

Policy
The specific objectives of the policy are as follows:

1.                                                           Ensure the Safety and Well-Being of the University Community
       Protect the lives and health of employees, students, contractors and visitors during and after a
       disaster by providing timely medical assistance, psychological support, and access to essential
       services. Special attention shall be given to vulnerable groups, including individuals with
       disabilities.

2.                                                           Maintain Continuity of Academic and Research Activities
       Ensure the continuation of academic and research functions by providing mechanisms for online
       and hybrid learning and safeguarding ongoing research projects, equipment, and data.

4.                                                           Build Institutional Resilience
       Foster a culture of preparedness, adaptability, and proactive response to both anticipated and
       unexpected crises.

5.                                                           Foster Collaboration and Partnerships
       Strengthen coordination with national, regional, and international bodies, including government
       agencies, academic institutions, and the private sector.

6.                                                           Align Recovery Efforts with Sustainability Goals
       Promote eco-friendly recovery measures and ensure that rebuilding efforts support the long-                                                                       term
       sustainability of the institution.

7.                                                           Enhance Awareness and Preparedness Among Stakeholders
       Provide ongoing training, establish effective communication channels, and involve the University
       community in disaster preparedness.

8.                                                           Strengthen Compliance with National and International Frameworks
       Ensure compliance with UAE laws and international standards for disaster risk management and
       recovery.

9.                                                           Promote Post-Disaster Learning and Continuous Improvement
       Establish processes for post-disaster evaluation and incorporate lessons learned into future
       disaster planning and recovery efforts.


Article (1): Governance and Responsibility

1.                                                           Crisis Management Committee (CMC)
       This Committee shall be the central governing body responsible for overseeing all disaster

       recovery efforts at UAE University. The Committee's tasks include coordinating recovery
       activities well, policy compliant and efficiently executing them.
       Composition of the Crisis Management Committee (CMC): The Vice Chancellor (Chair), Deputy
       Vice Chancellor for Administration and Finance (Deputy Chair), and representatives (members)
       from Office of the Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs, University Facilities unit, Information
       Technology Division, Human Resources Department, Office of the Associate Provost for Student
       Affairs, Operations and Emergency Section, Health and Safety, and other relevant departments.
       The Committee may also include liaison repres entatives from external national authorities such
       as NCEMA, MoHAP, TDRA, the UAE Cybersecurity Council, Civil Defense, and the
       Department of Health –                                    Abu Dhabi, as needed.

2.                                                           Disaster Recovery Coordinator (DRC)
       Designated by the Vice Chancellor, shall serve as the primary operational point of contact for all
       disaster recovery activities, ensuring the effective execution of the recovery plan and coordination
       across departments.

3.                                                           Departmental Roles and Responsibilities
       Departments are assigned specific recovery roles, including restoring academic activities,
       infrastructure repair, IT recovery, and ensuring stakeholder welfare.

4.                                                           Communication Protocols
       Under the supervision of the UOD, the timely arrival of internal and external communications
       must be ensured.


Article (2): Key Components of the Policy

1.                                              Regular risk assessments should be conducted by the Disaster Recovery Coordinator (DRC) to
       identify vulnerabilities and map potential disaster risks.

2.                                                       A comprehensive Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) should be maintained and updated periodically
       to reflect changing circumstances and emerging risks.

3.                                                     The Disaster Recovery Coordinator (DRC) activates a detailed disaster response plan, including
       evacuation procedures, emergency communications, and resource allocation strategies.

4.                                                                Recovery efforts shall prioritize the restoration of critical functions such as IT systems, academic
       services, and student welfare. Psychological and medical support will also be made available to
       those affected.

5.                                  The university works with regional and international governmental institutions to ensure an
       effective recovery process. This includes structured coordination with Emirati authorities such as
       NCEMA, the UAE Cybersecurity Council, Ministry of Health and Prevention, and local
       emergency services to align recovery actions with national frameworks.


Article (3): Implementation and Monitoring
Implementation procedures provide specific guidelines on how to implement disaster recovery,
including:

2.                                                           Regular Reviews: Scheduled drills and simulations to assess preparedness.

       Coordinator (DRC) and submitted to the University's Board of Trustees.

Article (4): Compliance

1.                                                                This policy complies with UAE federal laws and international frameworks. In particular, it aligns
         with the guidelines and directives issued by NCEMA, TDRA, and the UAE Cybersecurity
         Council, ensuring that recovery actions conform with national disaster and                                                                cybersecurity
         strategies.

2.                                   The university adopts international best practices in disaster risk management and recovery,
         integrating innovations and lessons learned from global disaster management strategies.


Article (5): Review and Updates
This policy is reviewed every three years by the University's Crisis Management Committee or as
necessary to incorporate new developments in disaster management, emerging risks, and stakeholder
feedback. This policy is updated based on lessons learned from past incidents and advances in disaster
management technology.


Article (6): Implementation Procedures
Detailed procedures accompany this policy, outlining activation, resource mobilization,
communication plans, phased recovery timelines, monitoring, and regular training to ensure readiness
and effective execution during crises.
