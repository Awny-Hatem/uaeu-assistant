Procedures of Policy No. (15) - Endo  wed and Named Professorial Chairs

1.                                                                 Endowed Professorships shall be approved according to the Signatory Authority.

2.                                                       Designated funds for Endowed Professorships shall be associated with that can be used
                 at the discretion of the appointee. These positions may be given a title to associate them
                 with the name of an individual or an entity.

3.                          Endowed Professor appointments shall be for a prescribed period and shall be
                 renewable upon the agreement of all concerned parties.

4.                              Named Professorships are not associated with funds; they are made to recognize
                 contributions of an individual or an entity.

5.                              Named Professorship appointments and terms shall be approved according to the
                 Signatory Authority.
