                                                                      Academic Appeals


I.                                                                                               Policy

                To ensure that students are treated equitably and grades assigned without
                error, the United Arab Emirates University shall establish processes to provide
                for the review of grades and dismissal for unsatisfactory academic progress.

                Appeal of a Course Grade

                A student may request a review of his/her final course grade by submitting a
                written appeal to the college or unit dean in which the course was taken.

                Appeal of Dismissal for Unsatisfactory Academic Progress

                A student may request a review of his/her dismissal for unsatisfactory
                academic progress by submitting a written appeal with specific evidence of
                error to the college or unit dean in which he/she is enrolled.

                An appeal will be considered only in cases where there is clear evidence of
                substantial error of fact or in grade calculation, demonstrated inequity in
                grading among students in the class or where there has been:

                         •             a failure to adhere to the stated requirements of the student’s
                                 curriculum;
                         •              a substantial and demonstrable irregularity in the assessment or
                                 examination process; or
                         •                                  a medical, family or personal emergency that has substantially affected
                                 the student’s academic performance.
