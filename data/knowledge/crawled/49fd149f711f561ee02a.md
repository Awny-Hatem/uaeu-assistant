## UAEU Expo 2020 Hub
The UAEU Expo 2020 Hub opened it’s doors to the community in September 2019 with the kick-off of the first event the UAEU Expo 2020 Bootcamp. The thriving Hub which is located in the UAEU Science and Innovation Park is a unique space offering a compelling range of events and a vibrant place for engaging the community in the co-creation of the UAEU Pavilion at Expo 2020. The Hub will play a pivotal role in the lead up to the World’s Greatest Show ‘Expo 2020’.
