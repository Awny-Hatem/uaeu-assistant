Digital Participation Policy

The United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) strives to enhance its efforts to achieve its
vision of "Regional and Global Leadership and Excellence in Higher Education and
Scientific Research". The university also focuses, in line with its institutional values, on
effective communication with internal and external stakeholders by providing multiple
channels that enable the exchange of opinions and suggestions.

The Digital Participation Policy applies to all topics, opinions, discussions, and surveys
raised and circulated via the institution’s digital participation tools on the website or the
(Sharik.AE) platform. It includes the following:

     •                                                        Digital Surveys: A tool to measure customer satisfaction, which may include
           open-                        ended questions to gather their feedback, suggestions, and ideas.
     •                                                        Suggestion and Complaint Forms: These forms allow users to submit their
           suggestions on any topics related to the university.
     •                                                        Polls: A tool for collecting public opinions on various topics related to the
           university’s interests to improve its services, where participation is through
           voting.
     •                                                        Digital Consultations: These aim to engage the university community and visitors
           to develop and improve its services. The feedback is then analyzed and used as
           input for decision-                                                 making processes that affect stakeholders, enhancing their
           satisfaction and happiness.
     •                                                        Social Media Platforms: Such as Facebook, X  -            platform, Instagram, and others.
     •                                                        Blog: An article written and published by the institution on a topic within its scope
           of work, aiming to educate and raise awareness among the public and highlight
           important topics.
     •                                                        Communication with higher management.
     •                                                        Any other channel adopted by the institution.

Electronic Participation Guidelines:

     •                                                        It is recommended to keep comment content relevant to the context.
     •                                                        Users should refrain from sharing personal data on the university’s pages and
           should communicate through official channels only.
     •                                                        Comments and questions will be handled in both Arabic and English only.
     •                                                        All personal data will be kept strictly confidential.

The United Arab Emirates University welcomes any feedback or participation via its
platforms. However, the university reserves the full right to remove or block any

Response and evaluation mechanism:

After collecting customer responses from all surveys and sorting them, the answers are
evaluated according to the  university’s needs, general directives, and applicable
policies, including laws and legislation. Then the most frequent answer is chosen
because it is the most important and influential and meets the interests and needs of the
audience.

Additionally, we publish a summary of the public comments received and our response
to each engagement opportunity. We also publish evidence that people's opinions have
been included in the actual decision-                                                    making process to underscore the value and
usefulness of digital engagements.

Announcing and promoting digital participation opportunities:

We publish our opportunities for digital participation quarterly on the      University’s website
and on the ‘                                              Sharik.AEwebsite. We enhance our opportunities for digital engagement
through our social media accounts. It is important to choose digital participation
according to the factors mentioned above, write in a language that is clear and
understandable to the average reader, and avoid publishing inaccurate or misleading
information. A digital participation is chosen according to several main factors, namely
its compatibility with the university’s mandates, its connection to the   university’s goals
and activities, and its contribution to achieving them.

We always welcome your interaction with us through our electronic channels.
