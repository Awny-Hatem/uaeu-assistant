Department of Statistics and Business Analytics

                                        Statistical Consulting Unit
                                      Workshops/Training courses
Title: Multi-response Data Analysis Using Envelope Regression Model
Statistical Software: R
Requirements: Familiarity with R Statistical Software and Regression Methods and interest
in conducting multi-response regression.
Instructor: Dr. Subhadip Pal
Target Group: Students and any other professionals including the research scientists working
in various applied research who use statistical methodology such as regression.
Date: 27 Jan.-2 Feb., 2025
Period: 2 days, 4 hours/session, 8 hours total
Platform: Face-to-face Meeting
Inclusive of Scientific Materials: PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.
Description:
The short course plans to introduce the multi-response regression model and the challenges in
analyzing such data. Thereafter, it introduces the basic concepts of Envelope regression in
the `efficient estimation’ of regression coefficients. Finally,              several    datasets appear  from
various  fields  of  studies  to  demonstrate  the  corresponding  computation  procedure  while
measuring the efficiency gain over the standard multi-response model.
Topics Covered:
Day 1
    -    Basics of Multi-response Regression, and R basics.
    -    Envelope Regression and application to datasets.
Day 2
    -    Different variants of Envelope models and Applications.
