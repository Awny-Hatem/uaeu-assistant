## Featured Programs > Orientation to Financial Management > Orientation to Financial Management
This two-day course is intended to assist managers whose work experience is not in the financial field to understand and use financial reports when making financial decisions. It serves as an introduction to financial statement analysis. Concepts and terminologies found in published financial statements of companies will be clarified and explained. Participants will also be equipped with financial decision-making tools to analyse a company s profitability, liquidity, and solvency.
