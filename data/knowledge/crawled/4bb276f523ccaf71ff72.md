Declaration of Financial Commitments


The United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) welcomes you to its academic community and looks forward to
supporting your educational journey and helping you achieve your academic aspirations.

This Declaration is intended to provide a clear understanding of the financial commitments associated with studying
at UAEU, ensuring that students are fully informed before completing the admission and registration process.

Please read this Declaration carefully before signing it. Ensure that you have reviewed the applicable tuition fees,
payment deadlines, and other applicable charges, and that you understand the financial commitments associated
with your study at UAEU.

If you have any questions or require further clarification, please                 contact   the University before signing this
Declaration. We will be pleased to provide any information or assistance you may need.

Fees and Payment Arrangements
 Application Fee
      •    A non-refundable application/processing fee of AED 210 (inclusive of 5% VAT) is payable by all
           applicants upon submission of their application.
 Tuition Fees and Financial Obligations
      •    Tuition  fees  are  payable  each  academic  semester  in  accordance  with  the               payment  deadlines
           announced by the University.
      •    The University publishes tuition fees, additional charges, and payment deadlines on the                       official
           Student Accounts Office webpage. These may be updated from time to time in accordance with the
           University's approved policies.
 Additional Charges
 Where applicable, students are responsible for:

      •    Any currency exchange differences and charges imposed by the student's bank or payment service
           provider.


 Payment and Refunds
      •    Students are responsible for paying all tuition fees and any other applicable charges by the published
           payment deadlines.
      •    Students wishing to withdraw from the University or from one or more courses must complete the
           official withdrawal procedures through the UAEU e-Services Portal.
      •    Any tuition fee refund, where applicable, will be processed in accordance with the                      University's
           Refund Policy in effect at the time the withdrawal request is submitted.

 Student Declaration

 By signing this Declaration, I confirm that:

      •    I have read and understood the University's policies, regulations, and procedures governing tuition
           fees and other applicable University charges, and I agree to comply with them, including any related
           amendments or updates.

      •    I have sufficient financial resources to meet my tuition fees, University accommodation fees (where
           applicable), and all other applicable University charges throughout the duration of my studies.                          I
           undertake to pay all amounts due by the published deadlines.

      •    I confirm that all financial information and declarations provided to the University are complete and
           accurate. I understand that providing any false          or incomplete information may affect the continuation
           of my admission or enrolment.

      •    I understand that failure to pay any amounts due to the University may result in the application of the
           measures  set  out  in  the  University's  policies,  including  restrictions  on  my  academic  account,
           registration, or the suspension or termination of my enrol           ment. The University also reserves the right
           to recover any outstanding amounts in accordance with the applicable laws of the United Arab
           Emirates.

      •    I confirm that I have reviewed the         tuition fees, University accommodation fees             (where applicable),
           payment deadlines, and additional fees published by the University.
