## Department of Nutrition and Health > Overview
Welcome to the Department of Nutrition and Health (DNH) under the recently established Division of Health Sciences in the prestigious College of Medicine and Health Sciences at the UAEU. DNH boasts of being the first in the United Arab Emirates to offer a degree program in nutrition over the past four decades. The department has grown since then and currently offers four distinct internationally recognized programs including the four-year undergraduate degree programs: BSc in Nutritional Science, and Bachelor in Dietetics, and the two graduate programs: MSc in Human Nutrition, and PhD in Nutritional Sciences.

The DNH programs are accredited by the UAE Ministry of Education. In addition, the Coordinated Program in Dietetics is accredited by the Accreditation Council for Education in Nutrition and Dietetics (ACEND) of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics (AND) in the USA. This is the only program in the country and among the five programs that are accredited by ACEND outside the USA. The Nutritional Science Program is accredited by the Association for Nutrition (AfN) in the United Kingdom. It is the third program accredited by AfN outside the UK. The MSc in Human Nutrition is an affiliated program with the University College London (UCL) in the UK.

We aim to prepare graduates to be the leaders and change agents in the field of nutrition sciences and dietetics contributing towards improving the health and wellbeing of the society at the national and international levels.

We are committed to providing the high standard of education and scientific research through our diversely qualified faculty and trained technical staff, state of the art facilities and infra-structure.

Our programs are best suited for individuals who are motivated to advance their knowledge and skills, and build their careers in the professions of nutrition and dietetics education, research and service .

## Department of Nutrition and Health > Research
The Department of Nutrition and Health engages in research related to major local and global health challenges and to space exploration. Our faculty members conduct different type of studies including in vitro, pre-clinical, and clinical studies. Some of the major areas of research pursued by the department include:

Community-based nutrition and health promotion: Investigating the interrelations between various health conditions such as cardiometabolic diseases, COVID-19, and eating habits, lifestyle, mental health and overall health of populations in the UAE and the Middle Eastern region; Initiating population-based birth cohort studies

Behavioral nutrition and interventions: Characterizing, understanding and modifying lifestyle behaviors such as salt consumption, using innovative approaches including digital tools

Nutrition, physical activity and wellness: Identifying ways to improve athletic and physical activity performance via sports nutrition

Diet sustainability: Assessing the awareness and ways to promote diet sustainability in UAE

Dietary bioactive compounds: Exploring how natural compounds, such as polyphenols can modulate the development of diseases; identification of natural compounds from local resources like date seeds and characterization of their bioactivity; modifying dietary fibers using novel methods and determining their physiological significance

Nutrition in Health and Disease Across the Life Span: Elucidating the role of nutrition in diverse health conditions such as autism spectrum disorder, post-bariatric surgery and pre-maturity

Life Sciences in Space research: Exploring lifestyle, physiological changes and potential countermeasures in ground-based analog studies

## Department of Nutrition and Health > Services
The Department of Nutrition and Health provides various services to UAEU and the community. The DNH laboratories have actively supported the research activities of faculty and students (both undergraduate and graduate).

At the local level of the UAEU community, the department’s laboratories provide supporting services to the university’s scientific colleges, such as the College of Agriculture and Veterinary Medicine, the College of Engineering, the College of Science, and Student Life Deanship, to support student and faculty research projects.

In addition, the DNH laboratories provide support to the Innovation Park and Khalifa Center for Genetic and Biotechnology, including the preparation and analysis of samples using various equipment in the DNH laboratories. In addition, DNH lab. staff provide technical consultations to these entities.

The DNH laboratories support research collaborations with various universities in the UAE including the University of Sharjah, Zayed University, New York University – Abu Dhabi. This support covers several technical and analytical aspects of research. DNH laboratories provide services to local organizations such as Abu Dhabi Agriculture and Food Safety Authority (ADAFSA), Abu Dhabi Quality and Conformity Council (QCC), Agthia, and sports clubs. Furthermore, DNH laboratories provide support to the Exchange students from various countries, including Japan, Sudan, and the Netherlands.

DNH laboratories provide services to sports clubs, such as Aljazira, including body composition and physical fitness measurements. DNH laboratories are used to introduce school students to UAEU as a platform to increase student recruitment.

DNH faculty members provide professional service at national and international levels, including contributions to the UAE National Nutrition Strategy 2022–2030, led by the Ministry of Health and Prevention (MOHAP) and serve in various external committees, including the committee s responsible for revising the Obesity Guidelines led by the Ministry of Health and Prevention (MOHAP). DNH faculty organized symposiums and workshops with the U.N. Food and Agriculture Organization (2019, 2020, and 2023).

The DNH faculty members have also contributed as resource persons in numerous conferences, workshops, and seminars. Moreover, two faculty members in DNH serve on the editorial boards of journals ranked in the top 10-15%, thus contributing to the visibility of the university.
