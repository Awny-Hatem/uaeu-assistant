## College of Information Technology > Vision, Mission & Goals
Vision

The Knowledge and Discovery hub for Information Technology

Mission

To ingrain a sustained societal impact through Information Technology education and research.

Goals:

1. Graduate highly skilled students and prepare them to be leaders in Information Technology disciplines.

2. Achieve excellence in research and innovation that are relevant to the nation and the region.

3. Provide our students with a distinctive learning experience in accordance with international accreditation standards, and in line with the job market needs.

4. Enrich lives and improve UAE society through community engagement, knowledge transfer, and industry partnership.

5. Foster an environment of a diverse community of exceptional faculty, students and staff.
