## Student Activities > Overview
The Student Activities Department at the United Arab Emirates University fosters students’success and takes pride in working with and helping students, faculty, alumni, and the UAEU community strive towards excellence. Our primary focus is to provide a collection of extra-curricular programs and activities that promote the development of the students’ academic experience at the University and aids their growth and development.
