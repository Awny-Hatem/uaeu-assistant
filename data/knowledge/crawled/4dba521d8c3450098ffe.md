Procedures for Obtaining a Residence Visa in the United Arab Emirates

Fees for Medical Tests (Health Test and Blood Test) (if eligible)

Air tickets and ground transportation (if eligible)

## International Students Services > The main required documents for all cases of extraction and renewal of student’s residence at the UAEU:
Original student passport.

Passport size photo.

“To Whom It May Concern” Letter from Admission & Registration that the student is registered and active\continuous at UAEU.

Original Blood test.

Copy of valid Health Insurance Card.

Copy of valid Emirates Identity Card.

Receipt of deposit the required amount of residency + administrative fees.

## International Students Services > In the case of a transfer of residence on the university, please add the following documents:
Certificate of the sponsor End of Service\ Work Termination and be sure of the sponsor leave outside the UAE, or in case of the sponsor's death: Sponsor Death Certificate.

Certificate stating that there are no presence of relatives, such as brother or mother the student can transfer his residence visa of them.

A written request from the student for visa transfer approval.

A copy of sponsor's passport with residence.

Receipt of deposit the required amount to extract the residence visa.

Receipt of deposit the required amount of the fees “In–lieu of Departure” .

## International Students Services > In the case of Residence extraction for the first time, please add the following documents:
Visa original entry

## International Students Services > The General Conditions to transfer residence to UAEU:
To be a UAEU registered student and be on continuous study

The completion of the required documents

Obtaining administrative approvals

There is no sponsor for the student inside UAE
