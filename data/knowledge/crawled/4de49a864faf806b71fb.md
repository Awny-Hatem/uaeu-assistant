## Admission to the DBA > Admission to the DBA
Admission into the UAEU DBA program will be selective in nature. A critical requirement is that admitted students be capable of undertaking doctoral-level activities. A DBA Admissions Committee, consisting of faculty members of the CBE along with the DBA Program Coordinator, will evaluate each applicant to determine their suitability for admission to the course.

Minimum Admissions Requirements

The following are the minimum requirements for admission to the DBA Program at UAEU.

A Master’s Degree from an accredited university recognized by the UAE Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, in an area appropriate to the academic area to which application is being made.

A minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale or equivalent, in the Master’s degree earned at an accredited university recognized by the UAE Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research.

A score of 6.0 or higher on the IELTS Academic exam or equivalent; this score must be less than two years old at the time of application and from our approved Centers (please contact Admissions for validation).

A minimum of five years of combined relevant work experience at executive/management level pre- or post-Master’s.

## Admission to the DBA > Interview
Interview

After submitting all of the required materials, the DBA Admissions Committee screens and ranks all applicants. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for interview with the Committee. During the interview, Committee members will be assessing candidates primarily on the following criteria:

English Language Proficiency; an ability to articulate thoughts in a coherent and logical manner

Conscientiousness; an ability to show self-discipline, goal-oriented, demonstrates a commitment to learning

Critical thought; demonstrates how professional work experience and previous research could relate to the DBA program

All candidates will be formally notified via email of their status.

Classes are scheduled to begin in August 2025 .
