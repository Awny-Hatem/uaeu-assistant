                                                                        University Business Cards


I.                                                                                               Policy

                    The United Arab Emirates University has established a standard format for
                    United Arab Emirates University business cards that is to be used for all
                    business cards issued to faculty and staff.  The design of the business card is
                    part of the University’s corporate identity and is the responsibility of the
                    Publications Department.

                    Faculty and staff may request business cards.  Staff in grades other than 1 to 4
                    who represent the University and have extensive dealings with the public may
                    also request cards with the approval of the Secretary General and their dean or
                    director.


II.                                                                             Application

                    This policy shall apply to all employees of the United Arab Emirates
                    University.
