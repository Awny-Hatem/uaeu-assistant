## Review of The Current Status of Climate Change Modeling in UAE and the Region > Review of The Current Status of Climate Change Modeling in UAE and the Region
Client | Ministry of Climate Change and Environment Projects (MOCCAE)
Project Description | The main objective of this project was to the review the literature related to climate change modeling and its impacts on United Arab Emirates (UAE) and the region. Although no new research is proposed at this stage, except for the discussion on the recent rainfall trends in UAE, the existing information and results of previous studies were integrated, discussed, and analyzed to provide a better understanding of possible impacts of climate change on the environmental system of UAE and the region.
