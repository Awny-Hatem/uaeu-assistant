## Vision, Value & Goal > Vision
Leadership and excellence in transportation services and publications provided to clients.

## Vision, Value & Goal > Goal
Provide safe transportation services on time and high-quality printing services for students, faculty and employees of United Arab Emirates University.

## Vision, Value & Goal > Value
Individuals: Emphasis on providing qualified staff with the appropriate skills to perform the required work in appropriate working conditions that lead to outstanding performance.

Productivity: Continuous monitoring and management of work systems to ensure efficiency and effectiveness in performance and within the appropriate cost.

Performance: Applying the best international systems and rules in an appropriate work environment that enables us to provide our services in the best possible ways.
