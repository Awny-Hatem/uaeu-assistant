## The Power of Emotional Leadership in School Work
Title: The Power of Emotional Leadership in School Work

Date: Monday, 15 September 2025

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Mr. Yousef Alblooshi

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: A training workshop designed to empower school leaders and teachers to apply emotional intelligence and using emotional leadership in the educational environment. The workshop emphasizes the power of school leadership in empathy, positive human relations, and balancing compassion with firmness in school leadership. It aims to create a stable, motivating, and collaborative learning environment built on trust and cooperation within the school community.
