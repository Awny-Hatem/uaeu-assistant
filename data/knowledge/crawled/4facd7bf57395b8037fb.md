9. Wireless Access

Overview
Defines management of all existing wireless infrastructure devices existing in the offices of
the UAEU and connected to the UAEU network, or that provide access to information
classified as strictly confidential or prohibited.

Scope
Applies to all UAEU Fac ulty, Staff and Students.

Objective
Ensures optimum and secure usage of UAEU network by Facult y, Staff and Students.

Policy
1.                                                           The usage of authentication protocols and infrastructure must be approved by UAEU.
2.                                                           All devices addresses (MAC) will be maintained, recorded and traced if required.
3.                                                       Wireless access points shall only be installed by the UITS Department at UAEU locations
      and UITS will provide the support and maintenance.
4.                                                           Approved encryption protocols will be use.
5.                                  The confidential exchange of protected access with high                                       -reliability technology will be
      enabled.
