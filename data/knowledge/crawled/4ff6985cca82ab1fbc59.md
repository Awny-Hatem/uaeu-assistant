Institutional Accreditation – What is it?
Institutional accreditation is a voluntary peer review process of a higher education institution such as
UAEU that aims to assure academic quality and accountability and to encourage improvement by an
international accrediting agency.

About WSCUC
The WASC Senior College and University Commission (”the Commission”) is an international
accrediting agency from the USA. Through its work of peer review, based on standards agreed to by
the membership, the Commission encourages continuous institutional improvement and assures the
membership and its constituencies, including the public , that accredited institutions are fulfilling their
missions in service to their students and the public good.

WSCUC Core Commitments
   •                                                                       Commitment to Student Learning and Success
   •                                                                       Commitment to Quality and Improvement
   •                                                                       Commitment to Institutional Integrity, Sustainability, and Accountability

The four Standards to be Judged by WASC

 Standard 1            Defining Institutional Purposes and Ensuring Educational Objectives
 Standard 2            Achieving Educational Objectives Through Core Functions

 Standard 3            Developing and Applying Resources and Organizational Structures to Ensure Quality
                       and Sustainability
 Standard 4            Creating an Organization Committed to Quality Assurance, Institutional Learning,
                       and Improvement
Click here to view the standards and criteria for review.

Benefits of institutional Accreditation for the Students, Faculty, Stakeholders and
University
   •                                           Maintains constant focus on educational effectiveness and the university’s processes, which
        helps identify areas in need of improvement;
   •                                                                       Boosts the university’s credibility and visibility in the international higher education sector;
   •                                                                       Assists in recruiting high quality faculty;
   •                                                  Provides UAEU graduates with more options for overseas study and employment. Graduating
        from an accredited institution indicates that graduates are ready to practice at a certain level.

Accreditation Milestones




















The Road to Accreditation

1.   Diagnostics Visit
With the participation of a number of consultants and many faculty members, administrators, and
students, many issues identified in the diagnostic visit have been attended to. The Vice Chancellor
commissioned a steering committee to oversee accreditation and preparation of the self-study.
Widespread involvement of the campus community has helped ensure that the SSR gives a genuine
and comprehensive portrait of UAEU. Meetings across the university (including accreditation working
groups, Deans, faculty and students) have critically reviewed our strengths and areas for further
development. Action plans are being developed to implement improvements.

2.   Mock Visit
A team of exper ts familiar with universities in the WASC region has reviewed our SSR and will conduct
a mock visit (Aug . 26-28) to test our preparedness for the real audit in November 2015. Students, faculty,
administrators and university stakeholders should be familiar with each of the four standards.

3.   Official Visit
The main purpose of the official site visit is to validate the claims made in the SSR.  So the UAEU
community need to be very familiar with the SSR. A major benefit of accreditation is improvement, so
focus on areas of improvement and the actions summarized in each of the  standards. Please be ready
to help wherever you can.
