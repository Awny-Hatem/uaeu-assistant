 Tuition Payment Installment Plan
        To arrange a tuition payment installment plan students must apply at the SAO before 01
         February 2018. If you were entitled to an installment plan in a previous semester you must
         apply again for the current semester.

        Non-payment of total tuition fees on or before 28 March 2018 will result in a hold on
         registration and records. A hold means that you will not receive course grades until you pay
         your fees. It also means that you will not be able to register for courses in the next semester
         until payment is made. Unfortunately, it may also mean forfeiture of grades and credits earned
         during the semester.

        (SPONSORED STUDENTS) Students whose tuition charges are paid, or guaranteed, by a
         sponsor must submit an official letter of financial agreement from the sponsor no later than 15
         February 2018, or risk having their registration for Spring 2018 cancelled.
