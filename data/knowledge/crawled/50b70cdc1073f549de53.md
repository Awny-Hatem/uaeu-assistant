FACULTY MEMBER

 Dr Coenie Nel is a passionate professional with more than 16 years of exposure to various national                      and    international infrastructure     projects.   After
 spending     valuable   project   time   with   engineering    consultants     and   contractors,    Dr   Nel   decided   to  follow his passion, Project Management. He
 combined that with his talents, leadership, and teaching, to follow a career in developing                 individuals  and   organisations     to  become    more    successful
 through    applying    the   principles  of   Project   Management.  He has also        managed various projects to successful completion on               behalf  of the   client
 organization.
 Dr Nel is a PMI-accredited Project Management Professional (PMP) and has facilitated more than 80 Project Management programs, nationally and
 internationally, on various levels for more than 9 years. He has delivered various successful Project Management                    development     programs    (individual and
 organizational)    in  the   private   sector,   with   clients  from   industries    such   as  the Petrochemical, Ship Building, General Subcontractor, Mechanical
 Engineering,    Electrical  Engineering,    Manufacturing, Design, Mining, and the Travel industry.
 Dr Nel recently successfully obtained his PhD degree, proving mastery in Project Management,                     Project   and   Organization strategy, and the integration
 of these concepts. He also holds master’s degrees in Business Administration               (MBA)     and Engineering (MSc.Eng Civil), as well as a baccalaureate in Civil
 Engineering    (B.Eng Civil). He is registered as a Professional Civil Engineer, PrEng, Engineering Council South Africa; and as a Project Management
 Professional, PMP, Project Management Institute (PMI)

    TARGET GROUP

    This   course    is  aimed     at   functional    level   managers      such    as   finance    managers,     product     managers, process managers, human
    resources managers, and customer            experience    managers     who    want    to understand the role and value of the marketing function and the
    interaction of marketing with        other organizational functions.
