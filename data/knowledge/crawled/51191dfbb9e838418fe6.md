How to use this page The "Year" link next to the degree program titles listed below indicates the year in which the program curriculum was revised. In general, a revised curriculum applies to the cohorts of students entering the program at or after the year the revisions were approved. For example, the entry for Electrical Engineering appears like this: Electrical Engineering 2003 2007 2009 You can view the curriculum for different years, or "cohorts".
