                                       Business Transportation Central Services
                                                                 (Inter- and Intra-City)


I.                                                                                               Policy

                The United Arab Emirates University provides inter- and intra-city business
                transportation central services to meet the needs of faculty and staff to process
                and finalize business transactions of the University in Al Ain and in cities
                across the United Arab Emirates.

                Business transportation services are administered centrally by the Director of
                General Services to ensure optimum use of University vehicles and to avoid
                wasting University resources.

                This policy governs only occasional business transportation requests by
                University units, as well as similar business transportation needs that can be
                managed centrally, including University mail delivery and distribution.
                Requests for such travel services must be scheduled through the General
                Services Department.

                Transportation needs of the Student Catering Department and the Campus
                Development Department, as well as unscheduled (emergency) transportation
                requests, are not governed by this policy.


II.                                                                             Application

                This policy shall apply to all employees of the United Arab Emirates
                University.
