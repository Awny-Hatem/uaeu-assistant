## University of the Future Exhibition
The University of the Future exhibition will attract visitors, who will be taken on an insightful learning journey that will help discover their authentic nature as pathfinders.

Pathfinders live their lives being driven by a purpose higher than themselves; they possess a growth mindset and act as entrepreneurs to co-create and produce abundance for the greater good. Indubitably, our education system is in need of a change. Visitors will realize the need for a new model of higher education; one that does not produce job seekers.

The visitors will be taken on this experiential journey by our two inspiring mascots. The duration of the exhibition is approximately 20 minutes from the welcome until the exit and the content will be displayed in three languages (English, Arabic and French).
