## Overview
The National Space Science and Technology Center (NSSTC) has been established jointly by UAEU, the UAE Space Agency and the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (ICT-Fund). The creation of the NSSTC was primarily motivated by UAEU’s desire to strengthen its role and contribute to the needs of the nation in terms of Space Science and Space Technology in order to become a the Space Science and Technology hub for the region.
