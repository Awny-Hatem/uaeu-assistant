6. Degree Completion and Graduation

Overview
Covers policies and procedures relating to the requirements for, confirmation of, and awarding
of academic degrees including: undergraduate degrees, structure of undergraduate degree
programs,  limits  on  transfer  credits  for          undergraduate  degrees,  application  for  degree,
certification of degree completion, graduation standing, and graduation honors.

Scope
Applies to all students and all academic and related administrative units of the University except
those in the College of Medicine and Health Sciences.

Objective
Ensures that the confirmation of completion and awarding of academic degrees functions of the
University is conducted in a manner that is consistent with academic principles, standards, and
expectations of the University appropriate to its educational mission and academic programs,
and operate with necessary integrity, fairness, consistency, and effectiveness.

Policy
1.  Undergraduate degree programs at UAEU will be reflective of the prevailing content,
    academic expectations, and learning outcomes of corresponding academic programs at
    internationally recognized public universities.
2.  The University will provide distinctive and high quality undergraduate degree programs by
    adopting    common academic requirements applicable to all students across the colleges,
    recognizing the educational needs and expectations of the nation in program creation and
    operation, and designing and conducting undergraduate degree programs to meet college or
    program-level international accreditation standards or equivalent recognition.
3.  The  University  will  recognize  outstanding  academic  achievement  through                        a  special
    designation at graduation.

Procedures of Policy No. (6) - Degree Completion and Graduation

1. Undergraduate Degrees
    A Bachelor’s Degree may be awarded only to students who have satisfied minimum credit
    hour  and  Grade  Point  Average  requirements  and  other  established  degree-specific
    requirements based on all applicable University Policies and Procedures.
    a)   The minimum number of earned credit hours required for a Bachelor’s Degree is (120).
         The minimum number of credit hours required for a student who transfers among UAEU
         Colleges may be higher than (120).
    b)   The number of earned credit hours required for a Bachelor’s Degree is dependent on the
         student’s program of study.
    c)   The minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) required for a Bachelor’s
         Degree is (2.00).
    d)   No student may be awarded a Bachelor’s Degree with a grade of “I” (Incomplete) on
         his/her academic record.
    e)   Upon the recommendation of the academic advisor and approval of the College Dean,
         a student may be allowed to register for additional courses in his/her College if he/she
         needs to raise the CGPA to the minimum required for graduation.


2. Structure of Undergraduate Degree Programs
    a)   Bachelor’s    Degree programs vary by College and may include, to different degrees,
         five specific components, each with a defined minimum number of credit hours, specific
         courses, and/or demonstrated proficiency.
         1)     General Education/Core Curriculum Requirements – The Core Curriculum is a
                University-wide  requirement  of  General  Education  courses  and  proficiencies
                applicable to all students.
         2)     College-wide Requirements – College requirements apply to all students in the
                specific college, and may vary depending on the student’s major field of study.
         3)     Major/Specialization Requirements – Major requirements apply to the student’s
                specific area of specialization including minors and concentrations, and may vary
                depending on the student’s major field of study.
         4)     Elective Courses – “Free Electives” are courses a student may take in or outside
                his/her College to complete his/her degree requirements.
         5)     A college    may propose one or more minors at their programs,                  which must      be
                reviewed and approved through University Procedures.
         6)     A student may apply to the College Dean to include an approved minor in his/her
                academic plan.

3. Limits on Transfer Credits – Undergraduate Degrees
    a)   Only    courses    from  institutions      approved  by  the      Ministry  of  Education  may  be
         transferred to UAEU for degree credit.
    b)   For UAEU Colleges and/or degree programs with specialized international accreditation
         or the equivalent, core courses (those courses required in the major/specialization of the
         College)  may  be  transferred  to  UAEU  for  degree  credit  and  applied  to  degree
         requirements only when taken at institutions holding identical or equivalent specialized
         international accreditation. The Dean of the receiving College may make exceptions to
         this limitation on a case-by-case basis.
    c)   A student transferring from another institution must complete at least (75%) of the total
         credit hours required for the degree, and all senior-level courses in the academic major,
         at UAEU.
    d)   Determination of the applicability of transfer credit to the requirements of a specific
         undergraduate degree is the exclusive decision of the College.
    e)   Acceptance of credits earned at other institutions subsequent to the student’s initial
         enrollment at UAEU must have the prior approval of the student’s College.
    f)   Only courses in which the final grade was (C-) or better will be considered for transfer
         credit.


4. Application for Degree
    An undergraduate student  expecting to complete his  or her degree requirements  and
    graduate in a given academic semester must submit the appropriate application to his/her
    College Advising Unit and to the Office of the Admission and Registration Deanship no
    later than the last day to drop a course without failure for the academic semester.


5. Certification of Degree Completion
    a)   The student’s Academic College confirms completion of degree requirements for all
         Bachelor’s Degrees.
    b)   Subject to confirmation in the University’s official Student Information System, the
         Office of the     Admission and Registration           Deanship     certifies degree completion and
         degree awards.
    c)   Degrees are awarded at the end of each academic semester – fall, spring, and summer semesters.
    d)   The official date of a degree award is the final day of the academic semester as specified
         in the Academic Calendar, regardless of the date when the degree is certified.


6. Graduation Standing
    The academic performance of Bachelor’s                  Degree recipients may be characterized for
    summary “Graduation Standing” as follows:

7. Graduation Honors
    a)   The  University  recognizes  two  levels  of  outstanding  academic  achievements                          for
         Bachelor’s Degree recipients at the time of graduation based on CGPA:
         1)   First Class Honors – CGPA of 4.00
         2)   Second Class Honors – CGPA of at least 3.60
    b)   To qualify for Graduation Honors, a student must have completed at least 75% of his or
         her degree credits at UAEU.
    c)   A student who failed a course calculated towards his CGPA  (F, FA and) will not qualify
         for graduation with honors.
