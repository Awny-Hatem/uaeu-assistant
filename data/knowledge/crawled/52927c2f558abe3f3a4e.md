## Research > Overview
The College of Engineering (COE) faculty members are dedicated to pursuing both basic and applied research that will meet the needs of the UAE and the world at large. Major focus areas of research within the College are strongly tied to the country’s strategic goals in terms of sustainable development for communities, energy, water, and the environment.

A large number of research grants are being undertaken by the College faculty and students, resulting in numerous publications, conference participation and patents during each academic year. Research projects include seed and interdisciplinary projects funded by College, University Grants funded through a competitive process, and externally funded research projects funded by different government and industries such as the Emirates Foundation, NRF “University-Industry Collaboration” grants (U-IRCA), Hamdan Award in Medicine, ADNOC, JCCP, and others. The College of Engineering maintains strong links with industry and provides research and consultancy services to meet their needs.

Faculty members have formed international collaborations through international funding agencies such as the British Council Collaborative Research Projects, PMI 2, or the joint research projects like the UAEU – SQU Joint Research Projects, or through joint supervision of PhD students with faculty members at other universities, e.g. Imperial College, University of Marseille, Florida State, U. North Carolina Greensboro, and others. Faculty members also engage in summer research collaboration visits with various universities and summer internships such as the JCCP/JPI research projects/internships.

Research outcomes include research reports to sponsors, journal and international conference papers, and other outcomes such as books and book chapters, patents and patent applications, and participation in scientific conferences.

## Research > Opportunities
The College of Engineering is dedicated to continuous engagement of its faculty members and students (graduate and undergraduate) in research and scholarly activities within their field of specialization. Research funding is available to COE researchers through several opportunities from the university, the college and local/international industries and government agencies.

1- UAEU Opportunities

Available to undergraduate and graduate students and all faculty members in the UAEU. Applications are submitted through the ERMSS system or to the Deputy Vice Chancellor for Research and Graduate Studies Office.

a) Ph.D. Scholarship:

Available for: qualified national students.

Applications are submitted through the College of Graduate Studies.

b) Ph.D. Fellowship:

Available for: qualified Ph.D. students.

Applications are submitted through the College of Graduate Studies.

c) Summer Undergraduate Research Experiences (SURE):

The purpose of the SURE program is to promote and support the engagement of undergraduate students in research, thereby, providing the students with exposure to and training in conducting research while working in teams with other undergraduate and graduate students under the supervision of a faculty member. Team members: one faculty member, one graduate student (Master or Doctoral level) and three to four undergraduate students.

Maximum Budget: AED 45,000.

Applications are submitted through the ERMSS system.

d) Research UPAR:

Maximum Budget: AED 500,000.

Available for: all UAEU faculty members.

Duration: two years.

e) Start Up Research Grant:

Maximum Budget: AED 500,000.

Available for: all newly appointed UAEU faculty members.

Duration: two years.

f) Center Based Research Grants:

Maximum Budget: AED 500,000.

Available for: all UAEU faculty members, projects have to be affiliated with one of the research centers in UAEU.

Duration: two years.

2- College of Engineering Opportunities

Available to COE faculty members. Faculty members are encouraged to involve undergraduate and graduate students in these types of research projects. Applications are submitted through the ERMSS system.

a) Seed Research Grants:

Maximum: AED 30,000 and a minimum of 20% of the budget should be allocated to undergraduate or graduate students.

Duration: one year.

b) Interdisciplinary Research Grants:

Maximum: AED 120,000.

Duration: two years.

Available for: COE faculty members as PI in collaboration with faculty members from other. Colleges within the UAEU.

c) Graduate Teaching Assistant for Master Students (GTA):

Available for: COE qualified master students with thesis option with monthly stipend for one year renewable for up to two years.

d) Graduate Teaching Assistant for PhD Students (GTA):

Available for: COE qualified PhD students with monthly stipend for one year renewable for up to three years.

e) Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA):

Full-time qualified graduate students can apply for GRA through the COE to be supported by either internally or externally funded research projects.

The purpose is to engage students in technical areas related to Aerospace Engineering. The projects must be conducted under supervision of a faculty advisor. A mentor from the industry is preferred for co-supervision. Two to four students and their advisors (with at least one from ME) can apply for awards up to AED 20,000 for an individual research, design, and/or development projects. Preference will be given to aerospace industry initiated and driven projects. The following are the needed student qualifications:

Full time students enrolled in one of the UAEU engineering, sciences, and/or technology degree programs.

Making satisfactory progress toward their degree program of study.

Enrollment in the UAEU Minor in Aerospace Engineering Program is desirable.

Minimum duration for the project execution/implementation is two semesters.

## Research > Emirates Journal for Engineering Research
The Emirates Journal for Engineering Research (EJER), ISSN: 1022-9892, was established in 1988. The journal was initially named ‘Dirasat Handasiyyah’ meaning ‘Engineering Studies’. In 1996 the Journal name was changed to the ‘Emirates Journal for Engineering Research’. (EJER)

The EJER publishes basic and applied research articles in the various fields of Engineering and Applied Sciences, including architectural engineering, civil engineering, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, chemical engineering, petroleum engineering, environmental sciences, computer sciences, and other related fields. The journal is intended to serve a wide range of professionals, scientists, specialists and researchers in all engineering areas.

The Journal is a peer-reviewed journal and is published quarterly, by the College of Engineering, United Arab Emirates University.

The current editor of the EJER is Prof. Mohammad Jawaid CSci FIMMM , Chemical & Petroleum Engineering Department, College of Engineering, United Arab Emirates University.

Please contact the Editor-in-Chief at ejer@uaeu.ac.ae for further information.
