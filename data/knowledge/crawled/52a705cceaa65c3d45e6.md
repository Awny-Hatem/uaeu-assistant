## Services
The Counseling and Wellbeing Service provides a range of individual and group services with preventive, developmental and therapeutic goals that will help students develop and progress by understanding the reality in which they live and overcoming the difficulties they face throughout the university years and beyond in order to prepare for a successful future. The services include:

Individual therapy.

Group therapy.

Psychological assessments.

Providing specialized consultations.

Provide resources and continuing education such as workshops and training programs to the UAEU community.
