# Our Startups

## Our Startups
This section features startups participating in the Business Incubator's programs.

## Our Startups > Datcin
Datcin is developing an organic antibiotic alternative that eliminates most bacterial diseases, improves feed efficiency, helps prevent antimicrobial resistance, and retains the benefits of conventional antibiotics. The solution has been successfully pilot-tested and aims to provide a more sustainable approach to animal health.

## Our Startups > Dovvi
Dovvi is a maternal-health wearable designed to track fetal movement and maternal vital signs, providing real-time reassurance, early risk alerts, and emotional connection throughout pregnancy. The solution aims to support mothers and healthcare providers with continuous and accessible pregnancy monitoring.

## Our Startups > Ghers
Ghers is an AI-powered marketplace that connects UAE farms directly with customers, eliminating intermediaries. By enabling farmers to earn more, customers to pay less, and produce to remain fresher, the platform aims to strengthen local agriculture and contribute to national food security.

## Our Startups > NovaPyr
NovaPyr is developing a patented oral GLP-1 tablet designed to replace injectable therapies. The solution aims to improve patient compliance and comfort while providing a more convenient approach to diabetes care worldwide.

## Our Startups > Cell Energy
Cell Energy offers an integrated and sustainable solution that uses hydroxyethyl cellulose (HEC) derived from date palm waste, combined with electrocoagulation and CO₂ capture. The technology is designed to treat brine, enhance oil recovery, and contribute to the UAE’s sustainability goals.

## Our Startups > AlphaTwisters.AI
AlphaTwisters.AI is an AI-driven biotechnology company developing next-generation antiviral peptides designed to eliminate respiratory viruses. The solution aims to transform antiviral treatment and contribute to the advancement of global immune defence.

## Our Startups > Dhoop
Dhoop develops an indoor sunlight exposure system that uses smart reflectors and controlled positioning to provide safe and personalized access to natural light indoors. The project addresses climate and lifestyle barriers to sunlight exposure and vitamin D, with potential applications in wellness, residential, healthcare, and built-environment settings.

## Our Startups > GlucoSense
GlucoSense is a non-invasive glucose monitoring solution that uses smartphone-based optical sensing and signal analytics to support frequent blood glucose monitoring without conventional finger-prick testing. The project aims to improve patient comfort, reduce recurring consumable needs, and encourage better adherence to diabetes monitoring routines.

## Our Startups > NOORACT
NOORACT is an AI-powered platform for creating interactive digital experiences across museums, malls, theme parks, and public facilities. The solution combines digital human interaction, robotics-enabled experiences, and Arabic-language and Emirati-dialect localization to help cultural and commercial venues enhance visitor engagement while reducing the time and effort required to create interactive content.
