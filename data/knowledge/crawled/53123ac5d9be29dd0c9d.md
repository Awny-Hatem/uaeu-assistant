## Application Process > Application Process
1. If you have fulfilled the admission requirements for the MPA Program, you will have to fill out the application form.

Application Form

2. Once you have done this, you will have to send it by email. Then you will receive a reply giving you an appointment for an interview with the MPA admission committee.

Documents
1- Copy of Valid passport; National ID; Family Book (for UAE nationals); Visa (for the international Applicants)
2- Original copy of your undergraduate transcript*/ degree with marks included
3- Copy of IELTS (Academic): 6.0 or equivalent
4- Two letters of reference
5- Your current CV / résumé
6- Guarantee for payment of the fees
7- 4 passport photos (6x4)/(with your name written on the reverse)
8- Required Application Processing fee **

*For UAEU graduates official transcript will be provided by university registrar **Non Refundable Application Program Fee of AED 1000

If you are a graduate of a university located outside the UAE, you need to obtain an equivalency of you degree from the UAE Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research. For details on the procedure, please contact the ministry at:

Telephone: +97126951280 / +97126951300

Mailing Address: PO Box: 45253, Abu Dhabi, UAE
