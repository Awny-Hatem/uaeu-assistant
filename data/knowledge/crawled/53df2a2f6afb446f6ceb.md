                                 Procedure for Using of UAEU Sports Facilities
                                                                     In the Islamic Institute



Major Procedural Changes in This Release

New procedure.


Procedure
    The                      Student                      Activities                      and                      leadership Department shall be responsible for
managing all university sports facilities in the Islamic Institute (Grass Soccer Field -
Gym - Open Courts - playgrounds - Swimming pools - indoor courts). This includes
scheduling and defining the cost for using these facilities by external parties and
based on the following guidelines:





Definitions:

        -                                           A period is one hour (morning or evening)

        -                                           Half  a day is  6 periods to be chosen (From 8:00 AM until 8:00 PM)
        -                                           A whole day is 12 periods ( From 8:00 AM till 8:00 PM)

Charges:

                      Charges that are collected shall be deposited into the non-class activities
                      fund to be used by SA&L and according to the university rules and
                      procedures.


Forms:
                               •                                 To request using the sports facilities for one semester, the attached Form
                                            (A) shall be used.
                               •                         To reserve one of the sports facilities for short period(s) of time, the
                                            attached Form (B) shall be used.


Terms and conditions:
      In   coordination   with   the   office   of  the Assistant Provost and Dean of Students, the
Secretary General will approve and publish terms and conditions for use of UAEU
sports facilities. All parties must abide by these terms and conditions.
