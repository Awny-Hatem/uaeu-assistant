## Emirates Health Services > Assessment
Assessment of Professional Skills and Competency

The Emirate Health Services (EHS) incorporates the Examination and Assessment Office.

The objectives of the examination and assessment office are:

To provide logistical support to faculty based examination Boards and to conduct Professional Licensing Examinations.

Development and storage of test items.

Construction of examination papers, OSCEs, vivas and other examination formats.

Vetting, acceptance and registration of candidates for examinations.

Administration of examinations.

Post hoc analysis of examinations.

Remitting results to appropriate authorities and candidates.
