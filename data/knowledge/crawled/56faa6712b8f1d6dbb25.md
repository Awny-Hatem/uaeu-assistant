## College of Science > Overview
The College of Science was among the first Colleges to be established since the inception of United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) in 1976. The College has five academic departments: Department of Biology, Department of Chemistry, Department of Geosciences, Department of Mathematical Sciences and the Department of Physics.
