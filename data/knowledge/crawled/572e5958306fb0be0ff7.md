## Counseling Unit
The counseling unit focuses its efforts on supporting exceptional children and their families by offering specialized counseling programs that help them overcome challenges and enhance their quality of life. Our main objectives include the following:

Providing Social Support : We identify and mitigate family and social factors that may hinder or nurture children' talents, providing comprehensive support to promote their achievements.

Providing Psychological Support : Our team offers psychological support to children with exceptionalities and their families, fostering emotional well-being and resilience within the community.

Supporting Parents and Families : We provide resources and guidance to help families understand and support their children’s unique needs, ensuring they feel equipped to navigate challenges.

Raising Awareness : We focus on disseminating best practices and successful educational programs to educators and parents, enhancing awareness and understanding of exceptionalities both in the UAE and globally.
