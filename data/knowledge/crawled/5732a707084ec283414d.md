## The Internship of a lifetime: Our journey towards Expo 2020
Excitement is in the air as we advance towards the highly anticipated EXPO 2020!

To make things interesting, UAEU is the only university that will have its own pavilion amidst hundreds of countries – an achievement within its own right!

We are six graduate students, who have been offered a tremendous opportunity to work alongside the UAEU EXPO 2020 team as part of our graduation project. We feel honored be able to engage with diverse minds, learn throughout the journey and be part of all of the excitement.

We have already been invited to attend meetings and take part in the whole process including brainstorming ideas and giving input on branding, events and so much more. Some of the tasks we will be working on includes developing content for the website and tv series, writing news stories, conducting interviews, managing events and creating social media content.

Expo 2020 is a massive event for the United Arab Emirates, and an opportunity everyone is eager to be part of. It is only the beginning, but our involvement promises to be a very enriching and rewarding experience!

By Fatima AlKaabi
