## 30 minutes for Us > 30 minutes for Us
Another current ongoing project of the center is 30 minutes for us, which allows colleagues to meet and talk about topics related to their wellbeing with the motive of building high quality connections. It is based on the high quality connection theory by Prof. Jane Dutton (University of Michigan) in which high quality connections can improve the organization and employee's capabilities.
