## Graduate Programs > Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential in Financial Engineering
The Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential (CBMC) in Financial Engineering is designed to provide participants with a strong foundation in derivative securities, financial engineering techniques, and risk management practices employed in modern financial markets. The program aims to develop the analytical and practical skills required to understand, value, and utilize financial derivatives for investment, hedging, and risk management purposes.

The program comprises 6 credit hours, delivered through two 3-credit-hour courses: Fundamentals of Financial Derivatives and Financial Engineering. It is intended for bachelor's degree holders from non-finance disciplines seeking to broaden their knowledge of finance and acquire specialized expertise in derivatives and financial risk management. The program is also suitable for professionals seeking advanced knowledge in these areas. In addition, it is open to high-achieving undergraduate students from non-finance disciplines who have completed at least 75 credit hours and attained a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0. This is a non-stackable, credit-bearing micro-credential program offered as a standalone professional qualification.

## Graduate Programs > Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential in Financial Engineering > Program Objectives
Equip students with the knowledge and practical skills in derivative securities and financial engineering required to support investment, hedging, and financial risk management decisions.

Develop students' ability to analyze market risks and evaluate financial instruments using quantitative methods, and financial engineering models.

• Enhance students' ability to communicate financial analyses and risk management strategies effectively to professional and non-professional stakeholders.
