## Message to Alumni > Message to Alumni
The Faculty of law has established the Alumni Association aiming at strengthening the communications with its alumni across the past years and also aiming at:

To reserve good relationships with its alumni.

To follow up the needs of the labor market in the UAE through staying in touch with the alumni.

Exchanging consultations, support and scientific opinions on common issues.

Providing communication channel for the alumni through which they can get in touch and jump over the gap between generations.

To invite the alumni to participate in the different events organized by the College in order to utilize their field experiences
