## Director Message
Welcome to the Emirates Center for Happiness Research. The Center is involved in cutting edge research in the field of happiness and wellbeing to advance our knowledge and understanding of how societies thrive.

We understand the importance of diversity and engaging everyone, fostering a sense of belonging and helping members of the community to unleash their talents and reach their full potential. Our passion to contribute to the field of happiness and positivity is deeply rooted in the rich traditions of the UAE, embracing wisdom that have strengthens our country, protects our families and inspires our youth.

Join us and together we can make meaningful change!

Dr. Muna Almansoori ,

Director of the Emirates Center for Happiness Research
