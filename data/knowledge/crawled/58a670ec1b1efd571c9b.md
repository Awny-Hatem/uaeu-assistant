## Counseling and wellbeing
Through this service, UAEU provides students with specialized counseling support so they can achieve a greater psychological, social and academic balance. This service helps students to face the difficulties and challenges they face in all aspects of their lives. • Also, it aims to help students understand themselves and identify their capacity to develop skills and overcome weaknesses. It also aims to help them build relationships with their surrounding family members, friends and classmates. They also aim to develop independence and self-reliance. Goals: • Providing psychotherapy and counseling services. • Conducting psychological assessments. • Promoting mental health and wellbeing of the UAEU community. • Providing consultation for other departments in the UAEU regarding psychological and wellbeing services. • Providing resources and continuing education such as workshops and training programs to the UAEU community. Our services: • Individual Therapy • Group Therapy • Psychological Assessments • Providing resources and continuing education such as workshops and training programs to the UAEU community.

## Counseling and wellbeing > Does communicate with parents and notify them of psychological difficulties experienced by the student? What are the limits of confidentiality in Counseling and Wellbeing Service?
Counseling and Wellbeing Service means to confidentiality and privacy, not to share any information from the requesting and unaware of prior written consent. In addition, is requesting privacy breach in only two cases and its flag and explaining in advance to the student before starting guided sessions. First case: the student poses a risk to themselves or others. Second case: the occurrence of sexual harassment against children.

## Counseling and wellbeing > How many and the necessary duration of meetings?
The number of sessions depends on the student's goals and what she/he want to achieve, where the total is between 6 to 8 sessions. The duration of each session 45 minutes to 60 minutes.

## Counseling and wellbeing > After the sessions began, how long it requires the student to notice the change in her/his personality?
The change process is not immediate but need time, effort, inner desire, determination, and persistence, so you should start with a practical plan for change and seek to implement them and modify them to suit the student to note the change. In addition, change depends on the student and the type of difficulty.

## Counseling and wellbeing > Will it be added in the academic record it took therapy sessions?
No. Counseling and Wellbeing Service is obliged to confidentiality of all student data and not share it outside of the center except in cases requiring it in advance and written approval of student knowledge. Where the requesting service center after 4-5 years to destroy the file.

## Counseling and wellbeing > You will analyze the personality of the student from the first session by reading the line or by looking at her/him?
No. Your personality analysis is not given nor have read the line but scientific tests supported student.
