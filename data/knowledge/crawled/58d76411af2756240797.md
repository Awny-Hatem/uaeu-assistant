5. Domain Names

Overview
Defines policy and procedures governing requesting and assignment of domain names                                                                                   .

Scope
Applies to all Colleges                                                                     and               Departments              that               appl y       for domain and sub-                     domain name
registration within the UAEU                                       for websites or other use of the Internet and guards against
improper use of other domain names            .

Objective
The objective of this policy is to ensure that all UAEU information and                                                  Internet services
operate under the uaeu.ac.ae domain name and not under other domain names.

Policy
1.                                                           The   Internet domain name assigned to UAEU                                                                is uaeu.ac.ae. UAEU                                recognizes uaeu.ac.ae
        as its official domain name.
2.                                                           UAEU                            owns the uaeu.ac.ae domain and governs its use for the benefit of its academic,
        research, communication, and other activities and interests. UAEU                                                       has the right to protect
        the use of its name in all situations.
3.                                  The integrity and reputation of UAEU rely on consistent and accurate content on the
        uaeu.ac.ae domain and on any websites that relate to, refer to, or could be perceived as
        representing UAEU.
4.                                                           UAEU                     Information Technology Services (UITS) manages the uaeu.ac.ae domain,
        coordinates naming for new domains, and manages website resources for the faculty,
        administrators, and students operating on behalf of UAEU or any of its units.
5.                                    UITS has sole rights of discretion in the administration of the domain name, and may
