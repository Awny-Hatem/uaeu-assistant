                   Electronic Submission Tutorial

                               Prepared by Dr. Abdurrahman Almekhlafi



  Dear all
  Here are the steps that you need to follow when submitting your manuscript via the journal site.
  Submitting your manuscript electronically will enable you to track your submission all the way
  through. For a successful submission, you have to follow the following steps:.

  1) Log in to the journal site using the link below.
                        http://scholarworks.uaeu.ac.ae/ijre/
  2) Click on Submit Article.

## PDF page 2
3) Create your own account by clicking on Sign up.
























4) Fill in the form and click on Sign Up.

  6) ) Log in to the journal site using the link below and repeat the second step.

                                            http://scholarworks.uaeu.ac.ae/ijre/



















  7) Login into your account using your email and the password you selected for yourself

 9) Once you accept, you will be able to start the submission process.
