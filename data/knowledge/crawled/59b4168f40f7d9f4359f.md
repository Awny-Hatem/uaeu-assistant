## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Greenhouses and Shadehouses
The greenhouse section comprises ten tunnel type greenhouses, which are used for research, education, demonstration and production. The picture shows the new biotechnology greenhouse, which can accommodate experiments on transgenic plants, and small-scale hydroponics. A part of the greenhouse section is currently under renovation.

Greenhouses with open soil are used for conventional and organic production of vegetables.

The farm also has three shadehouses, of which the largest accommodates the plant nursery. The nursery produces crop plants for transfer to the field and the greenhouse, as well as ornamental plants. The nursery is also used to train students on plant propagation.

Members of the UAEU can request greenhouse or shadehouse space for their research. Click here to download the greenhouse space request form.
