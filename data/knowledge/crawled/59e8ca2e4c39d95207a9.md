GRADUATION DATA
     The following are the graduation data of the College of Education CAEP programs –Early Childhood
Education (ECE), Special Education (SPED, and Health & Physical Education (HPE).

                    Table 1    shows    number of completers in the last three academic years. There was an
          average of 115 graduates from AY 2020-2021 to AY 2022-2023.

          completers      of  the  ECE  program,  33%  are          completers      of  the  SPED  program,  and          10%  are
