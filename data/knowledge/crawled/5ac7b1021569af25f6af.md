## Our Focus > Evidence-Based Research
To get a detailed insight by conducting research and analysis on the concepts of Positive Psychology and Well-being of the people living in the United Arab Emirates.

## Our Focus > Capacity - Building
To conduct Regional and International Conferences, Seminars and Symposiums in the areas related to Happiness and Well-being. Also, to offer short courses and certification in skills associated with happiness and Well-being.

## Our Focus > Professional Consultancy
To advise the government and the organizations on how to improve the well-being at workplace and how this can have a positive impact on their colleague's overall well-being.
