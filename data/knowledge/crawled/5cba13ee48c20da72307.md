## CETL Communities of Practice
The Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning (CETL) strongly supports the development of Communities of Practice (CoPs) as an effective approach to knowledge management and exchange within the university. These communities not only facilitate the sharing of expertise across UAEU but also provide faculty members with valuable opportunities to assume leadership roles and contribute their specialized skills to the broader teaching community.

## CETL Communities of Practice > Faculty Fellows Program (FFP)
Published academic-year scope: 2025-2026
The Faculty Fellows Program (FFP) is designed to recognize and empower exceptional faculty members and instructors who demonstrate leadership in teaching and learning. Faculty Fellows play a central role in organizing and leading professional development activities and events at UAEU through CETL.

Participants are selected through a competitive application process based on their unique expertise and proven commitment to pedagogical excellence. Through their leadership, Faculty Fellows promote innovation and engagement in teaching and learning across the university.
