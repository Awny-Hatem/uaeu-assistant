## AERO215 Thermofluids
This course aims to provide students with essential understanding of the concepts of applied thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and heat transfer. Topics covered include: Second law of thermodynamics analysis, gas power cycles, basics of compressible flows, momentum equation for finite control volumes, Bernoulli equation, Basic heat transfer by conduction and radiation.
Thermofluids (AERO215) > Credit Hours : 3

Thermofluids (AERO215) > Prerequisites
GENG220 with a minimum grade D
Thermofluids (AERO215) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Apply the first and second laws of thermodynamics to analyze gas power cycles.

Explain the basics of compressible flows and shock waves.

Describe fluid properties, the conservation of mass, momentum and energy, and Bernoulli equation.

Analyze flow inside a pipe and external flow forces (lift and drag).

Apply steady conduction, convection, and basic radiation heat transfer modes.

Demonstrate ability to communicate effectively.
