## Internal Medicine Department > Teaching > Undergraduate Teaching
The Department has major teaching input into the organ system course, clinical skills, and junior and senior clerkships. We also monitor the delivery of course objectives and carry out evaluations measuring the desired competencies including the final integrated examinations.

## Internal Medicine Department > Teaching > Postgraduate Teaching
Department members are involved in residency training programmes in preparation for the Arab Board and the MRCP (UK) diploma examination. We have a number of established examiners to Royal College of Physician of London who provide regular teaching sessions for residents and other junior doctors taking the MRCP diploma examination here in Al Ain.
