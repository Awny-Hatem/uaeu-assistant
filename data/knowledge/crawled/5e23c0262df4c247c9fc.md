## Success of CEC’s Bridging Courses at UAEU
Published academic-year scope: 2023-2024 | 2024-2025
The Continuing Education Center successfully established and operated a comprehensive Bridging Courses system within a short period of time by:

Developing a complete organizational framework and implementation structure.

Serving approximately 3,460 students during Academic Year 2023/2024 and the first semester of Academic Year 2024/2025.

Opening and operating 162 course sections while providing qualified instructors, facilities, assessments, and academic support.

Achieving measurable academic outcomes, with 2,332 students successfully completing Bridging Courses and retaining their university enrollment.
