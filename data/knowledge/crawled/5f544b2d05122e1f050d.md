11. Research Involving Human Subjects

Overview
Provides information related to UAEU requirements and resources for research involving
human subjects.

Scope
Applies to all activities that involve research with human subjects (in      whole or in part) for
which at least one of the following criteria is met:
1.                                                      The research is conducted by or under the direction of faculty members, staff                                 members, or
      students of the UAEU;
2.                                               The research is conducted by an external organization, with sponsorship from the UAEU
      or with participation of its faculty          members, staff          members, or students, or using any
      propert y or facility of the University;
3.                                           The research involves the use of UAEU’s public or nonpublic information to identify or
      contact human research subjects or prospective subjects.

The   Universit y   upholds the ethical rights of human subjects of the research project and gives
effect to that right by the application of this P  olicy.

      sensitivities in UAE                  society, and the reputation of the UAEU. This P                                      olicy is designed to
      ensure that human subjects are adequately protected during any research project
      conducted within or in connection with the UAEU            . Procedures must be designed and
      implemented with due care to follow University Policies and applicable l               aws.
