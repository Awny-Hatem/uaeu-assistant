## Academic Personnel Office > Overview
The Academic Personnel Office (APO) is responsible for facilitating the recruitment, appointment, development, appraisal and retention of academic employees, in accordance with University Policies and Procedures. The Office oversees, develops, interprets, and implements the University Policies and Procedures that are related to the faculty and other members of the academic personnel community. It provides technical and administrative services and acts as an up-to-date source of information in all areas related to academic personnel. The Office works closely with the Office of the Vice Chancellor, Deputy Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs (Provost), Faculties, Academic Departments, Human Resources, other departments, and Academic Employees on all matters related to academic personnel. The Director of the Academic Personnel Office provides leadership, management and administration to the APO, oversees its operations, and reports to the Provost.
