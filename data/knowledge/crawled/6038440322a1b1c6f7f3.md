## UAEU Mobile App
The UAEU Mobile app has been developed to enhance the overall academic experience for students, faculty, and staff at UAEU. The app has been designed in alignment with the Division of Information Technology's (DoIT) strategic goals, with a specific focus on the Smart Campus & Emerging Technology Solutions initiative.

The UAEU Mobile app plays a critical role in achieving these goals, by providing users with access to a range of features and services that are designed to streamline campus life and enhance academic performance.

## Service available to:
Faculty members, students, staff, alumni and general public.
