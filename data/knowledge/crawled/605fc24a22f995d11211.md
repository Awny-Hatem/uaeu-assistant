Mydan  Forum 2020

College of Education at UAEU cordially invites you to attend the online Mydan Forum 2020 that will take place through Microsoft Teamson
Tuesday, October 20 and Wednesday, 21 from 4-6pm.  The Forum aims to highlight the role of teacher professional learning in developing the
learners’ personal skills in parallel with academic skills.  The proposed topics offered by knowledgeable educators will enrich the teaching and
                                     learning process with quality practices in the field experience.
