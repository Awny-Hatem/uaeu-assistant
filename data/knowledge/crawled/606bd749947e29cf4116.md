## Field Experience Office > Framework of the Field Experience
The Framework of Early Field Experience in the college of Education at the UAEU is developed to enhance the clinical experiences for every pre-service teacher education student across all years of study. These includes classroom observations, assisting teachers, university supervisor, and developing and practicing the duties of a teacher, along with locally and/or internationally service learning and school attachments overseas.
