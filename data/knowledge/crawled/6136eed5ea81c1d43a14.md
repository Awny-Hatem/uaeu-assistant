1.                                                                                                                                INTRODUCTION OF OPEN DATA PAGE
         In alignment with its mission to serve the UAE community and uphold values of transparency, integrity,
         and    accountability,    United    Arab    Emirates    University    (UAEU)    is    committed    to    providing    unrestricted
         access   to   data   concerning   its   core   functions.   These   guidelines   delineate   the   procedure   for   governing
         the publication of data on the Open Data webpage on the UAEU website, encompassing the processes
         of identification, auditing , compilation, and publication of institutional data.

2.                                                                                                                                MAIN OBJECTIVES
         This          document          serves          a          pivotal          role          in          promoting          the          principles          of          transparency,          accessibility,          and
         accountability  in  the  realm  of  open  data  management .  The  purpose  of  preparing  this  document  is  to
         provide     guidelines     in     comprehensive     and     clear     instructions     on     how     data     users     and     the     organization
         should      handle,      publish,      and      manage      open      data      effectively.

3.                                                                                                                                DEFINITION OF OPEN DATA
         It        refers        to        the        data        that        is        primarily        intended        to        provide        an        open        and        comprehensive        view        of        the
         Education   sector,   allowing   any   individual   or   entity   to   access,   use,   reuse,   distribute   and   share   it   freely
         without technical, financial, or legal restrictions, taking in consideration the owner rights. Examples of
         Open     Data     documents:     Aggregated     Data,     Administrative     Data     and     Geospatial     Data.

4.                                                                                                                                SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
         The       guidelines       apply       to       any       data       users       who       are       administrators/researchers       from       any       government/
         private       organizations,       or       the       general       public       at       national       or       international       level.

5.                                                                                                                                OPEN DATA CHARACTERISTICS/FEATURES
              •                                     The          UAEU          Open          Data          webpage          publishes          institutional          data          encompassing          statistics,          facts,          and
                  reports               about               the               University  's               business               functions.
              •                                     Data must meet criteria for public disclosure, i.e.  , it can be made available for anyone to use, reuse,
                  distribute,   or   share   with   others   without   technical,   financial,   or   legal   restrictions.
              •                                     The  publication  of  data  on  the  Open  Data  Webpage  necessitates  approval  from  the  relevant  Data
                  Trustee, the senior University manager with the highest level of strategic and policy-setting authority
                  for the corresponding functional area.
              •                              Easily accessible and downloadable in various formats, such as XML  , PDF, Excel and CSV.
              •                                     Each report published on the open data view page includes a feature for user ratings.
              •                                     The UAEU open data policy can be accessed on the open data page.
              •                                     In addition to the published data, if users require additional data reports, a request form is provided
                  on the website for them to fill.
              •                                     The                   graphical                   representation                   of                   data                   and                   statistics                   is                   separately                   published                   within                   the                   “Data
                  Visualization”                                                                  webpage.
              •                                     The data-driven reports that support decision-making have been uploaded based on their type and
                  year.
              •                                     The        data        must        conform        to        the        Open        Data        Guidelines        of        the        mGovernment        posted        on        the        UAE
                  Government             official             portal             on             the             following             link.

6.                                                                                                                                GUIDING PRINCIPLES/PROCEDURES

          •                                     The University Outreach Department (UOD) engages with UAEU units responsible for key functional areas
             (e.g .  ,      Office      of      Institutional      Effectiveness,      Provost      Office,      Secretary      General,      Student      Affairs,      Research
             Office, etc.) at least once per calendar year.  This engagement is aimed at inquiring about the availability
             of data from their business functions intended for public access.
          •                                     Units may also contact , at any time, UOD to propose data for publication to the public.
          •                                     UOD oversees the implementation of the guidelines governing the publication of open data on the UAEU
             website.
          •                                     The Office of Institutional Effectiveness (OIE) is responsible for preparing and auditing statistics related
             to  student  admission,  enrollment ,  and  success,  as  well  as  faculty  and  staff  demographics.
          •                                     UOD and OIE are tasked with uploading open data files to the Open Data Webpage and Bayanat .ae
          •                                     The Customer Experience Support Unit (CESU) takes charge of managing University responses to public
             requests and inquiries submitted through the Open Data Form available on the Open Data Webpage.
          •                                     CESU directs these requests and inquiries to the relevant UAEU units and diligently follows up with the
             units   until   a   response   is   provided   to   the   requester.
