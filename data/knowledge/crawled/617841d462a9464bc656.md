## Happiness and Wellbeing Course
Another initiative of the center which was very successful was the Introduction of the Happiness and Wellbeing course which was taught to the Undergraduate students.

In this course the students were taught a number of scientifically validated strategies to boost their happiness and wellbeing across several contexts, such as their future workplace, home, academics and their community/social life.
