Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

UAEU SMART PLAN
    Your whole degree,
    planned in one place.

    Live for Biology students from 24 August



Dear Biology student,

From 24 August, you will plan your courses and register through Smart
Plan — and Biology is the first programme at UAEU to use it.

Smart Plan reads your academic record and lays out every course you
still need, semester by semester, through to graduation. Move things
around, swap courses, and see whether a change would delay you. When
your registration window opens, you register from the same screen.

Here is what you need to know.

Sign in


Use your normal UAEU username and password.

Doing things in your plan


Every task below starts the same way: press CHANGE on the course.

Register for a course

1   Open the shaded semester
    the one you can register for now.

2   CHANGE, then Mark to register
    on each course you want.

3   Press Check Plan, then Save
    the status must read Valid.

4   Press Update registration
    this completes your registration.

3   Press Update registration

Register and drop


You register from inside your plan — there is no need to go to Banner
separately.

To register

## PDF page 9
If registration is refused, the reason comes from Banner — a hold, a full
section, a prerequisite, or your window not open yet. Check for holds
first.


Three things to know


    Check your plan after every change.
1   Move a course or fill an elective, then press Check Plan before you
    save.

    Your advisor cannot register for you.
2   They can plan with you and mark recommended sections — you must
    sign in and register yourself.

    University rules are unchanged.
3   Holds, prerequisites, credit limits and registration windows work
    exactly as they do today.
