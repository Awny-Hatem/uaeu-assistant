## Student Success Indicators at UAEU > Overview
The success of our students and graduates, and their own estimation of the UAEU is at the heart of what we do within the academic program, through learning support services, and through the student life infrastructure.

Student success indicators including measures of enrolment, graduation and retention, attainment of learning outcomes, and students’ research experience are monitored by the University and shared with all stakeholders. These reports are central to the University’s internal processes for assessing students’ achievements and the effectiveness of teaching and learning.
