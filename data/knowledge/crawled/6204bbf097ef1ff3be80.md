## Year of Giving
The UAE government launched the National Strategy for the Year of Giving, to promote an integrated and sustainable regulatory framework for humanitarian and charitable work in the country, and to establish the culture of giving by involving all segments of society, and to meet various interests and fields in humanitarian and voluntary work.
