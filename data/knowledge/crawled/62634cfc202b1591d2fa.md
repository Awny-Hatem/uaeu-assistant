Akoun Business Idea Competition awards

    -   Student Meead Al Rashidi won first place for the annual Akoun Business Idea  Competition
    -   Student Khalid Murad won third place for the annual Akoun Business Idea  Competition

Two highly talented UAEU students won the first and Third places for the annual Akoun Business Idea
Competition.

The  Akoun  Business  Idea  Competition  awards  student  participants  from  UAE
colleges  and  universities  from  each  Emirate  for  their  innovative  ideas  and
challenging business plans. This year, Meead Al Rashidi, won the first prize of AED
25,000. And Khalid Murad, Mass Communication, won second prize (AED 10,000).

Akoun is an entrepreneurship awareness campaign designed to inspire and empower the youth of the
UAE to enter the private sector by setting up their own small/medium businesses

The competition is an initiative of the Abu Dhabi Council for Economic Development (ADCED) that is
supported by the Abu Dhabi Chamber of Commerce.

Kuder – Career Advisor Training (CAT)

In collaboration with Abu Dhabi Education Council (ADEC), the course Certified Career Advisor from Kuder was
offered to the center’s Career Counsellors. The course purpose is to equip individuals with the basic and
essential skills and knowledge needed to work with students in career exploration, career decision making, job
seeking,  and  job  placement.  The  course  consisted  of  ten  core  competencies  of  Career  Advising  by  the
International Association of Career Advisors (IACA). It is a ten days program, five days were offered on April
2015, the rest will be continued on October of the same year, and a summer project which will include the
implementation of the program.

Enhancement of Undergraduate Placement
    1-   The Job Navigator

The “Job Navigator” Online-posting tool has been established                  in
May 2011, in an effort to make recruiting from UAEU as easy and
efficient as possible, solidify partnerships with public and private
organizations,  provide  students  and  alumni  with  the  needed
placement  services  and  support  the         Emritization  efforts  of  the
UAE.

Throughout “Job Navigator”, the new job posting tool has significantly increased job placement opportunities
for UAE University graduates and has helped them in finding career opportunities related to their personal and
professional objectives. Furthermore, the new job posting tool has provided graduates with easy access to hiring
organizations of the UAE.

Since June 2014, the Job Navigator offered 1,246 vacancies for UAEU graduates at 81 partner organizations that
advertised jobs on the JN through the Center for Career Placement & Alumni. Of those 81, 45 are government
organizations (55%), 21 are semi-government (26%), 16 are private companies (19%).

Most of the advertised jobs were in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi (69%), Dubai (22%), Al Ain (5%), and (4%) in the
other Emirates of the UAE.

The UAE Young Ambassador Program – South Korea, China and Germany

To  emphasize  the  growing  importance  of  the  strategic  partnership
between  the  United  Arab  Emirates  and  China,  South  Korea  and
Germany, the Crown Prince Court of His Highness Sheikh Mohamed Bin
Zayed   Al   Nahyan   (CPC)   launched   the   “UAE   Young   Ambassador
Program”.

The program’s objective is to equip, develop and prepare young Emiratis to take on prominent future roles in
core   sectors,   lead   key  partnerships   and   strengthen   engagement   between   the   UAE   and   China/South
Korea/Germany. It is sponsored by the Crown Prince Court and jointly administered by CPC and the Ministry of
Foreign Affairs.

The CCPA coordinated the participation of UAEU students in the program. The program will take place in South
Korea, China and Germany over a three-week period in summer 2015 (starting end of June 2015) and will
comprise the following four main activities:

        Intensive language training
        Full-immersion experience of culture and values, including visits to significant cultural and heritage sites

Career Preparation Programs



Career Planning Services (CAP)

Throughout this Academic Year, the Center offered valuable career planning services aiming at assisting UAEU
students in setting goals and taking appropriate decisions related to their desired careers; in addition to,
equipping them with the needed employability skills to prepare them for the world of work. The total number of
the  various  programs  including  Group  Career  Counseling  Sessions,  Career  Workshops,  Career  Outreach
Programs, and Career Assessment Sessions is 56 attracting 2,050 attendees.

1) Individual Career Counseling Sessions and Consultations
The Center’s 2 Career Counselors offered 211 individual “one-to-one”                  sessions and consultations       that helped
UAEU male and female students in creating their own career plans and in developing a greater sense of self-
awareness relevant to their career development process.

2) Group Career Counseling Sessions
The Group Career Counseling sessions            are  designed to     provide   students with the needed support in issues
related to career exploration and career development through selecting the appropriate academic major. These
sessions help students in choosing the major that best suit their personality type, work attitudes, interests and
career choices.

3) Career Workshops and Outreach Programs:
Throughout this academic year, the CAP services offered a wide range of interactive workshops and outreach
programs to equip students with the needed employability skills and prepare them for the world of work. Major
offered programs included:



   How to Select Your Academic Major                           How to Get People to Say “Yes” at Work

   Effective Cover Letter & Resume Writing                     How to Shine at Work

   Steps to Interview Success                                  Business Etiquette


These workshops were offered by the Center’s Career Counselors as well as external presenters covering a
variety of career-related topics.


4) Career Assessment
Career Assessment tools are used to assist students in gaining a better understanding of their interests, values,
skills and work attitudes      and  to  help  career counselors      in supporting students in making the right decisions
about their careers. The Center will be looking for buying a new career assessment tool to provide better
services to UAEU students.
