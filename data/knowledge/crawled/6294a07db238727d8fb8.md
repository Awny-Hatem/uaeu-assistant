PhD studentships in the Department of Medical Microbiology and Immunology, College of
Medicine and Health Sciences.


Research projects:
Two PhD studentships available in the Department of Medical Microbiology and
Immunology (Viral Pathology Laboratory, Prof Gulfaraz Khan) investigating the role of
Epstein-Barr virus in the pathogenesis of Multiple sclerosis.

Requirement:
     •                                                                  Must have a Bachelor and Master’s degrees (minimum GPA: 3.0/4.00 for BSc and
           3.50/4.00 for MSc.)
     •                                                                  At least one of the degrees should be in: Veterinary Medicine, Medicine, Biomedical
           Science, Biological Science or closely related fields.
     •                                                                  Have an interest in virology and viral pathology.
     •                                                                  Have a score of at least 6.5 on the IELTS or an equivalent TOFEL score.
     •                                                                  Experience in working with animal models would be an advantage.


Studentship/Stipend
If accepted, the PhD candidate may be supported by the UAE University for 4 years with a
full tuition fee waiver, and health insurance. Competitive stipends of about 5,000 AED/
month may also be provided (for more detail see:
http://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/cgs/scholarship.shtml.

For more information, see the UAE University
https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/ or the CMHS https://cmhs.uaeu.ac.ae/en/                                           websites.

Only highly qualified candidates will be contacted.  Interested candidates can send CVs to:
G_khan@uaeu.ac.ae.
