Deanship of Enrollment



                                                    Obligation


Having been accepted to study at United Arab Emirates University in accordance with the
conditions and standards set forth by the university and within the limits reserved for
international students in the semester,


I hereby declare to:

1.  Approve and commit to paying the full tuition fees for each semester for the registered
    courses in that semester and the tuition fees incurred.

2.  Approve and commit to paying 50% of the tuition fees for the registered courses in that
    semester during the first week of study through the appropriate administration of the
    university.

3.  Accept and commit to completing the payment of the remaining tuition fees by the end of
    the fourth week from the beginning of the semester.

4.  This commitment is valid throughout the period of study of the student at the university.

5.  If the student does not pay the full tuition fees as mentioned above, the university will
    withdraw all courses registered during the semester and will take any other measures as
    deemed appropriate.
