## Eligibility
Applicants shall fall within the following categories:

## Eligibility > Faculty members, instructors, and staff
Full-time employees of the United Arab Emirates University, including faculty members, instructors, academic assistants, teaching assistants, research assistants, laboratory specialists, administrative employees, and other UAEU staff.

## Eligibility > Students
Undergraduate and postgraduate students enrolled at the United Arab Emirates University, provided that they have at least two academic semesters remaining before graduation at the time of application for the award.
