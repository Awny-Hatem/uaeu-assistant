## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > What you will learn?
The NRPP program is structured into seven integrated modules and culminates in a hands-on research placement. Each part of the program is designed to develop specific competencies aligned with national research priorities and your career development.

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Core Modules (Weeks 1–9)
Module 1: Research Methodologies

Learn the foundations of designing robust research studies, identifying valid questions, and conducting ethical inquiry.

Module 2: Industry-Aligned Innovation

Understand how research and innovation can solve real-world industry problems through multidisciplinary collaboration.

Module 3: AI in Research

Explore how artificial intelligence tools can support literature reviews, data analysis, idea generation, and visualization.

Module 4: Community-Driven Projects

Develop research proposals that tackle social, environmental, and economic challenges in the UAE and beyond.

Module 5: Research Paper Writing

Master the process of academic publishing — from topic selection and citation to responding to peer reviews.

Module 6: Transition to Professional Work

Enhance professional skills such as communication, teamwork, project management, conflict resolution, and workplace ethics.

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Hands-on Research Module (Weeks 10–12)
Module 7: Hands-On Research Project

Collaborate with a UAEU research team or faculty member on a live project. You’ll apply all your learnings in an actual research setting and be evaluated by a mentor.

Each module combines lectures, group activities, interactive workshops, and continuous assessments.

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Program Delivery Details
Duration: 12 weeks (Full-time)

Schedule: Monday to Friday | 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM (Fridays – 9:00 AM to 12:00 Noon)

Location: UAEU Campus, Al Ain

Certification: Official UAEU Certificate (upon successful completion with 70% minimum grade)
