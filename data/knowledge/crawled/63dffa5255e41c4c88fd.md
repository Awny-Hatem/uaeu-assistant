                                               Benefits for United Arab Emirates Nationals


I.                                                                                               Policy

                      It is the policy of the United Arab Emirates University to provide
                      compensation and benefits to United Arab Emirates Nationals according to
                      University policies and pertinent Federal Laws.


II.                                                                             Application

                      This policy shall apply to all United Arab Emirates Nationals employed by the
                      United Arab Emirates University.


III.                                                           Related Policies



IV.                                                              Administration

                       Questions concerning the interpretation and administration of this policy are
                       to be directed to the Director, Human Resources.
