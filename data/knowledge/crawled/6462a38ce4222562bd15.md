## Director's Message
Entrepreneurship makes us think of new ventures, the seeds for future growth and development, and entrepreneurial attitudes are fundamental to initiating and supporting change in companies and public institutions. UAEU has long pioneered entrepreneurial education in the GCC region, supporting its students with rigorous academic knowledge and updated theoretical and practical learning opportunities.

The Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research builds on this original vision by combining research, teaching, and practice to strengthen its impact in the UAE and abroad.”
