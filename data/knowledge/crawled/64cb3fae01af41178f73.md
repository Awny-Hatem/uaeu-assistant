# College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence > Research > Overview
CCAI faculty and students are engaged in cutting-edge applied research, developing solutions and products that serve the needs of society. CCAI research output has increased over the past few years with contributions to many prestigious and often cited publications and world-class journals, conferences, and books, as well as many international patents.

CCAI’s research covers a wide range of issues relevant to the needs of the nation. These areas include Data Science, Health, Education, Communications Networks, Transportation, Information Security and Privacy, Microelectronics and Energy. CCAI faculty have taken part in several internally and externally funded research projects in cooperation with ICT, SRC, the Emirates Foundation, and ADEC, among others. Some of these research projects have been participated in by UAE national research assistants, including undergraduate and graduate students from the CCAI.

## College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence > Research > Capabilities
The College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence (CCAI) offers research capabilities on a global scale. Our highly experienced faculty and graduate students can provide a wealth of standardized and customized solutions to serve society’s needs. The college has a broad range of active research interests that include the following:

The Internet of Things (IoT)

Artificial Intelligence

Bioinformatics

Communications and Mobile Networking Systems

Data Analytics/Data mining

E-Health

Grid/Cloud Computing

Smart Grid Systems

Information, Network and Cyber Security

Privacy

VLSI design

To support these research activities, the CCAI is home to some of the most up-to-date laboratories anywhere in the world. There are 20 specialized laboratories that include laboratories dedicated to Robotics and AI, Data Science, Information Security and Privacy, Drones and IoT, Grid and Cloud Computing, and Immersive Learning.

The CCAI hosts a distinguished lecture series to engage both faculty and students in current research activities. It also hosts the Middle East’s largest and most prestigious annual IT research conference, ‘The International Conference on Innovations in IT,’ which has become a hub for IT research innovation and has received worldwide recognition.

## College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence > Research > Opportunities
The College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence offers a remarkably diverse array of research opportunities for both faculty and students. Faculty and students can enjoy access to over 20 specialized research laboratories. Research support is provided through the following grants:

Internal Grants

These are university-funded grants that are available to all UAEU faculty through a university-wide competition. They include the following:

UAEU Program for Advanced Research (UPAR)

Research Start-up Grant Competition

UAEU Interdisciplinary Center-based Research Grant Competition

UAEU Summer Undergraduate Research Experiences (SURE) Program

Calls for proposals, proposal templates, and submission deadlines for internal grants are announced here.

External Grants

These are externally funded projects from international and national institutions interested in conducting research in information technology. The faculty, the college, or the university at large may initiate these opportunities. Externally funded grant opportunities, when available, are announced through the college website or here . Some of the current external grants available include:

The Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Fund

National Research Foundation (NRF)

Aerospace by Dassault Aviation

Abu Dhabi Environmental Agency

Space Science and Technology Center

Semiconductor Research Corporation (SRC)

## College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence > Research > Opportunities > College Research Support Programs
These are college-funded research opportunities available only to CCAI faculty and students intended to support research. They include:

The Faculty Research Seed Grant: A funding opportunity to support junior faculty to initiate a research project.

International conference travel funding at the college level: Each faculty member is given the opportunity for financial support to attend a reputable conference for a full paper review once a year.

## College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence > Research > Research Groups
The College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence (CCAI) seeks to foster high-quality research and to improve the college’s long-term research capacity by establishing and executing a research strategy that is designed to deliver excellent research outcomes commensurate with UAEU’s strategic priorities and the national drive towards a knowledge-based economy. To help achieve that, the CCAI established research groups with the aim to produce high-quality research and successfully engage the college in strategic sponsorship, partnership, and collaboration with national and international entities and to be the catalyst for interdisciplinary research at the UAEU. The Research Groups are in the areas of Data Science and Data Analytic, Cybersecurity and Privacy, Computer Vision, Intelligent Distributed Computing and Systems, AI and Robotics, Brain Computer Interface, and Connected Autonomous Systems and IoT
