                                                 Ethical Use of Computing and
                                           Information Technology Resources


I.                                                                                              Policy

               In support of its mission to provide excellent instruction, modern research and
               meaningful service, the United Arab Emirates University offers computing
               resources to its students, faculty and staff.  These resources contribute to the
               work of all members of the University community and, therefore, must be
               used with great care.

               It is the policy of the University to maintain access for its academic
               community to local, national and international sources of information and to
               provide an atmosphere that encourages  the free exchange of ideas and sharing
               of information.  Access to this envi ronment and the University’s computing
               and information technology resources is a privilege and must be treated with
               the highest standard of ethics.  Information technology resources include, but
               are not limited to, computers, software, networks, data and
               telecommunications technologies whether owned, contracted, leased or
               otherwise provided by the University.  The University expects all members of
               the community to use these resources in a responsible manner, respecting the
               trust through which these resources have been provided, the rights and privacy
               of others, the integrity of facilities and controls, and all pertinent laws and
               University policies and standards.

               Appropriate Use

               •                       All computing and information technology resources are the property of
                      the University and shall be protected against theft, malicious damage,
                      unauthorized access or disclosure, tampering or loss.

                Responsibilities

                •                                    Managers at all levels will be responsible for enforcement of this policy.
                •                         All users of computing and information technology resources must abide
                        by the terms of this policy.
                •                                   All users must respect the privacy and personal rights of others.
                •                All users must abide by applicable laws and University policies and
                        respect the copyrights and intellectual property rights of others, including
                        the legal use of copyrighted software.

                Enforcement

                Any activity which violates this policy may be just cause for taking
                disciplinary action as determined by th e Provost on a case-by-case basis.

                Although all members of the University community may have an expectation
                of privacy, in the course of responding to viruses, system failures, hackers or
                other incidents of misuse of technology resources, it may be necessary to
                examine materials on the network.

                (This policy was developed based upon EDUCAUSE best practices.
                Particular credit goes to Rutgers University.)


II.                                                                             Application

                This policy shall apply to all users of the United Arab Emirates University
                computing and information technology resources, including faculty, staff,
                students, guests, external individuals or organizations and individuals
                accessing internal or external network services, such as the Internet, via
                University facilities.
