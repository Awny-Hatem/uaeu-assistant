## Research > Monitoring climate – soil water interactions in the SVAT system of date palms
This project aims at the development of a date palm yield model which can be used to optimize irrigation management of date palms. In the first step the experimental set up was implemented and the first data were evaluated.

M.Sc. Students (from TU Dresden) Ms. Judith Schache, Ms. Isabel Bergmann and Dr. Franz H. Lennartz ( Franz.Lennartz@uaeu.ac.ae )
