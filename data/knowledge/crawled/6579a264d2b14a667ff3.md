Student Responsibility in Maintaining Good
Housekeeping in UAEU Hostel


 As residents of the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) hostel, students play a vital role in maintaining good housekeeping
 practices. By taking ownership of their living space, students can contribute to a clean, safe, and healthy environment that supports
 their academic success and well-being.

 Importance of Student Responsibility

 1.                                              Personal Space: Students are responsible for maintaining their personal space, including their rooms and belongings.
 2.                             Common Areas: Students should also contribute to maintaining common areas, such as pantry, bathrooms, recreation, and
       lounges.
 3.                                       Community Living: By respecting shared spaces and facilities, students can promote a sense of community and cooperation.

 Ways Students Can Contribute

 1.                                              Regular Cleaning: Students can participate in regular cleaning schedules and maintain their personal space.
 2.                                        Reporting Issues: Students should report any maintenance or repair issues to the hostel administration.
 3.                                       Respecting Rules: Students should respect hostel rules and regulations regarding cleanliness and waste management.

 Benefits of Student Responsibility

 1.                                               Student responsibility contributes to a clean and safe living environment.
 2.                                         By working together, students can promote a sense of community and cooperation.
 3.                                        Taking responsibility for one's living space can promote personal growth and accountability.

 Student responsibility is essential for maintaining good housekeeping practices in the UAEU hostel. By taking ownership of their
 living space and contributing to the maintenance of common areas, students can promote a clean, safe, and healthy environment
 that supports their academic success and well-being.

## PDF page 27
Fire Safety Responsibilities at UAE University


 Employee Responsibilities

 1.                 Awareness and Reporting: Employees are responsible for being aware of fire hazards and reporting them to the
       fmdhs@uaeu.ac.ae .
 2.                                            Fire Extinguisher Training: Employees should be trained in the use of fire extinguishers and know the location of fire alarms and
       emergency exits.
 3.                                        Emergency Procedures: Employees should be familiar with emergency procedures, including evacuation routes and assembly
       points.

 Student Responsibilities

 1.                                              Awareness and Reporting: Students should be aware of fire hazards and report them to their  supervisors
 2.                                   Following Emergency Procedures: Students should follow emergency procedures, including evacuation routes and assembly
       points, during fire drills or actual emergencies.
 3.                               Keeping Common Areas Clear: Students should keep common areas, such as corridors and stairwells, clear of obstructions
       that could hinder evacuation.

 Contractor Responsibilities

 1.                              Compliance with Fire Safety Regulations: Contractors working on campus should comply with UAE University's fire safety
       regulations and guidelines.
 2.                                      Fire Safety Risk Assessment: Contractors should conduct fire safety risk assessments for their work activities and implement
       necessary controls.
 3.                                       Reporting Incidents: Contractors should report any fire incidents or near misses to fmdhs@uaeu.ac.ae .

 Visitor Responsibilities

 1.                                 Following Emergency Procedures: Visitors should follow emergency procedures, including evacuation routes and assembly
       points, during fire drills or actual emergencies.
 2.                                        Reporting Fire Hazards: Visitors should report any observed fire hazards to the university authorities or security personnel.
 3.                                         Adhering to Safety Instructions: Visitors should adhere to safety instructions provided by university staff or security personnel.

 By fulfilling these responsibilities, employees, students, contractors, and visitors can contribute to a safe and secure environment at
 UAE University.

Dangers of Poor Disposal of Syringes


 Poor disposal of syringes poses significant risks to individuals and the community. Some of the dangers include:

 1.                                              Transmission of Infectious Diseases

 •                                                         Improperly disposed syringes can transmit  disease through needlestick injuries or other forms of exposure.

 •                                                         People especially waste handlers who come into contact with improperly disposed syringes are at risk of infection.

 2.                                Accidental Needlestick Injuries

 Improperly disposed syringes can cause accidental needlestick injuries, which can lead to infections and other health problems.

 Accidental needlestick injuries can also cause emotional distress and anxiety for those affected.

 3.                               Environmental Contamination
  Syringes that are not disposed of properly can end up in landfills or waterways, contributing to environmental contamination.

 Improperly disposed syringes can also pose a risk to wildlife, which may come into contact with them and suffer injuries or infections.

 4.                                Reuse and Misuse
 Improperly disposed syringes can be reused or misused, leading to the transmission of infectious diseases.

 The reuse or misuse of syringes can also contribute to public health risks, including outbreaks of infectious diseases.

 5.                               Contamination of Medical Waste
 If syringes are mixed with general waste, it increases risk for everyone who handles garbage.
