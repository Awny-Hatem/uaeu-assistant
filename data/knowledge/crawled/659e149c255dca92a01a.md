## Primary Objectives:
Leverage research investments by advancing commercialization of research outcomes

Transform the university innovation culture by preparing researchers to extend their focus beyond the research laboratory

Assist researchers in translating laboratory discoveries into commercializable products and technologies that will benefit Abu Dhabi, the UAE economy, and the global community.

Increase the economic impact of university funded research

## Program Structure and Team Commitment:
Action-oriented teaching and one-on-one coaching sessions (3.5h per week for first 8 weeks, afterwards 1h per week)

Additional time for industry engagement

“Demo/Deal Day” - Actual Pitch Sessions to UAEU SIP Management and industry partners

Best teams will receive further support from UAEU SIP and potentially external partners.

## Increased Research Impact
Transform research into real-world solutions that benefit industries, society & global challenges.

Move beyond citations—see your discoveries become products, services, and innovations.

## Enhanced Funding Opportunities & Financial Benefits & Royalties
Unlock new funding sources through commercialization grants, venture capital, and industry partnerships.

Boost grant competitiveness—funders prioritize research with commercial potential.

Earn royalties and equity by licensing innovations or launching a spin-off company.

## Career Growth & Industry Connections
Expand professional network by collaborating with industry leaders and investors.

Open doors to consulting, advisory, and leadership roles beyond academia.

## Access to Business Resources & Support
Get expert guidance from industry, investors, new venture creation, etc.

Learn business and entrepreneurship skills to navigate patents, licensing, and commercialization.
