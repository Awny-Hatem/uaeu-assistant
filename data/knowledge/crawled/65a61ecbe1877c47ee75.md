## Engineering Requirements Unit > Engineering Requirements Unit
The Engineering Requirements Unit (ERU) serves the engineering students by providing them with solid foundation in basic science, mathematics and general engineering fundamentals. Our Pre-Engineering program is designed to prepare students for all engineering major disciplines. The ERU manages and coordinates with the College of Science and the College of Business and Economics to offer all basic science and statistics courses that are required to be taken by all students in the College of Engineering. Moreover, the unit offers the general fundamental engineering courses that are needed for all our Engineering students. The curriculum at the College of Engineering is developed to help the freshman students understand more fully what the field of engineering is, the various opportunities available in the field, and the connection between their high school courses and future study of engineering. Therefore, the teaching methodology of the offered courses emphasizes hands-on learning, teamwork, effective use of technology, and strong communication skills.

The main tasks of the unit include, but not limited to, engineering students counseling and advising, periodic review and update of ERU courses, outcome assessment of all ERU courses following ABET and CAA criteria, and coordination with various departments to offer the required courses and make the necessary development and changes in these courses.

UAEU Writing Centers and Speaking Centers

The UAEU Writing Centers and Speaking Centers offer language support and academic skills training to all Engineering students. These Centers are located on both the male and female campuses.

The Writing Centers provide help and advice in all areas of English writing, for example drafting and editing reports, projects and resumes, and using resources to improve grammar and vocabulary. They are staffed by English language instructors, including ESP lecturers and student peer tutors.

The Speaking Centers offer students the opportunity to practice conversation and get advice and feedback on class presentations.
