## College of Information Technology > Facilities
The CIT is housed in the excellent College of Information Technology building, and has embarked on an ambitious project to deliver laboratory education of an unprecedented breadth and depth. The CIT boasts some of the most up-to-date laboratories in the world. There are twenty specialized laboratories for Robotics and Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Information Security and Privacy, and Drones. We also have laboratories specializing in the Internet of Things (IoT), Communication Networks, Grid and Cloud Computing, Digital Hardware and Circuit Design, VLSI, and Immersive Learning.
