to the company.

Current Job Opporunities at Etihad Airways:

Cadet Pilot Program – open to all majors

Etihad’s UAE National Cadet Pilot Programme prepares eligible Emirati candidates for the role
of pilot for its modern fleet. You’ll be trained at Horizon International Flight Academy in Al Ain,
UAE. The programme duration is 18 months.

On successful completion of the training, you will be granted a commercial pilot’s license. And
you’ll be on your way to the top, starting your flying career as a Second Officer. You can look
forward to flying state-of-the-art aircraft and meeting with one of the world’s fastest growing
airlines.

Graduate Management Program – open to all majors

Graduates will develop strong leadership capabilities and form a group of professionals who are
prepared to meet rapidly expanding and evolving leadership needs in various departments
within Etihad. This program includes rotational assignments/tasks in all key business areas; with
people and/or project management responsibilities pairing with management professionals as
well as periods of high intensity classroom training and self instruction.
