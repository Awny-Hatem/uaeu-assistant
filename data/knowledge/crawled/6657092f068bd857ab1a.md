## Overview
Welcome to the Division Of Information Technology (DoIT) at UAE University. DoIT serves as a centralized administration of Information Technology which provides a wide variety of technical services to UAEU community with enterprise infrastructure, applications, telecommunication and end-user support services.

As the main IT services provider at UAE University, DoIT values excellence, innovation, discovery and search for truth, academic freedom, collaboration, accountability of resources and relationship, sharing knowledge in a learning environment as well as the application of knowledge and discovery to enhance the quality of learning and research and work experience for students, faculty and staff.

DoIT is committed to achieve the highest level of excellence and reliability in all interactions with UAE University students, faculty, staff and alumni with a proactive approach to identify, share and solve problems promptly in an ongoing effort deliver its services.

DoIT is also committed to facilitate the sharing of IT resources, providing IT consulting, development, strategic planning and data centers’ services. DoIT is striving to ensure the cyber security of UAE University students, faculty and staff and the effective delivery of technology-based extraordinary solutions in support of the University community.
