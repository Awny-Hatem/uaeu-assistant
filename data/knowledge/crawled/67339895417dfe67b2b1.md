# STUDENTS HAPPINESS CENTER

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## Overview
The Student Happiness Center was established in order to reduce the difficulties and delays faced by students looking to avail of a particular service by supporting, advising and answering their queries and questions directly. If the center is unable to answer a student's query, t he center will direct the student to the relevant person.
