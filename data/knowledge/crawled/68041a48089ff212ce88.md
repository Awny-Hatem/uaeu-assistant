## Overview — continuation 2
To facilitate discovery of information patterns and trends, new approaches are required.
Data mining techniques can help companies discover information and acquire business intelligence from these massive datasets.
This course covers data mining for business intelligence.
Data mining refers to extracting or “mining” knowledge from large amounts of data and consists of techniques that aim at discovering patterns that can bring value or “business intelligence” to organizations, for example consumer behavior.
The course covers salient data mining techniques including classification, clustering, association rule mining, visualization and prediction.
