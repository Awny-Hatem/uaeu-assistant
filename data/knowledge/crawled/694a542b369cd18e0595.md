                Deadlines for the Settlement of Tuition & Fees for Spring 2017

                1st Payment Deadline (50% of total tuition fees ) on, 15 January 2017
                2nd Payment Deadline (remaining 50% of total tuition fees) on, 09 March 2017

                                    Tuition Payment Installment Plan
       To arrange a tuition payment installment plan, students must apply at the SAO before 09
        February 2017. If you were entitled to an installment plan in the previous semester, you
        must apply again for the current semester

       Non-payment of total tuition fees on or before 14 April 2017 will result in a hold being
        placed on registration and records. A hold means that you will not receive course grades
        until you pay your fees. It also means that you will not be able to register for courses in
        the next semester until payment is made. Unfortunately it may also mean the forfeiture of
        grades and credits earned during the semester.

       Sponsored students
        Students whose tuition charges are paid for or guaranteed by a sponsor must submit a
        valid official letter of financial agreement from the sponsor no later than 23 February
        2017, or risk having their registration for Spring 2017 cancelled.
