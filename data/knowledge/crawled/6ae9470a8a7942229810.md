Dear Students,

      The Student Welfare Fund/Student Services Wishes you all the best. We also take this opportunity to
      reiterate our commitment to improving the application process. With this in view, we trust that you will
      visit the Student Welfare Fund Website at the beginning of each semester to get informed about the
      conditions and required documents for availing of the Student Welfare Fund Services.

      Please note that the system will be available for both new applications and renewals for only 4 weeks
      form the beginning of each semester. Therefore, it is important that you upload the necessary
      supporting documents at the right location. Failing that, your application will be rejected.

      Moreover, to avoid any inconvenience, it is crucial that you bear in mind the following points:

               If your bank account changes, please provide us with your new IBAN through the system and
                inform us of this change through email.
               Financial Aid will stop for period of time after the end of the academic year. This period is
                necessary for the administrative procedures to take effect, at our end as well as that of the
                Zakat Fund.
               The renewal of your application is entirely yours and yours alone and must be done at the
                beginning of semester 1 and 2. We will not send you any reminders, should the financial aid
                from the Zakat Fund expires. You may download the Zakat Fund app to check when financial aid
                comes to an end. Follow the instruction here.

                The period of renewal                         Months (Last payments of financial aid)
                Beginning of First semester                   May, June, July, August, September, October
                Beginning of Second semester                  November, December, January, February, March, April

      Finally, if you have any comments or suggestions, please do not hesitate to let us know via email
      financialaids.office@uaeu.ac.ae. Your opinions are always appreciated.

      Thank your,

      Student Welfare Fund.
