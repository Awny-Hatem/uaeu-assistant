                                                       Providing Information to the Media


I.                                                                        Policy

                   The United Arab Emirates University is committed to providing information
                   about its programs and activities to the news media so that the public may, in
                   turn, be informed and aware.

                   It is important that information provided to the news media be accurate,
                   consistent and timely.  Therefore, all contact with the news media, both
                   initiated by the University and that in response to media enquiry, shall be
                   through the Director of Media Relations.  Students, faculty and staff should
                   contact the Director of Media Relations before they have contact with the
                   news media.


II.                                                                             Application

                   This policy shall apply to all contacts with the news media by faculty, staff
                   and students of the United Arab Emirates University.
