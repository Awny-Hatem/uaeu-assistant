Joy of Reading with Isobel Abulhoul














Isobel Abulhoul was born and educated in Cambridge,
United Kingdom, before moving to Dubai in 1968 (her
home ever since). There she co-                                                   founded the bookshop
chain, Magrudy’s, in 1975. In 2008, she founded and
became Director of the Emirates Airline Festival of
Literature. The festival won the Best Festival in the
Middle East Award in 2013 and 2014. Isobel has
received official recognition for her tireless
campaigning to improve literacy and foster a love of
books, particularly for children.
