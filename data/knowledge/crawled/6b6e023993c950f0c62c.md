## National Faculty Recruitment and Development Office > Overview
The United Arab Emirates University has established the National Faculty Recruitment and Development Office to support and develop exceptional UAE national talents who aspire to pursue higher education and distinguished careers in academia. This program is specifically designed for distinguished Emirati graduates who demonstrate outstanding academic performance, leadership potential, and a strong aspiration to pursue a career in academia.
