                                                                                 Contract Retail Services


I.                                                                                               Policy

                     1.                                        The United Arab Emirates University will provide contract retail
                                           services as needed.

                     2.                               Contracts are reviewed on an annual basis by the department
                                           administering the service and by the Contracts and Purchasing
                                           Department.

                     3.                                                           The department administering the service is responsible for ensuring
                                           service standards are maintained and that the conditions of contracts
                                           are met.

                     4.                                                                           The department administering the service will involve the users of the
                                           services within the University community in assessing the breadth and
                                           quality of services provided by retailers.


II.                                                                             Application

                     This policy shall apply to all services contracted by the United Arab Emirates
                     University.
