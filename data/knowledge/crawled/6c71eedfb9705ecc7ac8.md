Procedures of Policy No. (7) - Fa culty Excellence Awards Program

1. Eligibility
      a)                     Faculty members are eligible to apply for all awards categories. Instructors are eligible
             to apply for teaching and service awards at the College and University levels.
      b)             Applicants to College Excellence Awards must have at least two years of service at
             UAEU before applying. Applicants to University Excellence Awards must have at
             least three years of service at UAEU before applying. Applicants to the Distinguished
             Faculty Award must have at least five years of service at UAEU before applying.
      c)                No more than two nominations per award may be submitted by any College for any
             University award.
      d)                   Nominations must be for recognition of work done at UAEU.
      e)                      No faculty member may be nominated for more than one award at a time.
      f)                    Previous recipients of the awards are not eligible to apply for the same award for at
             least two consecutive years after being awarded.
      g)                   Awards are normally announced before the end of the academic year in which they are
             awarded.


2. Selection Committees
      a)                  College Awards
             (i)        Each Department nominates one faculty member to form a College Excellence
                   Awards Committee. One additional member is nominated by the Dean.
             (ii) Participation in evaluation and decisions related to award nominations shall be
                   limited to faculty members who ar e not applying for the awards.
             (iii)The College Excellence Awards Committee selects its own Chair.

      b)                University Awards
                                The Provost forms a University Excellence Awards Committee whose composition
             and Chair is at his/her discretion.


3. Selection Process
      a)            Nominations for any award may be made by Deans, Department Chairs, Program
             Coordinators, individual faculty members, and students. Faculty members may
             nominate themselves.
      b)        All nominations are directed to the Chair of the College Excellence Awards
             Committee.
      c)                      Assessments of eligibility and of excellence are made as per Policy and Procedures.
      d)          Based on the review of all applications and the recommendations of the College
             Excellence Awards Committee, the Chair of the Committee recommends nominations
             for all award categories to the College Council.

      e)                     Based on the recommendations of the College Committee, the College Council has the
            authority to determine the winners of College Excellence Awards. The Council

      f)           The University Excellence Awards Committee recommends to the Provost the

      g)             The Provost will make his recommendations to the Vice Chancellor who makes the
            final decision on these awards.


4. Nominations
      Each nomination is made to the Chair of the College Excellence Awards Committee and
      is supported by a portfolio that includes:
      a)              A letter of nomination/application, including a narrative statement of no more than
            two pages addressing the criteria for the particular award and describing why the
            contribution of the individual merits nomination for the Award;
      b)                   A current curriculum vitae;
      c)                 A documentation of the evidence demonstrating the nominee’s excellence in relation
            to the relevant award, including the following:
            (i)                Teaching: An overview of the nominee's contributions as a teacher and advisor
                   and a list of all courses taught during his/her service at the UAEU with course
                   number, course name, number of students enrolled, dates taught, and results of
                   teaching evaluations. Supporting documents will be requested as needed.
            (ii)        Scholarship: A description of the nominee's significant scholarly and/or creative
                   achievements, including a list of publicati ons. Evidence of these publications will

            (iii) Service: An overview of the nominee’s services to the academic, professional or
                   UAE community, with special emphasis on activity above and beyond assigned

5. Selection Criteria
       The general guidelines for selection of nominees for the different categories of the awards
       are given hereafter.

      a)                  College Award for Excellence in Teaching
            (i)                         Ability to stimulate students to learn.
            (ii)           Enthusiasm about subject matter and teaching.
            (iii) Innovative lectures or educational materials (laboratories, computer aided
                   instruction, class handouts, etc.)
            (iv) Concern for and dedication to students.
            (v)              Constructive use of evaluations (examinations, homework, etc.)
            (vi) Activities in support of teaching (academic advising, course and curriculum
                   development, publications related to teaching, etc.)
            (vii) Development of new courses or revitalization of existing ones.

b)                College Award for Excellence in Scholarship
      (i)              Significant record that emphasizes publications in international journals.
      (ii) Recognition among colleagues as a researcher/scholar/leader in the discipline.
      (iii)Sustained contribution to the advancement of scholarship.
      (iv) Activity in professional associations and holding positions that recognize the
            applicant’s scholarship and/or creative activity.
      (v)   Grants and contracts.

c)                     College Award for Excellence in Service
      (i)         Consistent excellent performance in professional service as evidenced by a high
            level of professional service activity; commitment to the quality of the service
            provided and breadth and depth of the service record.
      (ii) Evidence of outstanding leadership in a major service activity.
      (iii)Impact and significance of professional service as evidenced by perceived
            importance of work by others.
      (iv) Contribution to organizational improvement and advancement, and public
            recognition, etc.

d)                University Award for Excellence in Teaching
      (i)                         Innovative use of technology in the teaching and learning process.
      (ii)           Developing online courses.
      (iii) Using multimedia to produce educational materials to be used primarily for
             teaching.
      (iv) Extensive use of course management systems such as Blackboard.
      (v)     Innovative lectures or educational materials (laboratories, computer aided
             instruction, class handouts, etc.)
      (vi) Awards and honors received in recognition of teaching, advising, and mentoring.
      (vii) Organizing or conducting new courses or programs of study.

      (i)                         Significant contribution to the discipline.
      (ii)        Significant publication record in internati onal journals as evidenced by citations

      (iii) Recognition among colleagues as a researcher/scholar/leader in the discipline.

            and breadth and depth of the service record.
