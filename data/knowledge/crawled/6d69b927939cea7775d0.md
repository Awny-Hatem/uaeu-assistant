                                                                              Academic Progress


I.                                                                                              Policy


                  The United Arab Emirates University shall establish and apply standards of
                  academic performance to govern satisfactory progress toward the degree.

                  Evaluation of Student’s Performance

                  1.                                                                                        The student’s performance is assessed on a continuous basis during the
                                     semester.

                                     The course grade is divided into three pre-specified parts:  semester
                                     activities, mid-semester examin ation and final examination.

                  2.                                                                                         a)                      A student must repeat the compulsory courses that he/she fails.

                                     b)    A student must repeat the elective courses that he/she fails or
                                               substitute them with other elective courses in his/her curriculum.

                  All the courses that the student has completed must appear in his/her transcript
                  with the corresponding grades.

                  If a student fails a course and repeats it successfully, the failing grade is not
                  taken into consideration in calculating the grade point average.  If he/she fails
                  a course more than once and repeats it successfully, all failing grades are
                  ignored.  The maximum grade he/she may attain is C.

Honors

               First class honors is earned by students with a graduating grade point average
               of 4 points, while second class honors is earned by students with a graduating
               grade point average of at least 3.6 point s.  In order to qualify for graduation
               with honors, a student must complete a minimum of 75% of the credit hours
               required for graduation at the United Arab Emirates University.  A student
               who fails any course does not qualify for honors.

               Academic Probation

               a)                                                                                          For students admitted to the University in 2000, 2001 and 2002:

                              1.                                                                             If the cumulative grade point average of a student drops below
                                             1.75 points, after completing thirty credit hours, he/she
                                             receives an academic probation notice from the dean.
                              2.                                                   The study load of a student on academic probation must be
                                             limited to twelve credit hours per semester.
                              3.                                                                              A student on academic probation must raise his/her cumulative
                                             grade point average to at least 1.75 points within the next
                                             semester following receipt of the academic probation notice in
                                             order to remove the probation status.
                              4.                                           A student on academic probation who fails to achieve the
                                             minimum grade point average within the next semester will
                                             receive the second academic probation notice from the dean.
                              5.                                                            A student is granted the chance to continue his/her studies in
                                             the same academic program or change his/her specialization
                                             within the same college or transfer to another college.
                              6.                                                      A student gains a non-probation status if his/her grade point
                                             average is raised to 1.75 due to change of specialization or
                                             transfer to another college.
                              7.                                                                              If the student fails to achieve a cumulative grade point average
                                             of 1.75 points by the end of the semester following the second
                                             academic probation notice, the student is academically
