15. Endowed and Named Professorial Chairs

Overview
Provides guidelines related to Endowed and named Professorships.

Scope
Applies to the establishment of Endowed and named Professorships at the UAEU.

Objective
To support its Mission, the University looks favorably upon the establishment of Endowed and
named Professorships. This Policy sets out the framework governing such positions.

Policy
The University will support the creation of Endowed and named Professorships.

Procedures of Policy No. (15) - Endowed and Named Professorial Chairs

1.     Endowed Professorships shall be approved according to the Signatory Authority.

2.     Designated funds for Endowed Professorships shall be associated with that can be used
       at the discretion of the appointee. These positions may be given a title to associate them
       with the name of an individual or an entity.

3.     Endowed Professor appointments shall be for a prescribed period and shall be renewable
       upon the agreement of all concerned parties.

4.     Named      Professorships     are  not  associated  with  funds;  they  are  made  to  recognize
       contributions of an individual or an entity.

5.     Named      Professorship     appointments  and  terms  shall  be  approved  according  to  the
       Signatory Authority.
