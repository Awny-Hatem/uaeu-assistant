WASC SEN IOR COLLEGE AN D U N IVERSITY COMMISSION (WSCUC)

Institutional Accreditation What is it?
         ■                     Unlike professional accreditation (such as ABET and AACSB), institutional accreditation
               focuses on evaluating the institution as a whole
         ■                                  It is a rigorous, ongoing and lengthy process, by which the University demonstrates that it
               offers high-quality education - a judgment made by peers
         ■                                                 It helps the University identify areas that need further development

About WASC Senior College and University Commission (WSCUC)
         ■                           One of six regional accrediting associations that accredit all academic institutions in the
               United States
         ■                                     Ensures that institutions it accredits are fulfilling their missions in service to their students
               and the public good
         ■                                                 Only three international institutions (outside the US) are currently accredited by WSCUC

WSCUC Accreditation Requirements
    ►                                                  39 Criteria for Review (CFR) that clarify the standards
         ■                                                 Student learning and success
         ■                                                 Quality and improvement
         ■                                                 Institutional integrity, sustainability, and accountability

    ►                                                  4 standards built upon the core commitments, articulating good practice
       We need to show that:
         ■                                                 Our mission, purpose and educational objectives are appropriate for a leading university
         ■                                                 We achieve our educational objectives
         ■                                                 Our resources and systems ensure quality and sustainability
         ■                                                 We are committed to continued quality improvement

    ►                                                  39 Criteria for Review (CFR) that clarify the standards

Why Does International Accreditation Matter to UAEU?
         ■                                                                   It is a proven quality-improvement process, which will give external validation to our programs
         ■                                                 It will give us increased credibility within the international higher education sector
         ■                                                 It will aid in faculty and student recruitment

TH E STATUS OF ACCREDITATION

                                The status of accreditation indicates that an institution has fulﬁlled the requirements for
                                accreditation established by the WSCUC’s 2013 Handbook of Accreditation. This means that
                                the institution has:
                                                                    ■                                            Demonstrated that it meets the Core Commitments;
                                                                    ■                                            Conducted           a           self-review           under           the           Standards           of           Accreditation,           developed           and           presented
                                                                                                                 indicators            of            institutional            performance,            and            identiﬁed            areas            for            improvement;
                                                                    ■                                            Developed approved institutional reports for accreditation that have been evaluated by teams
                                                                                                                 of peer evaluators under the relevant institutional review processes;
                                                                    ■                                            Demonstrated to the Commission that it meets or exceeds the expectations of the Standards
                                                                                                                 of Accreditation;
                                                                    ■                                            Committed              itself              to              institutional              improvement,              periodic              self-evaluation,              and              continuing
                                                                                                                 compliance              with              Commission              Standards,              policies,              procedures              and              decisions.
