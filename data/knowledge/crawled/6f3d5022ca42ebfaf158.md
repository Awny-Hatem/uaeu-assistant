## Admission Requirements and Selection Process > Admission Requirements and Selection Process
The following are the minimum requirements for admission to Master of Business Administration Program at UAEU.

Bachelor Degree (any major)

A minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 (see exceptions below)

A minimum of three year of work experience

TOEFL or IELTS, please see in the following table the minimum scores required (see exceptions below).

Interview

Paper-based TOEFL | 550
Internet-based TOEFL (ibT) | 79
Computer-based TOEFL | 213
IELTS (Academic) | 6

Exceptions for TOEFL or IELTS

Applicants with an undergraduate degree from an English-medium institution who can provide evidence of acquiring a minimum TOEFL score of 500 on the paper-based test or an IELTS score of 5 (or equivalent), at the time of admission to her/his undergraduate program, are also eligible.

Applicants with an IELTS score of less than 6 (or equivalent) may get a conditional admission.

Exceptions for GPA

Professional experience will be counted for those applicants with a GPA less than 3.0.

Applicants with a GPA of less than 3.0 and more than 2.5 may get a conditional admission.

Please contact us to get more specific information.
