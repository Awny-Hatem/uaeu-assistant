                                                                         Use of University Facilities


I.                                                                                               Policy

                    1.                                                     The United Arab Emirates University facilities are intended for use
                                         primarily by University personnel for University purposes.

                    2.                                                                                    The University facilities may be used for other purposes, including use
                                         by students and employees for extracurricular activities, as well as by
                                         non-University personnel and for non-University purposes.

                    3.                            Application for non-University uses must be sponsored by a
                                         University dean, director or an individual above the level of a dean or
                                         director, and must be evidenced by an agreement that clearly identifies
                                         the respective rights, responsibilities, obligations and liabilities of all
                                         parties.

                    4.                                        The Secretary General will publish procedures in support of this
                                         policy.


II.                                                                             Application

                    This policy shall apply to all faculty, staff and students of the United Arab
                    Emirates University.
