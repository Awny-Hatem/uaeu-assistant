## Tuition Fee Payment Options
The Student Accounts Office provides flexible tuition fee payment options to accommodate students’ needs, in accordance with the approved policies and procedures of United Arab Emirates University (UAEU).

1. Full Payment of Tuition Fees

Tuition fees must be paid in full at the beginning of each semester and no later than the deadline specified in the Tuition Fee Schedule to ensure completion of registration requirements and continued access to university services.

## Tuition Fee Payment Options > Key Features
The total tuition fees due for all registered courses are paid in a single payment.

No application or request through the electronic system is required.

## Tuition Fee Payment Options > Payment Deadline
The table below specifies the deadline for full payment of tuition fees under the Full Payment option.

## Tuition Fee Payment Options > Tuition Fee Payment Deadline – First Semester 2026–2027
Payment Requirement | Payment Deadline
Payment of 100% of the total tuition fees for all registered courses. | 24 August 2026

## Tuition Fee Payment Options
2. Tuition Fee Installment Plan

Eligible students may apply for the Tuition Fee Installment Plan, which allows tuition fees to be paid in four installments during the semester, subject to the approved terms and conditions.

## Tuition Fee Payment Options > Eligibility and Requirements
To participate in the Tuition Fee Installment Plan, students must:

Submit an installment plan application through the University’s electronic portal within the designated application period.

Pay the first installment by the specified deadline.

Pay all subsequent installments by their respective due dates.

Complete course registration before applying for the Tuition Fee Installment Plan.

Be aware that any changes to the registered course schedule, including adding or dropping courses after approval of the installment plan, may result in cancellation of the plan and recalculation of tuition fees in accordance with the applicable University procedures.

## Tuition Fee Payment Options > Installment Plan Application Period
Applications for the Tuition Fee Installment Plan for the First Semester of Academic Year 2026–2027 will be accepted from 31 August 2026 through 05 September 2026 .

An advance payment shall be made on 24 August 2026, prior to applying for the installment plan.

## Tuition Fee Payment Options > Installment Payment Deadlines
Installment | Payment Deadline
First installment of the total tuition fees for registered courses | 30 September 2026
Second installment of the total tuition fees for registered courses | 30 October 2026
Third installment of the total tuition fees for registered courses | 30 November 2026

## Tuition Fee Payment Options > Important Note
The Tuition Fee Installment Plan is available during the First and Second Semesters only and is not available during the Summer Semester.

Students may apply for the Tuition Fee Installment Plan by following these steps:

Access the E-Service page through the UAEU website or the UAEU mobile application.

Select E-Service from the top menu.

Select Financial Services from the available options.

Select Student Tuition Installments and Payments , then click Submit to submit the installment plan application.
