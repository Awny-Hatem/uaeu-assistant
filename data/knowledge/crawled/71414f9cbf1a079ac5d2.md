Instructions for Using AccessMedicine on a Mobile Device

       1.  To use AccessMedicine on your device, you must create a username and password.

       2.  From a computer, go to AccessMedicine and click on the “My AccessMedicine” link (top, right-hand corner of screen).

       3.  When the pop-up screen appears, follow the instructions to create your personal AccessMedicine account.

       4.  Next, using your mobile device’s browser, go to http://m.accessmedicine.com.

       5.  Login using your AccessMedicine personal account username and password.
