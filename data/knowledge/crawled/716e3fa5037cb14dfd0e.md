## Library Overview
The UAEU Libraries are the gateway to finding a wide-range of resources and services put together to support the learning, teaching and research needs of the University community. They offer fine quality research services and resources, designed to meet the growing requirements of the University’s diverse academic programs. Currently, UAEU has two libraries – namely the Main Library (Crescent Building) and the Medical Library (College of Medicine and Health Sciences) that serve UAEU students, faculty, staff and community users.

Q: What are the Library open hours?

Library hours depend on time of the year, such as Regular Semester, Summer Semester, Ramadan, etc. It is best to see the current hours at our web page at https://www.library.uaeu.ac.ae/en/libraryhours.shtml, https://nml.uaeu.ac.ae/en/libraryhours.shtml

Q: Can the general community (non-UAEU) use the library?

A: Yes, the general community (non-UAEU) may use the library during appropriate male/female timings (see http://www.library.uaeu.ac.ae/en/libraryhours.shtml). If you wish to borrow any books, you can apply for Community User Membership, which requires a deposit and registration fee. For more information, please contact the circulation staff at circlib@uaeu.ac.ae or by telephone at 03-713-4684-(4677)-(4671)

How much money do I owe in library fines or fees?

The amount depends on whether or not you have overdue books that have accrued fines, and replacement and/or processing fees for lost or missing books. Usually, you will receive email notification for any overdue books or fees, but please check with the Lending staff to be sure at circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae or by telephone at 03-713-4684-(4677)-(4671).

How do I renew books?

There are a number of ways you can renews your books: Online at the Login page https://uaeu.account.worldcat.org/account/checkouts By email request at: circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae By telephone at 03-713-4684-(4677)-(4671) In person at any lending desk or self-check Machine.

How do I get clearance from the Library?

You should first make sure your library record is clear (all books returned, and any fines/fees are paid) by contacting the Lending Desk staff: By email request at: circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae Onsite at the Lending Desk By telephone at 03-713-4684-(4677)-(4671) In person at any lending desk
