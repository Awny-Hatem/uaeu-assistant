The second conference on Economic Policy and Research in the UAE and Beyond

February 23, 2023

Call for Papers


You   are   cordially   invited   to   submit   your   research   papers   for   presentation   at   the   second   conference
on “Economic Policy and Research in the UAE and Beyond” that is hosted by the American University
of      Sharjah      on      February      23,      2023.      This      one-day      conference      is      co-organized      by      the      UAE      Ministry      of
Economy,       American       University       of       Sharjah,       New       York       University       Abu       Dhabi,       United       Arab       Emirates
University,     and     Zayed     University.     The conference     will provide     an     opportunity     for     presenting     novel
research        findings        in                     Economics                     and                     Finance,                      as                      well                      as                      discussing                      current                      challenges                      and
opportunities, with a focus on the UAE economy. The conference will enable conversations between
policy makers, industry leaders, and academics.

The          organizing          committee          will          accept          paper          submissions          in          all          areas          of          Economics          and          Finance
through January 25, 2023. Notifications of acceptance will be sent by February 4, 2023.

Authors are invited to submit their papers in PDF format to econ_conference@aus.edu.

There are no registration fees. The conference organizers will cover lunch, dinner, and coffee breaks.
For   traveling   presenters, accommodation is   complimentary for   up   to   two   nights.   This   conference   is
generously sponsored by the American University of Sharjah.


Important Dates:

January 25, 2023: Paper Submission Deadline
February 4, 2023: Notification of Acceptance
February 23, 2023: Conference Day
