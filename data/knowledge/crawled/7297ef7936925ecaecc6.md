Statistical Consulting Unit

                                               Workshop #6

Title: Data Visualization with Tableau
Statistical Software: Tableau Desktop
Requirements: Hardware: A laptop capable of running the latest version of Tableau Desktop
Knowledge: Basic data literacy
Instructor: Dr. Kursad Asdemir (Tableau Certified Data Analyst)
Target Group: Anyone interested in gaining insights from data
Date: 19 Feb. – 25 Feb
Period: 3 days, 4 hours/day
Platform: Face to face Meeting
Inclusive of Scientific Materials: PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.
Workshop Fee: 1500 AED
Contact US: statconsult@uaeu.ac.ae

Description:
Public and private organizations have huge amounts of data. There is a great need for
individuals who can make sense of this data.  Tableau Desktop is a powerful data
visualization platform which can produce meaningful information from very large datasets.
It is intuitive, easy-to-use, and produces appealing visualizations.  The ability to use Tableau
Desktop is a vital skill of future managers and analysts who want success in using data to
improve performance and a valuable skill to include in your CV as a job seeker.
