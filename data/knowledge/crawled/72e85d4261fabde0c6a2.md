Procedures of Policy No. (9) - Disc ontinuation of Graduate Programs
     a)          The Dean of Graduate Studies, assisted by the College deans, has the primary
           responsibility for coordinating, reviewing, and recommending proposals for the
           discontinuation of academic programs.
     b)        Discontinuation of programs may occur in two ways:  suspension of academic
           programs and termination of academic programs.


1. Suspension of Graduate Programs
     a)              The request for suspension of an academic program may be initiated at any of the
           administrative levels within the University.  Any of the following criteria may form
           the basis for suspension of a program:
           (i)                        Evidence that the program is no longer contributing to the mission of the
                    Department, College or University;
           (ii)                     Substantial and significant decreases in student enrollment over a period of
                    several years;
           (iii)                     Failure of the program to graduate an average of three students per year over a
                    three-year period;
           (iv)                Low academic quality as indicated by results of program review or loss of
                    accreditation; or
           (v)                                            Insufficient human or financial resources to support the program.

     b.           Whenever the suspension of an academic program is initiated by a program or
           Department with the concurrence of the Department Chair and College Dean, the
           request must be approved by the Dean of CGS and the DVCRGS.  When program
           suspensions are initiated by the College dean, the Dean of CGS or the DVCRGS,
           sufficient opportunity must be provided to the program or Department to demonstrate

           program within the University or merger  with other programs may be considered.

     c.                     Suspension of a graduate program requires adequate notice.  Provision must be made
           to permit students in the program to comple te requirements for the degree.  During
           program suspension, no new students may be admitted to the program.  After
           appropriate review, recommendations for the suspension of academic programs are
           made by Dean of CGS and the DVCRGS.  Their recommendations for the suspension
           of academic programs are forwarded to the Vice Chancellor for final approval
           following review by the Academic Council.  Notice of the suspension of academic
           programs is submitted by the Vice Chancellor to the Chancellor and the University
           Council as an information item.  A program suspension may be lifted whenever the
           circumstances for suspension no longer exist.

2. Termination of Graduate Programs
       a)            Termination of graduate programs may be initiated by the respective Department,
               College or University and occurs under either of two circumstances:
               (i)                        Whenever there is approval by the Department, College and University on
                           terminating a suspended program.
               (ii)                             Whenever a graduate program remains under suspension for at least five years
                           and no action is taken to reinstate the program.

       b)         Program termination requires the recommendation of the Dean of CGS and the
               DVCRGS.  Their recommendations for the termination of academic programs are
               forwarded to the Vice Chancellor following review by the Academic Council.  The
               termination of academic programs is then recommended by the Vice Chancellor for
               approval to the Chancellor and the University Council.
