DEAR CAMPUS COMMUNIT Y

The past 16 months have been a turbulent time not only for the UAEU but for all higher education institutions
around the world. In March 2020 we were forced to switch overnight to online teaching and to send all our
students home.  Moving courses to online deliver y during the pandemic was not an easy task, yet all of you
worked as one community to accomplish this goal.

Words cannot express the pride I feel in the care and dedication of all our faculty, students, and staff who have
worked together to continue our students’ education during these extraordinar y times.

In December 2021 the UAE authorized the use of Sinopharm followed by the approval of other vaccines that hit
the market, bringing the hope that our students can return to campus.


REOPENING UAEU CAMPUS

While we are all anticipating returning to normal, we must proceed carefully. A Taskforce for reopening the
UAEU campus has worked on a plan with careful attention to ongoing changes and guidance being developed
by health authorities. We will continue to seek fur ther direction and to keep a close watch on health data as UAE
society gradually reopens. We will adapt our plans as warranted by developing conditions and guidance. Our
primar y goal remains the protection of lives.

Our major decision is to divide all undergraduate students into two groups. The first group will attend two
weeks of classes on campus (face to face), while the second group will attend classes online during the same
period. The two groups will alternate in a rotational system ever y two weeks until the end of the Fall Semester.
We will continue to obser ve the developing epidemiological information and direction from health exper ts.

Our goal in implementing these changes is the protection of our students, staff, faculty members, and members
of the wider community. Many of these changes require all of you once again to adjust to new ways of interacting,
and to adapt to new schedules and modes of teaching and learning.

Let us show strength of mind and team spirit as we reopen our campus. The success of this scenario depends on
all members of the UAEU community choosing to protect lives by wearing facemasks and by following all the
measures found in this brochure and in circulars sent by HR and Student Affairs.

With warm regards,




                                                    Professor Ghaleb Ali Alhadrami Al Breiki
                                                                                                       Acting Vice   Chancellor

Faculty Office Hours

•                                                                        Faculty should replace in person office hours with vir tual office hours and organize an appropriate means
            to schedule student access for an equivalent of the required hours.
•                                                                        Outside of classes in person on campus, all meetings with students should be held online.

Research and Laboratories

•                                                            All faculty members are granted access to their offices for the purpose of research.  Social distancing and
            occupancy protocols must be always obser ved.
•                                                                        Research collaboration should be done remotely when possible.
•                                                                        Librar y and other facilities will be open for use under social distancing protocols.
•                                                                        Additional protective protocols and guidelines will be posted in each laborator y.
•                                                                        Full personal protective equipment (PPE) must be worn, including a face mask and a laborator y coat.
•                                                                        Guests are not permitted in the laborator y.
•                                                                        Laborator y equipment and sur faces must be sanitized at regular required inter vals.
•                                                                        Staff occupancy is limited to %55 or less.
•                                                                         All personal in laboratories must sanitize their hands and gloves and dispose of PPE in the designated bins.

Laborator y Classes

•                                                                        Additional protective protocols and guidelines will be posted in each laborator y.
•                                                       Ensure that students are wearing full protective gear specified for the lab area and dispose of PPE in the
            proper bins.
•                                    Only faculty/staff teaching, and students enrolled in the laboratory class are permitted to enter the
            laboratory while the class is in session.
•                                                                        Always maintain 55 % or less of occupancy capacity.
•                                              Follow scheduled teaching times to permit students to properly dispose of PPE, allow for disinfection/
            cleaning, and to avoid gatherings in hallways.

Classrooms

•                                                                        Enrollment in courses will be adjusted to 55 % of occupanc y capacity, with no enrollment capacity overrides
            permitted.
•                                                                        Students and faculty members must wear face masks at all times.
•                                                                        No food or beverages are allowed in the classrooms.
