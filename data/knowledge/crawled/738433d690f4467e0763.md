## TOEFL iBT
The computer-based TOEFL iBT test, delivered via the Internet, is available at the UAEU TOEFL iBT test center. It was established in 2012 for internal candidates and to the external community. An increasing number of candidates pass through the center, including university students, candidates from the professional and education sector, and those taking the exam for job or emigration purposes. Each section measures a different skill, as follows:
