عن المجلة

About Journal

The International Journal for Research in Education started in 1984 under the name of Magallat Kulliyyat Al Tarbia at the United Arab Emirates University. The journal publishes original research in Arabic and English and serves educational and academic researchers and policy makers. It’s a refereed periodical that is concerned with publishing empirical research. The journal publishes educational research that pertains to contemporary educational issues, interdisciplinary research in education, conference and symposia reports, reviews of recent educational and psychological books in different fields of education.
