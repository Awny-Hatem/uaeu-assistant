                                                            Telephones – Direct Line and Mobile


I.                                                                                                     Policy

                      The telephone systems available at the United Arab Emirates University are
                      designed for business purposes only.  This includes calls through the
                      switchboard, fax equipment, mobile phones, direct lines and video lines.
                      Personal calls should be made only in the event of urgent necessity and shall
                      be reimbursed to the University, as appropriate.


II.                                                                                  Application

                      This policy shall apply to all employees of the United Arab Emirates University.
