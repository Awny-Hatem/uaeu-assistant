## Application Process
If you have a disability, it is helpful to let the Students of Determination Services office know as soon as you join the university. Following your contact with the center, an initial interview will be arranged. You will need to provide the completed medical documents as an evidence of your disability, medical condition, or specific learning difficulty. Such as a doctor s letter or educational psychologist s, report which contains a clear statement of the disability and recommended adjustments/accommodations.
