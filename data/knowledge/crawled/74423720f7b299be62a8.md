Department of Statistics and Business Analytics

                                        Statistical Consulting Unit
                                      Workshops/Training courses
Title: Applied Statistical Machine Learning
Statistical Software: R
Requirements: Familiarity of R Statistical Software and Basic Stat/Probability knowledge
Instructor: Dr. Subhadip Pal
Target Group: Professionals, graduate students, research workers
Date: 11 – 17 Nov., 2024
Period: 2 days, 4 hours/session, 8 hours total
Platform: Face-to-face Meeting
Inclusive of Scientific Materials: PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.
Description:
The short course plans to introduce basic statistical machine-learning algorithms from a high-
level view. Thereafter move forward with the data analysis of various types. The topics
included are a brief description of regression and high-dimensional regression with the analysis
of an appropriate data set. Various classification techniques and analysis of multiple nontrivial
datasets, such as the identification of object types from raw image data.
Topics Covered:
Day 1:   Basics of Statistical Machine         Learning, R basics. Application: Regression for high-
dimensional Data.
Day 2: Various Classification Techniques, Application: Classification based on raw image
data.
Workshop Fee: AED 1,000
