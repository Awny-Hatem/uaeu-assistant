## Online Program Payment > Online Program Payment > Withdrawal From Course Policy
If you withdraw from the course 2 working days before it commences, fees will be returned minus 15%.

If you withdraw from the course in the first day, fees will be returned minus 25%.

If you withdraw any time after the first day, no fees will be returned.

In case of course cancelation the full amount will be refunded to the customer.
