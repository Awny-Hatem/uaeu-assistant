         Release of Personal Information on Faculty, Staff and Students


I.                                                                        Policy

                  The United Arab Emirates University is committed to safeguarding the
                  privacy of faculty, staff and students.

                  To that end, the University will not publicly release personal information on
                  faculty, staff or students.  The Human Resources Department will verify
                  faculty and staff employment and the Admission and Registration Department
                  will verify student enrollment.

                  No other information will be made available without written authorization
                  from a faculty or staff member or from a student’s parent or guardian.

                  No photographic representation of a student, whether print, web, video or
                  film, will enter the public domain without prior written authorization from the
                  student and his/her parent or guardian.


II.                                                                             Application

                  This policy shall apply to all personal information on faculty, staff and
                  students and to photographs of students.
