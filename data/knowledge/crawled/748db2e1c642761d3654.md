                             Guide for Settlement of Fees – MEM Program, Spring 2014

     The Student Accounts Office (SAO), is responsible for the billing of all student fees and charges,
     and is located on the ground floor of Crescent Building, Wing D4, Male Registration Area,  Al
     Ain. Our working hours for student enquiries are 8am – 3pm, Sunday through Thursday.

     SAO Contact Information:
     Phone: +971 (0)3 7134284
     Email: Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae

     To view your account details,
          1.  Logon to https://my.uaeu.ac.ae by using your e-mail password
          2.  Select e-Services (top right hand corner), then Select  Student and  Financial Aid
          3.  Select Student Account, Select Account Detail for Term, then Select Term (201420)
          4.  Click Submit and your balance and amount due for payment will be displayed.

     Please note that an online payment facility is now available through e-services.

         To arrange a tuition payment installment plan, students must apply at the SAO before the
          13th March 2014. If you were entitled to an installment plan in the previous semester you
          must apply again for the current semester.

         Non Payment of total tuition fees on or before 11th May will result in a hold on registration and
          records. A hold means that you will not receive course grades until you pay your fees.  It also means
          that you will not be able to register for courses in the next semester until payment is made.
          Unfortunately it may also mean forfeiture of grades and credits earned during the semester.
