## UAEU Entrepreneurship Club Board Members at "Make it in the Emirates 2025"
Date: May, 19 - 22 , 2025

Title: UAEU Entrepreneurship Club Board Members at "Make it in the Emirates 2025"

Description: Dr. Ishara Maharaj and Dr. Paul Savage, Assistant Professors at the Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research, brought the Board Members of the UAEU’s Entrepreneurship Club to "Make it in the Emirates 2025," the nation’s flagship manufacturing and innovation event, held from 19–22 May at the Abu Dhabi National Exhibition Centre (ADNEC).

The event served as a unique platform for student leaders to explore the latest trends in AI-driven manufacturing, smart industrial transformation, and sustainable production, while connecting with top decision-makers and potential mentors.

The students could attend ministerial panels, startup pitch competitions, and strategic discussions, as well as visiting the stands and booths of companies making it in the UAE.

Their active involvement demonstrates UAEU’s dedication to nurturing the next generation of entrepreneurs and innovators.
