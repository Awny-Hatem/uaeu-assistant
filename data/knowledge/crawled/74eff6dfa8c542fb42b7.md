Student Accounts Office
                      Guide for Paying Fees for Bachelor Program for spring 2019



 The Student Accounts Office (SAO), is responsible for the billing of all student fees and
charges, and is located on the First floor of Crescent Building, Wing D4, Room 1020,
Al Ain. Our working hours for student enquiries are 8am – 3pm, Sunday through Thursday.

SAO Contact information:
             o   Phone:  03 7134265
             o   Email address:  Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae





 Payment method:
You can pay for the tuition via the e-payment ( online Payment), please log on to this page
to pay: https://ssb.uaeu.ac.ae/prod/bwykstpy.p_display_opt



                       Deadline for Settlement of Tuition & Fees for spring 2019
   1st Payment Deadline (50% of total tuition fees )                       13-17 January 2019
   2nd Payment Deadline (Remaining 50% of total tuition fees)              20 January – 07 February 2019


** Non-Payment of total tuition fees on or before 07 February 2018 will result to
withdrew courses that student registered.
