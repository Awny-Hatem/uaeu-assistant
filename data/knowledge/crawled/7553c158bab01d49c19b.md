## PDF page 5
Form Completion

   7. Please review the instructions mentioned at the top of the form carefully.
                   •                                      You must enter  your first choice for the  college you wish to transfer to, in addition to the
                         first and second majors (these fields are mandatory).
                   •                                      Attach the  required  documents, such as "Letter  of approval from sponsor " for
                         scholarship and study seat  students.

                   •                                      Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.























Important Notes :

1.                                      The list of colleges available for transfer different  for each student based on admission criteria  required
       for each college.
2.                                      The student must be in Good Academic Standing (Students  who are under probation are not allowed to
       apply for internal transfer).
3.                                      A student should not have finish more  than 50% of credit hours in his/her current  college to apply for
       Internal transfer.
4.                                      Students who have a scholarship need approval from the sponsoring and must be attached  to the online
       application.
5.                                      The student can cancel or modify the  transfer request  electronically before  the  college's decision.
6.                                     The student will be notified via email about the status of their application, whether it is accepted or
       rejected,   and they  can track the  application status online.
