1. Governance of Student Services

Overview
The Students     Affairs     Deanship     aims    to enhance    the learning environment at                    the University;
support students in the attainment of their educational objectives; foster in students a sense of
responsibility;   promote   an   environment of cultural diversity,               respect   for   others   and   a   student-
centered culture that serves           the academic mission of the University.

The establishment of this system of governance     operates to assure the alignment of the Student
Services        Policies with other University Policies and                                     ensure that these Policies are
contemporary, effective, and reflective of institutional values.
