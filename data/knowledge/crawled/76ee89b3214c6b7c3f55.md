## Program Structure > Program Structure
The detailed program structure shows that each semester consists of three core courses and a project-based course (GBUS 6X0). The project-based course is one of four courses distributed over four semesters and constituting a project-sequence. There are several project sequences available to students to choose from. Each project sequence is generated from a real business activity and developed by the organization’s subject matter expert and CBE faculty members. This document presents a roadmap of the projects sequences.
