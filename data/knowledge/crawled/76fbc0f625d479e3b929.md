16. Green Information Technology Policy

Overview
Defines Green IT best practices adopted by DoIT.

Scope
The scope of this policy is all DoIT activities, operations, facilities and equipment where green
IT best practices can be applied to minimize their negative impact on environment.

Objective
To minimize the negative impact on the environment.

Policy
DoIT is committed to the following:
1.  To follow best practices of environmentally sustainable computing.
2.  Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
3.  Reduce and eliminate the environmental impacts of our activities is an important part of
    our mission.
