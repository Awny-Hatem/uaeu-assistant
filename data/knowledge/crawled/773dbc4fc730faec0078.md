ENROLLMENT
              The following shows the enrollment data of the College of Education initial-licensure level programs:
         Early Childhood Education (ECE), Special Education (SPED                ), and Health & Physical Education (HPE).

              A.   EPP and Program Enrollment Trend from Fall 2022 to Fall 2025

                           From  Fall  2022  to  Fall  2025,  the  Educator  Preparation  Provider  (EPP)  experienced
                   substantial  and  sustained  growth  across  all  initial          ‑licensure  programs,  indicating  increasing
                   demand and strong program vitality.             Total enrollment increased markedly from 359 students in
                   Fall  2022  to  1,019  students  in  Fall  2025,  representing  nearly  a threefold  increase  over  the
                   four‑year period. The most significant growth occurred between Fall 2023 (554) and Fall 2025
                   (1,019), reflecting accelerated expansion beginning in Fall              2023 and continuing through Fall 2025.

                           ECE showed the strongest and most consistent growth among all programs. Enrollment rose
                   from 191 students in Fall 2022 to 691 students in Fall 2025, more than tripling within three
                   academic years. Growth accelerated sharply after Fall 2023, suggesting incr                        eased demand for
                   early childhood teachers and strong program attractiveness.

                           SPED enrollment increased from 126 students in Fall 2022 to 213 students in Fall 2025,
                   reflecting steady and sustained growth, highlighting continued demand for specialized education
                   professionals.
                           HPE, though smaller in        number, demonstrated stable and meaningful expansion, growing
                   from 42 students in Fall 2022 to 115 students in Fall 2025. Enrollment increased steadily each
                   year, indicating strengthened program visibility and relevance despite a smaller cohort base.

              C.   Fall 2024- Spring 2025 Enrollment by Nationality
                             The majority of students admitted and enrolled in ECE, SPED, and HPE programs are
                   Emiratis, with only 5.4% of the total student population being non-nationals. The EPP actively
                   recruits high school students from across the emirates through initiatives such as College Open
                   Days, High School Exhibitions, and School Site Visits, aiming to inspire them to become                           future
                   educators in the UAE.
