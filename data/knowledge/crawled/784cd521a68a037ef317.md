## Rooms Assignment Service
This service enables newly admitted and enrolled students to apply for on-campus student housing, and continuing students to apply for transfers between housing units, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

Start Service

## Rooms Assignment Service > Procedure
Sign in with UAE Pass or by UAEU username and password

Pay the housing fees (if applicable)

Select a building & room

Room assignment confirmation will be received by email.

Show all steps

## Rooms Assignment Service > Conditions & Requirements
Apply a room for New students: Student should be Enrolled in UAEU.

The student must be from outside Al Ain city by more than 50 km.

Housing fees should be paid before entering to housing unit for international students .

Trasnfer between Housing Unites for Current students: Student should be completed 60 credit hours (CHs) or more.

## Rooms Assignment Service > What are the payment options available for housing fees?
The payment process is made electronically by logging into the university website or bank transfer, and the cash payment option is not available.

## Rooms Assignment Service > Are there options available for installments or exemption from housing fees?
Housing fees are paid once before entering the housing units and cannot be paid in installments or exempted.

## Rooms Assignment Service > What are the housing units for female students?
Maqam 1, Maqam 2, Maqam 3, Maqam 4, New Campus (Al Rouda).

## Rooms Assignment Service > What are the housing units for male students?
Tawam, Saih bin Ammar, Maqam 5 (Al Ghadeer), Muwaiji, ZAFRAN.

## Rooms Assignment Service > What are the conditions of transferring between housing units?
The student must be enrolled and continuing in the university, and he/she completed 60 credit hours (CHs) or more.

## Rooms Assignment Service > When is transfer between housing units allowed?
The transfer between student housing units opens before the beginning of each semester.

## Rooms Assignment Service > How can we use medical reports to transfer to other housing units or a private room?
Submit a recent medical report issued by a government hospital or an approved specialist. The report must not be older than one month from the application submission date. Medical reports and supporting documents will be reviewed by the specialized Medical Committee, which meets every Thursday to evaluate cases and issue appropriate recommendations. Students will be notified of the application outcome within two working days following the Medical Committee meeting.
