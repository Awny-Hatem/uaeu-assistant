## Legal Clinic > Overview
The College of Law at UAEU has established a legal clinic/ learning center to provide legal solutions to the local community. This educational center implements the legal clinic approaches utilized by many law colleges around the world as a means for student learning as well as serving the legal needs of the local community.

The various services provided by the clinic include the provision of legal advice by students to clients. The law students are assisted in reviewing and drafting their advice by faculty members. The legal clinic also trains students in how to prepare drafted notes, legal papers and contracts or agreements regarding friendly settlements or legal problems. This all prepares future graduates to be leaders in their field.
