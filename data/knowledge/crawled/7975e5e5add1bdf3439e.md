                              Academic Program Change or Discontinuation


I.                                                                                               Policy

                A change in an existing academic program may be proposed and implemented
                when there is evidence that the change will result in program improvement
                and when resources sufficient to assure program quality can be committed.
                Proposed revisions in academic programs must be consistent with the United
                Arab Emirates University’s vision, mission and strategic plan.

                An existing academic program may be discontinued when there is evidence
                that it is not viable or that it is not an appropriate offering for the University.

                The University will establish procedures for the review of requests for
                program change or program discontinuation.  Proposals for academic program
                change or for the discontinuation of an academic program are reviewed and
                approved through the University’s procedures.

                This policy applies to changes in, or the discontinuation of, both degree
                programs and non-degree programs.  This policy applies only to program
                changes that affect the basic nature and overall content of a program and to
                the addition or deletion of concentrations within a major.  It does not include
                the proposal of a new academic program.


II.                                                                             Application

                This policy shall apply to all academic units of the United Arab Emirates
                University.
