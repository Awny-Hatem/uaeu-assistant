## All Non-Scholarship Bachelor Students Non-nationals/International non-scholarship
All Students Non-citizen non-scholarship wishing to use UAEU housing facilities must adhere to the following rules:

Only Students from outside the Al Ain City are hosted at UAEU housing.

Housing charge AED 5000 per semester including food service & transportation.

## International Exchange Students
Per Semester Housing Cost: AED 5,600 for (excluding Food Services)

Food services charges:

Breakfast AED 5.25

Lunch AED 32.25

Dinner AED 32.25

For more information kindly, contact Food Services Dept.: 03 - 713 5979

## International Exchange Students > Rules and Instructions For all Categories
Housing payment must be in advance and in one installment

Housing payment is non-refundable for any reason .

Payment must be completed before housing check in.

Payment can be made as e-payment , bank transfer or deposit slip to the university's cashier.

The housing office staff will not accept cash.

Student housing regulations and policies are applied.
