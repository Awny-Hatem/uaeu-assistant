## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Date Palms
Around 600 date palms grow on Al Foah Experimental Farm. These are used for research and educational purposes. Palms are from 4 to 30 years old, and are planted along roadsides as well as in orchards. Female cultivars represented on the farm are Sultana, Nabta Saif, Khinaizi, Barhi, Lulu and Khalas.

The Date Palm Friends Society headquarters are also located on Al Foah Experimental Farm.

Some date palm plantations were established to serve experimental purposes.
