## About CMHS > Overview
At the College of Medicine and Health Sciences, we are committed to fostering excellence in medical education, research, and healthcare. Our institution stands as a beacon of knowledge and innovation in the field of medicine, dedicated to producing highly skilled and compassionate healthcare professionals.

Shaping Healthcare Leaders Since 1984

Established in 1984 under the visionary leadership of His Excellency Sheikh Nahayan Mubarak Al Nahayan, Minister of Higher Education & Scientific Research, and Supreme Chancellor of the University, CMHS has been at the forefront of medical education, addressing the critical shortage of national physicians. With a commitment to excellence and a dedication to serving the healthcare needs of the community, CMHS has evolved into a distinguished institution over the years.

## About CMHS > Overview > Our Journey:
1984 - Inauguration: His Excellency laid the foundation for CMHS in 1984, recognizing the urgent need to bridge the gap in healthcare by nurturing local talent and creating a center of medical excellence.

1986 - First Medical Students: The journey began with the admission of the first batch of medical students in 1986, marking the commencement of a legacy in healthcare education.

1997 - Tawam Building Donation: A significant milestone was achieved in 1997 when the Tawam building was generously donated, providing CMHS with a state-of-the-art facility to enhance the learning environment for our students.

2007 - Post Graduate Program: CMHS took a progressive step forward in 2007 by introducing its first Post Graduate Program, expanding opportunities for advanced medical education and specialization.

2010 - EHS Establishment: In the same year, CMHS furthered its commitment to community well-being with the establishment of Emirates Health Services (EHS) as a registered healthcare providing facility.

2019 - Genetics and Genomics: The Department of Genetics and Genomics was inaugurated in 2019, reflecting CMHS's dedication to advancing medical research and embracing cutting-edge innovations.

2020 - Health Sciences Division: Continuing our journey of growth, the Health Sciences Division was added in 2020, fostering a multidisciplinary approach to healthcare education.

TODAY – Over 1,300 alumni, 1,000+ students enrolled in 13 programs, taught by 150+ academic staff

Explore the opportunities that CMHS offers for education, research, and community service. Join us in shaping the future of healthcare—where tradition, innovation, and excellence converge. Welcome to CMHS, where the journey to becoming a healthcare leader begins

## About CMHS > Research Labs
The research laboratories at CMHS are equipped with the necessary infrastructure and resources, including state-of-the-art equipment and technologies. In addition, a store is available for general consumables, chemical reagents, and kits. Research in our laboratories is further supported by the development and maintenance of central research facilities, such as the Imaging Unit, Analytical and Molecular Unit, Cellular Biology Facility, as well as Computational Facilities. These labs are equipped to support a wide range of functions, including cell culture, molecular biology experiments, DNA cloning, RT-PCR analysis, heterologous protein expression and purification in both eukaryotic and prokaryotic systems. They also provide resources for stereotaxic surgery of rodents, stereo investigator systems, and computational sciences.

## About CMHS > Research Animal Facility
The research animal facility at the CMHS is probably one of it s kind in the UAE. The aim of the facility is to support a proper, safe, and humane research effort by providing controlled environments for the care and maintenance of experimental animals. Learn more.

## About CMHS > Anatomy Dissecting Laboratory
The Anatomy Dissecting Laboratory is a key teaching facility for the training of medical students in Gross Anatomy. The Dissecting Room serves both undergraduate and postgraduate medical students and is accessible to students after regular classes.

The laboratory consists of three large rooms and several storage areas. It is equipped with numerous stainless-steel tables for the display of whole cadavers or body parts. Additionally, it features cabinets that house original bones, plastic models, and prosections of joints, limbs, the thorax, abdomen, pelvis, and perineum. The lab also contains a small collection showcasing different stages of embryonic development, as well as sagittal sections of the whole body, neatly displayed in Perspex glass frames. A full skeleton and its parts are available for student use, along with X-ray films, MRI scans, X-ray viewers, books, and videos to enhance the learning of anatomy in clinical medicine.

The Dissecting Laboratory boasts an excellent collection of coronal, sagittal, and frontal sections of the human brain. To support computer-based and e-learning, the laboratory is equipped with computers, TV monitors, and projectors.

## About CMHS > Basic Science Teaching Labs
These are multidisciplinary labs where undergraduate practicals are conducted. Students from the first to fourth year in the College of Medicine & Health Sciences utilize these facilities. The labs are equipped with modern de-fuming chambers, gas detectors, and a high-quality gas and compressed air supply system. They are spacious enough to accommodate around thirty students at a time. The Biochemistry, Physiology, and Pharmacology departments regularly conduct practicals in these labs.

The facility features high-quality lab infrastructure, with all labs connected to the computer network and equipped with internet access. The lab equipment is highly modernized, enabling students to adapt to sophisticated labs elsewhere. A number of computer-based practicals have been introduced, allowing students to keep pace with the increasing integration of computers in education. The use of animals as subjects has been significantly reduced; instead, highly protected and safe devices are used directly on human subjects. The labs are also equipped with modern recording and data collection systems.

## About CMHS > Microscopy Lab
The Microscopy and Imaging Facility provides excellent electron and confocal laser microscopy services to a wide variety of individuals and departments across CMHS and UAEU. The facility is equipped with Transmission Electron Microscopes, Environmental Scanning Electron Microscope, Confocal Laser Microscopes, and Fluorescence Microscopes. The labs are located on the second floor of Block D. Two of the labs have low benches, suitable for microscopy work, while the third lab features tall benches. The Anatomy, Pathology, and Microbiology departments utilize these labs. In addition to the labs, the area includes storage rooms, chemical stores, a pathology museum, and office space.

The microscopy labs are equipped with a sufficient number of high-quality microscopes, allowing each student to handle a microscope individually. They also feature a high-resolution CCTV system and a computer-aided image analysis system.

## About CMHS > Computer Labs
The College has three well-equipped computer labs. Each lab contains 31 networked computers, including one for the instructor. The instructor’s computer is connected to a projector or smartboard. These labs are used for computer-based courses, including statistics, computer-based laboratory teaching, online library and search tools.

## About CMHS > UAEU Medical Simulation Center
The UAEU Medical Simulation Center (iSTAR) is located within the College of Medicine and Health Sciences (CMHS) and spans both the ground and first floors, covering approximately 43,000 square feet. This purpose-built facility provides a comprehensive environment for simulation-based education, training, and assessment across undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional programs.

The center supports the development of clinical, procedural, communication, and team-based competencies through a wide range of simulation modalities, including high- and low-fidelity simulators, task trainers, and standardized patient programs. Designed to reflect real clinical environments, iSTAR enables safe, immersive learning without risk to patients.

A multidisciplinary team delivers structured educational programs and supports high-stakes assessments, including OSCEs, as well as national and international examinations such as Royal College and Arab Board assessments. Learn more.

## About CMHS > Assessment and Evaluation Center
The College of Medicine & Health Sciences houses a world-class assessment center, designed according to international guidelines for conducting online assessments. The center has a seating capacity of 180 candidates and includes its own server room, reception, waiting area, locker facilities, and washroom facilities. To accommodate gender segregation, the center is divided into two blocks. It is set up to meet international standards and is an authorized testing center. In addition to in-house CMHS student examinations, the center also hosts various external exams, including licensing examinations for medical professionals.

## About CMHS > Auditorium and Theatres
The College of Medicine & Health Sciences has a large multipurpose auditorium and two smaller theaters that can be utilized for educational activities by both internal and external users. The Khalifa Auditorium, accessible from the building's main entrance, can accommodate up to 660 participants. It is equipped with stage lighting, amplified audio, and a large screen projector. The two smaller venues, Yanah Theatre and Fatima Theatre, are designed for smaller groups and can each host up to 120 participants. These theaters are equipped with desks, seating, and microphones, making them ideal for seminars and presentations.

## About CMHS > UAEU Genomic Laboratory
The UAEU Genomics Laboratory has been established as a center of excellence for research to ultimately reduce the impact of genetic disease on the UAE community. This new laboratory contains state of the art equipment and technologies for studying Genomics, Metabolomics and Bioinformatics.

The Genomics laboratory is equipped with Next Generation Sequencing (NGS) platforms such as NovaSeq 6000 sequencing system, MiSeq Sequencing System in addition to Biomek i7 Liquid Handling system, Covaris LE220-plus- Focused Ultrasonicator, Automated DNA isolation system (QiACube and QiACube HT), RT-PCR- Machine (QuantStudio 7 Flex), PCR Robotic workstation, several PCR machines and other ancillary and small equipment.

Bioinformatics Platforms for ultra-rapid sequencing analysis are installed with full operational support from the CMHS and central IT team. An in-house information management system is being developed to track and mange samples that can streamline the workflow. Learn more.

## About CMHS > Community Service
Zayed Bin Sultan Center for Health Sciences

The United Arab Emirates University, with the generous support of the Zayed Foundation, has established the Zayed Bin Sultan Center for Health Sciences (ZCHS). This is the Colleges flagship research center for population health. The center was established to support and contribute to the UAEU's mission of becoming a world-class center for applied research, with national and international outreach and innovation whilst preserving the unique values of the UAE. The center will build an international reputation and intends to forge formal links with other universities and institutions through equitable memoranda of understanding.

Further details available: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/research/centers/zchs/

Membership of Expert Advisory Committees

CMHS is proud to provide academic staff members to chair and serve on expert advisory committees across local, national, regional, and international levels. These committees are crucial in shaping healthcare policies, guidelines, and protocols, contributing to clinical services, and providing expert consultation and training. Our faculty members bring their expertise to various sectors, including government departments, industry, and legal bodies such as the National Immunization Technical Advisory Group, and the judiciary.

At the regional level, our staff are actively involved in organizations like the Arab Board of Health Specializations and the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) Health Council. Internationally, CMHS faculty contribute to global health initiatives through participation in committees such as the World Health Organization (WHO) expert panels. Additionally, our staff participate as external examiners, assessors for promotion, and members of various other expert committees, ensuring the highest standards in healthcare and education. Their involvement reflects our commitment to advancing healthcare and education in the UAE and beyond.
