GRADUATION DATA
     The following are the graduation data of the College of Education CAEP programs –Early Childhood
Education (ECE), Special Education (SPED, and Health & Physical Education (HPE).


     A.   Number of Completers
                    There was a total of 125 Bachelor’s Degree completers in Fall 2020 and Spring 2021 in
          which 57% are graduates of the ECE program, 34% are graduates of the SPED program, and 9%
          are graduates of the HPE program. An increasing trend in the total number of completers for the
          last three terms is observed in the Early Childhood Education program while the Special
          Education program has an average of 23 completers per term. The Health & Physical Education
          program is expected to have more completers in the coming terms based on the 26% average
          increase per term in enrollment over the last five years (see Table 6.1 and Figure 6.1).

B.   Graduation Rates

               The 6-year graduation rates for five cohorts for the ECE, SPED, & HPE programs are
     shown in Figure 6.2. There is an average of 82.16% six-year graduation rate over the last five
     cohorts for the ECE program (see Figure 6.3), 89.30 % over the last five cohorts for the SPED
     program (see Figure 6.4), and 26.43% over the last four cohorts for the HPE program (see Figure
     6.5).
