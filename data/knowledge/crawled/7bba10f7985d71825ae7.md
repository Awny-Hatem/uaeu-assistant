       (ITSS)                     Integration (EAI)                  Technologies (ICT)

      leges, students affairs and administration units. Departments is adminis-
      trating all end user problems and development with a centralized point
      of contact.


 Enterprise Applications and Integration [EAI]

      EAI is responsible for providing application systems, integration, and

      development services to support University academic and administrative
       Functions.

 Infrastructure and Core Technologies [ICT]

      ICT is responsible for the development and evolution of the University‟s
      servers and network infrastructure.

What is UAEU Information Card ?



Once you receive the UAEU Information Card you will be able to access dif-
ferent UAEU online services using the login information provided inside.
This card entitles you access to:

   University‟s PCs
   Data Storage
   UAEU Portal


Login to UAEU’s PC

Active Directory [AD] is a Microsoft directory service used primarily in Win-
dows-based computers.  Its main purpose is to allow central authentication.
All faculty members and staff can use their AD account to login to any PC
linked to the UAEU domain also known as [ACADEMICS].

To login to a PC:

    Turn on the PC / Laptop linked to the domain
    Press Ctrl + Alt + Delete
    Type your username and password
    Select ACADEMICS from the drop down list provided next to log on to

Data Storage

1 GB storage space on the AD File Servers is dedicated for each user. This
network storage can be accessed anywhere within the campus and it is au-
tomatically mapped to the Z drive on any workstation linked to the domain.


UAEU Portal

UAEU Portal– is the UAEU gateway and starting point for each staff and fac-
ulty. By logging to UAEU Portal different online resources can be accessed
[http://my.uaeu.ac.ae].

Spam [Quarantine]

Due to the large amount of incoming spam e-mails, UITS has enforced an
anti-spam policy to reduce the amount of spam received in your Inbox.
There may be occasions when a legitimate email is tagged as spam howev-
er, you will be able to access these messages. These emails will have no
danger to the system if they are released from the Spam Quarantine Folder
to your email inbox. They have all been scanned for viruses.

When an email is tagged as spam, you will receive an email message in your
inbox to inform you about that. To take an action you can login by clicking
the link provided down in the email and enter your username and password.







You Can:
    Release        e-mail to your University e-mail inbox.
    Delete         Message from your spam quarantine folder.
    Blacklist      a message so that all future emails from the sender ARE
                   NOT delivered.
    Whitelist      a message so that all future emails from this sender ARE
                   delivered directly to your University email inbox.




         Note
 Another way to access your quarantine emails:
 https://fmc.antispam.uaeu.ac.ae

Unified Communication System

UAE University developed the unified communication system (UC) and is
offering services related to IP Phone, IP Fax, Voicemail and Presence. UC
enables you to receive your communications on one device, for example;
Your PC.

Services Available:


      IP Phone [Desk Phone]

You will have a physical (Desk Phone) connected with your PC. It allows you
to answer/place calls from the         desk phone itself  and from a PC  using
softphone. Voicemail is configured and integrated with Outlook; so you can
listen to your voicemails from Outlook or even by just dialing the voicemail
number from any phone.


          iPhone Application

You can use your Mobile to connect through to your desk phone. UITS has
started this service to iPhone users, who can now use their extensions
through their iPhone as long as they are connected to the University net-
work (wireless or VPN). To learn more visit UITS home page using the link:
http://www.uaeu.ac.ae/uits.              Under Hardware and Software click on
Technical support and check the tutorial section for more information.


  Cisco Personal Communicator

Allows you to do Instant messaging and make video calls using the Cisco
Personal Communicator from your PC. It also provides Presence information
about you; whether you are “on phone” or in a meeting (integrates with
Calendar). To enable this service you should contact UITS Helpdesk.

Remote Access Virtual Private Network [VPN]


UITS provides „off-campus‟ users with secure access to the UAE University‟s
internal network resources. You can access your desktops, servers or differ-
ent UAEU applications from any location outside University campuses
through SSL VPN. You can use a web browser to establish a remote access
connection.

To get SSLVPN account, you will be requested to fill the SSL VPN application
form available at MyUAEU Portal, under Helpdesk Channel.



Remote Dialup Access


Remote Dial-up service allows you to access local network services (Intranet)
over the telephone network. This service is provided mainly for those who do
not have high speed Internet access at home [ADSL].

   To access the service:

      Establish a dialup connection
      Enter your UAEU AD/PC Username and Password
      Dial the number 7556333
