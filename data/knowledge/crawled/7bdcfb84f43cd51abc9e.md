Statistical Consulting Unit

                                          Workshops/Training courses
                                                     Spring 2023

                                                  Workshop # 1

Title: Introduction to Data Analytics using SPSS - Introductory course
Statistical Software: SPSS
Requirements: Basic knowledge in statistics
Instructor: Dr. Ibrahim Abdalla
Target Group: Professionals, Under/Postgraduate students
Date: 27 Jan. – 29 Jan.
Period: 3 days, 4 hours/day
Platform: Face to face Meeting
Inclusive of Scientific Materials: PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.
Workshop Fee: 1500 AED
Contact US: statconsult@uaeu.ac.ae

Description:

The workshop introduces participants to IBM SPSS for Windows. Participants will be trained on
comparing and protecting original datasets, and on handling data files in a variety of formats, including
Excel, database tables, SAS, and Stata. They will also be trained on how to explore SPSS Data Editor
which provides for the display of data and variables. The workshop also offers hands on training on
data entry, variable, and data manipulation, including finding and replacing data and variables, data
transformation and descriptive statistics. In addition to other important SPSS features, participants will
also explore working with SPSS output, pivot table, and command syntax.
Topics Covered:

Day 1:

    •    Getting started with IBM SPSS for Windows.
    •    Data files in SPSS: Types of data files, Opening/Reading different data files.
    •    File information.
    •    Comparing datasets.
    •    Protecting original data.



Day 2:

    •    Data editor: Data view. Variable view. Entering data. Editing data. Finding
         cases/variables/imputation. Finding and replacing data and attribute. Obtaining descriptive
         statistics. Case selection.
