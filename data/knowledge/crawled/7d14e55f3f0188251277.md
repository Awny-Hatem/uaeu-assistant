## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Workers
Al Foah Experimental Farm would not be able to function in research and education without the great support of its well experienced workers. These men are trained to support a wide range of activities related to agricultural production, research and education.

For their research or educational projects on the farm, UAEU Faculty members can request support of our farm workers through submitting the filled ‘Labor Request Form' .
