Dear Vice Chancellor Alraeesi:

This letter serves as formal notification and official record of action taken concerning United
Arab Emirates University (UAEU) by the WASC Senior College and University Commission
(WSCUC) at its meeting June 27, 2025. This action was taken after consideration of the report of
the review team that conducted the Special Visit to United Arab Emirates University February
19-                                               21, 2025using the 2023 Standards of Accreditation. The Commission also reviewed the
institutional report and exhibits submitted by United                                                                                                 Arab Emirates University prior to the
Special Visit and the institution’s May 7, 2025 response to the team report. The Commission
appreciated the opportunity to discuss the visit with you and your colleagues Mohsen Sherif,
Provost; Amr Sweedan, Director of Academic Affairs and WSCUC ALO; Rami Beiram,
Associate Provost for Research; Walid Ibrahim, Director, Office of Institutional Effectiveness;
and Rami Hamdan, Advisor, Provost Office.Your comments were very helpful in informing the
Commission’s deliberations. The date of this action constitutes the effective date of the
institution’s status with WSCUC.

Actions

     1.                                                           Receive the Special Visit team report that focused on: (1) effectiveness of the University
           Council as the institution’s governing board; (2) representation of women and Emirati
           nationals in the faculty; (3) post-graduation employment rates; and (4) plan for growth of
           the institution’s research profile.
     2.                                                           Continue with previously scheduled reaffirmation review with the Offsite Review in fall
           2031 and the Accreditation Visit in spring 2032
     3.                                                           Schedule a Progress Report to be submitted by April 1, 2027 to address all requirements
           in this letter

Commendations

The Commission commends the institution                                      for:

     1.                                                           Demonstrated commitment to continuous improvement.
     2.                                                           Initiating proactive efforts to develop and expand UAEU’s faculty and staff as well as
           those in leadership positions consistent with the institution’s mission and priorities.

3.                                                           Growing and deepening employer relations and expanding student preparation for and
              awareness of post-graduation employment opportunities.
       4.                                                           Creating a comprehensive research growth plan and for efforts to promote and support
              faculty research.

Areas for Development

The Commission requires the institution to respond to the following areas for development                                                          :

       1.                                                           Continue to take actions that are designed to improve the effectiveness of the University
              Council as the university’s governing board and to ensure that policies crucial for
              strategy and university operations have appropriate and timely approvals. (CFRs 3.7, 3.9,
              3.11)
       2.                                                           Continue with implementation of board (University Council) self-study
              recommendations. (CFR 3.8)

In accordance with Commission policy, a copy of this letter is beingsent to the chair of                                                                         United
Arab Emirates University’s governing board.                                                                                                                         The Commission expects that the team report and
this action letter will be posted in a readily accessible location on the United Arab Emirates
University’s website and widely distributed throughout the institution            to promote further
engagement and improvement and to support the institution's response to the specific issues
identified in these documents            . The team report                        and the Commission’s action letter will also be
posted on the WSCUC website. If the institution wishes to respond to the Commission action on
its own website, WSCUC will post a link to that response on the WSCUC website           .

Finally, the Commission wishes to express its appreciation for the extensive work that United
Arab Emirates University undertook in preparing for and supporting this Special Visit review.
WSCUC is committed to an accreditation process that adds value to institutions while
contributing to public accountability, and we thank you for your continued support of this
process. Please            contact me if you have any questions about this letter or the action of the
Commission.
