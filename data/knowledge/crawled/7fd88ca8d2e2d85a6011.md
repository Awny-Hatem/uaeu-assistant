                                                                Courses and Terms of Study


I. Policy

                  The United Arab Emirates University shall adopt the credit hour system as a
                  basis for its educational system and shall organize its educational process on a
                  semester basis.

                  The academic year is divided into two semesters, the duration of which is
                  sixteen weeks, plus the final examinations period.  A summer semester may
                  be organized for a minimum duration of six weeks, with a minimum of fifteen
                  contact lecture hours corresponding to each credit hour.

                  The hours allotted for advising, registration and end-of-semester examinations
                  are in addition to the required contact lecture hours.

                  The College of Medicine and Health Sciences shall use a system through
                  which a student should pass all required stages in order to get the Bachelor
                  degree.

                  Curricula for the academic departments, units or programs shall be organized
                  in such a way whereby the minimum number of credit hours required for
                  obtaining a Bachelor degree is 120 credit hours.  Certain colleges may
                  establish additional requirements.


II. Application

                  This policy shall apply to all students at the United Arab Emirates University.
