6. Graduate Student Tuition, Fees, and Accounts

Overview
Covers policies and procedures relating to the management of graduate student tuition and fees
including assessment of tuition and fees, and tuition and fee payment.

Scope
Applies to all students and all academic and related administrative units of the University.

Objective
Ensures that the graduate student tuition, fee assessment, and student accounts functions of the
University are conducted in a manner that is consistent with academic principles, standards,
and expectations of the University, as appropriate to its educational mission and academic
programs, and with the necessary fairness, consistency, and effectiveness.

Policy
1.  Within the context and limits of applicable laws, the University is authorized to assess and
    collect tuition and fees for various academic and related services.

2.  Tuition    approved by the University Council               is charged for all instructions         to graduate
    students as specified in the Schedule of Tuition and Fees (Schedule).

3.  The responsibility for payment of tuition and fees rests with the individual student.

Procedures of Policy No. (6) - Graduate Student Tuition, Fees, and
Accounts

1. Tuition and Fees
    a)   The University will publish annually a Schedule of Tuition and Fees specifying relevant
         applicability and amounts.
    b)   Tuition is a charge for        educational services that       include teaching and instruction, is
         assessed on a per credit hour basis at rates established by the University, and may
         include purchase/rental of textbooks.  Specific tuition charges can be affected by student
         classification, residency, academic program, and degree level.
    c)   Fees are charges for academic, personal, and related support services provided by or
         through the University. These include application processing, special course activities
         and services, housing, dining, direct services for individual students such as medical
         insurance and residency fees, academic records services, tuition installment payment
         fees, fines and late processing/late action fees, textbooks, and other fees as specified by
         the University.
    d)   In the case of courses offered for zero (0) credit hours, tuition and fee charges equivalent
         to the instructional time of the course may apply.
    e)   Changes in the published Schedule of Tuition and Fees during the course of a given
         academic year may be made upon approval according to the Signatory Authority.


2. Payment of Tuition and Fees
    a)   Tuition charges are due and payable in full at the beginning of each academic semester
         according to the relevant deadlines in the Schedule.
    b)   Students paying all or a portion of their tuition charges directly (“self-pay” students) are
         eligible to participate in an installment payment program provided that the student
         makes the required initial payment by the initial payment deadline.
    c)   Students claiming external sponsorship for the payment to tuition charges must provide
         written confirmation of the sponsorship as specified in the Schedule not later than the
         initial  payment  deadline.    Sponsored  students  who  do  not  submit  the  required
         confirmation  of  sponsorship  and  continue  in  enrollment  will  assume  personal
         responsibility for all tuition charges and applicable fees.
    d)   Students who fail to pay all applicable tuition charges by the established payment
         deadline/s are subject to denial of academic services, late payment fees, requirement of
         advance payment of fees in full for future              semesters, and/or cancellation of current
         and/or future registration.

    e)   Fees are due and       payable at the time the services are provided, in conjunction with
         tuition payments, or as otherwise specified by the University.
    f)   Application processing fees, selected individual services fees, and required enrollment
