## Overview
Published academic-year scope: 2023-2024 | 2024-2025
The Bridging Courses in English and Mathematics were established in accordance with UAEU Chancellor’s Resolution No. (27) of 2023 concerning student admission standards at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) for the academic years 2023/2024 and 2024/2025.

This resolution stipulates the implementation of an English Bridging Course for conditionally admitted students and Mathematics Bridging Courses for provisionally admitted students. These courses are designed to enable such students to fulfill the requisite academic admission criteria and to facilitate their seamless and successful transition into full-degree university studies, in full compliance with the University’s official admission policies and regulations.

## English Bridging Course
The English Bridging Course is designed for conditionally admitted students who have not achieved the minimum English language proficiency required for direct admission.

Course Objectives

Develop students' academic English language skills.

Prepare students for success in university studies.

Enable students to meet final admission requirements within the specified period.

Completion Period

Students must Complete the direct admission English language requirements or successfully complete the English Bridging Course within one academic semester from the date of conditional admission.

## Mathematics Bridging Courses
The Mathematics Bridging Courses are designed for provisionally admitted students in selected colleges to strengthen their mathematical skills and enable them to meet college admission requirements.

Available Courses

Course | Target Group
MATH 097 | Students intending to enroll in the College of Law who have not met the Mathematics admission requirements.
MATH 098 | Students in the College of Business and Economics (CBE) and the College of Agriculture and Veterinary Medicine (CAVM).
MATH 099 | Students in the College of Science (COS), College of Engineering (COE), and College of Information Technology (CIT).

Completion Period

Students must Complete the Mathematics admission requirements or successfully complete the Bridging Course within a maximum of two academic semesters.
