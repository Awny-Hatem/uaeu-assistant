            Accounting System and Processing Accounting Transactions


I.                                                                                               Policy

                 1.                                                    Financial transactions will be recorded and reported consistent with
                                  the provisions of the By-law, the needs of the United Arab Emirates
                                  University and in accordance with generally accepted accounting
                                  principles, standards and practices applicable to Federal institutions in
                                  the United Arab Emirates.

                 2.                                   The Financial Affairs Department will maintain the University’s
                                  accounting system and financial operations to meet the needs of the
                                  University, in compliance with statutory and regulatory requirements
                                  including the University By-law.  It will record and report the
                                  University’s financial transactions and also provide financial
                                  information and reports to support the effective and efficient
                                  management and operations of the University.  The Financial Affairs
                                  Department will observe appropriate professional standards, due
                                  diligence and good business practices in fulfilling their mandate, roles,
                                  responsibilities and duties.


II.                                                                             Application

                 This policy shall apply to all operating units of the United Arab Emirates
                 University.
