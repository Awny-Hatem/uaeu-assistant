## 30 minutes for Us > Negative Thinking Traps
This session was delivered by Ms. Nadia Almheiri. She is a trainer, coach and human resource development expert. In her talk on Negative thinking traps she spoke about the difficulty one faces in reading the other persons mind and also to always have a positive outlook towards life and reduce the negative thinking. She also spoke about not blaming others for one s failures and to accept your mistakes and rectify them.
