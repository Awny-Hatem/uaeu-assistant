## College of Information Technology > Research Overview
CIT faculty and students are engaged in cutting-edge applied research, developing solutions and products that serve the needs of society. CIT research output has increased over the past few years with contributions to many prestigious and often cited publications and world class journals, conferences and books as well as many international patents.

CIT’s research covers a wide range of issues relevant to the needs of the nation. These areas include Data Science, Health, Education, Communications Networks, Transportation, Information Security and Privacy, Microelectronics and Energy. CIT faculty have taken part in several internally and externally funded research projects in cooperation with ICT, SRC and the Emirates Foundation, and ADEC among others. Some of these research projects have been participated in by UAE national research assistants, including undergraduate and graduate students from the CIT.
