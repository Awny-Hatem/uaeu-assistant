## Sport Science & Physical Activity Fair 2026
The Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair 2026 is a dynamic fair designed to bring together researchers, practitioners, students, athletes, and community members to explore the latest advancements in sports science and physical activity.

Held at the UAE University – Sheikh Zayed Founder Hall , the fair provides a modern, interactive environment divided into four specialized stations, each offering a unique experience in research, innovation, technology, and community engagement.
