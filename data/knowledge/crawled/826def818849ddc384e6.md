Agreement between a Graduate Student and
      the Office of Enrollment Deanship at the United Arab Emirates
                                       University (UAEU)


I, Your Name <                                                             >, Student ID <                       >, the
undersigned, fully understand that I am permitted to enroll into a graduate degree
program at UAEU pending my submission of the required equivalency(ies) of my
university degree(s) from the Ministry of Education of the United Arab Emirates, as
required and according to the UAEU policies and procedures. Furthermore, I fully
agree to the following:
    1.   I will be solely responsible for obtaining the required equivalencies of my
         university degrees/diplomas from the UAE Ministry of Education and to
         present the official documents to the UAEU Department of Admissions as
         soon as they become available and before I complete the requirements of my
         graduate degree program at UAEU.
    2.   UAEU is neither responsible nor liable for any matters, issues or
         circumstances related to my obtaining of the required equivalencies.
    3.   UAEU will not clear me for graduation from the graduate program in which I
         am enrolled and that I will not be entitled for a graduate degree from UAEU if
         I fail to submit the required official equivalencies by the time I complete the
         other requirements of my graduate program.
    4.   UAEU may cancel or suspend my enrollment or dismiss me from the
         University at any time due to not having the required official equivalencies in
         cases where the University is required to conform to new policies or
         governmental requirements.
    5.   If I am dismissed or am unable to graduate from UAEU due to my failure to
         submit the required equivalencies, I will not be entitled to any refunds or
         compensation of any dues or fees from the University.
