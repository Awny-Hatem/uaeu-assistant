## From First Chance to Impact: A Graduate's Success Story
Title: From First Chance to Impact: A Graduate's Success Story

Date: Thursday, 25 September 2025

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Mrs. Shamma Salem Al Darmaki

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: Shamma Salem Al Darmaki, a graduate of the College of Business and Economics at United Arab Emirates University, currently serves as Acting Branch Director at the Abu Dhabi Chamber. With over ten years of professional experience in business development, entrepreneurship empowerment, and strategic initiatives, she has led diverse teams in creating impactful programs that strengthen entrepreneurship and support key economic sectors in Al Ain and Al Dhafra. She has also contributed to building influential community and business partnerships and aligning initiatives with Abu Dhabi’s sustainable growth strategy. Believing in the power of innovation and institutional transformation, Shamma is committed to driving positive change and enhancing the competitiveness of the business environment. She represents an inspiring success story of a UAEU alumna who combines academic excellence with professional leadership and real-world impact.
