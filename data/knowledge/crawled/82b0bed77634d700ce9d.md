## Overview
The United Arab Emirates has taken center stage as a global epicenter for the promotion, celebration, and cultivation of the arts and the vibrant tapestry of art culture. In the heart of this growing cultural haven, numerous international art and literature exhibitions and festivals have found their home, making it a beacon for creativity and expression.

One of the prestigious initiatives is the Chancellor's Award for Arts, an endeavor designed to ignite the flames of creativity and leadership within the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) community.

Beyond the confines of the academic world, the Chancellor's Award for Arts competition seamlessly connects with the broader national strategy for cultural and creative industries. This constructive collaboration, between academia and the nation's vision, is a testament to the nation's commitment to harnessing the creative power of the arts to shape a brighter and more innovation future.

The award will actively involve students, faculty, and staff members of the UAEU community within the following artistic areas relating to this year’s theme: The Family .

Literature (including poetry, novels, short stories, and similar texts).

Painting (including oil painting, pencil drawing, watercolor painting, and similar techniques.)

Sculpture

Arabic Calligraphy

Visual Communication (Advertisement, Poster, Themed Design)

Music
