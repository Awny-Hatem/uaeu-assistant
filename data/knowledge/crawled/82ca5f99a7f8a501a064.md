## Awards > The Chancellor’s Innovation Award
The Chancellor’s Innovation Award competition aims to raise awareness of the importance of innovations, to support and encourage innovators to actively contribute to the transformation of the economy of the UAE into a knowledge-based economy and to contribute to attaining the comprehensive and sustainable social and economic development of the country.

## Awards > The Chancellor’s Award for Arts
The United Arab Emirates has taken center stage as a global epicenter for the promotion, celebration, and cultivation of the arts and the vibrant tapestry of art culture.
