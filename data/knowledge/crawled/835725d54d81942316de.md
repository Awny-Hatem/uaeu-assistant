## University Outreach Department > Overview
The University Outreach Department (UOD) at UAEU is the official source of the University’s news and information. The Department is committed to strengthening the University’s image and increasing brand awareness among the UAE Community and internationally. The Department manages and facilitates external relationships with the different communities including the Media, the government and the general public. Internally, UOD communicates the University's messages and announcements to faculty, administrators, and staff, as well as to students.

## University Outreach Department > UAEU Cultural and Social Club
UAEU Cultural and Social Club was established with the aim of connecting with faculty members, staff and their families at the university and finding a place that embraces the university community to enhance social and cultural interaction through various activities and active participation to enrich their university life. Al Multaqa also offers many services to the UAEU family through cultural, social, sporting and recreational activities, holding meetings, seminars, training courses, scientific programs, national and social celebrations.
