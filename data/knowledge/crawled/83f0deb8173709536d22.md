Statistical Consulting Unit

                                          Online Workshops/Training courses
                                                        Workshop #1

Title: Introduction to Applied Statistics Using Excel/SPSS

Statistical Software: Excel/SPSS

Requirements: No to basic (school-level) knowledge in statistics and Excel

Instructor: Dr. Zahirul Hoque

Target Group: People interested in basic statistical analysis using Excel or SPSSs

Date: 28 Feb., 2022 – 13 March, 2022

Period: 3 days, 4 hours/day

Platform: Zoom Meeting

Inclusive of Scientific Materials: Seminars include live lectures, hands-on exercise assignments,
and plenty of chances to ask questions. PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.

Description:

This workshop introduces participants to the descriptive as well as the common inferential techniques
used in different scientific fields. SPSS or Excel will be used to demonstrate the data analysis procedures.

Topics Covered:

Descriptive statistics, hypothesis tests and confidence interval for mean(s) and proportion(s), regression
analysis and tests for categorical data.

Workshop Fee: 1500 AED

Note: A certificate of completion of the workshop will be provided to participants.
