## Graduate Teaching Assistantship (All nationalities)
Graduate Teaching Assistants (GTA) are typically full-time graduate students who participate in teaching activities at the University in exchange for financial support.

Student Eligibility:

You need to be an active graduate student at UAEU.

You need to have Good Academic Standing (GPA 3.0/4.0).

Have no other financial sponsorship.

Benefits:

Monthly stipend.

Duration:

For a period of one year or less and may be renewed.

Duties and Expectations:

Each Assistant is expected to work on the designated assignments.

The supervisor is expected to provide the Graduate Teaching Assistant with oral or written assessment and feedback regarding his/her performance periodically.

How to Apply:

Graduate students interested in applying for Graduate Teaching Assistantships are advised to check with their respective Departments and Colleges.

Deadline:

Available any time and hence may be announced or made known to students in different media outlets at various times.
