## Adriaan De Man > Personal profile > Professional Information > Adriaan De Man
Doctorate Degree, Doctor in Archaeology, History & Archaeology, Universidade do Porto, 2009

Currently serve as Professor and Chair of the Department of Tourism and Heritage, having previously served as Chair of the Department of History and Archaeology. Have taught at several universities and gained extensive experience in higher education accreditation, quality assurance, and research funding through collaboration with 15 agencies across Europe, North America, and the Middle East. Also served as an auditor in Regional Security and Defence Dynamics at the Portuguese National Defence Institute, held several university administrative positions, and provided consultancy services to the private sector, particularly in heritage management and cultural tourism.
