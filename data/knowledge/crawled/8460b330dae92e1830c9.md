## USAGE OF UAEU DIGITAL SERVICES
Open Date : 05/01/2026 | Close Date: 06/30/2026

Objective

Engage stakeholders in the co-creation and improvement of UAEU services and policies through structured digital consultations in 2026, in alignment with TDRA Digital Participation requirements.

Expected decisions

Prioritize the development of digital services based on user needs and preferences identified through the survey results.

Continue engaging users through periodic surveys and feedback mechanisms to support the continuous improvement of digital services.

Recommendations

Focus on service readiness and technical support during the morning, which represents the peak usage period.

Continue developing the most frequently used services, including Registration, E-Student Services, Degree Works, and the UAEU Application and Portal.

Enhance awareness of digital services and the e-Library to increase user engagement and utilization.
