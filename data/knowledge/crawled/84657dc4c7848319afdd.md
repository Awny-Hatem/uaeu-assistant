10. Student Fees and Accounts

Overview
Covers policies and procedures relating to the management of tuition and fees.

Scope
Applies to all students and all academic and related administrative units of the University.

Objective
Ensures that the tuition and fee assessment and student accounts functions of the University
are conducted in a manner that is consistent with academic principles, standards, and
expectations of the University appropriate to its educational mission and academic programs,
and operates with necessar y integrity, fairness, consistency, and effectiveness.

Policy
1.                                                      Within the context and limits of applicable UAE federal laws, the University is authorized
      to assess and collect tuition and fees for various academic and related services               as
      approved by the Signatory Authority           . Tuition is charged for all instruction to graduate
      students.  Tuition and fee schedules are published on the UAEU website.
2.                                        Tuition charges are due and payable in full at the specified deadlines of each academic
      semester. The final responsibility for payment of tuition and fees charges    rests with the
      individual student, and the student will be held responsible individually for all properly
      documented financial liabilities to the University.
