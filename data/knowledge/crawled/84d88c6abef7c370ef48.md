## College of Education > Overview
Established in 1977, the College of Education (CEDU) at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) is one of the first four colleges in the University. Over the past four decades, CEDU has maintained its status as a pioneer in the country’s higher education system, by preparing educational leaders and teachers who shape the educational landscape of the UAE. CEDU faculty engage in research, provide consultancy, and hold a variety of forums, seminars, and professional training programs to meet the nation's educational needs.

## College of Education > Dean Message
Welcome to the College of Education at United Arab Emirates University (UAEU). By joining our community, you become part of one of the first four colleges established in 1977 by our founding father, H.H. Sheikh Zayed Bin Sultan Al Nahyan (may God rest his soul). Since its inception, the College of Education has been a beacon for preparing high-caliber educators. We take immense pride in our graduates, including teachers, educational leaders, and curriculum developers, who have contributed to advancing education throughout the United Arab Emirates. We honor our rich legacy, but we are not defined by the past alone. Our current achievements and key performance metrics demonstrate our ongoing progress. We are steadfast in our mission: to provide a high-quality program for preparing teachers and education leaders, to produce cutting-edge research that significantly contributes to existing knowledge on education, and to collaborate closely with local and international educational entities to put theory into practice. This helps to promote a knowledge economy and human capital, which is a primary priority for the United Arab Emirates.

Accreditation is quality assurance through external peer review. A university, college, or specialized program is accredited when it meets standards set by an accrediting organization that represents the academic community, professionals, and other stakeholders. Accordingly, institutions and programs must undergo regular reviews to maintain their accreditation.
