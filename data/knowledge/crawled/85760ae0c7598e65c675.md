## Department of Foundations of Education > Overview
The Department of Foundations of Education (FOED) is dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of teaching, research, and service. FOED faculty members are dedicated to providing high-quality instruction, conducting significant research investigations, and participating in local and national community service.

Faculty members strive to prepare their students to be educational leaders and future successful instructors. In addition to undergraduate teacher training programs, the FOED provides a Master of Educational Leadership and a PhD in Leadership and Policy Studies in Education. These are essential programs for training future scholars and practitioners.

## Department of Foundations of Education > Vision & Mission
Vision

The Foundations of Education Department aspires to become a pioneer in preparing distinguished leaders in education and educational research.

Mission

The Foundations of Education Department is committed to providing the best educational and research services through its Masters and Doctorate programs in light of community needs and international standards.
