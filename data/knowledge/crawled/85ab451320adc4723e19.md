## Providing electronic devices to financial ailing students
Through this service, UAEU provides laptops and Tablets devices to support deserving students from low-income families who are facing financial difficulties that might inhibit their ability to continue with academic studies. Those students have the right to apply for financial aid through E-services. Each case will be reviewed and evaluated based on conditions applied.

Start Service

## Providing electronic devices to financial ailing students > Procedure
Login to UAEU portal or mobile App

Choose Apply for iPad/Laptop Financial Aid request

Fill in the form

Upload the required documents

The responsible team will evaluate the request

Evaluation result will be received by email.

## Providing electronic devices to financial ailing students > Conditions & Requirements
The student should be registered on the electronic Financial Aid System

Uploading all the required documents

The student should be on regular study

Monthly income per person must not exceed 3000 AED for nationals and 1500 AED for non-nationals.

## Providing electronic devices to financial ailing students > Can I get new laptop or iPad in case I have lost or damaged my previous one?
No you will be given the item once in your university.

## Providing electronic devices to financial ailing students > Do I have to give the laptop or the iPad after graduation?
No there is no need of giving it back; it is for the student once you have taken it.

## Providing electronic devices to financial ailing students > In case of damage of the laptop or the iPad, will I be provided with a new item until the previous one is fixed?
No these is no such service.

## Providing electronic devices to financial ailing students > Is the iPad or computer repaired in case of damage?
No the Office of Financial Aid does not spend any money to repair.
