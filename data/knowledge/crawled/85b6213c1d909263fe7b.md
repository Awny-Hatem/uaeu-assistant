## Center for Public Policy and Leadership > Mission & Objectives > Mission
To contribute to the formulation of national policies informed by scientific research with the aim of fostering the UAE human and institutional capacities.

## Center for Public Policy and Leadership > Mission & Objectives > Objectives
To provide consultation services on public policy issues to decision makers in the UAE.

To contribute to enhancing national leadership capacities by offering effective professional programs.

To strategize qualitative public policies that reflect the visionary leadership of the UAE’s policy makers.
