## Khalifa Center for Genetic Engineering & Biotechnology > Work Experience at the KCGEB
KCGEB presents exciting short-term research hands-on training for undergraduate and postgraduate students of the UAEU, to build on essential skills and expertise to become a professional researcher. We offer structured programs to learn and work closely with our scientists. This renders a unique insight to our research work and encourages next generation scientists to build successful research careers.
