                                                                                            Senior Officers


I. Policy

                   The senior officers will be responsible for the operation of the United Arab
                   Emirates University.

                   Vice Chancellor

                   The Vice Chancellor is the chief executive officer of the University and is
                   appointed by a Federal decree upon recommendation by the Chancellor.  The
                   Vice Chancellor must hold a PhD and be  a known scholar with at least five
                   years of experience as a university professor.

                   The Vice Chancellor shall be responsible for the University’s academic,
                   administrative and community affairs; supervise the execution of its By-law,
                   regulations and policies; and represent it before the courts and other bodies.

                   The Vice Chancellor shall be responsible for the following:

                              •                                     Ensure the implementation of the University Council’s resolutions and
                                        policies.

                             •                                     Monitor the overall performance of the University.

                 Provost

                 The Provost is the chief operating officer of the University and is appointed by
                 a Federal decree upon the recommendation of the Chancellor.

                 The Provost shall be responsible for the following and any other duties
                 delegated or assigned by the Vice Chancellor:

                           •                                     Monitor the overall operations of the University.

                           •                     Ensure that University services are transparent and seamless to the
                                    faculty, staff and students.

                           •                                     Prepare the annual University's operating plan.

                           •                                     Assist the Vice Chancellor in preparing the University's budget.

                           •                                     Any other duties assigned by the Vice Chancellor.

                 The Provost, or whoever is acting, shall replace the Vice Chancellor during
                 his absence.

Secretary General

                    The University shall have a Secretary General at the grade of Undersecretary,
                    who is appointed by a Federal decree upon proposal by the Vice Chancellor
                    and the Chancellor.

                    The Secretary General shall head the University departments providing
                    financial and administrative services.  He shall supervise and liaise between
                    them and other departments of the University.


II. Application

                    This policy shall apply to all senior officers of the United Arab Emirates
                    University.
