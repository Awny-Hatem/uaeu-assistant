## Universities Challenges in Food Security and Sustainability > Overview
Welcome to The 2nd Universities Challenge in Food Security and Sustainability , organized by the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) as part of Global Food Week 2025, taking place from 21–23 October at ADNEC Abu Dhabi. This year, the challenge is inspired by the national initiative Plant Emirates, reflecting UAEU’s strong commitment to advancing food security, sustainability, and innovative agricultural solutions.
