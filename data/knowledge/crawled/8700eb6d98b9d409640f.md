## Overview
The Program Review and Accreditation Unit (PRAU) has four main responsibilities:

Oversees the implementation of the university policy on program review.

Supports all academic programs throughout the national accreditation by the Commission of Academic Accreditation (CAA).

Coordinates the CAA and WASC institutional accreditation processes.

Ensures that the eligible academic programs receive external accreditation according to the approved timeline.
