Enrollment Deanship
                                               Student Accounts Office
                Guide for Paying Fees for all other Master Program for spring 2020/2021.



The Student Accounts Office (SAO), is responsible for the billing of all student fees and charges, and is
located on the First floor of Crescent Building, Wing D4, Room 1050, Al Ain. Our working hours for
student enquiries are 7:30 am – 3pm, Sunday through Thursday.

SAO Contact information:
              o    Phone:  03 7134265,  037134287
              o    Email address:  Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae

 Payment method:
You can pay for the tuition via the e-payment (online Payment), please log on to this page to pay:
https://ssb.uaeu.ac.ae/prod/bwykstpy.p_display_opt  Or through UAEU Application.





                       Deadline for Settlement of Tuition & Fees for Fall 2020/2021
   1st Payment Deadline (50% of total tuition fees )                              23 – 27 Aug 2020
   2nd Payment Deadline (Remaining 50% of total tuition fees)                     30 Aug – 17 Sep 2020


** This Deadline dates is only for students who not want to apply for the Installment Plan.
*** Non-Payment of total tuition fees on or before 17 September 2020 will result in hold on
registration and records, and you will not receive course grades until you pay your fees, unfortunately
it may also mean forfeiture of grades and credits earned during the semester.




Important Note


To arrange a tuition payment installment plan, you have to apply on UAEU website through 30 Aug
2020 to 17 September 2020, and you have to apply the installment plan for each semester. Apply on
Uaeu (website or application) – E service – tuition installment.

(SPONSORED STUDENTS) Students whose tuition charges are paid or guaranteed by a sponsor
must submit a valid official letter of financial agreement from the sponsor no later than 17 September
2020.
