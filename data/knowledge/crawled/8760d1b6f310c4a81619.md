                                                                   Grading


I.                                                                                              Policy

             The United Arab Emirates University shall establish standards for course
             grades.

             Course Grading System

             At the end of a term or semester, courses shall be graded using letter grades.
             Each letter grade has corresponding quality points assigned to obtain a
             numerical measure of the student’s work and calculate his/her grade point
             average (GPA).  The following course grading system is applied throughout
             the University (with the exception of the College of Medicine and Health
             Sciences):


                      Percentage Scores                                   Letter Grade                                         Points

                             90-100 A 4.0
                             85-<90 B+ 3.5
                             80-<85 B 3.0
                             75-<80 C+ 2.5
                             70-<75 C 2.0
                             65-<70 D+ 1.5
                             60-<65 D 1.0
                             <60 F 0.0

Fail (F)                                                                                                                                                                                                                         F < 1.75 (Fail)                                                                                                                                    F < 2.00 (Fail)                                                                                                                                                                                                          -                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 -

                         A student can not graduate with a grade point average of less than 1.75 points
                         if admitted prior to and including 2002, and can not graduate with a grade
                         point average of less than 2.00 if admitted in 2003 and after.

                         Master degree students can not graduate with a grade point average of less
                         than 3.00 points.

                         Incomplete Grades

                         A student who misses the final examination of a course is given a failing
                         grade in that course.

                         If the student has a legitimate excuse, which has been accepted by the dean,
                         and if he/she has satisfactory scores in the semester activities and the mid-
                         semester examination (not less than 60%), he/she receives a grade of
                         incomplete.

                         The following conditions apply to a grade of incomplete:

                                     •                            The credit hours for the incomplete grade(s) do not count towards the
                                                  student’s study load in the following semester.
                                     •                          A student must take the final examination to eliminate an incomplete
                                                  grade at any time during the following semester; otherwise, he/she will
                                                  be given a failing grade.
                                     •                         When the student passes the incomplete examination, his/her score is
                                                  counted in the incomplete course.
                                     •                    The result of the course(s) is referred to the semester in which the
                                                  student registered for the course(s) and was granted the incomplete
                                                  grade.
