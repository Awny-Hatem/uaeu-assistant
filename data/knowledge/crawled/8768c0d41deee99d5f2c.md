## Reference and Research Support
The Library offers search, research and document management support to individuals, groups or classes. We can provide search instruction for specific databases like PubMed and SCOPUS and for point-of-care tools like Clinical Key and Up-to-Date. Help and guidance with research related software are also offered as well as systematic review service.
