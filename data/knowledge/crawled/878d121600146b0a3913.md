2023 CAEP Annual Report – Accountability Measures

Measure 4: Ability of completers to be hired in positions for which they have been prepared

      This annual report used data gathered from the 2021-2022 College Alumni Survey. The College Alumni
Survey is administered by the Alumni Affairs Unit under the Office Assistant Dean of Student Affairs and
analyzed by the Quality Assurance Laboratory. The survey requests information about the completers’
current employment status and information (position, company, manager’s information, and reasons for
unemployment).
      The 2021-2022 College Alumni Survey targeted only 451 graduates from Fall 2016 to Fall 2021 from
which 263 are graduates of Early Childhood Education (ECE), 169 are graduates of Special Education (SPED),
and 19 are graduates of Health and Physical Education (HPE) (first graduate cohort was Fall 2020). The
response rate in the survey was 33% or 148 out of 451.
    It is important to note that respondents of the 2021-2022 College Alumni Survey, who graduated from
the program at least six months previously and who are being employed in a position for which they were
prepared, were the only ones considered in the completer performance and other completer-related
assessments. However, for the employment status report, the scope of data is until the Fall 2021 cohort.

    4.1 Employment Information

              Data from the 2022 College Alumni Survey showed a 57% overall employment rate from the 148
         respondents –82 ECE, 55 SPED, and 11 HPE. Out of the 57% employed, 91.8% are employed as
         teachers in public schools, 4.7% are employed as teachers in private schools, and 3.5% are employed
         in other related fields.

4.2 EPP Programs Supporting Completer Employment

                    The EPP maintains its partnership and collaboration with the MOE, ESE, schools, and other
          stakeholders to ensure that its completers are provided with additional qualifications and
          opportunities for employment.

                    Below are some of the support programs provided to the completers (2021-2022):

               1.   The EPP has forged an agreement with the Emirates Schools Establishments to develop a
                    joint road map to prepare, qualify, and employ highly qualified teachers in public education
                    schools in UAE. ESE has developed a flexible and fast mechanism to enable the direct
                    employment of the EPP’s graduates. It will also ensure training and follow-up after
                    employment and share the results of the completers’ performance with the EPP to improve
                    and develop its programs.

               2.   The Ministry of Education initially coordinated with the EPP in September 2020 to offer
                    upskilling programs for graduates. The upskilling program intended to qualify graduates for a
                    teaching position in Early Childhood Education and be granted an opportunity to be
                    interviewed for a teaching role with the Ministry of Education. In August 2022, the ‘Upskilling
                    Program for Early Childhood Teacher Employability’ was reactivated by the Abu Dhabi Early
                    Childhood Authority (ECA) and is in the process of developing educational and practical
                    programs that achieve the strategic objectives of early childhood. It has also conducted
                    several workshops for the upskilling of fresh and old graduates of ECE.
