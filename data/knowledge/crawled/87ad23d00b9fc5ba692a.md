## Director’s Message
The commitment to serving children with exceptionalities, including children with determination and children with gifts and talents, represents the highest ideals of human nature, demonstrating both solidarity and a profound sense of community responsibility. The Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children, under the esteemed guidance of Her Highness Shaikha Mouza Bint Saif Al Nahyan, Chair of the Board of Trustees, is dedicated to providing comprehensive services that adhere to the most rigorous scientific and professional criteria.

The Center aims to empower children with exceptionalities, thereby enhancing their opportunities to realize their full potential and actively participate in a sustainable and inclusive society. The Center relies on specialized scientific programs and advanced research practices, utilizing their results in areas such as assessment, diagnosis, and intervention, ensuring the best solutions are provided to meet the needs of this precious group.

At the Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children, we not only serve children but also contribute to building a more inclusive and compassionate society, with a vision for the future that aims to enhance the quality of life for our children with exceptionalities.

I am pleased to welcome you to the Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children’s website, which serves as an effective communication platform to introduce our services as well as rehabilitation and educational programs specifically designed to enhance the various skills of children with exceptionalities.

The Center is dedicated to being a beacon of scientific research in the fields of Special and Gifted Education through its research unit, aiming to develop best practices and programs. It provides specialized consultations for the families of children with exceptionalities and professionals in the field. The Center also seeks to train school professionals and equip them with the necessary knowledge and skills to meet job market demands while fostering effective partnerships with community institutions concerned with the issues affecting children with exceptionalities.

At the Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children, we value every individual in society. We strive to empower children with exceptionalities to achieve their ambitions and actively contribute to building a bright and sustainable future. We appreciate your trust and interest, and we look forward to strengthening our cooperation and partnerships to achieve our shared vision of a more inclusive and exceptional society.
