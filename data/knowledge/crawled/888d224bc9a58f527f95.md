## Counseling and Wellbeing Service > Overview
The Counseling and Wellbeing Service provides a range of specialized counseling services that enable students to achieve greater psychological, social and academic balance. This service helps students with the difficulties and challenges they face in all aspects of their lives. The Counseling and Wellbeing Service aims to help students understand themselves and identify their ability to develop skills and overcome weaknesses. It also aims to help them build healthy relationships with their family members, friends and colleagues. Also, the service aims to develop independence and self-reliance.

Counseling services are provided by specialists in the field of psychology and counseling, who are keen to provide an atmosphere full of respect, acceptance and complete confidentiality for all issues that are dealt with.

The principle of focusing on the student is the basis of the service's efforts in providing services with the aim of student's growth, focusing on the students interests and contributions, and training them on the skills necessary to achieve their goals, whether on the psychological, social or academic level.
