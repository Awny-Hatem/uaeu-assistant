## Fire Response System
If you discover a fire and the fire alarm does not activate :

Activate the nearest manual call point.

Leave the building by the nearest exit.

Do not use the lift.

DO NOT RE-ENTER the building unless told that it is safe to do so.

If you hear the fire alarm :

Leave the building by the nearest exit.

Do not use the lift.

DO NOT RE-ENTER the building unless told that it is safe to do so.
