## Sandooq Al Watan Signing Ceremony
Date: November 26 th , 2024

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM

Title: UAEU Innovation Challenge Demo Day

Description: Sandooq Al Watan, a non-profit philanthropic organization that supports economic and social development initiatives, invited ECER to partner with them in advancing the UAE entrepreneurship research and policy agenda. In addition to several entrepreneurial initiatives, the creation of their National Incubator Network aims to aggregate information related to entrepreneurship and make it more accessible to all ecosystem players in the UAE. We look forward to a fruitful partnership that produces research insights for contextually relevant theory-building and practical purposes.
