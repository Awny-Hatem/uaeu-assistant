## E-Books
The National Medical Library offer students, faculty and staff a large collection of medical books in electronic format. The books are accessible on and off campus and can be located with the help the library discovery system: “ Summon ”, or directly via any of the below main e-book platforms including helpful navigation and study tools.

Platform | Description
AccessMedicine | AccessMedicine is an e-book collection that includes the latest editions of major medical titles such as Harrison’s Principles of Internal Medicine and CURRENT Medical Diagnosis and Treatment. |
ClinicalKey | The ClinicalKey platform includes a significant number of medical e-books. You can read online, either from your computer or on a mobile device through the ClinicalKey app. |
JaypeeDigital | JaypeeDigital is a comprehensive e-book platform for medical and health science students and professionals. In addition to e-books, JaypeeDigital also has videos of live surgery and journals in the fields of health sciences, medicine and pharmacology, etc. |
Oxford Public Health and Epidemiology | Oxford Public Health and Epidemiology provides access to over a hundred key public health and epidemiology titles. The platform is regularly updated with the latest books published by the Oxford University Press. |
ProQuest Ebook Central | ProQuest Ebook Central provides access to thousands of e-books in different areas including a large collection of titles from the fields of medicine and the health sciences. |
Stat!Ref | Stat!Ref gives access to a collection of electronic books and interactive learning tools in the fields of medicine and the health sciences. |

Ask at the library or contact us via e-mail: nml.fmhs@uaeu.ac.ae or phone: 03 713 7201 if you need help with navigating our e-collection.
