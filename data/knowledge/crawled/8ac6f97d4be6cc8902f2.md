## Department of Radiology > Overview
The Department of Radiology is a central participant in medical education leading to the MD and Bachelors of Medical Science degrees. The Department teaches all aspects of diagnostic radiology; to undergraduates with the emphasis on integration with other specialties. Faculty members of the Department focus on research in fields, relevant to the country's need, such as imaging in cancer, radio contrast media (RCM) research, assessment of genetic diseases and congenital malformations, neuro-imaging, and basic radiological research – including animal studies in collaboration with CMHS basic sciences departments and other UAE University colleges.
