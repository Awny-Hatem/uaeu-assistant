## Textbook Warehouse Services
Staff in the textbook warehouse follow-up on the textbook ordering requests with the vendors and receive and examine the purchased textbooks upon delivery before sending them to the various distribution centers. Faculty must fill in the textbook ordering form and send a copy to textbooks@uaeu.ac.ae and to the vendor.

The vendor pricing agreements for purchasing and supplying textbooks can be found here .

For further suggestions or inquiries, please contact us on textbooks@uaeu.ac.ae .
