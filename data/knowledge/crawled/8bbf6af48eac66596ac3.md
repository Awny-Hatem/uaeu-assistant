6. Graduate Student Tuition,              Fees, and Accounts

Overview
Covers policies and procedures relating to the management of graduate student tuition and
fees including assessment of tuition and fees, and tuition and fee payment.

Scope
Applies to all students and all academic and related administrative units of the University.

Objective
Ensures that the graduate student tuition,                                             fee assessment,             and student accounts functions of
the University are conducted in a manner that is consistent with academic principles,
standards, and expectations of the University, as appropriate to its educational mission and
academic programs, and with the necessary fairness, consistency, and effectiveness.

Policy
1.                                                        Within the context and limits of applicable laws, the  Universit y is authorized to assess and
     collect tuition and fees for various academic and related services.
2.                                             Tuition is charged for all instruction to graduate students as specified in the Schedule of
     Tuition and Fees (Schedule).
3.                                                           The responsibility for payment of tuition and fees rests with the individual student.
