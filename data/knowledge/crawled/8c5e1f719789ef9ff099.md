         Overview
Companies are struggling to implement strategy, despite elaborate strategic plans. They lack an
implementation    paradigm    and    mindset.    Project    management    provides    such    a    mechanism    to
proceed      from      strategic      management      and      planning      to      executing      those      strategies      through
projects.   This   course   is   aimed   at   empowering   business   managers   to   understand   the   value   of
project management and deliver projects successfully, and to cultivate the understanding of the
influence project management has on business strategy and long term sustained success.
         Course Description and Main Objective
To   have   successful   project   execution,   it   is   important   to   understand   the   functional   aspects   of
project      management,      i.e.,      methodologies,      tools      and      techniques.      Project      professionals      and
organizational managers must understand the value, role and function of project management in
delivering  successful  projects  and  its  role  in  the  successful  roll-out  of  business  strategy.  The
aim   of   this   course   is   to   empower   managers   to   use   the   basic   project   management   tools   and
techniques to deliver projects successfully. In addition, to build on this knowledge by supplying
further best practices to excel within the project environment                       and to    use   project management
to roll out business strategy successfully.
         Learning Outcomes
On       the       completion       of       this       course,       delegates       will       be       able       to       apply       the       generic       project
management functions, such as scoping, scheduling, costing as well                             as controlling, monitoring
and      closing      projects;      Apply      generic      project      management      processes;      Use      specific      project
management    supporting    functions;    Understand    the    strategic                role     of    project    management
 within  the organization; and Manage the key success factors of project management within the
business
        Content
This course will address 6 topics.

Topic 1: Introduction to Projects:
The  Project  and  its  role  in  the  Organization,  Defining  Project  Management;  Reasons  for  project
failure; The Characteristics of Projects; The Strategic Role of Projects within Organizations

Topic 2: The Project Manager:
The   Characteristics   of   a   good   Project   Manager;   The   types   of   Project   Managers;   The   Project
Manager and the Project Leader; Leadership styles and the Project Manager

Topic 3: Project Management and the Value of Project Management to the Organization:
Foundations  of  Project  Management;  Planning;  The  interdependence  of  Performance,  Time  and
