                           Mobile Phones, Cameras and Laptops with Cameras


I.                                                                                               Policy

                   In furtherance of its educational vision and mission, the United Arab Emirates
                   University shall establish regulations governing aspects of student life on
                   campus.

                   Students are prohibited from using mobile phones with cameras, cameras or
                   laptops with cameras on the girls’ campuses and in the girls’ hostels.
                   Procedures will be developed to govern the use of acceptable mobile phones
                   and devices.


II.                                                                             Application

                   This policy shall apply to all students at the United Arab Emirates University.
