## Computerized Accounting > Computerized Accounting
Certificate Program

This program familiarizes students with a wide variety of computerized business applications and achieves a high level of expertise in selected applications. These skills will enable graduates to qualify for entry-level positions such as Computerized Accounting Clerk, Bookkeeper, Accounting Assistant, Accounts Receivable/Payable, and Payroll Processer. This program provides an excellent opportunity for students currently working in the business field to upgrade their skills and knowledge in the field.

Program Outcomes:

Apply basic accounting concepts applicable to a business environment.

Perform the complete accounting cycle ot include journalizing, posting adjustments, preparing financial statements, and closing accounts.

Apply applicable federal laws for payroll and income taxes.

Exhibit intermediate proficiency with Microsoft Office: Word, Excel, PowerPoint; QuickBooks and the internet.

Courses In This Program Include:

Introduction to Computers and Application

Intermediate Computer

Advanced Computer

Critical Thinking

Computerized Bookkeeping

Computerized Accounting

Accounting Software

Employment Preparation for Business Environment
