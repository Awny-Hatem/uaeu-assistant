            UAE INNOVATES - UAEU

## PDF page 2
Under the slogan of “The UAE Innovates 2021” coinciding with the fiftieth year of the establishment of the United
Arab Emirates, the activities of the sixth cycle of the UAE Innovates 2021 Program were launched, in implementation
of the directives of H. H. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, the UAE Vice President and Prime Minister
and Ruler of Dubai. In conjunction with the vision making the UAE the most innovative and future-ready country
in the world.  The United Arab Emirates University has presented a wide range of events, lectures, conferences, and
educational workshops in the UAE Innovation Month.   It is an important national event celebrated by the United Arab
Emirates with the aim of disseminating knowledge and promoting a culture of innovation to achieve sustainable
development and wellbeing.

The United Arab Emirates University - the University of the Future - is the “National Flagship University” which
was established by the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan (may God rest his soul), which is keen to create a
generation of innovators contribute to human growth and prosperity and support the country’s position in creativity
and excellence. It has launched more than a hundred activities carried out by the various scientific colleges, research
centers and departments at the university and its partners during February.  The UAE Innovation Month activities are
various, which deal with space, energy, health, nutrition, agriculture, engineering, environment, information security,
robots, unmanned aircraft, artificial intelligence, and other fields, with the aim of enhancing the government’s
directions, in line with its strategic plan, to transform into a knowledge-based economy for vital and important
sectors.
