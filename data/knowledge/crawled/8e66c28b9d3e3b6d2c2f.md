## The Role of Media in Achieving Sustainability - 16/06/2024
As part of UAEU's roadmap towards COP28 and beyond, the United Arab Emirates University has initiated a Sustainability Lecture Series/Training Program. This program serves as a catalyst for driving positive change, equipping students, staff, and the wider community to confront the intricate sustainability challenges of the 21st century and emerge as well-informed, engaged, and effective leaders in their respective fields.

Our lecture series provides a platform for dynamic discussions, enlightening presentations, and collaborative exploration of diverse topics including sustainability, climate science, environmental policy, renewable energy, and more. By convening experts, scholars, students, and community members, our aim is to foster a vibrant intellectual atmosphere where ideas thrive, knowledge flows, and impactful initiatives take root.

Through this series, we aspire to inspire, educate, and empower individuals to become catalysts for positive change in their communities and beyond. Join us as we embark on a journey towards a more sustainable and resilient future for all.
