7. Password

Overview
Provides standards for defining passwords for UAEU             applications.

Scope
Applies to all personnel who have,    or are responsible for,            an account or                   any form of access
that supports or requires a password. This includes any system that resides at UAEU,                                                                          has
access to UAEU network, or stores any non-                                                                                   public UAEU information.

Objective
Effective password management will protect UAEU                                                       data and reduce the risk of unauthorized
applications access. The policy is to establish a secure i                                  nformation      technology environment
by enforcing the use of strong passwords.

Policy
1.                              Passwords are an important aspect of computer security. These are the front line of
        protection for user accounts. A poorly chosen password may result in the compromise of
        UAEU’s entire network.
2.                        All UAEU                  facult y                  members, staff                  members, students,                              alumni,             and contractors are
        responsible for taking the appropriate steps to select and secure their passwords.
3.                                                           All system passwords must be kept and maintained in a predefined secure location.
4.                                                           All passwords are to be treated as sensitiveand                               confidential UAE            U information.
5.                                                           The password policy is enforced by default.
