## Incoming Students
Students enrolled in undergraduate education at universities with exchange agreements with UAEU are eligible to study at UAEU for up to two semesters as specified in the exchange agreement and agreed upon by both institutions.

## Nomination and Application
Incoming students are nominated by their universities to study at UAEU. The list of nominees is submitted by the partner university to the Student Exchange Unit office. The application documents are submitted to UAEU through the office in charge of student exchange at the partner university.

## Academic Requirements
Language proficiency requirements: English language proficiency as demonstrated by a TOEFL score of 525 PBT, 70 iBT; or an IELTS score of 5.5. GPA admission requirement: Minimum of 3.0 on a 4-point scale or through approval of the concerned college at UAEU.

## Application Documents
Applications to study at UAEU should be submitted through the appropriate office of the partner institution. The application must include the following items:

Application form

Essay outlining the candidate's interest in studying at UAEU

One copy of the student's official transcript

Proof of English language proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS,…)

One passport copy (front page with picture and biographical information)

Four passport-size photographs

One copy of the applicant s high school certificate

Curriculum Vitae of the applicant

Introductory statement form from United Arab Emirates University

## Application Deadlines
Fall Semester: March 31

Spring Semester: October 30

## Study at UAEU
Incoming exchange students are affiliated to the UAEU college which corresponds to their degree major specialization. They may enroll in all undergraduate courses offered in the Fall and Spring Semesters, except those offered by the College of Medicine and Health Sciences, on condition that they meet the course prerequisites. Student study loads at UAEU vary from 12 to 18 credit hours. The language of instruction is English. A limited number of courses are taught in Arabic.

## Tuition
The tuition fee structure is determined in accordance with the provisions of the agreement concluded with the partner university.

## Accommodation
Residence halls for exchange students are subject to availability. Meal plans can be purchased directly at each residence hall. The cost of a shared room in a residence hall: 5,600 AED per student per semester.

The approximate cost of food in student residence halls is 2,000 AED per month for three meals per day. However, students are charged per meal rather than monthly.

## Health Insurance
Health insurance is mandatory in the UAE. The Exchange Unit will assist students with the application process. Read More

## Other Expenses
Student visa application fee: 356.20 AED

Changing visit visa to student visa: 660 AED

Medical screening test: 250 AED

Emirates ID and Residency: 761 AED.

All expenses are subject to changes by federal authorities.
