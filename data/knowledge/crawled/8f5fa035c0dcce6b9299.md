## College of Information Technology > Industry
The CIT provides opportunities for cooperation with industry partners and government agencies through joint research and/ or consultancy services. Our research partnerships and consultancies include: the National Science Foundation, IBM, SAP, EMC, Cisco, DASSAULT, FEWA, ADWEA, DEWA, Thuraya, Du, Etisalat, the UAE Air Force, ADEC, Abu Dhabi Police, Dubai eGov, Intel, Advanced Technology Investment Company and the Semiconductor Research Corporation.
