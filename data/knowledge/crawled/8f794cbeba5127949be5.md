# Transportation Services

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## Transportation Services
UAEU provides safe and convenient transportation services for students both on and off campus through a modern fleet operated by qualified drivers.

## Transportation Services > Services include:
Daily transportation between student housing and colleges.

Transportation within the university campus.

Weekly trips for students living in university housing to various Emirates and residential areas every Friday, with return trips to the University on Sunday.

Transportation services for students of the College of Medicine and Health Sciences.

Transportation for field training and educational trips within and outside Al Ain.
