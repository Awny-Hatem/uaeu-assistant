## Undergraduate Programs > Minor in Ecological and Environmental Biology
The minor serves to provide students with latest knowledge in environmental and ecological sciences and how to deal with current environmental problems.

## Undergraduate Programs > Minor in Ecological and Environmental Biology > Admission Requirements
Min grade requirement: None

Pre-requisite: None

Targeted students: All students except those with a major in Biology.

## Undergraduate Programs > Minor in Ecological and Environmental Biology > Program Objectives
Develop appreciation to the vast and vital areas in ecology.

Familiarize students with the interaction of physical and biological components of the environment and how each component influences the other.

Explore the diversity of organisms globally and locally, and the adaptations of selected groups.

## Undergraduate Programs > Minor in Ecological and Environmental Biology > Program Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

Demonstrate a thorough understanding of the importance of biodiversity for ecosystem functioning.

Describe and assess the human impact on the environment.

Describe ecological principles as a tool to solve environmental problems.
