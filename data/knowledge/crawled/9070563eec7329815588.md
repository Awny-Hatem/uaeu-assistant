## PDF page 3
Checklist for Complete Application:
Applications to study at UAEU should be submitted through the appropriate office of the partner
institution. The application must include the following items.
          1.   Application Form
          2.   Essay outlining candidate's interest in studying at UAEU
          3.   One copy of the student's official transcript
          4.   Proof of English language proficiency (such as TOEFL, IELTS,…)
          5.   One copy of passport (front page with picture and biographical information)
          6.   Four passport-size photographs
          7.   One copy of original High School Certificate.
