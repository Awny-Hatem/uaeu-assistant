## Multaqa > Al Multaqa Director Message
We recognize the importance of providing services for university staff and their families. We try to reflect this in our performance. The University Social Club (Al Multaqa) contributes to strengthening relationships between faculty and staff of the university and their families. It also aids the development of relationships with the wider community outside the university as we enjoy our social, cultural, artistic and sporting activities together.

Therefore, we try to create effective communication within the university through our teamwork. We are always open to dialogue and suggestions.

Your feedback, contributions and suggestions as to how we can improve our services is always welcome.
