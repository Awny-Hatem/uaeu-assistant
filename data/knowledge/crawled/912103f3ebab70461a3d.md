## Foreign Languages Program Unit (FLP)
The Foreign Languages Program Unit at the CEC has been providing language services to both corporate clients & private/general individuals, helping to bridge the gap posed by language & culture to achieve their true potential.

Fields of services provided by the unit :

Exams Preparation Courses (IELTS, TOEFL, CEPA)

General English Courses (CEFR: A1 – B2)

Intensive English Courses for Specific Purposes (CEFR: B2-C1): Business, Educational, Vocational and others.

Advanced English Courses (CEFR: B1-B2): Email Etiquette, Public Speaking, Presentation Skills, etc.

Special English Courses for Women Only

Translation Courses (Administrative and Legal)

private One-On-One Tutorials (IELTS, TOEFL, General English, Languages)

Other Language Courses: Japanese, Urdu, Korean, Spanish and others.

Other Customized Courses to Fit the Client’s Needs
