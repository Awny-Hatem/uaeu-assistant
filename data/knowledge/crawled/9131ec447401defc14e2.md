## ACCT101 Principles of Financial Accounting-CBMC
This course is designed to strengthen foundational knowledge and skills in financial accounting concepts, principles, and practices. The course introduces fundamental financial accounting concepts within an IFRS-oriented framework. It focuses on recording and analyzing business transactions, preparing financial statements, understanding the relationship among financial statement items, and applying the accounting cycle in service and merchandising organizations. The course also develops teamwork and communication skills through applied accounting activities, discussions, and presentations.
Principles of Financial Accounting-CBMC (ACCT101) > Credit Hours : 3

Principles of Financial Accounting-CBMC (ACCT101) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Explain the purpose and importance of financial accounting information for business decision-making, accountability, and professional practice.

Apply basic concepts of the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting in recording and analyzing business transactions for service and merchandising organizations.

Prepare financial statements of service and merchandising organizations in accordance with basic financial reporting concepts and principles.

Explain the interrelationship among items presented in the financial statements.

Demonstrate teamwork skills in professional and collaborative accounting activities, discussions, and presentations.
