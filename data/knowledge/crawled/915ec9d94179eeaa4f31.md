## CIVL200 Computer Aided Drafting
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_2906.shtml?id=CIVL200
This course introduces the basic principles of computer-aided drawings, including: geometric construction, dimensioning, drawing scales, layouts, and line convention. It includes two-dimensional sketching, orthographic projections, principal views, isometric, and sectional views. Students will gain an understanding of the essential techniques required to create precise and detailed technical drawings. The course also enhances students’ visualization skills for interpreting the geometrical features of three-dimensional objects, and equips them with the ability to create layouts for various civil engineering projects.

Computer Aided Drafting (CIVL200) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Describe the fundamental geometric concepts in engineering drawings.

Apply standard conventions of scales, line types, dimensioning, and lettering using CAD software.

Produce multi-view orthographic projection of 3D objects.

Create three-dimensional objects (isometric) drawings

Integrate drafting techniques in the preparation of civil engineering drawings.

Structural Analysis (CIVL310) > Course Learning Outcomes

Analyze simple, compound and complex trusses.
