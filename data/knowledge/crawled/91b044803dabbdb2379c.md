## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Student Activities
Students enrolled in horticultural and agricultural educational programs receive intensive practical training on the farm as part of their undergraduate and graduate studies.

Apart from this, the Al Foah Experimental Farm also offers some extracurricular activities that are open to all interested UAEU students. These offer additional opportunities for practical agricultural training, but also aim at allowing students to enjoy the farm environment, and to develop and implement new ideas without ‘grading pressure’.

Our student working groups meet regularly throughout the semester, while the ‘Learn & Help’ activities are offered on a daily basis.

## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Student Activities > Extra-curricular Student Working Groups
Exotic and Indoor Plant Collection This group is for female undergraduate students who are enthusiastic about raising and caring for exotic plants. |
Native Plant Garden & Landscaping This group is open to male and female students (graduate and undergraduate). |
Organic Farming This group supports the establishment of an organic alley cropping system for vegetables. Meetings are as required during the semester. The group is for female and male undergraduate students. |
World Crops Collection This group if for male undergraduate students who are interested in propagating and caring for crops from all parts of the world. |
