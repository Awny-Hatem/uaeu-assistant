## Surveys
Any University wide surveys which have to be administered to all students and/or employees should go through Strategy & Future Department. Currently, the following surveys are conducted by Strategy & Future Department. The reports are available to senior management via the UAEU Business Intelligence Platform.

Survey | Frequency
Course Evaluation | Per semester
Student Satisfaction with Programs and services | Per year (new starting Fall 2010)
Student Satisfaction with residences and Staff | Per semester
Alumni | Annually
New Hire Survey | Annually
Employee Survey (Satisfaction with Programs and Services) | Annually
Student Engagement Survey | Annually
New Program Feasibility Surveys | As and when required. These surveys target alumni, students and potential employers.
