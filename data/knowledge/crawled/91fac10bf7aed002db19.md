## Wellbeing Officers
The Wellbeing Officer project is an initiative by the Emirates Center for Happiness Research. The idea behind this project is to introduce the concept of increasing wellbeing at workplace. The wellbeing officers are a group of faculty and staff members who voluntarily decided to be part of this project and to assist in promoting wellbeing at their workplace.

## Ms. Shaikha Dhaen Al Rashdi
Ms. Shaikha Dhaen Al Rashdi is a Senior Administrative Officer at the Office of the Assistant Dean for Research and Graduate Studies at the College of Law. She is an active member of the Awnak Ya Watan Team and a volunteer with the Red Crescent for the 2026 Iftar initiatives, contributing meaningfully to community and humanitarian activities. Shaikha is passionate about collaboration and teamwork and is dedicated to fostering a culture of wellbeing, inspiring employees to achieve both balance and excellence.

## Dr. Tarek Deif
Dr. Tarek Dief is an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the College of Engineering, United Arab Emirates University (UAEU). He is actively involved in research and teaching in areas such as aerospace dynamics, autonomous systems, and engineering innovation, with a commitment to fostering wellbeing, collaboration, and engagement across the university community. Beyond his academic role, he enjoys supporting student-led projects and initiatives that promote curiosity, resilience, and a positive learning environment.

## Dr. Haifa Alqahtani
Dr. Haifa Alqahtani is an Instructor in the Department of Statistics and Data Analytics, College of Business and Economics (CBE), at United Arab Emirates University. Her well-being initiative, "No Soul Left Behind," aims to foster a community where everyone feels valued, supported, and connected—not only in their daily interactions but also at the very core of their well-being.

## Dr. Falah Al Marzooqi
Dr. Falah AlMarzooqi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Family Medicine at the College of Medicine and Health Sciences, United Arab Emirates University (UAEU), and a practicing family physician. His academic and clinical work focuses on well-being, mental health, parenting, and health promotion across the life course. He also leads the FAIR Study, a major research initiative exploring work culture and well-being within UAEU and related contexts, contributing to evidence-based strategies that promote healthier and more supportive communities.

## Dr. Adnan Aldarwich
Dr. Adnan Aldarwich is a Veterinary Laboratory Specialist in the Department of Veterinary Medicine, College of Agriculture and Veterinary Medicine (CAVM), at United Arab Emirates University. He is a member of the College's Events and Sports Activities Committee. He is also a member of the University's Animal Welfare Club, contributing to initiatives that promote animal health, welfare, and responsible animal care.

## Ms. Aisha Al Nuaimi
Ms. Aisha Al Nuaimi is a Project Coordinator at the College of Education, UAE University, and proudly serves as the College’s HR Wellbeing Coordinator. She is passionate about creating a positive and supportive work environment, regularly encouraging colleagues to engage in wellbeing initiatives and celebrating their achievements. Through her consistent follow-up and motivation, the College achieved first place among UAEU colleges in the Fitness Challenge in 2025, with two faculty members winning university-level awards. Aisha actively contributes to organizing and supporting wellbeing events and celebrations at the College, believing that appreciation, connection, and teamwork are key to a thriving university community

## Ms. Halima Almeqbaali
Ms. Halima Almeqbaali is a Laboratory Specialist and Safety Officer in the Department of Biology at the College of Science. She holds a Master’s degree in Public Health and has a background in Environmental Biology. She is actively involved in several department- and college-level committees focusing on student affairs, laboratory safety, and student engagement. Through these roles, she contributes to initiatives that promote student well-being, support academic success, and enhance campus engagement.

## Mr. Mused Al-Ward
Mr. Mused Al-Ward joined the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) in August 1998 and has served the university for 28 years. He is an experienced IT educator with over 30 years of academic and teaching experience across the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, and the United States. He currently serves as a Lecturer in the College of Information Technology (CIT) at UAEU, where he teaches computer science and software engineering courses.

## Mervat Ijha
Dr. Mervat Ijha is a dedicated instructor and certified corporate trainer with over 20 years of combined experience in higher-education teaching, curriculum design, and professional training delivery across the UAE and the wider region. Background in psychology and educational psychology, which has enabled her to deliver training programs under the umbrella of the Continued Learning Centre at UAEU, including specialized training for the Higher Diploma for Social Workers, drawing directly on her expertise as a psychologist and educational psychologist. Holds certifications in Emotional Intelligence, Relationships, and Behavioral Modification Techniques, and has been licensed by the Health Authority of Abu Dhabi (HAAD) since 2004. Skilled at adapting teaching methods to diverse learners, designing effective lesson plans, and translating psychological theory into practical, high-engagement training for governmental and non-governmental audiences. Certified Emotional Intelligence Trainer and Changing Attitudes & Behavior Trainer, with a growing public profile as a media commentator on psychology, well-being, and mental health, including guest appearances on national television.
