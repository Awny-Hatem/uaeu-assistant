## Restaurants and Retails Outlets > Overview
On the University campus, you can choose to enjoy a cup of coffee or have lunch in a diverse selection of the restaurants, cafeterias, pizzerias, cafés and other dining facilities. There is also a food court located in both the male and female campuses, which offer a wide selection of healthy and nutritious foods, including Oriental, Middle-Eastern, Asian, and European menus. Regular health inspections are carried out at all outlets to ensure that food safety standards are met at all times. Moreover, there are bookshops, accessories shops, supermarkets, retail shops and even a spa center spread across the female and male campuses.
