## Department of Pathology > Clinical and Community Service
Clinical Service:

Members of the department provide clinical service in their fields of expertise in anatomical pathology, clinical pathology and human genetics. The department has the only UAE nationals with fellowship training in renal pathology and pediatric pathology. Clinical service is provided at Tawam Hospital and Sheikh Khalifa Medical City.

Community Service:

Participation/organization of Cancer Awareness Campaigns.

General public educational workshops.

e.g. Emirates Medical Association Mini Medical School.

Organization and participation at pathology meetings, oncological and medical meetings.
