## Undergraduate Programs > Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential in Sustainable Resource Management in the Built Environment
The Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential (CBMC) in Sustainable Resource Management and AI Applications in the Built Environment is designed to develop essential competencies in environmental sustainability, resource efficiency, performance-based design, and AI-supported analytical and visualization approaches. The program equips learners with the knowledge and AI-supported analytical skills required to assess environmental impacts across the life cycle of buildings and infrastructure, and to formulate effective strategies for resource-efficient design strategies and operational practices, with particular emphasis on water management in hot-arid environments.

Learners will develop a solid foundation in life cycle assessment (LCA), environmental performance evaluation, and sustainable landscape and water management practices. The program emphasizes analytical thinking, AI-assisted decision-making, sustainability-oriented design approaches, and evidence-based decision-making to address complex environmental challenges in the built environment.

This micro-credential is intended for engineering students and professionals seeking to strengthen their expertise in sustainability assessment and resource management, particularly in arid and water-scarce regions.

## Undergraduate Programs > Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential in Sustainable Resource Management in the Built Environment > Admission
UAEU undergraduate students who have successfully completed at least one year of study with a minimum GPA of “2.5” and satisfy the prerequisite requirements of the constituent courses.

Non-degree learners and professionals holding a bachelor’s degree in engineering, Architecture, Construction, or a related discipline who wish to undertake the CBMC for continuing professional upskilling and development.

All applicants must satisfy the prerequisite requirements of the constituent courses and the English language requirements in accordance with UAEU regulations.

Equivalent academic background or relevant professional experience may be accepted.

## Undergraduate Programs > Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential in Sustainable Resource Management in the Built Environment > Targeted Students
Undergraduate students in Architectural Engineering

Undergraduate students from related Engineering disciplines.

Non-degree learners seeking undergraduate-level professional development.

Engineering and construction professionals seeking to enhance competencies in sustainable resource management and AI-supported environmental assessment.

## Undergraduate Programs > Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential in Sustainable Resource Management in the Built Environment > Program Objectives
Contribute to sustainable development practices in the built environment by integrating environmental considerations into design, planning, and project decision-making.

Support resource-efficient and environmentally responsible solutions, particularly in water-scarce and arid contexts, within professional engineering and design settings.

Apply interdisciplinary knowledge of sustainability, AI-supported environmental systems, and built environment practices to address real-world challenges.

Engage in continuous professional development and adapt to evolving sustainability standards, AI technologies, and industry requirements.

## Undergraduate Programs > Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential in Sustainable Resource Management in the Built Environment > Program Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

Apply life cycle assessment methods to evaluate the environmental impacts of built environment systems.

Analyse resource use and efficiency, particularly water systems, in buildings and landscapes.

Evaluate design alternatives using environmental performance assessment, sustainability criteria, and appropriate analytical tools.

Develop sustainable solutions integrating life cycle thinking, resource management strategies, and AI-supported design exploration where appropriate.

Communicate sustainability analyses and design proposals effectively in professional formats.
