  6.   Other


  6.1.       New Funding                          Dr. Walid Ibrahim, Director of the Office of Institutional
             Mechanism for Federal                Effectiveness, presented to the Board an overview of the
             HEI                                  new funding mechanism for federal higher education
                                                  institutions.

                                                  The presentation outlined a comparison between the
                                                  previous funding formula and the new formula, addressing
                                                  the following key areas:

                                                  •   The internal workings of both formulas,
                                                  •   The anticipated budgetary impact,
                                                  •   The projected    impact on student enrollment, and
                                                  •   The expected effects on       academic programs.

                                                  The Board noted that the new formula could potentially
                                                  lead to the phasing out of underperforming academic
                                                  programs, which would benefit the university and align
                                                  with the broader educational objectives of the UAE.
