## Tuition and Fees > Tuition and Fees
The details of the total fees for the 1.5-year (including the Summer semester) long MBA programs are as follows:

Details of Fees | Number of courses | Amount (AED)/unit
Application processing fee (nonrefundable) | - | 200
Online bridging course (nonrefundable) | 4 | 2000 *The student could be waived if equilvant courses were successfully passed at the undergraduate level.
Standard Courses | 9 | 5900
Capstone project | 1 | 12000
Leadership program | 1 | 12000
International trip (1 week) | 1 | A provision of a minimum of 20,000 AED should be planned by the student to pay the cost of the international trip

You may pay for each semester in full, or you may request to pay in 2 –3 separate installments. Students will not be admitted to the classes until payment is received.
