## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Al Foah Experimental Farm
The Research Farms Stations are a place where UAEU students, researchers, members of the public and private agricultural sector, and all others who love farming and healthy agricultural products meet. The Research Farms supports educational activities by dedicating all possibilities for UAEU students to complement their theoretical knowledge with practical training and experiences in the field. Aside from our involvement in UAEU research and education, the Research Farms stations actively engage with the community and supports agricultural education outside of the university through hosting practical workshops and organize school students visits. It also hosts a wide spectrum of research activities, most of which focus on the innovation strategies that support food security in the region and sustainable plant and animal production systems.

Mr. Bader Al Tamimi,

Farms Director
