## Career counseling and advising service > Conditions & Requirements
Student must be enrolled in UAEU

## Career counseling and advising service > What are the Career Counseling Services?
The Career Planning is a service offers for students to assist them in setting goals and taking appropriate decisions related to their desired careers; in addition to, equipping them with the needed employability skills to prepare them for the world of work including Individual Sessions, Group Sessions, Career Workshops and lectures, Career Assessments & Tests in additional of resources as publications.
