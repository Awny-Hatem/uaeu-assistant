## Experience. Connect. Discover
UAEU is about more than just the classroom learning experience, it is also about your own personal journey. With a variety of majors to choose from, state-of-the-art educational and recreation facilities housed on a modern campus, and a diverse student population, you won’t just receive a high quality education but will also grow as an individual. With the opportunity to develop academic, social and other personal skills in a supportive and well-resourced environment, UAEU graduates not only engage with their subjects but discover even more about themselves and the world around them.

## UAE – The Land of Opportunity
The United Arab Emirates (UAE) is situated in the southeast of the Arabian Peninsula, bordering Oman and Saudi Arabia. Four-fifths of the UAE is desert with contrasting landscapes – from the towering red dunes of Liwa to the rich oasis of Al Ain. From the Hajar Mountains to fertile coastal plains.
