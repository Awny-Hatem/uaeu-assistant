Overview of United Arab Emirates University
The United Arab Emirates University was established in 1976 by a decision of the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan, may
God rest his soul, and aspires to be a comprehensive research university. Being the leading university in the United Arab Emirates,
it provides a variety of high-quality and accredited undergraduate and graduate programs. These programs are offered into 9
colleges: Business and Economics, Education, Engineering, Agriculture & Veterinary Medicine, Humanities and Social Sciences,
Information Technology, Law, Medicine & Health Sciences, and Sciences. With distinguished elite of international faculty, a new
and state of the art campus, and a full range of services that provide support to students, the university provides an educational
environment unparalleled in the United Arab Emirates .

UAEU Alumni Award
Objectives:
      -     Consolidating and sustaining the relationship with the graduates.
      -     Communicating with each other and with students, faculty, and university staff to facilitate the exchange of knowledge
            and experience.
      -     Appreciating the achievements of university graduates, and emphasizing their role as leaders in various disciplines .
      -     Involving   graduat   es as one of the strategic partners in the main initiatives of the university.
Categories
            1- The United Arab Emirates University Alumni Award for Research, Innovation and Entrepreneurship.
            2- The United Arab Emirates University Alumni Award for Community Engagement.
            3-The United Arab Emirates University Alumni Award for Women Empowerment.
            4-The United Arab Emirates University Alumni Award Wall of Fame.

            The  University  awards  six  awards  annually  for  each  category  (3  awards  for  local  graduates  and  3  awards  for
            international graduates), and ten graduates are selected for the Wall of Fame (with 5 awards for local graduates and 5
            awards for international graduates).

General Criteria
      -     These awards are given to the United Arab Emirates University graduates only.
      -     Nominees are required to demonstrate tangible achievements in their respective fields.
      -     Winners may not be re-nominated for the same category, and it is permissible to nominate them for another category
            of the award after at least 3 years of obtaining the previous award.
      -     Nominees must have, during the nomination, experience of at least two years in the field of work, study or business
            after graduation from the United Arab Emirates University.
      -     Submission of a letter of recommendation in support of the candidate.

Nomination Requirements
      -     Contact details with the nominee and the nominator ;
      -     A recent CV of the     nominee     ;
      -      3-2 pages containing the      nominee’s     achievements      ;
      -       -Other  documents to support  the nomination:  statement of  achievements,  newspaper articles, research activities,
            website, etc ... (evidence)
