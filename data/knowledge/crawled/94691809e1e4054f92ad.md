# Institutional Excellence Award

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## Vision, Mission, Values and Goals > Vision
Achieve excellence and leadership of the United Arab Emirates University to be a global inspirational reference for excellence.

## Vision, Mission, Values and Goals > Mission
We seek to motivate leadership thinking and strengthen the role of the university units in achieving superior performance in order to reach the vision of the United Arab Emirates University and executing innovative initiatives in documenting and disseminating knowledge and best practices of the federal government worldwide.

## Vision, Mission, Values and Goals > Values
Innovation

Transparency

Initiative Spirit

Team-work

Integrity

Learning and Continuous Improvement

## Vision, Mission, Values and Goals > Goals
Improving the level of institutional and functional performance.

Endorsing the concepts and standards of quality and institutional excellence.

Motivating employees to create and innovate in university operations and services.

Appreciating and honouring leaders and excellent career cadres.

Exchanging and disseminating best practices among the different organizational units in the university.
