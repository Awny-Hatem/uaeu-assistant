## The Assessment Unit
The Assessment Unit is dedicated to creating a supportive and inclusive environment for exceptional Children, encompassing those with learning difficulties, giftedness, and disabilities. Our key objectives include:

Focusing on evaluation processes: by assessing psychological, mental, cognitive, and social abilities with the aim of identifying their individual needs.

Enhancing Assessment Tools : Our team is committed to using the best specialized assessment tools for exceptional children, which helps in creating appropriate intervention plans, ensuring targeted and timely support.

Pioneering Innovative Approaches : Our team strives to use pioneering and innovative teaching strategies that meet the educational needs of exceptional children.

Early Identification : We emphasize the early identification of children with disabilities and learning difficulties to prevent school failure and mitigate associated psychological and behavioral challenges. This proactive approach helps us provide timely interventions.
