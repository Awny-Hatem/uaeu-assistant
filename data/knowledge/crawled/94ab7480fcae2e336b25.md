## Overview
The UAE University Social Club (AL Multaqa) was established in 1998 to provide opportunities for UAEU employees and their families to become involved in various social, educational, cultural and sporting activities whilst working at the University.

The Multaqa is organizationally under the authority of the University Outreach Department, which consists of three main sections:

1. International Relations Section

2. Media Communications Section

3. Institutional Communications Section
