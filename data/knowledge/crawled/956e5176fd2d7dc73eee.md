## Obtain Residence Visa & Documents for International Students
This service allows international students Guarantor by the University to apply for a UAE residence visa and complete the required documentation and procedures in accordance with the University's regulations and the applicable laws and regulations of the United Arab Emirates.

## Obtain Residence Visa & Documents for International Students > Conditions & Requirements
Student must be Enrolled

## Obtain Residence Visa & Documents for International Students > What are the services offered by the International Student Services Office?
U.A.E. residence visas (for qualified students).

## Obtain Residence Visa & Documents for International Students > My visa is on my guardian what are the conditions that I must fulfill so that I can transfer my residence to the University’s sponsorship?
Termination of the Guarantor’s employment, death of the Guarantor in accordance with the new residency laws in the country, the father’s salary as the Guarantor must not be less than AED 4,000, the Guarantor is not present in the country for any reason, the Guarantor’s profession becomes one of the professions that are not permitted to guarantee others according to the laws of the country, the student must provide a stamped letter from the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs confirming that the current Guarantor is unable to renew the student’s residence visa in accordance with the laws of the country (in case the Guarantor is someone other than the father or mother), the student must obtain the required approvals to transfer the residence visa and must continue to be enrolled in their studies.

## Obtain Residence Visa & Documents for International Students > My residence visa is under the university’s guarantee and is about to expire. Can I renew it, and what documents are required?
Yes, you can renew your residence visa provided that you are continuing your studies. For renewal, please access the electronic services, select the “Residence Visa Renewal” service, then attach the required documents. • A recent photo with a white background

## Obtain Residence Visa & Documents for International Students > My health insurance is about to expire, how can I renew it to be sponsored by the university?
The university only renews and accepts those who are on its visa.

## Obtain Residence Visa & Documents for International Students > How can I contact the International student services office?
We are happy to answer your questions. You can contact us through the following channels: • Phone: 037136693 • Email: iso.office@uaeu.ac.ae • In person: Visit us during official working hours at our office in Al Liwan Building, first floor, Office 1006, next to the health clinic.

## Obtain Residence Visa & Documents for International Students > Can I renew my passport through the university?
Passport is a personal matter related to the student so the university is not authorized to renew it. The student has to renew it by him/herself.
