## Strategy and Future Department > Overview
The Strategy and Future Department plays a key role in supporting the university’s administration by facilitating informed decision-making and driving the strategic planning process. The department ensures the implementation of excellence standards across the university, disseminate the concepts of excellence, creativity and evaluate the best administrative and professional practices. It oversees the development, execution, and continuous monitoring of the university's strategic plan, ensuring alignment with Federal Government directives and higher education requirements.

## Strategy and Future Department > UAEU Strategic Plan
The 2023–2026 Strategic Plan serves as a forward-looking roadmap guiding the development of the United Arab Emirates University in alignment with national priorities and global trends. It reflects the University’s commitment to excellence in education, research, and innovation through a clear set of strategic priorities, goals, and initiatives. We invite you to explore this plan to learn more about the University’s goals, projects and the key steps being taken to shape a brighter and more impactful future.
