## PDF page 5
Available Documents
      •                                      Academic Transcript –         Arabic
             This service enables students to issue an official academic transcript in Arabic, including all completed
             courses, grades, GPAs, and the  student 's complete  academic record.

      •                                      Academic Transcript –         English
             This service enables students to issue an official academic transcript in English, including all completed
             courses, grades, GPAs, and the  student 's complete  academic record.

      •                                        Student Official  Schedule Letter
             This letter  is used to verify a student 's enrollment during the current  semester  and includes the
             registered   courses, class schedule,  and final examination schedule.

      •                                        Student Account  Letter
             This document provides an official statement  of the  student 's financial account, including all tuition and
             fees  assessed, payments made,  outstanding balances, and any remaining financial obligations expected
             to be settled prior to graduation, as recorded in the University 's financial system.

      •                                          To Whom It May Concern Letter
             This service provides an official letter confirming that the student is currently enrolled and studying at
             the United Arab Emirates University. The letter includes the student 's current academic status and may
             be submitted to external organizations when required.
