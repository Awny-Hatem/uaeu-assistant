                                             Assessment of Academic Programs


I.                                                                                               Policy

                The assessment of academic programs will provide for ongoing, systematic
                improvement of the programs offered at the United Arab Emirates University.
                Academic programs will be assessed at  regular intervals.  As part of that
                process, each program will be critically examined and analyzed for its
                effectiveness in meeting its mission and goals.  An external assessment team
                will visit the University and make an independent, professional assessment of
                the program and its operations against its own mission and goals and the
                accepted “best practices” standards of internationally reputed institutions
                within the cultural context of the region.  As a result of the self-study and the
                report of the external team, the dean will prepare a plan to enhance the
                effectiveness of the program, subject to the required approval, and report
                annually on progress in its implementation.

                All academic degree programs, the academic program model, the general
                education program and the University General Requirements Unit program
                will be assessed at regular intervals.  The schedule will be staggered so that
                some program assessment will be occurring each year.  As new programs are
                approved, they will be added to the review schedule.


II.                                                                             Application

                This policy shall apply to all academic units of the United Arab Emirates
                University.
