    THE COMPETITIVENESS OF AFRICA’S ECONOMIES


The  College   of Business    &  Economics    (CBE)    of the  UAE   University   has   developed    a seminar   series  during
which  topics  of relevance   for  the UAE    and  those  interested   in the  UAE   will be addressed.

Africa  isa  continent   with  54  countries   and   immense    riches.  Some    analysts   have  put  the  resource   reserves
of Africa  at more   than  US$80     trillion.While   there  is a perception    amongst    some   that  Africa  is riddled  with
risk and  challenges,    many   corporates,    SMEs,    and  entrepreneurs     have   identified  investment     opportunities
on  the  continent.   The   United   Arab   Emirates   is one   of the  prominent     investors   in Africa,  with  companies
such  as DP   World   growing   its footprint   on both   the east  and  west   coast  of Africa.

The  talk by  Dr. Sasidaran    Gopalan    willfocus   on  the nature   of competitiveness      and  how   Africa  shapes   up  in
this regard,  bearing   in mind   that  it is not  one  country.   In addition,  to  understand     how   others  have   tapped
into  the  many    opportunities    in  Africa,  the  Managing     Director    Fintech   &  Infrastructure    of  Tolaram,   Mr.
Navin   Nahata,   will set  out   the  drivers  of  their  decision   to  set up   business   in  Nigeria   and  thereafter    to
expand    into  Egypt,   Kenya,   Eswatini   and   South    Africa.  To  contrast    the  corporate    side  of  business,   the
Managing     Director   of Poultry   East   Africa  Limited   (PEAL),    Ms.  Shumei    Lam,   will explain   her  decisions   in
establishing   a poultry  business   in Rwanda.    Attendees    can  expect   to gain  insights  into  the  following:
                 The   drivers  of competitiveness     in emerging    markets
                 How    Africa  fares in the  competitive    world  of  business
                 Understanding      the  development     paths   of Tolaram   and   PEAL   in Africa
                 Crystalizing   lessons   learnt  for the  road  ahead

KEYNOTE SPEAKERS


              Dr.  Sasidaran     Gopalan:     The  Competitiveness         of Africa
              Assistant    Professor,    Innovation    in Government       &  Society-(UAEU        CBE)


          Sasidaran   Gopalan   iscurrently  an  Assistant  Professor  in the  Department    of Innovation  in Government     and  Society  at the College  of
          Business   and  Economics   (CBE).   Dr. Gopalan   obtained  his PhD   in Public  Policy from  George   Mason   University   (GMU)   in the  United
          States   of America    (USA).   Since  his  PhD,  he   has  held  various  academic    and   research  appointments     across  several   renowned
          universities in Asia, including  Nanyang   Technological   University  (Singapore),  Nazarbayev    University  (Kazakhstan),   National  University
          of Singapore   (Singapore)   and  the  Hong   Kong  University  of Science   and  Technology   (Hong   Kong).  Dr. Gopalan’s   research  assumes   a
          strong   interdisciplinary  orientation  and   falls under   the  intersections   of applied   macroeconomics,     international   economics,   and
          development    policy,with  a broad  focus on  emerging   markets.


              Mr.  Navin    Nahata:    Tolaram’s     Journey     into  Africa
              Managing      Director   Fintech    & Infrastructure,     Tolaram

          Navin  Nahata    isthe  Managing    Director  for Fintech  &  Infrastructure  at  Tolaram.  In addition  to leading  both  verticals, he  holds  the
          regulatory   role of  President   Commissioner     for Bank   Amar   (owned    by  Tolaram)   in Indonesia.   He  has  been   at  the  helm  of  the
          infrastructure  business   since 2016.  He  drove  mega   projects  such  as the  construction   of Lekki  Port –  named   "African  Infrastructure
          Deal  of the Year"  in 2019   by Project  Financial International  and  the  development    of Lagos  Free  Zone,  Nigeria's first privately-owned
          special  economic    zone.  Navin  transitioned   into  his current  role  from  being   the  CFO   formerly.  He  has   been  part  of  the  senior
          management     team  for 17  years, responsible  for setting the  strategic direction of the  business.

              Ms.   Shumei     Lam:   PEAL    in Rwanda
              Managing      Director   of  Poultry   East  Africa   Limited   (PEAL)

           Shumei   Lam  is the founder  and  Managing    Director of Poultry  East  Africa Ltd (PEAL),  Rwanda’s   largest  commercial   poultry  farm.

           In her firstvisit to Rwanda   in 2011,  she  realised that  chicken  meat  was  the most   expensive  meat   inthe  market  due  to lack of supply,
           and  because  of this, people  could only  afford to consume    chicken  once  a year. PEAL   was  established  to provide  a sustainable  solution
           to this problem   - a  social impact  investment    with the  objective  of providing   affordable  and  accessible  meat  proteins   to a growing
           population.

           Armed   with  zero  farming  experience,   Shumei   set off in 2012   to  build the  firstmodern    farm  in Rwanda.   PEAL,   which  started  as  a
