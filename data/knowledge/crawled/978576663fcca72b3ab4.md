## Multaqa > Athletic Activities
UAEU employees and their families can get involved in a number of sporting activities organized by Multaqa. These include:

Taekwondo

Karate

Tennis

Football

Basketball

Fencing

Volleyball

Squash

Aerobics for Women

## Multaqa > Taekwondo
Target Audience : Children between (5 - 14) years.

Course Commences : The beginning of each month.

Venue : University Social Club.

Day/Time : Saturday, Monday, Wednesday, from 5.00 to 9.00 PM. (One hour for each group).

Coach: Mufleh Naji (Instructor, Referee, Taekwondo International, 6 th Dan Black Belt)

Requirements:

Participants need to produce a fitness testimony endorsed by a guardian (parent).

Participants must wear uniform as determined by the coach.

Registration Fees: Dhs. 200 (Payable in advance at the social club)

Important Notice :

The last date for payment of course fees is on the tenth day of each month.

The number of places are limited and therefore priority will be given to those who register first.
