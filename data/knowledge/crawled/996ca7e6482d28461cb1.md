## Emirates Center for Happiness Research (ECHR) > Overview
To contribute to the government’s efforts towards realizing the United Arab Emirates Vision 2021, the United Arab Emirates University has established the Emirates Center for Happiness Research (ECHR). The UAE happiness Index is one of the key national performance indicators of the Vision 2021, of having a cohesive society and preserved identity.

Know More

## Emirates Center for Happiness Research (ECHR) > Vision and Mission > Mission
To enhance the UAE government’s commitment to happiness and well-being by conducting cutting-edge research in this field. Also, disseminating its

findings nationally and globally, monitoring and assessing the nation’s happiness indices and promoting virtues of well-being in the community.

## Emirates Center for Happiness Research (ECHR) > Vision and Mission > Vision
To be a leading center in Happiness and Well-Being research and a major contributor in promoting wellbeing in the nation and around the world.

## Emirates Center for Happiness Research (ECHR) > Director's Message
Welcome to the Emirates Center for Happiness Research. The Center is involved in cutting edge research in the field of happiness and wellbeing to advance our knowledge and understanding of how societies thrive. We understand the importance of diversity and engaging everyone, fostering a sense of belonging and helping members of the community to unleash their talents and reach their full potential.
