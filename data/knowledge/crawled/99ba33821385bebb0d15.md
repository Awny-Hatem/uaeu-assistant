## Competition Areas
Climate Change and Environmental Sustainability

Providing innovative solutions to mitigate the environmental impact of global climate change and environmental pollution.

Renewable Energy

Providing innovative solutions to renewable energy industry and providing innovative contributions into the applied research in clean Technology.

Water and Food Security

Providing innovative solutions which address the problem of water scarcity and food security in the United Arab Emirates.

Health and Medical Science

Providing innovative solutions to the healthcare industry in treatments and drugs, in hospital and care systems, in primary and acute care pathways and in chronic disease management.

Education and Lifelong Learning

Providing innovative solutions to educational institutions and universities with the goal of equipping students with innovative skills such as critical thinking, analytical thinking, problem-solving and creativity.

Space Science

Providing innovative solutions for the space industry and providing innovative contributions to applied research in space technology.

Mobility and Urban Development

Providing innovative solutions for ground, air, and sea travel as well as logistics with the goal of providing new products and services while making procedures more effective and save time.

Technology and Cyber Security

Providing innovative solutions to the IT industry to advance the development and use of technology to improve products and enhance services.

Social, Administrative and Economic Growth Innovation

Providing innovative solutions to social authorities addressing issues related to community development, living conditions, organizational processes, employment, and economic investment.
