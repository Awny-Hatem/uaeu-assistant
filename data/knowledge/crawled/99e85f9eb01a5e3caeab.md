## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Field Sites
Field sites on Al Foah Experimental farm are used mainly for production of vegetables, staple food crops, dates and fruits. The farm strives to test and demonstrate innovative production systems that may help to save water, and reduce the environmental impact of agricultural practices.

On Al Foah Experimental Farm, large areas of even land are available for scientific field experiments. A fully automated irrigation system can be used to control and adjust the irrigation water supply.

All soils on the farm are non-saline with favorable chemical and physical properties. With the assistance of highly qualified engineers and workers available, even technically challenging experimental approaches can be implemented.
