## E-consultation for People of Determination > Share Your Voice
Welcome to our electronic consultation platform dedicated for People of Determination. This survey aims to collect comprehensive feedback from People of Determination regarding their experiences during their first academic year, to understand the challenges, successes, and their needs.

Objectives:

Identify the obstacles that students of determination face during their academic and social lives.

Assess the effectiveness and availability of the facilities, resources, and support services provided by the institution.

Gather insights on how to improve the environment to be more inclusive, accessible, and supportive for students of determination.

Outcome and Decision: Based on the survey results and the challenges identified, recommendations can be proposed to improve the experience of students of determination during their first university year, to be shared with the relevant department.
