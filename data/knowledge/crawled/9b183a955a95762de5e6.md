                                              Establishing a New Academic Program


I.                                                                                               Policy

                  New academic programs may be developed and implemented at the United
                  Arab Emirates University when there is strong evidence of need for the
                  program and when resources sufficient to assure program quality can be
                  committed.  New programs must be consistent with the University’s vision,
                  mission and strategic plan, as well as market needs.  Proposals for new
                  programs should be developed through appropriate college processes and
                  must be reviewed and approved through the University’s procedures.

                  In the context of this policy, the term “new academic program” is defined as a
                  new degree (undergraduate or graduate), a new major, a new minor, or a new
                  certificate or diploma program.  It does not include a new concentration
                  within an existing major.


II.                                                                             Application

                  This policy shall apply to all academic units of the United Arab Emirates
                  University.
