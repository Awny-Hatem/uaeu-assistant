CANB602 - Advanced Cancer Biology (2 credit hours) Advanced Cancer Biology: This course will focus on molecular and cellular mechanisms behind cancer progression, invasion, metastases and consequences of cancer.
Particular focus on the control pathways will provide students the background to the cutting edge of drug design for targeted cancer therapy.
This course should provide the students with the tools to be able to identify the cellular and molecular changes lead to cancer development, invasion, consequences and therapy.
Students should be able to outline the cellular pathways involved in angiogenesis, invasion and metastatic progression and the basic principals involving inflammation and cancer and cancer immunology.
