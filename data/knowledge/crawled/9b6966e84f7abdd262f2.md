Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## PDF page 1
Download UAEU Smart phone apps via Internal Store

UAEU Smart phone apps can now be accessed from Internal Store instead of
the public stores of your Mobile Phone. The internal Store is called Intune
Company Portal.
Follow below steps to search and download any UAEU Smart phone apps.
       1- Search and download the internal store:
       In your phone’s App store or Play Store, search for Intune Company
       Portal then install it.

## PDF page 2
2- Open the newly installed Intune Company Portal. If prompted to Sign
        in, use your UserID/Password or UAE Pass and login as you normally
        login.

## PDF page 3
Depending on the device and OS version and type, you might see
          prompt to setup UAE University access. You can select Postpone (see
          below image). You can come back to the Setup later.

## PDF page 4
3- You should see list of UAEU Mobile Applications available for
download (e.g. UAEU CTS, UAEU Transportation)
