## General Education Program > Overview
The General Education Program (GEP) at United Arab Emirates University is an undergraduate curricular component shared by students of all colleges. It provides a common learning environment that is essential in unifying the students’ learning experiences through a coherent general education model that broadens their intellectual horizons and stimulates their intellectual growth.
