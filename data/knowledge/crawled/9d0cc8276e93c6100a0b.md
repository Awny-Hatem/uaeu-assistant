                                        To provide research assistance in conducting investigative research requiring
                                        the application of standard procedures. Performs some or all of the duties listed
                                        below, as assigned.

                                        Duties and Responsibilities:

                                             •    Researches and collects data through complex techniques and
                                                  procedures; library research; structures interviews; or through other
                                                  means for research projects.

                                             •    Searches sources such as reference works, literature, documents,
                                                  newspapers, and statistical records, to obtain data on assigned subject.

                                             •    Assists faculty members in their research by providing them research
                                                  material and statistical analysis.

                                             •    Interprets, synthesizes and analyses data using scientific or statistical
                                                  techniques.

                                             •    Modifies and plans research procedures or survey instruments.
Job Description:
                                             •    Assists in research design.

                                             •    Writes and edits material for publication and presentation.

                                             •    Reports on status of research activities.

                                             •    Schedules and coordinates research activities.

                                             •    Assists students in extracting court sentences, research work and
                                                  searching the internet.

                                             •    Monitors and maintains the legal library in the annex (Computer Lab)

                                             •    Generally assists in the academic and research activities organized by
                                                  the college.

                                             •    Prepares and maintains the annual research plan of the college
