## Graduate Programs > Master of Arts in English
The MA in English (MAE) offers specializations in the disciplines housed in the Department of Languages and Literature at UAEU, including literature and cinema studies, as well as theater and their intersections. Expanding on analytical and critical thinking skills gained throughout their undergraduate programs, students receive qualifications for careers revolving around informed, eloquent text analysis and creative expression. These careers include but are not limited to teachers, literary translators, editors, and writers. Successful graduates will further be capable to demonstrate the significance of creative representations of their own cultural background for global markets in the areas of literature, film production, and theater.

## Graduate Programs > Master of Arts in English > Program Objectives
Illustrate essential principles and advanced terminology in the field of English Studies

Apply solid knowledge about historical and cultural contexts in which to situate selected literary texts

Practice advanced research methodologies

Demonstrate different theories and approaches to text analysis

Practice the competencies required for the creation of literary print or digital texts

Evaluate discipline-specific teaching methodologies

## Graduate Programs > Master of Arts in English > Program Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

Assess advanced discipline-specific terminology in speech and writing

Critique a wide variety of literary texts from different periods and cultures

Explain advanced research methodologies

Appraise key critical approaches within literary criticism

Create literary print or digital texts

Prepare discipline-specific teaching methodologies
