## Find Research at UAEU > Welcome to UAE University!
In an effort to synergize inter-disciplinary clinical and translational research, UAE University is committed to using innovative research tools and information technologies to promote collaboration regardless of organizational affiliation or position within the bench-to-bedside-to-population spectrum of science. This expertise portal is a key component of UAE University mission to catalyze the application of new knowledge and techniques to clinical practice at the front lines of patient care.
