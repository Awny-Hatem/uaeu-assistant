             THE UAE & SINGAPORE: FROM THIRD TO FIRST WORLD IN ONE GENERATION



                       Date               :  8th September 2021, Wednesday
                       Fee                                :  Complimentary                                                 Time: 9:00 to 10:15 am
                       Webinar link  :                Click Here


          The College of Business & Economics (CBE) of the UAE University has developed a seminar series College o                                                             f Busi                                     ness &                                                                   Economic                                                                                                  s (CBE) o                                                                                                f the UAE                                                                  Uni                                      versity
          during which topics of relevance for the UAE and those interested in the UAE will be addressed.



          The      United      Arab      Emirates      was      formed      on      2      December      1971      and      will      be      celebrating      its      50th
          anniversary      on      2      December      2021.      During      this      period,      it      has,      through      exemplary      leadership,
         developed from a Third World Country to a First World powerhouse. In a similar vein, Singapore
          became    independent    on    9    August    1965,    and    also    developed    to    a    First    World    country.    It    has
          given rise to the saying, “from Third World to First World in one generation.”

          The talk will focus on the leadership and development paths of these two exceptional countries.

          A        lot        could        have        gone        wrong.        Despite        this,        their        respective        stories        serve        as        inspiration        for
          developing countries globally on how strong leadership and sound policy frameworks have led
          to stellar performance on so many performance metrics, such as the World Bank’s Ease of Doing
          Business, the WEF’s Global Competitiveness rankings, and the Global Innovation Index, to name
          but a few. Attendees can expect to gain insights into the following:
                             The role and importance of directive leadership and sound governance in developing
                             economies
                             Understanding the development paths of the UAE and Singapore respectively
                             Crystalizing lessons learnt for the road ahead
