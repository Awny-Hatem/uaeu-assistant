Overview of United Arab Emirates University
The United Arab Emirates University was established in 1976 by a decision of the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan, may
God rest his soul, and aspires to be a comprehensive research university. Being the leading university in the United Arab Emirates,
it provides a variety of high-quality and accredited undergraduate and graduate programs. These programs are offered into 9
colleges: Business and Economics, Education, Engineering, Agriculture & Veterinary Medicine, Humanities and Social Sciences,
Information Technology, Law, Medicine & Health Sciences, and Sciences. With distinguished elite of international faculty, a new
and state of the art campus, and a full range of services that provide support to students, the university provides an educational
environment unparalleled in the United Arab Emirates .
