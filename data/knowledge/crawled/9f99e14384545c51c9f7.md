Procedures of Policy No. (6) - Graduate Student Tuition, Fees, and
Accounts

1. Tuition and Fees
     a)          The University will publish annually a Schedule of Tuition and Fees specifying
           relevant applicability and amounts.
     b)             Tuition is a charge for educational services that include teaching and instruction, is
           assessed on a per credit hour basis at rates established by the University, and may
           include purchase/rental of textbooks.  Specific tuition charges can be affected by
           student classification, residency, academic program, and degree level.
     c)                Fees are charges for academic, personal, and related support services provided by or
           through the University, such as application processing, special course activities and
           services, housing, dining, direct services for individual students such as medical
           insurance and residency fees, academic records services, tuition installment payment
           fees, fines and late processing/late action fees, textbooks, and other fees as specified
           by the University.
     d)          In the case of courses offered for zero (0) credit hours, tuition and fee charges
           equivalent to the instructional time of the course may apply.
     e)                Changes in the published Schedule of Tuition and Fees during the course of a given
           academic year may be made upon approval according to the Signatory Authority.


2. Payment of Tuition and Fees
     a)                     Tuition charges are due and payable in full at the beginning of each academic semester
           according to the relevant deadlines in the  Schedule.
     b)              Students paying all or a portion of their tuition charges directly (“self-pay” students)
           are eligible to participate in an installment payment program provided that the student
           makes the required initial payment  by the initial payment deadline.

     c)          Students claiming external sponsorship for the payment to tuition charges must
           provide written confirmation of the sponsorship as specified in the Schedule not later
           than the initial payment deadline.  Sponsored students who do not submit the required
           confirmation of sponsorship and continue in enrollment will assume personal
           responsibility for all tuition charges and applicable fees.
     d)           Students who fail to pay all applicable tuition charges by the established payment
           deadline/s are subject to denial of academic services, late payment fees, requirement
           of advance payment of fees in full for future semesters, and/or cancellation of current
           and/or future registration.
     e)               Fees are due and payable at the time the services are provided, in conjunction with
           tuition payments, or as otherwise specified by the University.
     f)                         Application processing fees, selected individual services fees, and required enrollment
           and housing deposits are due and payable in advance.  Applications for admission will
           not be processed and specified individual services will not be provided in the absence
           of the required fees. Students who have not paid any required enrollment and housing
           deposits will be denied the associated services.
