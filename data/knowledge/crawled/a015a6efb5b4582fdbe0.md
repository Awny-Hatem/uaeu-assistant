## UAEU Expo Hub
The newly established UAEU Expo 2020 Hub located in the Science and Innovation Park at UAEU has been innovatively designed to host a number of activities and activations to engage, inspire and excite both the internal and external community about UAEU’s involvement at Expo 2020.

It is a place where collaboration, co-creation and sharing of knowledge is encouraged and celebrated. Our doors are always open, so stop by and get involved, get inspired and begin your journey of discovery.
