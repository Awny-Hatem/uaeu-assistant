1. Dean’s Welcome

Welcome to the first edition of the College of Business
and        Economics        (CBE),        United        Arab        Emirates        Univer-
sity                 (UAEU)                 newsletter.                 The                 newsletter                 celebrates
the       achievements       of       the       CBE’s       faculty       and       students
and             the             impact             they             had             on             industry             and             society
during the academic year 2024/25.


Some         of         the         key         highlights         include          the         CBE’s         Emir-
ates                  Center                  for                  Entrepreneurship                  Research                  which
has              been              involved              in              several              exciting              events              such
as        the        UAEU        Science        &        Innovation        Park:        Innovation                                                         Prof Mohamed Madi
                                                                                                                                       Dean, College of Business and Economics
Challenge    2024,    the    Emirati    Women’s    Day:    Women’s                                                                              United Arab Emirates University
Entrepreneurship            Networking            Event,            and            the            BUZZ
Festival   2024.   The   CBE   is   actively   supporting   Chapter
Zero     UAE,    an     initiative     supporting     board     directors     to
integrate                climate                governance                into                corporate                deci-
sion-making.


Our    undergraduate    students    won    second    place    in    an
International DevPost Hackathon and secured second
place at the 2nd GCC Industrial Engineering and Oper-
ations      Management      (IECOM)      Conference      in      Muscat,
Oman. CBE students are active researchers publishing
their  papers in Scopus  top  ranked journals.


The       CBE       has       had       an       exciting       range       of       seminars       and
workshops              organized              by              faculty              and              students.              For
instance,          the          Data          and          Business          Analytics          Club          has
been active in organizing seminars on IT for business.


Our        faculty        have        been        actively        engaged        in        research
with               publications               in               top               ranked               journals,               securing
research     patents,    and     prestigious     national     and     inter-
national              research              awards.


I hope you enjoy reading our newsletter.
