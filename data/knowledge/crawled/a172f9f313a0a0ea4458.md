Executive Summary




UAEU was established in 1976 by His Highness Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahayan. It was
envisioned to be a lighthouse of human thought, and a pioneering center for the development
of human wealth, dissemination of culture and deepening roots thereof, and the development
of society while sustaining its original elements and illuminating its heritage.

The ever-changing local and global environment raise the need for UAEU to evolve. Locally,
the UAE government has put human capital at the heart of its development plans. Globally,
trends  such  as  changing  employment  landscape  and  evolving  learners’  demands  are
changing the future of higher education. Local and global changes are affecting UAEU and
raising the need to transform it.

This strategy sets UAEU on a mission “The university contributes to developing a sustainable
educational and research future for the UAE, provides impactful societal contributions,
develops competitive capabilities by encouraging research collaboration and innovation,
establishes a stimulating environment for entrepreneurship and builds graduates’ skills in line
with the future labor market needs”. This mission entails that we will empower successful
future-ready learners, produce impactful research and innovation, have a strong societal
impact,  strengthen  collaboration,  provide  distinguished  services  and  proactive  student
experience,  achieve  organizational  excellence,  manage  innovation,  and  implement
transformational projects. All while staying true to our values of respecting heritage, integrity,
collaboration,  leadership  and  continuous  learning,  effective  decision-making,  effective
communication, dedication to knowledge, cultivating a culture of innovation, and reinforcing
competitiveness.

The strategy will be achieved by implementing specific activities across seven priority areas of
action, these are:
