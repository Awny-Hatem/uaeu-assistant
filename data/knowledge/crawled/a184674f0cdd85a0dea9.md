GRADUATION
           The following data represent the graduation rates for the College of Education's initial-licensure
      programs: Early Childhood Education (ECE), Special Education (SPED), and Health & Physical Education
      (HPE).

           A.   Number of Completers
                       Table 1 shows number of completers in the last three academic years. From Academic Year
                2022-2023 to Academic Year 2024-2025, there was an average of 1                    05 completers.
                       In Fall 2024 and Spring 2025, there were a total of 81 completers. Of these, 54% completed
                the ECE program, 33% completed the SPED program, and 12% completed the HPE program (see

           B.   Graduation Rates
                The   data  presented  below  demonstrates  the  six-year  graduation  rates  for  initial-licensure
                programs beginning with the 2014-2015 academic year. Notably, the Early Childhood Education
                program achieved its highest graduation rate of 90% in the 2018-2019 cohort, Special Education
                reached a peak of 97% in the 2015-2016 cohort, and Health and Physical Education saw its highest
                rate of 88% in the 2019-2020 cohort.
