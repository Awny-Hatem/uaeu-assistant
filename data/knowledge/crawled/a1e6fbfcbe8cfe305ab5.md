## The Role of Families in the UAE in Preserving Biodiversity and Protecting the Environment
Title: The Role of Families in the UAE in Preserving Biodiversity and Protecting the Environment

Date: Wednesday, 01 July 2026

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Ms. Fatima Al Hantoobi

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: This workshop aims to enhance environmental awareness among students and alumni by highlighting the vital role of families in protecting the environment and preserving biodiversity. It also seeks to foster a sense of social responsibility toward safeguarding natural resources for future generations.

The workshop will emphasize the importance of adopting sustainable daily practices that support the UAE's national environmental efforts while promoting a culture of sustainability within families and the wider community.

What Will Participants Gain?

Understand the concept of biodiversity and its importance to life, the economy, and society.

Learn about the role of families in promoting sustainable environmental practices.

Develop practical skills for adopting environmentally friendly behaviors in everyday life.

Increase awareness of the importance of protecting the UAE's natural resources and wildlife.

Strengthen values of environmental responsibility and positive citizenship.

Expected Impact

Increased environmental awareness among participants.

Greater adoption of sustainable practices within the community.

Support for the UAE's vision and initiatives in sustainability and environmental protection.
