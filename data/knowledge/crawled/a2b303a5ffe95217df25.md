Inter-relationship of Medline, MeSH, and PubMed



              MeSH is a database of terminology.

              Medline is a database of article records (citations).

              PubMed is the interface to a collection of Health Sciences & Biotechnology databases developed and
               maintained by the U.S. National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI).

              Both Medline and MeSH are hosted on PubMed.

              You use the MeSH database to efficiently locate article records in the Medline database.

              You can also search Medline via the PubMed interface without using MeSH.
