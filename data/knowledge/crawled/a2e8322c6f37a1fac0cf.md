                                                                                               University Vehicles


I.                                                                                               Policy

                       The United Arab Emirates University will maintain a number of motor
                       vehicles appropriate to its needs and resources.  The Secretary General will
                       maintain forms and procedures governing access to and use of University
                       vehicles.


II.                                                                             Application

                       This policy shall apply to all employees of the United Arab Emirates
                       University.
