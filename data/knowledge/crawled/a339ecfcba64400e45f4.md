                                                                                       Annual Report


I. Policy

                  At the end of each academic year, the Vice Chancellor shall prepare a detailed
                  report on the United Arab Emirates University’s scientific, academic,
                  administrative, financial affairs and student development.

                  The report shall address the specific goals and objectives established in the
                  annual plan and measure the performance of that plan.

                  The report shall also comprise a complete review of the various activities of
                  the University.  It shall state the University’s participation in scientific
                  research and community services and report on matters pertaining to its
                  faculty, education, examinations and their results, students and student affairs.
                  The report shall address opportunities to improve the operation of the
                  University, its efficiency and performance, and recommend appropriate
                  solutions and future development goals.


II.                                                                                 Application

                  This policy shall apply to all operating units of the United Arab Emirates
                  University.
