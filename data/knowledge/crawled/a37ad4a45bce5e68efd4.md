## GET YOUR TICKET TO THE EXPO 2020 Dubai > EXPO TICKETS TO GO ON SALE WORLDWIDE
Expo 2020 Dubai tickets are on sale worldwide from 18 July 2021, opening up a spectacular world of innovation and entertainment for every visitor across 182 visually striking and emotionally inspiring days from 1 October 2021 to 31 March 2022.

With about two months to go, Expo 2020 has announced three ticket tiers to ensure every visitor will be able to enjoy the spectacular event, regardless of the duration of their visit to Dubai. One-day tickets are priced at AED 95 (USD 26); multi-day tickets, offering unrestricted entry for 30 consecutive days, are priced at AED 195 (USD 53), and season passes, with unlimited entry for the entire six months of Expo 2020, are priced at AED 495 (USD 135).

More information on the Expo 2020 Dubai website.

## GET YOUR TICKET TO THE EXPO 2020 Dubai > PLAN YOUR JOURNEY AT THE UAE University Pavilion
The UAE University’s Pavilion is located in the Expo’s ‘Mobility district’. On-site shuttles connect the main Expo districts. We welcome private groups, schools and community groups for guided tours at the Pavilion.

Our pavilion hosts permanent and temporary exhibitions. All of the pavilion’s events and activities will be listed on our website very soon.

You will have to present an original identification document and a valid ticket at the Expo entrance gate. For your safety, backpacks and bags will be checked at the entrance.
