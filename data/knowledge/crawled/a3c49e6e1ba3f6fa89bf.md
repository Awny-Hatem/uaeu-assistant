## General Services Department > Overview
The General Services Department provides transportation and publication services to students, university community, including academics, administrators, and university clients, which contributes to support the educational process and the continuous pursuit to improve the services provided on the internal and external levels. The administration seeks to improve and develop the quality of services provided through the transportation and publications services sections in accordance with the objectives and vision of the United Arab Emirates University.
