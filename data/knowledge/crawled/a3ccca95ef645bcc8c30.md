## Medical Elective Program > Elective Rotations
The CMHS offers over 5 elective rotations in all major specialty areas to students who have completed their core clinical rotations. A maximum of one one-month rotations may be scheduled up to 6-9 months in advance. We need to know your area of interest (list two) and your preferred dates before an elective can be arranged. Once the department agrees to accept you, the clerkship coordinator will contact you via email.
