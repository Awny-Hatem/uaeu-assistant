## Development and Partnership Unit > Overview
The Development and Partnership Unit plays a pivotal role in advancing the strategic directives of the United Arab Emirates University, with a focus on achieving excellence in education and research. The Unit is dedicated to establishing and strengthening strategic partnerships with esteemed national and international entities. Its core functions include the development of high-quality initiatives, the attraction of meaningful collaboration opportunities, and the effective implementation of agreements and partnerships that support the University’s mission in the areas of education, research, and innovation. Through these efforts, the Unit actively contributes to the realization of the UAE Centennial 2071 vision.
