                 Policy for Awarding Continuing Education Units

I. Policy

         The UAE University has adopted the Continuing Education Unit (CEU) as the
         standard for appropriate education and training programs. The Continuing
         Education Center has been assigned the responsibility for reviewing and
         approving all education and training programs for adherence to the CEU criteria.
         Only those activities which have been approved by the Continuing Education
         Center will be allowed toward CEU.

II.   Application

         This policy shall apply to all colleges, units, and clubs operating within the UAE
         University.
