## Covid-19 Information > Testing
The World Health Organization and Center for Diseases Control regularly provide guidance and update on the appropriate use of testing for COVID-19 throughout the countries during this pandemic.

Currently there are two types of tests are available for COVID-19.

1. Molecular diagnosis of COVID-19 such Real-time reverse transcription (RT-PCR)

2. Antibody tests that can identify previous exposure to COVID-19 infection
