## Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a set of 17 interconnected goals that are meant to serve as a "blueprint for a better and more sustainable future for everybody." The SDGs were adopted by the United Nations General Assembly in 2015 and are part of a UN Resolution known as the "2030 Agenda," which aims to achieve them by 2030.

## Sustainability Club > Overview
To promote Abu Dhabi’s vision of sustainability, the United Arab Emirates University launches a sustainability club for students. The College of Science at the United Arab Emirates University launched a sustainability club on Wednesday 28th of September. 2016. Dr. Ahmed Murad, Dean of the College of Science; Dr. Ruwaya Al Kendi, Assistant Dean for Research and Graduate Studies, members and faculty attended the Al Ain campus. Dr. Murad said that this club is the first of its kind at the university and is due to the university encouraging students to participate in sustainability and spread awareness of the importance of environmental sustainability. He noted that the College of Science is committed to supporting such initiatives.
