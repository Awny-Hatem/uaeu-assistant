## High Energy Physics > ATLAS Hadronic Tile Calorimeter (TileCal)
The ATLAS Tile Calorimeter is a key component of the detector, designed to measure the energy of hadrons and jets produced in collisions. It consists of steel plates interleaved with scintillating tiles, providing efficient energy sampling and resolution. The calibration system of TileCal ensures accurate energy measurements by maintaining the uniformity and response stability of the detector over time. This is achieved through periodic calibration campaigns, laser systems, and radioactive source calibration, which are critical for achieving precise physics results.
