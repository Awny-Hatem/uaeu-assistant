Vision


At                       the                       AFAQ                       Management                       Trainee                       Program                       we                       are                       building                       a                       diverse                       resource                       of
high                     potential                     UAE                     national                     graduates,                     who                     are                     hungry                     for                     success                     in                     the                     Financial
Services             industry.             Your             future             success             could             be             built             on             a             fast             track             management             career
within NBAD.


Our Objective:
The     AFAQ     Management     Trainee     Program     develops     the     potential     of     young,     bright     graduates     and
encourages  UAE  Nationals  to  explore  new  horizons.  As  the  foundation  for  a  rewarding  career,  the
AFAQ Management Trainee Program offers support to:
•	     Equip  trainees  with  all  the  essential  technical,  personal  effectiveness  and  leadership  skills  they
    need
•	     Build   a   solid   foundation   of   knowledge   through   post-graduate   studies   in   Finance   at   a   reputable
    university
•	     Enable trainees to develop and build on their full range of competencies and skills
•	     Help trainees to take advantage of future job and career opportunities across the whole of NBAD
After graduating from the AFAQ Management Trainee Program, trainees are provided with a career
plan for their development and success within NBAD for the next two years. This enables trainees
to  understand  fully  how  they  fit  in  to  NBAD,  and  how  to  apply  their  acquired  skills  and  knowledge
successfully.      Of      course,      we      want      this      to      match      their      ambitions      for      their      chosen      career      path      in
Financial Services.
