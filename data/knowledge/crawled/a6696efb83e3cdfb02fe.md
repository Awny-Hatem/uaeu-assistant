## Forms > Manuscript Editing > Manuscript Preparation Services > Journal Selection Service
Selecting the right journal is critical to making sure your paper gets published. Often, papers are rejected because the research topic is outside the scope of the journal or is aimed at a different target audience. Our team of experts shortlists 3 to 5 best-suited journals for your manuscript and prepares a report listing the pros and cons of submitting to each.

## Forms > Manuscript Editing > Manuscript Preparation Services > Manuscript Submission Service
The process of submitting your manuscript to a journal is complicated and time-consuming. We will submit your manuscript with all the necessary information, and we’ll handle the rest.

## Forms > Manuscript Editing > Manuscript Preparation Services > Rapid Technical Review
Most scientific journals reject four out of every five manuscripts they receive. Using our Rapid Technical Review service, you can get our expert panelists, all of whom have published peer-reviewed papers, to review your paper and provide constructive comments so that you can improve your manuscript BEFORE submission to the journal of your choice.

Manuscript Editing Service The UAEU Manuscript Editing Service is to support your publishing output. Faculty and staff can have their manuscripts edited, proofread and checked for free before final submission for publication to peer-reviewed journals. UAEU Libraries have contracted a professional agency in the field with a pool of editors worldwide to do the editing services. We encourage all faculty, staff and graduate students to make use of the services. Please follow the guidelines to apply for this services. Guidelines

Researchers should contact the coordinator to request an order form

Fill in the order form to decide the type of service required

Make sure all sections of the form are filled out

Submit the order and request forms (attached) with a copy of manuscript (WORD format) including your suggestions for journal titles to the coordinator

The service takes 14 business days maximum. Other services take a little longer.
