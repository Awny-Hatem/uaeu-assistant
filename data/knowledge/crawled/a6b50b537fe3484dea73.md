2. Students register for the minor program in the 3rd year of their studies
3. Completion of the minor is noted on the students' printed transcript
4. Failure to complete a registered minor carries no penalty (e.g. the transcript does not refer to the minor at all)
5. Students cannot extend their undergraduate semesters just to complete the minor



The application should be submitted to the advising unit no later than November 1 for admissions to the minor program in the Spring
semester OR April 1 for the Fall semester
