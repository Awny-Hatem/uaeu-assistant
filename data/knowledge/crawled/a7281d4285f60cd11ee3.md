        Overview
When  progressing  up  the  managerial  ladder,  the  process  of  managers  having  to
stop   doing   some   things   they   have   done   in   the   past   and   start   doing   new   things
continues.     This     process     of     unlearning     and     learning     is     an     important     one     for
managers   to   be   successful.   The   Senior   Management   Program   (SMP)   has   been
designed to equip managers with the necessary knowledge and skills to function
effectively   in   a   UAE   and   regional   environment.   Due   to   the   constantly   changing
nature     of     this     environment,     the     content     of     the     SMP,     which     focuses     on     both
present  and  future  national  and  international  realities,  is  continually  adapted.  A
generalist rather than a specialist approach is adopted.

        Course Description and Main Objective
The  SMP  focuses  on  a  number  of  selected  management  functions.  Participants
eventually  integrate  these  into  a  group  project  which  they  present  to  a  panel  at
the end of the program.

        Learning Outcomes
After completion of the program, participants will be able to:

     Apply the principles underlying  entrepreneurship in  a business context
     Apply identified change models to real-life  situations and case studies
     Recommend ways of improving the  financial viability of organizations
     Assess the effect of economic drivers on  industries and companies
     Plan    and    execute    projects    and    processes    in       their    own     organizations     by
     utilizing      the        techniques       provided       by        systems       thinking        and       project
     management
     Develop an advanced marketing plan                   incorporating all the facets of a sound
      and comprehensive  marketing approach
     Develop a comprehensive business plan                    integrating all the disciplines of the
      program

Content
To   maximize   the   learning   experience   of   delegates,   the   program   is   divided   into
four workshops. The subjects on the SMP are as follows:
    Implementing Strategy
    Entrepreneurship
    Financial Management
    Leadership & Change Management
    Supply Chain Management
    Economics
    Design Thinking
    Marketing Management
    Information & Communications Technology
    Business Ethics and Corporate Governance
    Business Plan Template
    Presentations
       Faculty Member
The  UAEU  will  employ  faculty  with  superb  theoretical  knowledge  and  skills,  as
well  as  rich  practical  experience  to  ensure  that  delegates  complete  the  course
with developed practical skills and not just theoretical knowledge.
       Target Group
The majority of the participants in the SMP are middle and senior managers.
       Admission Requirements
No         formal         tertiary         qualifications         are         required.         Participants         should         have
appropriate  experience  and  a  basic  knowledge  of  the  different  program  topics.
The   SMP   is   presented   in   English   only.   It   is   preferred   that   participants   have   at
least        5        years        of        experience        at        middle        management        in        an        organization.
Participants  should  have  a  mastery  of  the  concepts  and  learning  content  of  the
Management         Development         Program         or         related         management         development
programs.
       Teaching Methods
    Workshops  will  be  presented  on  a              face-to-face   basis,   unless        synchronous
    online teaching is  mandated by the UAE Government
    Class discussions, case studies,  articles, and relevant videos
       Certificate
Delegates  who  successfully  complete  all  assignments  will  obtain  a  “Certificate
of    Successful    Completion”    by    demonstrating    their    knowledge,    understanding
and  skills  of  the  learning  outcomes  in  practical  assessments  during  and  at  the
end of the course.
