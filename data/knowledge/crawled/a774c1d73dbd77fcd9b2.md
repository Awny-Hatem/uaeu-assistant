## Textbooks Distribution Services for Students with Special Needs
Students with special needs can request to have their textbooks delivered to their hostels by completing the form and having it stamped by the hostel administration.

Students should send the completed form to the emails below:

Male students should submit the completed form to boys.tx@uaeu.ac.ae

Female students should submit the completed form to textbook-inquiries@uaeu.ac.ae

This service is available every semester during the book distribution period.

For further suggestions or inquiries, please contact us on: textbook-inquiries@uaeu.ac.ae .
