## Key Thematic Areas
The Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair is positioned to explore how existing research and practical knowledge can inform and enhance the field of sports science, physical activity, and health promotion. It aims to foster innovation, guide evidence-based practice, and create new insights that advance performance, well-being, and inclusivity in sport and physical activity. The fair also provides a platform to bridge the gap between academic research, professional practice, and community engagement in the field of sports and exercise sciences.
