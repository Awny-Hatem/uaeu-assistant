## Deputy VC for Finance & Admin. Affairs Message
The Finance and Administration Division supports the academic, research, and strategic objectives of United Arab Emirates University through an integrated framework that ensures the effective and responsible management of financial, human, and operational resources. The Division contributes to creating an enabling institutional environment that supports the University in fulfilling its mission and achieving its future aspirations.

The Division’s services include financial affairs and budgeting, human resources, facilities management, procurement, health and safety, as well as a range of essential administrative functions and services that support the University’s various divisions, colleges, centers, and units. These services ensure business continuity and enhance the efficiency of institutional operations.

We continuously work to develop the University’s administrative and financial framework, enhance the efficient utilization of resources, uphold the principles of governance, transparency, and accountability, and adopt leading institutional practices. These efforts enhance service quality and contribute to achieving the University’s strategic priorities.

We also continue to leverage modern technologies and artificial intelligence applications to enhance our services and operations, advance digital transformation, streamline procedures, improve operational efficiency, and provide reliable data and information to support informed decision-making. These efforts support the University’s aspirations toward a more agile, efficient, and sustainable institutional model.

At the Finance and Administration Division, we believe that institutional excellence begins with effective services and an integrated stakeholder experience. Accordingly, we place the needs of students, faculty, staff, and the wider University community at the heart of our efforts. We are committed to creating a safe, sustainable, and enabling university environment that supports education, research, and innovation.

We invite you to explore and benefit from the Division’s services. We also welcome your feedback, comments, and suggestions, which we consider an essential pillar of our continuous improvement journey and our efforts to enhance the quality of services provided to the United Arab Emirates University community.

Mr. Abdullah Khalifa Al Darmaki

Deputy VC for Finance & Admin. Affairs (Secretary General)
