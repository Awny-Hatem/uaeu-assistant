ABOUT CETL
The Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning (CETL) aims to enhance students’ learning experiences at UAE University, by
improving the way faculty and instructors teach their courses. CETL is composed of the following three units:

1.                 The Teaching Scholarship & Assessment Unit. This unit arranges comprehensive support for faculty who would like to conduct
and publish research on their teaching methodology. It also performs classroom observations for faculty who request informal feedback
about their teaching ability.
2.                  The Instructional Design Unit. This unit specialises in helping faculty to enhance the overall design and delivery of their courses,
by assisting them with the creation of interactive course content, updated curriculum design, and the support for transforming courses
to a ‘Smart’ or ‘Blended’ format.
3.                  Faculty Professional Development Unit. This unit focuses on providing professional development presentations, workshops, and
seminars, which focus on a variety of topics pertaining to emerging teaching pedagogies and innovative usage of educational
technology.


BLENDED COURSE TRANSFORMATIONS
The Blended Teaching & Learning initiative builds upon the ‘Smart Learning’ project by adding an online component to
the course transformation. This enables faculty to deliver some lessons via traditional face-to-face teaching, coupled with
other lessons that are taught via online instruction. This initiative is being managed by CETL with the support of the Smart
Teaching & Learning Committee.


eTEXTBOOKS
CETL has played a leading role in facilitating UAEU’s transition from traditional (printed) textbooks to the usage of digital
textbooks (i.e. ebooks). During 2017, with the support of CETL, approximately 40% of taught courses at UAEU
successfully made the transition to digital textbook usage. Another 40% of courses are going through this process in
2018, and it is hoped that all taught courses will use digital textbooks in 2019.
