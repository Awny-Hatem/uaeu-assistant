## UAEU Innovation Challenge Demo Day
Date: November 19 th , 2024

Time: 02:00 PM to 06:00 PM

Title: UAEU Innovation Challenge Demo Day

Description: The UAEU Innovation Challenge Demo Day marks the culmination of six months of intensive workshops, dedicated mentoring, and the hard work of our talented entrepreneurs. This event showcases the innovative projects developed by students and researchers from UAEU. Attendees have the opportunity to witness cutting-edge solutions and engage with the innovators behind them. The Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research (ECER) has joined as advisors to support the Science and Innovation Park’s important initiative.

In addition to the project demonstrations, the event features a panel discussion with esteemed guest speakers from Bartawi General Contracting, UAEU faculty, Mohammed Bin Rashid University of Medicine and Health Sciences (MBRU), and New York University Abu Dhabi (NYUAD). This discussion will provide valuable insights into the future of innovation and entrepreneurship in the UAE. An inspiring day of innovation and networking!
