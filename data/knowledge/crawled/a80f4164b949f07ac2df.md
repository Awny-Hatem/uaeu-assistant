## Evaluating and Enhancing Student Services at UAEU
Open Date : 02/01/2025 | Close Date: 04/30/2025

To engage students and the university community in assessing the quality and effectiveness of student services offered by the university and to gather suggestions for improvement to meet their needs and aspirations.

Description

As part of UAEU's efforts to enhance the quality of its student services and create an inclusive academic environment that supports students' experiences, a digital consultation will be launched. The aim is to gather feedback and evaluations from students, faculty, and alumni on the quality of the provided student services. These services encompass:

Outcomes

Enhance academic and social experiences through targeted improvements or additions.

Strengthen the university's role in meeting student needs, thereby increasing satisfaction and encouraging participation in campus activities.
