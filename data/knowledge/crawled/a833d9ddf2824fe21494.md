    THE UAE & SINGAPORE: FROM THIRD TO FIRST WORLD IN ONE GENERATION


 Date      :  09 September 2021, Thursday                                                         Time:  09h00 to 10h15
 Fee       :  Complimentary
 URL       :  URL to be obtained




The College of Business & Economics (CBE) of the UAE University has developed a seminar series during which
topics of relevance for the UAE and those interested in the UAE will be addressed. Experts in their fields will
be invited to convey their points of view.
The United Arab Emirates was formed on 2 December 1971 and will be celebrating its 50th anniversary on 2
December 2021. During this period, it has, through exemplary leadership, developed from a Third World
Country to a First World powerhouse. In a similar vein, Singapore became independent on 9 August 1965,
and also developed to a First World country. It has given rise to the saying, “from Third World to First World
in one generation.”
The talk will focus on the leadership and development paths of these two exceptional countries. A lot could
have gone wrong. Despite this, their respective s         tories serve as inspiration for developing countries globally
on  how  strong  leadership  and  sound  policy  frameworks  have  led to  stellar  performance on  so many
performance metrics, such as the World Bank’s Ease of Doing Business, the WEF’s Global Competitive                          ness
rankings, and the Global Innovation Index, to name but a few.              Attendees can expect to gain insights into the
following:
        The role and importance of directive leadership and sound governance                   in developing economies
        Understanding the development paths of the UAE and Singapore respectively
        Crystalizing lessons learnt for the road ahead
