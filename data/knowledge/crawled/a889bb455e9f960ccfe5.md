## Strategic Partners > UAEU Collaboration
The United Arab Emirates University proudly boasts an extensive array of dynamic, long-term strategic partnerships that are sure to captivate your attention. With a global reach, these alliances span governments, industries, and esteemed academic institutions. From pioneering research collaborations to innovative dual academic programs and enriching exchange agreements, our connections with universities worldwide are a testament to our commitment. With a network of over 82 countries, we continue to expand our global footprint, nurturing new bonds that drive our success. At the core of our achievements lies the essence of partnership, and UAEU eagerly awaits engagement from academia, government bodies, industries, and international research organizations.
