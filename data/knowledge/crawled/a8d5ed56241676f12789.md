## Message from the Chairperson of the Board of Trustees
On behalf of the Board of Trustees of the Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children, I welcome you all and am pleased to introduce the Center as an initiative dedicated to making a positive difference in the lives of exceptional children, including people of determination and gifted children. Providing quality education and support for exceptional children is an essential step toward helping them grow into confident individuals who can contribute meaningfully to society and participate in its development.

At Mouza Bint Suhail Center, we aim to provide high-quality services and support for exceptional children, guided by a clear vision inspired by the values of our leadership in the UAE. We are committed to creating an inclusive environment that empowers children of determination and gifted children through thoughtful programs and individualized care.

Believing in every child’s right to learn and thrive, the Center uses internationally recognized practices and builds strong partnerships with leading organizations and research institutions. Our programs combine education, care, and rehabilitation, and we are equally committed to preparing qualified national professionals in the field.

Our goal is to support children in reaching their potential, turning challenges into opportunities, and encouraging their creativity and achievements. Exceptional children need more than care; they need a community that believes in their abilities and shares in their success.

Finally, I extend my sincere thanks to all who support this mission. To everyone who contributes, supports, or carries forward the message of inclusion and empowerment, thank you. Together, we are creating a future full of opportunities for every child, because exceptional children deserve exceptional futures.

Sheikha Mouza bint Saif bin Zayed Al Nahyan,

Chair of the MBSCEC Board of Trustees

Apr 6, 2026
