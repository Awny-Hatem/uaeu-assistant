## Academic Advising Service
This service provides support and guidance to students throughout their university journey by advising them on the curriculum requirements, academic challenges they might face, and opportunities to develop strong leadership skills, and wise career choices.

Start Service

## Academic Advising Service > Procedure
Login to UAEU Log in to Portal or UAEU App via UAE Pass or UAEU email

Select E-Services

Select Academic Advising Service

Check/update required courses to register for each term

Submit for academic advisor's approval.
