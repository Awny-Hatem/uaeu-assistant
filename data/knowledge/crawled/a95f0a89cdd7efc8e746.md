## Welcome Message by the Chair of the Fair
It is my great pleasure to welcome you to the Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair 2026 . This event brings together professionals, researchers, students, and enthusiasts who share a passion for advancing knowledge and innovation in sport, exercise, and physical activity. Together, we celebrate collaboration, discovery, and the power of movement to improve lives and communities.

We are proud to host an exceptional lineup of exhibitors and presenters who bring cutting-edge research, creative solutions, and practical applications to the forefront. Their contributions not only showcase the latest developments in our field but also inspire meaningful discussions and future collaborations among participants.

I extend my sincere appreciation to our sponsors, partners, and organizing team for their dedication and support in making this event possible. May the Sports Science & Physical Activity Fair 2026 energize, inspire, and empower more active and healthy lifestyles in the United Arab Emirates and beyond.

With warm regards and best wishes for a successful conference,

Dr. Arto Grasten

Associate Professor, Chair of Physical Education Department

College of Education
