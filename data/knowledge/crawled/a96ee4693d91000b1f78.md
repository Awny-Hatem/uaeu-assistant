    Instructions Regarding Registration:

         Student in good academic standing may register 12 to 19 credit hours.
         Students who are under Probation cannot register for more than 13 credit hours.
         Honors students and students who expect to graduate in Spring Semester 2013/2014
         may register for up to 22 credit hours with college approval.
        Questions  regarding  section  capacities,  closed  sections  and  all  types  of  restrictions
         should be directed to your College Advising Units.
         Students who are failed in any of UFP course at the end of the Fall 2013/2014 will have
         to correct their registration.
         The student assumes total responsibility for securing his/her username and password
         and will be held responsible for any registration process that is done using it.
         After completing registration, the student should print his/her schedule for reference
         If you do not know your password please contact the Helpdesk at Tel: 03 -7136111.
