9. Discontinuation of Graduate Programs

Overview
Details  the  quality  assurance  framework  that  regulates  the                discontinuation      of  Graduate
Programs.

Scope
Applies to all Colleges and Departments of the UAEU.

Objective
Changing circumstances may dictate that a College discontinues a graduate program either for
operational or academic reasons. The University has identified key elements that ensure that
this process is carried out with due notice and without affecting the enrollment                         or rate of
progress    of  currently  enrolled  students.         These  elements  constitute  a  quality  assurance
framework that prescribes the requirements of proposals to discontinue graduate programs and
the processes through which they are approved.

Policy
1.  Changes in the needs of society and the interests of students require that the University has
    policies not only for the establishment of new graduate programs, but for discontinuation
    of programs that are no longer responsive to societal needs and student interests.  These
    policies ensure the wise stewardship of the University’s resources.

2.  The College of Graduate Studies has the primary responsibility for coordinating dis-
    continuation of existing graduate programs.  In this connection, the University must ensure
    adequate notice to students and faculty.

Procedures of Policy No. (9) - Discontinuation of Graduate Programs
    a)   The Dean of the College of Graduate Studies, assisted by the College deans, has the
         primary responsibility for coordinating, reviewing, and recommending proposals for the
         discontinuation of academic programs.
    b)   Discontinuation of programs may occur in two ways:  suspension of academic programs
         and termination of academic programs.


1. Suspension of Graduate Programs
    a)   The request for suspension of an academic program                  may be initiated at any of the
         administrative levels within the University.  Any of the following criteria may form the
         basis for suspension of a program:
         (i)   Evidence  that  the  program  is  no  longer  contributing  to  the  mission  of  the
               Department, College or University;
         (ii)   Substantial and significant decreases in student enrollment over a period of several
                years;
         (iii)  Failure of the program to graduate an average of three students per year over a
                three-year period;
         (iv)   Low academic quality as indicated by results of program review or loss of
                accreditation; or
         (v)    Insufficient human or financial resources to support the program.

    b.   Whenever a program or Department initiates the suspension of an academic program
         with the concurrence of the Department Chair and College Dean, the Dean of CGS must
         approve the request, after consultation with the Graduate Studies Council,                      and the
         Provost.  When program suspensions are initiated by the College dean, the Dean of CGS
         or the Provost, sufficient opportunity must be provided to the program or Department
         to demonstrate the need for continuation of the academic program.  In some cases,
         relocation of the program within the University or merger with other programs may be
         considered.

    c.   Suspension of a graduate program requires adequate notice.  Provision must be made to
         permit students in the program to complete requirements for the degree.  During
         program  suspension,  no  new  students  may  be  admitted                 to  the  program.    After
         appropriate review,      Dean of CGS and the Provost make recommendations for the
         suspension of academic programs.  Their recommendations for the suspension of
         academic programs are forwarded to the Vice Chancellor for final approval following
         review by the Academic Council.  Notice of the suspension of academic programs is

2. Termination of Graduate Programs
    a)   Termination of graduate programs may be initiated by the respective                          Department,
         College or University and occurs under either of two circumstances:
         (i)    Whenever  there  is  approval  by  the           Department,      College     and  University  on
                terminating a suspended program.
         (ii)   Whenever a graduate program remains under suspension for at least five years and
                no action is taken to reinstate the program.

    b)   Program  termination  requires  the  recommendation  of  the  Dean                        of  CGS,     after
         consultation   with   the   Graduate   Studies   Council,                and   the    Provost.      Their
         recommendations for the termination of academic programs are forwarded to the Vice
         Chancellor following review by the Academic Council.  The termination of academic
         programs is then recommended by the Vice Chancellor for approval to the Chancellor
         and the University Council.
