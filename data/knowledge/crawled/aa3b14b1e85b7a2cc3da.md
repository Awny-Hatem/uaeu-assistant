## High Energy Physics > Announcements > Exciting Opportunity: Join Our Experimental High Energy Physics Group at UAEU! 🌟
We are looking for a passionate and motivated PhD candidate to join the Experimental High Energy Physics Group at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU).

About the Role

The selected candidate will be part of our group working on cutting-edge research in collaboration with the ATLAS experiment at CERN. This is an excellent opportunity to contribute to groundbreaking projects and advance your career in high-energy physics.

Eligibility Criteria

A master’s degree in physics

IELTS/TOEFL certification (as per university requirements)

Prior knowledge in Python, Machine Learning, or other programming languages is preferred

For more information on admission criteria, please visit:

👉 UAEU Ph.D. Admission Criteria

If you need any assistance, please send your queries to Dr. Ammara Ahmad ( ammara.ahmad@uaeu.ac.ae )

This is your chance to be part of a dynamic research team and contribute to advancing high-energy physics on an international stage.
