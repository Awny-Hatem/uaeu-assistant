## Overview > Overview
The Student Learning Support Unit (SLSU) offers FREE academic support to UAEU students from the time they join the university through to the postgraduate level. The unit works closely with students, with the aim of helping them fulfil their academic potential and build the skills necessary to be successful in their future lives and careers.

SLSU contributes to improving retention rates and minimizing time to graduation, it also helps students develop as creative, successful independent learners. SLSU’s FREE programs, competitions, activities and events also provide students with opportunities to engage in campus life and make meaningful connections with groups and individuals outside their disciplinary areas, thus fostering a sense of belonging and community within the student body.

The unit works towards promoting student success university-wide through the Learning Centers . The centers - comprising the Tutorial, Speaking, Writing, and Technology Centers - support UAEU students university-wide, and at all academic levels. All members of the academic community are welcome to use our FREE services, which are available both face to face and online.

## Overview > Overview > Our Goals
Provide students with individual and group tutoring sessions, workshops and learning support materials that assist the development of disciplinary competence and associated skills.

Promote the development of academic literacy and communication skills through tutoring sessions, workshops, learning support materials, and other activities and services .

Provide orientation, and retention services enabling students to take ownership of their higher education opportunity and fulfil their academic potential.
