Statistical Consulting Unit
                                          Workshops/Training courses
                                                    Spring 2023

    Title: Power BI
    Statistical Software: Microsoft Power BI
    Requirements:
    Instructor: Dr. Chang Heon Lee
    Target Group: This workshop is designed for any users needing to learn essential MS
    Power BI skills or looking for a data visualization tool to make sense of corporate data.
    Date: 13 Feb. – 19 Feb.
    Period: 2 days, 4 hours/day
    Platform: Face to face Meeting
    Inclusive of Scientific Materials: PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.
    Workshop Fee: 1000 AED
    Contact US: statconsult@uaeu.ac.ae

    Description:
         Microsoft Power BI is a cloud-based business intelligence and analytics service that
         provides a full overview of your most critical data. In this Power BI workshop,
         participants will learn how to visually explore a diverse set of data through a free-form
         drag-and-drop canvas, a broad range of modern data visualization, and an easy-to-use
         report authoring experience.
    Topics Covered:
    Day 1: 1:00 pm - 5:00 pm, February 12th (Sunday)

         •   Introduction to Microsoft Power BI Platform
                  o   The introductory workshop is designed to explore the fundamentals of
                      Power BI interactive reports and the web-based Power BI service.
    Day 2: 1:00 pm - 5:00 pm, February 18th (Saturday)

         •   Learn Data Visualization using MS Power BI
                  o   This advanced module examines the Power BI features that are based in
                      advanced analytics and visualization. Data visualization is one of the
                      important parts of data analysis. Presenting the data in an understandable
                      and visually appealing format is essential for managers to communicate
                      better with.
