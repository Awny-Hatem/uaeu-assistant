## Graduate Programs > Master of Science in Software Engineering
The Master of Science in Software Engineering is a two-year graduate program that equips students with advanced knowledge and practical skills in modern software engineering, enhanced by contemporary Artificial Intelligence (AI) techniques. The curriculum covers system analysis and design, requirements engineering, software construction, testing and quality assurance, usability, and web technologies, while integrating AI-driven approaches to software processes and product quality. Through core courses, technical electives, and either a research thesis or a practicum project, graduates are prepared for professional roles in industry, research, and academia, supporting the UAEU Strategic Plan and advancing technological innovation and digital transformation in the region

## Graduate Programs > Master of Science in Software Engineering > Program Objectives
Assume technical and leadership roles in designing and delivering AI-enhanced software systems that serve industry and the community.

Apply professional, ethical, and responsible practices in the development and deployment of software and AI-driven solutions.

Advance expertise in software engineering and emerging AI technologies through continuous learning and professional growth.

Contribute to innovation and research that advance intelligent, data-driven software products, services, and knowledge.

Collaborate effectively across disciplines and cultures with academia, industry, and government at national and international levels.

## Graduate Programs > Master of Science in Software Engineering > Program Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

Apply software engineering knowledge, including appropriate AI techniques, to build robust, reliable, maintainable, and scalable software systems

Design intelligent complex systems based on efficiency, cost, and data availability.

Evaluate software systems and artifacts at different architectural and lifecycle levels, including AI-enabled components where appropriate

Analyze critically emerging software models, techniques, and technologies, particularly AI-related approaches.

Execute ethically project work or research contributing to AI-driven software engineering.

Demonstrate advanced oral and writing communication skills individually and collectively.
