  Meta Excellence Program
  This program is offered to COS students through UAEU’s
  Science and Innovation Park (SIP). The program’s objec-
  tives are:
  •     To    help    students    find    a    meaningful    job    in    their    field    of
        interest or to create their own job through entrepre-
        neurial training.
  •     To improve students soft-skills and to help them have
        a clearer career vision aligned with their personal pur-
        pose
  •     To secure meetings with external mentors to build their
        career roadmap
  The program has 4 training phases: Mindset Shif t, Private
  Sector Engagement, Focused-Track Employment or Entre-
  preneurship, and Incubation & Acceleration.
