ENROLLMENT DATA
    The following are the enrollment data of the College of Education CAEP programs –Early Childhood
Education (ECE), Special Education (SPED), and Health & Physical Education (HPE).
    A.   Program Enrollment Trend from 2017 to 2021

                   An increasing overall enrollment trend is observed from Fall2017 to Fall 2021 in all
         programs –ECE, SPED, and HPE. An average of 3% increase can be seen for ECE, 3% for SPED,
         and 31% for HPE, from Fall2017 to Fall2021.
                   From Spring2020 to Fall2020, there was a 5% increase in the overall enrollment and 2%
         increase from Fall2020 to Spring2021 for ECE. SPED maintained its overall enrollment from
         Spring2020 to Fall 2021 and 1% increase was seen during Spring2021. There was 25% increase in
         the overall enrollment in the HPE program from Spring2020 to Fall2020. It was able to maintain
         the figures from Fall2020 to Spring2021 (see Figure 5.1).

C.   Fall 2020- Fall 2021 Enrollment by Nationality

               In Fall2020 and Spring2021, majority of the admitted and enrolled students in the ECE,
     SPED, & HPE programs are Emirati (see Figures 5.5, 5.6, & 5.7). It is important to mention that
     UAEU is committed towards promoting the enrolment of students from diverse cultural
     backgrounds. In view of this, the Admission Office of the university participates in education
     fairs organized across the country as a way of attracting students from other nationalities to
     pursue degree programs in the university. As part of this program, the college of education sent
     representatives from each department to interact with prospective candidates about
     requirements, teaching areas and career prospects. In 2021, two of such programs (Naja Expo,
     Abu Dhabi and Sharjah Expo) were organized in the country which the university and CEDU took
     part to interact with the prospective candidates.
