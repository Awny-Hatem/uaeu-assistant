## UAEU Alumni Award > UAEU Alumni Award for Community Engagement
This award aims to recognize the achievements of United Arab Emirates University graduates who provide great services to society through the activities, programs and initiatives that they undertake, and to emphasize their active role in providing work that contributes to the development of society in all its institutions. In addition to creating a spirit of competition and consolidating the concept of social responsibility among community members.

Conditions

This award is given to distinguished graduates who meet one or more of the following:

His positive impact on society is demonstrated through his work related to social innovation.

Active participation in social and voluntary projects, humanitarian efforts and community development.

Has valuable contributions towards achieving sustainable development goals locally, regionally or internationally.

Has a tangible and clear impact on the development of society through teaching, academic research, administration, educational practices, or all of them.

Criteria

1- Excellence and Achievement (300 points)

This criterion focuses on the noteworthy and exceptional accomplishments of graduates throughout their careers. It also focuses on the quantity and quality of these accomplishments and whether they have resulted in continued success and contribution to their profession at the local, national and / or international levels.

2- Creativity and Innovation (200 points)

This criterion focuses on the level of distinction, exclusivity and innovation, as well as the uniqueness of the graduate's ideas and / or achievements. It also focuses on how the achievements of graduates have resulted in positive change or significant impact on society through creativity and innovation.

3- Support of UAEU (150 points)

This criterion focuses on the level of support provided to the United Arab Emirates University. Supporting the initiatives of the United Arab Emirates University through continuous engagement with the United Arab Emirates University, and actively participating in events or activities organized / approved by the university and / or submitted to the university in the form of endowment or financial contributions.

4- Level of contributions (local, regional and international) (150 points)

This criterion focuses on the outstanding contributions that alumni have made to local, regional and international communities, through professional activities, creative and innovative practices and ideas, which have had a significant impact. This effect can include professional and societal achievements.

5- Impact on Others / Adding Values ​​ (200 points)

This criterion focuses on the significant impact on others as a result of the graduates' outstanding achievements in community service in one chosen area (award category). The great impact on society is the result of continuous contribution of great time and effort to improve and develop the community.
