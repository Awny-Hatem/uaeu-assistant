This letter serves as formal notification and official record of action taken concerning                                   United
Arab Emirates University (UAEU)by the WASC Senior College and University Commission
(WSCUC) at its meeting February 22, 2019. This action was taken after consideration of the
report of the review team that conducted the Special Visit to  UAEU October 23-25, 2018.
The Commission also reviewed the institutionalreport and exhibits                                                   submitted by UAEUprior
to the Special Visitand the institution’s                                      December 19, 2018response to the team report. The

Paul Zingg, President Emeritus, California State University Chico and UAEU Board
Member.Your                       comments were ve ry helpful                                                                   in informing the Commission’s deliberations.
The date of this action  constitutes the effective date of the institution’s new status with
WSCUC.

Actions

       1.                                                            Receive the Special Visit team report that focused on: (1) governance; (2) faculty
              turnover; (3) affirmative action policy; (4) library; (5) English proficiency; and (6)
              graduate study and research resources
       2.                                                            Continue with the previously scheduled reaffirmation review with the Offsite Review
              in spring 2021 and Accreditation Visit in fall 2021
       3.                                                              Continue with the previously scheduled Mid-Cycle Review                                                    to begin May 1,2019

The Commission            commends UAEUin particular for the following:

       1.                                                            Exemplary attention to all WSCUC recommendations outlined in the 2016
              Commission Action Letter.
       2.                                                            Detailed analysis of the issue of faculty turnover, including an in-depth disaggregated
              st ud y.
       3.                                                            Support of Library initiatives, beginning with its recruitment and hiring of ke y
              leadership positions, andfunding for the design of detailed space planning that will
              result in positive changes in the user experience.
       4.                                                            The smooth transition in senior leadership of the university (chancellor,vice                                                             -
              chancellor, provost).
       5.                                                            The development of an ambitious strategic plan which seems to have strong campus
              buy-in and an impressive tracking system.

1.                                                            Strengthen governance by making the University Council more effective and by
               differentiating between its oversight role and that of university administration. (CFRs
               3.7 and 3.9)
       2.                                                            Ensure that consistency is maintained as more authority is delegated to deans in terms
               of salary and teaching load. (CFR 3.2)
       3.                                                            Evaluate (and revise as necessary) human resources administrative policies and
               procedures which may impede recruitment, hiring, and procurement. (CFRs 3.2 and
               3.6)
       4.                                                            Streamline procedures for supporting research. (CFR 3.7)
       5.                                                            Assess the effects of the increase in English Language Proficiency Standard test
               scores. (CFRs 4.1 and 4.3)
       6.                                                            Address the shortfalls in Library resources and staff funding by establishing new
               permanent baseline funding. (CFR 3.5)
       7.                                                            Diversify revenue streams in order to support the goals of increased research and
               graduate education. (CFR 3.4)
