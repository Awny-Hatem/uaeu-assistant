## Honor Student Council
The Honor Program is directed by the Honor Student Council, composed of:

An appointed Representative of the College of Science (Faculty).

A Director (a senior Honor student), and an Advisory Board which consists of five Honor students (one student from each Major).

Getting students involved will help to shape the future of the Honor Program. The Honor Council administrate the program requirements, assist in:

Recruitment and admissions to the Honor Program.

Organize activities and events that enhance the student experience, and alumni activities.

The Honor Council also maintains the organization of an Annual Honor Day.

The Director of the Council will also be an Honor ambassador that will represent the Honor Program of the college at a variety of internal and external campus events.
