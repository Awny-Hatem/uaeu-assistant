## Message of Al Mutaqa’s Head
We recognize that achieving leadership and excellence in providing services to UAEU’s employees and their families requires our attention to details and is important to have a good effect on the quality of the UAEU employees' performance. This believe has drove us to establish the University Social Club at UAEU with the aim of enhancing the relationships among UAEU faculty staff and their families and provide them with the opportunity to get involved in social, sports, cultural and artistic activities in a family atmosphere.

We always work to find effective channels of communication at the university to enhance the teamwork spirit in every part of our work or achievement in addition to opening avenues of dialogue and continuous communication among UAEU community and the external society.

Your contributions and suggestions are vital to achieve creativity and innovation in providing our high quality services that meet expectation of the UAEU community.
