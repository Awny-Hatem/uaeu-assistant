## Job Interview Preparation
Workshop Title: Job Interview Preparation

Date: Thursday , 30 July 2026

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:30 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Mr. Jihad Mustafa

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: The main goal of this workshop is to help you approach job interviews with confidence and make a positive, lasting impression. The workshop will cover:

Key aspects of interview readiness and preparation.

Best practices for showcasing your skills and strengths with confidence and professionalism while overcoming hesitation and anxiety.

Common interview mistakes that may affect your job prospects and how to avoid them.

Different types of interview questions and the purpose behind each one.
