## Department of Biology > Overview
The Biology Department is committed to providing solutions to the community through education, research, and partnerships with various organizations. The faculty and staff of the Biology Department are dedicated to delivering a quality education and experience to all the students.

We offer an outstanding and robust curriculum, in addition to a wide-scope of opportunities for mentoring, as a way of developing a comprehensive understanding of biology at the levels of molecules, cells, organisms and the environmental sciences.

We focus on the development of critical thinking and problem solving skills both in the classroom and in research. In addition to this, our graduate students can select from a number of experienced faculties and can explore interdisciplinary research in the areas of Environmental sciences, Biotechnology, genetics and engineering, among other disciplines.

We are also extremely proud that our Bachelor of Science in Biology program has received international accreditation by the Royal Society of Biology (RSB). This prestigious recognition, valid for five more years (ie. 2019-2029), underscores the program’s commitment to providing a high-quality education and preparing graduates for rewarding careers in the biological sciences.

The new University campus is equipped with state-of-art laboratories. The Biology Department continuously strives to match best internal standards. We welcome you and invite you to explore our Department website.

The Department of Biology provides an exciting learning environment for students. The department has a dedicated and professional team of faculty and staff who continuously update the academic courses and enrich the students’ experience and skills. We strive to reach out to the wider UAE community through research and development endeavors. The faculty members are involved in research projects spanning all fields of biological sciences. The department is proud to have been one of the founders of the Master Program in Environmental Sciences. Graduates from this program have been serving the UAE Community for over a decade.

Our objectives are:

To prepare students who can provide solutions to challenges in health, environment, energy, food and agriculture.

To be among the best teaching/research department in the region.

To prepare our students for future careers that serve the needs of the UAE and the region.

To prepare the students to fulfill their own personal dreams of a graduate education at a first class institution.

To generate world class research and to continue our collaborations with international scientists and agencies.

The Biology Department provides its students with a superb learning and research experience. The faculty members utilize high-tech labs armed with a state-of-the-art equipment enabling research from gene to genome. The laboratories house a vast array of equipment such as: an ion-torrent genome sequencer, traditional and real time PCR thermo cyclers, sanger DNA sequencers, microscopes, microbiology facilities, aquatic research tools, deep freezers, ovens, eliza readers, gel documenting systems, and an array of other equipment required to conduct research. The Biology Department laboratories are located in buildings E3 and F1.

The Biology Department can provide a range of consultancies in different areas. In the past the department has always provided solutions to the community through partnership and consultation. The areas of expertise within the department are:

Genomic studies of organism including humans.

Microbiological analysis of water and sewage effluents and microbiological surveillance of infectious diseases.

Genetic classification of wildlife of plants and animals.

Diagnosis of plant diseases, soil microbiology, soil fertility, and organic farming.

Scientific consultancy in marine and fresh water ecological issues.

Consultations on desertification issues.

Training on managing rangelands.

Training on applications of GPS and GIS in desert ecosystems.

Training and consultancy in plant biotechnology.

Training in DNA sequencing including new generation sequencing

Measuring plants antioxidant potentials and biological assay of oxidative stress markers in animal tissues.

Blood hematological biochemical analysis, and seroprevalence studies.

Water-borne diseases, toxoplasma gondii studies.

Malaria immunology and epidemiology.

Phytoplankton culture especially chlorella culture.

Fish behavior study projects.

DNA finger printing in plants and animals.

Protein banding pattern, DNA extraction and purification, database manipulation for DNA and protein alignment and prediction of a protein structure.

Identification of new drug targets for pharmaceutical applications.

Assessment of plant toxicity, assessment of protective potential of local plants.

Microbiological analysis of water, isolation and identification of cyanobacteria and other micro-algae, soil analysis, T and M typing of group A streptococci.

Pollutants monitoring, epidemiology of infectious diseases.

Clinical diagnostic microbiology, PCR techniques, PFGE techniques, cloning techniques.

High performance liquid chromatography.

Plant identification classification, and plant materials analysis.

Environmental impact assessment in the different fields of wild plants, rangelands, biodiversity and conservation.

Examining samples of infectious parasites.

Search for bioactive molecules in natural products and medicinal plants.
