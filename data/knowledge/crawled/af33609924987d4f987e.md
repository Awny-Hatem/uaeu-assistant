12. PhD Scholarships and Fellowships, and Graduate Assistantships

Overview
Defines terms, conditions and eligibility criteria associated with scholarships, fellowships and
assistantships offered by the University to graduate students.
The University awards Scholarships, Fellowships and Assistantships to graduate students for
two main reasons: (1) To enhance students’ access to graduate education by helping the
students financially and, hence, make their graduate education more affordable and (2) to
further the professional development of students as future teachers and researchers by
engaging them in teaching and research experiences in their areas of studies.

Scope
Applies to all eligible  graduate, degree-seeking students at UAEU.

Policy

Definition and Explanation of Terms

PhD Scholarships
1.                                                      PhD Scholarships are awards designated for students of UAE nationality who are enrolled
     full-time in a PhD program at UAEU. PhD Scholarships are awardedbased on evidence of
     academic achievement and potential for research. The University sets the criteria for
     awarding PhD Scholarships, and the University determines the number of PhD
     Scholarships it will make available in a given year.  Recipients of scholarships are
     required to provide teaching support                 services to the University during the term of their
     award.  It is the responsibility of the individual Colleges to ensure that each scholarship
     recipient is assigned the appropriate teaching dut y.
2.                                       Other providers (whether a foundation or any other external organization or individual)
     may fund additional PhD Scholarships. They may set specific conditions for recipient
     selection in addition to the University’s criteria. If this is the case, an agreement between
     that party and UAEU must be approved prior to granting any scholarships sponsored by
     the donor.
3.                                        Scholarship awards cover, at a minimum: (i) full cost of tuition and other fees, (ii) full
     cost of housing accommodation, (iii) a monthly stipend to cover living expenses, and (iv)
     health insurance for the awardee.     Based on the available resources, the University may
     also provide PhD candidates basic support for their dissertation research. Such support
     may       be complemented, depending on the research requirements,                          through       internal or
     external grants by the PhD advisor and the PhD student.
