## Graduate Degree Programs
Welcome to the Graduate Programs webpage at the United Arab Emirates University, the nation's most comprehensive higher education institution.

UAEU offers a diverse range of graduate degree programs at the master's and doctorate levels, catering to both professional and research-based orientations. While most graduate programs are offered at the main campus in Al Ain, some professional programs are also conveniently offered in Abu Dhabi. UAEU admits students in Master and Doctorate programs on full-time and part-time basis.
