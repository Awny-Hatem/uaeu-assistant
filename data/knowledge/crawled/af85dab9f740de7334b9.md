                                                                                                Definition of Terms


I. Policy

                       The terms and expressions used in the policies and procedures have the
                       following meanings, unless otherwise indicated in the text:

                       Chancellor:  The Chancellor of the United Arab Emirates University.

                       Academic Year:  A one-year period established annually in the official
                       University calendar.

                       Arab Employees:  Employees who hold the passport of an Arab country in
                       which their UAE residence visa is placed.  An Arab country is one that is a
                       member of the Arab League of Nations.

                       City of Residence:  The city within the country of residence to which airline
                       tickets will be provided.  (Refer to Country of Residence.)

                       Contract Year:  A one-year period commencing on the employee's date of
                       appointment and terminating on the day preceding the following anniversary
                       date.

                       Country of Residence: The location designated by the employee where
                       he/she has his/her normal place of residence, having a right to reside
                       permanently in that country and having a passport of that country in which is
                       placed his/her UAE residence visa.  This location will be stated in the
                       employment contract and is not normally subject to change throughout the
                       duration of the contract.

Day:  The word day always refers to a calendar day, unless otherwise
              specified.

              Eligible Dependents:  Eligible dependents are your spouse and:
              •                       The biological children of the employee or the employee’s spouse
                     who are not above eighteen years of age.
              •                       The biological children of the employee or the employee’s spouse
                     who are over 18 and up to 23 years of age and who are continuing
                     their studies full-time until the end of the first university degree.  This
                     portion of the definition does not apply for Health Insurance.

              Employee:  The terms employee,       he,      him and his all refer to both male and
              female employees, unless otherwise indicated.

              Expatriate Employee:  Any employee who is not a National.

              He/His:  Refers to either male or female employees.

              Immediate Family:  An employee’s immediate family for leave application
              purposes shall consist of spouse, son, daughter, brother, sister, father, mother,
              father/mother of spouse and grandparents.

              Law:  The Federal Decree law concerning the establishment and organization
              of the UAE University.

              National Employees:  Employees who are citizens of the United Arab
              Emirates.

              Non-Arab Employees:  Employees other than Arab employees.

              Pensionable Salary:  The salary used in connection with retirement
              deductions for employees who are UAE Nationals.

              Tuition Fees: Those fees detailed by the school on an official invoice to cover
              education fees as per current policy and procedures.  Tuition fees are those
              rates approved by the Ministry of Education.

              Work Day: A work day is a day on which an employee is normally scheduled
              to work.

              Working Hours for Staff:  Normal working hours are eight hours of work
              per day, forty hours of work per week.  Working hours may be modified by
              shift work and other needs of the University.


II.                                                                                 Application

              This policy shall apply to all faculty, staff or other appointments at the United
              Arab Emirates University.
