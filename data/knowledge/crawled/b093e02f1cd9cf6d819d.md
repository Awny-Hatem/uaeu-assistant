## ARCH313 Analysis and Design Principles for Building Structures
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_2898.shtml?id=ARCH313
The course presents the basic skills and techniques required for analysis and design of structural elements as members in a complete building structural system. The course discusses the preliminary sizing of members, calculation of design loads, and structural member forces calculation. In addition, the course covers equilibrium, reactions, member forces, and deflections for trusses, frames, and various structural elements of building system. It introduces students to design process of structural systems in buildings.

## ARCH525 Life Cycle Assessment in the Built Environment
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_2898.shtml?id=ARCH525
This course introduces the principles and applications of Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) in the built environment. It examines how environmental impacts are evaluated across the life cycle of materials, systems, and projects, from raw material extraction and manufacturing to use, maintenance, and end-of-life. Students are introduced to key LCA concepts, standards, system boundaries, functional units, inventory data, and impact categories. The course also explores environmental product declarations (EPDs), whole-project LCA, and links to circular economy and sustainability frameworks. Through lectures, case studies, and practical exercises, students learn to interpret LCA results and apply life-cycle thinking to design and decision-making.

## ARCH715 Energy Optimization in Built Environment Design
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_2898.shtml?id=ARCH715
This course is oriented towards the practical solution of optimization problems arising when designing an energy system or component applicable to the built environment. The general methodologies are provided, useful for a wide range of problem scales, with a focus on multi-variable mono-objective and multi-objective problems. These may include Optimum energy use strategies for buildings / cities, hourly energy simulation methods, building envelope and HVAC system energy optimization by computer simulation techniques; life-cycle cost analysis of building energy systems; case studies in building and urban applications

Develop a design or retrofit proposal that integrates state of the art environmentally friendly technologies from the perspective of sustainable development

## ARCH716 Advanced Intelligent Built Environment Systems

## ARCH850 Candidacy Exam
Official course record: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/courses/course_2898.shtml?id=ARCH850
The Candidacy Exam for the PhD in Architectural engineering is a non-credit Pass/Fail exam. Students have up to two trials to pass the course; upon passing the exam, the student becomes a PhD candidate. The course is structured to evaluate the student’s breadth and depth of fundamental knowledge in the area(s) closely related to the thesis work. The course committee, in consultation with the advisor, decides on the topic(s) of the course. The PhD student prepares a concise and comprehensive research proposal that clearly defines the research problem and objectives and outlines the research methodology that he/she plans to follow. The concerned PhD student should present and defend his/her proposal in front of the course committee.
Candidacy Exam (ARCH850) > Credit Hours : 0

Candidacy Exam (ARCH850) > Course Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, students will be able to :

Demonstrate the depth and breadth of their knowledge in the field of architectural engineering relevant to student’s research topic.

Propose a well-structured research project that outlines clear objectives, a detailed plan, and an appropriate methodology.

Demonstrate sufficient communication skills relevant to field of study and the research topic.
