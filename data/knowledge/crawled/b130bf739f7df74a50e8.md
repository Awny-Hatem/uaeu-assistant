                                                                                           Academic Records


I.                                                                                              Policy

                     The United Arab Emirates University shall establish procedures and practices
                     to record, maintain and secure students’ academic records.

                     The Admission and Registration Department will keep a file for each student
                     that contains all relevant documents.  The academic advising unit in each
                     college will maintain records for all students enrolled in the college.

                     Change or release of information from the student file will require permission
                     from the dean of the college or the head of the Admission and Registration
                     Department.  Grade changes require the approval of the dean and department
                     chair.


II.                                                                              Application

                     This policy shall apply to the academic records of all students at the United
                     Arab Emirates University.
