## The Great Student Competition > General Terms and Conditions
Participants should stick to the approved topics humanity , society or national message . or another approved topic suggested by students.

Students can only participate in one category and work/activity.

Participation is individual except for the short movie features and music groups.

The performance should be exclusively for the competition only.

It must be the student’s own work.

Registration through the official website, and submit your work through Registration Link .

No entry will be accepted after the deadline.

The student should briefly present their piece during an interview.

The finalists should provide a different performance at each level of the competition.

The finalists must attend every stage or be excluded.

The participants agree to appear in the media if they progress.

The university has the right to keep performance material and to publish it for promotional purposes. Students maintain intellectual property.

The participants guarantee that their work is original and does not violate the intellectual property rights of others. All work is completely that of the performer.

Participants cannot present or publish any of their work in any other competition, inside or outside the UAE, before or after the awards ceremony without prior written permission from the organizing committee.

The committee sets the competition standards and its decisions are final.

The participant cannot appeal the result. The result is final and not open for discussion.

Students will be disqualified from the competition, and ineligible for any awards, if they do not follow any rules or the terms and conditions.
