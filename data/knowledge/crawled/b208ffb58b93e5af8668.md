## The Great Student Competition > Theater (Monodrama)
To Register: submit video of no longer than one minute.

The theme should be suggested by the general topics (humanity, society or national message) or any other approved topic.

The competitor must submit the scenario to the competition committee at least 3 days before the audition.

The competitor can use scenario written by other authors.

The competitor must be able to show the message to the audience by speech / gesture / signal / body movement / silence / internal emotion, etc.).

The evaluation will be based on the competitor’s theater performance.
