HUMAN CAPITAL
  RESEARCH CENTER’S
       RESEARCH AWARD




  The Human Capital Research Center (HCRC) is pleased to launch an initiative for the year
   2023/2024 to motivate UAEU researchers to publish research papers in areas related to
                  the mission of the center in reputable peer-reviewed journals

                                               SUBMIT    NOW



  OBJECTIVE
Conduct research that contributes to the development of human capital in the UAE and beyond.

  RESEARCH AREA
Focus on enhancing human capital and developing qualified national competencies to meet the labor
market's future demands at the local, regional, and global levels.

We encourage the UAEU faculty and researchers to direct their research agenda toward human
capital and education areas to meet the HCRC objectives. This initiative includes the following:

   UAEU researchers publishing relevant research papers in reputable peer-reviewed journals and including
   the affiliation of HCRC will be entitled to receive the following awards.

        Corresponding Author of a Paper                       Graduate Student, First Author of a Paper

           in the Top 1%   22,000 AED                                 in the Top 5%   2,000 AED
           in the Top 5%   12,000 AED                                 in the Top 10%  1,500 AED
           in the Top 10%  7,000 AED                                  in the Top 25%  1,000 AED
           in the Top 25%  4,500 AED
           in the Top 35%  3,000 AED

   The HCRC covers the publication fees for the accepted articles.
   The journals should be listed in SCOPUS and/or Web-of-Science databases.
   The committee of the HCRC decides the appropriateness of the research papers to the HCRC objectives.
   The duration of this program might be extended based on the recommendation of the committee.


                    This motivation system is temporary and valid until      31st
                                                                             August 2024

HCRC    Research    Award
   Guidelines


                               Only current faculty and researchers who continue their employment at UAEU at the time of the
                              recognition will be awarded.
APPLICATION                   Graduate students are entitled to receive the awards within one year of their graduation and only if
ELIGIBILITY                   they maintain a residency status in the UAE.


                              Current graduate students, whether as corresponding authors or co-authors, are eligible to be
                              recognized under the motivation system.


                           Submission to HCRC
     1                        By submitting a research paper to a journal, the researcher must first fill the form and attach the
                              paper in the SharePoint.

                           Evaluation by HCRC:
                                The   committee   of   the   HCRC   will   review   the   submitted   papers   to   assess   their   relevance   and
     2                          alignment  with  the     objectives  of  the  HCRC  and  to  identify  publications  in  the  top  1%,  5%,  10%,
                                25%, and 35% journals.

                                Once a paper is accepted by the HCRC committee, corresponding authors who list the center as
                                their affiliation will be eligible for recognition in the top 1%, 5%, 10%, 25%, and 35% categories for
                                publications only in the same year as the paper’s publication.
      3                    Submission to Journal:
                              Once the paper is accepted by the HCRC, the researcher can submit it to the intended journal for
                              reviews.


                               If the paper is accepted by the journal, the HCRC will cover the publication fees associated with
                              the paper (no reimbursement).
PUBLICATION
FEES                          For the journals that offer limited access and open access, limited access should be selected. The
                              HCRC will not cover the open access option fees if limited access is offered by the journal.

                              The  HCRC  covers  the  publication  fees  for  the  accepted  articles,  provided  they  are  not  associated
                              with a funded project.


                              At the end of each calendar year's fourth quarter, the HCRC will award the researchers who have
                              successfully published their papers.

                              Researchers awarded from the HCRC               will not be eligible to receive awards from the Office of the
                              Associate Provost for Research (OAPR) for the same paper.

                              Only     corresponding     authors     from     the     UAEU     who     list     the     center     as     their     affiliation     will     be
                              recognized in the 1%, 5%, 10%, 25%, and 35% categories.

DISTRIBUTION                  In   cases   where   there   are   co-corresponding   authors   from   the   UAEU,   the   award   will   be   divided
                              equally  between  the  UAEU  faculty  members.  However,  if  only  one  corresponding  author  is  from
                              the UAEU, he/she will receive a proportional financial incentive.

                              Each submitted paper is eligible for only one award.

                              Each researcher is eligible to receive a maximum of two motivation awards in a year.

                              If a publication belongs to a journal with a higher overall ranking among all journals or in a specific
                              subject     area     or     subcategory,     the     publication     will     be     categorized     according     to     the     highest
                              applicable ranking.

                              If  a  publication  falls  within  the  top  15%  of  journals,  it  will  be  categorized  as  part  of  the  top  25%
                              ranking.
