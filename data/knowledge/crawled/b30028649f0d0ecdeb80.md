## Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research > Overview
The Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research at UAEU fosters entrepreneurial thinking and innovation. It combines research, teaching, and practical initiatives to support students and professionals alike. The center serves as a hub for world-class research and guidance on entrepreneurship and innovation policy. It connects scholars, practitioners, and policymakers to enhance UAEU’s impact regionally and internationally. Through education, research, and practice, the center drives sustainable growth and entrepreneurial development.

## Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research > Vision and Mission > Mission
We act as a research-driven institutional agent to aggregate scholars, practitioners, and policymakers

to enhance UAEU research, education, and policies in entrepreneurship and innovation.

## Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research > Vision and Mission > Vision
A hub for conducting and disseminating world-class research and providing advice for entrepreneurship and innovation policy and practice.

## Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research > Director's Message
Entrepreneurship makes us think of new ventures, the seeds for future growth and development, and entrepreneurial attitudes are fundamental to initiating and supporting change in companies and public institutions. UAEU has long pioneered entrepreneurial education in the GCC region, supporting its students with rigorous academic knowledge and updated theoretical and practical learning opportunities. The Emirates Center for Entrepreneurship Research builds on this original vision by combining research, teaching, and practice to strengthen its impact in the UAE and abroad.”
