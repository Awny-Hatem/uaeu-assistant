Overview
The Institute of Public Health (IPH) at the College of Medicine and Health
Sciences (CMHS) of the United Arab Emirates University plays a pivotal role
in cultivating excellence through         actively contributing to strengthening the
health system and enhancing public health outcomes through a tripartite
approach encompassing teaching, research, and service.
At the undergraduate level, IPH is instrumental in shaping the next generation
of medical professionals through its integration into the six-year MD program.
Moreover, IPH offers two advanced degrees tailored to meet the evolving
needs of the healthcare landscape: the Master of Public Health (MPH) and
the Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Public Health. These programs not only
foster academic rigor but also nurture critical thinking, leadership skills, and
a commitment to evidence-based practice, preparing graduates to tackle
complex public health challenges with confidence and efficacy.

advanced research methodologies, epidemiology, and biostatistics, as well as
public,  occupational,  and  environmental  health.  Its  research  portfolio  is

studies, along with intervention research targeting critical health issues such
as  non-communicable          and    communicable        diseases,  maternal  and  child

              Research Methodology and Study Designs
       From crafting research questions to designing study protocol

Overview
This workshop is tailored for healthcare professionals, researchers, and individuals
engaged in scientific inquiry. The workshop is designed to provide a comprehensive
exploration of research methodology and study designs, offering participants a solid
foundation in planning, conducting, and interpreting research studies. The workshop
covers a range of research methodologies and study designs, equipping participants
with the knowledge and skills needed to navigate the intricacies of the research
process. This workshop is designed to guide participants from the inception of their
research questions to the development of the study protocol.
Aim
The aim of this workshop is to master the concepts and methodologies behind
different study designs utilized in epidemiology, public health, and clinical research,
covering the following:
     ➢     Designing Your Research Question and Objectives
               ▪   Learn to formulate clear, focused research questions and objectives.
               ▪   Understand the importance of a well-defined research scope.
     ➢     Choosing the Right Study Design
               ▪   Explore    various    study    designs,    including    observational    and
                   experimental approaches.
               ▪   Gain insights into the selection criteria for different study designs.
     ➢     Data Collection Methods and Tools
               ▪   Overview of data collection techniques and tools.
               ▪   Practical guidance on selecting the most appropriate methods for
                   your study.
     ➢     Interpretation of Findings
               ▪   Strategies for interpreting research findings effectively.
     ➢     Case studies of published articles to navigate through real-world scenarios
           to enhance your analytical skills.
     ➢     Critical  appraisal  of  published  articles  to  navigate  through  real-world
           scenarios  to    critically  assess  the  quality  and  strength  of  the  published
           evidence.

Overview
Biostatistical analyses and interpretation are an integral part of scientific research.
From design to interpretation, in this workshop you will embark on a journey into the
world of Biostatistics with our comprehensive workshop designed for healthcare
professionals, researchers, and individuals seeking proficiency in statistical analysis
for biomedical and health sciences. The workshop is tailored to provide a balanced
blend of theoretical foundations and practical application through hands-on practice.

Aim
The   workshop       aims    to   empower   participants   by   enhancing   their   skills   in
comprehending   both   fundamental   and   advanced   principles   of   biostatistical
procedures employed in biomedical and health research. This aim will be achieved
through covering the following components:
       ➢     Introduction to key biostatistical concepts and terminology.
       ➢     Understanding the role of statistics in healthcare research.
       ➢     Exploring measures of central tendency and dispersion.
       ➢     Hands-on practice in creating impactful visualizations for data exploration.
       ➢     Delving into hypothesis testing and confidence intervals.
       ➢     Understanding p-values, statistical significance, and practical significance.
       ➢     Application of statistical techniques specific to the biomedical field.
       ➢     Regression analysis, survival analysis, and categorical data analysis.
       ➢     Hands-on sessions using widely used statistical software (e.g., R, SPSS, or
             SAS).
       ➢     Step-by-step   guidance   on   data   manipulation,   analysis,   and   result
             interpretation.
       ➢     Strategies for effective interpretation and communication of statistical
             findings.

 Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analysis in Evidence-Based
                                           Medicine
 Theoretical and Practical Aspects with Hands-on Practice to Enhance
                     Evidence Utilization and Implementation

Overview
Systematic reviews and meta-analyses (SRs & MAs) studies are considered to be at
the  top  of  the  hierarchy  of  scientific  evidence  due   to  their  rigorous  and
comprehensive  approach  to  synthesizing  research  finding.               SRs  &  MAs     serve  as
invaluable  tools  for  translating  research  findings  into  actionable  insights.             They
contribute to evidence-based practice, policy formulation, and the advancement of
scientific   knowledge,   collectively   working   towards   improving   the   quality   of
healthcare delivery and outcomes.            These studies are widely used by healthcare
practitioners, health policy makers, and researchers as a strong source of:
        ➢    Evidence-Informed Decision-Making process.
        ➢    Updating Existing and Developing New Clinical Guidelines.
        ➢    Continuous Professional Development.
        ➢    Identifying gaps in scientific evidence.
Aim
The aim of this workshop is to master the concepts and methodological aspects
behind this powerful research and evidence-based synthesis technique by achieving
the following learning outcomes:
        ➢    Understanding the principles and significance of systematic reviews and
             meta-analysis in evidence-based research.
        ➢    Gaining     insights   into   the   step-by-step   process   of        designing   and
             conducting systematic reviews.
        ➢    Learning     the   intricacies   of   meta   -analysis   and   its   applications      in
             quantitative research.
        ➢    Practical   Application      and    Hands-on   practice   sessions   to   reinforce
             theoretical concepts.
        ➢    Critical appraisal of published systematic reviews and meta                -analysis to
             navigate through real-world scenarios to critically assess the quality and
             strength of the published evidence.

  Operational Research and Translating Research Findings
                              into Policy and Practice
     Bridging the Gap Between Research Endeavors and Real-world
                                       Implementation


Overview
Operational Research is a discipline that uses analytical methods to make better
decisions  and  solve  complex  problems.  It  involves  the  application  of  specific
techniques to decision-making processes within organizations to improve efficiency,
effectiveness,  and  overall  performance.  This  workshop              is  designed  to  provide
participants with insights and skills related to conducting operational research and
effectively translating the research findings into actionable policies and practices.

Aim
With an aim to enhance translating research findings into actionable policy and
practice   by   bridging   the   gap   between   research   endeavors   and   real-world
implementation,   this   workshop   will   empower   participants   with   an   in-depth
understanding of the concept, methodology, and procedural aspects of operational
Research. The workshop covers the following:

           ➢     Introduction to operational research
           ➢     Problem identifying and formulation
           ➢     Data collection and analysis
           ➢     Alternative solutions and solution optimization
           ➢     Decision analysis
           ➢     Implementation through translating the results of the analysis into
                 actionable recommendations and helping organizations implement
                 changes based on those recommendations.
Benefit
Operational research is particularly useful in situations where decision-makers face
complex  and  uncertain  environments  and  need  a  systematic  and  quantitative
approach to support their choices.

                     Qualitative Research Methodology
                Concept, Methodology, and Hands-On Appraisal

Overview
Qualitative research is exploratory and aims to understand the depth and complexity
of a specific phenomenon. It is often used to uncover meanings, motivations, and
underlying patterns in human behavior through in-depth focus group discussions.
The workshop is tailored for researchers, social scientists, and those intrigued by the
depth of human experiences. It offers a comprehensive exploration of qualitative
research principles, methodologies, and critical appraisal.

Aim
The  aim  of  the  Qualitative  Research  Methodology  workshop  is  to  empower
participants with a comprehensive understanding of qualitative research principles,
methodologies, and critical appraisal skills. The workshop covers the following:
            ➢    Detailed overview of qualitative methods of data collection, including
                 observation, interviews, and focus group discussions.
            ➢    Designing a qualitative research project to respond to specific public
                 health problems or questions.
            ➢    Hands-on  experience  of  each  method  including  developing  data
                 collection guides, and techniques for managing data collection.
            ➢    Transcription   process   for   recorded   interviews   and   focus   group
                 discussions,  reflexivity  as  well  the  right  thematic  analysis  using
                 different techniques and approaches.
            ➢    Critically appraise and evaluate qualitative research reports in public
                 health or social and behavioral sciences.

Benefits
Participants will gain a deep understanding and gain advanced skills                    in qualitative
research,  from  conceptualization  to  critical  appraisal.  The  workshop's  hands-on
approach fosters practical skills, allowing participants to immerse themselves in the
qualitative  research  process.  By  the        end  of  the    workshop,  participants  will  be
equipped to design, conduct, and critically evaluate qualitative studies.

       An Introduction to Health Technology Assessment

Overview
The  workshop  aims  to  provide         an   introductory  training  on  Health  Technology
Assessment (HTA) to managers and healthcare decision-makers in the United Arab
Emirates  (UAE).  According  to       the  World  Health  Organization  (WHO),  HTA  is  a
comprehensive analysis that assesses any medicine, device, procedure, care setting,
or screening that may provide new or enhanced benefits to population health.
Generally, HTA captures the safety, efficacy, feasibility, and cost-effectiveness issues,
as well as social, economic, and ethical impacts. The workshop provides content
adapted from the WHO on conducting assessments or adapting assessment reports
from settings and applying their HTA results to evidence-informed policymaking.

The workshop is of interest to healthcare workers directly involved and/or interested
in HTA, including healthcare professionals, doctors, nurses, policymakers, students,
health insurance managers, and anyone interested in healthcare management.

Aim
The aim of the workshop is to provide participants with a thorough understanding
and practical experience on HTA, including:

     •    Understand the functions of HTA and its relation to healthcare decision-
          making.
     •    Explore the assessment process for new health technology and how this
          applies to a potential decision outcome.
     •    Explain to a patient or member of the public the process behind decisions
          about which healthcare treatments are funded.
     •    Understand the best international practices in HTA and the best practices
          to  assess  the  clinical  effectiveness  (benefits  and  harms)  and  cost-
          effectiveness of health technology.
     •    Investigate HTA and understand the evidence presented.
     •    Understand  the  best  approaches  and  models  for  assessing  the  other
          aspects/impacts of health technologies and the methods for integrating
          their societal impact.
