                                    Student Fundraising and External Contacts


I.                                                                                               Policy

                 In furtherance of its educational vision and mission, the United Arab Emirates
                 University shall establish regulations governing aspects of student life on
                 campus.  The University encourages the establishment of student
                 organizations to reflect student interests and enrich campus life outside the
                 classroom.

                 Fundraising Activities

                 Only University-chartered student organizations may engage in fundraising on
                 campus.  Any fundraising must be approved in advance in writing by Student
                 Affairs.  Fundraising will be approved for a defined duration of time at
                 identified campus locations.  Sale of food items will be approved only if
                 Student Affairs agrees to the sale.  Students are to remember that fundraising
                 activities are voluntary and to respect the preferences of other students, faculty
                 and staff.

                 Non-University Speakers or Performers

                 Only University-chartered student organizations may reserve space on campus
                 for events, and must request permission to bring in outside speakers or
                 performers.

Contact with Government Officials

                      department, such as a municipality or ministry, must be approved and initiated
