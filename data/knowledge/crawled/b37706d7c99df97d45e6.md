## Extracting Graduate's Students Documents
Through this service, Alumni can request documents such as transcripts and certificates. This service allows the option to request either printable or digital documents.

Start Service

## Extracting Graduate's Students Documents > Procedure
Log in to Portal or UAEU App via UAE Pass or UAEU email

Select " Extracting Graduate's Students Documents"

Select degree and document types (digital/printed), delivery method, and desired documents (transcript, attestation in Arabic or English, To Whom It May Concern, diploma).

Pay the fees (for printed documents only) and submit the request.

Show all steps

## Extracting Graduate's Students Documents > Conditions & Requirements
Must be an alumni of UAEU.

## Extracting Graduate's Students Documents > How do I apply for copies of graduate's Students Documents?
By visiting the alumni website.

## Extracting Graduate's Students Documents > What are the fees for extracting additional copies of graduation documents?
Digital documents are free of charge, as for the printable documents: Transcript 30 AED Attestation 25 AED
