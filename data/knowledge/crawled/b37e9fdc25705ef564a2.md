## Vision, Mission and Objectives > Vision
“Continuous contribution to the achievement of the University's objectives through the implementation of an effective and efficient risk management activity”

## Vision, Mission and Objectives > Mission
“The Risk Management Office should develop a comprehensive and exemplary risk management framework at the university level, and oversee the implementation of ERM tasks and activities by hedging and minimizing the possibility of discrepancies and uncertainty in achieving the objectives and better performance in the tasks and operations of the various organizational units”

## Vision, Mission and Objectives > Strategic Objectives
Developing risk management strategies

Evaluating and measure the potential risks at the university continuously

Disseminating the culture of risk management in the university community
