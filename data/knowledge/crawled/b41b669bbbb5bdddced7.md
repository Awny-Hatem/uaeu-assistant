## Advancement Criteria
For the first round, digital reproductions of the entry will be used to determine advancement in the competition.

Candidates advancing to the second round will be required to bring their original artwork to the University for in-person evaluation by the judging panel

If there is no reply from the judging committee by September 15, 2026, it means that applicants have not been selected to continue in the competition.

Submissions will be judged according to well-established artistic principles. Each artwork will be analyzed based on these criteria, which are applicable to all artistic fields.
