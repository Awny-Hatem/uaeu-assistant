## Evaluation of Filter Media Surface Area > Evaluation of Filter Media Surface Area
Client | Low and Bonar, USA
Project Description | Low and Bonar are manufacturer of environmental media for water and air treatment. The overall goal of this study is to assess the surface area and porosity of the proposed undisclosed media to ensure high rates of hydrogen sulfide biodegradation. This will be performed by Measurement of BET surface area and porosity, Initial media screening for hydrogen sulfide biodegradation, and full-scale evaluation of selected medias.
