## Partners and Sponsors > Abdulla Al Ghurair Foundation for Education
Abdulla Al Ghurair Foundation for Education (AGFE) creates opportunities to activate the untapped potential of Emirati and Arab youth, providing the scholarships, support and skills training that they need to thrive. The Foundation imagines a world where scholars become leaders in their communities and ambassadors of education in the region. AGFE aims to reignite what is already in the region’s DNA: a culture of giving back, a connectedness to community, and a spirit of generosity that creates impact.
