Tuition Payment Installment Plan
        To arrange a tuition payment installment plan, students must apply at the SAO before 17
         September 2015. If you were entitled to an installment plan in the previous semester you must
        apply again for the current semester

        Non Payment of total tuition fees on or before 29 October will result in hold on registration and
         records. A hold means that you will not receive course grades until you pay your fees. It also
         means that you will not be able to register for courses in the next semester until payment is
         made. Unfortunately it may also mean forfeiture of grades and credits earned during the
         semester.

        (SPONSORED STUDENTS) Students whose tuition charges are paid or guaranteed by a
         sponsor must submit a valid official letter of financial agreement from the sponsor no later than
         01 October 2015, or risk having their registration for Fall 2015 cancelled.
