                             Approval and Modification of Non-Credit Courses


I.                                                                                               Policy

                   Non-credit courses are collegiate-level courses offered by the United Arab
                   Emirates University that do not carry academic credit and are not a part of a
                   degree program.  All non-credit courses must be consistent with the
                   University’s vision, mission and strategic plan.  These courses are usually
                   offered by a center or institute and are expected to be self-supporting.  Prior to
                   implementation, proposals for new non-credit courses or substantive changes
                   to existing non-credit courses must be reviewed and approved through the
                   United Arab Emirates University procedures.


II.                                                                             Application

                   This policy shall apply to all operating units offering non-credit courses at the
                   United Arab Emirates University.
