## UAEU Expo 2020 Team Attend 4th International Participants Meeting
UAEU Expo 2020 Team joined a thousand delegates from around the world at the fourth International Participants Meeting (IPM) hosted by Expo 2020 Dubai. Expo 2020 Dubai shared their plans on how they will create an international platform for collaboration that will spark innovative solutions to today’s globally-shared challenges and updated the participants on the latest developments.

It was an ideal opportunity for UAEU’s Expo 2020 team to network and discuss collaboration possibilities with other local and international participants. Prof. Nihel Chabrak, Executive Director, UAEU Pavilion at Expo 2020 Dubai presented the University of the Future concept and UAEU’s participation at Expo to several interested participants in the IP Speaker Zone during the event.
