## High Energy Physics > Affiliated Faculty > Prof. Salah Nasri
Prof. Salah Nasri, Professor & HEP Group Leader

Email: snasri@uaeu.ac.ae , salah.nasri@cern.ch

Weblink: faculty.uaeu.ac.ae/snasri

Research Interests: Theoretical Particle Physics

Biography: Prof. Nasri earned his MSc in Physics from Syracuse University in 2000 and his PhD in 2003. He has held postdoctoral positions at the University of Maryland and the University of Florida before joining UAEU in 2007. He leads the UAEU-ATLAS collaboration, contributing to the LHC’s groundbreaking experiments at CERN.

## High Energy Physics > Affiliated Faculty > Dr. Mohamed Belfkir
Assistant Professor, Email: m_belfkir@uaeu.ac.ae , mohamed.belfkir@cern.ch

Research Interests: Experimental Particle Physics, Higgs and Double Higgs Physics, Dark Matter, Machine Learning Applications in HEP

Biography: Dr. Belfkir’s earned his PhD degree from Savoie Mont Blanc University in 2021 before joining UAEU. His work focuses on Higgs boson research and the application of artificial intelligence to enhance particle physics analyses. He is actively involved in dark matter studies at the LHC.
