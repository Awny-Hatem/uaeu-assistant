                                                                                              Material Disposal


I.                                                                                                     Policy

                   When it is apparent that assets or any other items are rendered surplus to the
                   needs of the United Arab Emirates University or are damaged beyond
                   economic and effective repair, procedures to dispose of such items will be
                   initiated.

                   Material disposal up to a limit of AED 25,000 is the responsibility of the
                   Secretary General.  Material disposal in excess of AED 25,000 is vested in the
                   Material Disposal Committee.  This Committee will be formed by the Vice
                   Chancellor and will meet at the call of the Secretary General.

                   Each dean or director will recommend to the Associate Provost for Academic
                   Affairs or the Secretary General whether an item is no longer needed by the
                   University and can be disposed of as excess material.  Material targeted for
                   disposal will fall under the following categories:

                                       a)                      needed, but beyond reasonable repair; and
                                       b)                   not needed.


II.                                                                                  Application

                   This policy shall apply to all assets of the United Arab Emirates University.
