## Happiness Research Talks
Happiness Research talk series titled Happiness Research: Trek to Wellbeing were introduced in collaboration with the Department of Government and society. These key-note lecture series on happiness research are an ongoing project which are an one-hour online lectures-discussions with the leading researchers from academia across the world. Such events will enrich the research culture at the UAEU and contribute to the development of the research on happiness and well-being, one of the national strategic goals at the United Arab Emirates.
