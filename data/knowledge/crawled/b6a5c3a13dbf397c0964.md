## Research Themes ​
The ARIFSID features a multi-disciplinary research, education, and outreach program with a focus on five research themes: ​

Theme 1: Urban Plant and Fish Farming ​ | Learn more ​
Theme 2: Urban Waste Material Recycling​ | Learn more ​
Theme 3: UAE’s Agricultural Genetic Resources​ | Learn more ​
Theme 4: One Health ​ | Learn more ​
Theme 5: Climate Change, Markets and Consumers​ | Learn more ​

​ All research and educational activities are tightly aligned with the UAE’s National Food Security Strategy, as well as the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
