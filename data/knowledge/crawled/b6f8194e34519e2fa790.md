## Services for People of Determination
Hema Team for People of Determination

The library provides assistance services for people of determination, aiming to facilitate all library services and offer special support through the Hema Team. Requests for assistance from the Hema Team should be submitted at least 48 hours before the required service.

Service Hours:

Monday to Thursday: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Friday: 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM
