                                                                              Registration Instructions

        1. Every student should meet with an academic advisor prior to registering.
        2. Questions  about section capacities, closed sections and restrictions should be directed to your college advising units.
        3. Students  in good academic standing may register          for 12 to 19 credit hours.
        4. Students under probation cannot register for more than 13 credit hours.
        5. Honors students    , and those who expect to graduate in Fall 2017 -2018, may register for up to 22 credit hours.
        6. Students  who have failed any of their UFP courses will have to correct their registration during the add/drop of the following semester.
