                                                                                                         Examinations


I.                                                                                              Policy

                      Examinations may be taken either in writing, orally, on-line or in a practical
                      setting in accordance with the United Arab Emirates University rules and
                      regulations.

                      All United Arab Emirates University classes will meet during assigned
                      examination periods.  No final examinations may be given before the start of
                      the examination period without prior approval of the Associate Provost for
                      Academic Affairs.


II.                                                                              Application

                      This policy shall apply to all students at  the United Arab Emirates University.
