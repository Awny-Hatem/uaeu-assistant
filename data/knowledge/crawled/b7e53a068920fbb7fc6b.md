6. Degree Completion and Graduation

Overview
Covers policies and procedures relating to the requirements for,                              confirmation                  of,                  and
awarding of academic degrees including: undergraduate degrees, structure of undergraduate
degree programs, limits on transfer credits for undergraduate degrees, application for degree,
certification of degree completion, graduation standing, and graduation honors.

Scope
Applies to all students and all academic and related administrative units of the University
except those in the College of Medicine and Health Sciences.

Objective
Ensures that the confirmation of completion and awarding    of academic degrees functions of
the Universit y is             conducted in a manner that is consistent with academic principles, standards,
and expectations of the University appropriate to its educational mission and academic
programs, and operate with necessar y in           tegrity, fairness           , consistency, and effectiveness.

Policy
1.                                  Undergraduate degree programs at UAEU will be reflective of the prevailing content,
      academic expectations, and learning outcomes of corresponding academic programs at
      internationally recognized public universities.
2.                                         The University will provide distinctive and high quality undergraduate degree programs
      b y adopting                                               common academic requirements applicable to all students across the colleges,
      recognizing the educational needs and expectations of the nation in program creation and
      operation, and designing and conducting undergraduate degree programs to meet college
      or program-level international accreditation standards or equivalent recognition.
3.                         The University will recognize outstanding academic achievement through a special
      designation at                                                             graduation.
