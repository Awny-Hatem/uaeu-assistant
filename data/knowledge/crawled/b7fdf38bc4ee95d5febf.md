## The Great Student Competition > Photography
To register: submit a JPEG image.

Submit a brief description of not more than 50 words.

The image should not include any written text.

The theme should be suggested by the general topics (humanity, society or national message) or any other approved topic.

The student can present the work in any appropriate manner.

The photograph must be taken by the competitor himself/herself.

The image should be a maximum of 70x50 cm and minimum 40x30 cm.
