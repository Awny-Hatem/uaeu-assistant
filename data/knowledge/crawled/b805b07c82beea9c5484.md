## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > About the Program
The National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) is a prestigious, full-time, 12-week training program developed by United Arab Emirates University to prepare UAE national graduates for careers in research, academia, and innovation.

Discover More

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Why Join NRPP?
The National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) is a prestigious initiative by United Arab Emirates University designed to equip the next generation of researchers with the skills, experiences, and mindset needed to thrive in academic, applied, and industrial research environments.
DISCOVER MORE

What to Expect?

As a participant in NRPP, you will be immersed in a challenging yet rewarding full-time training experience.
DISCOVER MORE

Who Can Apply?

The National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) is open to talented UAE Nationals who are alumni of the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) and are passionate about pursuing careers in academic or applied research.
DISCOVER MORE

What you will learn?

The NRPP program is structured into seven integrated modules and culminates in a hands-on research placement.
