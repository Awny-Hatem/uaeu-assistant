## Covid-19 Information > Faculty & Staff > Information for UAEU Faculty and Staff
Going to campus

Check with your college and department the need to go to the office.

Follow the regulation

Make sure to have a valid negative test to be presented monthly to security

Keep washing your hands while on campus

Keep to social distancing

Avoid face to face meetings with other colleagues

Use a hand sanitizer or wash your hands after touching surfaces and door handles

Stay home if you feel sick or you have flu symptoms

Contact your immediate supervisor if you test positive or if you suspect that you have contacted a positive case

Remote work

Make sure you are equipped with all technical support and technologies needed for online teaching and examination

Prepare your students

Taking a vacation and traveling

If you are planning for a vacation, remember that you need to get tested before traveling and that you should quarantine 14 days after your return

Avoid traveling to countries with high numbers of COVID-19

Take all the precautions needed while traveling, such as masks wearing, hand washing, and social distancing

Managing your work stress

Try to take breaks

Manage your time effectively

Discuss your concerns with immediate supervisor

Seek help if needed

Read about positive psychology

Try to exercise regularly

Walking in the open air will help you to relieve your stress

Relationship with other staffs and students

Try to keep in contact with your colleagues even online

Discussing plans and schedules with colleagues and students is essential
