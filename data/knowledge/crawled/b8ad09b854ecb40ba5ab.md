                                                                   Travel Air Tickets


I.                                                                                               Policy

               The United Arab Emirates University shall provide, as stated in the
               employment contract or offer letter, economy travel tickets, or other, for
               employee, spouse and a maximum of three dependent children upon
               commencement of employment and at the end of the contract in the United
               Arab Emirates and for each contract year from the United Arab Emirates to
               and from the employee’s country of residence for each year of service subject
               to the employee’s terms and conditions of employment.  At the discretion of
               the University, a cash amount may be paid in lieu of tickets equivalent to the
               cost to the University of purchasing the tickets.  Employees may choose to
               travel or not, as they wish.

                The country of residence shall be specified in the contract of employment and
                shall be determined by the passport on which the employee’s United Arab
                Emirates resident visa is stamped, except where the employee can demonstrate
                that his/her normal place of residence is a country other than the country
                which issued his/her passport.

                Dependent children are children who are less than eighteen years of age or are
               children who     are between 18 and 23 years of age     and are continuing their
               studies on a full-time basis until the end of the first university stage or its
               equivalent.


II.                                                                             Application

               This policy shall apply to all employees of the United Arab Emirates
               University.
