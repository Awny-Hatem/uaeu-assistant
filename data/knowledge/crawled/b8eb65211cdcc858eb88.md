## IT SUPPORT SERVICES ACHIEVEMENT > Annual Desktop/Laptop Upgrade:
All the desktops and laptops, which are owned by the university, are subject to a replacement cycle every year; DoIT evaluates desktop and laptop that need to be replaced in response to technological developments and ongoing changes in university requirements. In the year 2018-2019, there were 1,200 desktops and 360 laptops have been provided with the higher technical specification to full fill the operational requirements of our administrator and academics units.
