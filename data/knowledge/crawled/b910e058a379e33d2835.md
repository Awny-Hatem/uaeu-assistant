Title: The Impact of Academic Twinning on Student Outcomes

Date: Thursday August 28, 2025

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Dr. Zainab Al-Marzouqi

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: This workshop seeks to highlight the educational and cultural impact of school twinning projects by promoting the exchange of knowledge and experiences among students and broadening their intellectual and cultural horizons. It also clarifies the importance of twinning in developing students’ skills, communication, and intercultural dialogue, as well as fostering a sense of responsibility and teamwork among participants. The workshop presents quantitative evidence on how to utilize and apply twinning through joint projects and innovative activities. Moreover, it focuses on the role of twinning in refining students’ diplomatic skills and strengthening their national identity while embracing openness to world cultures.
