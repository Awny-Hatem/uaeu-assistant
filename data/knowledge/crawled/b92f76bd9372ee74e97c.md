## Prototype To Market
The Prototype-To-Market (P2M) is an action-oriented accelerator program that focusses on how to successfully Manufacture Startup Success. P2M experience is a process of intense, rapid, and immersive training and coaching aimed at accelerating the life cycle of young innovative startups, compressing years’ worth of learning-by-doing into just 11 weeks.

Target Audience: Students, Faculty / Researchers

## Program Overview:
23 Sep - 5 December 2025

11-weeks action-oriented accelerator program will focus on how to successfully Manufacture Startup Success .

P2M experience is a process of intense, rapid, and immersive training and coaching aimed at accelerating the life cycle of young innovative startups, compressing years’ worth of learning-by-doing into just 11 weeks

## Program Goals:
Develop a commercialize product

Commercialize the product (generate revenues and/or contracts/validation)

Deliver a professional business presentation during “P2M Demo Day”

## Program Structure:
Action-oriented teaching, followed by

One-on-one coaching sessions to ensure that the training lessons will be applied

“Demo/Deal Day” - Actual Pitch Sessions to UAEU SIP Management, judges and potential investors

Best teams will receive further funding and support from UAEU SIP and potentially external partners

## Real Market Access
Take your prototype out of the lab and into the market. Test, validate, and adapt your solution with real users and paying customers.

## Deep-Dive Coaching & Mentorship
Get Guided by top startup experts.

## AED 325,000 in Seed Funding
Top 3 ventures receive milestone-based funding (AED 150k, 100k, 75k) to accelerate development, customer traction, and fundraising readiness.

## Investor-Ready Pitching
Craft a pitch that speaks the language of investors. Rehearse, refine, and deliver your story with clarity, confidence, and credibility.

## National Exposure & Recognition
Stand out at the Grand Demo/Deal Day in front of top judges, investors, and ecosystem leaders. This is where serious startups are born.
