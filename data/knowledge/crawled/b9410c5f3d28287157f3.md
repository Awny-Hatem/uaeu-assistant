The  UAEU    Freshmen   Students Fanar  Program   (FSFP)  is a joint program
                         between    Student   Services,  Student   Activities, and   Residential   Life
                         Departments.  This isa transition program  designed to assistUAEU   freshmen
                         students intheir smooth  transitionfrom high school to college.The aim of this
                         program  is toimprove  UAEU    freshmen  students’academic  performance   and
                         leadership skills.
Mission


      The   UAEU    Freshmen    Students  Fanar  Program’s    mission  is to provide  UAEU
      freshmen    with  effective  advisement   and   a  number    of academic    supportive
      services  tosucceed  at the university  and  tobecome   lifelong learners. The  UAEU
      Freshmen    Students   Fanar  Program    guides  freshmen   students  to learn  how  to
      learn  throughout   their academic  careers  by providing   students with  a variety of
      supportive     services   including    orientation   sessions,   academic     advising,
      personal/group    counseling, study  skillsdevelopment,    as well as a wide  range  of
      other  services.
Vision

        Enhance the success of new students and provide
        leadership and academic advising programs at the
        best local and international levels
 Goals

 1.    Acquiring     in-depth    information     about   UAEU      services   and
       resources   for students.
 2.    Creating   a friendly,   welcoming     and  accessible   environment.
 3.    Helping    students    in  identifying    resources    to  achieve    their
       academic    goals.
 4.    Enhancing     students   leadership    skills
 5.    Assisting   students    to acquire    academic     and   study   behavior
       skills
 6.    Increasing     students    sense   of  belonging     to   the  university
       community     by  being   engaged    in leadership    activities.

UAEU Freshmen Students Fanar Program Learning Outcomes


The  Freshmen     Students   Fanar   Program    learning   outcomes    are diverse  and   they
explain  diversity  of learning   experiences   as follows:
At the  end  of the University   Fanar   Program,    UAEU     freshmen   students   should:



            Academic Success
            Receive the appropriate and high quality academic services
            efficiently and systematically.

            Academic and Career Selection
            Identify occupations compatible with their personality, ability and
            interests.
            Select a program of study that supports their academic and career goals.

           Knowledge of University Resources
           Identify and name two university resources available to assist them
           in achieving their academic goals.
           Be able to explain the student’s role and responsibility in academic
           advising vs. the academic advisor’s role and responsibility.

              Leadership Skill Development
              Demonstrate enhanced time management skills.
              Demonstrate enhanced problem solving and decision making skills.
              Demonstrate enhanced planning skill.

            Study Behavior
            Demonstrate knowledge of effective study behaviors such as note-
            taking skills, test preparation, finding information and library
            skills, and memory retrieval strategies.

The UAEU Fanar Program consists of
        six components as follows:





  Fanar  Program    Open   Day:  The  aim   ofSuccess   Program    open  day  is
  to orient  freshmen    students   about  the  UAEU     Freshmen     Students
  Fanar   Program    five components    and   to provide  freshmen    students
  with  an  opportunity    to  meet   the  Fanar   Program    Heroes    (Fanar
  Program    Club  Members).    In  addition  to that, the  University   Fanar
  Program     Open    Day   will  provide    freshmen     students   with   the
  opportunity    to develop   new   friends  and   to learn  more   about   the
  services  available  to  students   and  other  issues  essential  to  lifeat
  UAEU.

  Welcome     Aboard    Fanar    Freshmen     Students    (WAFFS):     In  this
  component    of the  UAEU    Student   Fanar   Program    (USSP)   freshmen
  students  will be  introduced    to new   concepts   of University   culture,
  assist students   in navigating   buildings,  residential   halls and   other
  related services,  and  allow  students  to become   familiar  with  UAEU.
  This  program    provide   freshmen    students   and   their  parents  with
  opportunities   to know  about   allthe university   support  services.



  Know    Your   Faculty   (KUF):   It is designed   to help  students  for the
  transition from  high  school  to the  university by  introducing   freshmen
  students to meet  with  faculty, staffand  administrators   and  learn about
  theirexpectations  so they can  be able to succeed  atUAEU.    This program
  allows freshmen   students  toknow   about  their own  college and  to adjust
  to  the  university   life and    curriculum    demands,    teaching   styles,
  behavioral  expectations,  classroom   designs,  and  students’ socialization
  patterns.

## PDF page 11
Fanar   Program    Leadership    Initiative  (FPLI):   This  initiative is designed
to  enhance    freshmen     students’   leadership    skills  by  giving   students
opportunities    to practice   a  range   of leadership    skills in  a  supportive
learning   environment.     Freshmen     students    will  be  trained   in  several
leadership   skills through   a  Training   Program    during   their first year. To
help students   to applying   the leadership   skills,the  freshmen   students   will
be  engaged    in  Fanar    Program     Leadership    Initiative  (SPLI).    In  this
initiative freshmen    students   are expected   to  plan  (during  first year)  and
implement     (during   second    and   third  semesters)    a  group    project   to
improve   the  university   community.    The   FPLI   approach    will provide   an
array    of  supports     to   students    namely     monetary      grant,   access,
resources..etc.  For  more  information,    click here


Fanar   Happiness     Program    (FHP):    The   aim  of  this  component     of  the
UAEU     Student    Fanar   Program     (USSP)     is to  increase   the  academic
success  of all freshmen   students   including   “people   of determination”    and
“at promise”   students  by  helping  them   to learn  and  apply  needed   skills to
be successful   in college. More    specifically, “people   of determination”    and
“at promise”   students  will  receive  a variety  of academic    learning  support
services  such   as  one-to-one    tutoring,   academic     advising,   counseling,
study  skills  development,     and   workshops.     Additionally,   the  proposed
program    will offer mentoring    program,    which   will be consisted   of senior
students  (mentors    will  be  selected  based   on  specific  criteria)  who   will
guide, support   and  advice  freshmen    students.


Fanar  Career   Exploring    Program    (FCEP):    The  aim  of this program    is to
help   freshmen     to   explore    a  wide    variety    of  disciplines,    topics,
occupations,    persons,    and   places.   Additionally,     this  program     help
students  to know   about   their aspirations,   strengths,  and  career  planning.
This  program    will offer  workshops     on  choosing   a field  of study,  career
explorations   and  self-awareness.
