## Student Exchange Unit > Overview
The Undergraduate Student Exchange Program at United Arab Emirates University enables undergraduate students from UAEU and partner universities to enroll in subjects at the host institution for credits, which will count towards their home institution degree. UAEU welcomes international students from our partner institutions. This adds to the cultural diversity of UAEU where students of 60+ different nationalities are currently studying. We offer students an exciting opportunity to study and work with others while growing both personally and professionally. Our students leave UAEU with broader horizons, many lifelong friendships, cultural sensitivity and global awareness.

## Student Exchange Unit > Incoming Students
Students enrolled in undergraduate education at universities with exchange agreements with UAEU are eligible to study at UAEU for up to two semesters as specified in the exchange agreement and agreed upon by both institutions.

## Student Exchange Unit > Outgoing Students
Students at the United Arab Emirates University who wish to study abroad for up to two semesters at any partner university can submit their application through the college coordinator for the student exchange program. The tuition fee arrangement is determined according to the terms outlined in the agreement with the partner university. The student is responsible for all other costs, including transportation, accommodation, insurance, and textbooks.
