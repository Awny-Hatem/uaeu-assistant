## Wellbeing Society > Team > Omar Saeed Aldhuhoori, President of the Wellbeing Society
Omar Saeed Aldhuhoori is the President of the Wellbeing Society. He is currently enrolled in the college of Business and Economics majoring in Finance and Banking at the United Arab Emirates University. Omar was raised in the United States of America where he acquired his early education. He is an enthusiastic young individual who has been actively engaged in many extracurricular activities. He has actively volunteered in many activities on and off campus.

Omar from his post of being a president aspires to improve the overall wellbeing of the University community. With the valuable input from his team members under his leadership he aims to carry out multiple initiatives that would help him achieve his goal of increasing positivity and promoting wellbeing within the University and supporting the center s vision, mission and goals.

## Wellbeing Society > Team > Khaled Al Marri, Vice President of the Wellbeing Society
Khalid Al Marri is the Vice President of the Student Wellbeing Society. He is currently enrolled in the college of Business and Economics at the United Arab Emirates University. Being a senior student at the University, Khalid aspires to put in all efforts through his position of being a Vice President of this society to contribute in making a difference in the life of the University

students. By doing so he believes in improving their quality of life which will have a positive outcome not only in their academic performance but overall character and personality development. His mission is to work with a strong team bonding with all the other team members and use his skills to make life on campus much better for all the students.
