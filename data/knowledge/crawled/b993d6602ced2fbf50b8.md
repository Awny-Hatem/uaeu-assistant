                                        Reporting Loss or Theft of University Property


I.                                                                                               Policy

                      All discoveries of loss or theft of United Arab Emirates University property
                      must be reported immediately to the Secretary General.  The University has an
                      insurance protection policy for certain University assets in the event of a fire
                      or theft.  Procedures for reporting are available in the Procedures Manual.


II.                                                                             Application

                      This policy shall apply to all faculty, staff and students of the United Arab
                      Emirates University.
