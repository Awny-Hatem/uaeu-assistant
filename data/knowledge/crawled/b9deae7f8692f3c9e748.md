## Objectives
The main objective of the Sport Science & Physical Activity Fair 2026 is to bring together experts and specialists in sports science and physical activity to discuss the latest innovations, exchange knowledge and experiences, and raise awareness of physical health.

Promote awareness of the importance of physical activity and physical fitness in enhancing overall health and individual well-being.

Encourage knowledge exchange on the latest innovations, technologies, and scientific research in sports science and physical activity.

Provide a collaborative platform for communication between researchers, practitioners, and stakeholders in the sports and health sectors.

Highlight challenges and opportunities within the sports and physical activity ecosystem, as well as incentives that foster increased community participation.

Develop strategic directions that promote regular physical activity across society and integrate active lifestyles into daily routines.
