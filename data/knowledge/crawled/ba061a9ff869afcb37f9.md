Notes:
1)	All	applicants	must	apply	online	and	submit	all	required,	original	documents	(e.g.,	English	test	scores)	to	the	Admissions	Department	by	the	deadline.
2)	Applications	received	after	the	deadline	will	be	processed	for	the	next	admission	cycle.
3)	UAEU	students	may	apply	for	admission	into	a	graduate	program	during	their	final	semester.	However,	admission	requires	the	completion
				of	all	existing	degree	requirements	before	acceptance	into	the	program.
4)	Applications	will	be	processed	and	an	admission	decision	will	be	rendered	within	30	days	after	receiving	the	application	and	all	supporting	documents.
