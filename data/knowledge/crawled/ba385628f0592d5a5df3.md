Welcome to the Student Leaders Program 2021! We pleased that you are interested in joining us. We have planned an exciting program that we feel will help you discover more about what leadership is. A traditional view of leadership is that of the 'individual leader' promoting command, power and authority. However, at the UAEU Student Leadership Program we want you to focus on a different approach. The focus is instead on values and integrity and the collaborative pursuit of the shared goal of change.
