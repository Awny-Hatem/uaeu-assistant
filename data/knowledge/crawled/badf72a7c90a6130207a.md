           Non-financial middle to senior managers seeking greater understanding of financial management.

           It is assumed participants comply with the following requirements:
                    ▪          English-language competence with sufficient ability to read and comprehend learning material, write assignments and follow/participate in
                                   discussions on complex financial management issues
                    ▪          Knowledge and understanding of the basic elements of financial management (read and understand financial                                                                                                                                                                                                                               statements                        )
