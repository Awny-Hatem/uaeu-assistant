                                       goal of this newsletter is to showcase our diverse and talented graduate
                                       students and their achievements. In addition, it provides information
                                       about our graduate programs (academic and professional master’s and
                                        doctoral degrees), which enroll over 1400 graduate students.











Graduate students within our 9 colleges contribute to the academic and research mission of UAEU. Therefore, we
believe it is essential to create an environment in which every graduate student will grow and thrive. We are also
proud of the rich diversity of our graduate students, including 53 different nationalities, representing many races,
ethnicities and beliefs.


CGS, in collaboration with other units in the university, supports the academic success, professional development,
and personal wellbeing of our graduate students from the time they join UAEU until they graduate. Through various
extracurricular social and professional development activities, CGS provides graduate students with the opportunity
to interact with their peers from across campus. The PhD Teaching Academy, which is offered in collaboration with
CETL (Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning), prepares students to become future faculty members.


UAEU offers a broad range of graduate programs consisting of 36 Master’s and 25 Doctorate programs. Our
