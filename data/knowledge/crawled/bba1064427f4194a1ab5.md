## Executive Education > Overview
The world of business in the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) environment is dynamic and fast-changing. It demands leaders who can proactively think, plan and execute in a wide range of complex and chaotic environments. The College of Business and Economics (CBE), building upon the reputation and success of UAEU across the region, is focused on educating individuals and organizations for higher levels of success.

## Executive Education > Public Programs for Professionals
This is a platform for lifelong learners. The available short courses and longer programs have been designed for those executives and managers who would like to develop certain competencies, either as part of a stand-alone functional course, or as part of an integrated comprehensive program. They have been developed based on our understanding of the unique competencies required to function optimally within the business community of the UAE and the wider GCC region.
