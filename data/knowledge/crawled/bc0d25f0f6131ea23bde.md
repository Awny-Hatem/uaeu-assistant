                                                                                              University Gift Items


I.                                                                                               Policy

                       The United Arab Emirates University will maintain an inventory of
                       appropriate gift or commemorative items that can be used to recognize visitors
                       to and guests of the University and to recognize faculty, staff and students.


II.                                                                             Application

                       This policy shall apply to all operating units of the United Arab Emirates
                       University.
