Bridging Program
Bridging courses are not counted as part of the degree requirements. The student could be waived from a bridging course if an equivalent course was successfully passed at the undergraduate level. The student is required to register for the online bridging courses and pass them successfully at his/her pace. However, the student is required to successfully pass each online bridging course before registering for the core or elective course requiring the online bridging course as a prerequisite.

Degree Requirements
The total number of Credit Hours required to complete the MBA program is thirty-six (36) hours distributed among 11 courses as follows:Core courses: 8 courses x 3 Credit Hours + MBA Capstone Project x 6 Credit Hours= 30 Credit HoursElectives courses: 2 courses x 3 Credit Hours = 6 Credit Hours
