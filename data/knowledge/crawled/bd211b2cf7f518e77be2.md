## Objectives
Enhancing human capital and developing qualified national competencies to meet the labor market future demands locally, regionally and globally.

Conducting studies and publishing research on human capital and productivity to distill and disseminate key policy-related research findings to a broad audience of policymakers, academic and professional agencies, and other researchers.

Analyzing the UAE labor market and predicting its future trends to assist the relevant institutions in determining how to match academic and professional disciplines with those trends.

Promoting a solid culture that may enhance the processes for talent management, human capital analytics, and employee productivity.
