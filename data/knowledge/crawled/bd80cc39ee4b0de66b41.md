## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > Overview
At the Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning (CETL), three dynamic units focus on enhancing educational practice. The Faculty Professional Development unit conducts workshops to develop faculty proficiency in both Blackboard Original and Ultra Experiences, covering course design, interactive content, and student engagement tools.

## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > Dr. Saed Alrabaee > Director, Center for Excellence in Teaching & Learning
Welcome to the Center for Excellence in Teaching & Learning (CETL) in the UAEU! As Director, I am excited to share our mission of empowering educators, fostering a culture of teaching, and learning excellence. With innovative instructional design, AI-enhanced teaching strategies, and innovative, evidence-supported professional development, we promote dynamic, effective learning for all. Our dedicated team members within the center work collaboratively to provide resources and support tailored to your needs. Together, we are building a vibrant academic community that values collaboration, innovation, and excellence in education.

## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > At CETL We Believe > In preparing future faculty
Through the PhD Teaching Academy, we equip PhD students with the teaching experience and mentorship they need to excel in academic roles.

## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > At CETL We Believe > In advancing learning through collaboration
We support faculty and student growth by creating a dynamic environment for exploring new teaching and learning methods.

## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > At CETL We Believe > In empowering educators:
By providing workshops and resources, we help faculty develop essential skills in innovative course design, student engagement, and effective use of educational technology.

## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > At CETL We Believe > In driving transformation
Our Instructional Design unit leads the UAEU Course Transformation Process, fostering impactful and forward-thinking teaching practices.

## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > Units > Faculty Professional Development
This unit plans, develops, and manages the delivery of workshops, seminars, discussion panels, and forums to promote teaching & learning excellence.

## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > Units > Instructional Design
This unit supports faculty initiatives including online and blended learning and innovative course designs and teaching methods to enhance teaching and learning.

## Center for Excellence in Teaching and Learning > Units > PhD Academy
A comprehensive blended program using a flipped classroom approach, offering both foundational education theory and, importantly, hands-on experience. This program fosters the critical and creative thinking skills essential for Ph.D. students aspiring to academic teaching careers.
