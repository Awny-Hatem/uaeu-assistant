## HE Dr. Mohammed Ali Al-Sheryani
Dr. Al-Sheryani holds a BA in Political Science from the United Arab Emirates University. He received his Masters and PhD in Political Philosophy from Cairo University in Egypt.

Dr. Al-Sheryani is considered one of the founders of the Supreme Council for National Security and he is currently the Director of Community Affairs in the Supreme Council for National Security.
