MSc in Forensic Science
United Arab Emirates University (UAEU)

About the Program
The MSc in Forensic Science (MSFS) at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU)
prepares graduates to apply advanced scientific principles to the investigation and
resolution of crime. The program combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on
laboratory experience, supporting the UAE’s national vision for innovation, security, and
justice.

Program Highlights
•   Duration: 2 years (Full-Time) | 3 years (Part-Time)
•   Total Credit Hours: 30 CH (21 CH taught courses in hybrid mode + 9 CH thesis research)
•   Core Disciplines: Forensic Toxicology, DNA Analysis, Criminalistics, Analytical
    Chemistry
•   Thesis: Research-based project
•   Practical Experience: Includes three high-quality forensic hands-on experiences
•   Collaboration: With law-enforcement and research agencies

Faculty
Faculty members from the College of Science, Information Technology, and Law, as well as
professionals from the Dubai Police, Abu Dhabi Police, and the Ministry of Justice, are
involved in teaching across the program.

Research
Research in the Forensic Science Program addresses critical needs in crime detection,
evidence evaluation, and public safety.

Key Research Areas include: Forensic Toxicology and Drug Analysis, DNA and Genomic
Profiling, Trace Evidence and Microscopy, Digital and Analytical Forensics, and Criminal
Justice Applications of Science.

Facilities and Laboratories
Students have access to state-of-the-art facilities within the College of Science, including
Analytical Chemistry, Forensic DNA, Instrumental Analysis, and Microscopy and Trace

Practical Forensic Laboratory Hands-On Experience
1.  Forensic Toxicology Lab – Analytical techniques for toxic substances and drug analysis.
2.  DNA & Profiling Lab – Extraction, amplification, and interpretation of biological
    evidence.
3.  Trace Evidence & Microscopy Lab – Microscopic and material analysis for criminal
    investigations.
4.  Instrumental Analysis & Case Simulation – Integration of analytical tools for forensic
    cases.

Career Opportunities
Graduates are equipped for careers in:

•   Police and Law Enforcement Agencies
•   Ministry of Interior and Justice Departments
•   Forensic Science Laboratories
•   Research Institutions and Universities
•   Health and Environmental Agencies

Scholarships & Funding
UAEU provides a range of scholarships and assistantships for outstanding graduate
students, including:

•   Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA)
•   Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA)
•   External Sponsorship Programs
•   Special Funding for Emirati Students

International Collaborations
The program maintains active collaborations with international universities, forensic
laboratories, and law-enforcement agencies, including:

•   King’s College London
•   Monash University Australia

Accreditations
The United Arab Emirates University is accredited by the Commission for Academic
Accreditation (CAA) of the UAE Ministry of Education. The Forensic Science Program aligns
with international forensic-science education standards and undergoes regular academic
review.
