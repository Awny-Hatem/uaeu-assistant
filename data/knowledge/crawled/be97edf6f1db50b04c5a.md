## External Projects > Developing a Database for Roadway Accidents in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi – Al Ain.
The study established a database of a representative sample of General Motor vehicles that have been involved in injury crashes in Al Ain. These accidents are thoroughly investigated to understand the true causes and mechanisms that lead to severe injuries and fatalities due to roadway crashes.
