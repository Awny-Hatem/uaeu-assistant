## Student Leaders Program > Educational Opportunity and Impact > SLP Leadership Certificate Requirement
The UAEU Students Leadership Program Leadership Certificate is available to all UAEU students.

In order to earn the Leadership Certificate, registered students must complete the requirements in one academic year. They also must attend (5) five seminars:

One seminar for Social Change Model

Three elective seminars. Any three from the following:

Personal Development

Organizational Skills and Team Building

Conflict Resolution

Book Substitution (read book to substitute ONE elective seminar)

Capstone (final seminar)

Be involved in on-going, meaningful community service for a minimum of 7 working days.

## Student Leaders Program > Educational Opportunity and Impact > Impact
This project will add value not just to the UAEU Student’ Affairs, its benefits extend to all UAEU students who have different needs and goals. This program will help students build their skills and CVs. It will support participants to engage with different community institutions and leaders. It will assist our students to be better prepared for their future career and challenges in a diverse society, such as the one we live in.
