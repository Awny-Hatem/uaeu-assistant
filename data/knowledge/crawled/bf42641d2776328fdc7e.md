## Overview
The Financial Affairs Division is responsible for thousands of individual accounts, official government funding and all University Financial transactions. It is supported by the Risk Management and Asset Control Department, Budget and Financial Analysis Department, and the Payable and Receivable Department.

Our team is united by a common aim to promote decision making and problem solving, to cooperate and communicate with the goal of improving resource utilization at United Arab Emirates University and to develop a culture of efficiency and effectiveness. We are committed to enhancing our processes to ensure transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement. We will help facilitate faculty, staff, students and the greater community of the University in developing solutions to overcome the University's financial challenges. We will continue to supervise the University's resources in an environment of quality and integrity and to strengthen relationships with its greater community through sincerity and hard work.
