## E-resources - Access and Tools
Through this service, the UAEU Libraries provides students, faculty, and staff with access to its electronic resources and research tools.

Start Service

## E-resources - Access and Tools > Procedure
Access the library website at https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/library

Enter the keyword in the search box.

Filter search results by applying relevant filters (e.g. Article,book,full text ).

Select the desired title .

Log in with a UAEU account.

Access the full text to read, print, or download the title.

Show all steps

## E-resources - Access and Tools > Conditions & Requirements
Providing an active account affiliated with UAE University

## E-resources - Access and Tools > If I am not a member of UAEU may I still access your e-recourses?
Yes, e-resources are available for the non member physically present in the library.

## E-resources - Access and Tools > I cannot access the e-resources even though I entered my username and password ,what do i need to do?
Please make sure that you are using the correct email username without the extension @uaeu.ac.ae and your password, or contact us via email: digital.library@uaeu.ac.ae
