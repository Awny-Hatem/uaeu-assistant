## UAEU Portal Focus Group > Purpose of this Consultation
UAEU Portal Focus Group (public engagement (co-creation/co-production).

Students were asked to scan a barcode with all of their comments and address them by the impact priority

Students were grouped and asked to re-design the current UI for the UAEU mobile app . They came up with a list of suggestions.

## UAEU Portal Focus Group > Outcome
1. Dashboard Improvements

Participants recommended displaying academic risk indicators with suggested actions for improvement.

2. Enhanced Information Display

The Announcements section should be renamed to "Important for You" and include a slider for multiple announcements.

3. Improved Access to Academic Tools

Quick Actions should prioritize the most frequently used services, including Blackboard, class schedules, and attendance.

4. New Features and Functionality

Students requested additional features such as Tasks and Deadlines, a UAEU Assistant chatbot, global search functionality, and the display of the current semester on the home page.
