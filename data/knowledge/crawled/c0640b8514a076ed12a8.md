## Program
There are some outstanding programs that can be joined in our lab such as:

## Program > Postdoctoral Fellowship
Duration: At least 1 year

Start date: Flexible (rolling application with no deadline)

Candidates: Candidates have graduated with a bachelor’s, master’s degree and Ph.D program in a relevant field such as Data Science, Healthcare, IT, Pharmacy, Biology, Math, etc.

The post-doctoral program offers postdoctoral candidates the opportunity to have a partnership with strong mentors and participation in cutting edge research. This program aims to position candidates for successful entry into early career development in top and high demand of Health Data Science field.

## Program > Postgraduate (Master & Ph.D)
The lab welcomes suitably qualified applicants for postgraduate study (Ph.D and Master). We offer students for pursuing their study in the lab research areas. Our supervision ecosystems are designed to facilitate discussion through expert supervision, presentations in reading and writing groups and masterclasses, as well as participation in the lab’s research seminars led by distinguished scholars and writers. Students can apply for financial aid. We will try to guide and help your application as best we can.

## Program > Research Assistant
A Research Assistant supports a research group which he/she is assigned to and helps in conducting its research activities. We recruit research assistants on the basis of specific projects. We are always open to hearing from undergraduates, postgraduate or recent graduates who would like to join our lab as a research assistant.​

Joining a research assistant offers hands-on experience to engage with researchers and content you learn previously. Taking advantage of these opportunities can lead you to completing an honors thesis, improve your graduate school prospects, or provide you with valuable mentorship and networking experiences. Don’t wait! Start now!

## Program > Internship/Attachment
Students (postgraduate and undergraduate), researcher and academician in any related field are invited to apply for internship or attachment programs. Applicants should have interest in the lab’s research areas. All interns/trainees/apprentices are paired with a mentor and participate in the lab activity and program.

Interns/trainees/apprentices can apply for financial aid. We will try to guide and help your application as best we can.

## Program > Pre-doctoral
A predoctoral fellow (pre-doc) is a researcher who has a master's degree (or equivalent university graduate education) or bachelor’s degree with excellent achievement, that enrolls in a preparatory program at university before admission to PhD (doctoral degree program) and often granted a stipend. As the name implies, predoctoral fellows often use their time as a fellow to develop their skills and résumé as a transition before applying to graduate school for a doctoral degree.
