## College of Agriculture and Veterinary Medicine > Consultancy Services
The CAVM provides opportunities for collaboration with industry partners and government agencies through collaborative research and/or consultancy services. Some research partnerships and consultancies include: Abu Dhabi Food Control Authority, Farmer Service Center, Ministry of Environment and Water, International Biosaline Center, Environmental Agency-Abu Dhabi, Abu Dhabi Municipality, Al Ain municipality, Alkharbiah Municipality, Dubai Municipality, Agrivita (grand Mills), Health Authority of Abu Dhabi, Ministry of Health, Al Ain Zoo, and Al-Foah Farm.
