## Doctorate of Business Administration > Doctorate of Business Administration
Today's complex, global competitive environments call for new models of performance and innovative solutions, that require organization leaders to be able to think in global terms and design systems that are innovative and responsive to global and societal trends.

The College of Business and Economics (CBE) at the UAEU has responded to this growing need with the introduction of the AACSB accredited, Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) program to be offered in the UAE capital, Abu Dhabi.

The program has been designed for research-oriented working professionals and senior level executives. It is aimed at enhancing management knowledge and equipping business leaders with broad research and process management skills to enable them to successfully tackle the interdisciplinary, big picture issues that characterize global business management today.

The part-time, academically rigorous program is delivered in a way that enables business leaders to realize those ambitions without compromising their professional endeavors.

We are confident that the CBE DBA, delivered by a dedicated doctoral faculty with world-class credentials, will help you move to the next level in achieving your career goals.
