## Financial support opportunities > College of Engineering Master’s Fellowship
Eligibility Criteria for the Fellowship

Students must have completed at least one semester with 9 credits in their Master’s program.

Must maintain a GPA of 3.8 or above.

Be enrolled full-time (min. of 9 credits/semester).

Be available at UAEU on a full-time basis (i.e. from 8 am to 5 pm).

Monthly Stipend: AED 3,000/month.

Commit to 5 hours/week as a Graduate Teaching Assistant (GTA) or Graduate Administrative Assistant (GAA).

The student must not have any other supporting financial resources, but may hold other scholarship awards simultaneously.

Awardees will receive an official award letter outlining the fellowship benefits and responsibilities.

Employed students are not eligible to apply.

The COE Fellowship will be suspended if any of the above conditions are violated, and the student will not be eligible to reapply.

To apply the student must complete the online application in the first week of the semester.

## Financial support opportunities > College of Engineering 25% Discount Award
Eligibility Criteria

New Students:

Must have a B.Sc. GPA of 3.5 or above.

Applicants must not hold any external sponsorship.

Currently, Enrolled Students:

B.Sc. GPA between 3.0 and 3.5 and M.Sc. GPA between 3.5 and 3.79.

B.Sc. GPA below 3.0 and M.Sc. GPA between 3.6 and 3.79.

M.Sc. GPA of 3.8 or higher who have not yet completed 12 credit hours.

Applicants must not hold any external sponsorship.

Conditions to Maintain the Scholarship:

Maintain a GPA of 3.5 or higher in all subsequent semesters.

Part-time students must maintain a minimum of 6 credits each semester.

Full-time students must maintain a minimum of 9 credits each semester.

Students who wish to withdraw from a section will be required to pay the full amount, without the discount.

To apply the student must complete the online application in the first week of the semester.

## Financial support opportunities > Graduate Teaching Assistantship
Discover more.

Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA) positions are offered on part-time bases, exclusively to enrolled postgraduate students in the department. The number of positions offered is determined at the beginning of the semester based on the number of courses offered and registered students in each course. The monthly salary will depend on the number of assigned courses and will be in the range of 1,500-3,000 AED per month. At the beginning each semester, and once the teaching load is finalized, a call for GTA application will be sent to all eligible students.

## Financial support opportunities > Graduate Research Assistantship
Discover more.

Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA) positions are provided within the budget of sponsored research projects. A GRA is assigned research tasks while he/she pursues graduate education. A faculty could submit for RA position application on an existing research fund or with a new research proposal. Once the application is approved and the position is formed, the faculty will be assigned as the hiring manager, and the selection of the candidate to be given the offer will be entirely up to him/her.

## Financial support opportunities > Graduate Administrative Assistantship
Discover More

Graduate Administrative Assistants (GAA) are typically full-time graduate students who participate in administrative activities at the University in exchange for financial support
