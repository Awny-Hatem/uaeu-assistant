## Academics > Grading System
Accreditation

UAEU is accredited by UAE Ministry of Higher Education & Research. College of Engineering is ABET-accredited, College of Business and Economics is AACSB-accredited, College of Education holds International recognition based on NCATE Standards, and the Bachelor's program in IT is accredited by the Computing Accreditation Commission (CAC) of ABET.

Study Scheme

UAEU follows a credit-hours System of (15-16) weeks duration per semester with Full Time method.

Teaching Method

Teaching method is regular attendance On Campus.

Teaching Language

All major courses are taught in English, except for the majors that are solely in Arabic.

Credits Transfer

Courses transferred from other universities are considered for approval. Approved-transferred courses do not count towards calculating the GPA.

Academic Probation Scheme

Bachelor Degree:

(Batches up to 2002) A student is put under academic probation, if, after completing 30 credit hours of study and his GPA is below 1.75 points.

(Batches 2003 - 2008) A student is put under academic probation, if, after completing 30 credit hours of study and his GPA is below 2.00 points.

(Batches from 2009) A student is put under academic probation, if, after completing 21 credit hours of study and his GPA is below 2.00 points.

Diploma: A student is put under academic probation, if, after completing 12 credit hours of study and his GPA is below 2.00 points

Graduate Studies: A student is put under academic probation, if his GPA is below 3.00 points, or he gets an (F /FA) in one of the courses.
