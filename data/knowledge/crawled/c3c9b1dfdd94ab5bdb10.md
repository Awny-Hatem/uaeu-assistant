## IELTS Results
Your IELTS results will be available 13 days after the test. Results will be shown on your Test Report Form. This report details your score for each of the four skills, as well as an overall band score. Candidates will receive only one copy of the Test Report Form. It's important that you keep it safe!

Candidates who took their exam through UAEU IELTS Centre can view their results in one of two ways:

Option 1: Visit the registration office at the Continuing Education Center on the 1st floor and show your ID.

Option 2: Visit IELTS online results and follow the instructions. You will need to enter your test date, identification document number, date of birth and candidate number.

Please note that centers are not permitted to give results over the phone, by fax, or by email. You can authorize a representative to pick up the Test Report Form for you by completing and submitting the authorization form to the IELTS administrator at the center for approval.

Enquiry on Results (EOR)

If you are not happy with your results, you can apply for an EoR at the centre

You must do this within six weeks of the test date

This service costs 700 AED which is fully refunded if your score improves.

You will receive a Test Report Form (TRF), with your overall score. The TRF includes your photograph for security purposes. Your centre can arrange for an official TRF to be sent free of charge to up to five organizations, if they are detailed on the Application Form. If not, the centre will charge you AED 150 for any additional official TRF. Candidates will be charged for courier costs (Local: AED 30, International: AED 80)
