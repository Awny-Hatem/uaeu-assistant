## Office of Associate Provost for Research
As the world rapidly evolves, driven by innovation, knowledge, and discovery, the United Arab Emirates University stands at the forefront, committed to creating a meaningful and lasting impact on future generations.

UAEU is the research powerhouse of the nation, a place where bold ideas take shape, pressing challenges are tackled, and transformative solutions are brought to life. As Associate Provost for Research, my mission is to empower our faculty and students to pursue research that not only advances knowledge, but also delivers real-world impact for the university, the UAE, and the global community.

We are building a dynamic research ecosystem that is collaborative, sustainable, and driven by purpose. From unlocking funding opportunities and safeguarding intellectual property to forging strategic partnerships with government, industry, and society, we are aligning our research efforts with national priorities and global challenges.

At UAEU, we believe the future begins here, sparked by curiosity, shaped by knowledge, and realized through discovery. Every problem we solve and every idea we explore moves us closer to a better, smarter, and more sustainable world.

Dr. Rami Beiram,

Associate Provost for Research
