College of Medicine and Health Sciences  (CMHS)



CMHS graduates have to do one year of internship                                               in the hospital (managed by SEHA, DHA and MOH) before getting the
license to practice or enroll in the residency program.  The internship experience does not have any associated credit
hours.

CMHS students have the opportunity to work at                                         overseas hospital or hospitals in the UAE in the specialty of their choice
during their internal or external elective courses which is part of the curriculum. During summer, students have options to
engage in a non-                                credit bearing activity such as research            study or hospita             l work.
