institutions      including      schools.           The      purpose      of      the      lab      is      to      stimulate      the      passion      for      physics      through      simple

important role of physics in the national development of science and technology.

Department Vision:

The    Department    of    Physics    at    UAEU    aspires    to    become    an    international    center    of    excellence    in    physics
education and research, with emphasis on areas of vital importance to the Emirates society. The department
also aims to obtain international accreditation to ensure international standards of its learning outcomes.

Department Mission:

The   department   is   committed   to   provide   high   quality   of   physics   education   by   continuously   improving   its
programs via benchmarking with international standards and by seeking international accreditation.

The department will maintain research activities in many fields of physics, with focus on areas of strategic
importance     to     the     nation,     aiming     to     enhance     research     output     and     attract     excellent     scholars     and     graduate
students to the department.

The department will continuously seek efforts to promote public awareness of the important role of physics
in  the  national  development  of  science  and  technology  and  its  direct  impact  on  the  economy  and  lifestyle
of the nation.



What is Physics?

Physics  is  the  study  of  the  laws  of  nature
that                   govern                   the                   behavior                   of                   the                   uni-
verse,                 from                 the                 very                 smallest                 scales
of                      sub-atomic                      particles                      to                      the                      very
largest        scales        of        cosmology.        It        applies
these         laws         to         the         solution         of         practical
problems                    and                   to                   the                   development                   of
new           technologies.           Physics           is           becoming
increasingly                                                                            interdisciplinary,                                                                             as
physicists             work             with             mathematicians,
engineers,                  chemists                  and                 biologists                 in
order               to               understand               and               solve               a               wide
range of problems confronting society.
