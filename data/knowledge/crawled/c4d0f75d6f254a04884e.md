## Enterprise Application and Integration (EAI)
Enterprise Applications and Integration Department (EAI) is committed to the University’s vision of transforming UAE University into a research focused institute through providing high quality IT services and resources to support the University’s academic and administrative functions. EAI has been contributing in an effective and innovative use of information technology by utilizing the current and emerging technologies to plan, design and develop application systems.
