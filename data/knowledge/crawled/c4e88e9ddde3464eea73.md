## Grants
Research Funding Overview

The Zayed Bin Sultan Center for Health Sciences has secured more than AED 66.4 million in competitive research funding between 2017 and 2026, supporting 131 funded research projects across a broad spectrum of research areas. This sustained funding success has enabled the supervision and training of over 69 postgraduate students, 45 undergraduate students, and 45 research assistants, significantly strengthening research capacity and talent development at the national level.

Funding Growth Over Time (2017–2026

Research funding at ZCHS reflects sustained growth, competitive strength, and strategic institutional advancement. The steady expansion of funded projects demonstrates the Center’s continued success in securing internal and external support while advancing high-impact, multidisciplinary research. The Center maintains an expanding network of national and international partnerships, including collaborations with Zayed University and NYU Abu Dhabi, as well as leading global institutions such as Asian Universities Alliance ( AUA), ETH Zurich, the University of Glasgow, the University of California, King Saud University, and Nazarbayev University, among others. These high-impact collaborations enhance research excellence, promote knowledge exchange, and further position ZCHS as a leading regional hub for interdisciplinary health research and innovation.

Funding Portfolio Breakdown (2017-2026)

ZCHS investigators actively compete in and secure funding through a broad range of competitive research programs offered by the United Arab Emirates University and its strategic partners. The Center’s funding portfolio currently includes:

55 UAEU Competitive Projects

50 Center-Based Research Grants

21 Joint & Collaborative Projects

4 SDGs Projects

This diversified structure reflects strong institutional engagement, interdisciplinary research capacity, and collaborative reach.
