## Sustainable Development Goals > SDG Art Competition
The UAEU SDG Art Competition will increase the awareness among students and UAEU community towards UN Sustainable Development Goals. The participating students become part of the team to support UAEU SDG mission by promoting social values and by educating the community through their creativity.

## Sustainable Development Goals > 2000 AED > General Rules & Regulations
Entries must follow the theme.

Digital drawings cannot be accepted.

Should include one drawing caption (About your Art).

Brief of the artwork must not exceed 30 words.

Last date to submit the artwork is 17 th Feb 2023

Judging Committee Decision is Final.

## Sustainable Development Goals > 2000 AED > Registration Details
Register yourself by sending email to sdg@uaeu.ac.ae

Selected artwork will be presented at the SDG Art exhibition during innovation month.

Winners will be announced by the end of exhibition.
