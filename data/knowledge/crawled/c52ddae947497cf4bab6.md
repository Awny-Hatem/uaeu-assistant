4. Missing Resident Student

Overview
Summarizes action to be taken following report of a missing student.

Scope
Applies to all students enrolled in the University.

Objective
The University cares about the safety and wellbeing of its students and takes all measures to
ensure both. This policy addresses the action to be taken if a resident student is reported
missing.

Policy
1.                            The University has the responsibility to take appropriate action to report a missing
      student.
2.                                    A student is presumed to be missing when his/her absence is inconsistent with his/her
      established patterns of behavior    and the deviation cannot be readily explained and when
      he/she cannot be reached within a reasonable period of time.
3.                              Before presuming that a student                                                                           is missing, reasonable measures should be taken to
      determine  if  the  student  is at his/her               off-campus place                                                        of residence and if             anyone familiar
      with the student               has seen or heard from him/her              recentl y or is aware of where he/she                                                       ma y
      be.
4.                                                       The Dean of Students is the responsible person             to take action when a student is confirmed
      missing.

Procedures of Policy No. (4 ) - Missing Resident Student

1.                                    If anyone believes a resident student is missing, it is essential for the safety and wellbeing
        of the University community that the pr oper authorities are notified immediately.

2.                         When a resident student is reported missing to the housing representatives, the Student
        Housing Director must be informed immediately. The housing representatives will
        attempt to locate the missing resident student. If the missing resident student is not found,
        the Student Housing Director must immediately inform the Dean of Students who will
        notify other appropriate authorities.

3.                              The Dean of Students will notify the resident student’s emergency contact or the next of
        kin no more than 24 hours after the resident student has been reported missing.

4.                             The Dean of Students has the right to inform the authorities if the student is missing for
        more than 24 hours.

5.                           If the student is found not to have been missing, accordingly the Dean of Students will
        investigate the matter.  The student may be subject to disciplinary action should it be
        determined that he/she has broken the Code of Conduct and/or other regulations.
