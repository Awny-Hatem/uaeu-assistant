## Department of Economics and Finance > Overview
Welcome to the Department of Economics and Finance at the College of Business and Economics. The Department’s faculty members conduct teaching and research in a wide variety of areas, including: labor economics, energy economics, economic development and international finance. This expertise translates into high-quality instruction at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

Our main contribution to undergraduate teaching is through the Bachelor of Economics. We provide a strong theoretical and empirical foundation through core courses (required), balanced with specialist elective courses, such as Energy Economics, Public Economics, Project Economics, and Applied Economics of the Middle East to name but a few. To see a full course list of what is currently available, have a look at our course catalogue.

We strongly encourage our students to join the Economics Club, which organizes a number of academic and social events during each semester, on the advice of faculty members.

Whether you are a prospective student, an academic interested in our teaching or research programs, or simply browsing, we hope that you will find the information you need. If you require any further information, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Dr. Osama Sweidan

Department Chair

## Department of Economics and Finance > Mission
To foster public sector and social innovation and renewal, through:

Sector-leading programs in public administration

Being the 'go-to' department for research and consulting in the region

Being to 'go-to' department for economic assessment of policies in the region

Being the 'go-to' department for development and social innovation in the region

## Department of Economics and Finance > Research
In the Department of Economics and Finance, we focus on the following key research areas:

Energy and Environmental Economics

Macroeconomics and Monetary Economics

Labour and Development Economics

International Economics

Asset Pricing

Corporate Finance, and Regulation

Energy, Sustainability, and Financial Innovation

Financial Modeling and Econometrics

Islamic and Entrepreneurial Finance

The department has a significant pool of international academic talent, who make substantial contributions to economics and finance research. They have sound expertise in publishing in top international journals. They also serve on the editorial boards of reputable regional and international journals, and act as peer and external reviewers and actively participate in international conferences in both disciplines.
