## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > Location
The National Research Pioneers Program is hosted at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) main campus in Al Ain .

Program sessions and research activities will take place across designated training venues within the campus.

Students are expected to attend in person, Monday to Friday, during working hours. Access to labs, collaborative spaces, and other university resources will be provided throughout the program.
