3. Faculty Responsibilities

Overview
Defines faculty responsibilities with respect to their roles as teachers, researchers, and
community members.

Scope
Applies to all UAEU faculty members            and instructors except those in the CMHS          .

Objective
Defines the responsibilities of the                faculty with respect to the central functions of the UAEU
and sets out the standards by which their contribution will be assessed.

Policy
1.                                                           Facult y members arebound by the Code of Professional Ethics.
2.                                          A faculty member is expected to spend at least 40 hours per week in teaching, research
        and service to the University and to the community.
