## Editorial Board
The Editorial Board provides strategic leadership for the journal, ensuring the highest standards of scholarly publishing, editorial quality, and international engagement. Working collaboratively with the Executive and Managing Editors, Associate Editors and specialist Working Groups contribute their expertise to guide the journal's editorial direction, uphold publishing excellence, and foster a vibrant global community of authors, reviewers, and readers.

Executive Editor: Edward A. Fox (USA)

## Editorial Working Groups
The Editorial Working Groups provide specialised leadership across key areas of the journal's operations. Together, they support editorial excellence, publishing innovation, ethical practice, and community engagement, ensuring the journal continues to evolve in response to the needs of the global scholarly community.

## Editorial Working Groups > Ethics & Policy Working Group
The Ethics & Policy Working Group provides leadership on editorial ethics, publication policies, and governance. The group supports the development and review of ethical frameworks, oversees best practices in peer review, promotes research integrity, and ensures that the journal operates in accordance with internationally recognised publishing standards.

## Editorial Working Groups > Publishing & Infrastructure Working Group
The Publishing & Infrastructure Working Group oversees the journal's publishing platform, technical infrastructure, and production workflows. The group guides the implementation of metadata standards, indexing and discoverability, digital preservation, and continuous improvements to ensure an efficient and sustainable publishing environment.

The Editorial Development & Content Working Group shapes the journal's editorial direction by identifying emerging topics, coordinating special issues, strengthening the quality of submissions, and supporting initiatives that enhance the journal's scholarly impact and international relevance.

The Community Engagement Working Group fosters relationships with authors, reviewers, readers, and partner organisations around the world. The group promotes the journal through outreach activities, supports reviewer and author engagement, and helps build an active and inclusive global scholarly community.
