## Project Management Unit (PMU)
The Project Management Unit (PMU) supports the University Information Technology division's commitment to more effective and efficient, consistent and repeatable project management practices, enabling project managers to successfully deliver their promised value to their customers.

The PMU at DoIT provides a project management culture that ensures common methodology and standards are shared across all IT projects and projects are delivered on time, within budget, and with the anticipated results.

The PMU centralizes and coordinates management of the IT portfolio of projects and provides status reporting of IT projects. This includes identifying, prioritizing, authorizing, managing, and controlling projects, and other related work, to achieve specific strategic business objectives.

To support consistent project management practices, the PMU provides tools, templates, training, project management framework and other resources to project managers, project team members, and project stakeholders for IT projects that are managed by DoIT.
