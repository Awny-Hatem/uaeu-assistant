Definitions and Abbreviations
The Definitions and Abbreviations used in these policies and procedures have the following
meanings, unless otherwise is indicated in the text:
−  Academic Year                     : A  one-year  period  established  annually  in   the  official
                                       University calendar
−  CIO                               : Chief Information Officer
−  Chancellor                        : The Chancellor of the United Arab Emirates University
−  Dean                              : The Dean of the College
−  DoIT                              : The University Information Technology Division
−  Faculty Member                    : Professors, Associate Professors, and Assistant Professors
−  HR Department                     : Human Resources Department
−  ID                                : Identification
−  IT                                : Information Technology
−  Law                               : Federal  Law  No.  (4)  for  1976  on  the  Establishment  and
                                       Organization of the UAEU
−  Manual                            : A policies and procedures manual is a functional guide for
                                       conducting the operations of a division or a department. The
                                       manual typically consists of policies, procedures and signatory
                                       authority matrix
−  Network                           : The United Arab Emirates University Network
−  Network Devices                   : any physical component that forms part of the underlying
                                       connectivity infrastructure for a network, such as a router,
                                       switch, hub, bridge, gateway, etc.
−  OS                                : Operating System such Windows, Mac, Linux, UNIX.
−  Patch                             : A fix to a known problem with an OS or software program.
                                       For the purposes of this
−  Policy                            : A policy is a guide to decision-making under a given set of
                                       circumstances within the framework of corporate objectives,
                                       goals and management philosophies
−  Procedure                         : A procedure is a series of steps followed in a definite regular order
                                       that ensures the consistent and repetitive approach to actions
−  Provost                           : The Deputy Vice Chancellor for Academic Affairs & Provost
