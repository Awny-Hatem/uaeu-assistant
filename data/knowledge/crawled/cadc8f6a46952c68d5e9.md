The Microsoft Gulf Intaleq Internship programme                      is designed to provide
                                                   business and technical students             entering their final year of university with
                                                   work  experience  at  Microsoft  that  will  help  them  to  transition  between
                                                   academic  and  professional  life.  Students  who  are  successful  in  the  Intaleq
                                                   program may also have the opportunity to join Microsoft’s Graduate Program –
                                                   Microsoft Academy of College Hires (MACH) once they have completed their
                                                   studies and become a part of Microsoft full time. The Intaleq program gives
students a mix of both real life on the job learning and classroom skills training that will aim to give them the opportunity
to grow their professional experience.

The Opportunities:
Opportunities in the UAE for 2011 are to be confirmed but may include

        Associate Account Manager responsible for driving opportunities with the Public Sector Account Manager and
         presenting offerings for Academic products in the education system.
        Associate Technology Specialist responsible for driving opportunities with the Account Manager and presenting
         Microsoft Technologies to customers.
        Associate Project Manager          responsible for assisting the Project Manager in project management & planning,
         coordinating resources and assisting in customer meetings.

Application Requirements:

To be eligible to apply for Intaleq opportunities you must:
    o    Be a pre final or final year student of a degree program related to business, sales & marketing or information
         technology
    o    A GPA equivalent to 3.0 or above
    o    Have a passion for technology
    o    Fluent Arabic, English and interpersonal, presentation and communication skills
    o    Be a strategic thinker
    o    Be able to balance individual creativity with working as a team
    o    Have a high degree of customer focus

Microsoft supports workforce diversity and welcomes
applications from Gulf National and Female graduates.
