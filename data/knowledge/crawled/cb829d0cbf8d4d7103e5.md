## Emirates Health Services > Overview
In June 2010, the Emirates Health Services (EHS) was established under the umbrella of the College of Medicine & Health Sciences (CMHS). The overall objective of the EHS is to facilitate clinical activity and provision of clinical teaching, assessment, advice, management and patient care. Potential recipients of the services include hospitals and healthcare facilities, health ministries, health care and other industries, national and international medical and health organizations, and members of the UAE community.

The main services provided by EHS are:

Professional skills development, lectures, seminars, workshops, conferences, and clinical training. These are conducted under the auspices of an active CME (Continuing Medical Education)/CPD (Continuing professional Development) program. EHS is able to award recognized CME points for continuing registration by medical licensing authorities. This is the responsibility of the CME Office of EHS.

Assessment of professional skills and competency. Post-graduate medical examinations for a license to practice in the UAE, and for membership of Royal Colleges and their Faculties are conducted by EHS. This is the responsibility of the examination and Assessment Office of EHS.

Provision of clinical services, including management of healthcare facilities, and supporting accreditation for hospitals and clinical facilities. This is a direct responsibility of the EHS Office.
