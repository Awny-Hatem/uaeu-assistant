## Field Experience Office > About FEO
Welcome to the Field Experience Office website!

The Field Experience Office (FEO) at the College of Education oversees school-based clinical experiences for candidate students under the guidance of expert faculty members and field professionals. To ensure candidates experience successful, positive, practical, and diverse field experiences throughout the program, the FEO supports students before, during, and after student teaching while providing opportunities to observe, interact and teach in authentic settings to develop knowledge, skills and competencies necessary to enter the education profession as highly effective and enthusiastic teachers.
