## Office of Associate Provost for Students Affairs > Admission & Registration Deanship
We hope to provide you with the necessary information and services to assist you in shaping your academic and future professional life. We invite you to visit our splendid campus in Al Ain, tour our state-of-the-art facilities, meet our enthusiastic students and elite faculty members, and learn for yourself how so many outstanding young men and women from around the world have chosen UAEU to achieve their academic and lifelong endeavors.

The Admission & Registration Deanship consists of three departments:

## Office of Associate Provost for Students Affairs > Admission & Registration Deanship > Admissions
The Admissions Department provides the necessary support to students who are soon to be admitted to the UAEU and to current students in all matters related to admissions. Students are admitted to UAEU on the basis of an assessment of their present academic qualifications and the instructional capacities of academic programs. UAEU offers students an unparalleled learning experience in an exceptional educational environment.

## Office of Associate Provost for Students Affairs > Admission & Registration Deanship > Registration
The Registration Department provides many services to current students & graduates that include managing student records, managing student registration schedules, liaising directly with faculty advising units, issuing official transcripts and graduation diplomas, processing grades, etc.

## Office of Associate Provost for Students Affairs > Admission & Registration Deanship > Student Accounts Office
The Student Accounts Office manages student account transactions for tuition, housing, general deposits and other charges on a student's account. We also manage the invoicing and processing of student refunds. Students who require a tuition payment plan can contact us for arrangements. We strive to provide excellent customer service and will respond to any inquiry you may have regarding your account.
