Requirement:
•    Have Master’s and Bachelor’s degrees.
•    At least one of the degrees (Master’s degree or Bachelor’s degree) must be in Biological Science, Biomedical
     Science, Pharmacy, Medical, Biomedical Engineering, Biomedical Informatics, Health Informatics or other
     related fields.
•    High interest in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.
•    Have good character and attitude.
•    A score of at least 6.5 on the IELTS or an equivalent TOFEL score.
•    A team player and have strong communication skills.
•    Good oral and written proficiency in English.
