## Covid-19 Information > Students
Published academic-year scope: 2020-2021
Dear Students,

We would like to congratulate you on the beginning of the second semester of the academic year 2020/2021, wishing you continued success.

We offer you a set of instructions that we hope everyone adheres to, for your safety and the safety of everyone.

It is necessary to present the results of the new Corona virus examination (PCR), up to 72 hours, before allowing students to board the bus and show the result through the Al-Hosn Application or through the oficial text message issued by the competent authories.

If there are members of your family infected with Covid 19 virus, the student must stay inthe residential units and not leave the emirate.

If your contact witha person infected with Covid 19 virus in the emirate, the student must not retun to the residence, and inform the health services unit on the following email: healht.services@uaeu.ac.ae

Students coming from outside the emirate of Abu Dhabi must abide by the state's procedures for re-examination (nose swab, PCR) on the sixth day, as the student will bear the costs of the medical examination.

Students must visit the residence clinic in B3 to perform the required tests, for AED 85/- (cash or visa)

## Covid-19 Information > Students > Information for Students > On site Classes
Students entering the University Campus are expected to present a COVID-19 negative PCR results as follows:

Not more than a month old for students living in Al Ain and Abu Dhabi

Not more than 48hrs for students coming from other Emirates and a repeat result after 6 days in Al Ain or Abu Dhabi

The results and your temperature will be checked at every entry

No entry is allowed without a face mask

Students are always expected to follow safety and social distance protocols. Keep updated.

Library

The main library opening hours are Sun Thurs 8.00am to 9.00pm

Please kindly follow the seating arrangements instructions and other protocols

Keep checking the UAEU website and email for any updates on regulations and library timings

University Housing

For students of the northern regions and their subordinate region (Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras al-Khaimah, Umm Al Quwain, Fujairah):

To enter the university residence, the result of the nasal swab test (PCR) is required. The result should be negative and not exceed 48hours.

Compulsory: A re-testing (PCR) for all students on the sixth day in case of staying in the residence.

Students from the Emirate of Abu Dhabi and its affiliates areas:

Compulsory: Entry to the university residence requires the result of the nasal swab test (PCR). The result should be negative and not exceed 48hours.

Students are required to sign in and sign out when entering and leaving the hostels.

How to protect yourself

Wash your hands regularly use soap or approved hand sanitizer.

Stay at home and in the hostel if no need to go out.

When you go out, keep your mask on.

Change your masks regularly.

If you are sick, stay

Stay away from infected cases even if they are family

What to do if you get sick?

Call Ministry of Health & Prevention on 800-11111, Department of Health Abu Dhabi on 800-1717 or Dubai Health Authority on 800-342 for COVID-19 related health

Stay isolated until you get tested unless you are advised to

Keep hydrated

Eat healthy

Get medical treatment if symptoms worsen

Dining halls/Restaurants

All students must follow the following precautionary measures:

Wearing masks during the period of stay in the dining hall. It is allowed to take off mask only while eating

Commitment to the distance between students which is indicated by stickers provided in the dining halls

Commitment to being present only for the minimum possible time, to allow the rest of the students to eat their meals

Obligation to wait in the designated places in case the dining hall is full

Students commitment to all restaurant regulations and obligations previously specified in the dining hall
