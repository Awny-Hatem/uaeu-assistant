## Technology Center > Technology Center
The Technology Center provides opportunities to build practical, in-demand technical skills. We offer FREE hands-on workshops in programming, data analysis, and emerging technologies, delivered as multi-session series so you can fully master each tool.

## Technology Center > Technology Center > Our current workshops include:
Introduction to Python Programming (typically 9–10 workshops to cover all the material)

Introduction to R (typically 9–10 workshops to cover all the material)

Introduction to MATLAB and Simulink (typically 8–10 workshops to cover all the material)

Handling Big Data with Excel

Introduction to MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Visio)

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Robotics (EV3 interface)

The Technology Center actively engages postgraduate (PG) students to help design and deliver additional topics based on emerging needs and is ready to collaborate with other colleges to extend these workshops across the university.
