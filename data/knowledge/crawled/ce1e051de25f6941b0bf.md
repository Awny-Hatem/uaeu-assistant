## Zeyad Jaffal > Personal profile > Research interests
Research interests include public international law, with particular emphasis on the general theory and sources of international law; international criminal law; the law of the sea and maritime security; international human rights law; international humanitarian law; and international counter-terrorism law. Current research also addresses the use of force, cyber operations and emerging technologies, international organizations, environmental protection during armed conflict, and the relationship between international law and global governance.
