             Conditions and documents required
             by students applying for financial aid
                (Cash Aid, IPad device, laptop)

## PDF page 2
Conditions for receiving cash financial aid:

1.  The student should be registered on the electronic Financial Aid System by logging into
    E-Services   and uploading all the required documents.
2.  The student should be on regular study.
3.  The monthly income average          shouldn’t exceed AED 2000 per one person in the student
    family for national students and AED 1000 for non-national students.
4.  The student should open his/her own bank account.

    * Note: The Financial Aid System registration opens at the beginning of each semester
    for four weeks, according to the academic calendar of the university.

## PDF page 3
Conditions for receiving an IPad device or a computer laptop:

1.  The student should be registered on the electronic Financial Aid System by logging
    into E-Services    and uploading all the required documents.
2.  The student should be on regular study.
3.  The monthly income average shouldn’t AED 2000                exceed per one person in the
    student family and AED 1000 for non national students.
4.  The student’s remaining hours to graduate shouldn’t be less than 60 credit hours.
5.  The student should be enrolled in courses that require the device.

    * Note: The Financial Aid System registration is opened at the beginning of each
    semester for four weeks, according to the academic calendar of the university.

## PDF page 4
The documents required for receiving financial aid services:
1.  A copy of the University Student Card.
2.  A copy of the Student’s Emirates Identity Card.
3.  A copy of the Student Passport and his/her family members (father + mother + unmarried
    and not working male sibling brothers under the age of 25 + female unmarried sisters who
    do not work as well as a copy of Khulasat           Al Qaid   for National students.
4.  Recent Certificate (not to exceed six months) showing the Total Income or Salary for the
    student guardian, or pension or monthly alimony of the student’s mother.
5.  A Recent Certificate from the Ministry of Social Affairs, for those whom receive aid.
6.  If the student’s guardian is unemployed: An Adoption Certificate from the Legality Court is
    required.
7.  In the event of student’s parent death: A copy of the Death Certificate.
8.  A copy of student’s Home Rent Contract.
9.  For those who have bank loans: a Bank Account Statement for six months showing the loan
    amount and monthly installments.
10. A completed Zakat Fund form  to be completed yearly                  in order to apply for Cash Aid. This
    form can be found on Financial Aid webpage.
11. A Certificate of To Whom it May Concern from the Bank showing the IBAN (International
    Student's Bank Account Number) that the financial aid will be transferred to if approved.
    *  Please enter the family passports copies or          Khulasat   Al Qaid into a single PDF file format.

## PDF page 5
Notes:

1.  If there is an individual family member of the student who is greater than 18 years old
    and does not work, he will be calculated as two members when studying the financial
    situation of the family, however evidence must be provided.
2.  The University has the right to request additional documents from the student or can
    exempt them from some of the documents listed according to the student's status,
    nationality and system acceptance of UAEU.
3.  The University has the right to pay out the aid or stop it according to the budget
    available from supportive external sources.
4.  In case of Zakat fund ending service, the documents should be updated at the
    beginning of each academic semester, there will be a stopping period for the cash aid
    until the cash aid request documents are updated, reviewed and transferred to the
    Zakat fund system.
5.  If some documents are missing, your request will be rejected.
6.  If you experience a problem in the system, please communicate with
    helpdesk@uaeu.ac.ac.
