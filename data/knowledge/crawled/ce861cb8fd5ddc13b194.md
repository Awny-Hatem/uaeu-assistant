          Students should attain a cumulative GPA ≥ 2.0
           1.    Successfully complete (27 - 30) credit hrs. Prior to joining the College including  the following:
                 -     21 credit hours offered by University General Requirements Unit.
                 -     6 - 9 credit hours including the following courses:
                      (Exploratory Educational Experiences and Communication Skills and/ Or Thinking Skills)
           2.    Pass the Basic Skills Test – BST - (Thinking Skills, Writing Skills, Reading Skills & Math Skills).
           3.    Obtain ≥ 2.0 (C) in the Exploratory Educational Experiences course mentioned in 3 above.
           4.    Pass an interview upon submitting her application.
Additional Condition for Students who selecting Elementary Education English Track:
Gain at least 5.0 on the IELTS Exam Or Overall GPA of at least 3.00 With at Least (C) in English / 3.
