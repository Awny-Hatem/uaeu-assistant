# FAQs - New Students

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## How can I view and pay my tuition fees online?
You can view and pay your tuition fees through the UAEU student portal. Check the following link for the steps: E-services

## What payment options are available for tuition and housing?
Payment options includes bank transfer and online payment using a smart wallet or bank cards through the student portal.

## Can I pay for my fees in installment?
UAEU offers a tuition payment plan that divides the term's outstanding balance into three payments. Visit the link here for policies, guidelines, and steps to apply for the service: Click Here

## How can I view my tuition fees plan “Installment service”?
You can view the Installment service plan through the UAEU student portal.

E-service > Student > Tuition Fee Installments

## How do I request a refund?
In the event that a student withdraws from a class during the term, refunds of tuition and fees will be calculated according to the following schedule: Click Here

The extra amount will be held as a credit in the student account and will be refunded after the student's graduation.

## Who can I contact for issues related to my student account?
You can contact the Student Accounts Office via email : Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae

## What should I do if I have a hold on my account?
A hold will be placed on your account if the payments are not made. The Student Accounts Office will monitor the payments, and if they are made, the hold will be removed. If the issue persists, you can contact the office for assistance: Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae

## How can I obtain a receipt for my tuition payment?
You can view your tuition fees receipt through the UAEU student portal by following these steps:

Student > Student Account > Statement and Payment History

## What are the penalties for late payment?
Students who fail to pay the tuition fees by the deadline will not be permitted to access academic services, and are required to cancel their current and future registrations. Additionally, newly admitted students will have their admissions canceled, while enrolled students will face suspension due to non-payment of tuition fees.

## What are the admission fees?
For all decrees, the admission fees are 210 AED.
