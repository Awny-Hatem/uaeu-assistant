## Internal Medicine Department > Facilities
The Department of Internal Medicine has the following resources:

One large-sized Audio-Visual Seminar Room in common use with other departments.

A Clinical Skills Stimulation Center in collaboration with the Medical Education Department.

Two well equipped laboratory units for research.

Four full time research staff and grant funded temporarily appointed research assistant staff.

The lab features a large facility capable of analyzing a variety of hormones utilizing Immunoassay (IA) and Enzyme-linked immunoassay (ELISA) techniques. An automated laboratory capable of measuring most routine substrates and clinical enzymes utilizing an Integra 400 plus and e411 immunochemistry analyzer from Roche. The lab is having capability of performing Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) and analyzing most cytokines, tissue markers using an ELx800 Micro plate Reader. Sample processing unit provides standard refrigerated centrifugation, freezer storage options to 70°, computerized labeling, and assistance with sample packaging for shipping.
