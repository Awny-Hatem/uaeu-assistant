## Speaking Center > Speaking Center
The Speaking Center provides opportunities to become more confident and effective speakers of English. We offer FREE practice in English speaking & listening, presentations, debates, public speaking, IELTS exam speaking and listening and interview skills. We also offer public speaking and debate workshops.

The Modern Language Program provides learning and practice Asian and European languages such as: German, French, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin Chinese, Arabic for non-native speakers, Spanish or Turkish.

The Speaking Center Blackboard features self-access learning resources to support a range of speaking skills: presentations, public speaking, discussions, and IELTS speaking exams.
