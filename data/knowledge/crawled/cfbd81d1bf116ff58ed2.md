## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Irrigation Lab
The Irrigation Lab serves mainly educational purposes. The students can conduct experiments which illustrate the basic relationships of pipe hydraulics. The lab also provides exhibits of valves, pumps and other important parts of irrigation systems. These exhibits are cut views, so the function of these parts is easily comprehensible. A test field where irrigation systems can be demonstrated is in the planning stage.
