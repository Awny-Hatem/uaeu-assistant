## MSTE > MSTE
7th Mathematics, Science & Educational

Technology Annual Forum ( 10 th March 2015 )

Smart Learning in Math and Science

Introduction:

MSTE seventh annual Forum is dedicated to exchanging innovative ideas about ‎improving mathematics and science learning using the latest advances of technology. It mainly focuses on students’ active learning of these vital ‎subjects in digital rich environments. It provides an opportunity for ‎mathematics and science educators as well as educators specialized in the ‎area of technology-integration into teaching and learning to exchange ‎experiences and explore innovations. The forum will consist of ‎discussions, research-presentations, and hands-on workshops about educational issues ‎and pedagogical innovations in the areas of mathematics, science and educational ‎technology. ‎
