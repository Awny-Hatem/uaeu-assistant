PROGRAM OVERVIEW


The Pathfinders Program will empower
participants, and guide them in choosing their
own path and making their own impact on the
world. The transformative transdisciplinary
curriculum offers participants a strong
connection to the real-world and uses a system
thinking approach, while enhancing their
entrepreneurial mindset.
Pathfinders will eventually become intentional
learners, always aiming to explore challenges
faced by our global society, and seeking the
skills and transdisciplinary knowledge necessary
to solve these challenges.

SCOPE AND ROLE


This internship takes place over two periods, the first one
is 20-weeks long (5 months), and the second is 16-
weeks long (4 months). This is a full-time opportunity,
that requires skills to engage in an immersive visitor
experience that reflects UAEU’s ambitious plans for its
pavilion during EXPO 2020.
Interns will offer support to visitors who wish to
experience the UAEU pavilion through its permanent and
temporary exhibitions, this includes support services and
assistance offered to special needs visitors. Interns are
also expected to manage all visitor queries, offer
guidance, troubleshoot and liaise with UAEU staff, and
support in merchandise sales.

PROGRAMME: OBJECTIVES



The Pathfinders Programme              is a six-month
programme               to pilot the Curriculum of the Future.
It is the outcome of the design thinking process
adopted by the UAEU Task Force to develop the
Curriculum of the Future.
It uses Expo 2020 as a testbed, to systematically
enrich the CoF.
The programme                              will be offered to 30 students or
fresh graduates that will be selected from different
countries with a focus on Arab and African
universities.
