## Excellence Awards
UAEU operates an Excellence Awards Program that gives formal recognition full-time faculty members and instructors who excel in their teaching, scholarship and service. The award gives formal recognition to individuals who have achieved this standard and have thus made outstanding contribution to the University’s Mission.

The award carries a certificate of appreciation and monetary value, and includes 7 different categories as follows:

College Award for Excellence in Teaching

College Award for Excellence in Scholarship

College Award for Excellence in Service

University Award for Excellence in Teaching

University Award for Excellence in Scholarship

University Award for Excellence in Service

Distinguished Faculty Award
