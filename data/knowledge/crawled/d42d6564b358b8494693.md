## Internal Audit Office > Internal Audit Office
The Internal Audit Office was established by Chancellor’s Decree no. 63 in 2013. The office is both independent and objective in fulfilling its responsibilities under the Chancellor’s direct authority. This guarantees an objective auditing process that retains full integrity and conforms to international standards.
