                                                                      Human Resources Committee


I. Policy

                     The Human Resources Committee shall be responsible for:

                                •                                  Reviewing and advising the Provost on human resources policies.
                                •                         Reviewing and advising the Director of Human Resources on human
                                           resources procedures.
                                •                            Reviewing and advising the Provost on various systems of incentives,
                                           remuneration and allowances granted to University personnel.
                                •                                   Exercising any other powers assigned to it by the Provost.

                     Meetings

                     The Human Resources Committee shall hold meetings at the invitation of the
                     Provost or the committee chair.


II.                                                                           Application

                     This policy shall apply to all personnel employed by the United Arab Emirates
                     University.
