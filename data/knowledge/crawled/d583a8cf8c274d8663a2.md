2. Undergraduate Admissions

Overview
Covers policies and procedures relating to:
1.                                                                         The admission of undergraduate students,                             including                 the        application for admission,
         transfer admission, readmission, admission with advanced standing, and
2.                                                                         Student identification cards and campus access for admitted students.

Scope
Applies to all undergraduate                                      students and all academic and related administrative units of the
UAEU, except those in the College                                   of Medicine and Health Sciences          .

Objective
Assures that the student admission function of the University
1.                                                                         Is conducted in a manner that is consistent with the academic principles, standards, and
         expectations of the University appropriate to its educational mission and academic
         programs; and             that it
2.                                                                         Operates with fairness, consistency,integrity and effectiveness                                   .

       the context of applicable general requirements established by the         University C            ouncil.

       current policies of the UAE Universit y.

       annually.
