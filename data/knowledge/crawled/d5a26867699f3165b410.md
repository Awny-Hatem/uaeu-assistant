## Document Delivery
Inter-library loan or document delivery is a service provided to UAEU affiliates* who are teaching or conducting research for obtaining documents not available in the University Libraries. Please check library resources for availability through the online catalog and databases before requesting documents.

Eligibility and Criteria

UAEU affiliates are eligible to request documents through Resource Sharing Service.

Requested documents should be relevant to current curriculum/research.

Requests can be submitted via using the online form below or printed and submitted to the Reference Desk in any University library.

On average, documents are received within two weeks of request.

Requestor will be notified by email upon receipt of printed document(s).

* UAEU affiliates include: Faculty, Instructors, Graduate and Undergraduate students and Administrative staff.

In case of error in the network, please contact Ms.Mariam Al Ahbabi and send your request to mryam.ayed@uaeu.ac.ae

Fields mark with * are mandatory.
