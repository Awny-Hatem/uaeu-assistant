## College of Medicine and Health Sciences > Overview
At the College of Medicine and Health Sciences, we are committed to fostering excellence in medical education, research, and healthcare. Our institution stands as a beacon of knowledge and innovation in the field of medicine, dedicated to producing highly skilled and compassionate healthcare professionals. Established in 1984 under the visionary leadership of His Excellency Sheikh Nahayan Mubarak Al Nahayan, Minister of Higher Education & Scientific Research, and Supreme Chancellor of the University, CMHS has been at the forefront of medical education, addressing the critical shortage of national physicians. With a commitment to excellence and a dedication to serving the healthcare needs of the community, CMHS has evolved into a distinguished institution over the years.

## College of Medicine and Health Sciences > Why Choose CMHS? > Status and respect in society
Doctors are regarded as representatives of dignity, responsibility, and community service. Local residents acknowledge the efforts and sacrifices that medical professionals undertake to uphold standards. Consequently, the community values doctors' perspectives on community welfare, along with their admirable conduct and ethical standing. While this place added responsibilities on doctors, it also underscores the notion that medical professionals serve as leaders within the local community.

## College of Medicine and Health Sciences > Testimonials > Meera Al Ameri
I graduated from the PhD program in Nutritional Sciences, in the Department of Nutrition and Health, College of Medicine and Health Sciences at UAEU, in Spring 2023. My PhD journey was greatly enriched with immense support from the faculty in both the Department and the College, which enabled me to achieve my academic and research goals. I am grateful to UAEU for providing me with a scholarship and the necessary resources to conduct my research. My project focused on the impact of bariatric surgery on pregnancy and its effects on maternal and neonatal outcomes in the UAE, contributing valuable insights to the nutritional care of pregnant women post-surgery. With the guidance of my professors and the university's financial support, I was able to publish my findings in top-ranked journals.

## College of Medicine and Health Sciences > Testimonials > Amna Almansoori
Alumni, PhD in Nutritional Sciences

I am incredibly grateful for the UAEU dietetics program, which has significantly enhanced my critical thinking and practical skills. The comprehensive curriculum, coupled with a strong emphasis on evidence-based practice, has equipped me with the tools to effectively assess, diagnose, and intervene in patients' nutritional needs. The NCP (Nutrition Care Process) has been instrumental in developing a structured approach to patient care. Through real-world clinical experiences outside the university, I have gained invaluable hands-on practice, applying my knowledge in a practical setting. Additionally, the program's focus on research, exemplified by my participation in SURE Plus, has allowed me to contribute to the field. Under the guidance of our esteemed faculty, I was part of a research team that leveraged AI to create educational videos about sodium-rich foods and strategies for reducing salt intake. This experience has not only strengthened my research skills but also provided me with the opportunity to directly impact public health. I am confident in my ability to excel in the field of dietetics thanks to the exceptional education and support provided by the UAEU, CMHs, and the Department of Nutrition and Health. Thank you for shaping me into a competent healthcare professional.
