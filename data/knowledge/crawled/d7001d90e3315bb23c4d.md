## Vision and Mission > Vision
To be a leading global innovation Center in the UAE dedicated to supporting children with exceptionalities.

## Vision and Mission > Mission
To deliver precise assessments and effective interventions for children with exceptional needs, fostering their growth as independent, lifelong learners. Through strong partnerships with families, educators, specialists, and the community, we nurture every child’s potential and support the development of educators, professionals, and researchers to create future-ready global citizens and leaders.

## Vision and Mission > Values
Diversity: We believe in creating an equitable, supportive, and diverse environment where every child, regardless of their ability, gender, religion, or nationality, has access to a respectful and nurturing atmosphere that fosters quality educational services.

Collaboration: We foster strong partnerships between families, educators, researchers, and the community to create a holistic support system for children with exceptionalities.

Innovation: We embrace the latest research, technology, and best practices to develop forward-thinking educational strategies and interventions.

Sustainability: We foster a healthier work environment and promote continuous growth, nurturing curiosity, resilience, and adaptability in both children and professionals.

## Vision and Mission > Stratigic Goals
Provide effective assessment, intervention, education, and counseling services for children with exceptionalities, guided by international best practices and driven by innovation.

Establish and sustain strategic collaborations with leading specialized centers and universities, positioning the UAEU as a recognized hub for special and gifted education.

Implement various initiatives to raise community awareness and foster active collaboration among professionals, parents, and future educators, achieving measurable growth in Diversity and Inclusion practices.

Develop highly skilled future professionals to meet evolving labor market needs locally, regionally, and globally.

Develop research capacity and foster innovation in areas related to exceptional children to advance knowledge, inform practice, and contribute to the continuous improvement of educational services.

Offer guidance, counseling, and/or consultation services for the parents, school professionals, and community.
