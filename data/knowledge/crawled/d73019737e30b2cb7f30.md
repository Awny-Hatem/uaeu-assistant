## About the Program
The College of Science committed to excellence and building an inclusive culture that welcomes high-achieving students in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Geology. College of Science’s Honor Program will provide opportunities for talented students to stimulate learning and help prepare them for prestigious jobs. All students in the program will adhere to pursue excellence in academics, leadership and service and they uphold high standards of personal and professional integrity.
