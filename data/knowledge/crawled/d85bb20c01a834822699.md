## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > What to Expect?
As a participant in NRPP, you will be immersed in a challenging yet rewarding full-time training experience. Here’s what you can expect:

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > What to Expect? > Rigorous Training
High expectations for attendance, engagement, and assignment completion

Interactive classes and workshops designed to challenge your thinking and expand your skills

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > What to Expect? > Supportive Environment
Daily support from program coordinators and faculty

Opportunities to work in teams and collaborate on real research challenges

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > What to Expect? > Continuous Assessment
Assessments are built into each module and may include group projects, presentations, reports, and participation

A final score of 70% or above is required to pass the program and receive the Certificate of Completion

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > What to Expect? > Hands-On Research Experience
Three-week research placements at UAEU centers or with faculty-led teams.

Each student is assigned a mentor who will guide, evaluate, and provide real-time feedback

Project work is a major component of your final grade and provides practical exposure to research careers

## National Research Pioneers Program (NRPP) > What to Expect? > Post-Program Recognition
Participants receive an official certificate of completion

Ongoing networking and follow-up support for graduate study, research assistant roles, and job applications
