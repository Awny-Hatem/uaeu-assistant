## 60th Annual ICSB World Conference > Social Program
The Dubai Creek Boat Dinner is not included in the registration fee. If you or your companion would like to attend this social activity please select the required quantity during check-out.

## 60th Annual ICSB World Conference > Cancellation Policy
All cancellations, alterations or transfers must be notified in writing to ICSB 2015.

Cancellations received on or before May 31, 2015 shall be refunded in full, less 50 USD to cover administration costs; no registration refunds will be made after this date.

As an alternative to cancellation your registration may be transferred to another member of your organization, subject to a USD 25 processing fee.
