7. Governance of Graduate Degree Programs

Overview
Describes the governance of the University’s graduate academic programs and explains how
this operates in practice with respect to:
1.  Creation of new academic programs or their component parts;
2.  Modifications to established academic programs or their component parts; and
3.  Periodic internal and external review of academic programs.

Scope
Unless specified otherwise, the policies in the following sections apply to all graduate academic
programs.

Objective
Graduate academic programs are designed, implemented and managed by the Colleges of the
UAEU under the oversight of the CGS, and in the context of University governance structures,
policies and procedures.  This governance operates to ensure the high standards of the graduate
academic programs consistent with the mission of the UAEU.

Policy
1.  The   Dean of CGS        is charged with       ensuring the high standards of graduate academic
    programs, and designing and implementing effective Policies and Procedures to achieve
    this. The Dean of the CGS is also responsible for ensuring compliance with Policies and
    Procedures, and for reporting instances of non-compliance to the Provost.

2.  Colleges will act appropriately to review their graduate programs with their constituencies.
    These measures may include the establishment of Academic Advisory Boards for graduate
    programs. Terms of Reference detailing mission, membership, function and activities of
    such Advisory Boards are to be set by the host College, and filed with the College of
    Graduate Studies.

3.  Policies and Procedures are set out in this Manual and published on the UAEU website.
    Proposals for additions, deletions, or other amendments to the Manual may originate from
    any user of the Manual, and go through the process set out for that purpose.

4.  Depending on the nature of the proposal, creation or modification of graduate academic
    programs is subject to approval of the College Council, the Graduate Studies Council, the
    University Program and Curriculum Committee, the Academic Council, and the University
    Council, as defined in the Signatory Authority.
