## The Great Student Competition > Short Stories
To register: submit a word file of 1,000 – 1,200 words.

The short story must be written by the participant himself/herself.

The topic should meet the general themes humanity, society or national message or any other approved topic.

The student has the freedom to present his/her performance in any appropriate manner.

The duration is 3 - 5 minutes.
