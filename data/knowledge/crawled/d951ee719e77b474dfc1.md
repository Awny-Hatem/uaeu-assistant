## Associate Provost for Student Affairs Message
The United Arab Emirates University believes that its mission goes beyond awarding degrees and rather prepares students capable of achieving excellence academically, personally, and professionally. Therefore, the university provides an environment rich in educational opportunities and practical experiences that promote the holistic growth of students through extracurricular activities, volunteer work, community initiatives, leadership programs, and other experiences that enrich their university journey.

The Student Affairs Sector is dedicated to providing a student experience that enhances the quality of university life and establishes a safe, supportive, and stimulating environment for learning and creativity. It enables students to develop their abilities, refine their skills, make sound decisions that can realize their academic and personal success, and build well-rounded personalities prepared for future challenges.

The sector also provides services and programs that support student well-being and enhance their integration into university life. The programs aim to foster independence through initiatives and activities that develop leadership skills, critical thinking, problem-solving, effective communication, teamwork, innovation, and resilience, enabling students to meet the demands of the future with confidence.

The increasing interest in student programs and activities reflects the positive impact of these initiatives. Students actively participate in student clubs and university events, contribute to community service, and represent the university in various forums. We invite all students to seize these opportunities, actively participate in university life, build positive relationships, join student clubs, and serve as ambassadors for the university at every opportunity. These experiences promote classroom learning, build a balanced personality, enhance the sense of belonging, and create a positive impact on the lives of students and the university community.

We are confident that your years of study at the United Arab Emirates University will be rich with knowledge, achievement, inspiring experiences, and lifelong memories.

Dr. Suad Mohammed AlMarzooqi

Associate Provost for Students’ Affairs
