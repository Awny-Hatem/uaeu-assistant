                                                                      LEARNING OUTCOMES
                                                                      The    participants    will    develop    answers    to    questions
                                                                      related to  their  own  business,  including:  What  are  the
                                                                      contemporary  digital   technologies   and   capabilities   that
                                                                      provide   new   sources   of  competitive   advantage   in   my

                                     Oxford University Press in October 2020.

                                     Professor  Farah  Kodeih,  PhD  is  a  tenured  Associate  Professor  in  Management  at  IESEG

                                     Business in Finland. She has a long track record of teaching Strategy and General Management

                                     and marginalized individuals into mainstream societies.
