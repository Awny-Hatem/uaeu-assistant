## Job opportunities for Graduates
The Center for Career Placement and Alumni (CCPA) orchestrates strategic linkages between UAEU graduates and a diverse pool of employers through its dynamic Career Readiness Unit. This is accomplished through a suite of activities, programs, and resources such as:

Job Discovery Opportunities : The Center for Career Placement and Alumni aims to link UAEU graduates with job opportunities in the job market by connecting them with different public and private sector employers offering job opportunities

Career Events: CCPA organizes a variety of career-centric events for both students and employers, including on-campus interviews, keynote presentations from major employers, and dedicated Career/Employer days.

Tailored Job Opportunities: CCPA ensures that its graduates stay informed by delivering a curated selection of available opportunities from partner employers. These include full-time roles, graduate training programs, enriching internships, and valuable mentorship schemes that cater to various professional needs and ambitions.
