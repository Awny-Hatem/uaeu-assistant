My GPA is 2.0 or higher. Can I repeat a course in which I received a C–, D+, or D?

Students with a GPA of 2.0 or higher are not permitted to repeat courses that have already been passed.

My GPA is below 2.0. Can I repeat courses in which I received a C–, D+, or D?

If your GPA is below 2.0, you may repeat up to two courses with grades of C–, D+, or D. However, you cannot repeat the same course more than once, and you must repeat the same course, not an equivalent or a substitute.
