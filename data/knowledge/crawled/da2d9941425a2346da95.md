HUMAN CAPITAL
  RESEARCH CENTER’S
       RESEARCH AWARD




  The Human Capital Research Center (HCRC) is pleased to launch an initiative for the year
   2024/2025 to motivate UAEU researchers to publish research papers in areas related to
                  the mission of the center in reputable peer-reviewed journals

                                               SUBMIT    NOW



  OBJECTIVE
Conduct research that contributes to the development of human capital in the UAE and beyond.

  RESEARCH AREA
Focus on enhancing human capital and developing qualified national competencies to meet the labor
market's future demands at the local, regional, and global levels.

We encourage the UAEU faculty and researchers to direct their research agenda toward human
capital and education areas to meet the HCRC objectives. This initiative includes the following:

   UAEU researchers publishing relevant research papers in reputable peer-reviewed journals and including
   the affiliation of HCRC will be entitled to receive the following awards.

        Corresponding Author of a Paper                          UAEU Student, First Author of a Paper

         in the Top 1%        17,000 AED                            in the Top 1%         5,500 AED
         in the Top 5%        10,000 AED                             in the Top 5%        3,000 AED

         in the Top 10%       6,000 AED                              in the Top 10%       2,000 AED
         in the Top 15%-25%   3,500 AED                              in the Top 15%-25%   1,500 AED



   The HCRC covers the publication fees for the accepted articles.
   The journals should be listed in SCOPUS and/or Web-of-Science databases.
   The committee of the HCRC decides the appropriateness of the research papers to the HCRC objectives.
   The duration of this program might be extended based on the recommendation of the committee.


                    This motivation system is temporary and valid until      31st
                                                                             December 2025

   Guidelines




                               Only current faculty and researchers who continue their employment at UAEU at the time of the
APPLICATION                   recognition will be awarded.
ELIGIBILITY
                              UAEU students are entitled to receive the awards within one year of their graduation and only if
                              they maintain a residency status in the UAE.


                           Submission to HCRC
     1                        By submitting a research paper to a journal, researchers must first complete the form and upload
                              the paper to the SharePoint.

                           Evaluation by HCRC:
                                The  relevant  committee  will  review  the  submission  to  assess  its  relevance  and  alignment  with
     2                          the objectives of the HCRC and determine its placement in the top 1%, 5%, 10%, and 25% journals.

                                Once a paper is accepted by the committee, corresponding authors who list the center as their
                                affiliation     will     be     eligible     for     recognition     in     these     top     1%,     5%,     10%,     and     25%     categories     for
                                publications only in the same year as the paper’s publication.

      3                    Submission to Journal:
                              Once the paper is accepted by the HCRC, the researcher may submit it to the intended journal for
                              review and should list the center as one of their affiliations.


                               If  the  paper  is  accepted  by  the  journal  and  not  associated  with  a  funded  project,  the  HCRC  will
PUBLICATION                    cover the publication fees directly (no reimbursement).

FEES                           For the journals that offer limited access and open access, limited access should be selected. The
                               HCRC will not cover the open access option fees if limited access is offered by the journal.



                              At the end of each calendar year's fourth quarter, the HCRC will award the researchers who have
                              successfully published their papers.

                              Researchers awarded from the HCRC                will not be eligible to receive awards from the Office of the
                              Associate Provost for Research (OAPR) for the same paper.

                              Only     corresponding     authors     from     the     UAEU     who     list     the     center     as     their     affiliation     will     be
                              recognized in the 1%, 5%, 10%, and 25% categories.

                              In   cases   where   there   are   co-corresponding   authors   from   the   UAEU,   the   award   will   be   divided
                              equally  between  the  UAEU  faculty  members.  However,  if  only  one  corresponding  author  is  from
