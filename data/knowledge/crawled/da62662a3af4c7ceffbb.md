## The Secretariat of Deans the Committee of Deans of Colleges of Education in the GCC > Overview
The Secretariat of the Committee of Deans of Colleges of Education in Universities and Higher Education Institutions of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) Countries includes all colleges of education deans in universities and higher education institutions of the GCC countries.

Regular meetings are held with the deans’ attendance to discuss recent developments and monitor the implementation of the committee's strategic plans. The College of Education at the United Arab Emirates University proudly hosts and chairs the Secretariat of the Committee of Deans of Colleges of Education in Universities and Higher Education Institutions of the GCC, based on the resolution of the esteemed Presidents and Directors of the universities and higher education institutions of the GCC countries during their twenty-fifth meeting, which was held on January 26, 2023.
