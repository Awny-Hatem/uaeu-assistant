## Zero Government Bureaucracy and Process Reengineering
Title Workshop: Zero Government Bureaucracy and Process Reengineering

Date: Wednesday , 15 July 2026

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Ms. Mariam Al Menthri

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: Enable students to understand the application of modern technologies in food inspection processes and the integration of health regulation with digital transformation and intelligent systems.

Learning Outcomes:

Understand the applications of digital inspection and their role in enhancing the efficiency and effectiveness of food inspection processes.

Identify food traceability devices and systems and understand their role in ensuring food safety and supply chain transparency.

Explain the role of artificial intelligence in analyzing data, detecting violations, and monitoring food safety risks to support inspection activities .
