## College of Information Technology > Location > From Dubai to CIT
Upon entering Al-Ain on Mohammed Ibn Khalifa St. go straight for SIX roundabouts(R/A) named:

Al Rumeilah R/A

Dolphin R/A

AlQattara Club R/A

Al Khrais R/A

Al Aulaimy R/A

Zayed Central Library R/A

Make right turn on Al Qasr R/A

Go straight for FOUR roundabouts:

Zayed Al Khaeer R/A

Jabal R/A

Al Diwan R/A

F.D.Foundation R/A

Make a U-Turn on Twam R/A

Go straight for approximately 2.4 Kilometers; (you will see two orange buildings on your right); turn right into the street leading to the orange buildings and then make another immediate right.

Follow the road to the CIT building.

## College of Information Technology > Location > From Abu Dhabi to CIT:
From Khalifa Ibn Zayed St (Abu Dhabi – Al-Ain road), Go straight to Twam R/A

Go straight past Twam R/A for approximately 2.4 Kilometers; (you will see two orange buildings on your right); turn right into the street leading to the orange buildings and then make another immediate right.

Follow the road to the CIT building.
