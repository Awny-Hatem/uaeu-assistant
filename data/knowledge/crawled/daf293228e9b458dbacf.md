## Student Responsibilities
Student Code of Conduct

The Student Code of Conduct establishes standards of expected behavior for students and student organizations at the University, and puts in place fair and efficient procedures for protecting student rights and handling student misconduct.

The University seeks to create an environment that promotes academic achievement and integrity, that is protective of free inquiry, and that serves the educational mission of the University.

Here you can view the student Responsibilities and the required standards .
