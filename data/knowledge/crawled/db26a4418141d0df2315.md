## Department of Integrative Agriculture > Overview
The Department of Integrative Agriculture is attentive with developing professionally qualified undergraduates to be able to lead the development of UAE society in the fields of Horticulture, Marine Fisheries, Animal Science, and Agribusiness.

## Department of Integrative Agriculture > Mission
To offer high quality programs and provide the country with qualified needed graduates.

To conduct high quality research to better meet national needs and international standards.

To establish effective partnerships with public and private sectors.

To provide outreach and community services.

## Department of Integrative Agriculture > Workers
Al Foah Experimental Farm would not be able to function in research and education without the great support of its well experienced workers. These men are trained to support a wide range of activities related to agricultural production, research and education.

For their research or educational projects on the farm, UAEU Faculty members can request support of our farm workers through submitting the filled ‘Labor Request Form' .
