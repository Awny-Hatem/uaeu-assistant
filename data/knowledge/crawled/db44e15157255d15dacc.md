## Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children > Overview
The Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children (MBSCEC) is dedicated to supporting children with exceptionalities, including those with determination and gifts and talents. Our mission is to provide tailored educational, Intervention, and counseling services to help children with exceptionalities achieve their potential and support them in their journey to success.

Know More

## Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children > Vision and Mission > Mission
To deliver precise assessments and effective interventions for children with exceptional needs, fostering their growth as independent, lifelong learners. Through strong partnerships with families,

educators, specialists, and the community, we nurture every child’s potential and support the development of educators, professionals, and researchers to create future-ready global citizens and leaders.

## Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children > Vision and Mission > Vision
To be a leading global innovation Center in the UAE dedicated to supporting children with exceptionalities.

## Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children > Director's Message
The commitment to serving children with exceptionalities, including children with determination and children with gifts and talents, represents the highest ideals of human nature, demonstrating both solidarity and a profound sense of community responsibility. The Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children, under the esteemed guidance of Her Highness Shaikha Mouza Bint Saif Al Nahyan, Chair of the Board of Trustees, is dedicated to providing comprehensive services that adhere to the most rigorous scientific and professional criteria.

## Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children > Center Units > Intervention Services Unit
Dedicated to creating a supportive and inclusive environment for exceptional children, encompassing those with learning difficulties, giftedness, and disabilities.

## Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children > Center Units > Professional Development Unit
Dedicated to empowering educators, parents, and future professionals with the knowledge, skills, and tools necessary to effectively support children with exceptionalities.

## Mouza Bint Suhail Center for Exceptional Children > Center Units > Research and Development Unit
