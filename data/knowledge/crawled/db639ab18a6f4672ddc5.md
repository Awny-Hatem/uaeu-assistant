## Website Information > Customer Charter of Finance & Admin. Affairs Division > Our Commitment to you
Receiving you with diligence and treating you with respect and attention.

Providing you with high quality service and fair treatment

Meeting your needs with professionalism through well-trained and qualified staff

Responding to your requirements and needs without any delay, and exerting all efforts possible to meet them in a timely manner.

Keeping you fully and clearly informed about our services and their requirements, and the time required for their delivery.

Simplifying procedures and reducing service delivery time, in order to improve the quality and speed of our services.

Delivering services and providing information through various communication channels

Your feedback and suggestions about our services are welcome at a time through our several communication channels.
