## Tell Us Your Story
The University Outreach Department is here to support the delivery of positive coverage of UAEU's activities and achievements across all external media. For this to happen we need to hear from you.

If you have a story that you think could be of interest to the wider world, please send details to UOD@uaeu.ac.ae . We work alongside the University’s communications team, which produces a range of internal and external publications.
