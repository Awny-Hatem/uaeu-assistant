## Undergraduate Programs > Minor in Mathematics
The department of Mathematical Sciences offers a Minor in Mathematics designed to provide students with a solid foundation in mathematical concepts and methods. The program aims to equip students from other disciplines with a background in mathematics that is both broad and deep, supporting their academic and professional growth.

## Undergraduate Programs > Minor in Mathematics > Admission Requirements
Minimum GPA requirement: 2.0

Minimum credit hours completed in student's major: 30 CHs

Pre-requisite: Passing the courses MATH 110 and MATH 140

Targeted students: All students except those with BSc in: Mathematics, Mathematics of data Science Programs.

## Undergraduate Programs > Minor in Mathematics > Program Objectives
Establish themselves as professionals equipped with mathematical skills.

Continue their professional development by pursuing further degrees related to mathematics.

## Undergraduate Programs > Minor in Mathematics > Program Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

Identify, formulate and solve mathematical problems by applying knowledge of mathematics.

Exploit data, use mathematical arguments in a clear well-organized and logical way and employ technology to assist in solving problems and to draw conclusions.

Communicate mathematical ideas effectively through presentations and reports with a range of audiences.
