Overview
Defines policy and procedures governing requesting and assignment of domain names                                                                                   .

Scope

Objective
The objective of this policy is to ensure that all UAEU information and                                                  Internet services

Policy
1.                                                           The   Internet domain name assigned to UAEU                                                                is uaeu.ac.ae. UAEU                                recognizes uaeu.ac.ae

Procedures of Policy No . (5) - Domain Names

      Subdomain names serve practical and technical purposes by providing an “address” for
      the website. In addition, subdomain names serve as communication vehicles to convey the
      identity of the College or Department, indicating the nature or content of information the
      site offers to its visitors. The following procedures support and promote best practices
      concerning subdomain names that connect to, refer to, or represent UAEU.


1. First-Level Domain (uaeu.ac.ae)
      UAEU owns first-level domain name: uaeu.ac.ae


2. Lower-Level Domains
      a)                     Lower-level subdomains in the uaeu.ac.ae domain can be assigned to:
            (i)      Enterprise services or applications (e.g. elearning.uaeu.ac.ae,
                     webmail.uaeu.ac.ae, remedy.uaeu.ac.ae)
            (ii)                 Accepted standards by the Internet-at-large community (e.g. www.uaeu.ac.ae,
                     ftp.uaeu.ac.ae, wiki.uaeu.ac.ae)
            (iii)                 Colleges (e.g. cit.uaeu.ac.ae, cbe.uaeu.ac.ae, cos.uaeu.ac.ae)
            (iv)         Departments that provide services to the entire UAEU community and the
                     external community (e.g. library.uaeu.ac.ae, cec.uaeu.ac.ae, multaqa.uaeu.ac.ae)
            (v)                             Server names which are part of an enterprise service (e.g. server1.uaeu.ac.ae and
                     server2.uaeu.ac.ae, which are main web-hosting servers)
      b)               An exception of the above categories will be evaluated by UITS and proper approval
            obtained from the concerned management unit.
      c)                      Conditions for valid subdomain names:

            (i)                         Use of the requested subdomain does not conflict with any UAEU Policy or
                     practice.
            (ii)                 The requested subdomain is not likely to be ambiguous if used without unit

            (iii)              Use of the requested subdomain should not conflict with any UAEU interest, as

            (iv)                    Domain names requested by the Colleges should be approved by the Dean.


3. External Domain Names
      a)            External domain names are those that do not end with uaeu.ac.ae domain. These
            domain names may be allowed if the proposed name will be used by many users from
            many different organizations outside UAEU (e.g. www.it-innovations.ae, www.ain.ae)
      b)                   Conditions for valid external subdomain names:
            (i)                            The proposed name is not likely to be confused with the name of a UAEU
                     department or unit.
            (ii)                     The proposed name signifies an organization or venture, commercial or non-
                     commercial that is not explicitly part of UAEU.
            (iii)                    The placement of the proposed name outside the uaeu.ac.ae will be evaluated by
                     UITS and proper approval obtained from concerned management.

4. General Rules for Valid Domain Names
       a)      Domain names may not contain dates for one-time events (Example:
              researchweek2011.uaeu.ac.ae will not be granted).
       b)                   Domain names are limited to 20 characters before the “.uaeu.ac.ae”.
       c)            Each domain name must use either abbreviations, acronyms or full words. Partial
              words are not permitted. (Example: lib.uaeu.ac.ae will not be granted; use a full name
              such as library.uaeu.ac.ae.)
       d)           Domain names must consist of numbers and/or letters and not punctuation marks.
              (Example: creativity-club.uaeu.ac.ae will not be granted.)
       e)     Domain names may not contain trademarked brand names. (Example:
              sunguard.uaeu.ac.ae will not be granted.)
       f)        Domain names that are clearly repetitive will not be granted. (Example:
              alumniof2002.uaeu.ac.ae is one of many similar names; a better alternative would be
              alumni.uaeu.ac.ae/2002.)
       g)     Sites for students’ organizations will be assigned URLs beginning with
              studentorgs.uaeu.ac.ae and will not have individual domain names. (Example:
              studentorgs.uaeu.ac.ae/enviro.)
       h)           Conference websites will be assigned URLs beginning with conferences.uaeu.ac.ae
              and will not have individual domain names. (Example:
              conferences.uaeu.ac.ae/mathday.)


5. Requesting a Domain Name
       To apply for a new domain name, check the services section on UITS web site. All
       domain name requests are reviewed by UITS.
