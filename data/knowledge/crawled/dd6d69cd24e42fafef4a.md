## Publications Section
Publications Section also provides various printing services to all university sectors through designs compatible with the university identity guide and other publications for external parties, in addition to meeting the needs of the university sectors and external parties for promotional and advertising gifts. This is done through continuous follow-up and management of work systems to ensure efficiency and effectiveness in performance and within the appropriate cost.
