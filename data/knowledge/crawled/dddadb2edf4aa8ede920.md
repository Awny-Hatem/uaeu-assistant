## Research > Overview
The College of Education (CEDU) faculty members are continually engaged in scholarship activities that contribute to the knowledge base in teaching, learning, and leadership within their field of specialization. Faculty research interests encompass four major specializations including curriculum and instruction, special education, educational leadership and foundations of education, and physical education.

During the past few years, CEDU faculty members have published many papers in Scopus-indexed journals, presented in conferences, and received internal and external grants. CEDU faculty members participate actively in journal editorships and in reviewing articles for several regional and international educational journals and national awards.

The CEDU facilitates and evaluates its faculty members’ research activities through the CEDU’s Research and Graduate Studies Committee.

The CEDU publishes the International Journal for Research in Education (IJRE) in Arabic and English, which serves educational researchers, practitioners, policy-makers, and administrators in the United Arab Emirates, the Arab region and beyond.

## Research > Capabilities

## Research > Publications > International Journal for Research in Education
The International Journal for Research in Education started in 1984 under the name of Magallat Kulliyyat Al Tarbia at the United Arab Emirates University. The journal publishes original research in Arabic and English and serves educational and academic researchers and policy makers. It’s a refereed periodical that is concerned with publishing empirical research. The journal publishes educational research that pertains to contemporary educational issues, interdisciplinary research in education, conference and symposia reports, reviews of recent educational and psychological books, and summaries of university theses and dissertations in different fields of education. ## Research > Industry
The College of Education maintains close relationships with educational agencies within the UAE and region, including the UAE Ministry of Education and the Department of Education and Knowledge in Abu Dhabi (ADEK) . The faculty provides expert advice and consultancy services pertaining to educational research, effective teaching practices, school leadership, special education, e-learning, and educational policy to government agencies, foundations, and private educational providers. The College’s outreach mission is to enhance learning for all students.
