How can you apply for " Installment Tuition Fee" service?



The Installment tuition fee service provides students with a method to pay their tuition
fees during each semester. The student may divide the tuition fee in three installments
within three months (for all Master and PHD students), or four installments within four
months (for only DBA students and student’s employee at UAE University), in which
the student will pay the amount agreed upon in the service within the specified date.

The "Tuition Fees" service can be submitted during the period
20 January – 07 February 2019, the application must be submitted during each
semester.
