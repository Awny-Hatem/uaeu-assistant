## Future Foresight Section
The Future Foresight Section aims to foster a culture of foresight throughout the university by integrating future foresight tools into the strategic planning process, ensuring alignment with evolving changes and achieving future readiness.

The section focuses on developing various studies and reports to explore future trends, challenges, and opportunities that could influence the university's strategic direction. By leveraging advanced analytical tools, scenario planning, and expert insights, the Strategy Foresight team anticipates changes in the educational landscape, technological advancements, societal shifts, and economic dynamics.

This proactive approach enables the university to remain adaptive and resilient, ensuring long-term sustainability and success.

Main objectives of the section:

Continuously track and evaluate emerging trends in education, technology, society, and the economy.

Provide regular reports and updates on significant developments that could influence the university's strategy.

Develop multiple future scenarios based on current and projected trends.

Assess potential risks and opportunities associated with each scenario to guide strategic decision-making.

Identify and propose innovative initiatives that align with future trends and the university's strategic objectives.

Foster a culture of innovation and forward-thinking across the university community.

Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders, including faculty, students, industry experts, and policymakers, to gather diverse perspectives on future trends and challenges.

Facilitate workshops, seminars, and forums to engage the university community in strategic foresight activities.

Provide strategic recommendations to university leadership based on foresight analysis and scenario planning outcomes.
