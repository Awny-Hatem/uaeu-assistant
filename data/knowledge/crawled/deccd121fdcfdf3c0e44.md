## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > Collaboration and Consultancy ​
The ARIFSID welcomes collaborations with industry and stakeholders of the public and private food and agricultural sector. We are always open to discuss new ideas and expand our research and educational activities in support of food security in arid environments.

Please contact us if you are interested in elucidating opportunities for collaborative research with us. The ARIFSID research teams are also able to provide consultancy in areas related to ARIFSID’s research portfolio.
