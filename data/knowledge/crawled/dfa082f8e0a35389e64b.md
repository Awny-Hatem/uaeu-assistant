## Shadi Abuayyash > Personal profile > Personal profile
Accomplished researcher, educator, and media expert with over 20 years of experience in academia, journalism, and communication consultancy. Demonstrates excellence in educational leadership, innovative research, journalism, and strategic communication, with significant contributions to digital media studies. Research focuses on the impacts of AI on public relations, strategic communication, online news consumption, and cultural representations in the digital public sphere.
