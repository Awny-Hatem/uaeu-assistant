## General Education Program > Program Learning Outcomes
After completing the five clusters, students should be able to:

Demonstrate knowledge of humanities, social sciences, natural sciences and applied information technology.

Communicate more effectively in oral and written English.

Develop quantitative and research skills.

Think more critically and creatively.

Apply ethics in their behavior and decision making.

Work collaboratively, learn independently and practice lifelong learning.

Exercise leadership and have a sense of responsibility as UAE citizens.

Have pride in Arabic culture and Islamic values.

Recognize and appreciate the diversity of the human experience.
