              since starting a business is not easy and there are risks involved. Therefore, it's beneficial for students to have a
              realistic understanding of what it takes to succeed as an entrepreneur.

LIFE IN THE UNIVERSITY:
                        A Journey to Remember




























As I reflect on my time as a graduate student, I can't help but feel a sense of nostalgia for the
days   spent   at   the   university   and   in   university   residence.   Those   first   four   years   were   a   long
road,  but  after  passing  the  first  year  or  course,  time  seemed  to  fly  by.  By  the  time  I  reached
year   three,   I   remembered,   God   willing,   where   I   was   in   the   early   days,   and   those   memories
cannot be forgotten.

Being  away  from  family  due  to  living  in  hostel  was  a  challenge,  but  as  the  years  passed,  I
realized  how  much  I  missed  the  university,  students,  and  friends.  Everyone  goes  on  to  live
their   lives,   but   the   university   has   a   way   of   bringing   students   together   and   turning   them
into  friends.  The  educational  staff  also  played  a  significant  role  in  shaping  my  academic
journey.

The     university     provided     recreational     facilities     such     as     a     gym,     activities,     and     sports
activity  rooms  (table  tennis,  billiards,  TV,  etc.).  Meals  were  also  provided  to  all  students,
and the housing was comfortable. The institution has admission criteria that select elite
students from the UAE and international students. This ensures that they do not feel odd
or    separated    from    their    families    and    can    contact    them    at    any    time.    The    internet    is
available throughout the residence, exterior corridors, and universities.

The    country    takes    good    care    of    its    students,    and    the    university    is    no    exception.    The
United    Arab    Emirates    University    is    one    of    the    greatest    universities    in    terms    of    study,
housing,  and  student  life.  It  is  enough  to  leave  a  positive  memory  of  the  institution.  As  I
look     back     on     my     journey,     I     realize     that     life     in     the     university     was     truly     a     journey     to
remember.

TOOLS TO HELP YOUR ONLINE
                                                       BUSINESS

                         Where can you get ideas of popular products to sell?



In the fast-paced world of online business, success depends on being able to find and
sell goods that fit the changing tastes and needs of customers.

Every  business  owner  wants  to  know  where  they  can  get  ideas  for  popular  goods  to
sell.  In  this  article,  we  talk  about  ‘Retail  market  databases’  which  can  give  your  online
business an edge over the competition. Take for example the story of Mrs Aysha in this
article.

Mrs.  Aysha  has  finally  taken  the  steps  to  actualize  her  long  dream  and  establish  her
first  business:  an  e-commerce  store  (website).  Her  e-commerce  store  is  initially  (for
now)  a  niche  store  selling  only  gift  items.  Yet  Mrs  Aysha  aims  to  eventually  compete
with leading e-commerce websites in the UAE and beyond.

So,  the  fundamental  question  is  this:  What  should  Mrs.  Aysha  sell  at  her  e-commerce
store?

Mrs Aysha can decide on this based on her preferences, conversations with her friends
and  relatives,  and  even  conducting  a  market  research  survey.  However,  there  is  one
method that can give her very detailed insights quickly: Retail Market Databases.

Retail    market    databases    provide    detailed    market    data    for    a    specific    market.    For
example,     market     data     for     the     leading     economic     sectors     within     the     UAE     can     be
purchased  from  market  data  providers.  These  sectors  include  petrochemicals,  trade,
real estate, construction, healthcare, retail, and e-commerce.

It is also possible to use web services that automatically collect market data from the
Internet,        and        process        and        present        that        data        through        analytics        platforms        or
dashboards.

To give an example, let us illustrate how niche gift products can be identified using the

AED     at     a     Homie     partnered     merchant,     you     get     a
minimum  of  20%  of  the  total  amount  instantly  sent
to your Homie wallet, which you can use to pay your

At Homie, the goal is to be the first 'proper' cashback
platform.      Go      out      there,      pay,      and      get      cashback
instantly       in       your       Homie       wallet,       which       you       can
actually transfer to your bank account. 'Get paid, for
paying'.                                                                                Mansur    Dzaurov

My advice for the youth,  who want to initiate startups is the following:

1-  Do  not  underestimate  your  abilities. Your capability to learn will surprise you, trust me. Your way of
thinking and processing information after your first 6 months will have you thinking 'I can't believe thats
how I used to think'. Its not just you, its all of us. Learning while doing is completely different to theory.

2-    There    is    no    such    thing    as    a    'crazy'    idea.   If   you're   brainstorming   for   a   business   idea,   don't   put
limitations  on  your  creativity.  The  initial  idea  might  sound  ridiculous,  but  think  on  it  long  enough,  and
your brain will figure out a way to make it work at some capacity.

3- Network!!! I don't know how else to emphasize it, networking is your biggest tool. The people around
you matter, immerse yourself in your space and you will be shocked at how many people will be more
than happy to help you. Me included, feel free to connect with me over LinkedIn, ask any questions, I'm
always open to help.

4-  Sort  out  your  LinkedIn.  You  might  not  think  that  it  matters  much,  but  believe  me,  it  is  an  amazing
tool to help you improve your network.

5-  Keep  your  character.  There  is  no  fixed  'blueprint'  for  a  successful  founder/employee.  Don't  feel  like
you   have   to   be   a   certain   way   to   get   hired.   The   market   is   full   of   people   who   say   what   they   think
companies want to hear, but not enough people who bring their own views/ideas/opinions to the table.

6-  Do  your  best  to  not  get  too  frustrated.        Without a doubt, you are going to get frustrated, there is no
controlling that, but do not let it get the best of you. I was (okay, maybe still am) a type of person who
feels defeated when things don't go my way. Take a step back, control your impulse, and go again.
