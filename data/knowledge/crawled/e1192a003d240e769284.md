NOTE
         FROM THE DEAN


         Research is the heart of intellectual progress and social transformation. It is with great pride that I present the
         2025 Research Report of the College of Humanities and Social Sciences ( CHSS) at United Arab Emirates
         University—a testament to our collective commitment to knowledge, innovation, and ser vice to society through
         impactful research publications in Scopus-indexed Q1 and Q2 journals.

         The work highlighted in this report reﬂects the passion and dedication of our faculty and students to address the
         most pressing issues of our time. From advancing sustainable communities and strengthening family wellbeing,
         to preser ving Emirati heritage and enhancing societal resilience, CHSS research continues to shape meaningful
         dialogue and informed action at both national and global levels.

         In 2025, our college has further strengthened its research culture by fostering interdisciplinary collaboration,
         empowering student researchers, and prioritizing scholarship that creates real-world impact. These
         achievements      reaffirm      our      belief      that      the      humanities      and      social      sciences      are      essential      to      building      inclusive,
         resilient, and for ward-looking societies.

         I extend my sincere appreciation to our faculty members, researchers, and students whose curiosity, creativity,
         and commitment have made these accomplishments possible. Together, we are advancing knowledge with
         purpose, strengthening our national research capacity, and contributing to a future shaped by insight,
         resilience, and shared human values.


                                                                                  Professor Mohamed Binhuwaidin
                                                                                  Dean, College of Humanities and Social Sciences
                                                                                  United Arab Emirates University

The College of Humanities and Social Sciences at United Arab Emirates University continues to demonstrate
strong research leadership through a rich and diverse body of scholarship produced in 2025. The research
presented  in  this  report  reﬂects  the  depth,  quality,  and  global  relevance  of  CHSS  faculty  contributions  across
the humanities and social sciences, addressing critical societal, cultural, environmental, and policy challenges.
In 2025, CHSS research spanned a wide range of disciplines, including culture and society, education and
human development, environment and sustainability, governance and public policy, health and wellbeing,
translation, media, and heritage studies. Faculty publications appeared in leading international
Scopus-indexed Q1 and Q2 journals and addressed themes such as sustainable tourism, climate resilience,
disaster risk reduction, smart cities, social cohesion, public leadership, language and cognition, media ethics,
cultural                 diplomacy,                 health                 equity,                 and                 Emirati                 heritage                 and                 national                 identity.                 Collectively,                 this                 work
demonstrates a strong commitment to interdisciplinar y inquir y and evidence-based solutions.

A   deﬁning   feature   of   CHSS   research   in   2025   is   its   balance   between   global   engagement   and   local   relevance.
While         many         studies         contribute         to         international         scholarly         debates,         a         signiﬁcant         portion         of         the         research         is
grounded              in              the              UAE              and              the              wider              region,              offering              insights              that              inform              policy,              support              sustainable
development, preserve cultural heritage, and enhance community wellbeing. This dual focus strengthens the
College’s role as both a global academic contributor and a national knowledge partner.

The            work            showcased            in            this            report            affirms            the            essential            role            of            the            humanities            and            social            sciences            in
