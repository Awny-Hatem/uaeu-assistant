## Engagement and User Services Section
The University Libraries offer diverse electronic and printed resources for the faculty and graduate students relevant to the scientific research programs and academic curriculum needs.

The References Specialist offers assistance and guidance for library users to have access to various online resources through computers or the online catalog.

The References collection includes a selection of books and periodicals which are not available for loan.

Users inquiries are answered during the official library’s official working hours in person, or by telephone, or via email , or through ask-Librarian service .

## Ask a Librarian
Ask a Librarian is an online reference service that allows library users to submit questions about the library’s collections and services or how to find information.

## Remote Resources Access
Remote online access to electronic resources is available to all members of the University community, by entering the username and password.

## LibChat Program
It is an online service provided by the library to facilitate the user’s access to its services through direct communication with the library specialist via written chat.

It serves as immediate support for the user through which he/she can request assistance, ask questions, receive guidance regarding library resources, and research inquiries.

The LibChat service can be accessed through the library's website.

## LibGuides
It is a service through which the library provides coordinated guides to users to navigate between its services and resources effectively.

These guides are designed specifically for the research topics and services that the library provides to its users.

Each guide created by librarians includes instructions and tips to enhance the user experience and can be accessed via the library website.

## Room Reservation at the Main Library
This service allows faculty members, students and employees to reserve rooms designated for meetings and events, as it provides them with a quiet space for study and research.

There are also designated halls for events and the provision of educational lessons. This service can be booked through the UAEU Libraries website.

## Library Literacy
The Unit of Outreach & Instruction Services provides Library tours and tailored instruction sessions to library users in coordination with university colleges, faculty members, and vendors. This service aims to educate the user/student about the basics of scientific research, how to use library resources and services, extract information, evaluate it, and use it to meet academic and educational needs, while considering copyright and academic integrity.

Faculty can request sessions by applying Request Library Instruction Session e-form.

To view the available workshops and sessions, you can browse the LibCal events calendar .

## Document Delivery Service
The Document Delivery service assists faculty and graduate students with their scholarly research by obtaining library materials unavailable in our collections.

Requests may be made through the online Document Delivery e-form, and may include copies of journal articles, chapters in books, conference proceedings and other documents.
