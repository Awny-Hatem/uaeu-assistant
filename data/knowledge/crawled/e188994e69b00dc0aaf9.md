## Mohammad Al Rashaida > Personal profile > Teaching
Dr Al-Rashaida teaches across undergraduate and postgraduate programs in special and inclusive education. His teaching covers:

Foundations of special and inclusive education

Curriculum and instructional strategies in special education

Assistive technology for learners with special educational needs

Inclusive education and the education of students with disabilities

Early intervention and family-focused practices

Autism spectrum disorders and developmental disabilities

Learning disabilities and gifted and talented education

Administration, management, and organization of special education services

Vocational rehabilitation and employment for people with disabilities

Qualitative and quantitative research methods in education

He supervises postgraduate research in areas such as inclusive classroom practices, differentiated instruction, disability inclusion in higher education, and the experiences of people with disabilities and their caregivers.
