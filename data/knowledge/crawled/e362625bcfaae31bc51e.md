## Music
The Music category celebrates the art of crafting and arranging harmonious sounds and melodies to convey emotion, narrate stories, and create meaningful artistic experiences. It welcomes performances that demonstrate technical command, artistic expression, and thoughtful engagement with Middle Eastern musical traditions, whether through traditional interpretation, original composition, arrangement, or carefully considered fusion. Submissions are welcome in Arabic, English, or a combination of both languages. Performances may be Solo or ensemble based.

## Music > Submission Requirements
Entries must not exceed two (2) minutes in length.

Only video recordings will be accepted in order to preserve the authenticity of the performance.

Video files must be under 10MB in size to align with website submission capabilities.

The recording must provide clear audio and unobstructed video so that the performance can be evaluated fairly.

Minor reductions in audio quality caused by file compression will not negatively affect artistic evaluation , provided the performance remains reasonably audible and clear. Judging will focus primarily on musical integrity, expression, authenticity, and overall artistic quality rather than production value.

Excessive editing, sound enhancement, or visual effects that compromise the authenticity of the submitted performance may result in disqualification.

## Technical Proficiency
Technical proficiency : Judges will assess the performer’s level of technical control and musical precision.

Instrumental/Vocal Mastery : How effectively does the performer control their instrument and/or voice? Are they in tune, technically secure, and performing with a high level of skill?

Tone Quality and Sound Production : Does the performer produce a clear, controlled, expressive, and stylistically appropriate sound?

Rhythm and Timing : Does the performer maintain steady rhythm, pulse, and timing throughout the performance?

Level of Challenge and Repertoire Suitability: Has the performer selected material that is appropriate to their level and artistic identity, and have they executed it convincingly?

## Authenticity and Interpretation
Authenticity and Interpretation : Judges will consider how well the performance reflects the traditions and musical language of the genre.

Understanding of the Genre : Does the performance stay true to the Middle Eastern musical traditions and stylistic conventions?

Maqam and Rhythmic Understanding: Where relevant, does the performer demonstrate accurate use of maqamat, modal movement, and traditional rhythmic cycles?

Stylistic Nuance and Ornamentation: Does the performer use ornamentation, phrasing, and expressive details in a controlled, tasteful, and stylistically appropriate manner?

Authentic Interpretation: Does the performer show cultural and musical sensitivity in interpreting the selected work?

## Musical Expression
Musical Expression : Judges will evaluate the artistic and expressive quality of the performance.

Phrasing and Musical Shape : Does the performer shape musical lines with sensitivity, intention, and coherence?

Emotion and Expressiveness : Does the performer communicate the emotional character of the piece effectively and convincingly?

Improvisation (Taqsim), where applicable : If included, how effectively does the performer incorporate and execute improvisational elements?

Text and Diction (for vocal performances) : Are the lyrics delivered clearly, accurately, and with expressive understanding?

## Originality and Creativity
Originality and Creativity: Judges will consider the performer s creative voice while also respecting the foundations of the tradition.

Arrangement : If the performer has created a new arrangement of a traditional piece, how effective, coherent, and innovative is it?

Composition : If the performer presents an original composition, does it show originality while reflecting musical elements relevant to the tradition?

Fusion : If the performance blends Middle Eastern music with another genre, is the fusion seamless, respectful, and artistically convincing?

Artistic Cohesion : Does the performance present a clear artistic vision, with all elements working together effectively?

## Performance and Presentation
Performance and Presentation: Judges will assess how effectively the performer presents themselves and communicates with the audience through the recording.

Stage Presence : Does the performer appear confident, engaging, and artistically committed in the recording?

Repertoire Selection : Is the chosen piece suitable for the performer’s strengths, level, and artistic identity?

Visual Presentation : Is the performer dressed appropriately and in a manner that reflects the tradition, concept, or fusion being presented?

Ensemble Communication : If the performance includes collaboration with other artists or instruments, how well do they listen, synchronize, balance, and perform together?

## Artistic Impact
Artistic Impact : Judges will consider the overall impression and resonance of the performance.

Engagement : Does the performance capture and sustain the listener s attention?

Memorability : Does the performance leave a lasting artistic impression through its conviction, depth, and clarity of expression?

Cultural Resonance : Where appropriate, does the performance meaningfully engage with cultural heritage, identity, or contemporary artistic expression?
