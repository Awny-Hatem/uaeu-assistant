## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > Developing Market Opportunities for Farmed Marine Fish and Unconventional Crops > Objectives
Studying consumer preferences, perspectives, and acceptance towards novel food.

Analyzing productivities and estimated market opportunities for novel food.

Estimating the demand for each type of novel food.

## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > Developing Market Opportunities for Farmed Marine Fish and Unconventional Crops > A glimpse at our research
A glimpse at our research:

Identifying Native Novel Food and Feed

Wide-ranging scoping review to summarize the existing literature on unused indigenous agricultural genetic resources of the UAE. Through the marketization lens, it will examine the possibilities of novel food, animal and fish feed, and farming practices.

Consumer Perceptions Towards Novel Food

Exploratory data collection through focus groups to understand initial perspectives by digging deeper into the thinking, knowledge, and attitudes of the potential consumer (population) toward the novel food, feed, and farming technologies explored in other subthemes.

Market Trend and Analysis

Questionnaire development for wide-scale distribution using the findings from the scoping literature review and qualitative data.
