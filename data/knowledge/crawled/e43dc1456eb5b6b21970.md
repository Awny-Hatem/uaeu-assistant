1. The UAEU College of Graduate Studies

Overview
Establishes the policies and related procedures for the operation of the College of Graduate
Studies (CGS) of the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) that reports to the Provost.

Scope
Applies to the CGS and all graduate programs of the UAEU.

Objective
The   University    is committed to the highest academic and administrative standards in the
operation  of  its  graduate  programs.  This  Policy  and  its  related  procedures  provide  the
administrative structure required for the effective governance and management of all graduate
programs and for ensuring that the expected standards are met.

Policy
The CGS of the UAEU is committed to:

1.  Developing and disseminating new knowledge in graduate, postgraduate and professional
    fields of study;

2.  Assisting all students in their search for knowledge;

3.  Helping students become more cognizant of their cultural and physical environments and
    of themselves as unique individuals;

4.  Developing in students the wisdom and skills needed to assume productive roles and
    leadership positions in their emerging society; and

5.  Encouraging the recruitment of faculty eligible to supervise graduate students.

Procedures of Policy No. (1) - The UAEU College of Graduate Studies

1. The College of Graduate Studies Mission
    The College of Graduate Studies will:
    a)   Serve as an advocate         for graduate education and research within and outside the
         University;
    b)   Establish policies and procedures in compliance with the best practices of the “Council
         of Graduate Schools” and other internationally recognized organizations to guarantee
         that the graduate-level learning experience is of the highest quality;
    c)   Foster and facilitate interdisciplinary/intercollegiate graduate learning and research
         activities;
    d)   Initiate and maintain an intellectual and professional environment that values and
         supports creative, scholarly, and scientific inquiry;
    e)   Serve the nation’s need for a well-educated and professionally-trained work force;
    f)   Educate qualified men and women to become competitive and capable of enjoying
         rewarding careers in government, academia, and the private sector; and
    g)   Respond to the growing needs of the UAE and the world for the extension of knowledge
         in all disciplines, and the development and dissemination of best practices in all
         professions.


2. College of Graduate Studies Structure
    a)   The CGS is an administrative unit within the Office of the Provost.
    b)   The CGS is headed by a Dean who is the Chief Academic and Administrative Officer
         of the College.


3. Graduate Studies Council
    The Graduate Studies Council is the legislative body of the CGS. It is chaired by the Dean
    of the CGS. Its members include the following:
    a)   Vice Dean of Graduate Studies
    b)   The Assistant Dean for Research and Graduate Studies of each College;
    c)   The Director of Research and Sponsored Projects Office;
    d)   A representative from the Admission and Registration Deanship;
    e)   Up to three senior UAEU faculty members at or above the rank of Associate Professor,
         selected by the Provost based on the recommendation of the Dean of the CGS, and
         appointed for a two-year term.
    f)   A graduate student, selected by the Provost based on the recommendation of the Dean
         of the CGS, and appointed for a two-year term.


4. Criteria for Selection of Faculty to Participate in Graduate Programs
    a)   In order to participate in the teaching of graduate courses, faculty must have a terminal
         degree in the teaching discipline, and either a strong record of research and scholarly
         activity or significant professional experience as a practitioner in an applied discipline.

    b)   In order to participate as the principal supervisor of a student at the master’s or doctoral

Terminal Degree
    Faculty  teaching  courses  and  supervising  theses  or                dissertations  in  graduate  degree
    programs should have a terminal degree in the discipline. Exceptions to the requirement for
    a terminal degree, which must be justified and documented by the college, are:
    a)   Limited to faculty members in applied fields who have a minimum of either:
         (i)  A master’s degree in the teaching field or a doctorate in a related field, and who also
              have extensive senior level experience in the applied field (such as corporate
              management or accounting);
         (ii) Experience in the applied field coupled with internationally recognized professional
              association  certification  (such  as  medical  board  certificate  or  Certified  Public
              Accountant).
    b)   Fully justifiable in terms of education, training, and professional experience;
    c)   Restricted to no more than 10% of all faculty in the institution.
