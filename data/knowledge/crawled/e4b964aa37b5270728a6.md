## Employers Services
The Center for Career Placement and Alumni (CCPA) at UAEU prioritizes forging robust ties between students/graduates and potential employers interested in recruiting top-notch students/graduates or offering internships. We present many employer engagement opportunities, including events, informational sessions, workshops, and networking gatherings, tailored to boost your recruitment efforts and foster student/graduate interest in your organization.

## Our services:
Recruitment Presentations: Make use of our dynamic online and on-campus platforms to showcase your organization's internship and employment opportunities to candidates at pivotal stages of their career journeys. Our expert Careers Team collaborates with you to craft effective marketing campaigns and ensures exclusive access to your chosen event day.

On-campus Job Interviews: Leverage our resources to arrange on-campus interviews with our talented students/graduates.

Employer Networking Events: Elevate your brand presence by actively participating in our esteemed employer networking events. These high-profile events provide an exceptional opportunity to engage with well-prepared students and significantly enhance your visibility on campus.

Employer Sessions: We facilitate informal drop-in sessions, offering students a relaxed environment to connect with you and seek insights into your organization and recruitment practices. This interactive setting allows for meaningful interactions and addresses any inquiries they may have.

UAEU Career Fairs: Pathways to Success is a significant event that plays a pivotal role in bridging the gap between academic accomplishments and professional opportunities for UAEU students and graduates. This event presents a remarkable opportunity for organizations to engage with our talented students and explore potential recruitment prospects. The primary goal of the event is to facilitate meaningful interactions between employers and graduates, enabling them to gain valuable insights into the current job market, build professional networks, and enhance career prospects
