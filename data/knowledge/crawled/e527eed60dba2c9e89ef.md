Conditions for receiving financial aid (cash):


The student must be registered with the electronic Financial Aid System. Log on to E-
Services   and upload all the documents required.
The student should be on a regular course of study.
You must meet Zakat eligibility criteria.
The student must have their own bank account.

    Note: Registration on the Financial Aid System opens for 4 weeks at the beginning of
    each semester as per the university academic calendar.

## PDF page 3
Conditions for receiving an iPad or laptop:

The student must be registered with the electronic Financial Aid System. Log on to E-
Services   and upload all the documents required.
The student should be on a regular course of study.
The average monthly stipend will not exceed AED 2,000 per person for UAE national
students or AED 1,000 for non-nationals.
The student should have fewer than 60 credit hours remaining before graduation.
The student must be enrolled in courses that require the use of the device requested.

Note: Registration on the Financial Aid System opens for 4 weeks at the beginning of
each semester as per the university academic calendar.

## PDF page 4
Documents required for financial aid:

1.A copy of university ID card.

2.Photocopies of the passports and UAE identity cards of every family member and a valid residence permit.

3.Copy of the family’s National Identity Booklet.

4.Salary certificate approved by employer (not more than six months old), or proof of pension or monthly
alimony statement (if mother is divorced).

5.A copy of employment contract stamped by the employer (for private sector employees).

6.A recent certificate from the Ministry of Social Affairs with proof of receipt of aid.

7.If the student’s guardian is unemployed, an adoption certificate is required, in addition to an end of service
certificate.

8.Court certificate for unmarried children over the age of 18 (for males; confirmation of non-employment
status: for females; confirmation of non-employment status + non-marriage).

9.In the event of a parent’s death, a copy of the death certificate is required, plus proof of inheritance of heir’s
rights.

10.An authenticated contract of lease.

11.Those with bank loans must provide assets of debt including remaining time, a record of monthly instalments
and a bank statement for the last 6 months.

12.A bank statement for the last 6 months.

13.A completed annual Zakat Fund form is required to apply for cash. This form is on the Financial Aid webpage.

14.A To Whom it May Concern certificate from the bank stating the IBAN (International Bank Account Number)
that any approved financial aid will be transferred to.

## PDF page 5
Notes:

1.All documents must be uploaded in PDF or image format.

2.Please put family passports or National Identity Booklet in one PDF file.

3.Insert a copy of a water and/ or electricity bill, a copy of maid or driver’s passport and school fees if
necessary.

4.If an individual in the family over 18 does not work they will count as two family members in assessing the
family’s financial situation. Evidence of this must be provided.

5.The university has the right to request additional documents from the student and will exempt them from
some documents according to status, nationality and acceptance by UAEU.

6.The university has the right to stop aid according to budget availability from external sources.

7.Zakat Fund documents must be updated at the beginning of each academic semester. The cash will cease
until documents have been updated, reviewed and transferred to the Zakat Fund system.

8.If any documents are missing your request will be rejected.

9.Choose a bank account that does not charge fees.

10.Information regarding eligibility for financial aid will only ever be disclosed in the presence of the student.

11.An iPad or computer granted to a student becomes their responsibility. UAEU will not repair or replace it.

12.If you experience any problems with the system please contact helpdesk@uaeu.ac.ac.
