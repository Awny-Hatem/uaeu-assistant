Job Description Form


                                             General Information
 Job Title:                        Student Administrative Role Assistant
 Division/Department:              Associate Provost for Students’ Affairs (APSA) Division
                                   Students' Life Deanship
 Section:                          Campus Life Section

 Tasks and Responsibilities
 •    Assisting in the preparation and organization of activities and events in the sports
      building.
 •    Assisting in the organization, supervision, and follow-up of students on trips.
 •    Assisting in organizing the entry and exit of students during events and activities
      related to the sports building.
 •    Entering match results in some sports competitions.
 •    Assisting in guiding students by responding to their inquiries.
 •    Assisting in translating announcements and informational posters in the building.
 •    Communicating with students by phone to confirm their registration when attending
      sports activities.
 •    Following up on student registration for personal lockers for users of sports activity
      facilities.
 •    Assisting in printing monthly statistics reports of sports building users.
 •    Registering students for external competitions and tournaments.
 •    Assisting in organizing some sports events.
 •    Registering users upon entering the sports complex.
 •    Guiding users, including students and university staff, to appropriate facilities within
      the sports complex.
 •    Monitoring the use of sports facilities such as (fields and halls).
 •    Other duties.
