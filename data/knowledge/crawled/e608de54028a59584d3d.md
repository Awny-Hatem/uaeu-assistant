## 30 minutes for Us > The Positive Photography
The Positive Photography session was delivered by Ms. Mandy Seligman wife of the founder of positive psychology, Martin Sigman who s main interest is to apply Positive psychology tools in home and family. In this talk she focused on wellbeing especially in the field of Positive photography and how this concept has changed during the Covid-19 pandemic.

This was a very interactive session in which she explained about the relationship between photography and wellbeing by linking it to many positive emotions and feelings like gratitude.
