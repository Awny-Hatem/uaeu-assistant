                                                                          Students with Special Needs


I.                                                                                               Policy

                     Students with special needs have the potential to be contributing members to
                     the United Arab Emirates society.  Their needs, however, may require that
                     they receive additional support, services or considerations if they are to realize
                     their potential as students at the United Arab Emirates University.  The
                     University will endeavor to support students with special needs where
                     resources are available.


II.                                                                             Application

                     This policy shall apply to all students at the United Arab Emirates University.
