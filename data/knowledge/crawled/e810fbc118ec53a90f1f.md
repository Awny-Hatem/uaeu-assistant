## Application Process > Application Process
1. If you have fulfilled the admission requirements for the MBA Program, you will have to fill out the application form.

Al Ain Application Form

Dubai Application Form

Abu Dhabi Application Form

2. Once you have filled in Al Ain Application and uploaded documents for Abu Dhabi Application, you will have to send all the documents listed below by email. Then you will receive a reply giving you an appointment for an interview with the Assistant Director of the MBA Program

These are the required documents that you should bring when you come for the interview:

Statement of Purpose explaining why you wish to take the UAE University MBA

Your current CV / résumé

Two letters of reference (including one from your employer)

Photocopy of your passport

Photocopy of your Family Book (for UAE nationals)

Photocopy of your National ID

Two passport size photos (with your name written on the reverse)

Original copy of your undergraduate transcript / degree with marks included

If you graduated from UAE University, you can submit a photocopy of your degree

If your degree is from a country other than the UAE, you must produce an equivalency document from the Ministry of Higher Education, certifying that the degree is recognized in the UAE, and that the GPA or grade is acceptable.

Original IELTS or TOEFL certificate

Application processing fee (Dhs 350)
