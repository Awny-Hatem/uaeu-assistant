## Department of Veterinary Medicine > Overview
Welcome to the Department of Veterinary Medicine at United Arab Emirates University.

As the home of the UAE’s first professional veterinary medicine program, our Department is proud to play a leading role in educating and preparing a new generation of highly skilled veterinarians. This journey reached an important milestone with the graduation of our first cohort of Emirati veterinarians in June 2019, followed by the second cohort in June 2020.

Our five-year Bachelor of Veterinary Medicine program combines a strong foundation in veterinary basic sciences with comprehensive, hands-on clinical training. Students develop their knowledge and professional skills through training organized by animal species and specialized veterinary disciplines, preparing them to serve the evolving needs of the veterinary profession.

Since March 2021, the Department has operated from its modern facilities in Falaj Hazza. The site includes eight state-of-the-art laboratories, teaching and research facilities, and dedicated offices for faculty and staff. These resources provide an engaging environment in which students can learn, practise, collaborate, and develop their clinical competence.

Our faculty members are committed educators, experienced veterinarians, clinicians, and innovative researchers. Their expertise supports every stage of the veterinary curriculum and contributes to research addressing national and regional priorities. The Department places particular emphasis on One Health, food security, animal health, and the conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity.

The Bachelor of Veterinary Medicine program is fully accredited by the UAE Commission for Academic Accreditation. The Department has also been a Provisional Member of the American Association of Veterinary Medical Colleges since 2018 and continues to strengthen its engagement with leading international veterinary education organizations. In addition, the program is a candidate member of the International Veterinary Students’ Association and has pursued international accreditation through the European Association of Establishments for Veterinary Education.

Whether you are a prospective student, current student, academic colleague, veterinary professional, researcher, or community partner, we invite you to learn more about our people, programs, facilities, and achievements.

We look forward to working together to advance veterinary education, animal welfare, public health, food security, and biodiversity in the UAE and beyond.

Professor Sameeh M. Abutarbush

Chair, Department of Veterinary Medicine

## Department of Veterinary Medicine > Mission
The mission of the Department of Veterinary Medicine is to promote, improve, and meet the veterinary, animal and public health needs of the United Arab Emirates.

## Department of Veterinary Medicine > Facilities
The Department of Veterinary Medicine at Falaj Hazza has sufficiently equipped laboratory facilities to impart technical and practical training to the undergraduate students.

Building # 1 at Falaj Hazza campus is developed into an ultra-modern veterinary laboratories complex (phase1) and veterinary teaching hospital (phase 2).

The following are the established laboratories at the Falaj Hazza campus.

Veterinary Anatomy Laboratory

Veterinary Histopathology Laboratory

Veterinary Bacteriology and Mycology Laboratory

Veterinary Immunology and Virology Laboratory

Veterinary Parasitology Laboratory

Veterinary Pathology/Clinical Pathology Laboratory

For further queries, please contact Dr. Adnan Al Darwich, Laboratory specialist, adnan.d@uaeu.ac.ae or Dr. Layaly Hamdan, laboratory specialist, layaly.h@uaeu.ac.ae
