## External Projects > Unification and Development of Standardized Technical Inspections of Vehicles
This study aimed at reviewing the state of practice in inspecting the vehicle related safety aspects in the UAE. The study investigated various aspects including the homogeneity among the various inspection centres in the UAE. The study also reviewed the International and the GCC Standards that deals with vehicle inspection, e.g. ISO Standards.
