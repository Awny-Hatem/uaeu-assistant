## Undergraduate Programs > General Education Requirements
The General Education Program (GEP) at UAEU is an undergraduate curricular component shared by all students. It provides a common learning environment essential in unifying the students' learning experiences through a coherent general education model that broadens their knowledge and stimulates intellectual growth. The GEP is structured into diverse and comprehensive themes, each pertaining to an overarching general education learning outcome.

## Undergraduate Programs > General Education Requirements > Program Objectives
Equip students with the fundamental knowledge, values, and skills necessary to succeed in their academic pursuits and professional lives.

Develop well-rounded critical thinkers prepared to contribute to the UAE’s economy as responsible and informed global citizens.

Enhance students' abilities to become lifelong learners who adapt to change and pursue ongoing personal and professional development.

## Undergraduate Programs > General Education Requirements > Program Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

Express their pride in the UAE Federation, the achievements of wise leadership, and its future visions.

Communicate effectively in both oral and written Academic forms of English or Arabic language.

Explain innovative solutions using Artificial Intelligence to address contemporary challenges.

Identify entrepreneurial opportunities to drive career and community development.

Demonstrate knowledge of sustainability issues and responsible use of resources to address sustainability challenges globally and locally.

Foster Foundations for Lifelong Learning and personal development through various knowledge areas
