## Call to UAEU Alumni
Are you a graduate of UAEU with more than three years' work experience in your field?

If you are currently working or retired, we invite you to become a volunteer at a University Alumni Professionals Program (KHIBRA).

The KHIBRA Program is a unique training program in the region; it brings together alumni professionals experienced in their various specialties with the university students and the faculty in training and workshops.

We aim through KHIBRA to building bridges of communication with the alumnus and appreciating their experiences.

As a volunteer in KHIBRA, your goal will be to design and conduct training, workshops, and lectures to the current UAEU students on various topics of your specialty, including technical and general life and workplace skills.

Confirmed and recognized as a UAEU Alumni Professional, you will also be invited to serve on industry liaison committees for individual colleges providing advice on the courses and programs relevant to your industry. Your knowledge of the field will guide the colleges in determining content and methods appropriate to graduates' employability.

KHIBRA members serve the University, industry, and community by acting as a catalyst for knowledge sharing, raising awareness, and building practice communities.

As an Alumni Professional, you will be invited to mentor current UAEU students to support their career path, find internship and work placement opportunities, and build a network of professional colleagues.

As a volunteer in KHIBRA, we will support and assist you in designing the training program in partnership with the university colleges and centers.

We will publish and promote your profile and the program in a catalog for faculty to choose from. We also support you with the necessarily needed logistics.
