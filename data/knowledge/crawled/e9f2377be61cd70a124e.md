## Project Management for Managers > OVERVIEW
Companies are struggling to implement strategy, despite elaborate strategic plans. They lack an implementation paradigm and mindset. Project management provides such a mechanism to proceed from strategic management and planning to executing those strategies through projects. This course is aimed at empowering business managers to understand the value of project management and deliver projects successfully, and to cultivate the understanding of the influence project management has on business strategy and long term sustained success.

## Project Management for Managers > COURSE DESCRIPTION AND MAIN OBJECTIVE
To have successful project execution, it is important to understand the functional aspects of project management, i.e., methodologies, tools and techniques. Project professionals and organizational managers must understand the value, role and function of project management in delivering successful projects and its role in the successful roll-out of business strategy. The aim of this course is to empower managers to use the basic project management tools and techniques to deliver projects successfully. In addition, to build on this knowledge by supplying further best practices to excel within the project environment and to use project management to roll out business strategy successfully.

## Project Management for Managers > LEARNING OUTCOMES
On the completion of this course, delegates will be able to apply the generic project management functions, such as scoping, scheduling, costing as well as controlling, monitoring and closing projects; Apply generic project management processes; Use specific project management supporting functions; Understand the strategic role of project management within the organization; and Manage the key success factors of project management within the business.

## Project Management for Managers > CONTENT
This course will address 6 topics.

Topic 1: Introduction to Projects:

The Project and its role in the Organization, Defining Project Management; Reasons for project failure; The Characteristics of Projects; The Strategic Role of Projects within Organizations

Topic 2: The Project Manager:

The Characteristics of a good Project Manager; The types of Project Managers; The Project Manager and the Project Leader; Leadership styles and the Project Manager

Topic 3: Project Management and the Value of Project Management to the Organization:

Foundations of Project Management; Planning; The interdependence of Performance, Time and Cost; The processes of Project Management; Different methodologies of Project Management; Impact of having a developed project management function; Improving the organization by improving the project management function

Topic 4: Front-end Loading and Planning for a Successful Project: The project and decisions needed; Projects, Programs and Portfolios; Front End Loading and its value for successful projects

Topic 5: Planning for a Successful Project:

Project Scoping; Project Scheduling; Project Costing; Project Integration Management

Topic 6: The Successful Execution, Control and Monitoring of Projects:

Project execution; Monitoring and Controlling the project to success; Closing the project
