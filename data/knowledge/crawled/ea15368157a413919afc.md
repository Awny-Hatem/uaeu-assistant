## Students Participation
Students can participate in the textbook distribution at the beginning of both Fall & Spring semesters through volunteering or part-time work.

Voluntary participation: Student can volunteer in textbook distribution by filling out the form or personally attending the textbook distribution during the announced period. Student volunteers can obtain volunteer certificate after accomplishing a minimum number of hours specified by the administration.

Part-time participation: part-time work students will be paid an amount specified by the library administration after filling out the form. Students will be allowed to work for a certain number of hours to open the participation opportunity for a larger number of students. Students selected to participate in textbook distribution will be informed by an email.

*Note: Students can only participate during the announced textbook distribution period. It is not allowed to insert parts of hours in application form (only full hours will be accepted).
