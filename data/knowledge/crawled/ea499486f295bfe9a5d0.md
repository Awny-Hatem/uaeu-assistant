ZERO GOVERNMENT BUREAUCRACY

Upon directives from His Highness Sheikh Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Vice President and Prime Minister of the UAE and Ruler of Dubai, "Zero Government Bureaucracy Program" has been launched. This program aims at enhancing and simplifying governmental procedures by eliminating, reducing, simplifying, and integrating unnecessary governmental procedures, regulations, and requirements to improve governmental work efficiency. Consequently, the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) intends to actively participate in executing this program by engaging all members of its community in identifying all forms of bureaucracy associated with its administrative and academic operations.

The involvement of the UAEU community in streamlining government bureaucracy is a key element in the success of the program. This is achieved through proposing innovative and practical ideas, regularly evaluating their effectiveness, and working on enhancing the quality of the implemented modifications.
