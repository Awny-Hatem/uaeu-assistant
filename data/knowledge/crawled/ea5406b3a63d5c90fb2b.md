# Health Insurance Cards

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## Health Insurance Cards
All students are required to have a health insurance. The Student Health Services is responsible for the issuing and renewing of health insurance cards for both local & international students who hold UAEU residence visa .

UAE local students are issued with THIQA Insurance cards.

International students sponsored by the university will be provided with a Daman health insurance card.

## How to Apply
Emirati Students : You can obtain Thiqa (2) insurance directly through the official Thiqa website.

International Students : Your health insurance will be issued automatically once your E- Visa request is approved. No further action is required on your part.
