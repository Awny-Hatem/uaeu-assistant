                                                                                                   Signing Authority


I.                                                                                               Policy

                       Signing authority represents the authority to commit and accept costs within a
                       department/unit.  All documents with a financial impact on the United Arab
                       Emirates University are required to be signed as evidence of approval.

                       Signing authority for various University transactions and documents are
                       reflected in the Signature Authority procedure.


II.                                                                             Application

                       This policy shall apply to all employees authorized to approve transactions at
                       the United Arab Emirates University.
