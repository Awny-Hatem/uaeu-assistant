                                          United Arab Emirates University Letterhead


I.                                                                        Policy

                    The United Arab Emirates University has established a standard format for all
                    letterheads and envelopes to present a unified image for the University and to
                    save both time and money in the preparation of these materials.

                    The design of the University letterheads and envelopes will be consistent with
                    the United Arab Emirates University Graphic Standards Manual, available
                    from the Publications Department.

                    University letterheads and envelopes shall be used only for official University
                    correspondence.


II.                                                                             Application

                    This policy shall apply to the design and use of United Arab Emirates
                    University letterheads and envelopes by faculty, staff and students.
