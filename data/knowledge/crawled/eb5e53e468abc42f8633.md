## E Journals
The National Medical Library hosts a large collection of medical journals via electronic subscription. The journals are accessible on and off campus and can be located with the help of the library’s search tool: ‘ Summon ’, or via the browsing tool: ‘e- journals’.

The journals are also accessible through the library’s journal app” ‘BrowZine’. Download the app from the app store and read, get notifications and build your own journal bookshelf directly on your computer or mobile device. Ask at the library or visit www.browzine.com to get started and for more information.
