Enrollment Deanship
                                               Student Accounts Office
 Guide for Paying Fees for the following Master Program for Fall 2018: Business Administration /
  Professional Accounting / Engineering Management / Governance and Public Policy Programs.



 The Student Accounts Office (SAO), is responsible for the billing of all student fees and charges, and is
located on the Ground floor of Crescent Building, Wing D4, Male Registration Area, Al Ain. Our working
hours for student enquiries are 8am – 3pm, Sunday through Thursday..

SAO Contact information:
              o    Phone:  03 7134265
              o    Email address:  Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae

 Payment method:
You can pay for the tuition via the e-payment ( online Payment), please log on to this page to pay:
https://ssb.uaeu.ac.ae/prod/bwykstpy.p_display_opt




 Deadline for Settlement of Tuition & Fees for Fall 2018:
                  1st Payment Deadline (50% of total tuition fees ) on, 26August  - 06 September 2018
                  2nd Payment Deadline (Remaining 50% of total tuition fees) on, 09 September – 19
                   October 2018

** This Deadline dates is only for students who not want to apply for the Installment Plan.
*** Non-Payment of total tuition fees on or before 20 September 2018 will result in hold on
registration and records, and you will not receive course grades until you pay your fees, unfortunately
it may also mean forfeiture of grades and credits earned during the semester.
**** Student have a chance until 30 October 2018 to pay the total amount or you will not be able to
register for courses in the next semester until payment is made.





 Important Note

        To arrange a tuition payment installment plan, you have to apply on UAEU website through 02
         – 20 September 2018, and you have to apply the installment plan for each semester. Apply on
         this page:  https://ssb.uaeu.ac.ae/prod/twbkwbis.P_GenMenu?name=bmenu.P_StuMainMnu  .

        (SPONSORED STUDENTS) Students whose tuition charges are paid or guaranteed by a
         sponsor must submit a valid official letter of financial agreement from the sponsor no later than
         20 September 2018, or risk having their registration for Fall 2018 will cancelled.
