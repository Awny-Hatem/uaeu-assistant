Free Proline


Time required: 3day

      Fully expanded 'sun' leaves from field-grown plants were sampled.
      Purified proline was used to standardize the procedure for quantifying
       sample values.
      Reagents. Acid-ninhydrin was prepared by warming 1.25 g ninhydrin
       in 30 ml glacial acetic acid and 20 ml 6 M phosphoric acid, with
       agitation, until dissolved.
      Kept cool (stored at 4°C) the reagent remains stable 24 hours

        Procedure
           A. Approximately 0.5g of plant material was homogenized in 10
               ml of 3% aqueous sulfos salicylic acid and the homogenate
               filtered and the Extraction was repeated.
           B.  Two ml of the aliquot is mixed with 2 ml of acid ninhydrin
               followed by 2 ml of glacial acetic acid.0
           C.  The mixture incubated at 100          C in hot water bath for one hour.
           D. The incubated mixture was transferred to ice water bath to stop
               the reaction
           E.  4 ml of toluene is added
           F.  After shaking the contents thoroughly, the toluene color
               complex read at 520 nm.
