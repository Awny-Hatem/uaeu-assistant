## Internship System
To succeed in a global marketplace, it is no longer enough for students to travel and study overseas. Students must be afforded the opportunities to develop the skills, knowledge, and attitudes that will allow them to navigate in work environments that contend with globalization, outsourcing, and emerging technologies. At UAEU, we offer to help students learn and meet the new demands placed upon them when they enter the globalized work world.
