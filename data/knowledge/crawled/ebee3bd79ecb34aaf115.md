## PDF page 5
Rationale for Composite Aerostructures Business in Abu Dhabi
     Commercial Aircraft  Market: Middle East and Asia
         These are the large growth areas for new aircraft purchases
         This significant investment will secure a position in the rapidly growing demand for air
         travel, which is expected  to continue growing at  5% per annum over the next decade

     Abu Dhabi’s 2030 Plan
         The current diversification policy, away  from the petrochemical sector, has led to the
         creation of several new productive high technology, capital intensive sectors
         As part of this plan, a significant investment has been made in developing an integrated
         aerospace business in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi
         With this investment, Strata will build an advanced airframe composite manufacturing
         facility in Al Ain:
              Production of high technology aerostructure products, using advanced composite materials
              Adoption of automation and industry leading practice production techniques

     Strata’s Mission
         To be in the top 3 Tier One companies in the GLOBAL AEROSTRUCTURES industry
         by 2015, in time for the development of the next generation of commercial transport
         aircraft
