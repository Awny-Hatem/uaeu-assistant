## Career Readiness
The Career Readiness Unit provides a suite of essential professional services such as career planning, career counselling, and career readiness programs. These services are designed to guide UAEU students in formulating achievable goals and making informed decisions that align with their envisioned career paths. Furthermore, these services equip students with the required employability skills, preparing them to leave a significant impact in the professional world. The array of services offered by the unit includes:

Personalized Counselling: The Centre’s Career Counsellors offer one-on-one sessions, aiding students in crafting and executing personal career plans while fostering a greater sense of self-awareness integral to their career growth journey.

Group Counselling Sessions: These are well-structured group meetings designed to equip students with the necessary support in matters concerning career discovery, professional progression, and various other career-related topics.

Working life skills (Jahez Program): The Jahez – Working Life Skills program is a tailored career growth pathway for students throughout their academic years at UAEU. It aims to nurture highly employable graduates by fostering self-awareness, assisting in the selection of an appropriate major, cultivating key employability skills, and improving career preparedness. This program serves to narrow the divide between academic pursuits and professional employment.

CV and Mock Interview Clinic: The CV review and Mock Interview Clinic aims to connect UAEU students and graduates with potential employers and career opportunities. Employers and faculty members play a crucial role in guiding and empowering students by offering assistance to enhance the clarity, relevance, and customization of their CVs for specific job roles. Additionally, in collaboration with partners, the Center organizes mock interviews for graduates and Senior Students to improve their communication skills and boost their confidence, significantly enhancing their employability and readiness for the job market.
