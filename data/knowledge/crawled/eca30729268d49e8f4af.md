Title: Blockchain Technology
Requirements: None
Instructor: Dr. Gurdal Ertek
Target Group: Any participant junior (3rd year undergraduate) and above (including
Master’s and Doctorate levels)
Date: February 27- March 5, 2023
Period: 2 days, 4 hours/session, 8 hours total
Platform: Face to face Meeting, with interactive session; participants must bring laptops.
Inclusive of Scientific Materials: PowerPoint Slide Presentations, Practice-Oriented
Research Papers, Sample Data, and E-Certificate

Description:
This  training  provides  a  comprehensive  overview  of             blockchain  technology,  as  well  as
cryptocurrencies as tokens of value on blockchain networks. The workshop covers key topics
from the state of the blockchain industry to          Web 3.0 dApps, including lessons learned from
Web 3.0 projects. Attendees will be given hands-on learning through comprehensive demos.
The workshop furthermore explores roles and careers in blockchain, as well as understand the
risks associated with      blockchains. Moreover, participants will be            able to access numerous
learning sources to gain further insight into the world of blockchain.
