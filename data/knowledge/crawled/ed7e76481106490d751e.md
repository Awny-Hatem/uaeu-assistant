## 30 minutes for Us > A Different Perspective
This session was delivered by Dr. Fadwa Al Mughairbi who is an Assistant Professor at the Clinical Psychology department at the United Arab Emirates University. Her session was delivered in Arabic and she spoke about the problems we tend to face in our lives and how we can transform negative thinking into positive ones and have a more positive outlook towards life.
