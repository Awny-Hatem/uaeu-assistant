## Research > Production of edible mushrooms on organic garden waste
This project aims at developing a relatively simple sustainable and novel technology for recycling locally available organic waste materials through using them as a substrate for edible mushroom cultivation. Apart from producing high value fruiting bodies, the mushroom mycelia would possibly also decompose residues like date palm bunch or leaf waste or mowed turfgrass, and render them a suitable soil amendment or animal fodder.

M.Sc. Student Ms. Yusra and Dr. Shyam S. Kurup ( Skurup@uaeu.ac.ae )
