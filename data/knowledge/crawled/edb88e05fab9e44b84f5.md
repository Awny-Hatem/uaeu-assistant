## Research and Development Unit
Aims to advance innovation, evidence-based practice, and continuous improvement in the fields of special and gifted education. By integrating research, data-driven insights, and Services development.

Objectives:

Advance Research Excellence Undertake and publish high-quality research that contributes to the advancement of special, gifted, and inclusive education, reinforcing the Center’s leadership in evidence-based practice.

Promote Evidence-Based Practices Develop and implement research-based models for early screening, diagnosis, and intervention to effectively support exceptional and gifted children.

Apply Research for Continuous Improvement Utilize research findings to assess and enhance the Center’s programs and services, ensuring ongoing development and excellence. Convert research outcomes into practical tools, resources, and recommendations that strengthen educational practices and professional growth within the Center and the wider community.

Enhance National and International Presence Represent the Center in academic forums, conferences, and research collaborations, and share innovative findings and best practices that contribute to local, regional, and international knowledge exchange.

Foster Collaborative Research Networks Establish strategic partnerships with universities, ministries, and specialized institutions to advance joint research initiatives, promote knowledge sharing, and align with national and international priorities in special and gifted education.

Promote Research skills and Mentorship Offer research training and internship opportunities that equip future professionals with essential research skills and prepare them to contribute meaningfully to the advancement of education and services for exceptional and gifted children.
