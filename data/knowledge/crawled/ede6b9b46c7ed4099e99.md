## Welcome to UAEU
The International Students Office (ISO) welcomes all international students, and values the culture they bring to the United Arab Emirates University. Our ISO team coordinates the administrative needs of international students and supports their academic and personal development and success.

## Featured Programs > PROGRAM SYNOPSYS
The Developmental Instruction Unit (DIU) is a one-year Program which helps students transition from high school to university by building up their communication skills (Academic Arabic and English) and mathematics proficiency (Algebra, College Algebra and Trigonometry) to levels required by the UAEU colleges.
