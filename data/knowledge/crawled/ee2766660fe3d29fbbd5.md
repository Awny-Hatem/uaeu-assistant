## Doctorate Programs
Published academic-year scope: 2025-2026
Note: The Application Fees excluded the Value Added Tax “VAT”.

**We would like to inform you that the United Arab Emirates University does not provide housing for graduate students. We encourage you to look for alternative accommodation options that suit your needs during your study period. There may be housing options near the university or in nearby communities that meet your requirements.

The published tuition fees are valid up to the academic year 2025–2026, and the University reserves the right to modify the tuition fees at any time in accordance with its approved policies and procedures.
