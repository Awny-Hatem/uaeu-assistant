                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     The College of Education offers a Master’s of Education Program

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     educational professionals. This program has been designed based on
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     the best international standards, to meet the needs and aspirations
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     of teachers, principals and others in education who are eager to
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     improve and update their professional status.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     The program provides candidates with the latest knowledge and

                                                                                                                                                                                                  RESEARCH CAPAB I   LITI   ES









                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 The College of Education (CEDU) is dedicated to continually engaging its
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 faculty members, graduates and undergraduate students in research and
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 scholarly activities that contribute to the knowledge base in teaching
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 and learning within their field of specialization. The CEDU fulfills a
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 broad mission by enriching the faculty and students’ research activities
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 by supporting interdepartmental or interdisciplinary collaboration,
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 nurturing faculty research, grant writing and liaising with internal and
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 external funding agencies.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 Faculty research interests encompass four major specializations,

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 of education and physical education.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  but are not limited to, topics such as teaching and learning in K-12
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 subjects, curriculum development and integration, literacy, evaluation
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 and assessment, inclusion, teaching and assessing students with special
