## Happiness Research Talks > Does Inequality hurt? Determinants and consequences of inferiority feeling
This talk was delivered by Jan Delhey a professor of macrosociology at the institute of social sciences at the University of Magdeburg, Germany along with Leonie C.Steckhermeier a research associate at the same University.

This research talk involved talking about the findings of their study on how feelings of inferiority can have a negative impact on a person s life satisfaction and happiness and how important it is to combat these feelings.
