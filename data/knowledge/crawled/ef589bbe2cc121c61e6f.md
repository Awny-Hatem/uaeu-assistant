## Undergraduate Programs > Bachelor of Arts in Translation Studies
The program responds to a growing demand for professional translators well-equipped to meet the needs of the multinational society of the UAE. The program is designed to provide theoretical and practical training for students to become professional translators, and to introduce them to the requirements of specialized translation and interpreting. The curriculum familiarizes students with problems they may face in English-into-Arabic and Arabic-into-English translation and interpreting and how to solve them. The curriculum includes various specialized courses such as legal, scientific and medical, journalistic, administrative, game localization and audiovisual translation. Students can also develop their skills in community, consecutive and simultaneous interpreting, three types of interpreting much required in the job market today. Recent developments in technologies including AI-assisted translation as well as professional aspects related to the fields of translation and interpreting will also be covered. The program offers internship opportunities for students to train in different institutions around the UAE.

## Undergraduate Programs > Bachelor of Arts in Translation Studies > Program Objectives
Develop students’ translation-oriented written and oral proficiency in Arabic and English.

Familiarize students with the theoretical aspects of translation and interpreting.

Develop students’ skills in translating and interpreting texts of different types from English into Arabic and vice versa.

Produce translators with market-oriented skills and ethics.

## Undergraduate Programs > Bachelor of Arts in Translation Studies > Program Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

Produce acceptable translations of various text types in English and Arabic as per quality parameters and ethical professional standards

Analyze the contrastive differences between Arabic and a foreign language at linguistic and cultural levels for the purpose of translation.

Explain theoretical concepts of translation and interpreting.

Conduct interpreting using various interpreting skills and techniques in different contexts both independently and within a team as per ethical professional standards.

Conduct translation-related research projects using appropriate research methods and ethical procedures.

Use technology in translation and interpreting.

Demonstrate an acceptable level of listening, reading, speaking and writing skills and of the related cultural and social knowledge in a third language.
