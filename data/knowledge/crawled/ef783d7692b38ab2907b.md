Intro   duction

                   The United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) is committed to promoting
                   transparency, innovation, and data accessibility by providing high                          - quality open data
                   to the public. These guidelines aim to support users in effectively accessing,
                   understanding, and util             izing UAEU’s open data resources.

                   1. Purpose of the Guidelines

                   These guidelines are designed to:

                          •                                          Facilitate easy access to UAEU open data.

                          •                                          Support users in understanding and analyzing datasets.

                          •                                          Promote responsible and effective use of data.

                          •                                          Enhance transparency and knowledge sharing.

                   2. Accessing Open Data

                   Users can access UAEU open data through the official Open Data webpage, which
                   provides:

                          •                                          Downloadable datasets (CSV, Excel               and XML     )

                          •                                          Interactive dashboards for data visualization

                          •                                          Detailed dataset descriptions (Metadata)

                          •                                          Data Dictionary for understanding data elements

                   3. Understanding Open Data Components

                   Each dataset includes the following:

                        3.1 Metadata

                           Provides essential information about the dataset to help users understand the
                           context and reliability of the data.

                                 •                           Dataset name and description

                                 •                           Source and data owner

                                 •                            Last update date

                     To effectively use UAEU open data:

                             •                                          Apply filters and visualizations.

                             •                                          Refer to the data dictionary when needed.

7. Data Quality and Updates

                   UAEU ensures:

                          •                                          Data accuracy and reliability               .

                          •                                          Periodic updates     .

                          •                                          Continuous improvement based on feedback                      .

                   8. Terms of Use

                   When using the open data of the UAEU, users must:

                          •                                          Cite the United Arab Emirates University as the data source.
                          •                                          Use the data responsibly and in compliance with applicable laws and
                                  regulations.
                          •                                          Not distorting, misusing, or presenting the                               data in a misleading manner            .
                          •                                          Comply with the UAEU Open Data Policy                           .

                   9. Requesting Additional Data

                   If the required dataset is not available                     ,  u sers    may  submit requests via the Open
                   Data Request Form      . Requests will be reviewed and processed by the relevant UAEU
                   units

                   10. Feedback and Support

                   UAEU encourages users to:

                          •                                          Provide feedback on datasets

                          •                                          Suggest improvements

                          •                                          Report issues or inaccuracies

                   11. Disclaimer

                   Open data is provided for informational purposes only. UAEU does not guarantee
                   the    completeness     of the data        or   its  continuous        availability          and  assumes no
                   responsibility       for   any decisions or actions taken                    based on th   is  data.   In the event of
                   any discrepancy between the published data and officially approved documents, the
                   University’s official records shall prevail.
