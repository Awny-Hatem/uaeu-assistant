Terms and Conditions of Summer
                                                                         Study outside the UAE University

The   University     students    may    register   in   courses    for  degree    credit   at  other   institutions

accredited by the UAE Ministry of Education during the summer semester in accordance with
the following rules and regulations:


1.  Terms and Conditions

    -    The start date of the summer semester offered by other institutions should not overlap
         with   the  end   of  the  spring   semester     at UAEU      by   more    than   one   academic     week.
         Additionally, the end date of the summer semester offered by other institutions should
         not  overlap    with   the   start  date  of  the   fall  semester    at  UAEU      by   more    than   one
         academic week.

    -    Students may      register for    specialized    courses    at other   institutions   provided that     the
         equivalency requirements listed under “Administrative Procedures” in this document
         are fulfilled.

    -    Students    are  not  allowed     to register   for  internship    at  UAEU      while   taking   summer
         courses at other institutions.
    -    Students may not       register for more than six (6) credit          hours in the summer semester
         except   for  honors and expected-to-graduate students who may register in courses up
         to nine (9)    credit  hours. Students under probation            cannot   register   in more than      one
         course (with a maximum limit of four (4) credit hours).
    -    Students    are  allowed    to  register   for  summer     courses    at more    than   one   educational
         institution   provided     that   the   maximum       number      of  allowed     credit   hours    is  not
         exceeded.
    -    In  case   of  discrepancies,     the  number     of  credit   hours   counted    towards     the  student
         degree will be based on those of the equivalent course offered by the other institution.

    -    The   course    to  be  studied    at other   institutions    should    be  equivalent     to  the  course
         required    in the   student's   study   plan   in  terms   of  scientific   content,   academic     level,
         language of instruction and the number of contact hours.
    -    Only those courses taken at other institutions             during the summer semester            in which
         the final grade is C- or higher will be considered for transfer for degree credit.
    -    Grades of summer courses successfully completed at other institutions will be given
         (pass) grade and not counted in the student’s Grade Point Average. The course will be
         transferred to UAEU for degree credit.

Terms and Conditions of Summer
                                                                         Study outside the UAE University

    -    The number of credit hours earned at other institutions               during summer semesters and

         accepted for degree credit is limited to a maximum of (15%) of the total credit hours
         required in the student’s academic major/specialization, and a maximum of (10%) of
         the total credit hours required for the student’s degree program.
    -    The total credit hours earned by transferring from other institutions and studying
         external summer semesters should not exceed (25%) of the degree program
         requirements.

    -    Students should register only in the courses approved by the academic department to
         be equivalent to courses offered at UAEU.
    -    Course grades are to be submitted directly to the University’s official student record
         system    by  the  individual     student   no  later  than   the  first  week   of  the   following    fall
         semester for students graduating in summer, or the last day for withdraw/drop without
         failure for other students.
    -    Colleges     and/or     degree     programs      holding     international      accreditation,     or   the
         equivalent, may require that summer courses be transferred to UAEU for degree credit
         only   when    taken   at  institutions   holding    the  same    international    accreditation    as  the
         corresponding College or degree program at UAEU.
    -    Colleges are allowed to impose additional requirements as deemed appropriate.
2.  Administrative Producers

    -    Students    who    wish   to  take   summer      courses    at other   institutions    should    submit    a
         request    to  the  College     advising    unit   along    with   the   course   description     and   the
         academic schedule showing the start and end dates of the summer semester.

    -    The   academic     department     at  the  College    that  offers   the  course   should    evaluate   the
         alternative course for possible equivalency to its             counterpart at UAEU. This process
         should involve checking the          course content, academic level, language of              instruction,
         total credit hours and class contact hours.

    -    The College advising unit may identify alternative courses in which the student may
         register in case he/she is not able to register in the originally proposed course.
    -    The   advising    unit  should    provide    the   registration   department      with   the  request    for
         summer study, indicating the course code and number,                   course title, credit     hours   and
         the equivalent course at UAEU.
    -    The registration department should provide the               student with a letter addressed to the
         Registrar of the other institution indicating the courses approved for registration.

    -    Students must sign a consent form not to violate the terms and conditions of summer
         study at other institutions prior to receiving the approval letter for the summer study.
