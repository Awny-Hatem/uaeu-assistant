## TOEFL ITP
TOEFL ITP refers to Institutional Testing Paper. The test is composed of three main sections, which assess your proficiency level in three main skills, listening comprehension, structure and written expression, and reading comprehension. Each section measures a different skill, as follows:

TOEFL ITP Test Sections

Listening Comprehension measures your ability to understand spoken English as it is used in colleges and universities.

Structure and Written Expression measures your recognition of selected structural and grammatical points in standard written English.

Reading Comprehension measures your ability to read and understand academic material written in English.
