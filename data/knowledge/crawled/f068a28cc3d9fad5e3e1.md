## PDF page 1
Internal Transfer between colleges

                  UAEU     regulations    allow  students to transfer internally from
                  one  college  to  another  only  once  during  their  degree
                  programs.  Students  are  required  to  submit  their  transfer
                  requests  electronically  or  via  the  UAEU  app  within  the
                  deadlines outlined in the academic calendar. To be eligible for
                  transfer, students must meet          all conditions specific to each
                  college.





To navigate the UAEU portal and initiate a college transfer, follow these steps:

     1.   Go to the UAEU portal: https://my.uaeu.ac.ae.
     2.   Select “eServices” from the menu.
     3.   Click on “Student”.
     4.   Find and click on “Student College Transfer” to begin the transfer process.
