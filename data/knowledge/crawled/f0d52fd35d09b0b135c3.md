## Outcomes
The Honor Program will offer excellent opportunities to prepare the most promising students in the College of Science to drive positive progress in science and technology. Honor students in the program are aimed to demonstrate:

Appreciation of distinct modes of opportunities in the UAE University.

Ability to conduct quality research in their major.

Professional quality skills in written, oral, and graphical communication.

Exemplary engagement in leadership, service, and cultural activities.

Competitiveness in applications for prestigious jobs, graduate schools, professional schools, and major scholarships.

In addition, the program will increase the visibility of Honor students within the job market of distinguished careers.
