## UAEU Alumni Award > Overview of UAEU Alumni Award
The idea of the "United Arab Emirates University Alumni Award" was launched within the framework of the direction towards strengthening the university's relationship with graduates and ensuring the sustainability of this relationship through concern for active graduates in society and appreciation of their efforts and distinction. It is an award that aims to establish a culture of excellence in higher education, entrepreneurship and research, and to promote and encourage effective trends by spreading the concept of permanent development for graduates of the United Arab Emirates University. The UAEU Alumni Award consists of four categories:

The United Arab Emirates University Alumni Award for Research, Innovation and Entrepreneurship.

The United Arab Emirates University Alumni Award for Community Engagement.

The United Arab Emirates University Graduate Award for Women Empowerment.

The United Arab Emirates University Alumni Wall of Fame.

It is an annual award given to local and international graduates of the United Arab Emirates University according to specific conditions and consistent with the concepts of quality and excellence.
