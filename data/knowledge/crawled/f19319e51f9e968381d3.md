## Business Incubation > Main Functions
Aims at improving the performance of early-stage startup and young SME nurturing talent and entrepreneurial skills, and provision of entrepreneurial training programs.

Creates favourable conditions for the newly established startup and for the expansion of often fragile new young companies.

Provide office space, ideation space and co-working space for young companies and early-stage startup in the pre-implementation phase. The utilization of the space includes shared services and facilities and specific services such as: training, marketing, financial appraisals, etc.
