## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > Vision and Mission > Vision
The ARIFSID strives to become a global leader in the provision of innovative technologies and management concepts tailored to support the sustainability, safety, and resilience of food supply systems in the world’s arid ecological zones.

## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > Vision and Mission > Mission ​
The ARIFSID is an international platform for interdisciplinary research and education that generates and disseminates knowledge and technological innovation in support of food security in the UAE and other arid environments of the world. ​

## ASPIRE Research Institute for Food Security in the Drylands > Vision and Mission > Philosophy ​
Contribution to the achievement of UN’s Sustainable Development Goals .

Long-lasting national and international partnerships in research and education .

Diverse industry collaborations. ​

Support for farmers and entrepreneurs in the food and agricultural sector .

Involvement of the public with a focus on the youth. ​
