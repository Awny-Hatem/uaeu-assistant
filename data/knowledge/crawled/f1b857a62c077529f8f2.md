                                             General Information
 Job Title:                       Recreational Facilities Student Supervisor
 Division/Department:             Associate Provost for Students’ Affairs (APSA) Division
                                  Students' Life Deanship
 Section:                         Campus Life Section

 Tasks and Responsibilities
 •    He has a leadership spirit.
 •    He has a hobby and skill in one of the following fields: drawing, musical instrument
      skills, fashion design and implementation skills, photography, filmmaking, etc.
 •    The ability to bear responsibility.
 •    Suggesting activities for students.
 •    Offering courses to students by students who have talents.
 •    Supervising the activity room.
 •    Recording the names and numbers of students when they visit the facilities.
 •    Supervising the social club in the student housing.
 •    Other duties.
