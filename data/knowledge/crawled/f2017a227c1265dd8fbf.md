## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Poultry Unit
The poultry unit is equipped for broiler and egg production. Currently the farm keeps around 6500 layer hens for egg production. Only high quality feed is used for the hens, and no antibiotics or other medicines are used during the production cycle. Around 45,000 eggs are produced by the poultry unit every week.

The eggs are printed and packed on the farm. Our poultry unit allows students to witness the full production cycle, from hatching over rearing to production.
