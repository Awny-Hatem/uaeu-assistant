## Website Survey Satisfaction
Open Date : 11/01/2025 | Close Date: 12/31/2025

Objectives:

To measure the ease of use of the university's website by students, staff, and faculty members.

To understand how easily academic and administrative services are accessed through the website.

To identify challenges faced by users, such as slow browsing or complex design.

To gather suggestions for improving the website interface, enhancing its structure, and adding new features.

To evaluate customer satisfaction with the website in terms of functionality, appearance, and its ability to meet users' needs.

Expected Results from the Survey:

Clear Insights into User Experience:

A better understanding of user challenges and needs.

Identifying elements that contribute to an improved user experience or cause frustration.

Data to Improve Performance:

Collecting specific data on technical issues such as slow loading or navigation errors.

Prioritizing website development based on the most common issues faced by users.

Improving Customer Satisfaction:

Enhancing the user interface and provided services to increase satisfaction and loyalty to the university.

Useful Suggestions:

Leveraging user feedback to add new features, such as improving search functionality or providing dedicated portals for students and faculty members.
