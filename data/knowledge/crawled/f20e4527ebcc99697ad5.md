## Department of Microbiology and Immunology > Facilities
The Department of Medical Microbiology & Immunology at the CMHS is globally recognized for its academic excellence and expertise in various disciplines, namely bacteriology, immunology, and virology. The department is equipped with state-of-the-art technologies, including flow cytometry and cell sorter, real time PCR, cell and tissue culture, biochemical analysis of gene expression, molecular and cellular phenotyping, and whole animal study capabilities and bioluminescent imaging. These technologies are routinely used to conduct scientific research in a broad range of areas including cancer immunotherapy, virus-induced malignancies, antibiotic resistance among bacterial pathogens, regulation of autoimmune diseases and development of retroviral vectors for gene therapy.
