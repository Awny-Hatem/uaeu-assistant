## Development & Empowerment Programs
KHIBRA Program (Benefit from Alumni Expertise)

An opportunity for alumni to share their expertise with the university and community through workshops, lectures, career mentoring, or success stories.

Auditing Students Program

An opportunity to enhance your knowledge and skills by attending UAEU courses delivered by distinguished faculty members without academic requirements or credit hours with the possibility of receiving a Certificate of Attendance from the Continuing Education Center upon meeting participation criteria.

Volunteer Opportunities

Enable alumni to make a positive contribution to the university and community by participating in various initiatives that promote a spirit of giving and belonging. Apply Now
