## Information Services (Ask-a-Librarian) > What are the timings of the library?
Library hours depend on time of the year, such as Regular Semester, Summer Semester, Ramadan, etc. It is best to see the current hours at our web page at https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/library/libraryhours.shtml, https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/ar/nml/libraryhours.shtml

## Information Services (Ask-a-Librarian) > How do I renew books?
There are a number of ways you can renew your books: Online at the Login page By email request at: circ_lib@uaeu.ac.ae By telephone at 03-713-4684/03-713-4678 self-check Machine In person at any lending desk .

## Information Services (Ask-a-Librarian) > How do I become a member of the library?
If you are not a member in the UAEU, you can get a membership in the library by visiting the lending desk.
