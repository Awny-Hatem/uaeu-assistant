## Residential Life > Overview
Enjoy a complete university experience at UAEU student housing, where residential life combines comfort, community, activities, and opportunities to develop leadership and personal skills. Student housing provides a safe and supportive environment that helps students succeed academically, build friendships, and enjoy a rewarding experience away from home, with the support of a qualified team dedicated to their comfort and well-being. Facilities include comfortable rooms, mosques, gyms and game rooms, outdoor sports courts, kitchens, laundry rooms, internet access, and cleaning services. Air-conditioned buses also operate daily between student housing and the University’s colleges.
