## The Role of Family and Community in Fostering Psychological Resilience
Workshop Title: The Role of Family and Community in Fostering Psychological Resilience

Date: Wednesday, 29 July 2026

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Dr. Mohammed Al Ahbabi

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: This workshop focuses on the role of the family and the broader social environment in strengthening psychological resilience by creating a supportive atmosphere that helps individuals cope with crises and overcome stress. It also explores practical strategies for fostering healthy family relationships, enhancing positive communication, and promoting hope and optimism to support psychological and social well-being.

Workshop Topics:

The concept of psychological resilience and its importance in coping with crises.

How families can provide emotional and psychological support to their members.

The importance of maintaining a daily routine to promote psychological stability.

Effective family communication during challenging times.

Promoting hope, optimism, and community solidarity during crises.
