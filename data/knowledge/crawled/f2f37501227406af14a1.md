Statistical Consulting Unit
                                                                Workshops/Training courses
                                                                                  Fall 2023
                                                                              Workshop #1
Title: Data Analytics using SPSS – Intermediate course
Statistical Software:               SPSS
Requirements: Introductory course in Statistics
Instructor:         Dr. Samir Safi
Target Group:             Postgraduate students and interested researchers
Date:     2 Oct.– 15 Oct., 2023
Period:       (12 Hours), 3 days, 4 hours/day
Platform:         Face-to-face Meeting
Inclusive of Scientific Materials:                       PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate
Description:
This workshop introduces participants to the classical methods and techniques of descriptive statistics and
statistical inference and applications from different fields. Topics include descriptive and exploratory data,
inference involving univariate statistical analysis, t-tests, and ANOVA.
Topics Covered:
Day 1:
           Introduction to appropriate statistical software to analyze data, (SPSS).
           Conduct appropriate exploratory analysis to describe the data.
           Data cleaning and manipulation.
Day 2:
           Describe the concept of sampling distribution and interval estimation.
           Introduction to Parametric and Non-Parametric tests.
           Analyze data to make inferences about one, or two population Means and Proportions.
Day 3:
           Compare several independent groups’ means (One and Two-Way factor ANOVA).
           Compare several dependent groups’ means (ANOVA for Repeated measures).
           Case study.
Workshop Fee:               AED 1500*
Contact US:           statconsult@uaeu.ac.ae
*20% off for UAEU students
