## Strategy and Future Department > Overview
The Strategy and Future Department is dedicated to supporting the university achieve its vision and strategic goals by ensuring a robust strategic planning process that enables informed decision-making and supports the institution's continued growth and organizational excellence. The department oversees the development and implementation of strategic and operational plans that align with national priorities and global educational trends.

Additionally, the department plays a crucial role in applying Institutional Excellence Standards to achieve excellence and leadership of the United Arab Emirates University.
