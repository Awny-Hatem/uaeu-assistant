## Financial Leadership and Early Professional Decision-Making
Title: Financial Leadership and Early Professional Decision-Making

Date: Thursday, 02 July 2026

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Mr. Amer Al-Ahbabi

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: Success in business is measured not by how you start, but by your ability to endure and create lasting value. This lecture approaches financial leadership as a daily decision: from understanding the UAE’s compliance landscape, to managing cash flow and hidden costs. From there it turns to building relationships, systems, and performance metrics on which any successful venture rests. It also carries a message for students and early-career professionals on building financial awareness early and the importance of professional certifications.
