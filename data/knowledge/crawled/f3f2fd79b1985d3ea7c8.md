GRADUATION DATA

    The following are the graduation data of the College of Education CAEP programs –Early Childhood
Education (ECE), Special Education (SPED, and Health & Physical Education (HPE).


    A.   Number of Completers

                   Figure 1 shows an increasing trend in the EPP program graduates. There was an average
         of 114 graduates from AY 2019-2020 to AY 2021-2022.

                   There was a total of 121 graduates in Fall 2021 and Spring 2022 in which 55% are
         completers of the ECE program, 32% are completers of the SPED program, and 13% are
         completers of the HPE program (see Table 1 and Figure 2).
