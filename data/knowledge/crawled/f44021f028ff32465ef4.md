## Overview
The Library Club aims to enhance communication and interaction between the Library of the United Arab Emirates University and its students. Additionally, it seeks to support and empower students by providing volunteering opportunities, organizing events, and serving both the university and local communities.

Values of the Library Club:

Respect

Cooperation

Creativity

Professionalism

Continuous Improvement

Hima Team

This team focuses on facilitating access for people of determination to library resources and services.

Library Resources Receiving and Delivery

This service is available on campus for delivering and receiving information resources from the library for students of determination residing in university housing.

Printing & scanning papers and sending them personally or via e-mail.

This service provides printing and scanning documents services for students of determination, with the option to send them either in person or via email.

Request a Library Companion

This service provides help for people of determination and guidance during library tours, such as: halls, printers, references, study retreats, etc.

Note: To apply for this service, requests must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance. Service hours are as follows:

Monday to Thursday: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Friday: 9:00 AM to 10:00 AM
