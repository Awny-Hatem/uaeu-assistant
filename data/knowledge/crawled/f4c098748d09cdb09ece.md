CHSS GRADUATE STUDIES FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

                                            (FAQ)

How do I apply?


Students wishing to enroll in a program can apply online through               the online

application system.

This online service allows you to:



   •   Complete the online application form.


   •   Save your application to complete later (p         lease note your Login ID and

       PIN).


   •   Upload documents in support of your application (each document

       separately).


   •   Submit your online application.


   •   Upload the security form.




What documents are required?



   •   Emirati students require a copy of passport, nationality ID and UAE

       identity card. International students inside UAE require a copy of

       passport, valid visa and UAE identity card. International students outside

       UAE need only a copy of the passport.

•   Copy of official certificates & transcripts (Mark sheet) from all colleges or

       universities attended (Master and Bachelor). Students who have Degrees

When will I receive a response on my application?


Results of the application will be announced at least a month prior to the

registration period. Applicants will find the result on the application system. For

any question regarding the application please contact the UAEU Admissions

Office: PostGraduateAdmissions@uaeu.ac.ae / +971 3 7134279.




How much do the Master’s Programs cost?

To view the tuition fees of the Master’s Programs, please visit the               Program and

Accommodation Fees webpage. Once accepted, applicants can pay their

Master’s Program fees using their bank card through E               -Services.

## PDF page 3
Are there scholarships available?

Yes. UAEU has a variety of financial support options available for its students.

To view the scholarships available, please visit the Scholarships, Fellowships,

and Graduate Assistantships webpage.




What is the duration of the Master’s Programs?

The Master’s Programs usually span 2 to 3 years depending on the program

and  student’s selection of thesis/non-thesis/internship options.




Can I pay for this program in installments?


Yes, installment payment is possible. The Installment tuition fee service is an

online service that provides students with an alternative method to pay their

tuition fees during each semester. The student may divide the tuition fee in

three installments within three months (for all Master and PHD students), or

four installments within four months (for only DBA students and student

employees at UAE University), in which the student will pay the amount agreed

upon in the online service within the specified date. The service opens at the

beginning of each semester and will be announced to all students by the

financial department.

## PDF page 4
What is the start date for the programs?

The start date for each semester as well as other important dates can be found

on the UAEU calendar webpage.




Is hostel facility available and if yes, what additional fees are required?


Yes, the university provides paid hostel facilities that include meals and

transportation to the university if necessary. To view the hostel fees, please

visit the Program and Accommodation Fees webpage.




In what language are the programs taught?


The language of instruction in UAEU is English. With the exception of

programs specified as Arabic-taught.

case of technical problems (login or password problems) please contact the
