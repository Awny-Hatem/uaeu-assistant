## IELTS - How to Apply
Registration Hours - ALAIN

07:30 am - 03:00 pm Monday to Thursday

Important registration information for IELTS on paper

If you’re not officially a resident in UAE, you will not be able to sit an IELTS on paper test. However, you can still take the test on computer so please do choose that option.

## IELTS - How to Apply > Fill in the Online Application form
Visit the Online Registration Page and complete your application form by following these steps:

Check availability of the test date and venues

Accept the IELTS Terms and Conditions

Fill in the IELTS online candidate registration form

Upload your Identity Document ( Valid Copy of your Passport and/or Emirates National ID)

Check your application details before you submit the application.

Submit your application by clicking on Apply Now. You will then receive an email containing details about your test booking. Please also note that the confirmation is subject to the verification of your ID document.

## IELTS - How to Apply > Make the Payment
Payment must be received within 3 days of your online booking, otherwise your registration will be automatically cancelled.You can pay by bank transfer, credit cards and/or online Cash will not be accepted.

Option 1: Payment online

If you choose to pay online please make sure your card is enabled and there are sufficient funds to cover the transaction. Also ensure you have your card details with you before starting the registration, as you will not be able to go back and pay online for the same date.
