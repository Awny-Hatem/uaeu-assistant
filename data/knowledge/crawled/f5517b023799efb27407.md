Student Accounts Office
                       Guide for Paying Fees for Bachelor Program for Fall 2018



 The Student Accounts Office (SAO), is responsible for the billing of all student fees and
charges, and is located on the Ground floor of Crescent Building, Wing D4, Male
Registration Area, Al Ain. Our working hours for student enquiries are 8am – 3pm, Sunday
through Thursday.

SAO Contact information:
             o   Phone:  03 7134265
             o   Email address:  Student_Accounts@uaeu.ac.ae





 Payment method:
You can pay for the tuition via the e-payment ( online Payment), please log on to this page
to pay: https://ssb.uaeu.ac.ae/prod/bwykstpy.p_display_opt




 Deadline for Settlement of Tuition & Fees for Fall 2018:
                1st Payment Deadline (50% of total tuition fees ) on, 26 – 30 August 2018
                2nd Payment Deadline (Remaining 50% of total tuition fees) on, 02 – 20
                 September 2018

** Non-Payment of total tuition fees on or before 20 September 2018 will result to
withdrew courses that student registered.
