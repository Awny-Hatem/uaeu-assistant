As part of its continuing commitment to publish statistical data that could be used in
planning and research, University administration is pleased to provide (on the occasion or
the graduation of  the  31th  students  cohort)   the following tables which contain  detailed

statistics that describe both current and historical profile of the graduates in the period
(1980/1981-2010/2011).   The tables  also  include  numbers of graduates by college and
by major of study.
