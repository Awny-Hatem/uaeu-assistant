                  Fees for Post-Graduate Programs Procedure


Major Procedural Changes in This Release

It is the policy of the United Arab Emirates University to charge appropriate fees to
students in post-graduate programs.  The purpose of this procedure is to assist post-
graduate faculty and administrators in understanding and recommending fees and
charges for post–graduate education.

Procedure

Determination of Program Fees

With this procedure faculty recommending post-graduate work will recommend fee
structures as well.  All fees are based upon a consideration of the actual costs to the
University of offering each program.  Fees should be reconsidered as cost data
indicate that fees are more than or less than necessary to sustain a program.

Application Fee

Every application for admission to a post-graduate program should be submitted with
an application fee.  This fee pays for the processing required to consider it.  The post-
graduate application for admission fee will be 200 AED per application.

Per Credit Fees

As each student registers for classes for a particular term, a fee will be levied based on
the number of credits for which the student will be enrolled.  The amount levied will

1.                                                                                                Tuition Per Credit

          Tuition is typically calculated on the basis of cost per student credit hour.
          Based upon costs for educating students in post-graduate courses, the tuition
          per credit hour will be 1,200 AED.  If the student were registering for nine (9)
          hours of course work, the tuition fee for that term would be nine times 1,200
          AED or 10,800 AED.

2.                                                                                               Laboratory/Access Fees

          These are fees that address costs which vary from one post-graduate major to
          another.  Some majors have higher costs for computer use, unique software,
          chemicals, travel and special insurance.  These costs are estimated during the
          first offer of a program and updated with experience.

3.                                                                                               Venue Fees

          The venue fee addresses the costs associated with instructional setting (as in
          another city) or method of instruction (the case of satellite or internet
          development and delivery).  Teaching courses in another city may incur
          additional costs for internet service, for lease of space, transportation of
          students and/or faculty, etc.  For courses taught on the Al Ain campus, the per
          credit venue cost will be 0 AED.  For those taught in Abu Dhabi, the per credit
          venue cost will be 800 AED.

Managing Fees

All cheques and invoices referring to a particular program must contain the
Classification of Instructional Program (CIP) code of the program.  This will make it
easier to trace the costs and revenues of each program.  For example, a cheque made
out to pay fees for the program in Environmental Science would show the payee as
“UAE University CIP 26.1307”.

Explanatory Notes:

        1.                                                             An application fee is not returned, whether or not the student is eventually
                admitted to a post-graduate program.

        2.                                                              An application fee must accompany each application.  If a student submits
                more than one application at the same time, the application fee must
                accompany each application.

        3.                                      A student who changes majors, or who prolongs completion of degree
                requirements will enroll for more than the minimum credits required for a
                master’s degree.  Each of those credits above the minimum will also be
                charged to the student at the per credit hour fee above.

        4.                                This procedure is applicable on all students joining the post-graduate
                programs starting the first semester of the academic year 2005-2006.
