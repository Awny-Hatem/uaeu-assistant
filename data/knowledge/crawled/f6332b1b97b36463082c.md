                           The 4th Leadership Forum
                   A PLATFORM FOR YOUTH TO VOICE AND DISCOVER THEIR CONCERNS







                                      22nd - 23rd March 2011

  AIESEC in a partnership with Careers UAE - the most established leading recruitment, education
  and training event, which held under the patronage of His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin
  Rashid Al Maktoum, Vice President and Prime Minister of the UAE, and Ruler of Dubai,
           cordially invites you to take part in The 4th AIESEC Leadership Forum "You Lead".

                                                     What is it about?
   "You Lead" is unique open forum where companies' representatives together with youth will be
    talking about such topics as Entrepreneurship and Leadership, Career Progression and
  Unemployment, and how International Experience brings additional value for young person
                                           during the job searching process.

                                        Why is it important for companies?
The Forum is great opportunity for companies to get access to ideas and opinions of young people
about relevant issues. To raise youth voices and create right platform for their development. Share
      what they expect from young employees and know what young people expect from them.

                                             Why is it important for you?
  It is opportunity to meet new people and discuss topic you are interested in. You will be one of
those, who will have unique chance to meet, talk and share your opinion and ideas with company's
   leaders. Certificates of active participation will be given to delegates, who attend the complete
                                                    program of the day.

            Forum venue: Dubai International Exhibition and Convention Center, Saeed Hall
                                                Forum Time: 10am - 2pm

      To know more about forum you can visiting AIESEC UAE web-page: www.aiesec.org/ae






                                  Registration will be open till 15th of March!
                               Lead or Follow - It is UP to YOU!
