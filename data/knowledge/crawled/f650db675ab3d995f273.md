## Department of Accounting > Overview
The Department of Accounting is synonymous with excellence in accounting education and research in the UAE and aspires to be a leading global center for accounting and financial thought in the wider GCC and the MENA regions.

We are committed to educating the next generation of Accounting, Auditing Professionals, expertly guiding our students through the challenges that come with continuous technological development.

The Department is the first AACSB- Accredited Accounting department in the GCC and MENA regions. AACSB Accreditation is the hallmark of excellence in business education, and has been earned by less than 5% of the world's business schools.

Furthermore, our Bachelor of Accounting program is accredited by the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) and the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW) , and has also rece ived endorsement from the Institute of Management Accountants (IMA) for its alignment with the Certified Management Accountant (CMA) certification. With these prestigious recognitions, our accounting students can accelerate their progress toward ACCA , ICAEW , and CMA professional qualifications.

Our undergraduate and postgraduate programs provide the practical and theoretical knowledge necessary for navigating and providing management and leadership of the complex global world of business; and helps students to develop the skills needed for active participation in society beyond graduation.

Faculty in the Department of Accounting mainly contribute to teaching in the following academic programs, offered by the College:

Bachelor of Accounting

Master of Professional Accounting

To find out more about the programs we have on offer, please use the links above, or look through our course list, available here .

We are very proud of our alumni, many of whom have significant positions both in government and in private enterprises, thanks to the rich academic experiences they acquired throughout their studies.

We hope that our website will help you find the information you need, be it details of our undergraduate and postgraduate programs, or the many services and activities available to our stakeholders. Please do not hesitate to contact us for any further details.

Chair, Department of Accounting

## Department of Accounting > Research
The Department of Accounting research is focused around the following main themes. Across all themes, research is guided by interdisciplinarity, policy relevance, societal impact, international collaboration, and alignment with the UAEU and national research priorities.

Financial Reporting Quality and Regulation

Auditing and Assurance

Governance, Accountability, and Management Control

Sustainability, ESG, and Integrated Reporting

Accounting Information Systems, Digitalization, and Analytics

Taxation and Tax Regulation

Accounting Education

The accounting faculty comprises a diverse and internationally engaged academic community, with a significant number of members holding academic positions and qualifications from institutions worldwide.

Faculty members consistently publish in leading, internationally recognized journals, reflecting the high quality, visibility, and global relevance of their research.

They actively contribute to the advancement and governance of the accounting discipline by serving on editorial boards and regularly participating as peer and external reviewers for prestigious international journals. This commitment is further demonstrated through their leadership roles and active involvement in top-tier journals and major global accounting conferences.

Research relevance and societal impact are strengthened by consistently securing competitive external funding from internationally recognized professional bodies and research centres affiliated with leading global universities, including the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (UK), University of California, Berkeley (USA), Boston College (USA), Newton (UK), and Erasmus+ (EU).
