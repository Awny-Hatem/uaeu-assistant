## Optimizing Job Market Skills for 2035
Title: Optimizing Job Market Skills for 2035 through Short-Term Learning Modules and Micro-Credentials

Date: Wednesday, 24 June 2026

Time: 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Dr. Dalal AlShamisi

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: The skills required by the labor market in the UAE and around the world are changing rapidly, necessitating the adoption of the concept of lifelong learning. This ensures that employees remain capable of contributing and achieving, regardless of the number of years that have passed since their university graduation. The UAE Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research has urged higher education institutions to develop short-term learning programs that address labor market skills in more than ten emerging fields, aligning with global scientific and social developments.
