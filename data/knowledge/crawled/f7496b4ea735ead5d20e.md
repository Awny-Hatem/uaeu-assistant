## International Students Office > Overview
The International Students Office (ISO) welcomes all international students, and values the culture they bring to the United Arab Emirates University. Our ISO team coordinates the administrative needs of international students and supports their academic and personal development and success.

The ISO assists and advises international students at the UAEU with all matters relevant to visa rules and regulations, financial issues, and health insurance in compliance with UAE and the UAE University’s policies. The ISO also provides liaison with governmental agencies and foreign embassies in an effort to support the students in their international affairs while enrolled at the UAEU.

To enhance international students’ personal and academic success through providing high quality orientation programs, services and skilled support.
