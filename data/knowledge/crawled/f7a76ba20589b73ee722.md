                                                                      International Business Travel


I.                                                                                               Policy

                     International travel is required from time to time to carry out the business of
                     the United Arab Emirates University.

                     International travel for business purposes is governed by this policy.  (This
                     policy does not govern travel for professional development, including research
                     purposes.)

                     Forms and procedures necessary to implement this policy are available in the
                     Office of the Secretary General.


II.                                                                             Application

                     This policy shall apply to all employees of the United Arab Emirates
                     University.
