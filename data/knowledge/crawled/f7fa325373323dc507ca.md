As part of its continuing commitment to publish statistical data that could be used in
 planning and research, University administration is pleased to provide (on the occasion of
 the graduation of  the  37th  students  cohort)   the following tables which contain  detailed

 statistics that describe both current and historical profile of the graduates in the period
 (1980/1981-2016/2017).   The tables  also  include  numbers of graduates by college and
 by major of study.
       We hope that you find the information useful.
