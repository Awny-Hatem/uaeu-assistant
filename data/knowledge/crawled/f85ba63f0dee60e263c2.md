## Field Experience Office > Events and Activities
The FEO in cooperation with TEC provides various training opportunities to the students’ candidates, faculty supervisors, school principals, and cooperative teachers on all aspects of the practicum and professional development to support their professional growth and assures successful filed experiences, along with the support and workshops delivered by our partnerships. Also, Multaga Meydan, provides presentations on topics related to clinical experiences, classroom management, and assessment outcomes for P-12 students.
