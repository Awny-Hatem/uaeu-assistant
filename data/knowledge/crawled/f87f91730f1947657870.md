6. Teaching Assistant Violations, Disciplinary Actions and Grievances

Goal
TA policies and its Code of Conduct describe the extents expected from the TA conduct and duties.
Violation of these policies and regulations is subject to disciplinary actions which may impose penalties.


Policy

1.   In case the TA is accused of a violation requires interrogation, he/she will be subject to the faculty
     disciplinary actions and policies relevant to university faculty violations and grievances.

2.   In special cases, committees may be formed for investigation by a decree of the Vice Chancellor
     including the responsibilities of the committee.

3.   The  resignation  of  TA  shall  not  impede  proceeding  with  his/her  disciplinary  interrogation
     procedures and it shall be prohibited to accept the resignation if the TA has been referred to the
     Investigation  Committee or Violations Committee.
