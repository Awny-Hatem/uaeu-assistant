                                                  Compensation Administration for
                                         Academic Administrative Appointments


I. Policy

                 The compensation administration policy is designed to offer competitive pay
                 and benefits to attract and retain highly qualified employees who support the
                 United Arab Emirates University’s ongoing pursuit of excellence.  The
                 University is committed to the establishment of compensation levels that are
                 equitable both inside the University and within the appropriate job labor
                 market.

                 Unless otherwise modified in another policy, academic administrators will be
                 paid based on their faculty rank, plus an increment, or as otherwise specified
                 in current University policies and practices until a new classification and
                 compensation plan has been approved.  The terms and conditions in current
                 contracts shall continue until expiration or termination.


II.                                                                             Application

                This policy shall apply to all academic administrative appointments at the
                United Arab Emirates University, except to the College of Medicine and
                Health Sciences.
