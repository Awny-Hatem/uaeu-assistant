## The World of Digital > Digital Strategy and Transformation > Digital Transformation and Innovation
The course enables participants with an overview of the digital forces transforming their industries, markets, and the world, including a comprehensive toolbox to identify and address the challenges digitization poses to their companies and industries. It will help to understand the economic logic and technical capabilities driving the on-going digital transformation and disruptions in diverse industries; analyses and navigate digital ecosystems, platforms, and new business models; recognize the opportunities and challenges created by AI and smart algorithms; design organizational structures, practices, and cultures that leverage the potential of contemporary digital technologies; and understand how to design and implement digital transformation programs.

## The World of Digital > Digital Marketing for Managers > Digital Marketing for Managers
Digital technologies are means to serve the end of marketing. This course is therefore designed to focus on how to develop a holistic and strategic understanding of digital transformation for marketing. Moreover, it also takes a practical approach to introduce useful tools and frameworks for planning and execution. Through faculty presentations, case studies, and small-group discussions, delegates will master the fundamental principles that remain valid in the digital economy; rethink the customer journey; investigate the latest trends in digital marketing; master how to leverage multiple digital channels to achieve marketing objectives; and deliver positive and relevant experiences across all channels and touchpoints.

## The World of Digital > Blockchain for Managers > Blockchain for Managers
Blockchain is the technology underlying new digital currencies such as bitcoin. It provides a very secure platform to transact on a global scale. This course will introduce delegates to the concepts of blockchain and how it is and can be used in the world of business today.
