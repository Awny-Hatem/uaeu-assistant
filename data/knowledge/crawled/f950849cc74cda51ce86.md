# Core Research Objectives

Source applicability: undated-current-page. Verification date confirms access, not the effective date of every rule. Undated, legacy and cohort facts must retain their labels.

## Core Research Objectives
Conduct fundamental and applied AI research addressing UAE's strategic priorities and global challenges

Advance theoretical understanding of intelligent systems across sectors including healthcare, education, environment, energy, and governance.

Foster interdisciplinary research collaboration among faculty, graduate students, government research institutes, and international academic partners.

Establish research training programs to develop national AI research expertise and scholarly leadership.

Serve as a catalyst for evidence-based policy research, ethical AI investigation, and translational research methodologies.
