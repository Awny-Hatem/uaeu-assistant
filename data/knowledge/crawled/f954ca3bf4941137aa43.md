## Sustainable Development Goals > SDG Reel Competition
To build further awareness of the Sustainable Development Goals among UAEU community, we are hosting SDG Reel Competition! Students who wish to enter the contest should create a reel on Instagram, addressing any of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals.

## Sustainable Development Goals > 2000 AED > Judging Criteria
Creativity

Relevance to the theme

Content clarity

Impact on the community

## Sustainable Development Goals > 2000 AED > Guidelines
Each participant can submit only one Reel.

The Instagram Reel has to be relevant to the competition theme.

Use #UAEU_SDG as a tag for their Reels in the caption.

Should include 5 lines description of the video.

Make sure their Reel has a maximum duration of 60 seconds.

Verify their Instagram account is set as a “Public account” to ensure viewability of all entries.

UAEU reserves the right to publish Reels in any online media for promotion.
