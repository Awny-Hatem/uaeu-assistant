## Uses of Artificial Intelligence in Legal Practice and Research – Google Tools
Workshop Title: Uses of Artificial Intelligence in Legal Practice and Research - Google Tools

Date: Wednesday, 19 August 2026

Time: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Location: Microsoft Teams

Workshop Presenter: Dr. Ammar Ali

Workshop Language: Arabic

About the Workshop: The workshop aims to equip participants with the knowledge and skills required to understand artificial intelligence technologies and apply them effectively in legal practice and research. This will contribute to enhancing professional efficiency, improving the quality of legal research and analysis, and supporting the accurate and effective completion of legal tasks.

By the end of the workshop, participants are expected to be able to:

Identify the fundamental concepts of artificial intelligence and its key uses in the legal field.

Distinguish between different artificial intelligence tools and select those most appropriate to the nature of legal tasks and activities
