## E-Journals
The National Medical Library offer students, faculty and staff a large collection of medical journals through electronic subscriptions. The journals are accessible on and off campus and can be located with the help of the library’s discovery system: ” Summon ”, or via the browsing tool: “ e-journals ”:

All journals are also accessible through the Library’s journal app “BrowZine”. Download the app from your app store and read, get notifications and build up your own journal book shelve directly through your computer or mobile device. Ask at the library or visit www.browzine.com to get started and for more information.
