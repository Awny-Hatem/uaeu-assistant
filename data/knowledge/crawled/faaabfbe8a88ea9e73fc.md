## UAEU Alumni Award > UAEU Alumni Wall of Fame
The United Arab Emirates University Alumni Wall of Fame aims to recognize outstanding achievements after graduating from the university, including professional achievements and / or work in the field of public service.

Conditions

It is required that the graduates nominated have:

Hold leadership positions within their communities and / or workplaces;

Made significant contributions to the care and / or success of those around them, provided that those contributions exceed what the average person in the same position might make, resulting from the effort to exceed the usual standards, accompanied by a demonstration of personality, integrity and leadership role of the nominee;

The nominee level of participation with the university, its students and professors, will be taken into consideration.

The names of ten selected alumni will be added to the wall of fame during the annual alumni meeting at the university .

Recommendations

All governmental and private institutions, departments and strategic partners of the United Arab Emirates University have the right to nominate whomever they deem appropriate for the Wall of Fame award from the graduates of the United Arab Emirates University who have clear contributions and distinguished achievements in their fields.
