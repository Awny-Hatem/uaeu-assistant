## Emirates Health Services > Professional Skills Development and Training
The Emirates Health Services (EHS) incorporates Medical Education which is responsible for organizing all Professional Skills Development Training activities.

The objectives include:

To survey the educational needs of regional physicians and to promote programmes of professional development to meet those needs.

To facilitate the provision of courses, symposia and a variety of educational programmes for medical practitioners and other health science personnel at local, regional and other venues using faculty members and other professionals in the region and beyond.

To collaborate with individuals and departments in the initiation, development and provision of professional skills and training programmes.
