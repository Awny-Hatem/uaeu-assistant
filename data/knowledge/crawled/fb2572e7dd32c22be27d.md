## Undergraduate Programs > Credit-Bearing Micro-Credential in AI in Gaming
The AI in Gaming is a 6-credit CBMC that introduces students to the application of artificial intelligence in game development and interactive media, with a particular focus on UAE culture, heritage, and tourism. The micro-credential aims to provide students with foundational knowledge and practical exposure to AI-driven techniques used in modern digital games and interactive systems.

The scope of the micro-credential emphasizes the development of both creative and technical skills required to design AI-enabled games, virtual heritage simulations, and gamified tourism applications that promote the UAE’s identity and destinations. Upon successful completion, students are awarded a Micro-credential in AI in Gaming , which is formally recorded on their academic transcript and is non-stackable toward any degree programs.
