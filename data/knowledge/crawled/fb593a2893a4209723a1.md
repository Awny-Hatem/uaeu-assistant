## PDF page 5
Conditions for obtaining financial aid :-

   Student should be continuing study
   The guardian of the student should be informed in advance that the student
    will receive a financial aid.
   The student should not  be paid a salary or regular  financial help from any
    other source within the university.
   Average monthly income for the family member shouldn’t exceed the AED
    2000 for UAE citizens, and AED 900 for non-citizens.
   Student GPA should be 2.00 and above.
     Fresh student are excepted from the previous GPA condition, because they
don’t have GPA for the first two semesters.
   The university have the right to request additional documents from the
    student or except some of the required documents according to the student
    status, nationality or admission system.

## PDF page 6
   Applying should be through eServices (during the specified time
    period)
   Availability of financial resources.
   Open a bank account for the student

 Rules for receiving financial aid :-
   The student is permanently excluded from the program for the
    following reasons:
 - If the student is an employee in the country, whatever the salary is.
 - If the student own his own car.
 - If the average monthly income for the family member exceeds the
AED 2000 for UAE citizens, and AED 900 for non-citizens.
   children under the age of education have the same weight when
    calculating the income of family members except in case  of
    disability, the disabled member is counted as two if he is over 18
    and not working.

## PDF page 7
Documents required to apply for financial aid :-

   copy of the university I D card + Student identity card.
   a copy of the identification papers of the  family members who do not work and
    mentioned in the request for financial assistance,  the student passport copy +
    copy of family book for UAE citizens, and for non-citizens a copy of passport for
    the student + Father + Mother + brothers and sister in the age of education.
   Recent certificate (maximum six  months) stating the total salary of the
    guardian, or pension or monthly alimony for divorced mother.
   In the case of work to the private sector copy of the employment contract
    approved by the Department of Labor (the private sector) in addition to the
    salary certificate.
   Recent Certificate from the Ministry of Social Affairs (if applicable).
   Death certificate of the guardian (if applicable).
