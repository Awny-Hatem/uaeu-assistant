## Al Foah Experimental Farm > Small Ruminants
Herds of goats and sheep are kept on the farm for research and educational use. The animals are of local breed and are well adapted to the climatic conditions of the UAE. Selection for breeding aims at improving fertility, body shape and environmental adaptation. Goat and sheep experiments often deal with the value of different types of fodder for meat production. Currently the farm keeps around 60 of each.
