Description:
The workshop introduces participants to IBM SPSS for Windows. Participants will be trained
on comparing and protecting original datasets, and on handling data files in a variety of formats,
including Excel, database tables, SAS, and Stata. They will also be trained on how to explore
SPSS Data Editor which provides for the display of data and variables. The workshop also
offers hands-on training on data entry, variable, and data manipulation, including finding and
replacing data and variables, data transformation, and descriptive statistics. In addition to other
important SPSS features, participants will also explore working with SPSS output, pivot table,
and command syntax.
