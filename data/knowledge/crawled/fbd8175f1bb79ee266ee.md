Explore business careers at Microsoft.

     The  'Microsoft      Academy  for       College     Hires'/  aka  'MACH'  is  Microsoft’s  graduate  recruitment  and
     development program that provides long term talent development for top new university graduates in our
     business.  If you're interested in a business career in                 sales  or  marketing        and are passionate about
     technology, we could help you realize your own potential with us!



                                                                                  When  you're  offered  a  full  time         graduate
                                                                                  opportunity   at   Microsoft,   you   stand   to
                                                                                  receive       world      class      learning      and
                                                                                  development.  We’ll  help  you          to  attain   the
                                                                                  tools to take on any challenge and the ability
                                                                                  to deliver solutions with the power to touch
                                                                                  the lives of    people all over the world.         With
                                                                                  close to 2,000 MACH employees in over 50
                                                                                  countries,  you'll  get  exposure  to  a  global
                                                                                  network   of   peers   and         the   support   to
                                                                                  transition  successfully  from  school  to  the
                                                                                  working environment.

     At Microsoft Gulf, while contributing           to the business success, you’ll have access to a graduate program
     that’s designed to ensure that you receive targeted experiences, community engagement, mentorship
     opportunities, career development support and exposure to institutional knowledge through a customized
     development curriculum.

     To qualify for MACH, you must:

             Be a final year student or a fresh graduate of a Business or IT related degree
             Have a GPA equivalent to 3.0 or above
             Be able to demonstrate a passion for technology
             Speak and write fluent English and Arabic
             Possess excellent communication and presentation skills as well as strong interpersonal skills
             Be a strategic thinker
             Be able to balance individual creativity with working as a team
             Have a high degree of customer focus

     Microsoft supports workforce diversity and welcomes
     applications from Gulf National and Female graduates.
