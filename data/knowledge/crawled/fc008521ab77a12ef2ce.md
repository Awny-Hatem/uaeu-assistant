Spectrophotometer 6320D

                      Switch           on             the                                      Spectrophotometer

                 The spectrophotometer has to be warmed up for 5 - 10 minutes. This is to stabilize the
                 machine and avoid fluctuations in the readings.


       Setting Measurement Wavelengths

       1         Press the up arrow key to increase the wavelength, press the down arrow key
                 to decrease the wavelength.

       Photometric Calibration

       1         Insert a cuvette containing the blank solution in the sample holder and close
                 the sample chamber lid.
       2         Press the CAL key, the ‘Calibrating…                ’ information box will be displayed and
                 then the readout up-dated to zero absorbance and 100%T.

                                    Absorbance and    %                          T Measurement

       1         Remove the cuvette containing the blank solution, then insert the cuvette
                 containing the standard solution into the sample holder and close the sample
                 chamber lid.
       2         Allow the reading on the display to settle and record this as the first result.

       3         If the optional printer is connected, simply press the print key to print the first
                 result. This will be printed along with the header text, giving details of the
                 instrument, the operator ID and the time and date of the last calibration. The
                 sample number will increment for each result printed, being re-set to 001
                 following a subsequent calibration.

       4         Unknown samples can be measured by inserting them in the sample holder as
                 above and press Read or Enter.

          5      Press Save for saving the results as a text file.
