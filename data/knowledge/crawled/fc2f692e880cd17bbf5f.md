## Ashraf Moustafa > Personal profile > Research interests
Dr. Ashraf Mustafa is an Instructor at Special and gifted Education department, He has clinical and educational experience working with children with Sensory Impairment, L.D, Autism, ADHD and gifted students.

Braille trainer, Also, he has the American license for Irlen Syndrome. Recently developed the 2D and 3D battery to measure cognitive abilities for the blind as the first battery targeting the visually impaired using assistive educational technology.

His research interests are in Sensory Impairment and Assessment for children with Sensory Impairment, developmental disorders and learning difficulties.
