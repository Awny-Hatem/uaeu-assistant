Cell Manufacturing Specialist

General Information
StromaBio UAE is looking for dedicated persons to manufacture cells for upcoming clinical
trials. The position is initially a two-year post with the possibility of extension pending further
clinical development. We will perform clinical trials in type 1 Diabetes and Frailty using
Human Mesenchymal Stromal Cells (MSC) as therapy. The work is in collaboration with
Swedish researchers, UAEU, the industry, and SEHA and will be supervised by the Abu Dhabi
Department of Health. The aim is to set up production of MSC using the same protocols
developed in Sweden over the last 15 years generating MSC with proven effect in several
different indications.
MSC are manufactured from human bone marrow and expanded for 4-5 weeks. The cells are
adherent and fibroblast-like in appearance. We need to hire three persons to manufacture the
cells, document the process, perform quality controls etc. all according to written standard
operating routines. All documentation will be in English. Mandatory training will be provided.

Responsibilities
        Assist in setting up a new production lab in Abu Dhabi Emirate (first in Abu Dhabi or
         Al Ain, second step in Abu Dhabi)
        Perform cell culture work aseptically, generating high-quality cell products suitable for
         clinical use (production is partially performed  without antibiotics so high-quality
         aseptic work is an absolute requirement, training will be provided)
        Ensure that the documentation is correctly filled out and up-to-date (very important for
         manufacturing and traceability of products)
        Report to StromaBio chief production officer and possibly a lab coordinator

Duties

        Expansion of clinical-grade human MSC
        Participate in training deemed suitable for the position (training in good manufacturing
         practice (GMP), in cell culture, flow cytometry, good clinical practice (GCP) etc.).
        Follow written protocols, perform controls to ensure that all relevant data is secured
        Not share data with anyone not specified in writing to belong to the production team
         (documents may contain personal data or data that will be used to apply for patents)
        Order consumables, medium etc.
        Maintain a high-quality lab (cleaning or manage cleaning of the lab, ensure that the
         equipment is serviced etc.).

        Computer skills: Office Programs (primarily Word and Excel)
