## Program objectives > Program objectives
After completion of this course, participants will be able to:

Use basic greetings

Talk about themselves and their families

Improve reading and listening skills

Use vocabulary and helpful phrases for travel

Conjugate verbs and use them in meaningful conversations

Appreciate Spanish culture

## Methods of instruction > Methods of instruction
Online material, quizzes and summaries prepared from different aspects.
