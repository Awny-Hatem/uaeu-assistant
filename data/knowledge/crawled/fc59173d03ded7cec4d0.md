## Department of Pathology > Teaching > Innovation Teaching
Pathology Department Instagram Educational site

This is an interactive educational project targeting medical students and residents. It presents clinically encountered clinical cases seen by pathologists as a member of the clinical patient managing team. The cases are presented as they are encountered in the clinical setting by presenting a brief pertinent history, the pathological findings and other supportive investigations, diagnosis and outcome/treatment options. It aids in the educational process through visual memory of the cases presented. Students regularly participate in preparing case discussions.
