## Scholarworks@UAEU > Browse Research and Scholarship Follow > UAEU's Journals
Scholarworks@UAEU is an open-access, sustainable and secure resource created to preserve and provide access to research, and creative works generated by the university community for the benefit of UAEU students, faculty, staff and the world at large. This service is managed by the UAEU Library with support from the Office of the Deputy Vice Chancellor for Research and Graduate Studies. Members of the UAEU academic community are invited to contribute scholarship for long-term preservation and worldwide electronic access.
