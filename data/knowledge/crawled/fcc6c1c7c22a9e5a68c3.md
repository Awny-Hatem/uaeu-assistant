ENROLLMENT
              The following shows the enrollment data of the College of Education initial-licensure level programs:
         Early Childhood Education (ECE), Special Education (SPED                 ), and Health & Physical Education (HPE).

              A.   Program Enrollment Trend from Fall 2021 to Fall 2024

                           The enrollment in the initial licensure-level programs of the Educator Preparation Provider
                   (EPP) has demonstrated a consistent upward trend from Fall 2021 to Fall 2024. On average, fall-
                   to-fall enrollment has increased by 13%. The average enrollment figures for the initial licensure-
                   level programs are as follows: 276 in Early Childhood Education (ECE), 151 in Special Education
                   (SPED), and 58 in Health and Physical Education (HPE).

              C.   Fall 2023- Spring 2024 Enrollment by Nationality
                             The majority of students admitted and enrolled in ECE, SPED, and HPE programs are
                   Emiratis, with only 5% of the total student population being                      non-nationals. The EPP actively
                   recruits high school students from across the emirates through initiatives such as College Open
                   Days, High School Exhibitions, and School Site Visits, aiming to inspire them to become future
                   educators in the UAE.
