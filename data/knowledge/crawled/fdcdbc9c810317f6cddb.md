## MSc in Forensic Science > Overview
The MSc in Forensic Science (MSFS) at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) prepares graduates to apply advanced scientific principles to the investigation and resolution of crime. The program combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on laboratory experience, supporting the UAE’s national vision for innovation, security, and justice.
