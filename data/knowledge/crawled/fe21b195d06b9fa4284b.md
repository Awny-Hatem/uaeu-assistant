## University Campus Vehicle Access Permit
This service enables female students at the Al Ain to access the parking facilities designated for them within the university campus.

Start Service

## University Campus Vehicle Access Permit > Procedure
Students receive a service activation notification and subsequently download the authorized mobile application to access the service.

Show all steps

## University Campus Vehicle Access Permit > Conditions & Requirements
The student must be admitted to the University and from Al Ain City.

## University Campus Vehicle Access Permit > What are the eligibility requirements for the female students’ parking access service, and what is the approved method of access?
The customer must be a UAEU female student in Al Ain and must download the approved application. The service is provided proactively to eligible students without requiring an application or any supporting documents. Access to the designated female students’ parking areas is provided through the approved application.
