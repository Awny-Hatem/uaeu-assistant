Statistical Consulting Unit

                                         Online Workshops/Training courses
                                                       Workshop #3

Title: Data Analytics using SPSS - Advanced course

Statistical Software: SPSS

Requirements: Hypothesis testing and linear regression

Instructor: Dr. Samir Safi

Target Group: Postgraduate students and interested researchers

Date: 28 March., 2022 – 10 April, 2022

Period: 3 days, 4 hours/day

Platform: Zoom Meeting

Inclusive of Scientific Materials:         Seminars include live lectures, hands-on exercise assignments, and
plenty of chances to ask questions. PowerPoint Slide Presentation and E-Certificate.

Description:

This workshop introduces participants to the advanced methods and techniques of statistical inference and
applications form different fields. Topics include inference involving univariate and multivariate statistical
analysis,

Topics Covered:

Review of    General Linear Model, Repeated Measures ANOVA, Mixed ANOVA, Analysis of ANCOVA,
Multivariate Analysis of Variance  (MANOVA), Multilevel (hierarchical) linear models.

Workshop Fee: 1500 AED

Note: A certificate of completion of the workshop will be provided to participants.
