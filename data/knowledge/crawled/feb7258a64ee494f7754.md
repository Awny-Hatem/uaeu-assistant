12. Student Volunteering

Overview
Details the terms in which volunteer services are enabled so that the University protects its
interests, the interest of the volunteer                          ,and the University                                                        community.

Scope
Applies to all those expressing interest in volunteering within t              he University.

Objective
The University strongly encourages the role of volunteers in the community.  The University
will provide and coordinate volunteer opportunities within the Universit y and with recognized
national organizations within the UAE.

Policy
1.                                  A student volunteer must be willing to provide service in                               accordance with University
      Policies, and Procedures.
2.                                                           The    University volunteers are bound by      University     Policies    with respect to their actions
      wherever they are representing the University.
3.                                                     University volunteers are not considered employees and therefore they are not eligible for
      any compensation or any University benefits.
