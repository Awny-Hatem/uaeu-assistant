## About COS > Overview
The College of Science was among the first Colleges to be established since the inception of United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) in 1976.

The College has five academic departments: The Department of Biology, the Department of Chemistry, the Department of Geosciences, the Department of Mathematical Sciences, and the Department of Physics.

All College programs are accredited by the Commission of Academic Accreditation, Ministry of Education, UAE, and all the undergraduate programs of the College are accredited by international accreditation organizations, where the B.Sc. in Chemistry has been accredited by both the Canadian Society for Chemistry in 2008 and by the Royal Society of Chemistry, the UK in 2011. The B.Sc. in Biology has been accredited by the Royal Society of Biology, UK in 2019, and the B.Sc. in Physics has been accredited by ABET-ANSAC, the USA in 2020. B.Sc. in Geosciences and Mathematical Science accreditation by ABET-ANSAC will come soon.

The College is implementing the use of modern technologies in both teaching and learning through smart classrooms and online technologies. The pandemic of COVID19 has accelerated the process of online teaching, where different programs adjusted to the new circumstances and have used the available technology to deliver different courses online, and a few more courses have been developed for a hybrid mode of teaching. The College labs are equipped with state-of-the-art experimental setups and computer simulations to make sure that our students go through an experiential nature of laboratory-based coursework and field practical experience. Furthermore, our students are required to spend the end of the program 2 months of internship to prepare them for the job market.

One of the main strengths of the College of Science is its faculty members who come from different backgrounds with excellent education from well-known international universities and with high caliber research credentials. Besides their high-quality teaching, they engage in cutting-edge research and involve their graduate and undergraduate students in their research projects. The research labs of the College are equipped with state-of-the-art research instruments. Some of these laboratories are the Nano-Physics Research Laboratory, Genomic Analysis and Microbial Studies Laboratory, Central Analytical Laboratory, Inorganic Geochemistry Laboratory, Geophysics, and Seismic Laboratory, and the Museum of Living Resources and Geological Museum.

## About COS > Dean's Message
Welcome to the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) College of Science (COS) website.

We, at the College of Science, strive to be a vibrant and creative scientific community that nurtures its students with the best education and impart in them the curiosity and eagerness to learn, help them thrive to become the next generation of scientists that positively contribute to the making of a brighter future.

At the College of Science, you will join a distinguished selection of faculty who graduated from the world’s best universities and who are eager to accompany you in your journey into building solid foundation in the basics of the field of science you would like to pursue, whether it is biological sciences, bio-chemistry, chemistry, geosciences, mathematics, physics or space science, in world-class programs that are accredited locally and internationally by well-known organizations and societies, such as the Royal Society of Biology, of Chemistry, Canadian Society of Chemistry, and the Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology-Applied and Natural Science Accreditation Commission (ABET-ANSAC).

In the College of Science, students are expected to be involved in original research, whether through their graduation projects, thesis work or by joining a research program of one of the faculty, where you will have the chance to use state-of-the-art equipment in the science research labs.

At the College of Science, we are dedicated to the career development of our students. We nurture and encourage them to become confident graduates with professional skills and qualities, and enthusiasm for their chosen careers.

Whatever the field of science our students pursue, we challenge them to broaden their horizons and help them to develop the capacity for independent thinking and a lifelong interest in learning, which are the hallmarks of successful leaders.

In partnership with the industry and government entities, we prepare our students for success and leadership in the highly competitive and rapidly changing global marketplace and contribute to the development of society by graduating a high-quality technical workforce.

Prof. Maamar Benkraouda

Dean, College of Science

## About COS > Accreditation
All College programs are accredited by the Commission of Academic Accreditation , Ministry of Education, UAE, and all the undergraduate programs of the College are accredited by international accreditation organizations, where the B.Sc. in Chemistry has been accredited by both the Canadian Society for Chemistry in 2008 and by the Royal Society of Chemistry, UK in 2011. ## About COS > Community Service
Giving Back to the Community

All departments within the College of Science (COS) are actively involved in community and consultancy services. The COS routinely welcomes local schools to visit the laboratories. In addition, departmental representatives participate in local schools’ science exhibitions and fairs in an effort to further raise the interest of high school students in science and technology.

The COS faculty members usually serve as manuscript reviewers for highly-rated international scientific journals and are regularly involved in reviewing promotion files for other regional universities. The College of Science offers a broad range of scientific and technical services to industrial partners and governmental agencies. Areas of services include desert ecology, aquatic ecology and coastal zone management, inorganic chemistry, organic chemistry, biochemistry, analytical chemistry, groundwater exploration, archaeological and heritages sites studies, forensic geosciences, mineral resources, applied mathematics, numerical analysis, nano-physics, material science and engineering, and nuclear physics.
