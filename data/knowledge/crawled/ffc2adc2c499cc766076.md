## Department of Pathology > Overview
Pathology is a wide-ranging discipline. It covers several fields; some of the themes in pathology are histopathology, cytology, hematopathology, clinical chemistry, and human genetics. The Department of Pathology provides teaching and clinical services to all the above disciplines. The Deparment is involved in several interdisciplinary research projects with other departments.
