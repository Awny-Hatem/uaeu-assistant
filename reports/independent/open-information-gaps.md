# Information gaps requiring an authoritative answer

These are not invented answers or closed factual-coverage items. The tests deliberately include questions for which the available official material does not establish the requested fact. Review date: 29 September 2026.

| Question | What was checked | What remains unverified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q040: On what date does Spring 2028 start? | The published UAEU academic calendar and an official-domain search for Spring 2028. The available reviewed calendar covers 2026–2027. | An applicable 2027–2028 calendar entry for the beginning of Spring 2028. Reusing a 2027 date or extrapolating weekdays would be fabrication. |
| Q070: How many parking warnings can I get before my account is suspended? | The current [Vehicle Access Permit service](https://coreservices.uaeu.ac.ae/ServiceCatalog/details?serviceId=117), the undergraduate handbook's permit section, and official-domain English/Arabic searches. | A parking-account-specific warning/suspension threshold. General student disciplinary warnings are not evidence for a parking-account threshold. |
| Q016: Does the general undergraduate graduation GPA rule also establish medicine's rule? | AE06 explicitly excludes CMHS. The [current MD catalog](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/catalog/undergraduate/programs/doctor-of-medicine.shtml), dated 22 September 2026, describes minimum 233 credits, three phases and the Final Integrated Examination, but gives no numeric graduation CGPA. A separately inspected CMHS brochure is dated 2014/2015 and describes different BMed/MD structures; its BMed GPA is not a current MD rule. | A current MD-specific numeric graduation CGPA, if one applies, and the student's applicable program/cohort rules. Policy exclusion establishes a scope boundary, not proof that the same number cannot appear in another policy. The current catalog supplies useful program requirements, but does not close the numeric question. |

The searches did not establish those facts; they do **not** prove that no university office, internal system or other public page has them. An institutional content owner should supply the exact current policy/calendar, applicable audience, effective date and section before these gaps are marked as covered.

Older published policies and the 2023 handbook also need an institutional owner to confirm whether they have been superseded. The response reports identify versions where available. A successful URL response, current page footer or recent retrieval date is not a certification that an old policy remains governing.
