## Limits

- Discovered, fetched, approved and indexed are separate counts.
- An indexed publication is not proof of current intake, effective policy or complete extraction.
- A missing/pending/blocked source is not an assertion that UAEU does not publish the information.
- Individual research theses, old news/jobs and duplicate viewers remain reference-only/excluded with their reasons.
- Program pages include minors, microcredentials and accelerated options, not just full degrees.
- A programme overview need not contain every course prerequisite or model plan; per-page field observations are not proved topic-wide knowledge gaps.
