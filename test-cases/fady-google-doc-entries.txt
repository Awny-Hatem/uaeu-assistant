Tester name: Claude (fixes after Dr. Fady's review)
Date: 18 November 2026
Version tested: Local — feat/student-profiles-and-power (commit d7dd18f), production build
Question or scenario: How to raise ceiling?
Expected answer: Recognises raise ceiling / رفع سقف = request a seat in a full section (CLOSED SECTION override); gives the procedure with source.
Official UAEU source supporting the expected answer: https://coreservices.uaeu.ac.ae/Calendar/Calendar?culture=en, https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcsae/registration/pdf/course-registration-service.pdf, https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcsae/registration/pdf/registration_program_spring_2015_en.pdf, https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcsae/registration/policies/undergraduate/3_undergraduate_registration_and_enrollment-en.pdf
Complete conversation:
Student: How to raise ceiling?
Assistant: **Getting a seat in a full section ("raising the ceiling" / رفع سقف)**
"Raising the ceiling" means asking for a seat in a section that is already full (e.g. 40/40). In UAEU's registration system this is a **CLOSED SECTION override**.
1. **Confirm it's full:** in *Student Registration → Register for Classes*, a **Closed Section** error means no seats are left.
2. **Ask your College Advising Unit** for the override. UAEU's registration guidance sends closed-section and section-capacity questions there, and the college that offers the course sets its section ceiling. Give the course code, the section/CRN you need, and why (for example, your study plan or graduation).
3. **Do it during registration or add/drop.** Spring 2027: add/drop starts **4 January 2027**; the last day to add is **15 January 2027** ([academic calendar](https://coreservices.uaeu.ac.ae/Calendar/Calendar?culture=en)).
4. **If approved,** add the section yourself (*Add → Submit*) and check it shows **Registered**. If not, choose another section or course with seats.

An override is a request, not a guaranteed seat.

[Dr. Fady set] Case 7 — How to calculate GPA
Tester name: Claude (fixes after Dr. Fady's review)
Date: 18 November 2026
Version tested: Local — feat/student-profiles-and-power (commit d7dd18f), production build
Question or scenario: How do I calculate my GPA?
Expected answer: Grade table, formula, worked example, term vs cumulative, portal for official GPA.
Official UAEU source supporting the expected answer: https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/about/policies/pdf/admission/4_grades_and_grading-en.pdf
Complete conversation:
Student: How do I calculate my GPA?
Assistant: **GPA and grading scale: how to calculate your GPA**
| Grade | Marks | Points |
|---|---|---|
| A | 90–100 | 4.0 |
| A- | 87–89 | 3.7 |
| B+ | 84–86 | 3.3 |
| B | 80–83 | 3.0 |
| B- | 77–79 | 2.7 |
| C+ | 74–76 | 2.3 |
| C | 70–73 | 2.0 |
| C- | 67–69 | 1.7 |
| D+ | 64–66 | 1.3 |
| D | 60–63 | 1.0 |
| F | 0–59 | 0.0 |

**Formula:** GPA = Σ (credit hours × grade points, also called quality points) ÷ total credit hours counted in the GPA, reported to two decimal places. UAEU calculates both a term GPA and a cumulative GPA.

**Example:** a 3-credit A (3 × 4.0 = 12.0), a 4-credit B+ (4 × 3.3 = 13.2) and a 3-credit C (3 × 2.0 = 6.0) give 31.2 ÷ 10 = **3.12**.

Transfer and non-degree credits are not counted; special grades and repeated courses follow separate rules. Your official GPA is in the student portal. This scale excludes the College of Medicine and Health Sciences.

Complete conversation:
Student: How to drop out of the uni?
Assistant: **Withdrawing from the university**
Talk to your academic advisor first; a leave of absence may also be possible.
1. **Submit the request through eServices** (withdrawal from the University, or registration suspension) within the allowed period. Stopping attendance is not a withdrawal.
2. **Timing (policy AE-03):**
   - by the **end of week 4**: that semester's courses are not recorded on your transcript;
   - **weeks 5–6**: you receive **W** in all classes, and it counts toward your withdrawal/leave chances;
   - **after week 6**: not allowed, except for a medical emergency or other compelling circumstances, by applying to your **college Dean** (if approved, **AW** in all courses).
3. **Fees and refunds:** the refund depends on the date you submit. For Fall 2026 full-semester courses, requests from 24 August to **18 September 2026** got a 100% refund; after that the full tuition is due. Check the current term's dates on [Withdrawal Deadlines and Financial Obligations](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcsae/student_account_office/refunds.shtml).
4. **Complete clearance:** any refund is processed only after the required clearance procedures.

AE-03 applies to undergraduates outside the College of Medicine and Health Sciences.

[Dr. Fady set] Case 10 — How to apply for graduation
Tester name: Claude (fixes after Dr. Fady's review)
Date: 18 November 2026
Version tested: Local — feat/student-profiles-and-power (commit d7dd18f), production build
Question or scenario: How to apply for graduation?
Expected answer: Steps (where/portal or College Advising Unit), deadline for current term with calendar link, eligibility, what happens next; current policy.
Official UAEU source supporting the expected answer: https://coreservices.uaeu.ac.ae/Calendar/Calendar?culture=en, https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/about/pdfs/policies/6_degree_completion_and_graduation-en.pdf, https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/en/dvcsae/registration/policies/undergraduate/6_degree_completion_and_graduation-en.pdf
Complete conversation:
Student: How to apply for graduation?
Assistant: **How to apply for graduation (undergraduate)**
1. **When:** in the semester you expect to finish, no later than that term's last day to drop without failure: **18 September 2026** for Fall 2026 and **5 February 2027** for Spring 2027 ([academic calendar](https://coreservices.uaeu.ac.ae/Calendar/Calendar?culture=en)). Combining this procedure with those calendar dates gives the deadline; the calendar does not separately label them as graduation-application dates.
2. **Where:** submit the graduation (degree) application to your **College Advising Unit** and the **Admission and Registration Deanship**. The published procedure doesn't name a portal screen, so ask your College Advising Unit for the application and how to submit it.
3. **Check you're eligible:** all degree requirements, at least **120 credits** (some programs need more), **cumulative GPA 2.00** or higher, and **no Incomplete (I)** grades.
4. **What happens next:** your college confirms you've completed the requirements, then Admission and Registration certifies the degree. Degrees are awarded at the end of each semester; the official date is the semester's last day.

Keep a copy of your application. To check a submitted application's status, ask the College Advising Unit or Admission and Registration with your request reference; the published procedure does not establish a specific private tracking screen. This is policy AE-06, which the Registration Department still lists as current; it doesn't cover the College of Medicine and Health Sciences or graduate degrees.
